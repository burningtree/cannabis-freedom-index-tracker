After ten days of joint raids in Amambay, Senad and Brazil's Polícia Federal finished Operation
Nueva Alianza XXVII on 3 September 2021. [@1] Agencia IP reported that agents dismantled 49 narcotics
camps on 218 hectares of illegal cannabis and seized 19,280 kilograms of marijuana already packed
for transport. [@1]

## Where they worked

With anti-drug prosecutor Celso Morales, early incursions focused on Cadete Boquerón, Cerro Kuatia
and Rosalina in the Capitán Bado area. [@1] Later raids moved to María Auxiliadora and Chirigüelo
near Pedro Juan Caballero, all zones Senad described as holding large illicit plantations. [@1,2]

## Traffickers' losses

Senad said the destroyed crops could have yielded about 654 tonnes of finished marijuana and that
the operation inflicted roughly US$20 million in lost revenue on trafficking groups in Paraguay,
with La Nación adding that Brazilian street values would be at least three times higher. [@1,2]
La Nación summarised the outcome as more than 670 tonnes of marijuana "anuladas" alongside the
49 camps, 19,280 kilograms of ready product and 218 eradicated hectares. [@2] Senad framed the
campaign as targeting organised crime finances rather than small farmers. [@1]
