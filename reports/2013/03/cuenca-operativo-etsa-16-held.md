From 10:00 to 21:00 on Wednesday 27 March 2013, five prosecutors and about 70 Ecuadorian National
Police officers searched seven homes in Cuenca during Operativo Etsa. [@1] The Fiscalía Provincial
del Azuay said the targets were three groups involved in robberies, vehicle-part theft, organised
crime and drug dealing around El Arenal market and the Feria Libre fairground. [@1,2]

## Seizures and detention

Police detained 16 people and seized evidence including 900 grams of marijuana, cocaine base, cash,
vehicle engine parts and radios. [@1,2] Prosecutor Mateo Ríos said a month of undercover work had
yielded videos and photographs used in court. [@2] On 28 March Judge Sonia Cárdenas held a flagrancy
hearing and ordered preventive detention for all 16 on drug-trafficking charges. [@2] The
Fiscalía’s bulletins name no further court outcome. [@2]
