Badan Narkotika Nasional (BNN), Indonesia’s national narcotics board, destroyed 35 hectares of
cannabis at Pulo Kemukiman Lamteuba in Seulimeum district, Aceh Besar, on Monday 13 May 2013. [@1]
BNN chief Anang Iskandar said residents had tipped the agency off about the fields, and that the find
showed public willingness to report cultivation in Aceh. [@1]

## Mapping then burning

After the tip, BNN coordinated with Aceh provincial police and the national geospatial agency; on
5 May teams mapped 18 cannabis plots covering 35 hectares. [@1] Officers from BNN and Aceh police
then uprooted and burned the plants; JPNN reported that Anang Iskandar spoke at the site about
switching growers to legal crops such as patchouli, jabon and turmeric through alternative
development. [@1,2]

BNN said the plants were about two metres tall and five months old, close to harvest, but no owner
was caught. [@1] Anang Iskandar told JPNN that eradication and arrests would continue alongside
community programmes so farmers would not rely on cannabis. [@2] The opened accounts give no arrest
count for this burn. [@1,2]
