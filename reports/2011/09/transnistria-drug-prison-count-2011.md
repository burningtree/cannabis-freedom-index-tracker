The justice ministry reported 241 people imprisoned for drug offences, 11% of its sentenced prison population, and more than 200 people serving suspended sentences under supervision. Fifteen prisoners had terms longer than fifteen years, and more than 100 convicted people were undergoing court-ordered drug treatment.[@1] These figures combine all drugs; cannabis was among the substances prosecuted under the criminal code.[@1,2] [@1,2]
