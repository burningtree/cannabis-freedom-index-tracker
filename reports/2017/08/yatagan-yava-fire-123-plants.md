The fire broke out in a cultivated field at Yava, on the Muğla–Aydın highway in
Yatağan district, and spread into maquis, the dense scrub of the region, fanned
by wind. [@1] Gendarmerie officers investigating it concluded that the blaze
began when a spark jumped while workers welded an advertising sign. [@1] They
detained the three men who had been working on the sign, named in the reports as
H.Y., T.Ö. and S.K. [@1] The three were taken to the gendarmerie command so that
their statements could be taken. [@1]

One firefighting helicopter from the Muğla Regional Forestry Directorate, 13
water tankers, a bulldozer, 52 firefighting workers and fire brigade teams
fought the fire. [@2] Crews put the fire out quickly, but it still damaged 3.5
hectares of farmland and maquis. [@1]

Searching the area, the gendarmerie found 123 cannabis plants, known in Turkish
as Hint keneviri, hidden in the brush. [@1] The plants were sent to the
provincial Food, Agriculture and Livestock Directorate to be destroyed. [@2] The
gendarmerie opened a separate investigation into the plants. [@1]
