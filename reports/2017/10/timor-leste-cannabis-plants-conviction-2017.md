A Dili court sentenced Australian resident Tony Crean to three and a half years in prison in a case involving cannabis plants, according to Lusa.[@1] The report says he was convicted for possession and trafficking but acquitted of consumption after a negative urine test.[@1] He had been held since January, and his defence said it would appeal.[@1] [@1]
