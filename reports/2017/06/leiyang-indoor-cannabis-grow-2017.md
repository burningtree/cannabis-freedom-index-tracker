Police in Leiyang, Hunan, arrested six people after finding 31 cannabis plants
in a grow tent in a rented flat, in what the Leiyang public security bureau
called the city's first case of illegal cannabis cultivation. [@1] The plants
stood in 14 pots inside a blue-cloth tent in the bedroom, under halogen lamps,
with thermometers and humidity meters. [@1]

The case began at about 7 pm on 11 June 2017, when officers from the Caizichi
police station stopped a man surnamed Liang near the gate of Lingxiu Jiayuan on
Detailong Road; police said he had a drug record and admitted using drugs. [@2]
He told them his girlfriend, surnamed Wu and a minor, might be involved in drug
use and dealing. [@1] At about 10 pm officers detained Wu and another suspect,
Liang Moucong, seizing 39.44 grams of methamphetamine, known in China as ice,
and 2.2 grams of magu, a methamphetamine pill, from Liang Moucong. [@1] They
then searched the flat he rented and found the tent. [@1]

On the night of 12 June, police arrested three more drug users, Wen, Jiang and
He. [@2] The three were placed in administrative detention. [@2] Liang and Liang
Moucong were sent to compulsory isolated drug rehabilitation. [@1] Police said
Liang Moucong had used drugs since 2012 and had been caught several times; after
his release he bought cannabis seeds online and built an indoor greenhouse to
grow cannabis for his own use. [@1] The case was still under investigation. [@1]
