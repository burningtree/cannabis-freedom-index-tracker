Police destroyed more than 10,500 wild Manchurian cannabis plants near the
village of Romny in Krasnoarmeysky district, Primorsky Krai, the regional police
press service said. [@1] The plants grew in seven separate patches in the
countryside around Romny, a village in the district. [@3] Together the patches
covered almost one hectare of land in total. [@1] Officers destroyed the
cannabis by treating the whole plot with agrochemicals. [@1] The eradication
formed part of a preventive police operation called Maks-2017. [@3]

The drug control department of the Dalnerechensky inter-municipal police
division found the plants near the village during the operation. [@2] The police
said the operation was intended to counter illegal drug trafficking and to
protect public health and safety. [@2] Samples of the plants were sent to a
forensic unit for testing, which confirmed they were cannabis used to make
recreational drugs. [@2] The regional police said expert analysis showed the
plants could have been used to make narcotics. [@1]
