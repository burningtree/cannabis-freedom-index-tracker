Eight first offenders from Bankir village on Malekula each received fifty hours of community work and six months’ probation for cannabis cultivation or possession.[@1] The case concerned conduct dating to 2009, with several defendants describing single small plants.[@1] The judge applied the 2013 personal-growing guidance and warned one defendant that his church belief did not permit him to continue growing cannabis.[@1] [@1]
