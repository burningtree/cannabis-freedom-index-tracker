About 298,500 cannabis plants spread over 149.25 acres (60.4 hectares) of forest
land in Sepahijala district, Tripura, were destroyed on Saturday 16 December
2023. [@1] It was one of the biggest destructions of cannabis cultivation
recorded in the state up to then. [@1]

The drives bring together Tripura Police, the Border Security Force, the Central
Reserve Police Force, the Narcotics Control Bureau and forest department
officials. [@1] In October 2023 Chief Minister Manik Saha urged the state's
youth to declare a war on drugs, saying it had already taken a serious toll
among students and young people. [@1]

Several hectares of forest land in the state have been freed from cannabis
cultivation in the past few days. [@1] The operations have shown that forest
land in the state was being used for marijuana cultivation. [@1] Separate
operations were carried out at various locations in the state as part of the
ongoing effort to wipe out the drugs menace. [@1]
