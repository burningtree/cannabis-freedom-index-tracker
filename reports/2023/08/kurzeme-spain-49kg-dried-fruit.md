Latvian police, working with Spanish colleagues, said they had stopped 49
kilograms of marijuana from being brought into Latvia. [@1] On 8 June, officers
and the Omega counter-terror unit detained four men in Kurzeme, born in 1978,
1980, 1984 and 1993. [@1] On 8 August police detained a fifth person, born in
1984. [@1] Five people were detained in all, and four of them were remanded in
custody. [@1]

Police had been told that marijuana was being prepared for smuggling from Spain
to Latvia. [@1] Investigators working with the Prosecutor General's Office of
Latvia, the Spanish prosecution service and Spanish police units found 49.4 kg
of marijuana hidden in a dried-fruit shipment at a logistics warehouse, intended
for sale on the Latvian market. [@1]

Criminal investigations in both Spain and Latvia were still under way when the
police account was published on 5 September 2023. [@1]
