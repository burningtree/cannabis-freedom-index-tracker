Himachal Pradesh police arrested 34 people during an overnight operation in the
Manikaran valley of Kullu district on 10–11 October 2023. [@1] Officers seized
5.71 kg of cannabis, 110.57 kg of hemp seeds and 91,000 rupees in cash. [@2] Six
cases were registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act. [@1] In the Gahar area, police seized about 2.20 kg of cannabis and
the 91,000 rupees from Ravi Bhadur of Nepal and 19 others, and arrested all of
them. [@1] At Rajag Thach, they seized 3.51 kg of cannabis and 110.57 kg of hemp
seeds from Shiva of Nepal and 13 others, and arrested all 14. [@1]

Police found 2.83 lakh (283,000) cannabis plants growing illegally on 235 bighas
of forest land, about 19 hectares. [@1] The Forest Department was asked to
destroy the crop. [@2] A case was registered against unidentified people for
more than 2 lakh plants on 150 bighas at Rajag Thach, near Grahan village. [@1]
Another 3,000 plants stood on one bigha belonging to Charanjeet of Grahan
village. [@1] About 50,000 plants were grown on 50 bighas of forest land at
Gahar village. [@1] A further 33,000 plants were found on 35 bighas near
Brahmganga forest. [@1]

Sanjay Kundu, the state director general of police, said 1,000 plants can yield
1 kg of charas, a hashish made from cannabis resin, so the crop could have
produced 283 kg of high-quality charas. [@2] He said illegal cultivation on
forest and private land in Kullu, especially the Manikaran valley, had continued
despite earlier destruction drives and needed different planning. [@2] Central
Range Deputy Inspector General Madhusudan led the operation, and three teams
trekked six to seven hours to reach the sites. [@1] Police said they would keep
zero tolerance towards drugs and ensure the Manikaran valley did not become a
"narco-tourism destination". [@1]
