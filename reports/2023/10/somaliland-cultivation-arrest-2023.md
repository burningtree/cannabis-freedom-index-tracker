A short report published on 13 October 2023 provides a rare concrete example of cannabis
enforcement in Somaliland. The Horn Tribune said the territory's police had arrested a man accused
of planting cannabis in the yard of a house he owned in Hargeisa.

The event is useful evidence, but its limits are just as important as the arrest itself. It was an
allegation at the time of publication, not a reported conviction, and the article did not identify
a sentence or even a formal charge.

## What police reported

The newspaper reproduced a brief statement attributed to Somaliland police. It identified the
suspect and said the house was in the Ga'an Libaah area and occupied by tenants. Traffic-law
enforcement police worked with the local district police station, according to the report. Two
local police commanders were named as reporting the arrest.

The article also stated broadly that cannabis was banned throughout Somaliland. It did not quote a
section of legislation or reproduce a court document. The arrest can therefore support the narrower
claim that police acted on an alleged cultivation case; it cannot by itself supply the missing
details of the governing offence or penalty.

## What the report does not show

No number of plants was given. The report did not say whether cannabis had been harvested, whether
any material was tested, or whether another person was accused. It provided no later court result.
One reported arrest also cannot establish how frequently police enforce the ban across Somaliland.

Those gaps are reflected in the [Somaliland country profile](https://cannabisfreedom.fyi/country/xs/). The profile records the
case in its cultivation, enforcement and arrests sections while stating that broader sentencing
practice remains unconfirmed. That is more precise than treating a single local report as a
national arrest rate.

## Why the event is in the history

Somaliland previously had no dated item in the index history. This report does not identify when
the prohibition began, but it gives the history a checked, country-specific enforcement event while
research for the underlying legislation continues.

Nothing moved in the index. An arrest report is evidence of enforcement, not a change in law, and
