The seizure happened at the India-Bangladesh border at Tarali, a village in
Bithari-Hakimpur gram panchayat under Swarupnagar police station in Basirhat.
[@1] When the troops chased them, the smugglers left a bag behind and fled. [@1]
A search of the bag found 6 kg of ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1]

The ganja had a market value of about ₹60,000. [@1] The BSF handed the cannabis
to the Tentulia customs office. [@1] The border force then began searching for
the smugglers, who had escaped. [@1]

The BSF guards the India-Bangladesh border in the Basirhat area. [@1] Smugglers'
activity along the Basirhat stretch of the border has been increasing. [@1]
