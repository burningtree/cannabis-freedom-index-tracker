A joint patrol of the RI–PNG border task force Yonif 725/Woroagi and Merauke
Customs found a cannabis field of about 200 square metres (0.02 hectares) in
forest at Km 56, Sesnuk District, Boven Digoel Regency, South Papua. [@1]
Commander Lt Col Inf Syafruddin Mutasidasi said the joint team found the field
while patrolling the forest around Km 56. [@1] The patrol took place on
Wednesday 14 June 2023, he said. [@1] Officers counted 116 cannabis plants in
the field. [@1]

The army said it did not know who owned the plants. [@1] The commander said he
was coordinating with Merauke Customs and Boven Digoel police for legal
follow-up. [@2] Syafruddin said the field was a serious concern for young
Papuans. [@1] He said the task force was still investigating the discovery and
asked the public to help watch for cannabis cultivation. [@1]

The discovery came from information developed after the April 2023 arrest of two
cannabis couriers by the task force's Pos KM 53. [@1] The commander said the
joint team with Merauke Customs was formed after that information. [@3]
