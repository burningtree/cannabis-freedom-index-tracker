A farmer’s tip led the Third Judicial District Drug Task Force and Greene County Sheriff’s Office to
a marijuana field about a mile off Warrensburg Road on the afternoon of 13 September 2007. [@2]
Task Force Director Scott Castle said one of four armed men guarding the plot was arrested that
afternoon while three others remained at large. [@1]

## Air and ground search

Tennessee Highway Patrol helicopter pilot Lieutenant Terry Botts helped verify the field and locate
the armed suspect, according to a Department of Safety release. [@1] The release described the
patch as 50 to 75 yards wide and 600 to 700 yards long, with an estimated eight to twelve thousand
plants five to fourteen feet tall and a street value of about one million dollars. [@1]

Greeneville Sun reporting from the scene said agents had watched the patch for several days before
moving in, and that officers worked until after dark cutting plants and hauling them out on all-
terrain vehicles. [@2] Sheriff Steve Burns estimated about 8,000 “well-budded” plants and said
suspects had been living in an abandoned log structure with food and cooking gear on the property. [@2]
The same article said officers seized a .22-calibre semiautomatic rifle and an “AK-47-type” assault
rifle abandoned in the field. [@2]
