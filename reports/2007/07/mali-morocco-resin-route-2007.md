In early 2007 a route for smuggling cannabis resin from Morocco through Mauritania, Mali, Niger and Chad and on into either Egypt or Sudan was identified.[@1] A total of 5.7 tons of cannabis resin was seized in five seizures in Niger in the 2006–2007 period.[@1] The case put Mali on a trans-Saharan trafficking map linking Moroccan resin to markets further east.[@1] [@1]
