Soldiers and police uprooted 375,000 fully grown marijuana plants worth about ₱75 million from a
mountain plantation straddling Sugpon in Ilocos Sur and Bacun in Benguet. [@1] Chief Superintendent
Leopoldo Bataoil, the Philippine National Police director for Region 1, said the plantation covered
about 25 hectares and that the operation had already lasted two days without all plants removed.
[@1]

## Discovery and reinforcements

Army Colonel Loreto Rirao said troops under Captain Edmund delos Santos of the 50th Infantry
Battalion, the 53rd Military Intelligence Company and the Ilocos Sur provincial police began the
mission on Saturday morning after finding the fields during an anti-rebel sweep. [@1] Reinforcements
and helicopter gunships were called in to speed uprooting and burning, Bataoil said, because
marijuana cultivation had become a major NPA income source in the area. [@1] Neither Bataoil nor
Rirao reported any arrests when the Star filed its story. [@1] Lieutenant General Bonifacio Ramos of
Northern Luzon Command praised the joint force, but the article gives no court or rebel response.
[@1]
