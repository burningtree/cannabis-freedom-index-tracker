On Wednesday 19 March 2025, gendarmes from the Escadron Départemental de Sécurité Routière
(EDSR) of the Réunion gendarmerie, working on an enquiry, found two illegal outdoor cannabis
plantations on the island. [@1,2]

The two sites held 592 cannabis plants in total; gendarmes destroyed all of them. [@1,2] Two
people were placed in garde à vue and received summons to appear before the courts. [@1,2]

Press summaries published in May 2025 reproduce the gendarmerie account but do not locate the
plots beyond “two cultures” on Réunion. [@1,2]
