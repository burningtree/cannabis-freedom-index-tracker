On 12 March 2025 operatives of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) seized seven
large cartons holding 60 parcels of Loud, a potent cannabis strain, at a logistics company in
Lagos after the shipment arrived from the United States. [@1,2]

On 24 March the agency arrested Arokodare Damil Ebenezer, 43, who runs Damillionz Takeout, at Bay
Lounge on Admiralty Way in Lekki while he waited for a fresh consignment. [@1,2]

Officers searched his Lekki residence the same day and recovered 94 grams of cannabis, a grinder
and other paraphernalia, bringing the total weight seized from him to 32.24 kilograms. [@1,2]

In a statement quoted by both outlets, NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi said Ebenezer told
investigators he had been in the illicit drug trade since 2017. [@1,2]
