On Friday 14 March 2025 police in Matadi, Kongo Central, arrested two cannabis suppliers
during Operation Ndobo. [@1,2] Radio Okapi named them as Mavambu Kitsofi, known as Muke
Tonga, and Barumé Moïse, an adjutant-chef at the headquarters of the 12th military region
of the Congolese armed forces (FARDC). [@1]

Officers seized 10 sacks of cannabis and presented the men to the press the same day,
Radio Okapi said. [@1] Each suspect was caught with five sacks and rolls of kraft paper
used to pack the drug for retail sale, according to the provincial police commissioner. [@1]

Okapinews reported that police apprehended Moïse and a civilian it named Mavambu Kinzonzi,
alias Moke Tonga, with roughly eight sacks and packaging paper during the same operation. [@2]
The provincial commissioner warned that some security force members were involved in the
illicit trade. [@1,2]
