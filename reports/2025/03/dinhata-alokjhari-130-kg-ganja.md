Dinhata police raided a house in the Alokjhari gram panchayat area of Dinhata-1
block, Cooch Behar district, on Friday 7 March 2025, acting on a tip-off. [@1]
Officers searched the house and recovered 130 kg of ganja. [@1] They arrested
the house owner, Tapas Roy, on a charge of keeping the drug. [@1]

The operation was led by Jaydeep Modak, inspector-in-charge of Dinhata police
station. [@1] Ganga Chhetri, block development officer of Dinhata-1 block, was
present. [@1]

Police said Roy had brought the ganja from various places and stored it at his
house. [@1] Police said he later smuggled the drug on. [@1] A case was
registered against him under specific sections. [@1]
