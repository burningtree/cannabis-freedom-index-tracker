Colonel Hossein Mafi, commander of Rey County police, told reporters on 17 Aban 1404 (8 November
2025) that officers had received information about the transfer and storage of a large quantity of
drugs in western Tehran Province. [@1,2]

Anti-narcotics police verified the tip, coordinated with the judiciary and raided the location in a
surprise operation. [@1,2] They seized 570 kilograms of marijuana and arrested two traffickers. [@1,2]

Mafi said the case was the largest marijuana seizure recorded so far in the current Iranian year
within his command area, and credited continuous intelligence work and public cooperation with
police. [@1,2]
