A Magadan court sentenced former local polytechnic teacher Sergey Karmazov
to six years in a strict-regime penal colony for illegal cultivation of
narcotic plants and manufacture of narcotic drugs, a RIA Novosti correspondent
reported from the hearing on 19 November 2025. [@1] Police had found two
equipped grow rooms at a relative's house where Karmazov and students had
gathered for a ritual that officers interrupted in January 2025. [@1]

Regional Interior Ministry spokesperson Kseniya Lukina told journalists that
officers seized 35 cannabis plants and several large bags of marijuana from
hidden rooms. [@1] Investigators also established an attempted sale of more
than four kilograms of dried narcotic plant material. [@1] Gazeta.Ru, citing
RIA, reported that Karmazov had tried to involve students in sacrificing a bull
for course credit and asked police to let him finish the rite. [@2]

The court found Karmazov guilty of cultivating narcotic plants and processing
psychotropic substances and imposed six years' imprisonment. [@1]
