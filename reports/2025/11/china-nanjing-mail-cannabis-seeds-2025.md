Jinling Customs officers at the Nanjing post office seized 25 cannabis seeds in
the inbound mail channel, Chinanews reported on 25 November 2025 from a
“Customs Release” WeChat notice. [@1]

After risk-based screening, one parcel showed an abnormal X-ray image. [@1]
Officers found loose magazine pages with several transparent bags tucked
between them; each bag held three to five seeds, 25 in total. [@1]

Customs destroyed the mail. [@1] Chinanews quoted article 352 of China's
Criminal Law, which can punish trading, transporting, carrying or holding
non-inactivated seeds or seedlings of controlled drug plants such as poppy when
amounts are large enough for criminal charges. [@1]

The notice did not give the sender country or any arrest. [@1]
