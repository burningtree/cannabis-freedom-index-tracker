Police in Kuresoi North intensified anti-narcotics operations with the arrest
of three suspected peddlers found with multiple rolls of cannabis sativa at
Kwa Manga in Nyota sub-location during routine patrol. [@1] [@2]

Officers searching the trio uncovered concealed narcotics plus packaging
materials believed used for distribution; the three were held at the
sub-county station pending processing and arraignment while police probed
the drugs' source, destination and any wider network. [@1]

Rural and peri-urban corridors like Nyota increasingly serve as narcotics
transit routes from production zones to urban markets, prompting
intelligence-led patrols and roadblocks. [@1]
