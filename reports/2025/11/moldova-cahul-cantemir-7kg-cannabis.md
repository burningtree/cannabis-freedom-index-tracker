Officers from the Interior Ministry's southern investigations directorate spent
November 2025 documenting several people suspected of growing, holding and selling
drugs in Cahul and Cantemir districts. [@1] [@2] On 28 November they searched the
homes of four people aged 39 to 53. [@1] [@2]

Inside they found hydroponic drugs and dried hemp plants. [@1] [@2] Police put the
finished plant material at about seven kilograms and said it could have fetched more
than two million lei on the black market. [@1] [@2] The drugs were sent for expert
analysis while investigators looked for other people involved. [@2] Moldovan law
allows three to seven years in prison for such offences if a court convicts. [@1]
