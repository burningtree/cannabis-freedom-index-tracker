Police in Tapanuli Selatan arrested a 45-year-old farmer identified by the
initials AN in Sihuik Huik village, Angkola Selatan, on Tuesday 11 November
2025. [@3] They found 29 cannabis plants growing among the oil palms on his
land. [@1] The arrest followed a report from residents suspicious of activity in
the palm-oil plot. [@1] Officers went to his house with the village head, and he
admitted planting the cannabis. [@1] They uprooted all 29 plants and seized them
as evidence. [@1]

AN told investigators he had grown cannabis since early 2025. [@1] He said he
bought cannabis for Rp45,000 from a man around February 2025 and planted the
seeds. [@2] Police said an acquaintance identified as D, who supplied the
seedlings, is still being sought. [@1] AN first moved nine seedlings into the
palm plot. [@2] He harvested the plants in mid-2025 and sold the cannabis to
others. [@2] He then planted 29 new plants in September. [@2]

AN is being held at the Tapanuli Selatan narcotics unit. [@1] He has been
charged under Articles 114 and 111 of Indonesia's 2009 narcotics law. [@3] The
charge carries a maximum of 20 years in prison. [@3] Police said the district
police would not stop pursuing drug trafficking. [@1] They asked residents to
report suspicious drug activity. [@1]
