On 19 November 2025 officers from the Dâmbovița organised-crime service, DIICOT territorial
prosecutors, Ploiești anti-mafia police and gendarmes executed seven home search warrants across
Dâmbovița County in a high-risk drug-trafficking case. [@1,2]

Investigators said that throughout 2025 three suspects repeatedly bought cannabis seeds through
online applications to start and expand illegal crops meant for sale. [@1,2] Hearings were held at
the DIICOT territorial office in Dâmbovița; the statements stress that the accused retain
presumption of innocence. [@1,2] The opened accounts name no plants or quantities seized during
the raids. [@1,2]
