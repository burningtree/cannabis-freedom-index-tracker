Two police officers were among seven suspects arrested on Wednesday evening
after a sting operation found them ferrying cannabis worth Ksh1.7 million in
Kikuyu, Kiambu County. [@1]

Acting on a tip-off, officers mounted a roadblock at Gitaru along the
Nakuru-Nairobi Highway targeting two vehicles at around 1:50 am; on
interception, 59 kg of cannabis sativa hidden inside three large bags was
recovered from one vehicle. [@1] A search of the second, escort vehicle
identified its driver as a constable attached to Buruburu Police Station and
his colleague, also a constable, from Juja Police Station; four other
occupants rode in the escort car. [@1]

Police opened investigations into possible links between the officers and a
larger narcotics trafficking network operating between Nairobi and Nakuru;
both vehicles were detained at Kikuyu Police Station as exhibits with the
suspects in custody. [@1]
