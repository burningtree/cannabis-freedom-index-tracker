Guangzhou Customs seized 68 cannabis seeds in the inbound mail channel,
according to a “Customs Release” WeChat notice reported by Chinanews. [@1]
Guangzhou Post Office Customs officers were screening incoming mail when an
item declared as a men's cotton T-shirt showed an unusual X-ray image. [@1]

Inside they found a white shirt wrapping sealed bags and plastic tubes holding
68 plant seeds. [@1] Technical testing confirmed all 68 were cannabis seeds. [@1]
Customs cited article 352 of China's Criminal Law, which criminalises trading,
transporting, carrying or holding non-inactivated seeds or seedlings of
controlled drug plants such as poppy in larger amounts, with up to three years'
 imprisonment, detention, surveillance or fines. [@2]
