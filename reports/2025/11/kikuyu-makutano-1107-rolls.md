Kikuyu police arrested four suspected drug peddlers in an intelligence-led
operation at Makutano following a public tip-off about an active distribution
network. [@1] [@2]

Officers raided early on Sunday, November 30, 2025, seizing 1,107 rolls and
6 kg of bhang with an estimated street value of Ksh 210,000; the suspects
were held in custody for processing and formal charging. [@1] [@2]

The NPS commended public cooperation behind the raid, reiterating its
commitment to sustained anti-narcotics operations. [@1]
