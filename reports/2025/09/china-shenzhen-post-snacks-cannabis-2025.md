Shenzhen Post Office Customs seized cannabis with a gross weight of 2,103.1 g in
transit cargo, according to a “Customs Release” WeChat notice reported by
Guangming Online and Southcn.com on 5 September 2025. [@1,2] On the day of the
check, officers screening transit parcels saw an abnormal X-ray image. [@1,2]
The parcel held eight boxes of snacks whose weight did not match the packaging;
officers judged it might conceal drugs. [@1,2] Inside the snack wrappers they
found plant material wrapped in multiple layers of plastic. [@1,2] A reagent
test was positive for tetrahydrocannabinol and laboratory analysis confirmed
THC in the plants. [@1,2] Customs said the case was transferred to its
anti-smuggling branch. [@1,2]
