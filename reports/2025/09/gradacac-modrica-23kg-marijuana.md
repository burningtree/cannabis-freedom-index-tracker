Drug officers from the Tuzla canton interior ministry searched a passenger car driven by N.K., born
in 1969 and living in Modriča, in Gradačac on 18 September 2025 under a Gradačac court warrant. [@1,2]
They seized plant material in the vehicle that looked like marijuana. [@1,2]

Follow-up searches of N.K.’s house, outbuildings, yard and a nearby plot in Modriča yielded a
larger stash police preliminarily weighed at about 23 kilogrammes, plus seven stalks resembling
Indian hemp about two metres tall. [@1,2] The cantonal prosecutor was notified and ordered N.K.
detained on suspicion of unauthorised production and trafficking in narcotics under the Federation
criminal code. [@1,2] Police said he would be handed to the cantonal prosecutor after processing,
alongside colleagues from Modriča station. [@1,2]
