On 2 September 2025 a joint patrol on the Keta railway in Xinjiang found a
large stand of suspected wild cannabis growing beside cropland between Hojirt
in Emin County and Tiechangou in Toli County. [@1] The team included officers
from the Kuytun railway public security bureau's Emin station, local police,
township officials and rail-side enterprises. [@1]

Emin station chief Song Yang said cannabis is a controlled drug plant even when
it appears to grow naturally, and that leaves, flowers and seed can be processed
into drugs. [@1] He warned that seeds can spread on train drafts and high winds
and sprout new patches elsewhere along the line. [@1]

The patrol cut and dug out the plants with sickles and spades and said it
 cleared more than 20,000 suspected wild cannabis plants, removing what it
called a source of poison and a safety risk to rail operations. [@1] Song said
drug plants would not be tolerated and that the clearance helped secure the line
during a sensitive period for transport. [@1]
