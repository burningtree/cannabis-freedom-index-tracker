Police in Diani, Kwale County, arrested two German nationals and a Kenyan
woman after a raid uncovered what officers believe to be a cannabis farm in
Mwabungo village. [@1]

Acting on a tip-off, officers stormed a residence in the Romika area and
found green plants suspected to be cannabis sativa being cultivated within
the compound. [@1] The suspects were Heck Klaus Edmun and Wegener Geb Sikora,
both German passport holders on tourist visas, and Kenyan citizen Natasha
Wangari. [@1]

Investigators processed and documented the scene before taking the trio
into custody; police confirmed the suspects would be arraigned in court.
[@1] The arrests came barely a month after police in the same area arrested
an Italian national and a Kenyan accomplice at a fortified one-acre grow
house in Mwabungo. [@1]
