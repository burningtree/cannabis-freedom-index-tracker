On 23 and 24 September 2025 prosecutors, police and military police searched 330 places in 20 jurisdictions and arrested 245 people for growing, using or possessing cannabis. They seized 1.68 kg of cannabis, 83 cannabis products, 97 vape pods and 581 pieces of paraphernalia. The High Prosecutors' Office said the operation was meant to uncover hidden users and reject calls for legalisation. [@1]
