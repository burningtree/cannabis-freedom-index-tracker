St Mary police say they acted on intelligence and carried out a snap raid at a
premises in Annotto Bay on Sunday 21 September 2025. [@1] [@2] They seized a large
quantity of vegetable matter resembling marijuana and found an unlicensed ganja farm
covering about two acres. [@1] [@2]

One person was taken into custody and another was being sought for questioning. [@1]
[@2] Photographs released by the division show officers among uprooted plants and
police vehicles at the site. [@1] Police said unlicensed cultivation helps fund
organised crime, including guns-for-drugs networks that undermine local security. [@1]
[@2] McKoy's News reported that investigations were continuing. [@2]
