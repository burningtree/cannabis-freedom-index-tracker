Prosecutors charged five people after police found 1,337 cannabis plants, weighing more than 143 kilograms, in the village of Vakiri in Sighnaghi municipality. Searches of homes linked to the suspects also turned up 3.3 kilograms of dried marijuana already packaged, drying equipment and digital scales. Two of the five were also charged with aiding the illegal purchase, storage and sale of marijuana, and prosecutors said two further people had assisted the growers. [@1]
