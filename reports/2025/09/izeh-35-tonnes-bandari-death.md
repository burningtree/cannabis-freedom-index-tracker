Khuzestan provincial police commander Brigadier Seyed Mahmoud Mirfeizi said Izeh
anti-drug officers learned traffickers were moving drugs through a mountainous area
of Dehdz county and put the suspects under surveillance. [@1,2] On Tuesday 16
September 2025, provincial and Izeh anti-drug police carried out two operations
with armed clashes against what the force called “merchants of death”. [@1,2]

Mirfeizi said officers seized 3.5 tonnes of marijuana, known locally as “gol”, and
arrested two traffickers. [@1,2] Second Lieutenant Asghar Bandari Dali, an Izeh
anti-drug officer, was shot in the head during the fighting and killed. [@1,2]

The two detainees were handed to the judiciary after case files were opened, the
commander said. [@1,2] Because the site was remote, roadless and unsuitable for
storage, and moving the volume was impractical, police destroyed the seized
marijuana on the spot after coordinating with the Izeh prosecutor, he added. [@1,2]
