Dehong prefecture police in Yunnan uprooted 436 illegally cultivated cannabis
plants after a targeted eradication drive, according to Yunnan political-legal
publicity republished on 28 September 2025. [@1,2]

The Dehong public security bureau combined drone aerial mapping with on-the-ground
“precision strikes” and education, the releases said. [@1,2] While checking
urban areas, fields and forest margins for drug-source plants, teams found six
illegal plots, formed a rapid task force with township officials, counted and
documented the plants, and then destroyed the cannabis. [@1,2] Growers were
punished administratively or criminally—the articles say only that planters were
“punished” (处罚) without spelling out numbers or sentences. [@1,2]

Officers also briefed residents on spotting cannabis and poppy plants and on
legal liability, urging tip-offs. [@1,2]

Neither repost names the growers or the exact day the drones flew before
publication. [@1,2]
