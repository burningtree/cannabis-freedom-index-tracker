On the morning of 16 September 2025 agents from Paraguay's anti-drug secretariat SENAD, with
special forces and the CODI–FTC joint task force, raided cannabis plots in Asentamiento Aguerito,
Santa Rosa del Aguaray, San Pedro department. [@1,2]

Anti-drug prosecutor Jorge Encina Alder led the operation through SENAD's Concepción regional
office. [@1,2] Teams burned two growing plots of three and five hectares and destroyed 950
kilograms of chopped marijuana packed in 63 bags. [@1,2]

SENAD calculated that 24,950 kilograms of marijuana were taken out of circulation and valued the
hit to organised crime at about USD 3,742,500 using an assumed USD 150 per kilogram on the
Brazilian market. [@1,2] The reports name no one arrested. [@1,2]
