Border Guard land patrols in the Al-Aridah sector of Saudi Arabia’s Jazan region arrested an
Ethiopian national for violating border security rules by attempting to smuggle 34 kilograms of
cannabis into the kingdom. [@1] Okaz dated the interception to 27 September 2025 and said initial
legal procedures were completed before the suspect and the seized drugs were delivered to the
competent authority. [@2,3]

Security agencies again urged citizens and residents to report drug smuggling or trafficking through
the kingdom’s emergency and anti-narcotics hotlines. [@2]
