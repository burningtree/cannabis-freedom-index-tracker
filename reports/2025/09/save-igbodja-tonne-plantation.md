Benin's Police républicaine discovered and dismantled a cannabis field at Igbodja, in the commune of
Savè in the Collines department. [@1,2,3] Le Béninois Libéré, citing Le Potentiel, put the plot at
about one hectare and said the harvest seized on site was estimated at more than a tonne of narcotic
product. [@1] Africaho and Benin Web TV likewise reported more than a tonne of cannabis ready for
sale. [@2,3]

Four people suspected of cultivating and running the plantation were arrested on the spot. [@1,2,3]
They were held by the police and were expected to be transferred toward the Office central de
répression du trafic illicite des drogues (Ocertid) and then before the Cour de répression des
infractions économiques et du terrorisme (CRIET). [@1,2,3] The sources say the field was intended to
supply a local and regional distribution network. [@3]
