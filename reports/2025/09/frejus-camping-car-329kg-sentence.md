Modane customs officers stopped a German-registered camper on the afternoon of 10 September 2025 on
the motorway area at the Fréjus tunnel. [@1] The driver and his passenger said they were returning
from holidays in Spain and heading to Italy. [@1]

Agents found two hidden compartments in the sleeping area and a third cache, with dozens of
heat-sealed bags totalling 329.28 kilograms of cannabis herb. [@1] The judicial tribunal in
Albertville sentenced the driver to three years and six months in prison and the passenger to the
same term with the sentence suspended, plus a permanent ban from French territory and a €500,000
customs fine for each. [@1]

Le Dauphiné, reporting the same interception, describes the driver as an Italian national sentenced
to 38 months in prison and the same €500,000 customs penalty. [@2] ICI notes the figures come from
a customs press release covering this case and a later, separate haul on the A43. [@1]
