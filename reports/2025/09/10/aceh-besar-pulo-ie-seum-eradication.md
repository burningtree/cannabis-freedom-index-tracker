On 10 September 2025 a joint BNN task force destroyed cannabis on two hillsides in Aceh Besar
regency, Aceh, after investigators mapped the plots between 31 August and 7 September. [@1,2]

At Desa Pulo in Seulimeum subdistrict, BNN said officers found about 1.3 hectares with roughly
3,500 plants and an estimated 1,400 kilograms of wet cannabis; at Desa Ie Seum in Masjid Raya,
about 0.7 hectare held roughly 1,500 plants and 900 kilograms wet weight. [@1,2] Led by cannabis
destruction task-force chief Commissioner Riki Kurniawan, 117 personnel from BNN, the military,
police, prosecutors, customs and local agencies burned about 5,000 plants on site, totalling
roughly 2.3 tonnes. [@1,2]

The releases describe the action as mandatory destruction under Indonesia's narcotics law and
warn growers face five to twenty years' imprisonment or life sentences, but name no one arrested
at these fields. [@1,2]
