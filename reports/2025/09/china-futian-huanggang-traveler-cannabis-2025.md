A male traveler who did not declare his luggage was stopped at Futian checkpoint
when Huanggang customs officers saw an abnormal X-ray image, according to a
“Customs Release” WeChat notice reported by Chinanews. [@1,2] Officers searched
his shoulder bag and found five transparent zip-lock bags of brown plant
material. [@1,2] A field test indicated cannabis; the Shenzhen customs forensic
centre confirmed tetrahydrocannabinol and identified the material as cannabis
with a net weight of 49.73 g. [@1,2] Customs said the case was handed to its
anti-smuggling department. [@1,2] The notices name no arrest, charge or
sentence. [@1,2]
