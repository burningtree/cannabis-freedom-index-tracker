Vienna police seized about a tonne of cannabis in a 3,200 sq m hall in Liesing that had been
disguised as a CBD production site, the force said on 11 June 2026. [@1,2] The raid took place on
9 September 2025 and led to the arrest of 13 people. [@1]

Officers found 9,720 cannabis plants, about 300 kg of harvested cannabis and about 500 kg of cannabis
ready for sale, for a total of 1,044 kg with a THC content of 15 to 20 per cent. [@1] They also seized
€1.4 million in cash, gold bars, a Rolex watch, false identity documents and a Glock pistol. [@1]
Police estimated the cannabis's value at €4.5 million. [@1,2]

Most of those arrested were gardeners from Serbia and Bosnia who were in Austria without permission,
police said. [@1] Three Austrian nationals aged 42, 46 and 55 are accused of organising production,
storage, recruitment, distribution and sales; one was arrested in Croatia and extradited to Austria
in February. [@1]

The operation was code-named "Psycho", after a nickname used for one of the main suspects, and
investigators had been watching the group since 2024. [@1] The force described the plantation as
highly professional and said the suspects had taken over the infrastructure of an earlier CBD
business. [@1]
