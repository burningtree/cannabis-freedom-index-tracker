Zagreb County police finished a criminal inquiry into a 44-year-old citizen of Bosnia and
Herzegovina suspected of unauthorised production and trafficking of drugs. [@1,2]

On Tuesday 30 September 2025 officers searched his house and outbuildings in Dugo Selo and found two
improvised indoor cannabis laboratories containing 132 plants between 80 and 100 centimetres tall,
225 grams of cannabis, a precision scale with drug traces and packing material. [@1,2] Investigators
said he used lighting, ventilation and air filtration to grow cannabis for resale, drying and
repackaging it on the same property, and they seized cash they linked to drug sales. [@1,2]

After the search police filed a criminal report and placed him in pre-trial detention. [@1,2]
