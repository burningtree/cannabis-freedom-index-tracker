On 25 September 2025 customs officers at the Monnaie toll on the A10 stopped a Bulgarian driver's
articulated lorry travelling from Málaga toward Nottingham, alerted by a strong smell from the
trailer. [@1,2] They found 513 kg of cannabis resin in 78 brown packages hidden among seven pallets
that also held disposable e-cigarette dispensers. [@1]

The 53-year-old, with no prior record according to ICI, denied knowing about the drugs and said two
Spaniards had loaded the pallets in Málaga. [@1,2] Tours criminal court convicted him in immediate
hearing on 29 September 2025, sentencing him to twelve months in prison with suspension, a five-year
ban on entering France and an 80,000 euro customs fine. [@1,2]
