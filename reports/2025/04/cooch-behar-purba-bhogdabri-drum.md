Police arrested Samrat Mandal, the owner of the house. [@1] The arrest was for
the offence of keeping narcotics. [@1] Officers of Sitalkuchi police station in
Cooch Behar district recovered the cannabis after acting on a tip-off from a
secret source. [@1] The recovery was made on Saturday morning in Purba Bhogdabri
village, in Bhaoirthana gram panchayat, the village-level council. [@1] The
house, an ordinary-looking building inside the village, had been made a base for
illegal trade. [@1] Police had information that illegal activity had been going
on there for a long time, and they raided the home of village resident Samrat
Mandal that morning. [@1]

A thorough search of the house found nothing, but the officers refused to give
up and then noticed a mound of earth at one side of the yard. [@1] Digging into
the mound, they found a large drum wrapped in plastic, which police likened to
treasure. [@1] The drum had been hidden under the ground and held 35 kg 500 g of
cannabis, known locally as ganja. [@2] Police registered a case against Mandal.
