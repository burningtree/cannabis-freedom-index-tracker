Ranks from the Guyana Police Force Narcotics Branch and Regional Division Six carried out a joint
eradication exercise at Kuyarakuru Creek and Sabina Point on the Upper Berbice River on 15 October
2025. [@1,2] Police Headquarters said the teams found four separate farms covering about sixteen
acres (roughly 6.5 hectares) with an estimated 95,000 cannabis plants between two and seven feet
tall. [@1,2]

Officers also found a 30-by-20-foot makeshift camp holding about 90 pounds (roughly 41 kg) of dried
cannabis. [@1,2] They burned the plants, the dried stock and the camp. [@1,2]

No arrests were made and investigations continue. [@2] The Guyana Police Force put the total street
value of the destroyed narcotics at GY$70,968,116, including GY$32,186,000 for the standing plants
and GY$38,782,116 for the dried cannabis. [@1,2]
