Guangzhou customs at Baiyun International Airport seized 3,975.22 g of
cannabis in the transit cargo channel, according to a “Customs Release” WeChat
notice reported by Southern Plus on 18 October 2025. [@1]

Officers screened a consignment declared as “bedside tables, makeup racks and
similar items” and saw an abnormal machine image. [@1,2] They opened it and
found 16 bags labeled as instant coffee; inside each bag was vacuum-packed green
plant fragments. [@1,2] Rapid field tests were THC-positive and a technical
laboratory confirmed the material as cannabis at 3,975.22 g. [@1] A later
Chinanews report on the same seizure quoted an officer saying the bags smelled
strongly when opened and gave a rounded total of 3,975 g. [@2]

Both outlets repeated customs reminders that smuggling or trafficking drugs,
including cannabis, triggers criminal liability regardless of quantity. [@1,2]
Neither named an arrest or destination. [@1,2]
