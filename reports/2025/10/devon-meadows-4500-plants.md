Victoria Police executed search warrants at about 7:30 am on Tuesday 7 October 2025 at an eight-hectare
Devon Meadows property and a Keysborough home in Melbourne’s south-east. [@1,2] Eight people ran as
officers arrived at the large block; all were arrested after a chase captured on police drone footage
published by Nine News. [@2]

Over two days police dismantled cannabis grown inside gardening igloos and removed about 4,500 plants
of mixed maturity, which Victoria Police valued at AUD 15 million. [@1,2] AAP said the plants would be
destroyed after forensic examination by a botanist. [@1] Officers also seized a firearm, amphetamines,
cocaine, ammunition and cash. [@1]

Five men aged 26 to 49 were charged with cultivating a large commercial quantity of cannabis and
remanded from Melbourne Magistrates’ Court; three other men were released on intent to summon. [@1,2]
Sergeant Josh Gordon said the case showed how alleged syndicates hide commercial grows behind
legitimate-looking horticulture. [@1]
