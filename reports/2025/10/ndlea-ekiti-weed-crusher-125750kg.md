Between 17 and 20 October 2025, operatives of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency
(NDLEA) in Ekiti State destroyed cannabis farms in the Igbara Odo and Ilawe Ekiti forests and
arrested three suspects found on one plantation. [@1]

In Igbara Odo forest the command said it destroyed 72,500 kilogrammes of fresh cannabis on 29.7
hectares, burned nursery beds awaiting transplant, and recovered 84 bags of dried cannabis weighing
2,316 kilogrammes. [@1] In Ilawe Ekiti forest agents destroyed another 53,250 kilogrammes. [@1]
State commander Rufus Aina put total destruction at 125,750 kilogrammes and further recoveries at
3,456 kilogrammes; the agency hired labourers to help uproot the fields. [@1]

The Ilawe raid, led by deputy commander Muhammed Yaro, ended with three men detained on the farm.
[@1] National spokesman Femi Babafemi named them as Matthew Emmanuel, 26, James Moses, 27, and
Israel Samuel, 20, and said 53,250 kilogrammes were cleared from 21.3 hectares there with 1,140
kilogrammes of dried skunk recovered. [@2] The state command tied the work to Operation Weed Crusher
2025 but did not report charges or court dates. [@1]
