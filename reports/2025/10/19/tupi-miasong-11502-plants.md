On the morning of 19 October 2025, agents of the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency's Region 12
office, with police from Police Regional Office 12, uprooted 11,502 fully grown marijuana plants
in the hinterland of Sitio Benigno Aquino, Barangay Miasong, Tupi, South Cotabato. [@1,2]

Director Benjamin Recites said villagers and barangay officials had tipped off his agents to the
farm, which he linked to men also suspected of selling methamphetamine locally. [@1] Residents
said at least four men, including a leader known only as Jan Cris, fled as teams approached from
two directions; none were arrested. [@1]

Agents took samples for laboratory testing, then burned the rest on site with a chemical
incinerant in front of barangay officials and police. [@1,2] Recites valued the destroyed plants
at more than PHP 2.3 million and said PDEA would pursue the propagators in absentia using the
specimens. [@2]
