Land patrols of Saudi Arabia’s Border Guard in the Al-Raboah sector of the Asir region foiled an
attempt to smuggle 74 kilograms of hashish and 45,000 amphetamine tablets on 14 October 2025.
[@1,2] The agency said initial legal procedures were completed and the seized material was handed
over to the competent authority. [@1,2]

Security authorities repeated their appeal for citizens and residents to report drug smuggling or
trafficking through the kingdom’s emergency numbers and the General Directorate of Narcotics
Control’s 995 hotline and email address. [@1]
