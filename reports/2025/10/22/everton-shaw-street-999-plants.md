On 22 October 2025, Merseyside Police executed a warrant at a premises on Shaw Street in Everton
and found 999 cannabis plants growing across two floors. [@1,2]

The force said many internal doors had been barricaded to delay entry and that Scottish Power staff
attended to make the building safe and assess possible illegal electricity abstraction. [@1,2]

Inspector Leigh Price said the setup was well organised and removed a serious local safety risk
from unsafe power tapping. [@1,2] Police said enquiries continued to identify those responsible and
appealed for information. [@1,2]
