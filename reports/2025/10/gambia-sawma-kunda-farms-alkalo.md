DLEAG's Central River Region command, with the Basse and Lower River Region
commands, the Police Intervention Unit and the Fire and Rescue Services, raided
suspected cannabis-growing sites in a sting that began around 2:00 am on 10
October. [@1,2] Officers uncovered two cannabis plantations on the outskirts of
Sawma Kunda village and in nearby Mbafaf. [@1,2]

Nine people were arrested, including Momodou Camara, 60, the alkalo (village head)
of Sawma Kunda, and two alleged child offenders aged 15 and 17. [@1,2] The other
suspects were named as Kaka Jawo, 26, Essa Camara, 27, Samba Barry, 26, Samba Bah,
21, and Abdoulie Bah, 24. [@1,2] DLEAG spokesperson Ousman Saidybah said the arrest
and interrogation of 28-year-old Essa Jawo of Mbafaf was pivotal, leading officers
to the rest of the suspects. [@1,2]

Two motorcycles believed to have been used in the cultivation operation were
recovered, and the suspects remained in custody while investigations continued.
[@1,2] DLEAG described the bust as a significant breakthrough. [@1,2]
