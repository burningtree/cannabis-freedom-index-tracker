On Tuesday 28 October 2025, as part of the nationwide anti-narcotics campaign Operation
Thunder, the Royal Eswatini Police Service destroyed three hectares of illegal dagga fields
and large quantities of harvested dagga in the Nhlambane area of Sihhoye, under Tshaneni
Police Station jurisdiction. [@1] The fields lay along the Nkomanzi River. [@1]

Deputy police spokesperson Assistant Superintendent Nosipho Mnguni said REPS was using
intelligence, aerial reconnaissance and mobile units to find hidden plantations and that field
operations were being intensified. [@1] The report names no arrests or suspects linked to
the Sihhoye site. [@1]

Police photographs published with the story show officers spraying standing plants and burning
harvested dagga. [@1]
