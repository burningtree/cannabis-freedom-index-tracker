Just before 20:00 on Saturday 4 October 2025 Merseyside Police patrols in Liverpool city centre
found a large cannabis farm inside a warehouse on Kitchen Street. [@1]

Officers counted more than 650 cannabis plants spread across five separate growing rooms. [@1,2]

They arrested 21 men at the scene and later charged each with being concerned in the production of
a Class B drug, namely cannabis. [@1,2] Merseyside Police named all 21 defendants, aged 22 to 53,
with addresses in Liverpool, London, Birmingham, Middlesbrough and several other English cities as
well as men listed with no fixed address. [@1]

The men appeared at Liverpool Adult Remand Court on 6 October and were remanded in custody pending
further hearings, according to the force. [@1] BBC News reported the same charges and remand
after magistrates' court appearances. [@2]

The October releases did not give a police valuation of the crop or say whether electricity had been
abstracted from the supply. [@1,2]
