On 16 July 2025 detectives from Victoria Police's Southern Metro Region Divisional Response Unit
executed a search warrant at a rural property on Hardys Road in Tooradin. [@1,2]

Inside a crop house they seized more than 1,200 cannabis plants, which police described as a
commercial quantity with an estimated potential street value of about AUD 3.6 million. [@1,2]

The property was illegally connected to the street electricity supply, and specialists had to
isolate power before officers could enter safely. [@1,2]

A 39-year-old St Albans man and a 43-year-old man of no fixed address tried to run from the
property but were arrested with help from the drone unit and dog squad. [@1,2] Both were charged
with cultivating a large commercial quantity of cannabis and remanded to appear at Melbourne
Magistrates' Court on 17 July. [@1,2]

A 48-year-old Doncaster man was also arrested at the scene, interviewed, and was expected to be
charged on summons according to the police release quoted by both outlets. [@1,2]
