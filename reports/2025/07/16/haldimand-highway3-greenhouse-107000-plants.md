On 16 July 2025 members of the Ontario Provincial Police Provincial Joint Forces Cannabis
Enforcement Team, supported by emergency response and regional street-crime units, executed a
Cannabis Act warrant at a large greenhouse property on Highway 3 in Haldimand County. [@1,2]

Inside they found more than 107,000 illegal cannabis plants in various stages of growth and about
950 pounds of dried bud, which police valued at more than CAD 55 million together with lights,
machinery and processing gear seized as offence-related property. [@1,2]

Sixteen people aged 24 to 58, most listed with no fixed address, were charged with cultivating
cannabis without authorization and possession for the purpose of distribution; police named them in
the OPP-led release quoted by Global News and said they were released pending court dates. [@2]
The investigation was continuing in the August accounts. [@1,2]
