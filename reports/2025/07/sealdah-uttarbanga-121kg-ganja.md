Eight people were detained at Sealdah station in Kolkata after Railway
Protection Force (RPF) officers found 121 kg of cannabis on the 13148 Uttarbanga
Express. [@1] Those detained were four men, two women and two juveniles, and the
cannabis was hidden in six bags. [@1] Officers searched the train after a
tip-off, once it had arrived from north Bengal. [@1] The search took place
shortly after the train reached Sealdah on 26 July 2025. [@2] The cannabis was
recovered from the possession of the suspects. [@2]

An Eastern Railway official valued the cannabis at more than Rs 24 lakh (2.4
million rupees). [@1] The seized cannabis was valued at approximately Rs 24.2
lakh (2.42 million rupees). [@2] The eight detainees and the cannabis were
handed over to the Government Railway Police (GRP) at Sealdah, the state force
that handles crime on the railways. [@1] A case was registered under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, India's narcotics law.
[@2]
