A notorious drug trafficker was apprehended along the Voi-Mombasa Highway
with 40 kg of skank, a potent form of cannabis smuggled from southern
Africa, in what authorities called a major breakthrough. [@1]

Michael Charles Chacha, 47, was arrested travelling in a public service
vehicle; the drugs hid in a suitcase that had reportedly passed undetected
through the Taveta border point, bound for a drug cartel in Mombasa. [@1]
Passengers reportedly commended the officers' swift, discreet action. [@1]

Anti-narcotics officers say skank originates in Eswatini and moves through
South African ports into Tanzania before reaching Kenya, and that Chacha had
long run a Coast-region distribution network under surveillance for months;
he was held at Coast Regional Police Headquarters pending arraignment on
trafficking charges. [@1]
