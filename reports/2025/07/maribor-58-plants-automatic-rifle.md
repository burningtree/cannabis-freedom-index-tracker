Maribor police detained a 50-year-old local man on 30 July 2025 on suspicion of unlawful production
and trafficking in prohibited drugs and related offences. [@2] In a house search in the Maribor
area they found a room adapted for cannabis cultivation with 58 plants and about 500 grams of dried
plant material. [@1,2]

Officers also seized an automatic rifle with magazines, a box of 466 rounds and two pieces of M81
artillery-shell explosive. [@1,2] The Maribor directorate said that once inquiries were complete it
would file a criminal complaint with the competent prosecutor’s office. [@1]
