Zhenlai County police in Jilin cleared more than 3,000 wild cannabis plants
during a summer patrol campaign, a county police statement republished on 15
July 2025 said. [@1]

On 1 July, Zhenlai township officers found 1,589 cannabis plants beside the west
road at Xinghuacun and removed them with equipment support, the account said.
[@1] On 8 July, Momoge township officers spent hours searching and uprooted
1,500 plants. [@1] Earlier in the push, Heicheng police had cleared more than
100 plants identified as wild industrial hemp on 24 June and 79 cannabis plants
from a residential flower bed on 1 July, it added. [@1]

The bureau said the combined summer total exceeded 3,000 plants and promised
more patrols and publicity. [@1] It named no arrests. [@1]
