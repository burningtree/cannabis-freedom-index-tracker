Criminal investigators from the Ljubljana police directorate carried out a house search in the
city in late July 2025 at premises linked to a 35-year-old Slovenian and a 54-year-old Serbian.
[@1,2] They found several rooms fitted out for cannabis cultivation and seized 650 plants plus a
large amount of cash. [@1,2]

Both men were suspected of unlawful production and trafficking in prohibited drugs. [@1,2] Police
took them to an investigating judge, who ordered one held in pre-trial detention and the other
under house arrest. [@1,2] The Ljubljana directorate said inquiries were continuing. [@2]
