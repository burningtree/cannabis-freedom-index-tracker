Agencia IP and La Nación reported that Paraguay’s National Anti-Drug Secretariat finished the
Amambay leg of Operation Nueva Alianza 51 on 7 July 2025 after a week of raids with Brazil’s
Polícia Federal, military CODI units and prosecutor Rossana Coronel. [@1,2]

SENAD said teams eradicated 208 hectares of marijuana in forest areas around Cadete Boquerón and
Trabuco, equal to about 624,000 kg of cannabis still in growth, and incinerated more than 24,000 kg
of chopped and pressed marijuana ready for sale. [@1,2] They also dismantled 69 clandestine camps used
for processing and shelter. [@1,2]

SENAD valued the lost criminal revenue at more than USD 19 million in Paraguay and about USD 97
million at estimated Brazilian retail prices, the main destination for the crop. [@1,2] IP noted that
after the Amambay phase the same operation would continue in the Mbaracayú forest reserve in
Canindeyú. [@1]
