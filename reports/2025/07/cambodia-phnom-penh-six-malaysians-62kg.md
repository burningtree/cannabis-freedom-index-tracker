Six Malaysian men aged 27 to 70 were arrested at the departure terminal of Phnom Penh International Airport on 16 July 2025 as they were about to board a flight to London via Hong Kong. [@1,2] Cambodia's Anti-Drug Department, with customs and airport officers, found nearly 62 kg of dried cannabis concealed in four suitcases linked to the group. [@1,2]

Malaysian police identified all six detainees after the arrests were first reported by the Phnom Penh Post. [@1] The Phnom Penh Municipal Court prosecutor's office said the men would be charged with illegal transportation of drugs under Article 40 of Cambodia's Law on Drug Control, which carries two to twenty years in prison. [@1,2]
