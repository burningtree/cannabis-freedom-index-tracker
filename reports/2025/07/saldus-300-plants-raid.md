In late July 2025 officers from the Kurzeme regional police found a specially prepared backyard
grow at a property in Saldus municipality. [@1,2]

They seized more than 300 marijuana plants and dried plant matter, ordering forensic tests on the
amount and composition. [@1,2] Police opened a case over large-scale unauthorised cultivation and
group possession of narcotics, offences that can carry up to ten years' imprisonment under Latvian
law. [@1,2]

Several people were detained and treated as suspects while investigators clarified their roles; all
had been in contact with police before. [@1,2] The force reminded readers that suspects remain
innocent until convicted. [@2]
