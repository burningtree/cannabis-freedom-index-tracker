On 30 July 2025 gendarmes from the Haut-Nyong company, led by Chief Warrant Officer Bekonde, dismantled
a cannabis plantation covering nearly 20,000 square metres in a hard-to-reach part of Mindourou in
eastern Cameroon. [@1,2] Sub-prefect Jérémie Moamossé and company commander Captain Bidja Bidja Martial
supervised the raid after intelligence pinpointed the field. [@1,2]

The teams arrested a suspect known as Jordan, who ran a snack bar beside Mindourou’s bilingual high
school. [@1,2] Actu Cameroun said he allegedly sold cannabis, tramadol and other illicit drugs to
pupils from the bar. [@1] Lebledparle described the bust as a significant win for the national
gendarmerie’s anti-trafficking work in the region. [@2]

Neither outlet reported how many plants were counted or whether other suspects were sought. [@1,2]
