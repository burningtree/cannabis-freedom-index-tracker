Latvian police in Latgale searched three addresses in Krāslava and the surrounding municipality on
30 and 31 July 2025 during two linked inquiries into illegal drugs. [@1,2]

On 30 July officers acting on intelligence found a marijuana grow in pots inside a greenhouse on a
private plot in Krāslava municipality. [@1] They removed 89 plants and opened a cultivation case
against a man born in 1979. [@1]

The next day police searched a flat and house in Krāslava linked to a man born in 1998. [@1] They
took twelve bunches of dried plant material, a three-litre jar of similar material, cuttings drying
for processing and trimmings, totalling about 40 grams of dried marijuana by police estimate pending
expert tests. [@1]

The second case concerns possession and dealing offences under Latvian drug law. [@1] TV3 carried the
same police account when the release was published on 8 August 2025. [@2] The force reminded readers
that suspects remain innocent until proven guilty in court. [@1]
