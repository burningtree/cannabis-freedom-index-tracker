On 30 July 2025, the Brussels correctional court sentenced a young Albanian man to 36 months in
prison for smuggling cannabis through Brussels Airport. [@1,2]

Customs officers had stopped him on 22 April 2025 after he arrived from Bangkok and found 15
packages in his suitcase; tests showed 21 kilograms of cannabis. [@1,2] The prosecution said he
claimed he had met a woman in Thailand who asked him to transport the bag without payment and that
he was to leave it at the airport for her sister to collect. [@1,2]

The public prosecutor called that account implausible and sought 36 months; his lawyer asked for a
suspended sentence, citing a clean record and work as a driver under electronic monitoring. [@1,2]
The court imposed an effective 36-month term. [@1,2]
