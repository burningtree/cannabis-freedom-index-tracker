On 23 July 2025, National Drug Law Enforcement Agency officers in Lagos intercepted Chidi Agbafo
on a commercial bus along the Epe–Ajah expressway. [@1,2]

Spokesperson Femi Babafemi said Agbafo was carrying 21.7 kilograms of Colorado, a synthetic cannabis
strain, some of it concealed in sachets used for moi-moi, a steamed bean pudding, together with
3.8 litres of codeine-based syrup bound for Warri and Oghara in Delta State. [@1,2]

Babafemi disclosed the arrest in a statement issued on 27 July 2025. [@1]
