Police in Xinhe County, in China's Xinjiang region, say they have arrested
five people and seized 797.83 g of cannabis in a case that ran from Yining to
Xinhe. [@1] The county public security bureau said officers found a local man,
identified only as Ai, had bought four packages of cannabis in Yining through
an intermediary for 23,000 yuan and drove them to Xinhe County. [@1]

On 15 July 2025 officers arrested Ai at a residential compound in Xinhe Town
and seized 388.14 g of cannabis on the spot. [@1] Xinhe police then opened a
task force and worked with Yining's anti-drug brigade. [@1] On 27 July they
arrested a man identified as Pa during an active drug deal in a Yining
barbecue restaurant, seizing 397.56 g of cannabis, 20,000 yuan in cash and a
mobile phone. [@1] A search of Pa's home turned up another 12.13 g of
suspected cannabis, two cannabis plants and drug paraphernalia. [@1]

In total the bureau said it seized 797.83 g of cannabis and two cannabis
plants, broke one criminal case and one administrative case, and detained five
people; the work was still under way. [@1]
