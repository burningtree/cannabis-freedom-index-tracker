Takeo provincial governor Vie Samnang, who chairs the provincial unified command, led a joint force
on the morning of Sunday 2 February 2025 to illegal cannabis plots on Bayang Kor Mountain in
Kirivong district. [@1,2]

Police, military police, provincial military operations troops and Kirivong district
administration officers cut and burned plants at seven sites at Changkan rock on the Bayangkor
range in Prey Thom village, Preah Bat Choan Chum commune. [@1,2] They destroyed 3,365 marijuana
plants covering about 1,890 square metres and removed six water tanks used to irrigate the
fields. [@1,2]

Neither source names anyone arrested or identifies who planted the crops. [@1,2]
