Military police burned 7,755 marijuana plants on 14 February 2025 in the Keng Raing Valley at
Pooswek village, Rasmey Samaki commune, Oral district, Kampong Speu province. [@1]

At 7:36 a.m. seven Oral district military police under district commander Lieutenant General Som
Puttara worked with eight officers from the provincial military police crime suppression department
led by director Nol Dara. [@1] They found three farms totalling about one hectare and destroyed
the plants by fire. [@1,2]

Police are searching for whoever planted the crop; neither outlet reports an arrest at the
scene. [@1,2] A Cambodia Expats Online summary of the same operation describes three plots with
3,075, 3,420 and 1,260 plants respectively. [@2]
