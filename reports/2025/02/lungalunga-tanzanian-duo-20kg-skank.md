Police arrested two Tanzanian nationals on the Lunga Lunga-Mombasa Highway
with 20 kg of high-grade bhang, disrupting a cross-border trafficking
operation feeding the Mombasa market. [@1]

Yunus Ali, alias Dogo Yunus, and Hemed Abdalla, alias Chotara, hid the
skank — worth about Sh600,000 — inside sacks disguised as chicken feed;
officers acting on intelligence intercepted the vehicle after it crossed
from Tanzania. [@1] Skank is a potent greenhouse-grown variant of cannabis
sativa, more lucrative on the local market. [@1]

Security sources said the pair had been on the radar for years, moving
consignments from Tanzania through porous border points; both were detained
pending trafficking charges as police pursued the wider network. [@1]
