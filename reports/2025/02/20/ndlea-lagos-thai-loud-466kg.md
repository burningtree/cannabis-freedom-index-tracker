On 20 February 2025, operatives of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency arrested
24-year-old Thai national Pattaphi Wimonnat during inward clearance at Terminal 2 of Murtala
Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja, Lagos. [@1,2]

Spokesperson Femi Babafemi said officers found 43 parcels of Canadian Loud, a synthetic cannabis
strain, weighing 46.60 kilograms in her luggage after she arrived on a Qatar Airways flight from
Thailand via Doha. [@1,2]

Babafemi said she confessed to being a hired courier and told investigators a drug cartel had
promised USD 3,000 if she delivered the consignment in Nigeria. [@1,2]
