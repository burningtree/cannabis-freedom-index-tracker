Shenzhen Post Office Customs seized 675.9 g of suspected cannabis in the mail
channel, according to a “Customs Release” social media notice reported by
Chinanews on 27 February 2025. [@1]

Officers screening transit cargo found a parcel declared as “custom clothing”
with an unusual X-ray image and opened it the same day. [@1] Inside were two
layers of plastic bags holding plant material with a gross weight of 535.5 g
and a separate plastic-wrapped block weighing 140.4 g that officers treated as
suspected cannabis resin. [@1] On-site reagent testing showed
tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) positive reactions. [@1]

Chinanews said the case was transferred to the customs anti-smuggling
department for handling. [@1] It did not name any suspect or destination
country. [@1]
