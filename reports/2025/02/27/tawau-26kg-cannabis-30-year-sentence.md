On 27 February 2025, High Court judge Datuk Duncan Sikodol in Tawau, Sabah, sentenced Utuh Derman
Isim, 47, to 30 years in prison and 12 strokes of the cane for trafficking cannabis. [@1,2]

The court had convicted Isim of possessing 26,215.3 grams of cannabis at the staircase of a
building in Bandar Sri Indah at about 5 p.m. on 12 September 2022. [@1,2] The charge under
Section 39B of Malaysia's Dangerous Drugs Act carried a mandatory death sentence or life
imprisonment; after abolition of the mandatory death penalty, the deputy public prosecutor urged a
hanging while the defence asked for leniency as a first offender supporting his mother. [@1]

Sikodol ordered the prison term to run from Isim's arrest date on 12 September 2022. [@1]
