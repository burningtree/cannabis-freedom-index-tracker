During the week of 17–21 February 2025, forces across England and Wales took part in the third
Operation Mille intensification against large-scale cannabis cultivation by organised crime
groups. [@1,2]

The National Police Chiefs' Council said the phase included 368 warrants and searches,
48,328 cannabis plants seized with an estimated street value of GBP 48,328,000, 242 arrests, 65
weapons recovered including 14 firearms, and GBP 183,590 in cash. [@1]

Assistant Chief Constable Adam Ball, who led the operation, said police also referred 19 people
suspected of modern slavery and human trafficking for support. [@1] Regional releases described
parallel activity in the South West, where five forces reported 32 searches and 2,240 plants
destroyed in the same national week. [@2]

Ball said almost half of raided addresses had no one present, matching a trend toward empty
factories. [@1]
