On 13 May 2025, agents of the Oklahoma Attorney General's Organized Crime Task Force, with the
Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, Oklahoma City police, the Oklahoma State Bureau of
Investigation, federal agencies and Cleveland County deputies, searched three illegal marijuana
farms in Cleveland County near 183rd Street in Lexington. [@1,2]

Attorney General Gentner Drummond said the operation seized almost 65 pounds of processed
marijuana and nearly 29,000 plants. [@1,2] Agents arrested two Chinese nationals, Ruibin Wu and
Xihai Yin, on complaints including aggravated manufacturing, possession with intent to distribute
and possession of a firearm during a felony. [@1,2]

The investigation began in March after a tip about suspicious grow activity. [@1,2] Drummond said
illicit farms often front organized crime and environmental harm. [@1]
