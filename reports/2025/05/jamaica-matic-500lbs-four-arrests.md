Detectives assigned to the Westmoreland Police Division seized over 500
pounds of compressed ganja during an operation at a house in Matic District,
New Works, in the parish on Friday, May 9, 2025. [@1][@2]

Reports from the Negril Police are that at about midnight, the police team
conducted a search of the premises and its occupants, discovering several
plastic bags containing compressed packages of ganja. [@1][@2]

Four men were arrested in relation to the seizure, and their identities were
withheld pending further investigation. [@1][@2]
