Beijing customs at Capital International Airport seized four cannabis vape
cartridges with a gross weight of 60 g from an inbound passenger’s luggage,
according to a “Customs Release” notice carried by Chinanews and Guangming Net
on 28 May 2025. [@1,2]

Officers said an X-ray image looked abnormal, so they searched the bag and found
four unidentified oils inside a branded e-cigarette pod box. [@1,2] On-site
testing was cannabis-positive and a professional laboratory confirmed
tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) in all four cartridges. [@1,2] The notices repeated
that smuggling or transporting drugs, including cannabis, triggers criminal
liability regardless of quantity. [@1,2]

Neither outlet named an arrest or said whether the case was referred to criminal
investigation beyond standard customs handling language. [@1,2]
