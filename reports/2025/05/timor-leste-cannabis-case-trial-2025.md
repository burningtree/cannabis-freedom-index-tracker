The Dili court began the pre-trial debate in the case against 13 defendants charged after the 12 August 2022 raids; nine appeared and four were abroad. The 2022 seizure included hashish, hashish chocolate and 0.50 g of methamphetamine, with $4,139.25 in cash. Witnesses described the intelligence-led raids and disputed who had detained the first five suspects. [@1,2]
