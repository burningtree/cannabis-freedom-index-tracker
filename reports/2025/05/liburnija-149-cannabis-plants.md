Drug officers from the Primorje-Gorski Kotar police, working with colleagues in
Opatija and special units, searched homes, a house under construction and vehicles
at several Liburnija sites on 14 May 2025 under warrants from the Rijeka county
court. [@1] They seized more than 140 cannabis plants between 27 centimetres and
one metre tall and about 7,300 grams of marijuana, along with scales, fans,
reflectors and cash in several currencies. [@1] Police said the four Croatian men,
aged 44, 49 and 60, had grown the plants this year to sell for profit. [@1] After
the investigation they were charged with unauthorised production and trafficking
of drugs and handed to a pre-trial judge. [@1]

Burin reported on 17 May that an investigating judge at the Rijeka county court
had ordered all four men held in custody because they might reoffend if left at
liberty. [@2] Its account of the same case put the haul at 149 plants and about
7.3 kilograms of marijuana spread across grows in Mošćenička Draga, Veprinc and
Rukavac. [@2]
