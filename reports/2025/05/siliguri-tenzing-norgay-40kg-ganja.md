Police from the Pradhan Nagar station of the Siliguri Metropolitan Police
arrested four people for possessing 40 kg of ganja in Siliguri. [@1]

According to police sources, the four had been waiting in the Siliguri Junction
area to catch a bus to Dakshinbanga, the Bengali name for the southern part of
Bengal. [@1] They were to be produced before the Siliguri court on Tuesday. [@1]
