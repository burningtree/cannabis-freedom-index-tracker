On the night of 14 May 2025 Mendocino County Sheriff's Office deputies were sent to a disconnected
911 call traced to the 82000 block of Mina Road north of Covelo. [@1,2]

The caller, an unidentified man, said he needed help for a friend before the line dropped. [@1,2]

While deputies searched Mina Road, passers-by told them an unresponsive man lay on Hulls Valley
Road about a mile from that intersection. [@1,2] In the 23000 block of Hulls Valley Road they
found an adult man dead from apparent gunshot wounds at 00:42 on 15 May, with multiple firearms
and spent cartridge casings nearby. [@1,2]

Deputies followed a blood trail for about a quarter mile to what both accounts described as a large
illegal marijuana grow with hoop houses and camp trailers. [@1,2]

Separately, a man with gunshot wounds arrived at Adventist Health Ukiah Valley; deputies linked his
injuries to the same shooting and had him moved to a hospital outside the county. [@1,2] On the
morning of 15 May another wounded man was found on Hulls Valley Road several miles away; he told
deputies he was shot in the same incident and was airlifted for treatment. [@1,2]

By 20 May both survivors were in stable condition and had been interviewed, the sheriff's office
told the Ukiah Daily Journal. [@1] Detectives prepared search warrants for the grow and the
shooting scene and said evidence pointed to illegal cannabis cultivation controlled by a drug
trafficking organization, without naming the group. [@1,2]

The sheriff's office had not yet confirmed the dead man's identity in these releases and scheduled
an autopsy. [@1,2] No suspects were identified. [@1,2]
