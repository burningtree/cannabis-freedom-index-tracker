On 29 May 2025 Namibian Police in Otjozondjupa, after an intelligence-driven operation that began
near Okahandja, uncovered a cannabis plantation on a commercial farm at Hochfeld. [@1,2] Deputy
commissioner Edna Nawa said irrigation was in use and that a green plantation of about 1.55 hectares
was still being cultivated. [@1] Five corrugated-iron rooms were used for safekeeping, drying,
packaging and loading. [@1]

Police said they found dried cannabis weighing over 512 kg (street value given as N$5.1 million),
packed bags of cannabis weighing nearly 11,000 kg (about N$10.8 million), packed skunk weighing
725 kg (N$36,280), and two half Mandrax tablets (N$120), for a total estimated value of more than
N$52 million. [@1]

Armand Carel Schultz, 27, a South African national and farm manager, and David Strauss van der
Linden, 43, the farm owner, were arrested and later sought bail in the Okahandja Magistrate's Court.
[@2] New Era also names farm driver Johannes Kamati, 61, and Jakob Ganai, 43, as suspects in the
same case. [@2] The accused faced charges including possession and dealing in drugs, organised crime
and racketeering. [@1,2]
