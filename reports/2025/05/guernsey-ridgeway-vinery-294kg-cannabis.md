Guernsey Customs and the Economic and Financial Crime Bureau had watched a rented unit at
Ridgeway Vinery in Pointes Lane, St Andrew's, during a long investigation into Matthew
Stewart Lovell, 49, and Richard Michael Le Cras, 47. [@1] [@2]

On 22 May 2025 officers used an angle grinder to cut a lock on a trailer linked to Le Cras. [@1]
Inside a black metal chest they found 29.4 kg of cannabis and cannabis resin and 191.3 g of
ketamine. [@1] DNA from the chest linked both men to the drugs. [@1] [@2] Le Cras was arrested
that day; Lovell had already been arrested on 4 April after surveillance at the same vinery. [@1]
[@2]

The Royal Court later heard that the pair supplied MDMA, ketamine, cannabis and cannabis resin
from the site; Lovell was jailed for 20 years and six months and Le Cras for 13 years and seven
months in September 2026 after guilty pleas. [@1] [@2] Island FM said investigators also traced
about £460,000 in criminal cash from Lovell's banking. [@2]
