Forest police in Hunchun, Jilin, say they uprooted more than 8,000 wild cannabis
plants during a joint patrol with local army units and the Hunchun forestry
bureau, Chinanews reported on 26 June 2025. [@1,2]

Officers said the stand ranged from about 30 cm to 1.5 m high, scattered in weeds
with no signs of cultivation. [@1,2] After identifying the plants as wild
cannabis, teams cleared them plant by plant over four hours in difficult forest
terrain and burned them on the spot. [@1,2]

The bureau said it would keep joint patrols and periodic eradication in the
forests. [@1,2] No one was arrested. [@1,2]
