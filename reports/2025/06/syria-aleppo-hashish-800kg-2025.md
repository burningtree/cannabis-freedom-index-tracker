The anti-narcotics branch in Aleppo arrested two Lebanese nationals and seized about 800 kilograms of hashish and 200,000 narcotic pills. The head of the Drug Enforcement Administration said the men were trying to smuggle the shipment and that the drugs would be destroyed. It was one of the largest cannabis seizures reported since the fall of Assad. [@1]
