The People's Court of Lusong District in Zhuzhou, Hunan, publicly sentenced
defendant Li to one year in prison and a 10,000-yuan fine for selling cannabis
leaf on two occasions, Rednet reported on 23 June 2025 in a court bulletin
ahead of the International Day against Drug Abuse. [@1,2]

The court found that on an unspecified day in May 2024 Li sold buyer Liu about
10 g of cannabis leaf for 2,000 yuan on the roadside outside a bar in Wenyi
Lane, Lusong District. [@1,2] On the evening of 3 November 2024 Li sold Liu
about 5 g more for 1,400 yuan on the roadside in front of Zhuzhou Central
Hospital. [@1,2]

Police telephoned Li on 26 February 2025; he came in and confessed. [@1,2] The
court held that Li knowingly sold cannabis leaf, that his surrender counted as
voluntary surrender meriting a lighter sentence, and that he qualified for
leniency after pleading guilty. [@1,2] It ordered 3,400 yuan of criminal
proceeds continued to be recovered and forfeited to the state treasury. [@1,2]

The posts did not describe any separate sentence for Liu or quote a lab
certificate for the leaf weight. [@1,2]
