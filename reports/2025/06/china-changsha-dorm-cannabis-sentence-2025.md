Changsha County People's Court sentenced defendant Zhang to ten months in
prison and a 6,000-yuan fine for illegally cultivating cannabis in a company
dormitory, Rednet reported on 26 June 2025 in a court publicity release ahead
of the International Day against Drug Abuse. [@2] Guangming republished the same
account on 27 June. [@1]

The court found that in June 2024 Zhang took nine cannabis seeds from a friend,
germinated eight of them in paper cups and moved the seedlings into flowerpots
about 20 cm wide at a company dorm in Huangxing, Changsha County. [@1,2] On 19
August 2024 at about 17:00 he packed the eight plants into a box and moved
them to a company rubbish station; police arrested him at about 22:00 the same
night and seized the plants. [@1,2]

Zhang had already been convicted twice for illegal cultivation, receiving
four months’ detention and a 1,000-yuan fine in 2021 and seven months’
imprisonment and a 5,000-yuan fine in 2022. [@1,2] The court held that planting
again after those sentences met the threshold for illegal cultivation of drug
crops. [@1,2]

The reports did not spell out Zhang’s full name or any sentence for the friend
who supplied the seeds. [@1,2]
