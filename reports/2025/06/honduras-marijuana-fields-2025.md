The Honduran armed forces said they had secured 13 illegal marijuana fields
with about 180,000 plants so far in 2025, beside 37 coca plantations with
more than 770,000 bushes.[@1]

The latest marijuana field, at Tocoa in Colón, held about 36,000 plants on
eight manzanas.[@1]
