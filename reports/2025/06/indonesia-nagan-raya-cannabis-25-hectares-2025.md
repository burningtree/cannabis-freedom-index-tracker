Police destroyed cannabis fields across 25 hectares in Beutong Ateuh Banggalang,
Nagan Raya regency, Aceh. [@1] The operation covered eight sites. [@1] The
destruction took six days, from 19 to 24 June 2025. [@1] Customs officers joined
the police teams. [@2] It followed the arrest of two suspected traffickers,
Yusni of North Aceh and Khairul of Central Aceh. [@1] Police said their
information showed the cannabis had come from Blang Meurandeh. [@1] The Aceh
customs office said the case began with the seizure of 27 kilograms of dried
cannabis in Bener Meriah regency on 22 May 2025. [@2] Two suspects identified by
the initials YH and KR were detained in Lhokseumawe and Central Aceh in
mid-June. [@2]

The joint team included Bareskrim, the national police's criminal investigation
department, Aceh regional police, Nagan Raya district police and the Aceh
customs office. [@2] The fields were spread across Blang Meurandeh and Kuta
Teungoh villages. [@1] Police estimated 40,000 plants per hectare, about one
million plants in total, with a combined weight of about 200 tonnes. [@1] At one
site the plants stood 1.5 to 2 metres tall. [@3] Officers pulled the plants up,
piled them and burned them on site. [@3] Local residents helped with the
destruction. [@3]

Brigadier General Eko Hadi Santoso, director of the national police's drug
crimes unit, led the destruction. [@3] The customs office described the
operation as part of government efforts to cut the drug trade in Aceh. [@2]
Police said the operation ran safely and all personnel returned. [@1]
