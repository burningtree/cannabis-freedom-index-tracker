Three men who grew cannabis at a remote pig farm and drove to Shanghai to bury
sales for online buyers were sentenced for selling and transporting drugs,
Legal Daily reported on 26 June 2025. [@1] Tencent News carried the same court
account on 24 June. [@2]

Fan, a pig-farm worker, bought 20 cannabis seeds online, planted two plants
behind the farm and later expanded onto a hillside shack with two childhood
friends, the court heard. [@1,2] After a successful buried “drop” sale in
Shanghai, the group looked for new buyers in August 2024, drove to Putuo
District and were stopped by police on 3 August 2024 with nine bags of cannabis
leaf; officers also destroyed plants at the farm. [@1,2]

Putuo District People's Court convicted Fan and the two others of selling and
transporting drugs, noting partial attempts and guilty pleas, and sentenced each
to between six and ten months in prison plus fines. [@1,2] Judge Shen Hengzhi
told Legal Daily that cannabis is a controlled drug in China regardless of
foreign legalisation debates. [@2]

The sentencing notices did not break down individual prison terms or give the
net weight of the nine bags. [@1,2]
