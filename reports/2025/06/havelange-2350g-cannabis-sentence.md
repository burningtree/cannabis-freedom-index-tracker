Namur criminal court on 26 June 2025 convicted a 24-year-old from Havelange of drug dealing on
recidivist facts. [@1,2] Police had stopped him in Bouge on 18 January 2025 while he drove a rented
Opel Corsa drunk, disqualified and uninsured, finding about €2,000 on him and €8,000 in the centre
console. [@1,2] A search at his Havelange home seized 2,350 g of vacuum-packed marijuana and 34 g of
cannabis resin. [@1,2]

He admitted the drugs but said he sold under pressure from a gang he owed for earlier drug debts; the
prosecutor sought three years, noting deliveries across Wallonia, yet phone evidence did not show a
structured organisation. [@1,2] The court imposed two years in prison with five years' probation on
the remainder after time served, ordered €8,972 forfeited with the narcotics, and declined the
defence challenge to the home search. [@1,2]
