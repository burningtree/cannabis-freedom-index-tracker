In the evening of 23 June 2025 officers from Singapore's Central Narcotics Bureau (CNB) entered a
home in the Balam Road area, near MacPherson, and arrested a 33-year-old Singaporean man on
suspected drug-trafficking offences. [@1,2]

A search of the flat recovered about 2,722 grams of cannabis from several hiding places; CNB
estimated the drugs at more than S$65,000 and said they could supply about 390 abusers for a week.
[@1,2] The bureau reminded the public that cannabis is a Class A drug and that trafficking more
than 500 grams can attract the mandatory death penalty. [@1,2] Its statement said inquiries into
the man's activities were continuing and did not name him. [@1,2]
