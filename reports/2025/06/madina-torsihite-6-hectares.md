Police Adjunct Chief Commissioner Arie Sofandi Paloh said Mandailing Natal officers burned six hectares
of marijuana on Tor Sihite hill at Rao Rao Dolok village, Tambangan district, on Wednesday 25 June
2025. [@1] ANTARA reported that a public tip led to monitoring that first mapped one hectare before
drone footage revealed about six hectares in total. [@1]

Paloh said nobody was at the remote plantation when the fields were destroyed. [@1] Officers took 140
plants to Polres Madina as evidence while burning the rest on site. [@1] He thanked residents for
reporting hillside cultivation and asked for continued help against drug production. [@1]

ANTARA carried a Polres Madina handout photograph from the operation. [@1]
