Oruba Police Station officers apprehended a 32-year-old trafficker moving
bhang worth Sh13.7 million in Migori County. [@1] [@2] On a residents'
tip-off, officers led by Suna West County Police Commander Chrispine Ogutu
intercepted the black Toyota Kluger, registration KBS 517N, on Giribe-Masara
Road. [@1] [@2]

Driver Edwin Okondo Nyagaga carried eight sacks of narcotic weighing 457.6
kg; police also recovered four different number plates believed used to
evade enforcers. [@1] [@2] Nyagaga was held at Oruba station for processing
and arraignment while detectives probed the consignment's origin. [@1]
