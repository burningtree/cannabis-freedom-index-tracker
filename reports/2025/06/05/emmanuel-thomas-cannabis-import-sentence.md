On 5 June 2025, Judge Alexander Mills at Chelmsford Crown Court sentenced Jay Emmanuel-Thomas, 34,
to four years in prison after he admitted fraudulently evading the prohibition on importing
cannabis. [@1,2]

About 60 kilograms of cannabis was found in luggage at London Stansted Airport on 2 September 2024.
[@1,2] Sentencing remarks say Emmanuel-Thomas, a former Arsenal and Ipswich Town forward, recruited
his girlfriend and another woman to collect the drugs in Thailand and fly them into the United
Kingdom, messaging them when they were stopped and asking them to delete their chat. [@1]

The judge said he played a significant role for financial gain despite recent employment as a
footballer and that his conduct put the couriers at risk of imprisonment. [@1] The BBC reported
prosecutors put the street value at about GBP 600,000. [@2]
