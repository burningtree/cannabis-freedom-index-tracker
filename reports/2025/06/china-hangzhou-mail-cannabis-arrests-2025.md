Hangzhou customs anti-smuggling officers say they have arrested two people over
a cannabis parcel mailed from the United States to Hangzhou, with the second
 suspect caught at Shanghai Pudong International Airport on the evening of 26
June 2025. [@1]

On 18 May 2025 post-office officers inspected an inbound personal parcel
declared as “beef, fries and pea snacks”. [@1] The X-ray image did not match
food; inside, besides cheap snacks, were four plastic-wrapped bags of green-grey
plant material that officers treated as suspected cannabis. [@1] On 19 May the
Zhejiang public-security forensic centre confirmed cannabis, totalling more than
400 g. [@1]

Investigators identified a Hangzhou recipient, a man surnamed Xu born in the
1990s, and let the parcel deliver under control before detaining him when he
signed for it. [@1] WeChat messages on his phone linked him to a man surnamed
Zhang born in the 1980s who arranged overseas supply and a dedicated phone
number for drug parcels while Xu paid and received mail, the account said. [@1]
Both men had increased their use and agreed to sell cannabis to fund it, it
added. [@1]

Zhang had left China; Hangzhou customs pursued him and detained him on 26 June
when he entered through Pudong, completing the pair’s arrest, the article
said. [@1]
