On the morning of 26 June 2025, the International Day against Drug Abuse,
police in Hutubi County in China's Xinjiang region uprooted wild cannabis
across several townships. [@1] Chinanews Xinjiang said the county anti-drug
committee ran a public education event in Century Park the same day while
stations in Ershilidian, Dafeng, Queer Valley, Shitizi and Dongquan acted
together on eradication. [@1]

At one idle plot in Ershilidian's jurisdiction, officers confirmed a wild
cannabis stand and cleared it after survey. [@1] Ershilidian station deputy
chief Wumaierjiang Nu'er told the outlet his unit deployed more than 20
officers to two sites with nearly 2,000 plants combined and asked residents to
report any wild cannabis they saw. [@1]

County anti-drug chief Ren Xueliang said the bureau kept a zero-tolerance stance
on drug crime as new drugs became harder to spot. [@1] The same morning, about
100 county and station officers in total destroyed more than 20,000 wild cannabis
plants, the report said. [@1] No suspects were named. [@1]
