Tuyên Quang provincial police said on 5 June 2025 that, with Hà Nội city detectives and the
Ministry of Public Security drug-crime department, they had broken a large online cannabis
trafficking network and charged ten people. [@1] [@2]

Raids in Tuyên Quang, Hà Nội and Ho Chi Minh City netted more than 200 kg of dried cannabis
in total, according to the announcement. [@1] [@2] In Hà Nội seven suspects were held and
police seized 191 kg of dried cannabis, nearly 160 g of cannabis oil and 591 e-cigarettes
filled with cannabis oil. [@1] [@2] In Ho Chi Minh City two people were detained with 20 kg
of dried cannabis, and in Tuyên Quang another suspect was caught with 186 g. [@1] [@2]

Investigators said the group sold through social media, hid drugs in ordinary parcels, used
delivery firms and took cryptocurrency to hide payments. [@1] [@2] The case began in
September 2024 when Tuyên Quang drug police spotted suspicious online cannabis sales. [@1]
[@2] Vietnamese law treats cannabis like other hard drugs; the file remained open for further
investigation. [@1]
