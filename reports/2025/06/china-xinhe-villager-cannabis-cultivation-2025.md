After a tip from residents, police in Xinhe County, Xinjiang, arrested a
villager and uprooted 63 cannabis plants he had grown among cotton behind his
home. [@1] The Yuqikat Town police station in Lanpa Village received the report
on 1 June 2025 that the man, identified only as Ke, might be cultivating
cannabis. [@1] Officers confirmed the plants contained tetrahydrocannabinol. [@1]

On 2 June the town station and the county criminal investigation anti-drug unit
arrested Ke at his home and removed all 63 plants before they matured. [@1] Ke
told investigators that while cleaning in May he had found cannabis seeds left
from years earlier and planted them, believing private use in a hidden field
would go unnoticed. [@1] Police said he had been administratively punished for
cannabis use in 2014. [@1] He first denied using drugs but a urine test showed
use in mid-May, which he then admitted. [@1]

Ke's wife said she had tried to stop the planting and helped police after the
arrest; she asked for firm punishment so he would “wake up”. [@1] Police said
the case remained under investigation. [@1] Chinese criminal law treats
cultivation of drug plants as illegal regardless of quantity, with harsher
penalties when amounts are large; small plantings can draw administrative
detention and fines under the public security law. [@1]
