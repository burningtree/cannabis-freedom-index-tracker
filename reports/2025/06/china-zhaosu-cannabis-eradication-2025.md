Police stations under Zhaosu County's urban police bureau in Xinjiang sent
officers through farmland, abandoned factories, empty courtyards and woodland
in June 2025 to search for cannabis and uproot any plants found. [@1] The force
said it worked to a “find one plant, remove one plant” rule aimed at zero
cultivation and zero harvest. [@1]

During the sweep the stations cleared more than 4,200 cannabis plants and said
the action checked illegal cultivation and removed a drug source. [@1] Officers
also explained cannabis features, harms and legal penalties to people nearby and
 urged them to report illegal planting. [@1] Zhaosu police said they would keep
patrolling and educating residents on eradication work. [@1]
