Detectives in the Olomouc area detained three men and a woman in early June 2025
as two pairs of drug dealers. [@2] A 52-year-old woman and a man ten years
younger had obtained methamphetamine, known locally as pervitin, since about
2023, used it themselves and sold it on, and also grew cannabis in a garden.
[@2] In the second case, a 37-year-old man had grown and sold cannabis since the
previous year. [@1] He then began distributing methamphetamine with the help of
an accomplice a year older. [@1] The older accomplice also stole copper cables,
police spokeswoman Ivana Skoupilová said. [@1]

The two men were also supplying methamphetamine to the first pair, investigators
found. [@1] House searches at the detainees' homes found drugs and items
connected with drug crime. [@2] Investigators said the dealing had earned them
several tens of thousands of crowns. [@1]

All four were charged with illegal production and other handling of narcotic and
psychotropic substances and poisons. [@2] The woman and her accomplice were
remanded in custody, while the two men were prosecuted at liberty. [@2] Police
said the youngest man, aged 37, faces up to 10 years in prison and the others
one to five years. [@1]
