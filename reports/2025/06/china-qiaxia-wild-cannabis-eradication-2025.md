On 16 June 2025 officers from the Qiaxia border police station under Tacheng
border management saw a villager’s message in a WeChat group during a
province-wide anti-drug live stream, Legal Daily reported. [@1]

The tip pointed to fields and woodland near the village. [@1] Police went to the
site, confirmed wild cannabis, and uprooted more than 15,000 plants, the
article said. [@1]

The piece covered several other same-day clearances in different cities and did
not name arrests in the Qiaxia segment. [@1]
