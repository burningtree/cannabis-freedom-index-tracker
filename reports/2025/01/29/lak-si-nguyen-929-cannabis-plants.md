Acting on neighbourhood complaints about smell and unlicensed foreign workers, Immigration Division
1 led a search on 29 January 2025 at a four-storey commercial block in Soi Ngamwongwan 32, Thung
Song Hong, Lak Si district, with Prachachuen police and officials from the Department of Thai
Traditional and Alternative Medicine. [@1,2]

Officers found climate-controlled grow rooms on the upper floors and storage on the ground level,
with 929 cannabis plants in various stages of growth plus harvested material. [@1,2] They arrested
Nguyen Ngoc Huan, a 49-year-old Vietnamese man who said he had tended the crop for about four months
for 20,000 baht a month from a Thai employer he knew only as Ton, and charged him with working
without permission and processing controlled cannabis for trade without a licence. [@2]

Department officials said the building lacked a valid Bangkok cultivation permit although a company
registered elsewhere held unrelated paperwork; legal action against the owner was pending. [@1,2]
