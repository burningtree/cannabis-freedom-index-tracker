In two raids on a bush allotment at Hauloto on 2 and 6 January 2025, Tonga
Police arrested six people.[@1]

In the first they seized 21 packs of cannabis weighing 8.17 g and five
packs of methamphetamine; in the second, two packs of cannabis weighing
1.54 g and 27 packs of methamphetamine.[@1]

All were charged with possession of illicit drugs and drug utensils, and
the couple arrested in the second raid were kept in custody.[@1]
