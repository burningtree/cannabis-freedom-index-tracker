The Kuala Lumpur High Court sentenced a former air steward and two other men to life imprisonment and 18 strokes of the cane for trafficking 707.05 g of cannabis and cultivating plants. The trafficking charge carried 12 strokes and the cultivation charge six, to run concurrently from their arrest in December 2021. The offences predated the 2023 reform, and the court imposed life imprisonment; the trafficking charge carried the possibility of death. [@1,2]
