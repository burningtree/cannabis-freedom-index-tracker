Sri Lanka Navy personnel recovered about 90 kg and 45 g (wet weight) of Kerala
cannabis adrift in the sea area of Kappaladi, off Kalpitiya, on 10 January 2025.
[@1] An Inshore Patrol Craft detected three polythene sacks drifting in the area
and recovered them. [@1] The sacks contained 30 parcels of Kerala cannabis. [@1]

The operation was conducted by SLNS Vijaya of the Northwestern Naval Command,
which deployed inshore patrol craft. [@2] The Navy said it suspected smugglers
had abandoned the consignment because constant naval operations stopped them
bringing it into the country. [@2] It put the gross street value of the seized
cannabis at over Rs 36 million. [@2] The stock was kept in naval custody until
it is produced for onward legal action. [@2] The Navy said it keeps a
round-the-clock watch on the island's coastal and sea areas to stop smuggling by
sea routes. [@2]
