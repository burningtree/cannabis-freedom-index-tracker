Police arrested two people linked to international trafficking networks after finding more than 85 kilograms of hashish and marijuana, plus 70,000 psychotropic tablets, at a coastal hideout in Quriyat. The Royal Oman Police said the drugs had been loaded into a vehicle for trafficking and consumption. The statement did not split the cannabis between resin and herb or name the source country. [@1]
