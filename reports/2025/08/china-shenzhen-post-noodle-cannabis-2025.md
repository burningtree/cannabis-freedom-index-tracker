Shenzhen Post Office customs seized cannabis weighing 1,724.1 g gross from a
transit parcel, according to a “Customs Release” notice republished by Southern
Net on 30 August 2025. [@1]

The shipment was declared as “Thai noodle snacks”. [@1] Officers said the X-ray
image was abnormal; inside were 15 bucket instant noodles with obvious
repackaging. [@1] Further opening revealed plant material in plastic wraps that
field-tested positive for tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), and a professional
laboratory confirmed THC in the batch. [@1] Customs said the case was transferred
to the anti-smuggling department. [@1]

Southern Net repeated criminal-liability reminders for drug smuggling but did
not name any suspect. [@1]
