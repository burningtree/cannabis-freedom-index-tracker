Police in Ranoli, in Rajasthan's Sikar district, seized more than 64 kg of
cannabis plants, known locally as ganja, hidden in a millet field. [@1] The
plants were growing near Garhwalon ki Dhani, in the Trilokpura area. [@1] The
plot lay in the village hamlet itself, not in a remote area. [@1] Millet stood
on three sides and a well on the fourth. [@1] From a distance the plot looked
like an ordinary millet crop. [@1] Officers searched the field during an "area
domination" campaign, a police sweep run under the direction of Jaipur Range
Inspector General Rahul Prakash, after a tip-off. [@1] The campaign ran from 30
to 31 August 2025. [@2]

Ranoli station house officer Rajesh Kumar said the plants had a market value of
about 9 lakh rupees, or 900,000 rupees. [@2] Revenue records list the plot as
jointly owned by 19 people, among them Ramuram. [@2] No arrests were made, and
Kumar said the accused would be arrested once the investigation established who
had cultivated the field. [@1] The case was registered under the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@2]
