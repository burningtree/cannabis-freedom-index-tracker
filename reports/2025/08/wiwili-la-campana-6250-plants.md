On 20 August 2025 the Nicaraguan army’s 6th Regional Military Command said soldiers
working under the state “Containment Wall” strategy seized and burned 6,250
marijuana plants in La Campana, in the Wamblán micro-region of Wiwilí,
Jinotega. [@1,2]

The command said troops detained Nicaraguan citizen Bismark Antonio Díaz González,
32, who was in charge of caring for the plantation, and turned him over to the
authorities. [@1,2] Canal 10 reported that he was held in police cells and placed
at the disposal of the prosecutor’s office. [@2]

The army issued information note 060/2025 on 21 August 2025; Nicaraguan outlets
published it on 22 August. [@1,2]
