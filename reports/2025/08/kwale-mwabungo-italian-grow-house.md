The Anti-Narcotics Unit with Kenya Police arrested an Italian national and
his Kenyan accomplice after uncovering a cannabis farm and laboratory in
Mwabungo village, Ukunda, Kwale County. [@1]

The targeted one-acre compound hid behind towering perimeter walls and a
heavily secured gate, under discreet surveillance for days on intelligence
of suspicious activity. [@1] Inside, investigators found a grow house with
mature cannabis plants under controlled conditions, laboratory equipment and
chemicals believed for narcotics processing, and packaging materials bearing
labels of known illegal substances. [@1]

Arrested were 37-year-old Gianardi Giulio and Moses Nanoka Egadwa, held as
detectives pieced together the operation's scale, tracked accomplices and
probed links to a broader Coast trafficking network exploiting
international routes. [@1] Formal charges were expected in the coming days.
