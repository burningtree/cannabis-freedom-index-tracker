In August 2025 Norwegian Customs (Tolletaten) opened a larger consignment from the United States at
the air-freight section at Oslo Airport Gardermoen and found about 200 kilograms of marijuana in
eight packages. [@1,2,3] The recipient was registered as a firm in Oslo and the goods were declared
as kitchen utensils; Customs said the marijuana itself was not otherwise concealed inside the
boxes. [@1,2,3]

Customs contacted the police and replaced the drugs with legal goods for a controlled delivery. [@1,2]
The consignment was taken to the listed address and then moved to another address, where police
intervened and arrested several people. [@1,2] Those people were charged, and the case was still
under investigation when Customs published the haul on 23 January 2026. [@1,2]

Customs called the find among the largest marijuana seizures ever made in Norway's postal stream. [@1,2]
Section head Hans Wilhelmsen said smugglers were becoming more risk-willing and using post more often,
and that Customs had seized about 1.5 tonnes of marijuana in Norway in 2025, more than double the
year before. [@1,2,3]
