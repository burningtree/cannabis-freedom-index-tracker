On 8 August 2025 officers from the Ukmergė district police detained three local
residents — two men and a woman in their early thirties — whom they accused of
growing cannabis to supply the market. [@1] [@2] Searches turned up two outdoor
plantations in remote woodland and 432 cannabis plants in total. [@1] [@2]

Police said the group ran a seedling laboratory in a block-of-flats basement before
moving plants to secluded forest plots. [@1] [@2] Officers also seized dried cannabis
products, fertiliser, milligram scales, ammunition and a non-firearm weapon. [@1]
[@2] A Ukmergė court remanded one man for three months; he had previous convictions
for similar offences. [@1] [@2] All three face charges of illegal possession with
intent to distribute and illegal cultivation. [@1]
