At a press briefing in Ogwashi-Uku on 15 September 2026, Delta State NDLEA Commander Halilu
Hamidi said his command had destroyed cannabis farms totalling 36.2 hectares in three
operations since July 2025. [@1,2]

Hamidi said 10.2 hectares were cleared at Emu-Obiogo village in Ndokwa West local government
area on 15 August 2025. [@1,2] Tmy Newspaper, reporting the same briefing, placed the
destruction among intelligence-led efforts to stop Delta becoming a cultivation and transit
hub. [@2]

Neither account names anyone arrested at Emu-Obiogo or describes how the crop was destroyed.
[@1,2] The command's September 2026 figures also included separate farms at Obiaruku and
Orogun in May 2026, covered in other reports. [@1]
