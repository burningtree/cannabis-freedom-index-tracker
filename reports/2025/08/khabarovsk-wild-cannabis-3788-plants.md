During operation Mak-2025, drug-control officers in Komsomolsky district of
Khabarovsk Krai, together with the regional FSB office, military personnel and
district officials, found wild cannabis covering 189 square metres near the
settlement of Molodezhny on abandoned farmland. [@1] They burned 659 plants on the
spot, and the destroyed material weighed 198 kilograms according to the police
account relayed by Izvestia. [@2]

In Nikolaevsky district, near Nikolaevsk-on-Amur, officers working with the
regional Interior Ministry and city administration located a second wild patch of
about 375 square metres. [@1] They burned 3,129 cannabis plants there, with a
reported mass of 94 kilograms. [@2] Both outlets published the regional UMVD
Telegram announcement dated 5 August 2025. [@1]
