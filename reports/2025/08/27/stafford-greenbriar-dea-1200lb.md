On the morning of 27 August 2025 agents from the Drug Enforcement Administration's Houston division
and the Stafford Police Department, with other local and federal partners, executed a search
warrant at 4003 Greenbriar Drive in Stafford. [@1,2]

The DEA told FOX 26 that investigators found a significant illegal marijuana warehouse at the
address. [@1] Stafford police and the DEA said they seized about 1,200 pounds of marijuana; KHOU
reported more than 1,000 pounds from the same operation. [@1,2]

Eighteen people working inside the building were detained. [@1,2] The DEA described them as
undocumented nationals of Mexico, Honduras, Guatemala and El Salvador and said they were turned
over to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement for deportation processing rather than being named
in drug-trafficking charges in these accounts. [@1,2]

KHOU said Houston police, the Harris County Sheriff's Office, Texas Department of Public Safety
and U.S. Border Patrol also took part. [@2]
