On 9 December 2025 law enforcement in South Carolina arrested 12 people indicted in Operation
Ganjapreneur, a State Grand Jury investigation into wholesalers of illegal THC products sold through
Midlands shops. [@1,2]

Teams searched six warehouses, six homes, one retail business and two storage units in Richland and
Lexington counties, loading about eight box-truck loads weighing more than 30,000 pounds (15 tons),
including marijuana, pre-rolls, vapes, oils, dabs, syrups and edibles that authorities said tested
illegal despite packaging. [@1,2] They also seized proceeds and assets worth about $2 million. [@1,2]

Attorney General Alan Wilson said the targets were products marketed to children and teens; SLED and
the DEA Atlanta Division joined the State Grand Jury Division in the takedown. [@1,2] All 12 defendants
faced narcotics charges on 40 counts in the two counties; bond hearings followed on 11 December
before Judge Heath P. Taylor. [@1]
