The Specialized Criminal Court in Sana'a sentenced Ahmed Abed al-Dhabian to death for buying, possessing, transporting and selling hashish, and ordered his movable and immovable property confiscated. A second defendant received 25 years for drug trafficking, and three others five years each for possessing hashish with intent to use. Amnesty International recorded four drug-related death sentences by the Houthi authorities in 2025; its execution figures for Yemen that year list only murder and rape. [@1,2]
