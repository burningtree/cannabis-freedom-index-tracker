On Tuesday 16 December 2025 the narcotics directorate of Kinshasa’s judicial police seized
56 sacks of cannabis hidden in a container during control operations at Mitendi, in western
Kinshasa, the Congolese press agency ACP reported from a police source. [@1]

The same source said cannabis suppliers were also arrested at Mitendi with several sacks
and rolls of kraft paper used to pack the drug for retail sale, as part of Operation Ndobo,
and that those detained were repeat offenders. [@1] A senior judicial police officer quoted
by ACP urged the public to report trafficking networks and lamented that some security
personnel were involved in the trade. [@1]

The report did not say how many people were arrested or what happened to the seized cannabis
after the operation. [@1]
