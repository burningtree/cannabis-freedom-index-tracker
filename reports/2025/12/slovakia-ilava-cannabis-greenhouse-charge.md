A 44-year-old man from the Ilava district has been charged with the crime of
unauthorised production and possession of narcotic and psychotropic substances.
[@2] The charge followed a search of a family house in one of the district's
villages, where officers seized several cannabis plants and equipment used to
grow and process them, the regional police said. [@2] According to the findings
so far, he grew and kept the plants for his own use. [@1] He was briefly
deprived of his liberty during the procedural steps and then released; he is
being prosecuted at liberty. [@1]

Police said he grew the plants in a greenhouse on the grounds of the family
house from about April until 14 October 2025 and dried them in the attic. [@1]
The processed cannabis was kept in the garage and in the attic of his house.
[@1] Officers also seized cannabis seeds, a grinder and other items, and called
in the Police Corps' forensic institute to examine the material. [@2]

The Trenčín regional police announced the case on social media. [@2] The force
described it as another success in suppressing drug crime. [@2]
