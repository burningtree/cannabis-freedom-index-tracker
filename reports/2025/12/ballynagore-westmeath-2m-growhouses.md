Under warrant on 31 December 2025, Gardaí from Westmeath units and the Regional Support Unit searched
two residential properties in Ballynagore, Co Westmeath. [@1,2] Each held a sophisticated cannabis
growhouse; officers seized plants valued at about €500,000 and harvested herb worth about €1.75
million. [@1,2]

Technical examinations of both scenes were completed and the drugs were sent to Forensic Science
Ireland for analysis while investigations continued under Operation Tara. [@1,2] Neither the Garda
press release nor The Irish Times report named any arrest. [@1,2]
