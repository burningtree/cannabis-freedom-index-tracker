Kibiku Police Station officers with the Kirangari Location assistant chief
intercepted a large bhang haul in Kiambu County on Monday, December 8, 2025,
after a public tip-off. [@1]

The raid recovered 1,508 rolls and 1.5 kg of unprocessed bhang with an
estimated street value of Ksh 200,000; one suspect was arrested and held
pending processing and arraignment, with exhibits secured at the station.
[@1]

The NPS statement credited public cooperation, noting most such arrests
follow prompt citizen reports. [@1] The raid came a day after a Thika
operation netted 33 rolls and 26 sachets from one suspect, and days after
Kiugoine officers took two people with 38 rolls and a Kikuyu operation held
four with 1,100 rolls. [@1]
