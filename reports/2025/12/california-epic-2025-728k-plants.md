On 18 December 2025 Attorney General Rob Bonta said EPIC teams had eradicated 728,458 illegally
cultivated cannabis plants and made 204 arrests during the 2025 season. [@1,2] EPIC is a multi-agency
task force led by the Department of Justice that targets unlicensed outdoor and trespass grows tied
to environmental damage, lost tax revenue and labour exploitation. [@1]

## Operations

The attorney general’s office counted 605 operational sites in 34 counties, 170 weapons recovered,
and infrastructure removed that included dams, water lines and containers of toxic pesticides such
as carbofuran. [@1,2] Mendocino County alone accounted for 93 sites and 105,053 plants destroyed,
followed by Kern with 127 sites and 114,341 plants and Riverside with 40 sites and 113,377 plants.
[@1] Kings, Inyo, Madera, Placer, Santa Barbara and Solano saw reconnaissance only without reported
eradication totals. [@1]

## Partners

Bonta said EPIC works with the California National Guard Counterdrug Task Force, State Parks, Fish
and Wildlife, the U.S. Forest Service, Bureau of Land Management, DEA and local sheriffs. [@1] The
2025 statistics sheet matches the 728,458 plant total and 204 arrests against prior seasons going
back to 2017. [@2]
