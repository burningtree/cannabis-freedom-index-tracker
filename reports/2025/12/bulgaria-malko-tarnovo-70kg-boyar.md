At the Malko Tarnovo checkpoint, border police dog Boyar discovered over 70
kilograms of marijuana during a joint inspection of two vehicles heading to
Turkey. [@1]

Boyar indicated where the narcotics were hidden in the cars; border police
and customs officers found that eight gas cylinders were not filled with
gas but instead contained packages of dry green plant material. [@1]

The seized marijuana totalled 70.064 kilograms, valued at €573,170 by a
court-appointed expert, and the four men travelling in the vehicles were
arrested. [@1]
