On 22 December 2025, in the Hadayek October police district of Giza governorate,
the facts later charged against blogger Manar Sami, her husband and a third man
took place. [@3] The Public Prosecution's referral order covers Karim Alaa, 26,
a trader; his wife Manar Sami Ali Abdel Fattah, 26, described as a housewife;
and Mohamed Sami, 26, a trader, all living in Hadayek October and held on the
case. [@3] The order accuses the three of possessing and holding hashish for
trafficking, along with cocaine, a fentanyl derivative and tramadol, and
separately accuses Karim Alaa of holding an unlicensed folding knife described
as a deer-horn clasp knife. [@3]

Investigators said officers arrested Manar Sami, her husband and another person
in Hadayek October and found drugs on them and in their car. [@1] The arrest
record says the first suspect had five hashish slabs, ten further pieces of the
same drug, cash and a mobile phone. [@1] The second suspect had a bag with 20
packets of cocaine, 22 grams of ecstasy tablets and a folding knife. [@1] Manar
Sami's bag held bags of ecstasy tablets weighing 327 grams, a velvet box with
gold jewellery, cash and phones. [@1] A leather bag on the rear seat held 45
hashish slabs, and another bag in the dashboard held cocaine powder. [@1]
Confronted with the seizures, the suspects admitted possessing the drugs for
trafficking, said the car and phones were used to move and sell them, and said
the cash and gold were proceeds of that trade. [@1]

Youm7 reported that investigators referred Manar Sami, her husband and another
person to trial for possessing cocaine, a phenethylamine derivative and hashish
for trafficking in Hadayek October, Giza. [@2] The first hearing for the
blogger, her husband and their associate before the competent criminal court was
set for 12 December. [@1]
