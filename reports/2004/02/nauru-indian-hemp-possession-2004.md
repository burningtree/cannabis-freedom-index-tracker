The Supreme Court dismissed appeals against three-month sentences for possessing Indian hemp under section 10(1)(c) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1968. It was the last reported pre-2004 cannabis prosecution and showed the old law's three-month statutory minimum. [@1]
