Assistant Commissioner Peter Marshall said Operation Ella, carried out between November 2003 and
April 2004, targeted cannabis growers and suppliers and recovered or destroyed an estimated
115,000 plants. [@1,2] Police also seized 92 kilograms of dry cannabis plant material, which they
described as worth NZ$500,000 at a conservative estimate. [@1,2]

## Organised links and other drugs

Marshall said the operation was aimed at organised criminals, noting that officers found seven
clandestine drug laboratories and seized 76 firearms during the work. [@1,2] Police charged 619
people with offences including cannabis cultivation and possession for supply, theft, burglary, and
manufacturing Class A and B controlled drugs. [@1,2]

Marshall said the mix of charges showed that many of those involved in cannabis were also active in
other drug lines. [@1,2] He credited public tip-offs for much of the success. [@1,2] Police also
recovered more than NZ$300,000 worth of stolen property, including cars, boats and an earth-moving
digger. [@1]
