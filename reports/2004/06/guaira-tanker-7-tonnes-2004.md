Federal Police in Guaíra stopped a tanker truck on the Ponte Ayrton Senna, which links Paraná to
Mato Grosso do Sul, and found 7 tonnes and 129 grams of pressed marijuana hidden inside the tank on
18 June 2004. [@1] Driver Tadeu Wiginieski, 53, of Curitiba’s Campo Comprido neighbourhood, was
booked for drug trafficking after officers moved the vehicle to the Guaíra Federal Police office for
a closer search. [@1]

## Tar residue masked the load

The truck, registered in São José dos Campos, had carried Araucária pitch to Cerrito, Paraguay, and
returned with tar residue still staining the tank, according to the Federal Police press office
quoted by Tribuna do Paraná. [@1] Agents found brick-shaped blocks in a compartment; weighing took
most of the day as the tally rose from an initial two-tonne estimate to more than seven tonnes. [@1]
The newspaper said the haul nearly matched the 7.7 tonnes stored at the local Federal Police depot
from earlier cases, and that officers called it the largest marijuana seizure in Paraná. [@1]

Consultor Jurídico later reported that Wiginieski told police he knew he was carrying drugs and
that the load had come from Paraguay; an appellate court in 2005 upheld his conviction for
transporting the marijuana. [@2] Tribuna’s seizure report names no other arrests and does not
mention the ammunition the appellate judgment lists. [@1,2]
