On 6 May 2004 customs officers in Reykjavík seized a postal shipment containing 2.5 kg of hash.
[@1] The parcel was addressed to a man in his twenties in the Suðurnes region. [@1] Police in
Keflavík took over the investigation. [@1]

On Friday 7 May 2004, at 12:22, Keflavík police arrested two men at a home in Reykjanesbær who had
accepted the parcel. [@1] Three people had been arrested in the case by the time of the release, and
one was remanded in custody until 25 May. [@1] The police said the investigation was continuing and
named no charges. [@1]
