Senad, Paraguay’s anti-drug secretariat, spent three days with support from Brazil’s Federal Police
destroying marijuana crops inside Morombí Natural Reserve, which spans Caaguazú and Canindeyú. [@1]
ABC Color reported that helicopter overflights located 85 hectares of cannabis that agents then
eradicated on the ground. [@1]

## Camps and tonnage

Senad said the work removed about 255 tonnes of marijuana from circulation and destroyed eight
camps that stored 150 kilograms of chopped cannabis. [@1] La Nación described the same acreage and
tonnage after three days concentrated in the reserve, with Senad special forces flown in by a
Paraguayan air force helicopter. [@2]

ABC Color quoted Senad as saying the operation had already cost trafficking groups about US$7.5
million. [@1] La Nación’s account of the same deployment put the financial loss to traffickers at
about US$75 million, without explaining the difference in the opened text. [@2] Neither report names
anyone arrested during this phase. [@1,2]
