Antwan Terry, 36, and Anthony Terry, 38, were arrested by the Ramsey County
Sheriff's Office in St. Paul, Minnesota, on 27 October. [@3] Las Vegas police
had identified the brothers as suspects in the killing. [@2] They were booked
into the Ramsey County Adult Detention Center to await extradition to Las Vegas.
[@3] Both face charges of open murder with use of a deadly weapon, four counts
of robbery with use of a deadly weapon, four counts of first-degree kidnapping
with use of a deadly weapon and conspiracy to commit robbery. [@3]

Reynaldo Rodis II, 45, was shot dead on 15 October. [@4] He was found inside
Elevated Life, a marijuana shop, barber and meditation studio on East Flamingo
Road in east Las Vegas. [@1] Las Vegas police said three men entered the
business intending to commit a robbery. [@3] They went to a room where marijuana
was kept, according to an arrest warrant. [@4] The warrant says one suspect
pointed a rifle at an employee and ordered her to sit on a bench. [@4] Police
say one suspect put marijuana into a bag while another rummaged through the
room. [@4] Rodis tried to stop the robbery as the men were leaving and was shot
in a struggle. [@4]

An arrest warrant said surveillance video showed Anthony Terry at the business
during the robbery and later kicking a cord that disconnected a camera. [@4]
Phone records placed his phone near the business and showed calls between the
brothers during the robbery. [@4] A search of his apartment turned up an orange
hoodie that appeared to match one worn by a suspect in the video. [@4]
