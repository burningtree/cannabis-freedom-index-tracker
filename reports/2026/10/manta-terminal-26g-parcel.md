No one was detained when Ecuador's National Police seized a parcel of marijuana
at Manta's bus terminal on Friday 30 October 2026. [@1] The National Canine Unit
found the package during an anti-drug check of interprovincial freight
warehouses inside the terminal, in Manabí province. [@1] The parcel had been
sent from Quito as an interprovincial consignment and was flagged by a trained
detector dog named CUMI. [@1]

During the routine inspection of dozens of packages, CUMI alerted on a cardboard
box in the Quito–Manta load, and officers isolated it for a check. [@1]
Specialists from the Judicial and Anti-Narcotics Police opened the consignment
and a preliminary identification test on the greenish plant material was
positive for cannabis sativa, which police call marihuana. [@1] The official
weighing put the seizure at 26 grams, which police estimated as about 52 doses
intended for local sale in Manta and its outlying neighbourhoods. [@1]

The load was logged as evidence and placed in the chain of custody at the Manabí
Anti-Narcotics Headquarters stores. [@1] The Attorney General's Office received
the crime report and opened an inquiry into the names of the sender and
recipient on the shipping waybill. [@1] Police Zone 4 said random checks of
courier agencies, transport cooperatives and logistics sites in the Manta
district would continue. [@1] The National Police said it would keep watching
key passenger and freight points to slow small-scale trafficking in Manabí. [@1]
