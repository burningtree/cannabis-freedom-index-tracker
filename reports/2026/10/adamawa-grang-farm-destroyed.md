The Adamawa State Command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency, with the 271 Task Force
Battalion of the Nigerian Army in Michika, uncovered a cannabis farm in the mountainous terrain
of Grang village, Zah, in Michika Local Government Area. [@1,2] Acting on intelligence, the
joint operation uprooted and destroyed the suspected Cannabis sativa plantation and recovered
30 bags of dried cannabis weighing 428 kilogrammes. [@1,2]

The operation failed to catch its targeted suspect, identified as Baba Luka Lada Drang, who
went into hiding. [@1] The command says it has intensified surveillance to catch him, and the
recovered exhibits remain in custody for further investigation. [@1]
