Officers of the Satresnarkoba, the narcotics unit of Serang police (Polres
Serang), arrested a 35-year-old man identified as DS on suspicion of selling
cannabis at Taman Tugu Jam, a park on Jalan Sudirman in Serang city, Banten, on
22 October 2026. [@1] The operation began with information from residents, said
AKP Bondan Rahadiansyah, head of the unit. [@1] A team led by Ipda Wawan
Setyawan went to the park and checked DS there. [@3] DS, from Panjang Utara
village in Panjang district, Bandar Lampung, was suspected of waiting for a
customer when officers detained him. [@2] A search of the bag he carried found a
package of cannabis wrapped in duct tape. [@1] Police also seized a backpack and
a mobile phone, which they suspected were used for drug deals. [@1] He was taken
with the evidence to Serang police headquarters for questioning. [@1]

During questioning DS admitted the cannabis was his. [@1] He told investigators
he had been selling it for about two months. [@1] He said he got it from a man
known as MM in Kebon Jeruk, West Jakarta, and police have put MM on their wanted
list (DPO, daftar pencarian orang). [@1] DS said he worked as a construction
labourer and painter, and that his earnings did not cover his living costs. [@1]
Bondan said investigators were still tracing where the cannabis came from and
hunting MM. [@1] Banten News reported that police had not disclosed how many
transactions DS had made or who else was involved. [@4]
