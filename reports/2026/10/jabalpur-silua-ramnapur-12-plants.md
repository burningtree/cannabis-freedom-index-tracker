Bargi police arrested a man on 29 October 2026 after searching a field at
Ramnapur in Jabalpur district, Madhya Pradesh, where cannabis was growing
illegally. [@3] He was named as Sumerra Gond, 50, son of Basori Lal Gond and a
resident of Silua Ramnapur. [@2] The search followed a tip from an informer, and
officers took independent witnesses with them to the field. [@1]

Twelve cannabis plants, between about 2.5 and 7 feet tall, were standing among
rahar, or pigeon pea. [@2] Officers uprooted the plants, removed the soil and
weighed them at 7 kg 200 g. [@2] Police valued the seized plants at about 70,000
rupees. [@1] Khabar Abhi Tak put their value at more than 1 lakh rupees. [@4]

Police registered case 574/26 against Gond under sections 8 and 20 of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@3] Police said the raid was
part of a district campaign against illegal narcotics cultivation and
trafficking. [@2]
