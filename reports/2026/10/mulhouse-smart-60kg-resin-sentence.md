A Spanish man in his sixties was convicted by the Mulhouse court on Wednesday 28
October 2026 over almost 60 kg of cannabis resin found in his car. [@1] The
court sentenced him to 18 months in prison with continued detention. [@1] It
also banned him from French territory for 10 years and ordered him to pay a
customs fine of €177,260. [@1]

Customs officers had stopped the man, almost 70, on the evening of Sunday 25
October 2026 as he drove his Smart on the A36 at Burnhaupt-le-Bas. [@2] At the
La Porte d'Alsace rest area they found the resin in a compartment under the
driver's seat. [@2]

He told the court he had been approached by traffickers while his car was being
repaired at a garage in Barcelona, and that he was to take the goods to Alsace
in exchange for the repair and €600. [@1] He said he knew the load was illegal
but believed he was carrying no more than 5 kg. [@2] He had already been
convicted of transporting drugs in 2019 and was said to have made several trips
between Spain and France. [@1]
