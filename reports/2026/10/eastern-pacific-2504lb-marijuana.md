Three people were detained after a go-fast boat carrying marijuana was
intercepted in the Eastern Pacific. [@2] The Joint Interagency Task Force South
(JIATFS), a US task force, said it helped authorities from a partner nation
detect, monitor and support the interception. [@2] JIATFS announced the result
on 29 October 2026. [@1] It said the seizure was 2,504 pounds of marijuana,
about 1,136 kilograms. [@1]

It described itself as an alliance of 20 international partners and 13 domestic
partners. [@1] In this case its published role was detection, monitoring and
support alongside the unnamed partner. [@1]

The marijuana interception was one of two separate Eastern Pacific actions
reported together. [@2] The other, announced on 28 October, was a Costa Rica
Coast Guard recovery of about 70 floating packages, about 3,675 pounds of
cocaine, near Costa Rica, with a US maritime patrol aircraft and JIATFS helping
to locate the cargo. [@2] The two seizures together were put at about 6,179
pounds, about 2,803 kilograms, of narcotics. [@1]
