Anti-crime police (BAC) in Aureilhan, near Tarbes in the Hautes-Pyrénées,
interrupted a suspected drug deal in the Cité des Arrious at about 17:30 on
Wednesday 28 October. [@2,3] They checked a car whose passenger had been seen
exchanging something with a young woman on foot, who ran off when the officers
appeared. [@2] Two men in the car were arrested, and a third suspect was picked
up the following day. [@1]

A bag at the passenger's feet held drugs and €440 in cash, and a search of the
car turned up more drugs and another €60. [@2] Officers seized 34.92 g of
heroin, 23.33 g of cocaine, 56.71 g of cannabis resin and 42.21 g of cannabis
herb. [@2] The cannabis totalled 98.92 g. [@1,3] Police valued the drugs at about
€3,200 and also seized €500 in cash. [@2]

The two men were placed in police custody. [@2,3] They said the bag belonged to a
third man, whom the BAC arrested the next day. [@2] A search of his home found a
'cocotte', a small wrap of cocaine, and three bars of cannabis resin whose
packaging matched that in the bag. [@2] After their custody ended, all three men
were summoned to appear before the tribunal to answer for their acts. [@2,3]
