On the night of 21-22 October 2026, Delhi Customs officers at the Air Cargo Complex seized 23.220
kilograms of hydroponic marijuana, acting on intelligence from an earlier bust of 148.675 kg of
Bangkok ganja. [@1,2] Officers intercepted a Bangkok consignment declared as lamps; X-ray imaging
showed anomalies, and dismantling the wooden pallet base exposed 23 vacuum-sealed packets of
compressed leaf concealed in the structure, examined in the importer's presence. [@1,2]

The load was valued at about ₹8.12 crore, with a street value near ₹23 crore, and was seized with
its packing under the NDPS Act. [@1,2] One more person was arrested on 22 October and produced
before a magistrate that evening. [@1,2] Across the investigation Delhi Customs has seized nearly
172 kg and arrested two people, after the first seizure exposed a syndicate now under coordinated
follow-up at 11 locations in three states. [@1]
