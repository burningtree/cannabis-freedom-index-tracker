Tambov district police and the municipal administration destroyed wild cannabis
in the village of Tatanovo during the third stage of operation Mak-2026, the
Tambov region Interior Ministry said. [@1] Officers found narcotic plants spread
over more than 500 square metres and burned the vegetation on site. [@1]

MK Tambov quoted the same account from the regional police press service on
28 October 2026. [@2] Both outlets said the operation targets illegal plots,
growers and trafficking routes for plant-based drugs. [@1]
