Marra, Pilerne, in Bardez taluka, Goa. [@1] Police identified him as Danny Frank
Braganza, 49, a resident of Andheri East, Mumbai. [@2] A team led by police
sub-inspector Akshay Fatarpekar acted on specific information, accompanied by
two panch witnesses, local residents who witness police searches. [@2] Braganza
was placed under arrest at 9.50pm after police said they observed the Supreme
Court's prescribed guidelines. [@2]

tops, suspected to be ganja. [@1] A case was registered under the NDPS Act,
India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@3] Further
investigation is in progress with Fatarpekar at Saligao police station. [@1]
