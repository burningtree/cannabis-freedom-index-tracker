Around 400 cannabis plants were found being looked after by a
"gardener", Grimsby Crown Court heard. [@1]

Associated British Ports managers grew concerned when electricity use
spiked at a building on Grimsby docks; Humberside Police raided the
Hutton Road property on 12 June and found plants in three downstairs
rooms with bypassed electrics and liquid fertiliser. [@1] Guxim Sheti,
35, of no fixed address, was inside; he said he had entered the UK
illegally from Albania five months earlier, had stayed 20 days watering
the plants for a promised £10,000, and had no previous convictions. [@1]
Recorder Caroline Sellars jailed him for 18 months, said he would
undoubtedly be deported, and ordered the cannabis and equipment
destroyed. [@1]
