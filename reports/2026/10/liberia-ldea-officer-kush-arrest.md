Officers of the LDEA's Grand Gedeh County Detachment intercepted 44-year-old
Kolubah Coleman, an officer assigned to the agency's Maryland County Detachment,
during an intelligence-led search of a maroon pickup truck with licence plate
A53809. [@1,2]

The search uncovered 26 boxes of tramadol valued at about LRD 520,000 (US$2,787)
alongside 8.23 kilograms of suspected kush valued at LRD 31,274 (US$165). [@1,2]
Coleman was formally charged with illegal possession of narcotics and forwarded
to the local court for prosecution. [@1,2]

The agency reaffirmed that nobody is above the law regardless of rank. [@2] The
arrest raised questions about internal oversight within the country's primary
drug-enforcement body; Coleman maintains the presumption of innocence. [@1,2]
