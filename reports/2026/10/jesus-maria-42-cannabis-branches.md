Two young people aged 19 and 21 were arrested in Jesús María, in Córdoba
province, after police intercepted them with what officers described as illicit
substances. [@1] The interception was a preventive procedure run by the Colón
departmental police. [@1] It took place on the afternoon of Thursday 29 October
2026. [@1] Officers found that the pair were carrying plant material compatible
with cannabis sativa among their belongings. [@1]

The Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico (FPA), the anti-narcotics police force, was
called in immediately, and its experts carried out the corresponding field
tests. [@1] The FPA formalised the seizure of 42 branches with dried leaves.
[@1] Both of those detained were placed at the disposal of the courts for an
alleged infringement of National Narcotics Law No. 23.737, Argentina's national
narcotics statute. [@1]

Law 23.737 is Argentina's national narcotics statute, known as the Ley Nacional
de Estupefacientes. [@1] The 42 items seized were branches with dried leaves,
cut plant material rather than whole plants. [@1]
