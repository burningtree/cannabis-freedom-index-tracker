Acting on the police commissioner's festival-season drug crackdown orders
and an informer's tip about a narcotics-laden pickup, Sigra police under
inspector Shivakant Mishra cordoned off the old iron tank area at
Pachpedwa and stopped a suspicious goods pickup whose driver tried to flee.
[@1] Under ten sacks of cattle feed the team found 30 packets of cannabis
weighing 31.275 kg, valued at Rs 15 lakh. [@1]

The driver, Rajkishore Gaur of Nawada in Bihar's Buxar district — spelt God
in parts of the report — is described as a longtime interstate smuggler moving
narcotics across the border under animal-feed cover, whose strong smell masks
cannabis. [@1] He said the consignment was meant for festival-time sale. [@1]
He was to be sent to jail on Thursday. [@1]
