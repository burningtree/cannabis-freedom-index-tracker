Around 2 AM on Monday, police on the Ranitaar-Beltar inner road at Chargare
in Chaudandigadhi Municipality-1 found a Bolero pickup van standing
abandoned. [@1,2] Inside the vehicle, registration Ba 14 Cha 2255, were 29
plastic sacks packed with 940.44 kg of marijuana. [@1,2]

No driver or owner was present at the parked jeep, and police said they are
investigating the whereabouts of the driver and others behind the load while
working to identify its owner; the vehicle and sacks were transferred to the
local police office. [@1,2] The
Chargare find was one of two Udayapur operations reported together that
morning: the same night's sweep also netted 22-year-old Bishwaraj Rai with
110 kg near Saguri Bridge in Katari, and a 159.4 kg Belaka pickup seizure
followed that evening, bringing the district's three-day tally to 1,209.84
kg. [@1,2] Investigations into both cases continue. [@1] The district police place the
Chargare find within seizures made between 11 and 12 October across the
Belaka, Chaudandigadhi and Katari municipalities. [@2]
