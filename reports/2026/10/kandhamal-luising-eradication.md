On 14 and 15 October 2026, Kandhamal police destroyed about 30 acres
(12.1 hectares) of cannabis grown in remote forest under Luising Gram
Panchayat, within Gochhapada police station limits. [@1,2] Officers used
drones to watch the area during the drive. [@1]

Police arrested nine men from the villages of Raigada, Dhakudi, Rukpadar,
Rutungia, Regadulu, Pipalpali, Badul and Ghusurumaha. [@1,2] The operation
was led by the Sadar Phulbani sub-divisional police officer P. P. Subhrajit
Biswal with Gochhapada inspector Subhrajit Samantray. [@1]

Bharasa Live published the names of the arrested men, aged from 24 to 40 and
drawn from Kandhamal and neighbouring Boudh district. [@2]
