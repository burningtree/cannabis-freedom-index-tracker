Three men aged 33, 37 and 43 and two women aged 26 and 46 were arrested in three
simultaneous search warrants in Mexico City, the city's Public Security Ministry
(SSC) said. [@3] The SSC announced the operation on 29 October 2026. [@3] The
warrants covered properties in the alcaldías (Mexico City's boroughs) of
Magdalena Contreras, Venustiano Carranza and Coyoacán that the SSC described as
used to store, sell and distribute drugs. [@1] A control judge granted the
warrants after a prosecutor presented evidence gathered in intelligence and
field work. [@2]

At the first property, on Calle Huayatla in Colonia Huayatla, Magdalena
Contreras, they detained a 26-year-old woman and a 33-year-old man. [@2] There
they seized 15 kg of marijuana, 75 doses of marijuana, 300 g of cocaine and two
phones, along with methamphetamine pills. [@2] At the second, on Calle José
Rivera in Colonia Moctezuma Primera Sección, Venustiano Carranza, they detained
a 43-year-old man and seized 100 doses of cocaine, 100 doses of marijuana, 500 g
of marijuana and a phone. [@2] At the third, on Calle Malvas in Colonia Jardines
de Coyoacán, Coyoacán, they detained a 46-year-old woman and a 37-year-old man
and seized 10 kg of marijuana and a phone. [@2]

The SSC put the haul at 1,344 suspected methamphetamine pills and more than 25
kg of marijuana. [@1] All five were handed to the Public Prosecutor's Office,
which will decide their legal situation. [@1]
