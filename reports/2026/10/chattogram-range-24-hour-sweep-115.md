Police in Chattogram Range arrested 115 people on drug charges. [@2] The arrests
came during a 24-hour operation across the range. [@1] The operation was carried
out on Friday 30 October 2026, and the range office announced the results in
Chattogram the next day. [@2]

The 115 drug arrests comprised four people accused of carrying drugs, 67 of
consuming drugs and 44 of drug trading. [@2] Thirty-five people were jailed by
mobile courts in the anti-drug operation, 33 drug cases were filed and 7,610
taka was collected in fines. [@1]

The drug arrests were part of a wider sweep that also targeted juvenile gangs,
mugging, extortion and militancy, the range office said. [@2]
