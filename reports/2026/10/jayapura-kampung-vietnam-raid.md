The Papua Police Narcotics Directorate raided an alleged drug-trafficking operation in Kampung
Vietnam, Argapura, South Jayapura district, Jayapura City, on Saturday 24 October 2026. [@1,2]
Officers detained 10 suspects: six Papua New Guinean nationals and four Indonesian citizens.
[@1,2]

They seized two sacks holding a total of 10 kilogrammes of dried cannabis, plus two air
rifles, one softgun, two bayonets and two machetes. [@1,2] The raid was part of efforts
against suspected drug distribution in the Jayapura area, and the suspects and evidence were
handed over for further legal proceedings. [@1,2]
