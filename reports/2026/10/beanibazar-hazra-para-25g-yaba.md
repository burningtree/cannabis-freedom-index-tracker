Border Guard Bangladesh (BGB) carried out a special anti-drug operation on the
Beanibazar border in Sylhet and recovered yaba and cannabis. [@1] At noon on 29
October 2026 a special patrol team from Borogram border outpost, under
Beanibazar Battalion (52 BGB), conducted the operation. [@1]

A BGB source said the team acted on secret information and searched Hazra Para
in Dubag Union No. 3 of Beanibazar upazila, Sylhet district, about 300 yards
inside Bangladesh from the border zero line. [@1] From that area the patrol
recovered 28 yaba tablets and 25 grams of cannabis. [@1]

The authorities said this kind of strict surveillance and operation by BGB was
continuing in the border area to stop drug trafficking and maintain law and
order. [@1] They said the subsequent legal process over the recovered drugs was
under way. [@1]
