The anti-narcotics squad of West District, Delhi Police, busted an inter-state cannabis supply
network in the capital on Sunday 25 October 2026, arresting three accused and seizing 26.46 kg
of marijuana. [@1,2] The suspects, Chandan Kumar, Nitesh Kumar and Vicky Kumar, all aged 22 to
25, were booked under the NDPS Act. [@1,2]

Chandan Kumar, 22, was nabbed after his e-rickshaw was scanned during a routine Khyala patrol,
yielding 16.906 kg of ganja, with another 5.354 kg recovered from his rented JJ Colony
premises. [@1,2] He disclosed that the ganja came from Odisha through courier parcels arranged
by Nitesh Kumar of Bihar, hidden in saree boxes, sound boxes and RO-purifier cartons for
distribution among Delhi peddlers. [@1,2] Nitesh allegedly ran the three-state operation by
mobile phone, putting false addresses on consignments so couriers would call his own number to
arrange receipt. [@1,2]
