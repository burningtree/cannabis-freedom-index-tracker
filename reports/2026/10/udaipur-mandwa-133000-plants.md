Under Operation Ganjagaveshana, the NCB's Jodhpur zonal unit with ANTF
Rajasthan and Rajasthan Police raided Mandwa village in Kotra tehsil, Udaipur
district, on an ANTF tip-off and confirmed cannabis growing over about 57
hectares across 56 land records entries. [@1] About 70 personnel with 10
labourers and three tractors destroyed roughly 1.33 lakh plants in the
presence of the deputy superintendent of police, the patwari and independent
witnesses. [@1]

The potential ganja value was estimated at Rs 199.50 crore. [@1] Crime
8/2026 was registered at NCB Jodhpur and the investigation continues. [@1] The
drive follows the Vision Document on Narcotics Control 2026-29 goal of a
drug-free India through action at the source. [@1]
