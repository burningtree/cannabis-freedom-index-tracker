Members of the Secunda Flying Squad on patrol on the N17 near Leandra spotted a light-blue Honda Civic with Gauteng plates on 19 October 2026. [@1,2] When they tried to stop it, the driver abandoned the car and fled on foot. [@1,2]

Officers found dagga loaded on the rear seat and in the boot, weighing 103 kg with an estimated street value of about R800,000. [@1,2] The vehicle and the dagga were seized for further investigation. [@1]
