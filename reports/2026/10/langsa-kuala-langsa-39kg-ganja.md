Officers of the drug unit (Satresnarkoba) of the Langsa city police seized
almost 40 kg of cannabis in Kuala Langsa, a gampong (village) in West Langsa
district, Aceh. [@1] The raid took place at about 11:30 pm on Wednesday 21
October 2026. [@1] The two men detained were AN, 27, a farmer, and MM, 20, a
student. [@1] Both came from Serbajadi district in East Aceh regency. [@1]
Langsa police chief AKBP Hyrowo said the cannabis weighed 39.898 grams, about
39.9 kg. [@1] Investigators suspect the pair acted as dealers (bandar) or
distributors (pengedar). [@1] The case was recorded in a police report dated 21
October 2026. [@1]

The seizure was part of Operasi Sarang Narkoba 2026, which the Langsa police ran
from 9 to 27 October 2026. [@1] Police said the operation produced ten drug
cases, nine of them involving methamphetamine (sabu) and one involving cannabis.
[@2] It was carried out with the Langsa district office of the National
Narcotics Agency (BNNK) and Langsa customs. [@1] Fourteen men were detained
across the operation as a whole, twelve in the methamphetamine cases and two in
the cannabis case. [@1]

Police announced the operation's results at a press conference in Langsa on 29
October 2026. [@2] They said the two men would face legal proceedings under the
applicable drug laws. [@1]
