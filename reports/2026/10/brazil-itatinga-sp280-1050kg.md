Federal and highway police found 1,088 blocks of marijuana weighing 1,050.2 kg in a vehicle on the
SP-280 highway at km 201 in Itatinga, São Paulo state, on the morning of 2 October 2026. [@1,2] The
drugs were hidden in cardboard boxes in the cargo area. [@2]

The operation was carried out by the Federal Police's Integrated Force against Organised Crime
(FICCO/SP) and the Military Highway Police. [@1,2] The Federal Police said the vehicle had come from
Maringá, Paraná. [@1]

The driver was arrested and taken to the Federal Police station in Bauru, where he remained at the
disposal of the courts. [@1,2] The Federal Police did not name him. [@1]
