Police raided a tea-and-snacks shop in the Ranitalab police-station area of
Patna district and arrested one young man at the scene. [@1] The shop stands
beside National Highway 139, near Kab-Nisarpura village. [@1] Officers recovered
420 grams of ganja, the name used locally for cannabis, from the shop. [@1]

Dainik Bhaskar said the raid followed a secret tip and that the campaign against
drugs would continue. [@1]
