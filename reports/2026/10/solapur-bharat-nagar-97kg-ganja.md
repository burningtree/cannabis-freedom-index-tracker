Solapur city crime branch officers seized 96 kg 910 g of ganja from a house in
Bharat Nagar and arrested two people. [@1] Police named them as Mujammil Altaf
Shaikh, 20, and Nilofar Irfan Shaikh, 39, both of house number 400 near the
Bharat Nagar sports complex at Kumbha Naka. [@2] Officers on patrol near the
sports complex on 28 October 2026 saw a man filling a bag with suspicious
material outside a house. [@1] A police constable searched him and found ganja
with buds in the bag. [@2] Questioned, the man admitted that more ganja was
stored in the house. [@2]

Police raided the house, found Nilofar Shaikh there, and seized 48 packets of
ganja packed in plastic tape. [@2] A forensic team confirmed that the seized
stock was ganja. [@2] Police valued the ganja at 4,845,500 rupees, about 48.45
lakh. [@2] With a mobile phone worth 25,000 rupees, the property seized came to
4,870,500 rupees. [@2]

A case was registered at Sadar Bazaar police station, and assistant police
inspector Bharat Chandanshive is investigating. [@2] Police commissioner M.
Rajkumar launched the anti-drug campaign that the operation formed part of. [@2]
