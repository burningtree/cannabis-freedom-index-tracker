On 30 and 31 October 2026, police in Skopje, Prilep, Demir Kapija, Bogovinje and
Štip detained 13 people aged between 18 and 46, the Ministry of Interior said.
[@1] Marijuana, white powdered and granular substances, and tablets were found
and seized from them. [@1] In Skopje, police detained nine people — six from the
capital, a 19-year-old from the Ilinden neighbourhood, a 35-year-old from the
village of Novo Selo and a 46-year-old from Kočani — after marijuana, white
powder and white granular material were found on them. [@1]

Prison police searching a 32-year-old convicted man at Prilep prison found
powdered substances, several tablets of different colours and items containing
powder. [@1] In Štip, police detained a 26-year-old man from Skopje. [@1]
Officers noticed he was using marijuana and seized the cigarette butt he
discarded. [@1] In the village of Bogovinje, police detained a 44-year-old man
from Pirok after marijuana was found on him. [@1] In Demir Kapija, a 29-year-old
man from the village of Korešnica was detained after a search found white powder
and a tablet. [@1]

The authorities were informed in all the cases, and the ministry said
appropriate charges would be filed once the cases were fully documented. [@2]
