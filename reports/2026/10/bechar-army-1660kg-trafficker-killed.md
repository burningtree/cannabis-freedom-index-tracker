In its 4 October 2026 release, the Ministry of National Defence said combined Army detachments
in the 3rd Military Region at Béchar foiled two attempts to move a large quantity of drugs
into the country during search operations on 1 and 2 October. [@1] One trafficker was killed
and 1,660 kilogrammes of treated kif and three pairs of day binoculars were seized. [@1] The
ministry presented the haul as proof of its units' resolve to block attempts to flood the
country with drugs. [@1]
