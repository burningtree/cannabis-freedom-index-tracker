On Friday 16 October 2026 Lincolnshire Police executed a warrant at a property in Welbourn, near
Lincoln, and found a cannabis grow spread across multiple rooms. [@1,2] Officers seized 165 plants
that had begun to flower and were, they said, about two to three weeks from harvest. [@1,2]

The electricity supply had been bypassed; National Grid attended to make the property safe. [@1]
Based on the size and stage of the grow, police estimated it could have generated an annual turnover
of around £350,000. [@1,2]

A 65-year-old man and a 27-year-old man were each arrested on suspicion of cultivating cannabis,
producing a Class B controlled drug and abstracting electricity without authority. [@1,2] Both were
released on bail while enquiries continued. [@1,2] Officers dismantled the grow and removed the
plants. [@1]
