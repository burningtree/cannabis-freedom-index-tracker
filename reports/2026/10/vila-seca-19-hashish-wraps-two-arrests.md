Local police in Vila-seca arrested two men on suspicion of a crime against
public health, Spain's drug-trafficking offence, after stopping a rental car
early on Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1] Officers pulled the vehicle over at
about 1.30 am in Plaça de Ramon Muntaner, found its documents in order and
identified the driver and his passenger, then noticed a strong smell of drugs.
[@1] One of the two was already listed in police databases in connection with a
suspected crime against public health in Reus, which prompted officers to search
the men and inspect the car. [@1]

The search turned up foil-wrapped packets, some inside a tobacco pouch and
others in a small wallet under the passenger seat. [@1] In all, officers seized
19 wrappers of hashish and marijuana and €300 in cash in small-denomination
notes. [@1] One of the men also handed over a small piece of hashish from his
jacket pocket, and other sums of cash were found among the men's belongings.
[@1] The local police said the presentation of the substances, together with the
split bills, was an indication that they could be intended for sale rather than
personal use. [@1] The car was towed to the municipal depot. [@1]
