A large-scale ganja cultivation case surfaced in the state: before the crop
could be harvested and disposed of, the Jodhpur Narcotics Control Bureau and
the Anti-Narcotics Task Force destroyed the crop grown on about 10 bighas on
Wednesday, a haul whose market price was estimated at Rs 12 crore. [@1]

NCB zonal director Ghanshyam Soni said an Operation Shankar was run after
7,577 plants were found planted in a field at Kotda in Udaipur; they would
have produced 2,273 kg of ganja valued at about Rs 12 crore on the market.
[@1] He added that Rajasthan's patwaris have been directed to inspect their
areas regularly during girdawari and immediately report suspected narcotic
cultivation to the girdawar and tehsildar. [@1]

Joint teams of NCB Jodhpur, ANTF Rajasthan and Rajasthan Police traced the
illegal cultivation at Nayavas village under Kotda tehsil and acted; an ANTF
team in plain clothes had first kept the crop under secret watch at the spot.
