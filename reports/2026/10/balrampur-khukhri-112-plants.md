Police arrested Kabir Say Tekam after finding 112 cannabis plants growing among
vegetables on land in his possession in Khukhri village, Balrampur district.
[@1] The raid followed a tip from a patwari, a village revenue official, who
told police on 3 October 2026 that Tekam was growing ganja, or cannabis, among
his vegetable crop. [@3] Officers from the Bariyo police outpost searched the
plot in Rakhetpara, Khukhri. [@2] Tekam admitted during questioning that he had
planted the cannabis, police said. [@1]

The plants, of various sizes, were counted in front of witnesses and a seizure
memo was prepared. [@1] Police produced Tekam before a court and sent him to
judicial custody, said Alexius Toppo, sub-divisional officer of police in Kusmi.
[@1] A case was registered against him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, Haribhoomi reported. [@2]

The 112 plants weighed about 36 kg, police said. [@1] Chhattisgarh.co put the
total weight at 36.3 kg. [@3] Police estimated the value of the seized cannabis
at 350,000 rupees. [@2]
