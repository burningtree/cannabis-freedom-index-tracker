The Upper East Regional Police Command arrested a 36-year-old man over
alleged drug distribution at Paga in the Kassena-Nankana West District: the
suspect, Tumbana Ane, was apprehended in an operation that seized 38 parcels
of dried leaves suspected to be Indian hemp. [@1] [@2]

The substances were found in a search of Ane's residence, where officers
also recovered other prohibited substances including Tramadol as
investigations into his alleged drug trade intensified. [@2] The search also
uncovered a suspected escape tunnel, which police believe may have been a
hiding place for illicit substances or an escape route; its discovery raised
further concerns about the activities run from the residence. [@2]

A police statement released on Monday, October 5, 2026 said Ane was in
custody and expected to be arraigned, and preliminary investigations linked
him to alleged brothel operations at an illegal mining site at Wuru, a
suburb of the Kayoro community in the same district. [@2] Police said
investigations were ongoing to establish the full extent of his activities
and identify connected individuals. [@2]
