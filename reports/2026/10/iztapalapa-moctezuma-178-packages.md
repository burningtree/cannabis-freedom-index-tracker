Three men aged 20, 22 and 57 were arrested in the raid. [@1] The raid happened
in Iztapalapa, in the Santiago Acahualtepec neighbourhood. [@1] It took place at
a property on Callejón Moctezuma. [@2] Officers had a judicial warrant obtained
after investigation, intelligence work and field surveillance. [@1] The
operation involved officers from the city's Public Security Ministry (SSC), the
Mexico City Prosecutor's Office (FGJ), the Navy (Semar) and the National Guard.
[@1] The authorities said they used no force and followed police protocols and
respect for human rights. [@1]

Inside, officers found 72 blue plastic bags and 24 clear plastic bags, plus a
sealed Ziploc bag, all holding dried green plant material resembling marijuana,
with the Ziploc bag holding loose material. [@2] They also seized 36 yellow
plastic bags and 28 white paper wrappings of a solid substance resembling
cocaine, and 17 clear plastic bags of a substance that could be methamphetamine.
[@2] In total, the authorities counted 178 bags and wrappings, plus the loose
plant material and a mobile phone. [@1]

The three men and the seized items were handed to the Public Prosecutor's
Office, which will decide their legal situation as the investigation continues.
[@1] The property was sealed and remained under guard while investigations
continued. [@1]
