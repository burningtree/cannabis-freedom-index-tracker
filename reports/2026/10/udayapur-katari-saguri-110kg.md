On Sunday night police arrested Bishwaraj Rai, 22, of Tapli Rural
Municipality-2, near Saguri Bridge in Katari Municipality-6 with about 110
kg of marijuana. [@1] Officers say he was carrying the load from Junglebo
along the Tintale route toward Siraha when he was stopped at around 10:20 PM.
[@1,2] A second account places the arrest in the forest of Sanghuri Khola in
the same ward and gives the quantity as 100 kg rather than 110 kg. [@2]

Rai was arrested on drug-trafficking charges and is in custody while
investigations continue into both this case and the abandoned-Bolero seizure
found the next morning at Chargare. [@1] His arrest opened the three-operation
Udayapur sweep of 11-12 October, which also took in the 940.44 kg Chargare
Bolero and the 159.4 kg Belaka pickup for a combined 1,209.84 kg, with the
seized drugs and vehicles transferred to local police offices. [@1,2] The
district police describe the three seizures as falling within a single
36-hour period. [@2]
