Jashpur police seized 440.63 kg of ganja hidden under chilli sacks at the Lavakera
barrier and arrested two men allegedly transporting it from Odisha to Uttar Pradesh.
[@1,2]

On the morning of 2 October 2026 at about 9 am, acting on a tip-off that smugglers were
entering Jashpur from Odisha, the Anti-Narcotics Unit and Tapkara police stopped a Tata
Eicher mini-truck at the Lavakera check barrier near the Odisha border. [@1,2] The upper
part of the truck was loaded with chilli sacks; underneath, officers found 440 packets
weighing 440.63 kg, valued at about Rs 2.2 crore. [@1,2]

The arrested men, Mohammad Wasim (28) and Mohammad Asif (34) of Mundera village in
Prayagraj district, Uttar Pradesh, were travelling from Odisha toward Uttar Pradesh.
[@1] This was one of 16 ganja-smuggling cases in Jashpur since January 2026, totalling
about 1,906 kg worth Rs 8 crore with 22 arrests. [@1]
