On 29 October 2026, Ramnagar police searched the home of 58-year-old Rajendra
Bais, also called Lallu Bais, in Majhatolwa village, Maihar, and seized 870
grams of ganja from the yard behind the house. [@1] Officers found him at home.
[@1] Because an arrest was judged unnecessary, they released him after serving a
notice under section 35(3) of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, the
criminal-procedure code. [@1]

Sub-inspector Kuldeep Patel, in charge of the Maryadpur outpost, had been told
that Bais, son of Ramsaroop Bais and a resident of Majhatolwa, was hiding
illegal ganja in that yard. [@1] A team led by Ramnagar station officer
Inspector Vijay Tripathi raided the spot the informant named. [@1] The cannabis
was wrapped in a plastic sheet under dried cow-dung cakes. [@1] Police valued
the 870 grams at 8,700 rupees. [@1]

A case was registered against him under sections 8 and 20(b) of the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and taken up for investigation. [@1] The
search was directed by Maihar Superintendent of Police Awadhesh Pratap Singh and
Additional Superintendent Dr Chanchal Nagar, with guidance from Amarpatan
subdivisional officer Khyati Mishra. [@1] The station said action against the
drug trade was continuing. [@1]
