Two men aged 18 and 21 were arrested and a 15-year-old was apprehended at Campo
Grande's bus terminal in the early hours of 29 October 2026, the city's
Municipal Civil Guard (GCM) said. [@1] Guards patrolling the boarding area
around 1:50 am noticed that one passenger was nervous and smelled cannabis. [@1]
With the passengers' permission they searched the suitcases and found tablets of
a substance that looked like cannabis. [@1] The two passengers said they were
travelling together and carrying drugs, and named a third person, who was found
shortly afterwards. [@1] All three told guards they had collected the drug in
Ponta Porã, a town on the border with Paraguay, and were taking it to Curitiba.
[@2]

Denar, the specialised anti-drug trafficking police station, confirmed that the
three suitcases held 91.9 kg of cannabis, divided into 262 portions. [@1] One
suitcase weighed 30.4 kg, the second 27.3 kg and the third 34.2 kg. [@2] The
three and the drug were taken to the Depac Cepol police station in Campo Grande.
[@1] The case was registered as drug trafficking with suspected interstate
transport and remains under investigation by the Civil Police. [@1]
