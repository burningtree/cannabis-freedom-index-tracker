Officers of the Unidad de Acción Preventiva (UAP), the preventive-action unit of
the Policía de Mendoza, apprehended two women in Godoy Cruz after the pair tried
to leave when police moved to identify them. [@1] The flight started there and
ended a few blocks away, where both women were stopped. [@1]

In the search that followed, officers found packets of marijuana among one
woman's belongings. [@1] Both women were placed at the disposal of the Oficina
Fiscal de Godoy Cruz, the local prosecutors' office that handles cases from that
department. [@1]

The stop came during a series of preventive patrols the same unit carried out
across Gran Mendoza, the urban area that includes the provincial capital,
Guaymallén and Godoy Cruz. [@1]
