A Proceeds of Crime Act hearing at Cardiff Crown Court on 22 October 2026 heard
that Louis Moates had benefited from drug dealing by £166,184 but had only
£1,734 in available assets. [@1] Judge Vanessa Francis ordered him to pay £1,784
within 12 weeks or serve an additional month in prison in default. [@2]

Police first searched his home on 25 August 2023. [@1] They seized cannabis,
weighing scales and a grinder. [@1] The cannabis weighed 122g and had a street
value of £660 to £960. [@1] A second search on 20 June 2024 found only his
partner at home. [@1] It seized 148.3g of cannabis worth £790 to £1,530, plus
143 codeine tablets and 141 diazepam tablets. [@1] Moates was arrested at his
workplace. [@1] Messages on two mobile phones showed he supplied crack cocaine
and cocaine. [@1]

Moates, of Hollybush, Whitchurch, Cardiff, later pleaded guilty to being
concerned in the supply of Class A drugs, possession with intent to supply Class
B drugs and possession of Class C drugs. [@1] The sentencing hearing was at
Newport Crown Court in January 2026. [@1] He was sentenced to a total of 187
weeks in prison. [@1] The court heard he had no previous convictions. [@3] His
barrister said he had spent significant periods homeless, which coincided with
his offending. [@3]
