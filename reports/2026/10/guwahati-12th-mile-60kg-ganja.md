Guwahati police seized around 60 kg of suspected cannabis and arrested a man
from a rented house in the Ambher area of 12 Mile near Jorabat on Friday
evening. [@1] [@2]

The arrested person was identified as Sunil Kar, 51, who resides at 12th Mile,
Ambher under Basistha Police Station; his permanent address is in Sorubhagia
village under Jakhalabandha Police Station in Nagaon district. [@2] The raid
was conducted by a team from Basistha Police Station led by officer-in-charge
Kapil Pathak following a tip-off about the suspected storage of cannabis at
the rented house. [@1] [@2]

The seized substance was tested using a drug-detection kit, which returned a
positive result for ganja. [@2] Police registered a case in connection with
the seizure and launched an investigation into the matter. [@1]
