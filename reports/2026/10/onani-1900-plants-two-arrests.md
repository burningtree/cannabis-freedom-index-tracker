Carabinieri of the Bitti company, with the helicopter squadron Cacciatori di
Sardegna, found a field hidden in thick vegetation near Onanì, in Nuoro
province. [@1,2] ANSA says it held more than 1,900 plants of cannabis indica,
ready for harvest. [@1] La Nuova Sardegna puts the count at about 1,900, in
an advanced stage of ripening. [@2]

Two men were caught tending the crop and arrested in the act. [@1,2] A judge
confirmed the arrests and ordered a third man, described as their accomplice,
to remain in the town where he lives. [@1,2] The plants were sampled for a
laboratory test of their THC and then pulled up to be destroyed. [@1] La
Nuova Sardegna says an irrigation system was dismantled and seized with the
tools used to tend the plants. [@2]

The same accounts describe a separate find of about 1,000 plants at Lula.
[@1,2] That operation is not this one. [@2]
