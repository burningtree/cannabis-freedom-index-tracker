Seven people were arrested on drug charges in West Baton Rouge Parish and three
more were still wanted, deputies said on Friday. [@1] Kiddiocus Sims, 37, of
Port Allen, was arrested on two counts of distributing marijuana, listed in the
charges as Schedule I. [@1] Cardell Lomas, 31, of Sunshine, was arrested on two
counts of possession with intent to distribute marijuana. [@1] Trent Anderson,
31, of Baton Rouge, was arrested as a principal to distributing marijuana. [@1]
Steven Gordon, 37, of Plaquemine, was arrested on a second-offence marijuana
possession charge. [@1]

Donald Pierson, 52, of Port Allen, was still wanted on a marijuana distribution
charge. [@1] Keri Norder, 41, of Baton Rouge, was wanted on heroin and
amphetamine charges. [@1] Dquan Lee, 25, of Baton Rouge, was wanted as a
principal to fentanyl distribution. [@1]

Other charges in the roundup involved methamphetamine, fentanyl and amphetamine.
[@1] The charges also included possessing a firearm in the presence of a
controlled dangerous substance. [@1] One charge accused a suspect of illegal
possession of a controlled dangerous substance in the presence of a juvenile.
