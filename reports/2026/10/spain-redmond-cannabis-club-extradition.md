Sonny Redmond, 33, consented to his extradition at a hearing in London on Monday
and did not contest the case, so his return to Spain is imminent. [@1] He was
arrested in the UK on a Spanish arrest warrant, which is believed to have been
issued after he broke the conditions of his bail in Spain. [@1] He had been
released on bail last November, around seven months after his arrest over an
armed raid on a Costa Blanca cannabis club. [@1] Redmond is from Bootle,
Merseyside. [@1]

In October 2024, two men including Redmond broke into Fulgencio’s in the La
Marina urbanisation armed with machine guns. [@1] They threatened an employee
and stole around €5,000 of products and €600 in cash, then demanded €25,000 from
the owner and a 50 per cent share of future profits. [@1] One message sent to
the owner said they would bring the war to him and that they understood he had a
wife and children. [@1] Another warned of blowing his head off and was
accompanied by videos showing the gang’s weapons. [@1]

He is believed to have left the country after the incident and was arrested on
the Orihuela Costa on April 8. [@1] He tried to flee Guardia Civil officers by
running into a house, where he held an elderly foreign couple hostage. [@1]
After a prolonged struggle in which three officers were injured, he was subdued
and arrested. [@1] Two sub-machine guns were found at his house with over 300
rounds of ammunition. [@1]

Weeks later, a gunman sprayed his car with bullets in an underground car park in
Orihuela Costa. [@1] He was sitting in his car at around 10pm on December 11
last year when an unknown assailant opened fire in the car park in Lomas de Cabo
Roig, with up to 15 bullets piercing his vehicle. [@1] The shooting left him
with gunshot wounds to his arm, leg and chest. [@1] He was rushed to Torrevieja
Hospital, where he underwent emergency surgery and spent time in intensive care.
[@1] After recovering, he returned to the UK and appeared at Westminster
Magistrates’ Court on June 11. [@1] He had been due to stand trial in Liverpool
for possessing a knife and a mobile phone while serving a previous prison
sentence, but the Crown Prosecution Service has since dropped the case. [@1]
