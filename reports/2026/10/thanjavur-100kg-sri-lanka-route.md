Acting on information that ganja was being smuggled through Thanjavur
district to Sri Lanka, a special police team checked vehicles on Medical College
Road near the outer ring road and seized 100 kg of ganja from a mini-goods
carrier on 14 October. [@1] The team handed the contraband and the vehicle to the
Medical College Police. [@1]

The arrested men were identified as Vijidharan of Alankudi in Pudukottai
district, Agastin of Villupuram, and Vignesh and Purushothaman of Vellore.
