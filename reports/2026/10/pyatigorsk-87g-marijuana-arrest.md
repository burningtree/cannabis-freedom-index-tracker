Police in Pyatigorsk detained a 22-year-old local man suspected of distributing
drugs. [@1] Drug-control officers stopped him on a city street after he tried to
flee when he saw them, throwing away a polymer bag. [@1] An examination showed
the bag held more than 87 grams of marijuana. [@1] He was taken to the police
station. [@2]

A search of the kitchen in his home found a plastic jar containing marijuana,
electronic scales and zip-lock bags. [@2] He told officers he sold the drug
through a messaging app, leaving it in hidden stash points known as zakladki.
[@1] He had done this for about a month, not only in Pyatigorsk but in other
settlements of the Caucasian Mineral Waters (KMV) resort area. [@3]

The investigative department of the Pyatigorsk police opened two criminal cases
for attempted illegal sale of narcotics and combined them into one proceeding.
[@2] The charge covers sale through the internet by a group acting in prior
collusion in a significant amount. [@2] Police said they were still establishing
the circumstances and possible accomplices. [@3]
