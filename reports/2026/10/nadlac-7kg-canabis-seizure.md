Border police at Nădlac, working with organised-crime officers in Arad under DIICOT
prosecutors, caught a 21-year-old passenger on an international coach from Spain on the
evening of 12 October 2026. [@1,2] He was carrying more than seven kilograms of cannabis
meant for sale, the Romanian Border Police said. [@1]

On 13 October DIICOT-Arad ordered his detention for international and domestic trafficking
in risk drugs and asked the Arad rights court to hold him in preventive arrest for 30 days.
[@1,2] Mediafax reported the same route and quantity and said the man had been taken into
custody on Tuesday after the Monday night stop. [@2]

Both releases stressed that suspects remain presumed innocent with full procedural guarantees.
