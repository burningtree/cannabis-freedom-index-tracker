The case began with a report from residents of avenue de la Libération in Le
Coteau, a town near Roanne, about drug dealing. [@1] Officers of the anti-crime
squad, the Bac, at the Roanne police station kept watch after the report, and at
about 6 pm on Tuesday 27 October 2026 arrested a 24-year-old man from Le Coteau
in the middle of a drug transaction. [@1]

A search of his home after the arrest found about 40 g of cannabis resin and
herbal cannabis. [@1] He was already known to the courts for similar offences
and was brought before a prosecutor under the CRPC, France's guilty-plea
procedure (comparution sur reconnaissance préalable de culpabilité). [@1]

He was convicted of drug trafficking and sentenced to 10 months in prison,
suspended with probation, and ordered to complete community service (travail
d'intérêt général). [@1] The CRPC is a procedure in which a defendant admits the
offence and a judge approves the sentence proposed by the prosecutor. [@1]
