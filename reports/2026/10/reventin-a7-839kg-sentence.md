Shortly after midnight on the night of 19 to 20 October 2026, customs officers
at the Reventin-Vaugris toll on motorway A7 in Isère checked a southbound
vehicle and found Moroccan resin packed in suitcases; the load weighed 837 kg.
[@2] On instructions from the Vienne prosecutor the 23-year-old driver was
handed to the Lyon branch of the Ofast anti-narcotics office. [@2]

On Friday 23 October 2026 the Vienne criminal court sentenced Amra Cerbah, 23,
from Neuilly-sur-Marne, to ten years in prison. [@1,3] He told the court he had
agreed under family pressure to run ten go-fast trips from Spain to repay debts
left when his brother Mohand-Achour Cerbah was stopped near Clermont-Ferrand on
9 April 2026 with more than a tonne of cannabis and later received five years.
[@1,3] Customs valued the resin in his car at about €2.52 million; sentencing
reports describe 839 kg. [@1,3]

The October articles do not record further co-defendants or fines beyond the
prison term. [@1,3]
