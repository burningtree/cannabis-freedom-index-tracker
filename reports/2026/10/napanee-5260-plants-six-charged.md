Ontario Provincial Police dismantled an illegal cannabis production site on County Road 12 in
Greater Napanee after opening an investigation in September 2026. [@1,2] On Thursday 24
September, officers of the Provincial Joint Forces Cannabis Enforcement Team with several OPP
units executed a search warrant at the property. [@1,2] Four people were arrested at the site
while two others were caught after fleeing on foot. [@1,2]

Police seized 5,260 cannabis plants valued at approximately $7.9 million, along with $1,900 in
Canadian currency and a 2015 Lexus RX350 worth about $18,000. [@1,2] Investigators also found
fire, electrical and property safety concerns; hydro service was disconnected, fire officials
issued fines and municipal officers issued compliance orders. [@1,2]

The six accused, aged 38 to 72 and mostly from Scarborough and Markham, each face charges of
cultivating illicit cannabis and possessing cannabis for distribution under the Cannabis Act.
[@1,2] They are scheduled to appear in the Ontario Court of Justice in Napanee on 15 December.
[@1,2]
