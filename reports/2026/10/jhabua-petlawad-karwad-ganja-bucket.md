Petlawad police arrested a man in the middle of the market with a plastic bucket
of ganja that he was allegedly carrying around to sell. [@1] The police named
him as Rakesh, son of Ramapati Pathak, and said he had been selling the drug
illegally from the bucket in Karwad village. [@1] Officers found the ganja
packed in bags inside the bucket, along with a few paper packets. [@1]

Police said a team cordoned off the area on the information and caught him. [@1]
They seized about 1 kg 280 g of ganja from him, which police valued at an
estimated 25,600 rupees. [@1] A case was registered against him under various
sections of the NDPS Act, India's main narcotics law. [@1]

The arrest took place in Jhabua district, in the state of Madhya Pradesh. [@1]
The NDPS Act is India's narcotics law, and it bans the possession and sale of
ganja, or cannabis, without a licence. [@1]
