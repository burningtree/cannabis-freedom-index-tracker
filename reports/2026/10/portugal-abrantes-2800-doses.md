GNR officers arrested a 50-year-old man and seized 2,800 doses of cannabis and nine plants at a house
in Vale das Mós, Abrantes, on 19 October 2026. [@1,2]

The arrest followed a month-long investigation by the criminal investigation unit of the Abrantes
territorial detachment; officers executed a search warrant at the home. [@1,2] They also seized a
heater, a vacuum-packing machine and a scale. [@1,2]

The man was made a formal suspect (arguido) and released subject to an identity-and-residence order;
the case was sent to the Abrantes court. [@1,2]
