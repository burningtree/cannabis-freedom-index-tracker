Sitamarhi police destroyed seized cannabis on 29 October 2026 under the
Nashamukt Bihar Abhiyan, the state's drug-free Bihar campaign. [@1]
Superintendent of Police Amit Ranjan ordered the formal destruction of illegal
drugs held at police stations across the district. [@1] The action followed a
court order and the prescribed standard operating procedure, and other senior
officers were present. [@1] Sitamarhi Police said the superintendent directed
that day's compliance with the standard operating procedure for drugs seized in
cases at various stations in the district. [@2]

Police figures put the destroyed amount at 3,656.953 kilograms of ganja and
33.528 kilograms of charas, or cannabis resin, together 3,690.481 kilograms.
[@1] The same weights were given in the district police statement posted that
afternoon. [@2] The consignments had been recovered in separate police cases and
were destroyed only after the legal process was finished, at stations in the
district, with safety standards observed. [@1]

The administration said the process would continue strictly so that the illegal
drug trade could be stopped. [@1] Police said the anti-drug campaign would be
intensified to keep the younger generation out of addiction, and that
destruction after raids and seizures was meant to break traffickers' morale.
[@1] After Bihar's liquor prohibition, police have also been taking continuous
action against other intoxicants. [@1]
