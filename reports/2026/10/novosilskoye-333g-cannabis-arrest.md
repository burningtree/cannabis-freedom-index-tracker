Police in Lipetsk Oblast's Terbunsky municipal okrug detained a 37-year-old
resident of Novosilskoye village over illegal drug storage, the Lipetsk regional
broadcaster Vesti Lipetsk reported. [@2] During a search of the man's home,
officers found and seized cannabis, also called marijuana, with a total weight
of 333.2 grams, the press service of the regional Interior Ministry said. [@2]
Lipetskiye Novosti, a Lipetsk regional news site, said the search took place
during a police check at the household of a man born in 1989. [@1] The site said
officers from the police's drug control group recorded illegal storage of a
plant-based narcotic in a large quantity. [@1]

The investigative department of the Russian Interior Ministry's Terbunsky
division opened a criminal case under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal
Code. [@1] Lipetskiye Novosti said the article's sanction provides for three to
ten years in prison. [@1] Vesti Lipetsk said the man faces up to ten years in
prison. [@2]
