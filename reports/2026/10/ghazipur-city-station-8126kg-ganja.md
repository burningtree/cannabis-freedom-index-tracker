Railway Protection Force (RPF) and Government Railway Police (GRP) officers
found 8.126 kg of ganja in an unclaimed trolley bag on the Amritsar Express at
Ghazipur City railway station on 28 October 2026. [@1] No passenger claimed the
bag when the officers questioned travellers in the coach. [@2] Police estimated
the ganja's value at Rs 4,06,300. [@3]

The RPF post at Ghazipur had been told by the divisional security control room
in Varanasi that a bag was lying under berth 27 of coach B-6 of train 15933.
[@2]

The GRP prepared a recovery memo, took the ganja into custody and began further
action. [@2] The recovery was made under 'Operation Narcos', according to
Hindustan. [@3]
