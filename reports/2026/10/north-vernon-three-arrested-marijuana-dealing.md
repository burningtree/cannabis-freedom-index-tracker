A narcotics investigation by the North Vernon Police Department ended in three
arrests and two further search warrants in Columbus on 28 October 2026. [@1]
Local police took three men into custody and recovered suspected cocaine,
marijuana and THC products, the Madison Courier reported. [@2] The search
warrants were executed on Franklin Street in Columbus by the Columbus Police
Department SWAT team and JNET. [@2]

Terry Winchester II and Jacob M. Kennedy were arrested on charges of dealing in
cocaine, dealing in marijuana and possession of a controlled substance. [@2]
Steven James Chandler was arrested on charges of possession of a controlled
substance and visiting a common nuisance. [@2] Additional charges were being
filed as a result of the search warrants. [@1]

The Madison Courier reported that the three men were arrested locally and that
the Columbus warrants followed. [@2] Task Force Commander Graham Heffelfinger
said a lot of officers had put time and effort into the operation, recognized
North Vernon officers Watts and Gibbons and thanked the Columbus SWAT team and
JNET. [@1] The Madison Courier noted that all parties are presumed innocent
unless and until proven guilty in court. [@2]
