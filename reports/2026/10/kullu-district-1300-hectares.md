Police in Kullu district, Himachal Pradesh, registered 13 first information
reports (FIRs), the initial record of a criminal case, against people involved
in illegal cannabis cultivation in September and October 2026. [@2] Five cases
were filed at Manikaran police station, two each at Patlikuhal, Sainj and Sadar
Kullu, and one each at Bhuntar and Brau. [@2]

The joint drive ran from 9 September to 24 October 2026, police said, and
destroyed illegal cannabis cultivation across nearly 1,300 hectares of the
district. [@1] Teams worked in forest beats and villages under Patlikuhal police
station, including Naggar, Fozal, Halan, Neri, Khakhnal, Soyal, Jana, Rumshu and
Sajla. [@1] Under Manikaran police station they covered the Kasol forest range
and the Parvati Valley around Grahan, Malana, Tosh, Kutla, Barshaini, Rasol,
Pulga and Manikaran. [@1]

The campaign was carried out jointly by the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB), the
Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN), the forest department, district police, the
special task force (STF) and the Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP). [@1] Police
said the aim was to eliminate illegal cultivation and cut the sources of illicit
drug production and supply. [@1] They said the campaign would continue. [@1]

Malana, in the Parvati Valley, is known for producing high-quality charas, a
cannabis resin. [@1] The district administration appealed to residents to
support a drug-free Kullu campaign. [@2]
