Officers of Paraguay's National Directorate of Tax Revenue (DNIT) found the
parcels during checks of express courier cargo at Silvio Pettirossi
International Airport, in the cargo depot of the national civil aviation
authority (DINAC), on 30 October 2026. [@2] One shipment from Miami held 6 kg of
suspected marijuana, hidden in packets inside two packs of paediatric wet wipes.
[@1] A second check at the depot known as Ticket 1 found four parcels with 46.5
kg of suspected ketamine, vacuum-sealed and concealed in boxes for printers.
[@2]

The four ketamine parcels were consigned to different recipients, and neither
account gave further details about them. [@1] Together the two finds amounted to
52.5 kg of suspected drugs, Diario HOY reported. [@1] The National Anti-Drug
Secretariat (SENAD), Paraguay's anti-drug agency, took charge of both
procedures, and officers drew up formal records of what was found. [@2]

The DNIT said it keeps checking goods that enter Paraguay through express
courier services, to detect irregularities and put them before the competent
authorities. [@2]
