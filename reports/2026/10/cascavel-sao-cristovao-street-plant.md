On the morning of Friday 30 October 2026, military police in Cascavel, Paraná,
seized one cannabis plant about 40 centimetres tall from a public street. [@1]
The plant was on Rua Francisco Beltrão in the São Cristóvão neighbourhood. [@2]
No one was named or taken into custody. [@1]

The team was sent after the communications centre received a report of a plant
resembling cannabis, known locally as maconha, on the public way. [@1] At the
scene, residents and passers-by told the officers where the plant was. [@2]
Officers judged that the plant had morphological features like cannabis and
seized it. [@2]

The plant was sent to the 1st clerk's office of the 6th Military Police
Battalion, the 6º BPM, for the usual steps. [@1] Officers drew up a termo
circunstanciado, a written police record of the occurrence rather than a named
arrest. [@2]
