In Edo State, NDLEA operatives intercepted a black Toyota Camry carrying 1,049 blocks of
compressed cannabis weighing 702.83 kilograms at Otuo in Owan East local government area on
5 October 2026. [@1,2] They arrested Monday Orji, 52, Monday Lawal, 40, and Precious Uduegele,
20, in connection with the load, The Telegraph Nigeria reported from the agency statement. [@2]

The Guardian Nigeria carried the same Otuo figures in a 12 October roundup of nationwide
October operations. [@1] The bulletin did not describe the truck’s destination. [@1,2]

Nigeria’s anti-drug law carries long sentences for trafficking at this scale; the October
statements reported no court dates for the three detainees. [@2]
