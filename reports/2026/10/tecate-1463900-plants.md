The Second Naval Zone of Mexico's navy said it found and burned 15 plots of suspected marijuana south of the municipality of Tecate, Baja California, during standing searches for drug-production sites. [@1,2] The plots covered more than 3.8 hectares and held about 800,000 plants, which were incinerated on the spot. [@1,2]

On four drying racks beside that crop the navy counted about 663,900 further plants and burned those too. [@1,2] The two counts come to 1,463,900 plants. [@1] Representative samples were turned over to the Fiscalía General de la República, the federal prosecutor, for the investigation file. [@1,2]

The statement names no one arrested and does not give the day the plants were burned. [@1,2] El Imparcial's account, published on 20 October 2026, follows the same navy figures. [@2]
