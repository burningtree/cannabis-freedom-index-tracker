On 7 October 2026, Ontario Provincial Police dismantled suspected illegal cannabis production
sites in Stone Mills Township and Madoc, seizing more than $1.8 million in cannabis and stolen
property across two separate raids. [@1] The investigations began in September and involved the
Provincial Joint Forces Cannabis Enforcement Team, with emergency response and asset forfeiture
units assisting the searches. [@1,2]

At a County Road 41 property in Stone Mills, officers seized 409 plants valued at about $245,000,
processed cannabis worth roughly $960,000 and a stolen vehicle valued at $80,000. [@1,2] Fire
officials issued fines and by-law officers compliance orders over fire, electrical and property
hazards, and hydro service was disconnected. [@1,2] At a Cooper Road property in Madoc the same
day, officers seized 663 plants valued at approximately $663,000. [@1]

No individuals were found at either property during the searches, and both investigations remain
ongoing. [@1,2]
