On Thursday 1 October 2026, Cairo’s criminal court of appeal sentenced a hotel
manager in central Cairo and a Brazilian guest to one year in prison each,
reducing an earlier three-year term. [@1] Investigators said the Brazilian woman
asked the manager to buy hashish so she could bake a “hashish cake” in the hotel
kitchen, mix it into chocolate, and serve it to a Chinese friend staying at the
same hotel as a prank. [@1,2,3]

The Chinese guest became severely ill after eating the cake and was taken to
hospital; urine tests found cannabis. [@1,2,3] Police arrested the manager and
the Brazilian woman, and the public prosecution sent them to the criminal court,
which convicted them on 25 May 2026 and jailed them for three years with hard
labour. [@2,3] The appeal court substituted a one-year sentence for each
defendant. [@1]
