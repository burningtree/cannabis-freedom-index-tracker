Civil police in Tocantins took four people to the station in São Valério da
Natividade on Thursday 29 October 2026 after serving seven home search warrants.
[@1] Of the four, police recorded two people caught in the act: one on suspicion
of drug trafficking and one for illegal possession of a firearm. [@1] The
circumstances of those cases, and whether other people were involved, were still
being investigated. [@1] AF Notícias reported that civil police did not detail
the legal situation of the other two people taken in. [@2]

During the searches, officers found about 10 kilograms of cannabis and 1
kilogram of a substance identified as cocaine base, which police counted as
about 11 kilograms of drugs. [@1] The raid was coordinated by the 95th Civil
Police station in São Valério da Natividade together with the 8th Specialized
Division for the Repression of Organized Crime (Deic) in Gurupi. [@1] The
warrants were issued by the courts after a police investigation. [@1] Delegate
João Paulo Sousa Ribeiro, head of the 95th station, said the seizure came from
investigative work that identified the targets and gathered the material needed
to carry out the court orders, and that inquiries would continue to clarify the
facts and identify possible participants. [@1]

The operation mobilised 28 civil police officers from units linked to the 7th
Regional Civil Police Station, plus members of the municipal civil guard of São
Valério da Natividade. [@1] Dogs from the kennel unit of the 4th Military Police
Battalion helped the searches, and delegate Jacson Ribas of the 88th Civil
Police station also took part. [@1] The action was part of Operação Protetor das
Divisas e Fronteiras, a programme coordinated by the Ministry of Justice and
Public Security through the National Secretariat of Public Security (Senasp)
against organised crime in border and state-boundary regions. [@1]
Investigations into the origin and destination of the drugs, and into other
possible participants, were still under way. [@2]
