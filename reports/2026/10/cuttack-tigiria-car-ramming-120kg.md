On a tip-off about ganja smuggling, a joint team of Athagad and Banki excise
officials tried to intercept a car, registration OD 02R 8811, coming loaded
with cannabis from the Tigiria area near Nuasadak Chhak under Tigiria police
station limits in Cuttack district. [@1] The car rammed the excise team's
vehicle and sped off toward Athagarh, with the joint team in pursuit. [@1]
The chase ended near Marugoth Chhak, where the car crashed into a road
barrier and stopped; under cover of darkness the traffickers abandoned
the four-wheeler on the spot and fled toward the forest. [@1] Inspection of
the car turned up 120 kg of ganja, estimated worth Rs 12 lakh, which
officials seized along with the vehicle. [@1] The bylined 11 October account
presents the smugglers' flight as reported; it reports no arrests and names
no injuries from the ramming. [@1] The article spells the district "Kataka"
throughout. [@1]
