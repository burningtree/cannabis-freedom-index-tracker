Nine people, men and women aged 22 to 32, were placed under formal investigation
(mis en examen) at Nanterre, in the Hauts-de-Seine, between 16 and 22 October
2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking, criminal association and money
laundering. [@1] Eight of them were remanded in custody and one woman was
released under judicial supervision. [@1]

The nine are suspected of running a cannabis herb and resin trade in northern
Hauts-de-Seine, the Val-d’Oise and the Hérault, the southern department around
Montpellier. [@1] Le Parisien calls the arrangement atypical: the cannabis was
produced and processed on a farm in the Hérault. [@1] The factory stood a few
kilometres from Montpellier. [@1] The site was a CBD — cannabidiol — factory
that in fact supplied “real” cannabis to dealers in the Val-d’Oise. [@1]

Hidden in the south-east, it served as a resupply point for go-fast convoys from
the Paris region, the fast road runs used to move drugs between cities. [@1] The
charges followed the dismantling of a go-fast network linking the Hérault to the
Île-de-France, the Paris region. [@1]
