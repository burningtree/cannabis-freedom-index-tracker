Authorities cleared another 331 acres of illegally cultivated cannabis in
Killa Abdullah district on 23 October under the Anti-Cannabis Campaign 2026,
bringing the total cleared since the drive began to 992 acres. [@1] The
operation covered Gulistan and Dobandi through the district administration,
the Anti-Narcotics Force, the Excise and Taxation Department, the Frontier
Corps, police and other agencies. [@1]

Joint teams destroyed cannabis over 285 acres in the Abdul Rehman Zai and
Qandeel Saigai areas of Gulistan and 46 acres in Dobandi subdivision. [@1] The
district administration supervised the operation while the Gulistan assistant
commissioner coordinated the field teams. [@1] Officials said the departments
work through a joint enforcement mechanism to find and eliminate illegal
cultivation, and that operations across the district would continue. [@1] The
reports name no arrests. [@1]
