The Special Judge-II court acquitted Baldev and Suman in the Gutkar case, where
police said they found 1.111 kg of charas in a car. [@1] The court based its
decision on contradictions between the statements of police officers and
independent witnesses. [@1] It found that an independent witness, Gopal, did not
confirm the recovery. [@1] Another witness's statement and the police testimony
also differed over the time and the action, the court found. [@1] It held that
the prosecution had failed to prove the charges beyond reasonable doubt and
acquitted both men. [@1]

According to the prosecution, police searched a car standing near the Hanuman
temple in Gutkar on the night of 25 February 2023. [@1] Police said the charas
was found in a pocket on the driver's seat cover. [@1] Balh police said at the
time that they had stopped an Alto car at a checkpoint in Gutkar and recovered 1
kg 111 g of charas from the two men inside. [@2] The car was coming from
Nerchowk towards Mandi. [@2] Police arrested both occupants. [@2] A case was
registered against both under sections 20 and 29 of the NDPS Act. [@1]

Mandi superintendent of police Shalini Agnihotri said the men would be produced
in court and police remand sought. [@2] She said police would investigate where
the charas came from and where it was to be delivered. [@2]
