Padangsidimpuan police chief AKBP Noval N.G. Desky said officers seized 36.23
kg of ganja and 502.47 grams of meth, along with 33 meth pipes and seven empty
clip bags, through case disclosures and an Operasi Sarang Narkoba sweep of 11
sites between 9 and 22 October. [@1]

Four disclosed cases involving meth and ganja yielded 499.87 grams of meth and
32.73 kg of ganja, with four suspects held. [@1] The 11-site operation added
2.6 grams of meth and about 3.5 kg of ganja and saw seven more men held,
initially classed as three users, three alleged dealers and one alleged
courier, pending the investigation. [@1] Urine screening across the activities
found 12 drug-positive people, nine men and three women. [@1] The city
government backed the drive, and police appealed for public tip-offs. [@1]
