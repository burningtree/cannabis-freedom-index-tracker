Aberdeen police pulled over Jose Estrada's vehicle on 16 October 2026 for a
moving violation and expired registration and inspection. [@1] Estrada, of
Rowland, North Carolina, was arrested and taken before a Moore County
magistrate. [@1] He was charged with misdemeanor possession of marijuana and
misdemeanor possession of marijuana paraphernalia. [@1] Officers found marijuana
in two locations inside the car. [@1]

Police Chief Brian Chavis said there was an obvious odor of marijuana coming
from the vehicle and that Estrada confirmed he had marijuana in it. [@1] After
officers removed Estrada from the driver's seat, they found a handgun concealed
in the compartment of the driver's side door. [@1] A check of the gun's serial
number showed it had been reported stolen. [@1] Estrada was also charged with
felony possession of a stolen firearm and misdemeanor carrying a concealed
weapon. [@1] Officers had been told he had an outstanding order for arrest out
of Robeson County for assault with a deadly weapon. [@1]

Estrada received a $2,000 secured bond on the new charges and a $500 secured
bond on the outstanding Robeson County charges. [@1]
