On 8 October 2026 special agents from Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (SENAD)
regional office in Bella Vista Norte expanded operations in Amambay department, entering
the forest at Estancia La Niña. [@1,2]

Diario HOY said they found a protection and storage hub and then a large marijuana
production camp, eradicated nine hectares of crops, burned the precarious installations
and eliminated about 27 tonnes of marijuana. [@1] Nuestra Realidad Noticias gave the same
hectare and tonnage figures and said prosecutor Rossana Coronel coordinated the
intervention from the start. [@2]

Neither account reports an arrest. [@1,2] The photograph published with Diario HOY's
story shows SENAD personnel during the La Niña operation. [@1]

The same rural property had been hit in late September 2026 when other SENAD teams cleared
16 hectares; that earlier phase is a separate event. [@1]
