Three men were charged after Ontario Provincial Police searched an address in
Midland, Ontario. [@1] Officers from the Southern Georgian Bay and Huronia West
community street crime units, with the Central Region repeat offender parole
enforcement team, went there on Tuesday 27 October 2026 to arrest a person
wanted on an outstanding warrant. [@2] During the search they found a loaded
firearm, about 935 grams of suspected cocaine, 489.7 grams of suspected
methamphetamine, 63 grams of cannabis concentrate known as shatter, and 31.2
grams of individually packaged tobacco. [@2] The OPP estimated the street value
of the drugs at $100,190. [@2]

Alexander Youmans, 22, of Henvey Inlet First Nation, was charged with firearm
and drug offences. [@2] Matthew Bowman, 42, of Midland, faced firearm and
drug-trafficking charges. [@2] Brody Leclair, 27, of no fixed address, was
charged with firearm and drug offences. [@2] He was also charged with possessing
cannabis with the intention to sell or distribute. [@2] Leclair remained in
custody and was due before the Ontario Court of Justice in Barrie on 4 November
2026. [@2]

The OPP said it remained committed to disrupting the distribution of illegal
drugs and enhancing community safety. [@2]
