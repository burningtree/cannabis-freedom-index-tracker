Military police arrested a man for drug trafficking on the afternoon of Friday
23 October 2026 at kilometre 252 of highway SP-350, in the rural zone of Itobi,
São Paulo. [@1] Officers had been tipped by the municipal civil guard of São
José do Rio Pardo that a suspect was buying drugs in large quantity, and they
located the car he was driving. [@1] Portal Rio Pardo, which placed the stop on
the night of the same Friday, said the car was a white Volkswagen Gol and that
the driver was caught in the act on suspicion of trafficking. [@2]

While officers followed him, he threw a bag that they later found, containing
nine wraps of cannabis. [@1] A personal search also turned up eight microtubes
of cocaine. [@1] In all, police seized 36 grams of cannabis, eight grams of
cocaine, a mobile phone and the car. [@1] The photo caption on the Gazeta report
put the seized drugs at 45 grams altogether. [@1]

He was taken to the police station in Casa Branca, where the case was recorded
as drug trafficking, and he remained detained at the disposal of the courts.
[@1] Duty delegate Guilherme Souza Gomes Filho ratified the in-flagrante arrest
for drug trafficking and ordered the car and phone held for forensic
examination. [@2] Before that, the man was taken to the municipal polyclinic in
Itobi for a body examination, which found no injuries, and he was then held in
the local public jail. [@2]
