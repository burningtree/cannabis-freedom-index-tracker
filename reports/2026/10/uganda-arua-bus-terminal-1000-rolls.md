Police in Arua City arrested Godfrey Eguma at Link Bus Terminal on the morning of 22 October 2026
with more than 1,000 rolls of suspected marijuana, the West Nile regional police spokesperson
said. [@1,2] He was intercepted at about 8:30 am during a routine check of passengers' luggage as he
prepared to travel to Masindi district. [@1]

Eguma allegedly told officers the rolls were intended for sale to workers at the Kinyara sugar
plantation in Masindi. [@1,2] He was detained at the Arua Central Business Division police station
and is expected to be charged under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (Control)
Act. [@1]

Police said they were still looking for the source and supplier of the drugs. [@1,2]
