Kalmar District Court sentenced a 40-year-old man from Torsås municipality to two years in prison for
gross drug offences and drink-driving, SVT reported on 22 October 2026. [@1,2]

Police found 53 cannabis plants, 38 of them in the garden, and equipment for growing and processing
cannabis in his home. [@1,2] The charges covered more than 10 kg of cannabis, including growing
plants, harvested material and plant parts. [@1,2]

The court rejected his claim that the crop was only for personal use, pointing to the size of the
operation and sealed packages labelled with strain, weight and THC content. [@1,2] The growing
equipment, which the man said was worth about 250,000 kronor, was forfeited. [@1,2]
