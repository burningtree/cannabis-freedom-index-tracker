The Jordan Customs Department patrols foiled an attempt to smuggle narcotics
early Friday, October 9, 2026, seizing 296 slabs of hashish and six kilograms
of marijuana. [@1][@2]

The operation was carried out by the department's Anti-Smuggling Directorate
patrols within their area of jurisdiction, as part of ongoing efforts to
combat smuggling and prevent narcotics from entering the Kingdom, according to
a Customs statement issued Friday. [@1][@2]

The department said the operation reflected an integrated approach and
effective cooperation with various security agencies, and reaffirmed its
commitment to protecting Jordanian society and the national economy through
close coordination with the Public Security Directorate's Anti-Narcotics
Department. [@1][@2]
