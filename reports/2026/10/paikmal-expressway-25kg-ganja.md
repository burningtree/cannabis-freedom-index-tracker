Paikmal police arrested three accused after identifying and seizing 25 kilograms
of ganja during vehicle checks carried out in Bargarh district, Odisha. [@1]
Ganja is the name used locally for cannabis. [@1] Police said that another
accused was absconding and had not been detained. [@1] The three people were
arrested in the same incident in which the cannabis was seized. [@1]

The ganja came to light during vehicle checks on the Biju Expressway. [@1] As
part of those checks, officers inspected a car with a Chhattisgarh registration
number. [@1] Searching that car on the expressway is what brought the ganja to
light. [@1] Paikmal police were the officers who identified the 25 kilograms and
took possession of it. [@1]

Police seized three mobile phones from the accused. [@1] They also seized the
car that officers had inspected on the Biju Expressway. [@1] After seizing the
phones and the car, police began an investigation. [@1] Police said the ganja
they had identified and seized in the district checks weighed 25 kilograms. [@1]
