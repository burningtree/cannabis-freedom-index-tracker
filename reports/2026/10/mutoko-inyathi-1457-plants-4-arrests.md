The Zimbabwe Republic Police arrested four suspects in separate cases of
alleged unlawful dagga cultivation in Mutoko and Inyathi, following the
recovery of hundreds of cannabis plants. [@1]

The first arrests were made on October 16, 2026, after officers in Makosa
acted on a tip-off and arrested Edmore Muhomba, 55, and Michael Chipuriro,
35, at Mutize Village in Mutoko; police recovered 1,457 dagga plants,
averaging 1.3 metres in height, from the suspects' garden. [@1]

In a separate incident on the same day, police in Inyathi arrested Tendayi
Muleya, 32, and Supayi Muleya, 22, for alleged unlawful cultivation of dagga
at a mine compound, recovering several cannabis plants from the suspects'
garden. [@1] The arrests form part of the police's ongoing efforts to combat
cultivation and distribution of illegal drugs. [@1]
