A team deployed from the Armed Police Force's Nocha Border Outpost recovered
the load at Ramchowk in Jahada Rural Municipality-4 at around 11 PM on
Saturday night, after the pickup van's suspicious India-bound route drew
attention. [@1,2] When the force tried to stop the van, numbered Pra 1-01-00
Cha 7422 and bound for India, the driver abandoned it and fled. [@1,2]

Searching the abandoned vehicle, officers found 19 packets of marijuana
hidden between chayote and cabbage in the vegetable load. [@1,2] Superintendent
Gehendra Bahadur Bhandari, chief of the Armed Police Force in Morang,
confirmed the total weight of 570 kg in the Biratnagar-dateline account
published 11 October. [@1,2] Both the marijuana and the pickup were handed to
Nepal Police for investigation and action, and the hunt for the driver
continues. [@1,2] The published photograph shows the impounded pickup at the
APF post flanked by two personnel and stacks of black-wrapped packets. [@2]
