On Wednesday 7 October 2026 authorities held a public destruction ceremony at Biakato’s local
stadium in Mambasa territory, Ituri province. [@1,2] Security services burned at least five sacks
of hemp recovered from several commercial houses, together with strongly alcoholic drinks and
expired products that the reports treated as prohibited or unsafe. [@1,2]

Deputy territorial administrator Colonel Tshisimbi Maxime, on mission in the area, said the
operation aimed to discourage hemp sales and other banned or expired goods, Dépêche.cd and Le
Bastion.cd reported. [@1,2]

Civil-society coordinators Peresi Mamboro of the Nouvelle Société Civile Congolaise and Amitié
Bwira of the Assistance nationale pour les droits de l’homme welcomed the burn, while residents
who watched called for further raids to identify sellers. [@1,2]
