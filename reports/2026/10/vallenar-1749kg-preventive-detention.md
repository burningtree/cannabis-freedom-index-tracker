Atacama’s specialised anti-drug prosecutors formalised a Bolivian truck driver on
14 October 2026 for illicit drug trafficking after Carabineros found 1,749 kilograms
of marijuana packed in 85 sacks inside a truck on Route 5 Norte south of Vallenar.
[@1,2] During a public hearing the court granted the Fiscalía’s request for preventive
detention for seven months, finding his release would endanger public safety. [@1]

Investigators said the driver abandoned the vehicle when he noticed police and was
detained hours later near the scene. [@1] Other cars had acted as “punta de lanza”
scouts ahead of the lorry. [@1] The truck carried a Starlink antenna, handheld radio
and tracker that prosecutors said linked him to the group. [@1]

Zone commander General Julio Marabolí told G5noticias that territorial police began
the stop and OS7 Atacama took over under prosecutor orders, removing more than 1.7
tonnes from circulation. [@2] Regional prosecutor Juan Andrés Shertzer said such loads
pointed to organised trafficking that could supply domestic or export markets. [@2]
