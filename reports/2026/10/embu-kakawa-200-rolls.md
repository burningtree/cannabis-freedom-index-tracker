Police officers in Embu County arrested a person of interest after recovering
200 rolls of suspected bhang during a targeted anti-narcotics operation at
Kakawa Market. [@1]

The National Police Service said Makima Police Station officers conducted the
operation on Sunday, October 11, 2026, to curb the sale and distribution of
narcotics; the suspect was found in possession of the 200 rolls of suspected
cannabis sativa. [@1]

The suspect was held at Makima Police Station pending arraignment, with the
rolls kept as exhibits, as the NPS urged the public to keep sharing timely
information. [@1]
