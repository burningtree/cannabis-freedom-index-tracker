On Wednesday 7 October 2026, around 14:00, officers of Santa Cruz’s vehicle-theft prevention
directorate checked a vehicle on the La Angostura–Samaipata road, near the grotto past La
Angostura. [@2] The vehicle got past the first control. [@3] After a chase it was found with the
engine running, the key in the ignition and nobody inside. [@2,3] El Día described it as a grey
SUV. [@2]

Inside were seven cardboard boxes and two jute sacks holding 346 packages. [@1,2] A field test was
positive for marijuana, prosecutor Raúl Torrez of La Guardia municipality told reporters. [@1,2]
Departmental police commander Colonel Edson Fernández gave the same account of the chase and the
preliminary identification. [@3] The packages were taken to the special force against drug
trafficking. [@1,2] No one had been arrested. [@1,2] The weight, origin and destination were still
to be established. [@2,3]
