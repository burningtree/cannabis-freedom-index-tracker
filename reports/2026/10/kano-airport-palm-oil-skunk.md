During the outward clearance of an Ethiopian Airlines flight to Jeddah via Addis Ababa
on Friday 16 October, NDLEA operatives at Mallam Aminu Kano International
Airport stopped female passenger Fatimah Ahmad and found 511.75 grams of skunk
cannabis in parcels hidden in a gallon of palm oil in her luggage. [@1,2]

Ahmad told investigators the gallon had been given to her by her cousin, Abubakar
Yahaya, who officers promptly arrested at the airport car park. [@1,2]
