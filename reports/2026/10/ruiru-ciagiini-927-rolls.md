Police seized 927 rolls of bhang in separate anti-narcotics operations in
Kiambu and Kirinyaga counties, with one suspect arrested in connection with
drug trafficking. [@1] [@2]

In Kiambu County, officers from Ruiru Police Station raided the Wataalam area
following an intelligence-led operation, recovering 596 rolls of bhang. [@1]
[@2] The drugs were secured and booked as exhibits at Ruiru Police Station as
officers pursued other individuals suspected of being connected to the
distribution network. [@1]

In Kirinyaga County, officers from Ciagi-ini Police Station, working with
National Government Administration Officers, intercepted a person of interest
in Ciagi-ini village found trafficking 331 rolls of bhang and in possession
of crude weapons. [@1] [@2] The suspect was arrested and detained at
Ciagi-ini Police Station pending arraignment. [@1]

The National Police Service said the operations formed part of its ongoing
campaign against narcotic drugs and criminal distribution networks, urging
residents to share information on suspected drug activity. [@1]
