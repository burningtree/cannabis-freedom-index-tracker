During Operation Forefront on Wednesday 14 October 2026, West Mercia Police
forced entry to a disused factory off Birmingham Road on a Kidderminster
industrial estate and found a cannabis cultivation spread across several rooms
with hydroponics, chemicals and extractor fans. [@1,2] The force estimated the
operation at about £1.2 million and said the plants would be seized and
destroyed. [@1,2]

Two men, aged 44 and 32, were arrested on suspicion of cultivating cannabis and
held while enquiries continued. [@1,2] Detective Sergeant Kirsty Hill said the
setup was large and sophisticated and that cannabis production can feed organised
crime including modern slavery. [@1,2] The opened releases do not count plants or
give weights. [@1,2]
