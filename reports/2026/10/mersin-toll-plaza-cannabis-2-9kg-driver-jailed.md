A.R.A., 48, was at the wheel when gendarmerie officers stopped his car at the
Mersin Gişeleri toll booths, the city's motorway toll plaza, on 29 October 2026.
[@2] Officers acted on information that the car, plate 44 ABY 169, was carrying
drugs from Malatya to Mersin. [@2] A search of the car found 2 kg 900 g of kubar
esrar, a cannabis preparation. [@2] A.R.A. was detained after the search. [@1]

Proceedings were started over the seized cannabis. [@2] A.R.A. was processed
under Article 188 of the Turkish Penal Code, which covers drug manufacture and
trafficking. [@2] He was brought before the judicial authorities, where a court
ordered him held in pre-trial detention — tutuklama, the order that keeps a
suspect in custody pending trial. [@2] He was then sent to Mersin E Type Closed
Prison. [@2]

The operation involved the highway gendarmerie company and the provincial
anti-narcotics branch. [@2] Provincial gendarmerie teams put the car under
surveillance as it drove from Malatya towards Mersin. [@1]
