The Umbutfo Eswatini Defence Force intercepted more than 430 kilogrammes of dagga worth an
estimated E378,965 in three incidents along the kingdom's border areas on 26 October
2026, UEDF Public Affairs Officer Major Sandile M. Gwebu announced on 28 October. [@1]

The largest seizure came at a checkpoint at Machobeni in the Shiselweni Region at about
9pm, where the driver of a white Ford Focus sedan abandoned the vehicle and fled on foot;
a search uncovered 10 bags weighing 292 kilogrammes worth E241,000, and the South
African-registered car and consignment were handed over. [@1] At about 9:45pm at Mpuluzi
in the Hhohho Region, soldiers stopped a red Toyota Tazz sedan with 13 bags and five
rolls weighing 135.04 kilogrammes valued at E135,040; Siyabonga Dlamini (24) and Mpendulo
Mlotjwa (23), both of Silele in Shiselweni, were arrested with the vehicle and drugs. [@1]
