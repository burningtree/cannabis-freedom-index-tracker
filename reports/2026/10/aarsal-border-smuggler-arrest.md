On 9 October 2026, as part of ongoing surveillance of the Lebanese-Syrian border and illegal
crossings used for smuggling, an armed clash occurred on the outskirts of Aarsal between
Lebanese Army personnel and Lebanese citizen A.B., who is involved in smuggling activities;
no injuries were reported. [@1]

The clash took place while the individual was attempting to smuggle drugs from Lebanese
territory into Syrian territory. [@1] He was arrested, and approximately 60 kg of hashish,
35 kg of Captagon, and 22 kg of marijuana were seized in his possession, along with a
handgun and a quantity of ammunition. [@1]

The seized items were handed over to the relevant authorities, and an investigation was
launched with the arrestee under the supervision of the competent judicial authorities. [@1]
