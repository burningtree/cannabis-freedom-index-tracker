At 4:45 on Wednesday 14 October 2026, officers of the GRAD Spécial, the special anti-drug squad, entered the village of Orbaf, in the Dabou area, under an operation called Épervier Spécial. [@1,2] A reconnaissance had already pointed them to a house suspected of holding drugs. [@1,2]

At 5:00 they searched the house in the presence of one occupant. [@1,2] They found 80 blocks of cannabis and 14 balloons of a cannabis variety they called OG Kush. [@1,2] Two people who were in the house were arrested. [@1,2]

As the officers prepared to leave with the two and the drugs, a group of young men from the village tried to stop them. [@1,2] Some had sticks and stones, and some had blades. [@1,2] The police got out of Orbaf with the suspects and the seizure. [@1,2] Neither report records an injury or damage. [@1,2] The inquiry was continuing, to identify anyone else involved. [@1,2]
