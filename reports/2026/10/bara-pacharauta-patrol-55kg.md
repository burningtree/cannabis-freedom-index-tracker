Police arrested 45-year-old Rajkishor Prasad Yadav, a resident of Ramnagar
Tole in Ward 3 of Pacharauta Municipality, with 55 kilogrammes of marijuana
in Pacharauta Municipality of Bara district. [@1] A patrol team from the Area
Police Office in Simraungadh noticed Yadav acting suspiciously on a road in
Ward 6 at around 10:45 PM on Saturday night, and a search by the team turned
up the 55 kg in his possession. [@1]

Following the seizure Yadav was taken into custody and transferred to the
Area Police Office in Simraungadh. [@1] Police say the investigation is
continuing under the Narcotic Drugs Control Act, but have disclosed nothing
about the origin or intended destination of the cannabis, and further
investigation is underway. [@1] The report, datelined Bara and bylined Pushpa Raj Khatiwada on
11 October, carries a reporter-credited photograph that the text-only page
copy does not expose for verification, so no image is listed. [@1]
