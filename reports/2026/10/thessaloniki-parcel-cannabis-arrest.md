Officers from the Subdirectorate for Combating Organised Crime of Northern
Greece arrested a 38-year-old Greek man in Thessaloniki on the morning of
Tuesday 27 October 2026, moments after he collected a parcel mailed from the
United States. [@3] The parcel had been flagged by Greece's drug enforcement
coordination body. [@1] The Σ.Ο.Δ.Ν., Greece's coordinating body for drug
enforcement, assigned the case to the drug enforcement department with a
prosecutor's approval. [@2] Officers tracked it and arrested him as soon as he
picked it up. [@1] Police did not release his name. [@1]

The parcel contained 2,090 grams (4.6 pounds) of hydroponically grown cannabis,
known in Greece as skunk, split into four plastic packages. [@1] A search of his
home and possession found another 819.26 grams of cannabis, 115.61 grams of
cocaine in two packages, eight pills, a grinder with cannabis residue, three
scales, 470 euros, two mobile phones and handwritten notes. [@3] In total,
police seized about 2.9 kilograms (6.4 pounds) of cannabis. [@1]

Police said the man had been in trouble with the authorities before for similar
offences. [@1] He was to be taken before a prosecutor. [@2]
