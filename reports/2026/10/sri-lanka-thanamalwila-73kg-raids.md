Acting on information, Police Special Task Force officers from the Kataragama
camp carried out two raids in the Gemunupura area of Thanamalwila on the evening of
Wednesday 14 October. [@1,2] They arrested one suspect with 37 kg and 860 grams of
dried cannabis and a second with 35 kg and 210 grams, 73.07 kg in total.
[@1,2]

The two men, aged 39 and 56 and both from Kuda Oya, were handed with the
drugs to Thanamalwila Police Station, which is investigating further. [@1,2]
