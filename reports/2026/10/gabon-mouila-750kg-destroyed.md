The prosecutor's office of the Mouila court of first instance recently
destroyed 750 kg of cannabis, packed in fifteen 50 kg sacks, on judicial
orders. [@1] The drug had been seized in various operations and kept under
seal for several years, and was valued at about 10 million FCFA. [@1]

The operation, run with the judicial police units, was ordered to free the
court's evidence stores, prosecutor King Simba explained. [@1] The stock came
from seizures accumulated over several years, not only the past judicial year.
[@1] The destruction was presented as part of the fight against cannabis
trafficking in Ngounié province. [@1]
