On 22 October 2026, agents of Honduras's National Anti-Drug Police (DNPA) and the National Directorate for Prevention and Community Security (DNPSC) seized 131 pounds of suspected marijuana in Tocoa municipality, Colón department. [@1,2]

The find was made in the La Bolsa area after intelligence and surveillance work under Plan Operativo Escudo. [@1,2] Several rectangular packages of dry plant material were hidden inside a car that authorities said was used to move the drugs; both the cannabis and the vehicle were impounded. [@1,2]

Representatives of the Special Prosecutor's Office against Organized Crime (FESCCO), based in La Ceiba, Atlántida, coordinated the case to continue investigations and any criminal charges. [@1,2] Neither outlet reported arrests or named suspects. [@1,2]
