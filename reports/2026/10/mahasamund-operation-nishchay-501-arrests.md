Mahasamund police arrested 501 people in 177 ganja-trafficking cases between 1
January and 18 October 2026 under Operation Nishchay, a named police drive, and
said 70 more accused were still absconding. [@1] Of those held, police counted
289 as transporters. [@1] In what they called an end-to-end investigation,
officers arrested 145 accused in Odisha on the supply side and 67 on the onward
side. [@1] The largest groups by home state among those arrested were 162 from
Odisha, 138 from Chhattisgarh, 103 from Maharashtra, 73 from Madhya Pradesh and
54 from Uttar Pradesh. [@1]

Police said they seized about 10,000 kilograms of ganja, cannabis, which they
valued at about Rs 50 crore. [@1] They also seized vehicles used to move the
drug, valued at about Rs 10 crore, including buses, trucks, pick-ups, ambulances
and courier vans. [@1] Investigators said the consignments were brought from
Odisha and sent on to Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat,
Rajasthan and Haryana. [@1] Loads were hidden under fruit, in chambers inside
containers, in ambulances, in vegetable vehicles and among packers-and-movers
goods. [@1]

Mahasamund police said the campaign would continue in order to break the
network, and that they were preparing legal action against movable and immovable
property bought with the proceeds. [@1]
