In a large-scale raid on suspicion of organised weapons and drug trafficking, police searched
several premises in Dortmund and Waltrop on Tuesday 6 October 2026: four flats, a company and
two snack bars. [@1,2] In the Waltrop suspect's Dortmund company, investigators found a
professionally run cannabis plantation as well as several firearms, whose examination was still
ongoing, plus digital data carriers. [@1,2]

The operation targeted four suspects: men aged 29, 35 and 36 from Dortmund and a 43-year-old
from Waltrop, described by police as Turkish or German-Turkish nationals under investigation for
trading weapons, ammunition, cocaine and cannabis. [@1,2] The 43-year-old was provisionally
arrested and remanded in custody at the prosecutor's request; an existing warrant was also
executed against one of the Dortmund suspects, where officers found a live firearm with 52
rounds of ammunition. [@1,2]
