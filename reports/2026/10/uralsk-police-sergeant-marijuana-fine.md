A specialised administrative-offences court in Uralsk, the city also known as
Oral, heard a case against a police officer for using drugs without a medical
purpose. [@1] The officer, named in the court materials only as Z., held the
rank of police sergeant and served in a convoy unit of the regional police
department. [@1] The court found him guilty and imposed an administrative fine
of 173,000 tenge. [@1] The ruling had not entered into legal force. [@1]

The case began in preventive checks by the personnel inspectorate of the West
Kazakhstan police department, which sent him for a medical examination for
banned substances. [@1] The examination found narcotic substances of the
cannabinoid group. [@1] In a handwritten explanation in the administrative file
he wrote that he had used marijuana and admitted guilt, and he did not take a
second medical examination. [@1]

The press service of the West Kazakhstan regional court said the conduct was
classified under part 1 of article 440-1 of Kazakhstan's Code of Administrative
Offences. [@2] The court treated guilt as proved by the administrative-offence
protocol, the medical conclusion, the written explanation, police reports and
other materials in the file. [@2] NUR.KZ, citing the same court press service,
also reported the fine of 173,000 tenge and that the ruling had not entered into
force. [@2]
