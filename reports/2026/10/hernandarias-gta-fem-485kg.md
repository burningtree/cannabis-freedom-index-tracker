Agents of SENAD, Paraguay's national anti-drug secretariat, detained a father and his son in the
Caacupemí neighbourhood of Hernandarias, Alto Paraná department, and seized 48.5 kilograms of
cannabis of the "GTA FEM" variety — 46 packets hidden in a secret compartment of the vehicle the two
were travelling in, the agency said. [@1,2]

SENAD said the load was destined for the Brazilian market, where its value would reach an estimated
US$400,000. "GTA FEM" is grown from feminised seeds and reaches concentrations of up to 30% THC, the
main psychoactive compound of cannabis, making it a high-value "premium" product, according to the
agency and the officers who intervened. [@1,2]
