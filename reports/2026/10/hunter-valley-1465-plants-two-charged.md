Police seized another 925 cannabis plants from three homes in Muswellbrook
and Singleton Heights, taking the total under a Hunter drug investigation
to 1,465 plants with a potential street value of about $4 million; all
were destroyed. [@1] [@2]

On 29 September officers executed warrants in Cessnock and Denman, finding
280 plants and an illegal power connection at the Denman property and
arresting two men aged 36 and 56. [@1] [@2] The 1 October searches found
all three homes illegally connected to the grid. [@1] Both men face
commercial cultivation, enhanced indoor cultivation and drug-premises
charges; the younger also faces organising drug premises and electricity
diversion counts. [@1] Bail was refused on 30 September ahead of a
25 November Newcastle Local Court date. [@1] An earlier 21 July Cessnock
search had found 260 mature plants plus seedlings, dried cannabis and
hydroponic gear. [@1] [@2]
