Officers of the Policía Nacional Civil (PNC), El Salvador's national civil
police, arrested Salvador Antonio Ramón, aged 44, after finding portions of
cannabis on him. [@1] The institution said those portions were worth
approximately $400. [@1] The procedure took place on the night of Thursday 29
October 2026 in Ciudad Delgado, San Salvador Centro. [@1] Agents found him
during a preventive patrol in the colonia Santa Marta, a neighbourhood of that
district. [@1]

According to the police report, the substance was found and seized during the
intervention. [@1] The PNC identified the drug as cannabis, but did not state
its weight or how many portions were seized. [@1] The $400 is the economic
valuation published by the authorities, not cash taken from him. [@1]

The institution said Ramón would be referred for the offence of possession and
holding of drugs. [@1] The information released so far concerns the arrest, and
no judicial decision in the case had yet been announced. [@1]
