The presidency said that on 21 October 2026 National Police in Ciudad Bolívar, in the south of
Bogotá, seized more than 700 kilograms of marijuana that had come from Cauca in a van carried on a
tow truck. [@1] Officers arrested a woman and handed her to the prosecutor's office. [@1]
Authorities estimated the load at more than 150 million pesos for the criminal groups. [@1]

El Tiempo, citing the metropolitan police, put the weight at 705 kilograms and said the stop was
on the boundary between Bosa and Ciudad Bolívar, when the criminal-investigation section inspected
the truck. [@2] Packages smelled and looked like marijuana. [@2] Investigators said the load was
headed for the Carvajal neighbourhood in Kennedy, to be split and sold, and that the seizure
blocked about 70,000 doses. [@2] The woman who was driving was held for trafficking, manufacture
or carrying of drugs. [@2]

The city government's account, drawn from the district security secretariat, matches the tow-truck
van, the Cauca origin, the Kennedy destination, the arrest and the estimates of more than 150
million pesos and 70,000 doses, and says the find followed a citizen report. [@3]
