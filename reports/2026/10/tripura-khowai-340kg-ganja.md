A joint team of Assam Rifles and Customs officials intercepted a Ford EcoSport
in the Mungiakami area of Tripura’s Khowai district on Tuesday, 6 October 2026.
[@4] A search of the vehicle led to the recovery of 340 kg of ganja. [@1]
Officials valued the haul at around Rs 1.74 crore. [@1] The driver fled from the
spot, and no arrest was reported in connection with the seizure. [@2]

The vehicle allegedly used to transport the contraband was also seized. [@4] The
seized cannabis and the vehicle were handed to Customs for further investigation
and legal proceedings. [@1] The operation followed intelligence inputs and
sustained surveillance aimed at tracking suspected narcotics movement in the
area. [@1] Assam Rifles said operations against drug trafficking networks along
routes in the Northeast would continue to be intensified. [@4]

A police official said dry ganja produced in Tripura is not consumed locally and
is smuggled to various states, including Bihar and Uttar Pradesh, where it
fetches higher prices. [@3] The official said consignments are often seized from
trucks, small vehicles and even passenger trains during transport. [@2]
