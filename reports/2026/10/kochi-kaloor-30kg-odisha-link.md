Police seized more than 30 kg of ganja worth around Rs 50 lakh from a man
at Kaloor, Kochi, early on Thursday as part of the Operation Toofan
anti-narcotics drive. [@1] The arrested man is Abdul Hai Molla, 22, a native of
Murshidabad district in West Bengal. [@1]

According to police, Molla travelled from West Bengal to Odisha, bought the
ganja cheaply, and carried it to Kerala to supply wholesale dealers, riding to
Vijayawada and then taking another train to Ernakulam Junction. [@1] He was
caught heading toward Kaloor. [@1] Investigators believe he was a key link
in an Odisha-to-Kerala network, had been under surveillance, and are tracing
who ordered the consignment. [@1] A DANSAF team led by assistant commissioner Ananthalal
made the arrest and handed him and the drug to North police station, where a
case was registered. [@1]
