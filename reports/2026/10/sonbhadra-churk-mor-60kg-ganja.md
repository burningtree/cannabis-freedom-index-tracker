Police in Sonbhadra district, Uttar Pradesh, arrested a truck driver and seized
60.525 kg of ganja, or cannabis, hidden among sacks of cashew husks in his
Eicher canter. [@1] The driver, Rishi Kumar Yadav, is a resident of Kishanpur
village in the Kotwali Dehat police station area of Etah district. [@2] Officers
from the Anti Narcotics Task Force (ANTF) unit in Meerut, the Special Operations
Group (SOG) and Robertsganj police stopped the canter at a vehicle check near
Churk Mor late on Monday 26 October 2026. [@2] The check followed information
about a cannabis consignment, police said. [@3]

They found the cannabis packed in three plastic sacks among the sacks of cashew
husks. [@2] Police valued the consignment at about Rs 30.25 lakh. [@1] Yadav
told police he had collected it in the Bhubaneswar area of Odisha and was to
hand it to a person in Etah. [@2] A man from Odisha supplied the canter and
offered him Rs 40,000 on top of his fee, police said. [@3]

Police said Yadav had brought cannabis this way before. [@3] They said cases
including fraud and arms offences had been registered against him at Kotwali
Dehat in Etah. [@3] Police registered a first information report (FIR) at
Robertsganj police station under the NDPS Act, India's main drug law, and Amar
Ujala reported that Yadav was challaned, or sent to court, on Tuesday. [@3]
Police said they were trying to identify others involved in the smuggling
network. [@2]
