Police in charge of counter-narcotics destroyed more than 3,000 acres of
hashish farms in northern Afghanistan's Badakhshan province over two weeks,
the provincial police office said in a statement on 5 October 2026. [@1]

A total of 3,361 acres of hashish farms were destroyed across Badakhshan in
the anti-drug campaign, the statement said, warning that stern action would
be taken against anyone cultivating poppy, hashish or other illegal crops.
