The Guardia Civil Municipal, the municipal police of Soledad de Graciano
Sánchez, San Luis Potosí, reported seizing 28 bags of marijuana over four days.
[@1] It presented results for 26 to 29 October at the Mesa Estatal de
Coordinación para la Construcción de la Paz, the state security coordination
meeting. [@1]

The same tally listed 13 doses of methamphetamine, two replica firearms, three
prohibited weapons and four vehicles with theft reports. [@2] The session was
chaired on behalf of Governor Ricardo Gallardo Cardona by state government
secretary J. Guadalupe Torres Sánchez and state security and citizen protection
secretary Jesús Juárez Hernández. [@1]

The force acted on Mayor Juan Manuel Navarro Muñiz's instruction to coordinate
with other levels of government. [@1] Its director general, comisario Víctor
Aristarco Serna Piña, presented the results at the meeting. [@1] The force also
reported 201 requests for citizen assistance attended during the period. [@1] At
the close, representatives of the three levels of government reaffirmed their
commitment to preventive action and institutional coordination. [@1]
