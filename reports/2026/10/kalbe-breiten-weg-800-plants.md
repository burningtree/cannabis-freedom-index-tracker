On Friday afternoon 2 October 2026 patrol officers in Kalbe (Milde), Sachsen-Anhalt, smelled cannabis
coming from a vacant building on Breiten Weg. [@1,2] Inside they found an indoor plantation of about
800 plants of different heights and two Albanian nationals aged 32 and 56, who were provisionally
arrested. [@1,2]

Police from the Stendal inspection, the Altmarkkreis Salzwedel precinct and the state criminal
office dismantled the grow with help from the Technisches Hilfswerk. [@1,2] On Saturday 3 October,
on application by the Stendal prosecutors, a judge remanded both men in custody. [@2]

MDR noted that this was already the second large cannabis find in Kalbe in 2026 and that
investigators would check for a link; that earlier case is not part of this report. [@1]
