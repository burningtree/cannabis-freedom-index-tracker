On 28 October 2026 the Strumica police department filed a criminal complaint against a company in the village of Borisovo, in Novo Selo municipality, and against its manager. [@1] Police name the manager as V.K., from the village of Novo Selo and living in Strumica. [@1]

Officers of the Strumica department and officials of the health ministry's commission inspected the rooms where the company stored dried medical-cannabis flower. [@1] They found staff records and other required papers missing. [@1] They also found a shortage of 7,813.49 kilograms of dried flower, for which the company had no proper documents or cover. [@1,2]

24chasa, citing the interior ministry, said the complaint was filed after officials finished documenting actions taken in February 2026. [@2] The police notice does not say where the missing flower went, or that anyone was arrested. [@1]
