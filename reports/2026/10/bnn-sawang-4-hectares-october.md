On Wednesday 4 October 2026 the National Narcotics Agency (BNN) led another cannabis
eradication in Aceh province, acting director of narcotics Kombes Pol. Guntur Aryo Tejo
said on the agency's public gallery page. [@1]

Officers destroyed fields at two heights in North Aceh: one hectare at 223 metres above
sea level in Aluele Modek hamlet, Teupin Rusep village, and three hectares at 120 metres
in Cot Rawatu hamlet, Jurong village, Sawang district. [@1]

The published photographs show personnel in the plantation, uprooted stalks, loads being
carried through the crop and on-site burning on the same day. [@1] The page does not
report arrests or name cultivators. [@1]
