Two people were arrested after a counterfeit-money investigation led officers to
a residence where narcotics, additional counterfeit money, ammunition and a
rifle were seized, according to the Laredo Police Department. [@1] The suspect
was identified as 38-year-old Eduardo Cisneros, police said. [@1] A woman
identified as 36-year-old Veronica Gutierrez was also at the residence. [@1]

Officers responded Oct. 28 to separate incidents involving counterfeit money at
an auto center in the 2800 block of Jaime Zapata Memorial Highway and a gas
station in the 13600 block of FM 1472. [@1] He allegedly used counterfeit money
to purchase items at both locations. [@1] With assistance from the LPD Real Time
Crime Center and the Flock Safety System, officers located the suspect vehicle
parked outside a residence in the 3200 block of Boulanger Street. [@1] When
officers detained Cisneros, they noticed clear plastic wrapping containing a
white rocky substance on the front porch where he had initially been
encountered, according to police. [@1] LPD Texas Anti-Gang investigators took
over the case, and further investigation resulted in the seizure of small
amounts of various narcotics, additional counterfeit money, ammunition and a
rifle. [@1] A consensual search of Gutierrez’s purse led to the discovery of
several clear plastic baggies containing a black tar-like substance and several
pills, police said. [@1]

Jail records show Gutierrez was booked into Webb County Jail and charged with
three counts of possession of a controlled substance. [@1] She was released two
days later after being placed on three personal recognizance bonds totaling
$15,000. [@1] Cisneros was booked into Webb County Jail Oct. 29 and charged with
possession of a controlled substance, 4 to 200 grams; possession of marijuana,
more than 4 ounces but less than 5 pounds; possession of a controlled substance,
less than 1 gram; display of a fictitious license plate and two counts of theft
of property between $100 and $750. [@1] He remained in custody as of Friday
afternoon on bonds totaling $45,000 and an ATF hold. [@1]
