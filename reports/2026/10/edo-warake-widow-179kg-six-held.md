The NDLEA's Edo State Command arrested an 85-year-old grandmother with
179 kg of cannabis in Warake community, Owan East, during coordinated
raids that netted 407.39 kg of cannabis in total. [@1] [@2]

Grace Ehimeka, popularly known as Mama Grace, was picked up on 16 October
2026 at her residence with 15 bags of cannabis; she confessed to owning
the drugs and blamed hardship. [@1] [@2] Five others fell in the same
sweep: Ali Garba, 57, with 5.85 kg in Ivbiaro on 15 October; Habib
Braimoh, 45, with 100 g the same day; Musa Mubid, 35, with 2 kg of
cannabis plus seeds, loud and pharmaceutical drugs in Warake; Ayo
Aigberadion, 53, with 220.8 kg in Ewe/Irue on 16 October; and Hope Okoro,
35, with 237 g of loud in Auchi. [@2] Commander Mitchell Ofoyeju warned
that old age shields no trafficker from prosecution. [@1] [@2]
