The judicial police brigade of the Baraki administrative district, acting for
the Algiers wilaya security services, broke up a criminal network specialising
in drug trafficking, the wilaya security's communication unit announced. [@1]
In-depth field investigations first exposed how the network operated and
identified each member, letting detectives move in and arrest the five
suspects one after another rather than in a single sweep. [@1]

Searches of the suspects' homes then uncovered the drug concealed on the
premises: 14 kg 489 g of treated kif, described in the headline as nearly
15 kg. [@1] Officers also seized 5 million centimes identified as trafficking
proceeds and a passenger vehicle, the whole operation conducted under the
supervision of the territorially competent parquet. [@1] Once the legal
procedures were completed, all five suspects were presented before the
Republic's prosecutor at the El Harrach court to face charges. [@1] The case
was reported around 12 October 2026, the date stamped on the seizure
photograph published with the article. [@1]
