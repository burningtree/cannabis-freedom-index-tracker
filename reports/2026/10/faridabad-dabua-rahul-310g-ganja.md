Faridabad police arrested Rahul, who lives in Dabua, on 29 October 2026. [@2] A
police spokesperson said the Crime Branch team at AVTS Bhupani acted on secret
information and caught him at Laser Valley Park in Dabua Mandi. [@2] Officers
searched him and recovered 310 grams of ganja (cannabis). [@2] Alive News also
reported that the Bhupani crime-branch team detained Rahul near Dabua Mandi with
310 grams of ganja, which it said he was accused of selling. [@1]

The spokesperson said police registered a case against him under the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, India's main anti-drug law, at the
police station concerned and then acted as the rules require. [@2] Alive News
said the NDPS case had been filed and that further legal action had begun. [@1]
The spokesperson described the arrest as one of two separate ganja-selling cases
the crime branch handled that day. [@2] Faridabad police said their campaign
against people involved in the illegal drug trade was continuing. [@2] Alive
News described the same drive against drug trafficking as still under way. [@1]
