On secret information, Sepahijala District Police with the 81st battalion of
the BSF, TSR battalions, forest officials and other agencies raided the forest
areas of Dhanirampur from 8.30 a.m. to 1 p.m. on 16 October. [@1] They found
five cannabis plots holding about 7,000 mature plants across roughly five acres
and destroyed them on the spot. [@1]

A follow-on foot search with drone cameras covered about 1,000 acres in the
Bijoynagar area, where cannabis had grown last year; this time no fields were
found. [@1] Sonamura officer in charge Inspector Tapas Das briefed the media
and said such operations would continue the next day. [@1] The reports name no
arrests. [@1]
