In mid-October 2026, NDLEA operatives in Kaduna State raided Kayimi village in Igabi Local
Government Area and arrested three men with 496.5 kilograms of cannabis sativa. [@1,2]

Mohammed Sani, 48, Zaharadeen Shafiu, 29, and Yakubu Abdullahi, 20, were held in the
village raid, according to the Kaduna command and NDLEA headquarters. [@1,2] The state
commander's statement quoted in Dateline Nigeria praised the intelligence-led operation but
did not report further arrests at the farm. [@1]

The same Kaduna command separately seized tramadol tablets in Kaduna city in another case;
this report covers only the Kayimi cannabis seizure described in both sources. [@1,2]
