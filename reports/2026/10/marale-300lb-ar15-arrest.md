On 22 October 2026, agents of the Criminal Investigation Directorate (DPI), from departmental
criminal investigation unit UDIC-8, arrested José del Socorro Ávila Escobar in a mountainous area
of Marale municipality, Francisco Morazán department, after surveillance in the sector. [@1,2]

They seized roughly 300 pounds of suspected dry cannabis in about 15 to 18 sacks and an AR-15 assault
rifle without a serial number. [@1,2] Police said the drugs would be counted and tested to confirm
weight and substance. [@1,2]

Ávila Escobar was handed to prosecutors on preliminary aggravated drug-trafficking charges affecting
public health; the weapon would be investigated separately. [@1,2] Reports did not name any
accomplices. [@1,2]
