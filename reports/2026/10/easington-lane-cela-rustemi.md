In July 2026 Northumbria Police, tipped off about drug production, went to a property on High Street,
Easington Lane, Sunderland, found the back door unlocked, saw a bypassed electricity meter and roof
vents, and discovered 270 cannabis plants in growing areas with heating, lighting and
ventilation. [@1,2] Elton Rustemi was watering plants; Adriatik Cela was found hiding in a loft;
both were arrested. [@2]

At Newcastle Crown Court on 15 October 2026 both Albanian nationals, of no fixed address, pleaded
guilty to cultivating cannabis. [@1,2] Recorder Harry Vann jailed each for 12 months and said they
face deportation, which they had volunteered for. [@1] Cela, 49, said he had been in the UK about 20
days after arriving in a lorry and that his role was watering plants. [@2] Rustemi, 40, said fellow
Albanians had taken him to an already-running farm and left food; he had been there one to one and a
half months. [@2]
