The Hyderabad zonal unit of the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence had
developed specific intelligence about an Indian passenger flying into
Hyderabad from Bangkok, Thailand on Monday 12 October, suspected of carrying
high-grade hydroponic weed in his baggage. [@1,2] Acting on that tip, DRI
officers intercepted him after landing at Rajiv Gandhi International Airport
in Shamshabad and took up his check-in baggage for examination. [@1,2]

Inside the baggage was an electric geyser, an unusual item for air luggage;
when officers opened the appliance, they found 73 packets of a greenish
substance weighing 7.750 kg packed inside it. [@1,2] A narcotic drugs
detection kit test on the substance indicated ganja, and the load was
identified as high-grade hydroponic cannabis with an estimated illicit-market
value of about Rs 2.71 crore. [@1,2] The cannabis was seized, the passenger
was arrested under the provisions of the NDPS Act 1985, and the DRI said
further investigation is in progress. [@1,2]
