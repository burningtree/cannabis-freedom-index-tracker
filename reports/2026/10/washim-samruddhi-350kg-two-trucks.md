Washim police seized 350 kg of ganja from two trucks in two separate
operations on the Samruddhi Expressway, confiscating contraband worth about
two crore rupees as investigations began. [@1]

On information that ganja was being smuggled in a truck around Malegaon on
the expressway, a vehicle check found the contraband being transported in it;
police seized 150 kg of ganja from that truck. [@1] A second operation took
place around Karanja on the expressway: on information from Chhattisgarh's
Mahasamund district superintendent Prabhat Kumar that ganja was being
secretly carried inside an Eicher truck's cabin, the vehicle was checked and
district traffic branch police recovered 200 kg of ganja from it. [@1]

Police superintendent Nikesh Khatmode Patil said 350 kg had been seized in
the two anti-ganja operations carried out that morning: in the Malegaon
limits operation, patrolling police grew suspicious of a truck and stopped
it, and the driver abandoned the vehicle and fled; 150 kg was seized from
it. [@1] Police said exactly how many accused were involved would emerge
only after investigation, and a large smuggling racket may be exposed by the
major action. [@1]
