On 3 October 2026, the urban security unit of Dakar's central police station
said it had arrested two men and seized 18 kg of cannabis in Keur Massar, in
the Dakar region. [@1,2] The operation followed intelligence about a cannabis
trafficking network in the area. [@1,2]

Officers watched a house and saw the main suspect arrive, whom they believed was
there to collect drugs. [@1,2] They searched the home, arrested both men and
found the cannabis in bags in the living room. [@1,2] Under brief questioning,
the main suspect admitted owning the drugs; the other said he was only visiting.
[@1,2]
