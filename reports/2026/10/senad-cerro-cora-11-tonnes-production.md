On Wednesday 8 October 2026, agents from Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat
(SENAD) dismantled a cannabis production complex in Colonia Cumbre, in the Cerro Corá
district of Amambay department. [@1] [@2] The regional office in Pedro Juan Caballero
led the raid after intelligence pointed to processing sites in the forest. [@2]

The team found separate areas for cultivation, drying and storage. [@1] [@2] SENAD
said it destroyed three narco camps in the middle of the production zone. [@1]
[@2] Officers counted 2,100 kilograms of marijuana drying on wires and another 650
kilograms spread on tarpaulins. [@1] [@2] They also eradicated about three hectares
of cannabis still in an early growth stage; the agency estimated that crop could
have yielded about 9,000 kilograms if it had matured. [@1] [@2]

Taken together, SENAD reported more than eleven tonnes of marijuana eliminated in
different stages of production. [@1] [@2] HOY added that the load was probably
destined for Brazil because of the price cannabis fetches there. [@1] La Tribuna
said work in the area would continue under prosecutor Jorge Sosa. [@2]
