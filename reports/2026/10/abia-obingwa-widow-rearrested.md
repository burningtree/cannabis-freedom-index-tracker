An 83-year-old woman has been arrested again by the NDLEA for drug
offences, less than two years after she was convicted for the same crime.
[@1] [@2]

Operatives arrested Mrs Uloma Uchechi Sunday on 7 October 2026 at her home
in Ite-Uzo, Umuaguma, Obingwa LGA of Abia State, finding methamphetamine
and Colorado, a synthetic cannabinoid, tied to her waist with a wrapper on
intelligence that she runs a drug joint harbouring criminals. [@1] [@2]
She was first arrested on 17 May 2025 at the same residence with her
daughter Chisom, 32, with 50 g of methamphetamine, 522 g of skunk, 260 g
of fresh cannabis leaves and 12 g of tramadol; both pleaded guilty and were
sentenced to 12 months' imprisonment or a N200,000 fine each, paid the
fines and were freed. [@1] [@2]
