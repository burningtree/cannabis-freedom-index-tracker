Marjon Rexha, 40, of no fixed address, was sentenced to 26 months in prison at Northampton Crown Court on Monday 5 October 2026 after he pleaded guilty to producing a Class B drug. [@1,2] Recorder Cameron Crown ordered the term, the forfeiture and destruction of the drugs, a £228 surcharge, and deportation to Albania again. [@2]

Officers from the Kettering neighbourhood team had forced entry at a house on Rothwell Road on 21 May 2026 under a Misuse of Drugs Act warrant. [@1] Rexha was upstairs in the living quarters, the only person in the house. [@1] They found 221 cannabis plants at different stages of growth in five rooms and a garage. [@1] Custody checks showed the Albanian national had entered the UK in breach of a deportation order. [@1] The court heard he had been deported in July 2024 and had come back on a date that was not known. [@2] He was charged with production and with that breach, appeared at Northampton magistrates' court the next day, and was remanded until the crown court. [@1]
