On Monday 26 October 2026 the Northern Directorate of the Polícia Judiciária searched a warehouse
in Vila Nova de Famalicão and arrested a 38-year-old man. [@1,2] He has no criminal record and
was held for drug trafficking and possession of a prohibited firearm. [@1,2] He was to be brought
before a judge for a first interview, where coercive measures would be set. [@1]

In all, officers seized about 100 kilograms of hashish, a large-calibre pistol, magazines and
ammunition, a ballistic vest, 16,000 euros in cash, a money-counting machine and other evidence.
[@1]

The inquiry began when two suspicious postal parcels from northern Europe were found to hide a
large amount of high-purity cannabis resin. [@1] The Judiciary Police said parcels of that kind
were being sent with some frequency. [@1] The warehouse search then turned up a further quantity
of the drug, a firearm and other prohibited objects, which are included in the totals above. [@1]
