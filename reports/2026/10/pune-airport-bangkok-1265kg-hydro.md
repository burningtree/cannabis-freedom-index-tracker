Customs officers arrested an Indian passenger who arrived at Pune International
Airport from Bangkok, and a court sent him to judicial custody. [@1] The Air
Intelligence Unit, with the Airport Branch of the Pune Customs Department, made
the seizure on 19 October during an anti-smuggling operation at the airport in
Lohegaon. [@2] Officers intercepted him after advanced passenger profiling and a
routine X-ray of his check-in baggage. [@2]

From him they seized 1.265 kilograms of hydroponic ganja, which customs valued
at 44 lakh 27 thousand rupees. [@1] The Indian Express reported the same seizure
as 1.265 kg, with an estimated illicit-market value of Rs 44.27 lakh. [@2] The
cannabis had been hidden in specially made false compartments in the lining of
his bag. [@1] A customs officer said two rectangular, slab-shaped, vacuum-sealed
packets of a yellowish-green dried floral substance were recovered from the
inner lining of a black trolley bag. [@2] A preliminary field test identified
the substance as high-grade marijuana, also known as hydroponic weed. [@2]

An offence was registered against him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, 1985, and he was arrested. [@2] Further investigation was under
way to trace the alleged smuggling network and identify others involved in the
supply chain. [@2]
