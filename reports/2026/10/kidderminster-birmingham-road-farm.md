On Wednesday 14 October 2026, as part of Operation Forefront across Worcestershire, West Mercia
Police forced entry into a disused factory off Birmingham Road on an industrial estate in
Kidderminster. [@1,3] Officers found a sophisticated cannabis set-up across several rooms, with
hydroponics systems, growing chemicals and extractor fans. [@1,3,4] Police estimated the farm at
about £1.2 million. [@1,3,4]

Two men aged 44 and 32 were arrested on suspicion of cultivating cannabis. [@1,3] The next day
West Mercia Police charged Lucas Povilonis, 32, of Accrington Road, Burnley, and Arunas Janusonis,
44, of Berry Street, Burnley, with being concerned in the production of cannabis. [@2,4]

At Kidderminster Magistrates' Court on Thursday 15 October both indicated guilty pleas. [@4] The
case was sent to Worcester Crown Court for a sentencing hearing listed for 12 November, and both
men were remanded in custody. [@4]
