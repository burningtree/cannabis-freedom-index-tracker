After partly covert inquiries into suspected illegal drug and cannabis dealing, prosecutors in Bonn
obtained search warrants for five properties in Troisdorf, Sankt Augustin and Ruppichteroth. [@1,2]
On the morning of Tuesday 27 October 2026 officers from the Rhein-Sieg criminal police carried them
out. [@1,2]

In a detached house in Troisdorf-Spich they found three cellar rooms fitted out as a plantation with
about 900 cannabis plants still in an early growth stage. [@1,2] The utility company was called
because of suspected tampering with the electricity supply, and the technical relief organisation
helped remove the plants and equipment; drug-detection dogs were used during searches in Sankt
Augustin. [@1,2]

Police also seized several mobile phones and other possible evidence. [@1,2] Three suspects aged 42
to 49 were photographed for identification records and then released. [@1,2] Criminal investigation
department 3 is examining their roles and how the plantation was run. [@1,2]
