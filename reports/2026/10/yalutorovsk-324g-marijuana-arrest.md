Yalutorovsk drug-control officers stopped a local man born in 1977 on Serina
Street during the afternoon of 16 October 2026 while checking operational
information, Tyumen Seichas reported. [@1] The man, who had previous drug
convictions, told an officer he had cycled to pick wild cannabis outside the
city for personal use. [@2]

Police took him to the station and, with witnesses present, found a black
plastic bag with plant material in his bag. [@1] Testing showed it was marijuana
weighing more than 324 grams. [@2] Investigators opened a case under part 2 of
article 228 for large-scale possession and released him on a travel
restriction. [@1]
