West Yorkshire Police have dismantled an industrial-scale cannabis
cultivation site in Bradford, arresting and charging one man with
production of a Class B drug. [@1]

The force's Bradford City team, supported by Bradford West officers,
raided a property on Little Horton Lane and seized a farm estimated to
be worth £2 million. [@1] Nam Bao, 45, was remanded in custody pending
further proceedings. [@1]
