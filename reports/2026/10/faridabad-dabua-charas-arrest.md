Police in the Dabua police station area of Faridabad arrested a young man
accused of selling charas, a hand-made cannabis resin. [@1] Officers recovered
110.80 grams of charas, the hand-made cannabis resin, from him. [@1] The accused
was identified as Dharmbir, a resident of the Pilani police station area in
Jhunjhunu district in Rajasthan. [@1]

Dharmbir was living in a rented house in Faridabad at the time of the arrest.
[@1] Police registered a first information report (FIR) against him, the
document that starts a criminal case in India. [@1] They said legal action was
being taken against him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act, India's main anti-drug law. [@1]

Faridabad is a city in Haryana state in northern India, on the southern edge of
the national capital, Delhi. [@1]
