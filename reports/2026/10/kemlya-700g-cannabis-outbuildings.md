A 37-year-old resident of Kemlya in Mordovia's Ichalkovsky district has become
the suspect in a criminal case. [@1] Police found plant material in his
outbuildings on 20 October 2026, the Mordovian Interior Ministry said. [@1]
Mordovmedia reported that officers searched the outbuildings while inspecting
the grounds around the 37-year-old man's home. [@2]

In a sack, paper wraps, several packets and a shoe box, they found seeds, parts
and stems of the plant and loose material, the ministry said. [@2] Officers also
seized items used for smoking. [@2] Testing established that the seized material
was cannabis and marijuana with a combined weight of more than 700 grams. [@1]

The man told police that he had bought seeds of a drug-containing plant and had
grown it on his own plot for personal use. [@1] Mordovmedia reported that
officers identified him on 20 October while checking operational information.
[@2] A criminal case was opened against the Kemlya resident for illegal
possession of narcotic drugs. [@1]
