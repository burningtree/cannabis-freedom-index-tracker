Police in Mirzapur district destroyed ganja, heroin and diazepam seized in
drug-trafficking cases on Friday, 30 October 2026. [@1] Superintendent of Police
Aparna Rajat Kaushik ordered the destruction under an ongoing drive to clear
case property (माल मुकदमाती), the seized goods held as evidence in criminal
cases. [@1] The drugs came from 20 cases: 15 registered at Katra police station,
two at Chilh and three at Padri. [@1] Officers destroyed 5.800 kg of ganja, 202
g of heroin and 108 g of diazepam. [@1]

Police valued the destroyed drugs at about ₹43,30,540, or 43.30 lakh. [@1] All
20 were cases under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act.
[@3] The destruction took place in the presence of the district-level Drug
Disposal Committee (DDC). [@1]

The drugs were burned in an incinerator run by Sangam Mediserv Private Limited
at Dhanupur, in the Handia police area of Prayagraj district. [@2] Police said
the disposal followed the prescribed standards and rules. [@2] Under the
superintendent's direction, case property is being cleared at police stations
across the district. [@2]
