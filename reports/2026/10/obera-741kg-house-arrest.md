Federal judge Alejandro Marcos Gallandat Luzuriaga ordered 169 days of house arrest, until 25 March, for a 19-year-old charged with storing cannabis in Oberá. [@1] Prosecutor Viviana Alejandra Vallejos, with assistant prosecutor Alfredo González de la Mata, charged him the day before the prosecutors' office published the account on 13 October 2026. [@1] The judge gave the investigation 90 working days. [@1]

The inquiry began on 4 October, after the dangerous-drugs division of the Misiones police in Jardín América reported a maroon pickup at a house in Oberá that appeared to be loaded with drugs. [@1] Officers found the vehicle carried a duplicated plate. [@1] For three days they watched a black car coming and going. [@1]

On a Monday night the pickup tried to leave. [@1] Police stopped it. [@1] J.L.P.S. was caught after he tried to run and dropped a phone. [@1] The driver left the vehicle and got away. [@1] El Territorio says the man who was caught is from Panambí and writes his name as Juan P. S. [@2]

In the abandoned pickup, officers found 29 bundles weighing 673.116 kg of cannabis: 13 of pressed bricks and 16 of buds, plus a handheld radio and false documents. [@1] The vehicle was listed as stolen in Buenos Aires. [@1] A search of the house found six more bundles, 67.940 kg, a large scale, a firearm, packing tape, notes and papers in another man's name. [@1] The prosecutors' office describes the load as more than 740 kg. [@1]

Prosecutors said the man who fled, an Argentine who had recently entered from Brazil, was the formal tenant of the rented house. [@1] They also said he and the man in custody were renting three flats by word of mouth in the Miguel Lanús neighbourhood of Posadas. [@1]
