Düsseldorf police drug investigators received tip-offs about a multi-family house on Löricker Straße
in the Lörick district from which a strange smell could be noticed. [@1,2] On site they also heard
clear ventilation noise. [@1,2]

Entering the house over the weekend of 2–4 October 2026, officers found 290 man-high cannabis
plants. [@1,2] The building had been fitted for cultivation: four floors had ventilation and lighting
systems. [@1,2]

A 26-year-old Albanian suspect was arrested and brought before an investigating judge, who ordered
pre-trial detention. [@1,2] The investigation continues. [@1,2]
