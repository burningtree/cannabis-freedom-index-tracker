On Monday 28 September 2026 at about 22:15, customs officers from the Cierp-Gaud
brigade stopped a Polish-plated Renault Talisman at a roundabout in
Saint-Lary-Soulan in the Hautes-Pyrénées. [@1,2,3] The Ukrainian driver, resident
in Poland, said he was returning from Spain through the Aragnouet–Bielsa tunnel
and heading to Brive for the night. [@1,2]

Officers found the floor had been altered. [@1,2,3] Under non-original carpeting
they opened two metal boxes beneath the front seats holding 32 heat-sealed bags
of cannabis herb totalling 32.8 kg; customs valued the load at EUR 131,200. [@1,2,3]
They also seized EUR 405 in cash, cloths soaked in white spirit to mask the smell,
two mobile phones and a laptop. [@1,2,3]

At Tarbes criminal court on Thursday 1 October the man was sentenced to two years'
imprisonment with a committal warrant, a ten-year ban from French territory, customs
fines of EUR 131,200 on the drugs and EUR 405 for customs laundering, and
confiscation of the cannabis, cash and devices. [@2,3] La Dépêche reported that the
court also offered conditional release with expulsion instead of serving the full
term. [@1]
