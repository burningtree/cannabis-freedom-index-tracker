Police destroyed cannabis and hemp cultivation in the remote hills of Nadi Fala
Sadi, in Udaipur district. [@1] The fields belonged to Kavitram, Naklaram,
Holaram, Kiran, Raviya and Narayan. [@1] The joint team from Mandwa police
station and the district special team, or DST, acted on a tip-off at dawn, led
by station house officer Moonglaram and DST in-charge Chandrabhan Singh. [@1]
The operation was supervised by additional superintendent of police Mukul Sharma
and Kotra circle officer Dungar Singh. [@1] Police said the accused had fled and
that officers were raiding possible locations to catch them. [@2]

Police put the seized crop at 1,149.5 kg (more than 11 quintals) of green
cannabis and hemp plants, including 17.45 kg of ganja and 1,132.05 kg of bhang
plants. [@1]

They said the operation targeted a network of illegal cultivation and
trafficking, and that searches for the main accused were continuing. [@1]
