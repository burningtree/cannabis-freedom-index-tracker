Police found a cannabis plantation in a steep forest area of Erymanthos municipality in Achaea on
the morning of 4 October 2026 and uprooted 330 plants. [@1,2] The plants were up to about 2.10
metres tall and grew in a cleared, specially prepared plot. [@1,2]

Officers from the Corinth drug squad, helped by the Corinth crime-prevention unit, also seized
branches and pieces of plants that had been cut and spread out to dry, weighing about 50 kg, and
irrigation equipment. [@1,2]

No arrests were reported. [@1] The Corinth drug squad is leading the preliminary investigation, and
police said they were still looking for the people who cultivated the plot. [@1,2]
