On Thursday 15 October 2026 the public prosecutor’s office at the Mambasa peace court intercepted a
young man presented as involved in hemp trafficking at Mambasa-centre, in Ituri province. [@1,2] The
parquet seized one and a half sacks of the substance and placed the suspect in custody, according to
Maître Jospin Paluku Mbowa, territorial coordinator of the Nouvelle Société Civile Congolaise (NSCC)
in Mambasa. [@1,2]

The NSCC told RFE1 News and Bunia Actualité that information it gathered pointed to a lieutenant in
the 31st brigade of the FARDC as the presumed owner of the hemp, but the outlets stressed that this
had not been established by investigators and that no reaction from the parquet or brigade command
was available. [@1,2]

Paluku Mbowa welcomed the parquet’s action and urged residents of Mambasa-centre to report suspected
hemp sales to judicial or intelligence services. [@1,2] He linked illicit drug commerce to insecurity
in the territorial capital and called on authorities to pursue the case. [@2]
