Officers of the State Security Service's Tashkent department with the capital's Main Department
of Internal Affairs stopped a man born in 1993 living in Yunusabad district while he was in
transit, as part of an operation against online drug trafficking. [@1,2] A search in front of
witnesses found three small containers holding 257 grams of hashish oil on him. [@1,2]

A search of his flat immediately afterwards uncovered another 2.831 kilograms of hashish oil
and an electronic scale used for packing and weighing. [@1,2] Investigators say he worked as a
courier ("zakladchik") and supplier for an illegal online drug store. [@1,2] A criminal case
was opened under the relevant articles of the Criminal Code, and work is under way to identify
his suppliers, the store's organizers and the money flows. [@1,2]
