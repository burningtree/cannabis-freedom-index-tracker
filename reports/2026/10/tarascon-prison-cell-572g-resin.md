On 27 October 2026, guards at the Tarascon detention centre in Bouches-du-Rhône
found suspected drugs and a phone in a single cell, the prison officers' union
FO Justice said. [@1] The find was 572 g of a product that appeared to be
cannabis resin. [@1] Officers also found 21 g of white powder that appeared to
be cocaine. [@1] A mobile phone was found as well. [@1]

FO Justice said the seizures showed the officers' work and revealed the scale of
the problem. [@1] The union said about 60 phones had been seized across the
prison since the start of October, on top of repeated drug finds. [@1] It said
trafficking fed debts, pressure and tension in detention. [@1] Officers bore the
consequences daily and were the first exposed when violence broke out, it added.
[@1]

FO Justice demanded a "fouille XXL", an extensive search of the prison, with
reinforcements, support from the ERIS regional intervention teams and
dog-handling units. [@1] It called for regular, targeted and reinforced cell
searches to stop drugs and banned objects entering and circulating. [@1] It
asked for enough staff to carry out the searches and keep officers safe day to
day. [@1] The prison's local management and the Marseille interregional prison
service (DISP) had to answer the demands quickly and precisely, it said. [@1]
