An anti-crime patrol set up surveillance near rue Chopin in Besançon at about 7
pm on 27 October 2026. [@1] Officers soon spotted a man on the car park carrying
out several deals in succession, first with one vehicle and then with a second.
[@1] The man in the second vehicle handed over zip bags containing 5 g of
cannabis resin and 2 g of herbal cannabis, along with 990 euros in cash. [@1] He
was arrested and placed in police custody, a term for detention for questioning,
after a search of his car found 9.3 g of cocaine. [@1]

The other man, who was on foot and had started the deals, ran off when he saw
the officers, throwing an object over a hedge. [@1] He was quickly caught,
arrested and held in police custody with 340 euros in his jacket. [@1] A search
of his home found 600 euros in cash, 33 g of heroin and 516 g of cannabis resin.
[@1]

The 18-year-old said he had been reselling drugs since that same day and refused
to give the identity of the man who gave him his orders. [@1] He said he had
bought the 5 g of resin just before his arrest from a person he could not
identify, and that the 990 euros came from sales made that day. [@1] He was due
to be presented to the Besançon prosecutor on 26 June 2027. [@1]

The 28-year-old admitted that he had at least acted as a "nourrice", a stash
keeper, for a week and supplied a few customers near his home. [@1] He was
sentenced to five months in prison and taken to the maison d'arrêt, Besançon's
remand prison. [@1]
