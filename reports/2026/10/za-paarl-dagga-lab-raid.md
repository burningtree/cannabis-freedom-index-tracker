Crime Intelligence and the Paarl Crime Prevention Unit executed a search and seizure warrant at a
house in De Villiers Street, Lemoenkloof, on Tuesday 27 October 2026 at about 10:30 am, uncovering
a secret dagga laboratory. [@1,2] Officers found two men aged 32 and 38 and a 44-year-old woman
on the premises, along with large amounts of various cannabis products, cannabis trees, magic
mushrooms and equipment used to cultivate cannabis. [@1,2]

All three were arrested for dealing in drugs and were expected to appear before the Paarl
Magistrate's Court on Thursday 29 October. [@1,2]
