The Muzaffarpur unit of the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence, working with
the Bihar Special Task Force, ran a joint operation against a racket moving
Nepal-origin charas into India for onward transport to Indore in Madhya
Pradesh. [@1,2] After five days of continuous surveillance in remote areas
along the Indo-Nepal border, the team got a specific input on Friday about a
charas store in Raxaul and the presence of the alleged mastermind. [@1]

A coordinated search in the early hours of Saturday recovered ten packets of
charas, a cannabis-based narcotic, weighing 5.14 kg and valued at about Rs
5.14 crore. [@1] Two men including the suspected mastermind were arrested on
the spot under the NDPS Act, officials said the same day. [@1,2] A later
analysis of Bihar's drug routes cites the case as an example of the state
serving as a corridor for narcotics moving between regions. [@2] The bust was announced on Saturday evening. [@1]
