Ulcinj police arrested an Albanian citizen found with over 160 grams of
marijuana, the Police Directorate (Uprava policije) announced. [@1] Acting on
operational information, officers stopped a Mercedes with Albanian plates on the
Ulcinj–Sukobin main road in Vladimirske Krute, searched the people and the car,
and seized about 163 grams of marijuana. [@1] The car carried two Albanian
citizens from Shkodra, A.Q., 26, and H.M., 19. [@1]

A search of A.Q. found a PVC bag of marijuana weighing about 102 grams in his
jacket, and a search of the vehicle found another PVC bag of the same drug
weighing 61 grams. [@2]

Police informed the duty state prosecutor at the Higher State Prosecutor’s
Office (Više državno tužilaštvo, VDT) in Podgorica, then brought both men to the
Ulcinj Security Department for criminal processing and questioning. [@1] On the
order of the prosecutor at the Higher State Prosecutor’s Office in Podgorica,
police arrested A.Q. on suspicion of unauthorised production, possession and
placing of drugs on the market, and said he would be taken to the prosecutor
within the legal time limit. [@2] Officers temporarily seized the Mercedes from
A.Q. for further checks. [@1]
