Florida Highway Patrol troopers booked Jeremiah Baxter into the Palm Beach
County Jail after a stop on Interstate 95 near Boynton Beach. [@1] The arrest
report identifies him as 29. [@1] A 3-year-old was in the truck as her father
allegedly continued driving after troopers activated their lights and sirens.
[@1] Baxter shouted that his daughter was in the truck, and troopers confirmed
the 3-year-old was in a car seat in the rear passenger-side seat. [@1] The
child’s mother arrived and took custody of her. [@1]

Troopers responded after the Lake Worth Regional Communications Center relayed a
caller’s report that a black Dodge Ram was swerving, traveling without
headlights and being driven by someone who appeared to be drinking and using a
phone. [@1] A trooper saw the truck traveling north without headlights or
taillights and following another vehicle too closely. [@1] After the trooper
activated the patrol car’s lights and siren, Baxter continued driving at about
40 mph in a 65-mph zone and kept going after a public-address order to stop.
[@1] A second trooper moved in front of the truck, and Baxter stopped. [@1]
Troopers allege Baxter refused commands to show his hands and get out of the
truck, then resisted as they removed and handcuffed him. [@1] Baxter told
troopers he had not been drinking and that he delayed stopping because he was on
the phone and adjusting a neck pillow around his daughter. [@1] The trooper
reported a slight odor of alcohol on Baxter’s breath but said there were no
other signs of impairment and did not pursue a DUI case. [@1] No alcoholic
beverages were found in the truck. [@1]

During an inventory search, troopers say they found two bags containing a total
of 2.7 grams of marijuana in a backpack, along with pre-rolled tips. [@1] Field
tests were positive for marijuana. [@1] He faces four misdemeanor charges:
failure to obey a lawful order, resisting an officer without violence,
possession of less than 20 grams of marijuana, and possession of drug
paraphernalia. [@1] He also received a civil traffic citation for driving at
night without headlights or taillights. [@1]
