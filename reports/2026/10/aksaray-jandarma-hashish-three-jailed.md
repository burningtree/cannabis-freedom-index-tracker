Gendarmerie officers detained four people in Aksaray city centre after searching
two addresses, their homes and their persons. [@3] A court ordered three of them
held in pre-trial detention, known in Turkey as tutuklama, and released the
fourth under judicial control. [@3] The three jailed were named by initials as
M.V., 29, M.K., 35, and F.D., 29. [@2]

The searches found 1,134 synthetic pharmaceutical pills, an unlicensed pistol,
45 grams of methamphetamine and 21 grams of synthetic cannabinoid, known in
Turkey as bonzai. [@3] At the homes of M.V. and M.K. and in the vehicle F.D.
used, officers also found 2,320 grams of kubar esrar, a cannabis preparation
also called hashish. [@1]

The operation was run by the Aksaray provincial gendarmerie's narcotics branch
and the central district gendarmerie. [@1] The Aksaray Chief Public Prosecutor's
Office coordinated the investigation, and the suspects had been under technical
and physical surveillance before the raids. [@3] DHA described the operation as
aimed at people selling drugs on the street. [@2]
