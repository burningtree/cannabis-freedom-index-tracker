MIDC police in Mumbai arrested two men for allegedly possessing and attempting
to sell 1.896 kg of hydroponic cannabis in Andheri East. [@1,3] Police identified
the men as Omkar Rajendra Pawar, 28, and Piyush Madhukar Ghongade, 22, and said
they were apprehended near JK Industrial Estate on Paper Box Road on Thursday.
[@1] The arrests followed a tip-off that two men would arrive near the
industrial estate to sell the contraband, and officers laid a trap, an MIDC
police station official said. [@2]

A search recovered 1.896 kg of hydroponic cannabis from a red bag. [@2,3] Police
valued the cannabis at Rs 66.36 lakh, about 6.6 million rupees. [@1,3] The men had
travelled from Satara district to Mumbai to sell the contraband, police said.
[@1,3] Police said both were residents of Koregaon in Satara district. [@2]

A case was registered under the NDPS Act. [@1,3] Police said further investigation
would examine the supply and distribution network. [@2,3] Hydroponic cannabis is
grown in a water-based mineral nutrient solution rather than in soil. [@2]

Sub-Inspector Ranjit Gondkupe of the MIDC police station and the anti-narcotics squad were patrolling on the night of 29 October and spotted the two men at about 7.45 pm. [@3] Police gave the men's addresses as Bhakarwadi and Wathar Station in Koregaon, Satara district. [@3]
