The NDLEA arrested 62-year-old Anetor Gloria Ubong, a Nigerian-American realtor
living in Richmond, Texas, who holds Nigerian and US passports, after she
arrived in Lagos aboard a United Airlines flight from Houston on Wednesday 21
October. [@1,2] A search of her two checked-in suitcases found 9.45 kg of
high-grade cannabis, known as loud, in 18 parcels hidden inside a box wrapped as
a gift. [@1,2]

Ubong reportedly told questioners that her daughter and the daughter's future
brother-in-law had delivered the box to her US home for onward delivery in
Nigeria to her daughter's fiance, identified as Jibola. [@1,2] The agency did
not say whether the daughter, the prospective brother-in-law or the intended
recipient had been arrested or questioned. [@1]
