Nymburk detectives charged a 31-year-old man in early October 2026 with growing
cannabis for his own use at his home in quantities far above the legal limit.
[@1] Police said he kept more than 50 plants there, many times the three plants
Czech law allows a person to grow for personal use. [@1]

According to the police account, he grew the marijuana for several months in an
indoor grow he set up in the attic of his house in the Nymburk district. [@1]
During a search of the house, officers seized 52 cannabis plants, already dried
cannabis and the complete grow room. [@1] The equipment included LED lighting,
fans, a hydroponic plastic tray with an irrigation system, and pumps and hoses
to distribute and circulate water. [@1]

Police found no evidence that he sold cannabis to anyone. [@2] He was charged
with the unauthorised growing of a plant or mushroom containing a narcotic or
psychotropic substance, and other handling of it, for personal use, an offence
that carries up to three years in prison. [@1]
