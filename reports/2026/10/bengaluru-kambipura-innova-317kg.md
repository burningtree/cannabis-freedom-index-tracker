On 11 October 2026 Kumbalagodu police received an informant's tip, recorded
in a Commissioner of Police press note, that a large quantity of ganja was
moving from Odisha to Kerala through Bengaluru in an Innova car, and
registered an NDPS case. [@1,2] On 12 October a team staked out the indicated
spot and intercepted the Kerala-bound car near Eidgah Ground at Kambipura,
apprehending its two occupants. [@1,2]

Both men, from Kerala, confessed they had flown down to Bengaluru on 6
October, hired a self-drive rental car in R.T. Nagar, driven to Odisha and
bought the drug cheap from an unidentified seller for resale to customers in
Kumaramanagalam, Kerala at a higher price. [@1,2] Back in Bengaluru on 11
October, they spent the night in a Bommasandra hotel and were caught the next
day heading for Kerala. [@1,2] The car held about 317 kg of ganja, seized
with the vehicle at Rs 1.78 crore, Rs 3.17 crore at market rates. [@1,2] The
operation ran under DCP Soumyalatha S.K. and ACP Satyavati K.S. with PI Kumar
leading the station team; the hunt for the Odisha suppliers and sale-side
helpers continues. [@1,2]
