Police stopped two vehicles near the community of Rosario on the road between Guayaramerín and
Cachuela Esperanza, in Bolivia's Beni department, close to the Brazilian border. [@1] Officers
first intercepted a station wagon carrying jute sacks of a green substance they took for marijuana.
[@1] A pickup then tried to avoid the checkpoint and was stopped kilometres further on, with more
packages of the same substance. [@1]

Field tests were positive for marijuana. [@1] Beni's departmental prosecutor, Orlando Aramayo
Chávez, said the load was wet marijuana weighing 971.9 kilograms, and that both vehicles were
seized. [@1] The men held were named as Heiner R. M. H. and Freddy E. C. M., aged 33 and 27. [@1]
They are under investigation for trafficking in controlled substances and were taken before the
duty investigating judge in Guayaramerín. [@1]

The assigned prosecutor, Elmer Aguilar Gamarra, said he learned of the case on 20 October 2026
from a report by the Intelligence and Special Operations Group in Guayaramerín. [@1] El Mundo
carried the same weights, names and place from the prosecutor's office. [@2]
