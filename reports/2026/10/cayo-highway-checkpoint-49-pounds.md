Police arrested and charged three people after seizing 49 pounds of
suspected cannabis during a highway checkpoint operation on Monday night,
20 October. [@1]

Mobile Interdiction Unit and Highway Interdiction Team officers manning
the checkpoint signalled a silver Ford Escape to stop; told of the
intended search, the SUV sped off, and officers pursued and intercepted
it while two men fled. [@1] A search found several parcels of suspected
cannabis. [@1] Whitney Doris Alford, 21, of Unitedville and Lisa Marie
Gonzalez, 31, of Santa Elena were detained at the scene; Francisco
Ernesto Landaverde, 23, of San Ignacio later turned himself in. [@1] All
three were charged with possession of controlled drugs with intent to
supply. [@1]
