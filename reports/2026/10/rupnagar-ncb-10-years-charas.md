The Narcotics Control Bureau's Chandigarh Zonal Unit secured the conviction of three drug
traffickers caught moving 3.65 kg of charas hidden in a car speaker system. [@1,2] A
surveillance team had intercepted the car on Sirhind-Patiala Road in Morinda, Rupnagar
district, on 1 July 2021, after which the seven packets of round and finger-shaped charas in
the boot speaker were found; all three occupants were arrested at the spot. [@1,2]

In its judgment of 21 October 2026, the Special Court under Judge Lukhvinder Kaur Duggal
held the NDPS Act's mandatory provisions complied with. [@1,2] On pronouncing the sentence on
22 October, the court gave each of the three accused – Kuldeep Singh alias Golu and Sanjeev
Kumar alias Mithoo of Nabha, and Ashok Kumar of Patiala/Chandigarh – 10 years of rigorous
imprisonment plus a Rs 2 lakh fine. [@1,2]
