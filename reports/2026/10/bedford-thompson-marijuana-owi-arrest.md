Amanda Thompson, 35, of Springville, faces preliminary charges of operating
while intoxicated (OWI), OWI with a controlled substance, OWI endangerment,
possession of marijuana and possession of paraphernalia. [@1] She was arrested
in Bedford, Indiana, early on Sunday 25 October 2026 after police saw her
vehicle strike a curb. [@1] The arrest log names her as Amanda Jean Thompson,
gives the arrest time as 4:28 a.m. and records the marijuana count as possession
with a prior conviction. [@2] Police recorded the traffic stop in the 1300 block
of 7th Street, at Shorty's Bar & Grill. [@2] According to a probable cause
affidavit filed by Sgt. Nick Crulo, the incident began at about 4:20 a.m. [@1]
Crulo was traveling north on Lincoln Avenue between 7th Street and 6 1/2 Street
when he saw a dark Ford SUV drive onto and off the west-side curb and turn onto
7th Street. [@1] The vehicle turned into an alley connecting 7th Street and John
Williams Boulevard, parked and switched off its lights. [@1] When Crulo
approached, he reported smelling marijuana, and Thompson handed him a glass
smoking device from the center console. [@1] Asked about hitting the curb, she
told the officer she “always hits curbs.” [@1]

Officers noted poor balance, glassy eyes, slurred speech and the smell of
alcohol on her breath, and she admitted drinking a small amount earlier in the
evening. [@1] A preliminary breath test gave a reading of 0.08 BrAC. [@1] She
was taken to IU Health Bedford for a blood draw at 5:43 a.m. [@1]

Officers found a tinted pill bottle containing marijuana, which a field test
confirmed. [@1] Neither passenger claimed the marijuana, and Thompson told
officers it was “community” marijuana given to them by someone. [@1] As officers
conferred, Major Emmons saw her push an item into her pants; a subsequent search
found a hidden marijuana vape pen, which she admitted smoking earlier in the
evening. [@1] She was booked into the Lawrence County Jail, and her vehicle was
released to the passengers. [@1]
