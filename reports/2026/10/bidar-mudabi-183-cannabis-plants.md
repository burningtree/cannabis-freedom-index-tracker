Mudabi police arrested a man who had grown ganja, or cannabis, on his field on
the outskirts of Mudabi village, in Basavakalyana taluk of Bidar district. [@2]
Officers seized 183 raw plants weighing 8 kg 820 g, which police valued at Rs
4.41 lakh. [@1] Police acted on information that cannabis was being grown in the
field, and the raid was carried out on the direction of Bidar Superintendent of
Police Pradeep Gunti. [@1] The field is within the limits of the Mudabi police
station. [@1]

The accused was handed over to judicial custody. [@3] A case was filed at the
Mudabi police station. [@3]

Speaking about the raid, Gunti said that growing, consuming and supplying
narcotics is a crime and that anyone involved in such activity would face legal
action. [@1] He urged the public not to take part in such activity and ruin
their own and their families' futures. [@1] He added that the district police
would keep up their operations to make Bidar a drug-free district. [@2]
