Charlie Williams, 21, admitted possessing cannabis with intent to supply at
Chester Magistrates' Court on 25 September and returned to the same court on 26
October 2026 to be sentenced. [@1] The court imposed a community order running
until 25 October 2027, with 100 hours of unpaid work. [@1]

His phone and cash were taken from him under an order made by the court. [@1]
The cannabis was forfeited and ordered to be destroyed. [@1] Williams was
ordered to pay £85 in costs and a £114 surcharge. [@1] He possessed cannabis, a
Class B drug, in Ellesmere Port, Cheshire, on 23 February. [@1] Prosecutor Mark
Baker told the court that officers in Ellesmere Port stopped and searched him
that day and found snap bags of cannabis, cash and a phone. [@2] Baker said the
cannabis had a street value of between £2,000 and £3,300. [@2] He added that
Williams had a previous conviction for drug dealing. [@2]

Stevie Bate, defending, said Williams had pleaded guilty at the earliest
opportunity and was afforded full credit. [@2] She accepted that his previous
convictions were an aggravating factor. [@2]
