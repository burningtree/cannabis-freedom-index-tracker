During a routine paneling inspection of incoming freight at a courier warehouse in Baguio
City on Tuesday 27 October 2026, a PDEA-Cordillera narcotics detection dog alerted handlers
to a suspicious parcel. [@1] With the hub’s management, officers opened it and seized
979.62 grams of dried marijuana leaves that PDEA valued at P117,554.40 and 14.67 grams of
high-grade marijuana known as kush valued at P17,604.00, about P135,158 in total. [@1]

The Cordillera Land Transportation Interdiction Unit and its K9 team carried out the
interdiction. [@1] No suspects were arrested at the warehouse. [@1] Tracking records
indicated the parcel was sent from Cavite City and addressed to a barangay in Baguio;
authorities were investigating the sender and consignee and said charges could follow.
[@1,2] PDEA-Cordillera said it would keep watching express delivery networks. [@2]
