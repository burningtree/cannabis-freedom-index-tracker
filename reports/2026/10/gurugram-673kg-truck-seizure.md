Gurugram Crime Branch Sector 65, led by sub-inspector Arun, stopped a Rajasthan-registered truck near Rajiv Chowk on the Delhi–Jaipur expressway around 1 am on 27 October 2026 after a tip-off. [@1,2]

Officers found 673 kg of marijuana concealed beneath 767 bags of plastic granules that the drivers were carrying from Odisha toward Bhiwadi in Rajasthan. [@1,2] Police valued the drugs at about ₹1.25 crore. [@1,2]

The driver and truck owner Mohammad Sabir, 34, and co-driver Mohammad Sanawar, 19, both from Khairthal district in Rajasthan, were arrested. [@1,2] An FIR was registered at Sadar police station under section 20(b)(ii)(C) of the NDPS Act and a court remanded them to police custody for ten days. [@1,2]

Assistant commissioner of police (crime) Naveen Sharma said Sabir was flown to Visakhapatnam on 23 October to load the truck and that this was his third such consignment for ₹2 lakh per run. [@1]
