Hoang Anh Vo, 41, Pham Van Houng, 36, and Anh Tran, 29, were sentenced at Bradford Crown Court on
Monday 19 October 2026 for producing cannabis at a warehouse unit at Station Mills on Station Road
in Wyke. [@1,2] Vo and Houng each received three years after a trial; Tran, who had pleaded guilty in
January, received 31 months. [@1,2]

West Yorkshire Police had raided the unit in July 2025. [@1] Officers found Vo hiding in a cupboard
and Houng and Tran under a pile of rubbish. [@1,2] The basement held 23 growing rooms with lighting,
transformers, filters and fans, and 3,068 plants from seedlings to flowering. [@1,2] The premises also
had living quarters and a cooking area. [@1,2]

Prosecutor Gabrielle Coates told the court the plants could have produced cannabis worth between
£1.3 million and £3.7 million. [@2] Judge Sophie McKone said the three men had worked as gardeners in
an industrial-scale operation and accepted they had played a lesser role after being hired when they
were desperate. [@1,2] The court heard they could be deported to Vietnam after their sentences. [@1,2]
