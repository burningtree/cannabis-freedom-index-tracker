Caulfield Divisional Response Unit detectives arrested and charged two men on
Thursday 29 October 2026 after executing a warrant at an address on Peace Street
in Highett. [@2] The detectives had been investigating drug trafficking. [@1]
Both men are from Highett. [@1] One is 48 years old and the other is 67. [@1]

The 48-year-old was charged with trafficking a commercial quantity of
methylamphetamine, possessing cannabis, possessing a prohibited weapon and
dealing with property suspected to be proceeds of crime. [@1] Police remanded
him to appear at Melbourne Magistrates’ Court on 21 January 2027. [@2] The
67-year-old was charged with cultivating a cannabis plant, trafficking cannabis
and dealing with proceeds suspected to be proceeds of crime. [@1] He was bailed
to appear at Moorabbin Magistrates’ Court on 11 March 2027. [@2] The older man’s
cannabis charges are cultivation and trafficking, while the younger man’s
cannabis charge is possession. [@2]

During the search, investigators seized quantities of methylamphetamine and
cannabis, hydroponic equipment, a large quantity of liquid believed to be 1,4
Bute, and cash. [@2] Victoria Police listed the hydroponic equipment separately
from the drugs. [@1] The liquid was described only as believed to be 1,4 Bute.
[@1] Cash was seized, and both men were charged with dealing with property or
proceeds suspected to be proceeds of crime. [@1]
