On Wednesday 28 October 2026, the Military Police of Pernambuco found a large
site used to grow and process cannabis in the rural area of Alagoinha, in the
state's Agreste region. [@1] The operation was that Wednesday, 28 October. [@1]
A group of suspects left the area and fled through the caatinga scrub as
officers approached, and they were not found during the operation. [@1] CBN
Recife reported that the suspects had escaped and that no arrests linked to the
case were known. [@2]

Teams from the 8th Independent Military Police Company (8ª CIPM), with the
intelligence unit and the Tactical Group, carried out the operation. [@1] Police
said they seized about 250 kilograms of cannabis ready for use and destroyed a
plantation of about 120,000 plants. [@1] According to the corporation, the site
also held about 750 kilograms of cannabis still being prepared, which officers
eradicated and burned along with the plants. [@1] CBN Recife gave the same
figures: about 250 kilograms seized ready for use, about 750 kilograms in
preparation incinerated, and about 120,000 plants destroyed. [@2]

Officers also collected irrigation equipment, food, four plants set aside as
samples, a trimming machine used to process cannabis, two mobile phones and a
Starlink kit for internet access. [@1] Two mobile phones and a Starlink kit used
for internet access were also seized. [@1] After the plantation was found, Civil
Police teams from the Institute of Criminalistics and Rastro/GGPOC were called
to examine the site, collect traces and look for evidence that could identify
those responsible. [@1] Everything seized was sent to the duty police station in
Belo Jardim. [@1]
