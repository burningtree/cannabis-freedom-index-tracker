Military highway police stopped a truck at Assis, in São Paulo state, on the afternoon of
21 October 2026 and found 1,402.1 kg of cannabis in 44 cardboard boxes hidden among a load of
nappies. [@1,2] The driver, a 43-year-old man, was arrested for drug trafficking. [@1]

The stop was made by the Tático Ostensivo Rodoviário of the 2nd Highway Police Battalion with the
FICCO, an integrated anti-organised-crime task force, during Operation Impacto. [@1] The driver
appeared nervous and gave contradictory answers about the route, prompting officers to search the
trailer. [@1,2]

He told police that an unknown person had taken the truck in Rolândia, in Paraná, to be loaded with
the drug, and that its likely destination was Miracema, in Rio de Janeiro state. [@1] He was taken
to the federal police station in Marília and remained in custody there. [@1,3] The highway police
estimated that the seizure cost the traffickers R$2.8 million. [@1]

In a statement on the wider operation, the federal police put the Assis load at 1,412.1 kg and said
two other men were arrested with 726.15 kg in São Paulo city the same day, bringing the operation's
total to 2,138.25 kg. [@3]
