Rhode Island State Police arrested Eric Thomas, 24, of Cranston, Angell
Martinez, 20, of Providence, and Jayden Martinez, 18, of Providence on Thursday
8 October 2026 after troopers carried out search warrants. [@2] The High
Intensity Drug Trafficking Area (HIDTA) Task Force, assisted by the Violent
Fugitive Task Force and the Cranston Police Special Investigations Unit, said it
had concluded a narcotics and firearms investigation into an organised criminal
street gang operating in the Providence and Cranston areas. [@1] The group was
alleged to be associated with firearms-related offences and narcotics
trafficking. [@1]

During the court-authorized search warrants, officers seized two illegal
firearms and considerable ammunition. [@1] They also seized multiple extended
magazines, 50 grams of cocaine, 40 grams of fentanyl, marijuana and $5,000 in
cash. [@1] The three face charges including possession of a firearm by a person
prohibited, possession of a firearm while committing a controlled-substance
offence, and possession with intent to deliver over an ounce of a Schedule II
controlled substance. [@2] The drug charges listed also include possession with
intent to deliver fentanyl, cocaine and marijuana. [@1] State police said the
search warrants resulted from an investigation into multiple violent criminal
groups. [@2]

State police said the warrants were part of an 18-month investigation in which
the HIDTA task force conducted dozens of search warrants against members of
violent groups. [@1] The 18-month tally was 18 arrests, 20 firearms, dozens of
extended magazines, large amounts of ammunition and seven auto-sear switches.
[@1] An auto-sear switch is a device used to convert a semi-automatic handgun
into a fully automatic machine gun. [@1] The HIDTA task force includes state
police, the Cranston, Johnston, Coventry and Narragansett police departments,
the DEA and the Rhode Island National Guard counterdrug programme. [@1]
