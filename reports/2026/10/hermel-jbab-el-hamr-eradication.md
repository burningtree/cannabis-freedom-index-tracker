The Lebanese Army command's Directorate of Orientation announced on 26
October 2026 that a unit had destroyed about 180 dunums (18 hectares) of
cannabis in the Jabab al-Hamr area of Hermel, in the Baalbek-Hermel
governorate. [@1] The army estimated the seized crop at about 60 tonnes. [@1]

The Sobhiye newsletter, summarising the announcement the next day, confirmed
the 180-dunum, 60-tonne figures for the Hermel destruction. [@2] It added
that troops in Brital arrested a wanted man suspected of leading a kidnapping
and robbery gang, seizing a rocket-propelled grenade and drugs at his home. [@2]

The army's own statement likewise reports the Brital–Baalbek raid, saying
troops detained the wanted man at his home after surveillance and monitoring. [@1]
