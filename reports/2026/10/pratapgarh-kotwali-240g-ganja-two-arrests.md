Kotwali police in Pratapgarh, Rajasthan, seized 240 grams of ganja and arrested
two men during a patrol under Operation Trinetra. [@1] The patrol had been
moving along a dirt path from the bypass road towards the excise road. [@2] A
search of the polythene bag he was carrying found the 240 grams of ganja, and
officers arrested him. [@2]

Police traced Meena and arrested him as well. [@2]

A case was registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act, India's main drug law. [@2]
