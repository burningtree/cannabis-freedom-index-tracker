Yemariam Teshome, 21, a Canadian resident of Ontario, appeared in Bermuda's
Supreme Court and pleaded not guilty to charges that she conspired with others
not before the court to import cannabis into Bermuda between an unknown date and
21 June 2026. [@1] She also denied a charge that she possessed a quantity of the
drug with intent to supply on that same date. [@1] The Royal Gazette reported
that she is accused of smuggling more than $1 million of cannabis into the
island. [@1]

Puisne Judge Alan Richards said the quantity of drugs seized in the case could
be valued at more than $1 million depending on how it was sold. [@1] He
adjourned the matter until 11 December to be mentioned and set a fixed trial
date for 15 February. [@1]

The importation charge covers a period that begins on an unknown date and ends
on 21 June 2026. [@1]
