A 19-year-old man was arrested at Larnaca airport on Friday morning 23 October 2026 after about
28 kilos of cannabis was found in his luggage. [@1,2] He had flown in from Thailand via Dubai, a
police source told one outlet. [@1]

Acting on information, customs officers working with the anti-drugs unit (YKAN) stopped him and
found 26 packages containing about 28 kilos of cannabis in two of his suitcases. [@1,2] The
suspect holds a British passport and was due before Larnaca District Court on Saturday for a
remand order. [@1,2] The unit is trying to trace the people in Cyprus the drugs were intended
for, and Interpol is expected to be informed. [@1,2]
