Police Commissioner Harmanbir Singh Gill ordered the destruction, in which
officers put the drugs into the mill's boiler; DCP (Investigation) Prabhjot
Singh Virk, ADCP (Investigation) Jayant Puri and ACP Kapil Kaushal oversaw the
operation. [@1]

The cannabis destroyed was 50.012 kg of charas, a hashish made from cannabis
resin, 2.170 kg of ganja, or cannabis flower, and 198 g of bhang, a cannabis
preparation, about 52.38 kg in all. [@1] The haul also included 18.499 kg of
heroin, 71.8 kg of poppy husk, 3.353 kg of intoxicating powder and 857 g of
smack. [@2] Officers also destroyed 15,831 intoxicating capsules, 3,955 tablets,
76 injections and five bottles of cough syrup. [@1]

Police said the drive against drug trafficking and the illegal trade was
continuing, and that narcotics recovered in drug cases are being disposed of
under the prescribed rules alongside legal action. [@1]
