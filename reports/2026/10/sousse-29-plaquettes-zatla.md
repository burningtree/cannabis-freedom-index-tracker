On Sunday 18 October 2026, national security units in Sousse city arrested a man they suspected of
drug trafficking. [@1,2] Tunisie Numérique said the arrest followed an ambush. [@1] La Presse,
citing a security source, said it followed field inquiries. [@2]

Officers seized 29 slabs of cannabis resin, which La Presse called zatla. [@1,2] Both reports say
he had been expected to sell the resin to young people, including pupils and students in the Sousse
area, and that he used a private car, which was also seized. [@1,2] Tunisie Numérique said the
search for other suspects was continuing. [@1] La Presse said the precise circumstances and any
court outcome had not been given. [@2]
