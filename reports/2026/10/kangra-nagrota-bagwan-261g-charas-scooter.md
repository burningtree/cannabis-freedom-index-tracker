The police team was on patrol in Nagrota Bagwan when it received a tip that a
man on a scooter was buying and selling drugs. [@3] Officers set up a checkpoint
on Masal Road and searched the scooter in front of independent witnesses. [@3]
In the scooter's storage box they found 261 grams of charas, a hand-rubbed
cannabis resin, in a paper envelope. [@3] Superintendent of Police Kulbhushan
Verma said the charas was recovered from the rider, Kartik, on 27 October. [@1]

Kartik, a resident of Tharu village in Nagrota Bagwan tehsil, is about 21. [@3]
The village lies near the Jalvimbi post office, the police account said. [@2] A
case, numbered 160/2026 and dated 28 October 2026, was registered against him at
Nagrota Bagwan police station under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act, India's narcotics law. [@3] Police took the charas and
the scooter into custody. [@3] Police said the investigation was continuing.
