Three young men born in 2004 were detained in a Tracker car while distributing
drugs. [@2] The raids on 21 October 2026 took place in Uchtepa and Bektemir
districts of Tashkent and in Ohangaron district of Tashkent region, run by the
Interior Ministry's operational-search department, its regional units and the
State Security Service. [@2] The three are suspected of acting as warehouse
keepers and as distributors who left drugs in hidden places. [@2]

Searches of their homes, rented premises and adjoining land found about 500
grams of marijuana, more than 2 kilograms of mephedrone, a synthetic stimulant,
1,000 zip bags and scales. [@3] The Tashkent police statement put the total haul
at 52,800 single doses. [@1]

The operation continued in Yukori Chirchiq district, where police detained
people who had come to collect synthetic drugs hidden in open fields, along with
their accomplices. [@2] Those detained included students at various educational
institutions and previously convicted citizens. [@1] Investigators say the group
had worked for several months for an online drug store, first as zakladchiki —
couriers who leave drugs in hidden drop points — and later as warehouse workers.
[@3]

A criminal case was opened under Article 273, part 6, paragraph "a", of the
Criminal Code, which covers making, processing, buying and storing drugs for
sale. [@1] The investigation continues. [@3]
