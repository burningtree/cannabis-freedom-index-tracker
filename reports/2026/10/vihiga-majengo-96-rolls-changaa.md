Police in Vihiga County seized 440 litres of illicit brew and 96 rolls of
bhang from a rental house at Majengo Estate after a public tip-off. [@1]

County Police Commander Inspector Ben Baraza said officers raided the house
on Saturday, October 3, 2026, finding 440 litres of chang'aa in 22
20-litre containers and 96 rolls of bhang. [@1] The exhibits were booked at
Vihiga Police Station. [@1]

The pursued occupant, a well-known 27-year-old man, escaped through a window
before arrest; police launched a manhunt and warned landlords against
renting premises for illicit alcohol and narcotics. [@1]
