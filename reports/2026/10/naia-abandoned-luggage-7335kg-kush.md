Personnel from the Bureau of Customs found 7,335 grams of marijuana kush in three unclaimed
pieces of luggage abandoned at the Ninoy Aquino International Airport on 26 October 2026.
[@1,2] The kush was concealed in food-labelled aluminium packaging. [@1,2]

Customs found the drugs during X-ray screening, manual inspection and K-9 examination with
the NAIA Inter-Agency Drug Interdiction Task Group, the police Aviation Security Group and
PDEA. [@1,2] The bags were traced to two passengers who arrived from Bangkok, Thailand, on
24 September and never claimed them; PDEA took custody of the P7,335,000 haul. [@1,2] No
arrests were reported. [@1,2]
