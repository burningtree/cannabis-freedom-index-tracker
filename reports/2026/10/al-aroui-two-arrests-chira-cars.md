Officers of the judicial police in Al Aroui arrested two young men in two
separate operations on suspicion of possessing and trafficking drugs, and seized
drugs, cash and cars. [@1] In the first operation, they arrested a man who was
the subject of several search warrants at national level and seized large
quantities of chira, the cannabis resin known in Morocco as hashish, and kif,
cannabis. [@1] Officers also took cash and weapons from him and seized two
Mercedes cars used for his movements. [@1]

The second operation led to the arrest of another man suspected of trafficking
cocaine, with cocaine, cash that police believe came from sales, and two other
vehicles seized. [@1] Police suspect that the cars seized in both operations
were used in trafficking. [@2] The first suspect was brought before the
competent court after the legal procedures were completed. [@2] The Al Aroui
police commissariat presented the two operations as part of its continuing
campaign against drug trafficking and psychotropic substances. [@2]
