Near the Nepal-India border area, the Armed Police recovered ganja hidden in
a paddy field at Jahada in Morang: 210 kg of ganja was recovered on Monday
evening from the paddy field at Naya Basti in Jahada rural municipality-1.
[@1]

After receiving special information, teams deployed from Armed Police Force
Nepal No. 3 Battalion headquarters Kankalini Morang and BOP Jahada Bhutaha
searched around 9 at night and found the ganja packed in black plastic and
hidden. [@1]

According to Morang Armed Police chief SP Gehendra Bahadur Bhandari, the
ganja was found while examining a suspiciously kept object in the paddy
field; the recovered ganja was kept for necessary investigation and legal
process. [@1]
