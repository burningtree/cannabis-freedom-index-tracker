On 16 September 2026 NDLEA officers seized 197.8 kg of cannabis sativa from the home of
41-year-old Fajana Dada Oluwaseyi in Ibadan, Oyo State, but he escaped arrest at that time,
the agency said in a 25 October 2026 statement. [@1,2]

On Monday 19 October 2026 operatives arrested him in the Adamasingba area of Ibadan after
further work on the case, according to the same release summarised by NigeriaUpdates. [@1,2]

Chairman Mohamed Buba Marwa commended the commands involved in the wider October enforcement
drive; the statement did not report any court appearance for Oluwaseyi. [@1]
