Gendarmes of the brigade territoriale mobile Est-Lioran, a mobile gendarmerie
unit, stopped a 45-year-old man at Saint-Georges, in the Cantal department, on
16 October 2026. [@1] He was a Sanflorain — someone from Saint-Flour — who lived
in the village of Coren, and the officers placed him in garde à vue, French
police custody for questioning. [@1] His roadside test was positive for cannabis
and cocaine, and he was already known to the gendarmerie. [@1] He was arrested
for drug use and for keeping cocaine, cannabis and undeclared weapons at his
home. [@1]

He was summoned on Monday 26 October 2026 to a comparution sur reconnaissance
préalable de culpabilité, a plea-bargain hearing in which the defendant admits
the facts and accepts the sentence the prosecutor proposes for a judge to
approve. [@1] The court sentenced him to eight months in prison ferme, a term to
be served rather than suspended, and he could have it adjusted to serve under an
electronic bracelet. [@1]
