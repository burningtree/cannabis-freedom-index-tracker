Two suspects were arrested and more than 29 kilograms of suspected cannabis
seized in separate anti-drug operations in Bungoma and Murang’a counties. [@1]
The operations were conducted by detectives from the Directorate of Criminal
Investigations’ National Anti-Narcotics Investigations Bureau (NANIB), working
alongside NACADA and National Government Administration Officers. [@1] In
Bungoma South Sub-County, detectives raided the homestead of Salama Saubu Dauda
in the Mandizini area, where they recovered 13.35 kilograms of suspected
cannabis valued at approximately KSh 400,500. [@1] A search of her main house
and an unoccupied rental house also led to the recovery of an electronic kitchen
scale, 24 pairs of scissors, cello tape, polythene papers, 30 packets of Rizla
papers and three rolling flags, items believed to be used in preparing and
packaging the drugs. [@1]

In a separate operation in Murang’a East Sub-County, 30-year-old Louse Muthoni
was arrested in Gatitu Village, Gakurwe Sub-Location, while allegedly loading a
large consignment of cannabis onto a motorcycle for transportation. [@1]
Detectives there recovered 15.93 kilograms of cannabis valued at KSh 477,900,
and officers also seized 41 packets of Rizla papers, a rolling flag and a red
Boxer motorcycle, registration number KMCN 257S, suspected of being used to
transport the drugs. [@1] The two seizures brought the total amount of suspected
cannabis recovered to 29.28 kilograms, with an estimated street value of KSh
878,400. [@1] The DCI said it remains committed to dismantling drug trafficking
networks and urged members of the public to report suspected criminal activities
anonymously. [@1]
