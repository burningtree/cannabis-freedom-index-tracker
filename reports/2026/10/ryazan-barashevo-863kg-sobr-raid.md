Ryazan drug-control officers, with SOBR Kolovrat troops from the regional
Rosgvardiya command, detained a 52-year-old Ryazan man at his home in
Barashevo village in Sasovsky district on 7 October 2026, Rosgvardiya
reported. [@1] Investigators had learned the previously convicted man was
using the country house to store drugs. [@2]

After a court-approved search, officers found two large sacks of plant
material in an outbuilding among household goods. [@1] Testing showed
8 kilograms 630 grams of marijuana. [@2] The man told investigators he had
grown cannabis on his plot in summer and processed it for himself; police
said they were checking that account. [@2]

The Sasovsky district investigator opened a large-scale possession and
manufacturing case under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal Code. [@2]
A court ordered him held in custody pending trial. [@1]
