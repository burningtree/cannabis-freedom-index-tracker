Two men were arrested and sent to jail after excise officers in Kalahandi
district found 100 kg of ganja in an auto-rickshaw. [@1] The two are Sriram
Gaud, 30, of Bihar, and Bhola Harpal, 35, of Bhuanapada village near Tusura in
Balangir district. [@2] They were produced in court in Kalahandi on Wednesday 28
October. [@2]

The interception happened on Tuesday 27 October, when a Kesinga excise team on
patrol detected the auto-rickshaw on national highway 59. [@1] Officers found
100 kg of ganja, a pistol, five live rounds and a mobile phone in the
auto-rickshaw. [@3] The Times of India reported that the ganja was packed in
five plastic bags of 20 kg each. [@1] The excise department also seized the
auto-rickshaw used to carry the ganja. [@3]

Excise officials estimated the seized ganja's market value at ₹11.5 lakh. [@3]
The Times of India put its value at Rs 10 lakh. [@1] The Times of India reported
that the consignment was being taken from Kandhamal district to Bihar. [@1] M K
Pradhan, officer in charge of the Kesinga excise station, said the seizure was
still being investigated. [@1]
