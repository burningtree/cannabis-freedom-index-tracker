Rostov-on-Don drug officers detained a 27-year-old local man suspected of
illegal drug possession on 26 October 2026, the North Caucasus Interior
Ministry transport directorate told ROSTOF.RU. [@1]

During a personal search they found a plastic-wrapped packet of green plant
material in his bag. [@1] Expert testing identified it as marijuana weighing
more than 200 grams. [@2]

Police said he had collected wild cannabis in a shelterbelt in Aksay
district, dried and ground it for personal use rather than sale. [@2]
Investigators opened a criminal case for large-scale possession without
intent to supply, which can carry up to ten years in prison. [@1]
