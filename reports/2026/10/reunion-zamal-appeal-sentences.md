After the prosecution sought up to ten years, the Saint-Denis court of appeal
increased the sentences of three of four men prosecuted for trafficking zamal
between Réunion and Mauritius. [@1] Bertrand De Boisvilliers and Laurent Mariaye
were sentenced to seven years' imprisonment, Gaëtan Petitdemange to five years,
and Guito Louise's four-year term was left unchanged. [@1]

De Boisvilliers, former chief of staff of the Sainte-Suzanne town hall and owner
of the boat l'Abyss, described himself as a mere transporter tasked with handing
the goods over at sea to Mauritians; the prosecution called him an essential link
in the maritime network. [@1] Petitdemange presented himself as a lookout who
opened the road during transports. [@1] All four admitted the substance of their
involvement while minimising their roles, for trafficking that ran from July 2023
to April 2025. [@1]
