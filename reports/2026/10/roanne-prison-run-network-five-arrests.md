An anti-drug operation in Roanne ended with five arrests and the seizure of
cocaine and cannabis. [@1] Three people were arrested that day, and the inquiry
then led investigators to two suspected organisers, bringing the total to five.
[@1]

The drugs were seized in a search of an "appartement nourrice", a flat in Roanne
used to store them. [@2] Officers found 435 g of cocaine and 1.7 kg of cannabis.
[@1]

Both suspected organisers had already been convicted twice for identical
offences, and they are due to be tried in December. [@2]
