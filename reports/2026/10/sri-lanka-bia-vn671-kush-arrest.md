Officers of Sri Lanka Customs' Narcotics Control Unit arrested a 24-year-old
Indian national at Bandaranaike International Airport at about 1.15 a.m. on 26
October. [@1] The passenger had arrived on Vietnam Airlines flight VN671 and
was trying to exit through the Green Channel of the arrival terminal when
officers noticed his suspicious behaviour. [@1]

His luggage was scanned, and after suspicious items showed up, officers opened
it and found several sealed polythene packets that proved to hold 5.1 kg of
kush cannabis with an estimated market value above Rs 51 million. [@1] Customs
handed the suspect, the drugs and the other evidence to the Police Narcotic
Bureau for investigation and legal proceedings. [@1]
