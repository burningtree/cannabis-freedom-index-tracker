On 21 October 2026 customs officers from the Frouzins inland surveillance brigade stopped a car at
the A66 toll at Mazères, in Ariège. [@1,2] In the boot and rear of the vehicle they found 186.45 kg
of cannabis resin and 16.4 kg of herbal cannabis. [@1]

The Spanish driver and his Colombian companion, neither previously known to French justice, were
held; OFAST in Haute-Garonne took the inquiry. [@1,2] Prosecutors said the drugs had been loaded into
a hire car in Spain for delivery to Italy through France. [@1,2]

Tried in immediate appearance at Foix on 27 October 2026, the driver received four years in prison
with continued detention and a ten-year ban from French territory. [@1,2] His partner received three
years, of which two were suspended, with detention for the firm part and a five-year territorial
ban. [@1,2] They were jointly ordered to pay a customs fine of €617,750, the stated market value of
the drugs. [@1,2]
