Police seized a commercial quantity of suspected dangerous drugs during a
raid on a compound in the village of Yarka in the Western Galilee. [@1]

The haul included about 8 kilograms of hashish and about 1,000 Captagon
pills, allegedly intended for distribution; officers also confiscated
communication devices and a digital surveillance system said to be used
for criminal monitoring. [@1] Two local suspects were arrested and taken
to the Western Galilee station for questioning. [@1]
