Police in Aktobe detained a 33-year-old man in a dacha settlement, an area of
summer-house and garden plots. [@1] Officers stopped him for a check there and
found a bag of plant material on him. [@1] A further search of his home turned
up another bag, holding 15 bundles, and a bag with similar contents in a barn on
the property. [@1]

Experts identified the seized material as marijuana. [@1] The Aktobe region
police department, cited by Diapazon, put the total weight at more than 1.7
kilograms. [@1] A medical examination confirmed that the man was intoxicated
with drugs at the time he was detained. [@1]

The police statement said officers recorded a case of illegal storage of
narcotic substances during "Safe Home" (Безопасный дом), an
operational-preventive operation. [@2] It said the necessary measures were under
way to establish all the circumstances of the case. [@2]
