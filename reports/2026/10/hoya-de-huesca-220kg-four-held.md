A nature-protection patrol of the Huesca Guardia Civil noticed signs of an outdoor cannabis plot
on a hillside in the Hoya de Huesca on 17 October 2026. [@1] Two days later, on 19 October,
officers from the Huesca company dismantled the plot and arrested the people they believed were
responsible. [@1]

On the ground they found 72 flowering cannabis plants and the remains of about 1,000 plants
already cut. [@1] Nearby was a large shelter used for drying, a living area, irrigation tanks,
fertiliser, and equipment including destemmers, fans, a heat cannon and a generator. [@1] La
Vanguardia, citing the same operation, said the people running the site had been living in tents.
[@2]

Officers seized the 72 plants and about 220 kilograms of cannabis buds, which they put at a market
value of about 413,000 euros. [@1] The four men arrested are Albanian, aged 36 to 50, and live in
Barcelona. [@1] They are suspected of cultivating a controlled drug and of belonging to a criminal
group. [@1] The ROCA team of the Huesca company handed them to the duty court in Huesca, which
ordered pre-trial detention without bail. [@1] Posts from Ayerbe, Almudévar, Grañén, Huesca and
Gurrea de Gállego took part, with the nature-protection patrol. [@2]
