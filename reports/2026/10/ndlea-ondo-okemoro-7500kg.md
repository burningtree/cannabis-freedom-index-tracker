On Saturday 17 October 2026, Ondo State NDLEA operatives acting on credible intelligence raided a
cannabis farm in the Okemoro Isinbode camp forest and destroyed 7,500 kilogrammes of skunk growing
on three hectares. [@1,2] Five suspects were arrested and named: Asikpo Ekpeyoung, 43; Solomon
Okpani, 22; Ubong Friday, 35; Joseph Friday, 22; and Ekenimi Seth, 35. [@1] Officers kept 92 bags
of skunk weighing 1,267 kg, 11 kg of seeds and 3 kg of fresh stems as exhibits, 1,281 kg in
total. [@1,2]

A day earlier in Owo, operatives tracked 53-year-old Okunebe Ndidi to his hideout in Ameyinti and
recovered 25 bags of cannabis weighing 375 kg. [@1,2] The command said the operations showed its
commitment against cultivation, trafficking and distribution across the state. [@1]
