On 25 August 2026, customs officers from Lons-le-Saunier searched a 33-tonne lorry at Dommartin-lès-Cuiseaux and found a little over 22 kg of cannabis. [@1,2] The driver, a 22-year-old Serbian working for his parents' haulage business, was arrested. [@1,2]

At the Chalon-sur-Saône criminal court on 22 October 2026 he admitted transporting the drugs for €2,500. [@1,2] The court sentenced him to 18 months in prison, banned him from French territory for ten years and ordered a €96,346 customs fine matching the value of the load. [@1,2]
