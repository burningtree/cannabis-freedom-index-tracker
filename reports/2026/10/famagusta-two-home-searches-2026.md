Officers from the Narcotics and Anti-Smuggling Directorate searched the home of
H.H.H., a 42-year-old man, in Famagusta, northern Cyprus, at about 12:30 on 29
October 2026. [@2] Narkotik ve Kaçakçılığı Önleme Müdürlüğü is the Turkish name
of the directorate that carried out both searches. [@1] Inside, they found a
hand-rolled cigarette with a burnt tip, believed to contain a synthetic
cannabinoid, and a pair of scissors believed to carry drug residue. [@1] The
items were taken as evidence, and H.H.H. was arrested. [@2]

At about 15:00 the same afternoon, officers from the same directorate searched
another home in the city, where İ.K., a 39-year-old man, lived. [@2] They seized
about 2 grams of methamphetamine and 2 grams of cannabis, which the police
called hint keneviri, the Turkish term for Indian hemp. [@1] Those substances
were also taken as evidence, and İ.K. was arrested. [@2]

Two people were arrested in the two searches. [@1] The Police Press Office gave
the account of both searches. [@2] Police said investigations into both cases
were continuing. [@2]
