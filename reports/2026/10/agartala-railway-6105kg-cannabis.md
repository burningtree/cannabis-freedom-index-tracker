Tripura Railway Police (RPF and GRP) caught one person, Rahul Miah (19), son of Ali Akbar
Miah, on platform no. 1 of Agartala Railway Station — in front of the parking area, between
the RPF post and the pay-and-use toilet — with a bag holding a total of 6.105 kg of suspected
contraband ganja, at about 10 AM on 28 October 2026. [@1] The operation was carried out by SI
Pankaj Biswas and other Agartala GRPS staff; Miah, originally of Bagma Biren Colony (R.K. Pur,
Gomati district), was residing at Sonamura Purb Para in Sepahijala district. [@1]

The destination market value of the seized ganja was estimated at approximately Rs 3 lakh on
the black market. [@1]

Railway Police arrested him and seized the contraband, registering Agartala GRPS case no.
2026GRP075 under sections 20(b)(ii)(B)/29 of the NDPS Act, 1985. [@1] Inspector Raju Bhowmik,
Officer in Charge of Agartala Railway Police Station, described the operation to the media
and said such operations against illegal contraband would continue. [@1]

A second account of the day's work reports GRP seizing a combined 16.275 kg of ganja worth
Rs 8 lakh in two operations at the station, which would include this 6.105 kg seizure. [@2]
