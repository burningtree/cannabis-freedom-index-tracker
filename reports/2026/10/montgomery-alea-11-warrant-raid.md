Officers on the Alabama Drug Enforcement Task Force executed 11 narcotics search
warrants simultaneously at locations across Montgomery on Tuesday 27 October
2026. [@2] The searches ended a five-month investigation led by the Alabama Law
Enforcement Agency's State Bureau of Investigation and the Alabama Attorney
General's Office. [@2] Rodriquez Oates, 39, was arrested and charged with one
count of trafficking marijuana. [@2] Leon Howard III, 42, was arrested and
charged with one count of second-degree receiving stolen property. [@2] Both
were booked into the Montgomery County Jail. [@2]

Officers seized 15,932 grams of marijuana, or about 35 pounds. [@1] Officers
also took 516 grams of MDMA, or ecstasy, equivalent to about 3,400 pills. [@2]
They seized 243 grams of synthetic marijuana, commonly called spice. [@2] A
further 33 grams were cocaine and crack cocaine. [@2] Detectives found five
firearms, including one confirmed stolen in Montgomery. [@2] They recovered
three AR-style upper receivers with magazines and ammunition. [@2] The operation
also yielded $14,157.93 in US currency. [@2]

State Bureau of Investigation agents assigned to the task force, the agency's
Metro Area Crime Suppression Unit, SWAT team and aviation unit also took part.
[@2] Local partners included SWAT teams from the Montgomery, Prattville and
Wetumpka police departments and sheriff's offices in Montgomery, Autauga,
Chilton and Lee counties. [@2] Agents from Homeland Security Investigations, the
Drug Enforcement Administration and the Federal Bureau of Investigation also
helped. [@2] ALEA said additional charges and arrests may follow and that the
investigation remains open. [@2]
