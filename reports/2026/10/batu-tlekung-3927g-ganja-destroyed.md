Police in Batu destroyed 3,927.13 grams of cannabis, almost four kilograms, at
the Tlekung landfill in Junrejo district, Batu city, on Thursday 29 October
2026. [@1] Police have named a 33-year-old farmer from Ngroto village in Pujon
district, Malang regency, identified by the initials DK, as a suspect in the
case. [@1] Batu police chief AKBP Panji Gedhe Prabawa led the destruction. [@1]
Police described the destruction as a form of transparency meant to ensure drug
evidence is destroyed under the law. [@1]

The case was uncovered by Satresnarkoba, the narcotics unit of Batu police. [@1]
Police said it began with a shipment of about five kilograms of cannabis sent
through a jasa ekspedisi, a courier and freight-forwarding service. [@1] Police
said they received information about a suspicious parcel on Monday 12 October
2026. [@2] They detained DK, a resident of Pujon in Malang regency, on Tuesday
13 October 2026. [@2] The parcel held five packets of one kilogram each, which
police valued at Rp25 million and said were meant for distribution. [@2] DK told
investigators he bought the cannabis through social media from a man identified
as H, who is on the police wanted list. [@3] H is suspected to be in Padang,
West Sumatra. [@3]

Police accused DK under Article 114(2) or Article 111(2) of Indonesia's 2009
Narcotics Law, charges that carry six to 20 years in prison. [@1] Police said
the investigation was continuing before the case file was sent to prosecutors.
[@2] The police chief urged residents of Batu to report suspected drug use or
dealing. [@1]
