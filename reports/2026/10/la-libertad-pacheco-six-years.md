On 28 October 2026 El Mundo reported that the Second Sentencing Court of Santa Tecla had sentenced Emilio Adonay Pacheco Arévalo, 22, for possession and keeping of drugs. [@1] The paper quoted a post that day from Centros Judiciales El Salvador, which reports the courts of the Supreme Court of Justice. [@1]

The court said two police officers stopped him on the afternoon of 30 April 2026 at kilometre 11 of the road to Puerto de La Libertad. [@1] A search found 84.4 grams of cannabis. [@1] The court found him criminally responsible and imposed six years in prison. [@1]

The headline says he was condemned to 22 years. [@1] The quoted court post and the body of the article say six. [@1]

The same article then describes another case, in Acajutla, which is not part of this report. [@1]

The photograph, credited to the courts, shows Pacheco seated in a courtroom. [@1]
