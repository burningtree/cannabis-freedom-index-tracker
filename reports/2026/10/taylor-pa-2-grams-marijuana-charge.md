Taylor police arrested a borough man on Monday 26 October 2026 after the U.S.
Marshals Service, the federal agency that pursues fugitives, alerted them to an
active felony warrant. [@1] At about 12:30 p.m., officers took Joseph Austin,
29, into custody at a Keyser Avenue residence. [@1] Once at the police
department, officers searched Austin's backpack. [@1] They found inside the
backpack a plastic bag labeled “simply herb” containing about 2 grams of
marijuana, according to a complaint. [@1] Austin, of 27 William St., was held
for out-of-state extradition — the process of handing a suspect to another state
— and was charged with possession of marijuana. [@1]

The search that found the marijuana took place at the police station, after
Austin's arrest on the warrant. [@1] A preliminary hearing, at which a district
judge decides whether the charge should go forward, is scheduled for 10 a.m. on
Dec. 9 before District Judge Laura Turlip. [@1]
