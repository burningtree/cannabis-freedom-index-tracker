Officers from several areas of the Jefatura Departamental Concordia carried out
a saturation operation at different points in the city on the afternoon of
Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1] The operation left several men detained and at
least 45 wrappers of cocaine seized, as well as doses of marijuana. [@1] The
deployment produced three positive procedures with specialist personnel and the
courts involved. [@1] The procedures were part of a preventive operation by
different units of the Entre Ríos police to reinforce police presence and detect
possible narcomenudeo, or street-level drug dealing, in different parts of
Concordia. [@1]

At Sargento Cabral and Cortada 51, Grupo Especial Concordia officers surprised a
man who tried to hide items in his clothing when he noticed the police. [@1]
During the pat-down, officers detected several wrappers that gave off the
characteristic smell of marijuana. [@1] After the formal search, the wrappers
containing the substance were seized. [@1]

At Las Heras and Boulevard Yuquerí, officers saw two men apparently exchanging
small items. [@1] A narcotics detection dog named Mali gave a positive alert.
[@1] With judicial authorization, a search seized 20 wrappers containing
cocaine. [@1] In the Villa Busti neighborhood, a man threw a bundle and tried to
flee when he saw the officers. [@1] He was quickly intercepted and restrained by
officers. [@1] Officers checked the discarded items and found 25 wrappers that
tested positive for cocaine. [@1] In total, the operation seized at least 45
wrappers of cocaine and an unspecified number of wrappers of marijuana. [@1]

Prosecutors Martín Núñez and Luciana Musuliotis ordered the detention of the men
involved, who were placed at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
