The Arga Makmur District Court in Bengkulu read verdicts in two cannabis cases
on 29 October 2026. [@1] M. Santri bin Ujang Sarkawi was sentenced to 10 months
in prison and three months of rehabilitation in case 143/Pid.Sus/2026/PN Agm.
[@1] Kiki Juliansyah bin Ali Nulhakim received the same 10-month term and three
months of rehabilitation in case 144/Pid.Sus/2026/PN Agm. [@1] Santri had
obtained two packets of ganja — Indonesian for cannabis — from a man named Widi
Herianto, gave one to Kiki and kept the other for himself. [@1] Kiki ordered the
cannabis through Santri, transferred Rp100,000 to a Dana e-wallet number Santri
gave him, and collected it at Santri's house on 20 April 2026. [@1]

When officers arrested and searched Kiki, they found two rolled joints and two
small packets of cannabis weighing 2.37 grams net. [@1] Both men were convicted
of using Category I narcotics for themselves. [@1] The panel was led by Carolina
Taruli Vienna. [@1] The panel had chosen the self-use charge under Article
127(1)(a) of Indonesia's 2009 narcotics law. [@1] It first pursued the
restorative-justice mechanism allowed by Article 204 of the 2025 criminal
procedure code. [@1]

The National Narcotics Agency (BNN) office in Bengkulu city took the role of the
victim party. [@1] The mediation produced a written peace agreement dated 10
September 2026. [@1] The agreement committed the defendants to medical and
social rehabilitation. [@1] BNN assessments diagnosed hallucinogen-use disorders
in both and recommended three months of inpatient rehabilitation. [@1]

The court counted the time both had spent in arrest and detention against their
sentences and ordered them to stay in custody. [@1] It treated the
restorative-justice agreement, their lack of prior convictions and their remorse
as mitigating factors. [@1]
