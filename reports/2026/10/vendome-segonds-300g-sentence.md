On 20 October 2026 the judicial court in Blois handed down sentences in a
Vendôme cannabis case dating to a March 2022 raid. [@1]

Axel Segonds, 29, described as the main figure, received three and a half years
in prison with a committal warrant, a 5,000 euro fine and a ten-year ban on
holding weapons. [@1] A 25-year-old co-defendant was sentenced to three years
with eighteen months suspended on two years' probation; La Nouvelle République
said the firm portion had already been served. [@1] Three other men were given
two years with simple suspension and 1,500 euro fines, and a woman convicted
only of drug use was fined 350 euros. [@1]

When the network was broken up in Vendôme in March 2022, investigators seized
three bars of cannabis weighing 300 g in total, a semi-automatic pistol in 7.65
calibre with ammunition, and a machete. [@1] Six people had been due to answer
before the court on 15 September 2026; the prosecutor had then sought six years
against the main suspect before deliberation was set for 20 October. [@1,2]

Fréquence 3 reported on 22 September that the prosecutor had asked for six years
against the principal suspect in the Vendôme traffic dismantled in 2022 and that
a decision was expected on 20 October. [@2]
