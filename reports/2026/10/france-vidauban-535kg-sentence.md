On Saturday 24 October 2026 customs officers from the Fréjus brigade checked a Spanish-plated
Renault Clio hire car at the Vidauban Sud rest area on the A8 motorway in Var. [@1,2] The lone
driver, a 20-year-old Spanish national, said he was travelling from Spain to Italy to visit his
girlfriend. [@1,2]

In the boot, officers found a sports bag and a suitcase holding ten heat-sealed plastic pouches
with 5.35 kg of cannabis herb and 10 g of cannabis resin; customs valued the load at 53,610
euros. [@1,2]

The man appeared in immediate trial at Draguignan criminal court on Tuesday 27 October. [@1,2] He
described himself as a seasonal cook who was unemployed and said he had agreed to transport the
drugs for money promised on arrival; the court treated that as his account only. [@1,2] Judges
sentenced him to one year in prison with a committal warrant, five years' exclusion from French
territory, a customs fine of 53,610 euros matching the seized value, and confiscation of two
mobile phones; the drugs were to be destroyed. [@1,2]
