Police at the advance post of Kabaléya district in Kindia arrested a motorcycle
rider during a surveillance operation. [@2] The National Police said the rider
was carrying more than 300 kilograms of suspected chanvre indien, the French
term for cannabis. [@3] Officers also seized two motorcycles during the same
intervention, according to the Directorate General of the National Police. [@2]
The National Police said the operation formed part of efforts by the security
services to intensify the fight against drug trafficking across Guinean
territory. [@1]

The suspect was handed to the regional police directorate in Kindia after his
arrest. [@3] La Guinée info said he was still to be presented to the competent
judicial authorities. [@2] Guinee7 also reported that he was still to be
presented for the procedure to continue. [@3] Mosaiqueguinee reported that he
had already been presented to the judicial authorities. [@1]
