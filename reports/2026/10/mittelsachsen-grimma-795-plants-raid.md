On 27 October 2026, the Dresden customs investigation office, on orders from the
Chemnitz public prosecutor, searched four addresses in Grimma, Rochlitz and Erlau in
Saxony. [@1,2] Inside they found an indoor cannabis plantation with 795 plants and
about 4.7 kg of marijuana already harvested. [@1,2]

Two men were arrested and remanded in custody. [@1,2] Investigators say a group of
seven men aged 23 to 60 is suspected of running the site in a serious breach of
Germany's cannabis consumption law. [@1,2] Officers also seized grow lights,
ventilation gear, two air rifles, shotgun shells, an anti-aircraft shell, a drone and
electronic devices. [@1]

The customs service said the 4.7 kg alone had an estimated street value of about
47,000 euros. [@1,2] Enquiries by customs and prosecutors were continuing. [@1,2]
