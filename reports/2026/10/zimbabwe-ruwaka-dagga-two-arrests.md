Acting on a tip-off, Zimbabwe Republic Police officers searched a field in
Ruwaka Village, Chimanimani, and found 770 dagga plants, known locally as
mbanje, growing alongside a maize crop. [@1,2] They also recovered processed
dagga. [@1,2]

Officers arrested 73-year-old Lovemore Zambasira and 38-year-old Liberty
Maunganidze on Monday 26 October. [@1,2] The pair were in custody and expected
in court on charges of unlawful cultivation of dagga. [@1] Police said growing
dagga without a licence remains a criminal offence and that operations against
hidden plantations would continue across the country. [@1]
