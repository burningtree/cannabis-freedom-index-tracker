Kaup police arrested two men for allegedly selling ganja to tourists at
Yardi Beach in Uliyargoli village, seizing 5.71 kg of the contraband valued
at Rs 2.85 lakh; Rs 225 in cash and two mobile phones were also recovered in
the October 27 operation. [@1] [@2]

The arrested men were Areef, 38, of Vinayanagar in Belapu village of Kaup
taluk, and Akeef, 34, of Hoode in Paduthonse village of Udupi taluk. [@1] A
case was registered and further investigation was underway. [@2]

Kaup Circle Inspector Azmath Ali had received credible information about the
beach sales and led the raid. [@1]
