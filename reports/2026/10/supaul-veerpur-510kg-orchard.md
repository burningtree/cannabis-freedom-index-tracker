Jawans of the 45th battalion's Fatehpur border post moved on a tip-off that
suspicious goods lay hidden in an orchard near Fatehpur village, about 700
metres inside Indian territory from border pillar 204. [@1,2] A thorough search of
a mango orchard turned up 13 bundles of ganja weighing about 510 kg. [@1,2]

Though the surrounding area was also searched, no suspect was caught at the
spot. [@1,2] After the legal formalities, the SSB handed the consignment to
Veerpur police station for further action. [@1,2] The team included assistant
sub-inspector Bhupendra Singh and three other jawans; acting commandant
Jagdish Sharma said border vigilance continues. [@1,2]
