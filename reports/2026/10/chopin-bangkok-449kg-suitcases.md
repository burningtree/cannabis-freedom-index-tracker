Customs and Tax Service officers from the Mazovian Customs and Tax Office in Warsaw checked baggage
at Chopin Airport after a flight from Bangkok and selected two suitcases belonging to a 33-year-old
Greek national. [@1,2] He said he did not know the codes to open them. [@1,2]

Once opened, officers found four vacuum packages of green plant material wrapped in orange tape;
field and laboratory tests confirmed marijuana. [@1,2] They seized 44.9 kilograms, valued by KAS at
more than 2.2 million zlotys on the black market. [@1,2]

The man was arrested. A court ordered three months' pre-trial detention. [@1,2] Importing a
significant quantity of narcotics carries three to 20 years in prison and a fine; the Mazovian
office is investigating under the Warszawa-Ochota district prosecutor. [@1,2]
