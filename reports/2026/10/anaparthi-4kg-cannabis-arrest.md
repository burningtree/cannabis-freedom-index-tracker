Anaparthi police arrested a young man over the illegal transport of cannabis.
[@1] He was Kollati Sri Teja, 19, known as Diaper, a resident of Anaparthi
village opposite GBR College. [@1] Police caught him carrying the cannabis,
known as ganja, in a bag. [@1] They had received a tip that cannabis was being
moved along a canal road in the village. [@1] The information came at about 5.30
pm on 28 October 2026. [@1] Sub-inspector V. Sujatha and her staff carried out
the check with witnesses present. [@1]

Officers seized about four kilograms of cannabis, valued by police at about
200,000 rupees (2 lakh), and nine motorcycles identified as stolen in various
areas. [@1] Questioned over the cannabis case, Teja told police he had stolen
nine motorcycles within the limits of various police stations. [@1] Officers
recovered the motorcycles on the details he gave. [@1] The thefts came to light
during the cannabis investigation and became a talking point locally. [@1]

Anaparthi police registered a case and took further legal steps. [@1] They said
investigation was continuing into the thefts linked to the recovered motorcycles
and other aspects. [@1]
