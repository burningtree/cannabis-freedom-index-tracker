On Wednesday 28 October 2026 officers of the Amambay departmental anti-drug unit searched the
rural property Estancia Pica Pau, in the colony of Cerro Kuatiá, Capitán Bado. [@1] They cut and
burned two hectares of cannabis that was at harvest. [@1] In the same part of the property they
found about 2,000 kilograms drying and stored, and burned that as well, with a rough camp and the
gear used to run the crop. [@1]

The work was done under SUMAR, the action plan against drug abuse, and was overseen by the
Amambay specialised anti-drug prosecution. [@1] Amambay Urgente described the same search, the two
hectares, the 2,000 kilograms and the burned camp, and said what was done was turned over to the
courts. [@2] Neither account named an arrest. [@1,2]
