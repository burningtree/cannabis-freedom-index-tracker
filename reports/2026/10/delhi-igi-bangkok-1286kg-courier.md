Delhi Customs officers stopped an Indian passenger arriving from Bangkok on Air India flight
AI-2335 at Terminal 3 of IGI Airport on 6 October 2026 after targeted profiling. [@1,2] He
was diverted at the Green Channel, and examination of his metallic silver trolley bag revealed
13 polythene pouches of suspected ganja weighing 12,859.5 grams, valued at about Rs 4.5 crore.
[@1,2]

The passenger was arrested, with the arrest recorded under Section 43(B) of the NDPS Act and
the substance seized under Section 43(A); a case was registered under Sections 8, 20, 23 and
29. [@2] Further investigation is under way. [@1,2]
