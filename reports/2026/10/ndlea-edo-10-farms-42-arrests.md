The Edo State Command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA)
arrested 42 suspected drug traffickers in September 2026. [@1] Mitchell Ofoyeju,
the agency's commander in Edo, presented the figures in the command's monthly
operational results. [@1] Twenty of those arrested were men and 22 were women,
which Ofoyeju said showed growing involvement of women in drug trafficking in
the state. [@1] The command destroyed 10 cannabis farms covering 3.69 hectares,
with an estimated yield of 9,248.2575 kg. [@1] Two farms were at Monday Camp in
Ovia South-West and two in Igwe-Oke Community Forest in Owan East. [@2] One farm
was in Okagboro Forest and three in Ukporwoya Forest, both in Owan West, and two
were at Eko Camp in Illushi, Esan South-East. [@2]

The command seized 271.92348 kg of illicit substances, including 245.29 kg of
cannabis sativa, 25.97 kg of psychotropic substances, 0.66 kg of methamphetamine
and 62.6 litres of codeine syrup. [@1] The command put its September total at
9,520 kg of illicit substances once the destroyed farms were included. [@2]

Gospel Mudiaga Odeh, known as Omega, narrowly escaped arrest and was declared
wanted. [@2] His 26-year-old salesgirl, Ojugo Mercy, was arrested with 989 grams
of Loud, 13 grams of Colorado and 83 grams of methamphetamine. [@2] He fled in a
black Toyota Matrix, which was recovered with 65 grams of cannabis, N1.16
million and 55 Ethiopian birr inside. [@2] Ofoyeju said the operations were
intelligence-led and mandated by NDLEA chairman Mohamed Buba Marwa. [@1]
