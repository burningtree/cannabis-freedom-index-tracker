Drug-control officers from the Tula region Interior Ministry, working with
Belev police, searched a 32-year-old local man's flat in Belev on
16 October 2026, the Tula news wire reported. [@1] They seized plant
material and more than 100 cannabis plants. [@1]

Expert testing at the regional forensic centre identified the dried material
as marijuana weighing at least 459 grams, a large amount under Russian
law. [@2] The man told officers he had grown and stored the plants only for
personal use and not for sale. [@1]

Investigators opened a criminal case under part 2 of article 228 of the
Criminal Code for large-scale possession. [@2] A judge ordered him not to
leave his place of residence while the case continues. [@1]
