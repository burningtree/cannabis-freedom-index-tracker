From 19 to 25 October 2026, the Police Regional Office Cordillera ran 12 anti-drug operations
across the region, arresting eight suspects and seizing P2.7 million in illegal drugs. [@1] The
week's work mixed five buy-busts with five marijuana eradication operations and two police
responses. [@1]

Authorities destroyed 1,150 marijuana plants and 1,200 seedlings, and seized 20 kilograms of
marijuana leaves and fruiting tops alongside a small amount of shabu. [@1] Benguet accounted for
P2.68 million of the total, with smaller confiscations in Baguio City, Abra and Kalinga. [@1]
Regional police urged communities to keep reporting suspicious activity. [@1]
