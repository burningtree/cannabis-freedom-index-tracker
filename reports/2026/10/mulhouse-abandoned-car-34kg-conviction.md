A 40-year-old man was convicted by the Mulhouse court on Wednesday 28 October
2026 over 34 kg of cannabis found in an abandoned car in the city a year
earlier. [@1] He had been convicted twice before, in 2009 and in 2022. [@1]

Police from Mulhouse's brigade des stupéfiants, the city's anti-drug squad, were
on an operation for another case on 7 October 2025 when they found a large
quantity of drugs in the Bourtzwiller district. [@1] The drugs were in a Citroën
R Cross parked on rue de Vittel. [@1] A drug dog alerted at the vehicle, and
officers saw transparent bags sticking out of a sack in the boot. [@1] The
parquet, France's public prosecutor's office, gave permission, and the officers
opened the boot. [@1]

The inquiry identified the man from fingerprints left on a soda can and led to
his arrest in Montbéliard. [@1] The case took a full year to reach the Mulhouse
court. [@1]
