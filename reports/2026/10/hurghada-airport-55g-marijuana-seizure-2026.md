Customs officers at Hurghada International Airport found plant material in the
luggage of a passenger arriving from Turkey before he left the customs area.
[@1] The passenger had flown in from Sabiha Gökçen International Airport in
Turkey. [@2] A hand search of the luggage found a black bag hidden inside. [@1]
The bag held a green herb that customs suspected was marijuana. [@2] Customs put
the weight of the seized plant material at 55.5 grams. [@2]

While officers inspected arriving passengers, customs officer Umm Hashim Amin
suspected the man as he passed through the customs gate. [@2] X-ray screening
confirmed the suspicion. [@2] The operation was led by Hisham Mahmoud Sharara,
director of the airport customs office. [@2]

Customs took legal measures, filed seizure report No. 9 of 2026 and held the
seized material. [@2] Officers Shireen Mohamed and Ahmed Omar Abdel Azim
inventoried and sealed it under customs security chief Abdel Mohsen El-Shaeny.
[@2] The seizure followed instructions from Ahmed Amawy, head of the Customs
Authority, and Atef El-Sayed, head of the Red Sea and Southern Region customs
administration, to tighten control at airports. [@2]
