Excise Enforcement officers arrested Vaidya Praveen, a 25-year-old from
Narayankhed, after intercepting him near Pedda Shankarampet in Medak. [@1]
Praveen was allegedly transporting ganja, the local name for cannabis, on a
motorcycle after procuring it from other areas. [@1]

The team seized 4.1 kg of ganja and the motorcycle. [@1] Officials estimated the
ganja to be worth ₹2.5 lakh (250,000 rupees). [@1]

Assistant Commissioner D Gayatri said officials handed Praveen over to the
Narayankhed Excise Station for further action. [@1]
