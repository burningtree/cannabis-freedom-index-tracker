The DCI arrested four persons of interest and seized narcotics, illicit
alcohol and other items in coordinated operations across Kisii, Kitui and
Kajiado counties, per a DCI statement issued on Tuesday, October 20, 2026.
[@1]

In Riosiri, Kisii County, detectives acting on a public tip-off raided a
suspect's house, unearthing 1.63 kg of cannabis, 70 litres of chang'aa, 14
packets of Supermatch and nine of Oris cigarettes suspected counterfeit,
plus cigarette-rolling materials. [@1]

Along the Thika-Garissa Highway in Kitui, officers at the Kanyonyoo
roadblock intercepted a Nairobi-to-Garissa passenger bus, recovering 30 rolls
of suspected bhang and Rizla papers from a backpack; investigators believed
the consignment was bought in Gatitu, Thika, for sale at Ukasi Market in
Mwingi East. [@1]

In Kajiado South, DCI detectives with Kimana Police Station officers
intercepted a white Toyota Sienta on the Oloitoktok-Emali Road at Kimana on
a tip-off, recovering four sacks of suspected bhang. [@1]
