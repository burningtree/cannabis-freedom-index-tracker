Ruben Flutey, 34, was sentenced to 10 years and five months in prison at the
Wellington District Court in October 2026. [@1] He was convicted on 18 charges,
including importing and supplying methamphetamine and participating in organised
crime. [@2] The charges also included supplying cannabis, ecstasy and ketamine.
[@1] Police called him a pivotal player in the Wairarapa organised drug supply
chain. [@3]

His arrest followed an investigation by the Wairarapa CIB into methamphetamine
distribution between March 2023 and December 2024. [@1] Detective Senior
Sergeant Phil Skoglund said the network supplied millions of dollars' worth of
methamphetamine across the country. [@1] Police seized nearly $200,000 worth of
assets in a series of search warrants, including drugs, cash, a classic car,
motorbikes and jet skis. [@1] Four of Flutey's associates had also been
sentenced, while another four were due in court in November and December. [@2]

Skoglund said the investigation and sentencing were a 'major blow' to organised
crime in the Wairarapa region. [@1] "Methamphetamine, and organised crime,
contributes significant harm to our communities and destroys families," he said.
[@1] He said there was no place in the region or country for behaviour like this
and that offenders would be held to account. [@1]
