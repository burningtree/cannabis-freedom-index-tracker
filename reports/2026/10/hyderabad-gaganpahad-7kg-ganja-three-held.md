Excise police arrested three friends in Hyderabad on the morning of Saturday 31
October 2026 and seized seven kilograms of ganja from them. [@1] The State
Excise Task Force team, led by Inspector Nagaraju, intercepted them at
Gaganpahad as they prepared to hand over the seven kilograms to local peddlers.
[@1] Those arrested were Chevula Mahesh Kumar, 34, an HR professional at Bharat
Fin Machine in Bengaluru, and Gampa Prashanth, 21, and Dandu Manoj Kumar, 24,
both from Nellore district. [@1] The Telugu-language Janavaani named the same
three men. [@3] It reported that the seized ganja and the car were handed over
to the Rajendranagar excise station. [@3]

Officers also seized the hired car used to carry the ganja and the suspects'
mobile phones. [@1] Deccan Chronicle put the value of the seven kilograms at
about Rs 3.5 lakh, or 350,000 rupees. [@2]

According to excise officials, Mahesh Kumar financed the operation and the three
friends hired a car to travel to Araku, near Visakhapatnam. [@1] They allegedly
bought the ganja there at Rs 5,000 a kilogram and planned to sell it in
Hyderabad to local dealers at Rs 50,000 a kilogram. [@1]
