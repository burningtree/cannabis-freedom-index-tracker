Armed Police Force officers seized a Bolero and a motorcycle carrying cannabis
at the Gagan Khola bridge in Sukhipur municipality-10, Siraha. [@2] The
interception came at about 12:30 am on 1 October 2026 after a special tip-off,
when a joint team from the force's No. 7 battalion headquarters in Siraha and
its Golbazar and Sukhipur security posts followed the Bolero (Ba 14 Cha 294) and
an Apache motorcycle (Sa 6 Pa 4859). [@2] The cannabis weighed 1,102.655 kg, and
the drivers fled leaving the vehicle and motorcycle behind. [@1] Armed Police
Force Superintendent Suman Jung Thapa said the load was packed in 26 sacks and
put it at 11 quintals, 2 kg and 655 grams. [@2]

Seeing the police vehicle, the drivers and other people in the vehicles
abandoned them on the bridge and ran off. [@2] The cannabis was found loaded in
the vehicles. [@2] Police said they were searching for the people who fled. [@2]
The seized goods were to be handed to the relevant agency for necessary action.
