Gendarmes arrested three people, two men and a woman aged 26 to 46, at a hangar
in Redessan on 26 October 2026. [@1] It formed part of a judicial inquiry into
drug trafficking. [@1] Officers moved in at around 6am. [@1] All three were
placed in police custody (garde à vue). [@2]

Inside, officers found 36 cannabis plants growing in an elaborate makeshift
greenhouse protected by video surveillance and security equipment. [@1] They
also found €6,000 in cash. [@1] Midi Libre put the total value of the
merchandise at nearly €50,000. [@1] The 46-year-old suspect, from the
Alpes-Maritimes, rented the hangar and grew the plants himself. [@1] Midi Libre
described him as the suspected organiser of the network. [@1]

InfOccitanie reported that the network operated in the suburbs of Nîmes and in
departments bordering the Gard. [@3] All three were brought before the Nîmes
judicial court on 29 October over unauthorised production, possession,
transport, acquisition and sale of narcotics, and laundering. [@2]
