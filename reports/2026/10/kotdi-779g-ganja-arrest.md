Kotdi police in Rajasthan's Pratapgarh district seized 779 grams of ganja and
arrested one man. [@1] The arrest was made on 26 October 2026, when a patrol on
the dirt road between Shauli and Kotdi stopped a man who turned back after
seeing the police team and searched his bag. [@2] He told police he was
Laxmansingh son of Gordhansingh Rajput, 48, a resident of Shauli in the Kotdi
police station area. [@2]

Police registered a case against him under section 8/20 of the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, India's main drug law, and began
investigating. [@2] The patrol was part of Operation Trinetra, a Rajasthan
Police campaign against illegal narcotics trafficking in Pratapgarh district.
[@2] Station officer Thakraram led the team, under district police chief Vishal
Jangid and the supervision of additional superintendent Jaydev Siyag and Arnod
circle officer Chandrashekhar Paliwal. [@2] The team also included head
constable Harishkumar and constables Rakesh and Jaypalsingh. [@2]
