In its operational report for 7 to 13 October, released on Wednesday 14
October, the National People's Army said combined detachments working with
the security services had foiled attempts to introduce 4 quintals and 82
kg of treated kif from the Moroccan borders and arrested 47 traffickers in
operations across the military regions. [@1] The same week also yielded
368,937 psychotropic tablets. [@1]

The report framed the week as sustained counter-terrorism and organised
crime work; alongside the drug figures it lists seven arrested terror-group
supporters, three illegal gold miners shot dead, 385 people held in the
south, and 291 blocked clandestine departures at sea. [@1]
