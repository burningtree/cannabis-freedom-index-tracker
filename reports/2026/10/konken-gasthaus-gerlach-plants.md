In early October 2026 officers of Polizeipräsidium Westpfalz and the Landeskriminalamt searched the
empty former Gasthaus Gerlach in Konken, Kusel district, after the local energy supplier reported
tampered cables spotted when the street lost power. [@1,2]

A multi-storey lighting and air system fed a professional indoor cannabis plantation; Technisches
Hilfswerk crews helped dismantle it. [@1,2]

On later inquiry the Westpfalz police press office told Die Rheinpfalz that officers had secured 640
plants, 632 cuttings and about 50 kilograms of harvested buds, with an estimated street-sale value
near one million euros. [@1] Who built and ran the grow was still under investigation when those
figures were published. [@1,2]
