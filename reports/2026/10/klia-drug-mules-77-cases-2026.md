Bukit Aman Narcotics Crime Investigation Department director Datuk Hussein Omar Khan said on 26
October 2026 that 77 cases linked to drug-mule syndicates at Kuala Lumpur International Airport
(KLIA) were recorded between January and 15 October, with 93 people arrested. [@1,2] The cases
involved 2,526.28 kg of drugs with an estimated value of RM202.76 million. [@1,2]

Cannabis accounted for the overwhelming majority: 2,407.27 kg across 64 cases, alongside 77.84 kg
of methamphetamine, 22.11 kg of ketamine, 15.59 kg of cocaine and 3.48 kg of a ketamine-cannabis
mixture. [@1] Of the 93 arrested, British nationals were the largest group at 26, followed by 23
Malaysians and 11 Singaporeans; the rest came from Japan, France, Hong Kong, Indonesia, India,
Ireland, Portugal, Germany, the Netherlands, Greece, Sweden, Slovakia, the United States, Brazil,
Uganda, Zambia, Thailand and China. [@1]

Of the 77 cases, 41 were handled by airport auxiliary police and four by the border control
agency, 25 came from police and narcotics-division actions, and seven follow-up operations led to
12 more arrests and 571.59 kg of cannabis flowers seized. [@1] Two days later the home minister
announced more stringent inspections at KLIA to combat the smuggling. [@2]
