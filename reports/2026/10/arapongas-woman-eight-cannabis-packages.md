The case was registered in Arapongas on the morning of Friday 30 October 2026.
[@1] Military Police were called to the Hospital Norte Paranaense (Honpar) at
about 11:23, after a woman was admitted with severe stomach pain and said she
had swallowed drugs. [@1] She told officers she had swallowed the packages to
carry them to her partner, who is in prison. [@3] The prison is the
Penitenciária Estadual de Londrina (PEL), the state prison in Londrina. [@1] The
attempt to transport the drugs ended with the woman in hospital. [@3]

When she could not expel the material naturally, doctors at Honpar performed
surgery. [@2] According to the Military Police, the surgery removed eight
packages of cannabis, which officers seized. [@1] The drugs were sent to the
22nd Police Subdivision (SDP) in Arapongas and presented to the competent
authority. [@1]

The case was presented to police authorities for legal measures. [@3] The case
was to be investigated by the authorities. [@1]
