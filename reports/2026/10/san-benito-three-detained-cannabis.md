Three people were detained during a search for suspected retail drug dealing in
San Benito, in Paraná department, Entre Ríos. [@1] Officers of the Entre Ríos
police seized packets of cocaine and cannabis in the raid. [@1] Besides the
three detentions, officers identified 13 other people linked to the procedure.
[@1] The search was carried out on the orders of Guarantee Court No. 4, under
Judge Julián Vergara, with the specialised prosecution unit led by prosecutor
Santiago Alfieri. [@2]

During the search of the property, investigators seized two packets of cannabis
weighing 4.2 grams. [@1] They also seized 11 packets of cocaine with a total
weight of 7.1 grams. [@1] Officers took $1,042,600 in cash, mobile phones, a
security camera and other items they treated as relevant to the investigation.
[@1] All of the material was left with the judicial authorities handling the
case. [@1]

The operation was led by the Operations Division of the Dangerous Drugs
Directorate, with a tactical team from the Special Operations Company of the
public-security directorate. [@1] The investigation was framed under provincial
Law No. 10.566, which regulates the pursuit of narcomenudeo, small-scale drug
dealing, in Entre Ríos. [@1] The Dangerous Drugs Directorate said further
procedures aimed at investigating alleged drug sales were continuing in
different parts of the province. [@1]
