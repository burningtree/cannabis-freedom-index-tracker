The raid was near Yash dhaba in Savarna's fields on the Shegaon–Khamgaon road,
and the other items were two two-wheelers and two mobile phones. [@1]

The action was carried out under Mission Parivartan, a Buldhana police drive
against illegal drug sales, on a confidential tip. [@1] District Superintendent
Nilesh Tambe directed the operation, with Additional Superintendents Amol
Gaikwad and Piyush Jagtap guiding it and crime-branch inspector Hemant Thakare
leading the team. [@1] Assistant inspector Yashoda Kanse and other local
crime-branch staff took part. [@1] A case was registered against both accused.
