The criminal court in Saint-Nazaire (tribunal correctionnel) gave a detainee
three extra months in prison on 13 October 2026. [@1] The detainee was 37 and
had six convictions on his record. [@1] The offence was riding a scooter under
the influence of cannabis. [@1] He had contested the facts before admitting
them. [@1] He appeared by videoconference from custody. [@1] He was two years
into a three-year sentence. [@1]

Gendarmes stopped him at 15:45 on 28 May 2024 in Montoir-de-Bretagne and found
him positive for drugs at the handlebars of a borrowed scooter. [@1] He told the
court he was coming home from work and had stopped smoking some time earlier.
[@1] Asked why he was in prison, he replied that he no longer really knew. [@1]

Sentence reductions had brought his release within about three months. [@1] He
had said he would be free in January 2027, and the extra three months should end
that. [@1] He lowered his head when he heard the sentence and, when it was over,
asked: "C'est bon, on a fini ?" [@1]
