Lalsot police station, in Dausa district of Rajasthan, arrested two people in an
early-morning raid on suspected hideouts. [@3] Officers said the checks were
meant to stop the sale of illegal drugs and liquor. [@1] The action was in the
Sansi settlement of Chandsen village. [@1]

Police said 45-year-old Rajanti Devi, wife of Mukesh Sansi, was found with 270
grams of ganja, cannabis, which they seized before arresting her. [@2] A
separate case was registered against her under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, India's narcotics law. [@3] In the same
settlement, officers arrested 23-year-old Rohit Malawat, son of Raju Malawat.
[@3] From him they seized 384 pavvas of country liquor and 96 pavvas of Royal
Stag English liquor. [@2] A pavva is a small sealed pouch of liquor, and Dainik
Bhaskar put the two liquor counts together at 480 pavvas. [@1] The liquor case
was registered under the Excise Act. [@2]

Station house officer Harlal led the team, with assistant sub-inspector
Amarsingh, head constable Lokendra Singh, constables Lahrilal, Praveen Kumar,
Rajendra Kumar, Girraj and Sumer Singh, and women constables Maujanti and Mansa.
[@3] Police said further action against illegal drugs and liquor in the area
would continue. [@3]
