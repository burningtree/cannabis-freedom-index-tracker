On Tuesday 13 October 2026 a joint team uprooted and burned about 5,000 marijuana seedlings in forest at Sitio Pingew, Proper Tacadang, Kibungan, Benguet. [@1] Pilipino Star Ngayon said the seedlings were worth about 200,000 pesos and that the work fell under the police programme Oplan Ultimate Grass Cutter. [@1]

The team was drawn from Kibungan municipal police, the 1st and 2nd provincial mobile force companies, the provincial drug enforcement and intelligence units, and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency in the Cordillera. [@1] They had found two marijuana plantations there. [@1] No cultivator was caught during the operation. [@1]

The photograph published with the article shows officers with a Kibungan police placard dated 13 October 2026 for Pingew, Proper Tacadang. [@1] That board records site 01 as an estimated 2,000 seedlings on 50 square metres, with a standard drug price of 20,000 pesos. [@1]
