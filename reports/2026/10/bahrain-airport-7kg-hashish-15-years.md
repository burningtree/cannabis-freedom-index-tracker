Bahrain’s First High Criminal Court jailed two Asian women for fifteen years
each and fined them BD10,000 apiece after convicting them of smuggling about
seven kilograms of cannabis resin through Bahrain International Airport. [@1,2]
Customs officers scanning their two suitcases saw high-density images, searched
the bags after both women denied carrying prohibited items, and found the drugs.
[@1,2]

The women told anti-narcotics investigators they had agreed with an organizer to
collect the suitcases in an Asian country and deliver them in Bahrain in
exchange for a paid holiday. [@1,2] The court ordered all defendants deported
after they serve their sentences and confiscated the seized material. [@1,2] A
third woman was exempted from punishment after she helped police identify another
suspect in the network. [@1,2]
