Kumbalagodu police arrested two men from another state for transporting ganja and seized
317 kg of the drug along with an Innova car on Tuesday 13 October 2026. [@1,2] Acting on an
informant's tip under a press note from the Commissioner of Police's office dated 11 October,
officers stopped the rented car the suspects were using to move narcotics from Odisha to
Kerala via Bengaluru. [@1,2]

The seized ganja was valued at Rs 1.78 crore, with a street value of about Rs 3.17 crore.
[@1,2] Police identified the arrested men as Kerala nationals; the operation was led by DCP
Saumyata SK and ACP Satyavati KS. [@1,2]
