Örebro District Court has sentenced a man in his 50s to five years in prison for a gross drug offence, SVT reported on 22 October 2026. [@1] Sveriges Radio, reporting the next morning, said he is from Ulricehamn and that he is to stay in custody until the prison sentence is enforced. [@2]

Police had been watching him when a refrigerated truck delivered several pallets of fabric softener to the company where he worked, in Örebro. [@1] Behind the pallets were seven brown boxes. [@1] A later search found the drugs in the boxes: nearly 100 kg of cannabis. [@1,2]

He admitted receiving the load. [@1] He said a debt was to be repaid by his taking 20 kg of cannabis. [@1] The court did not believe that account. [@1]

SVT's photograph, credited to the police investigation, shows four of the boxes. [@1]
