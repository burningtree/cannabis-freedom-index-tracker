Officers searched a home in the town centre of Pietramelara in the early hours
of 29 October 2026. [@2] The search was carried out by the Pietramelara
Carabinieri station, with support from the operational section of the Capua
company. [@1] The Carabinieri said the search followed an investigation in the
area, information gathered from residents and a series of observation services.
[@1]

Inside a thermos the officers found 15 doses of hashish weighing 42.1 grams in
total. [@1] They also found 160 euros in cash and materials used to package the
drug. [@2] The hashish, the cash and the packaging materials were seized and
made available to the judicial authorities. [@1]

The Carabinieri arrested two people in the act: a 49-year-old man of Romanian
nationality and a 54-year-old woman, both living in the home that was searched.
[@3] Both were placed under house arrest at their home after the formalities.
[@1] The Carabinieri said the responsibility of the arrested people must be
established in later stages of the proceedings, with the presumption of
innocence until a final conviction. [@1]
