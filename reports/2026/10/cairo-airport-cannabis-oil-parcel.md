The Al-Nozha prosecution, headed by counsellor Moataz Zakaria, ordered a foreign national held for
four days while it investigated a postal parcel of cannabis oil at the cargo village of Cairo
International Airport. [@1] Youm7 said he was found with six bottles of the oil hidden in a parcel
of shampoo bottles sent from the United States. [@1]

The General Administration of Postal Parcel Customs, under Ahmed Ismail Goweily, and Egyptian Post
Customs, under Medhat Allam, stopped the shipment with airport anti-drug officers. [@1] Tariff
officer Badour Hamdy Abdel Aziz suspected a parcel from the United States declared as hair- and
hand-care products, weighing 6 kilograms and holding six containers. [@1] Allam sent it to the
airport branch of the anti-drug administration, which said the contents were cannabis oil and
valued them at about 12 million Egyptian pounds. [@1]

Customs drew up seizure report number 5 of 2026, sealed the parcel with customs wire and lead, and
held it for the air import and export customs directorate. [@1] Mwatana published the same customs
account of the six containers, the 6 kilogram weight, the oil finding and the 12 million pound
estimate, without the detention order. [@2]
