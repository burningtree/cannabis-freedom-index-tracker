Police ranks led by a Gazetted Officer ran cannabis eradication exercises at Wiruni and Sandhills
in the Upper Berbice River between 01:40 and 19:10 on Wednesday 21 October 2026. [@1,2] Across two
open savannahs they found seven cultivations covering about 28 acres (around 11.3 hectares),
holding an estimated 310,500 plants between one and six feet tall. [@1,2]

The sites also held seven makeshift camps and six drying sheds, along with about 440 pounds
(about 200 kilograms) of dried cannabis. [@1] Everything was destroyed by fire at the locations;
police put the combined value at GY$165,071,544. [@1]

No arrests were made, and investigations are ongoing. [@1]
