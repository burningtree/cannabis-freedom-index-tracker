Prosecutors and police in Covasna county said a 62-year-old Sfântu Gheorghe man ran an indoor
cannabis grow in 2026 to supply the black market. [@1,2] On 1 October they searched his home
and seized 60 plants in various stages of growth and 5.4 kilograms of cannabis packed in nine
vacuum-sealed bags. [@1,2]

DIICOT Covasna ordered his detention for risk-drug trafficking and, on 2 October, asked the
Covasna court to hold him in preventive arrest for 30 days, according to the police communiqué
carried by AGERPRES. [@2] Digi24 quoted DIICOT saying the same cultivation timeline but did not
name him. [@1]

Romanian law treats risk-drug trafficking seriously; neither outlet reported a sentence. [@1,2]
