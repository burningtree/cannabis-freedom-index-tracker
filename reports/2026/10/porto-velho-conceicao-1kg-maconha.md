Military police in Rondônia arrested Ryan Frazão de Lima and seized drugs, two
precision scales and a vehicle in the south zone of Porto Velho. [@1] The action
followed anonymous reports of possible drug distribution in the state capital.
[@1]

According to the police record, teams from the 5th Battalion were told a man was
using a white Honda HR-V to sell and deliver drugs at different points in the
city. [@1] During a preventive patrol, officers found a vehicle with those
characteristics at the junction of Rua Nova Esperança and Rua Juazeiro. [@1] The
driver was stopped and searched, but nothing illicit was found on him at first.
[@1] A search of the car then found two portions of substances resembling
cocaine and crack, and a precision scale in the boot. [@1]

Questioned by the officers, he admitted the seized material was his and said he
kept other drugs in his apartment on Rua Ceres, in the Conceição neighbourhood.
[@1] With a team from the 9th Battalion, police went to that address. [@1] In a
wardrobe they found a tablet of about one kilogram of a substance resembling
cannabis, known locally as maconha, and another precision scale. [@1]

He was placed under arrest and taken to the police station with the drugs, the
seized equipment and the vehicle for the legal procedures. [@1]
