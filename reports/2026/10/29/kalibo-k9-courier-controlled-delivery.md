On 27 October 2026 personnel from PDEA-Aklan, the PDEA Region 6 seaport interdiction unit, Aklan
provincial police and Kalibo municipal police used a drug-sniffing dog during a routine inspection
at a courier warehouse on Diversion Road in Barangay Nalook, Kalibo. [@1,2] The dog alerted on two
parcels that held about 1.02 kg and 500 g of suspected dried marijuana and 25 disposable vape
cartridges labelled “KALIFA KARIS”, “PEACE” and “Cali Honey” that PDEA said contained suspected
cannabis oil. [@1,2]

After samples went for laboratory examination, PDEA Region 6 ran an authorised controlled delivery.
[@1,2] On 29 October the recipient refused a parcel with about 1.02 kg of marijuana and the 25
cartridges, but accepted a second parcel at 2:46 p.m. in Kalibo. [@1,2] Officers arrested a
suspect known only as alias “Kanggeng” and recovered a brick of suspected marijuana flowering tops
weighing about 505 g, which PDEA valued at PHP 130,000 on the street and PHP 60,000 at standard
drug price. [@1,2] They also took a mobile phone, packaging, a parcel box with the suspect’s name
and a “HAZY KATE” sticker. [@1,2]

PDEA said the case was its first successful K9-assisted courier warehouse interdiction in Western
Visayas that led to a controlled delivery and arrest. [@1,2] The suspect remained in PDEA custody
and faced a possession charge under Section 11 of Republic Act 9165. [@1,2]
