Acting on confidential information, Tripura Police intercepted a vehicle at the Jherjheri
Naka Point in Churaibari, Kadamtala, on the night of 28 October 2026, and searched it after
suspecting contraband on board. [@1,2] Dharmanagar Sub-Divisional Police Officer Jayanta
Karmakar, DCM Suman Das, forensic experts and Kadamtala Police Station personnel joined the
search at the spot. [@2]

Officials found a specially designed concealed compartment inside the vehicle's body holding
17 packets of dry cannabis of varying sizes, which were seized in the presence of officials
and witnesses. [@1,2]

The driver, Kishan Ghosh (29), son of late Kartik Ghosh and a resident of the East Pratapgarh
area of Subhashnagar in Agartala, was taken into custody along with the vehicle. [@2] During
preliminary interrogation he allegedly said the cannabis had been loaded at the Khayerpur
bypass area in Agartala and was meant to be smuggled into neighbouring Assam. [@2]

Kadamtala Police Station Sub-Inspector Manoj Kumar Pal said the estimated black-market value
of the consignment is around half a crore rupees (Rs 50 lakh). [@2] Police registered a case
under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act and began investigating the
trafficking network, including the source and intended recipients of the consignment. [@2]
