On Monday 19 October 2026 Judge Wong Siong Tung of the Sibu High Court sentenced Tiong Soon Hieng,
41, to life imprisonment and 12 strokes of the cane for trafficking 5,081.06 grams of cannabis. [@1,2]
The judge said the death penalty was not necessary and ordered the prison term to run from Tiong's
arrest on 6 January 2023. [@1,2]

The charge under Section 39B(1)(a) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, punishable under Section 39B(2),
said he trafficked the cannabis at about 10.05am on that date in front of a coffee shop on Jalan
Dermaga in Bintangor. [@1,2]

Deputy public prosecutor Mark Kenneth Netto, appearing for Royal Malaysian Customs, urged a
proportionate sentence in the public interest. [@1,2] Defence lawyer Francis Wee said Tiong's wife
had left during the trial and that his mother was caring for their children; he asked for a lighter
term given time already spent on remand. [@1,2] Tiong expressed remorse. [@1,2]
