On Thursday 15 October 2026 the investigation and research service of the Collines police zone,
with the intervention unit, dismantled a cannabis grow in Ellezelles. [@1] They seized 144
cannabis plants, two dryers holding 317 branches, 4.43 kilograms of cannabis flowers, 1.3
kilograms of cannabis residue, a press with a hydraulic pump for making resin, and growing
equipment. [@1]

La DH said the Collines police and the Mons-Tournai prosecutor's office announced the result, and
that the inquiry continues under the prosecutor. [@1] L'Avenir published the same account, and
said the police zone had given it on Friday morning with the prosecutor's agreement, as part of a
local drive against drugs. [@2] Neither report named an arrest. [@1,2]
