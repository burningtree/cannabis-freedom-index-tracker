Turkish police detained 42 suspects on 13 October 2026 in a crackdown
across 13 provinces on an international drug network linked to over 10
tonnes of narcotics, the Interior Ministry said. [@1]

The Istanbul-centred operation involved 2,000 officers under the Istanbul
Chief Public Prosecutor's Office with Interpol-Europol support; analysis
of encrypted communications exposed trafficking in heroin, cannabis and
cocaine totalling 10.1 tonnes. [@1] The ring organised production,
shipments and distribution across countries and provinces, with ringleaders
managing transport logistics and chemical supplies. [@1] Assets worth over
7.65 billion liras (about $153 million) — bank and crypto accounts of 163
people, 556 properties, 155 vehicles and stakes in 71 firms — were seized.
[@1]
