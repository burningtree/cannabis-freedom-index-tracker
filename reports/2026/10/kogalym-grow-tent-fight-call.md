Patrol officers in Kogalym, in Yugra, went to a report of a fight on the
stairwell landing of an apartment block and detained a 30-year-old local man for
an administrative offence. [@1] When they inspected his flat, they found a grow
tent in one room with four fabric bags of seedlings that looked like cannabis.
[@2] They also found a sheet of paper with a green substance, a bottle cap
wrapped in foil with traces of the substance and a paper wrap with similar
contents. [@1]

Forensic testing confirmed that the seized material contained narcotic drugs and
that the plants belonged to the genus Cannabis. [@1] The total mass of the
seized plants and their parts after drying was 10.46 grams. [@2] The Kogalym
police investigation department opened a criminal case under Part 1 of Article
228 of the Russian Criminal Code, which covers acquiring, storing, transporting,
making or processing drugs without intent to sell in a significant amount. [@2]
The suspect was placed under a written undertaking not to leave the area and to
behave properly. [@2] Police said they were continuing to establish all the
circumstances. [@1]
