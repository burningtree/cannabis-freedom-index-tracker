An army unit moved on a cannabis-planted area estimated at about 60 dunums in
the town of al-Kanisa, Baalbek, destroying the kif crop on Saturday 10
October, according to the army's official statement published that day with
its own photographs and video. [@1] In the field the unit detained 17 Syrian
workers without legal papers and seized the harvest equipment found in their
possession. [@1]

The seized items were handed over and interrogation of the detainees began
under the supervision of the competent judiciary. [@1] The official
photographs show masked soldiers pulling mature plants by hand while an armed
comrade stands guard, and a red tractor with a plough attachment turning the
rows under in a second pass. [@1] The crop stands in red soil stretching
toward the hills around the town, with the tractor photographed working the
rows under a clear sky. [@1] The statement headline gives the area as
about 60 dunums, which converts to roughly six hectares. [@1]
