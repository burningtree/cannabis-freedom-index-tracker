Raipur police arrested three people, including a woman, near the Telghani Naka
overbridge under Ganj police station on 28 October 2026. [@2] Officers seized
14.970 kg of ganja (cannabis) from them. [@4] Police valued the haul at about
₹7,48,500. [@4] The arrests were made by a joint team of Ganj police station,
the Anti-Crime and Cyber Unit and the Anti-Narcotics Task Force, acting on a
tip-off. [@1]

The three were Gajendra Panchal, 34, Neeraj Tomar, 21, and Shabana Lodhi, 33.
[@3] Panchal and Tomar are from Bharatpur district in Rajasthan, and Lodhi is
Tomar's wife. [@2] All three were also living in Raipur. [@3] Lodhi had
previously been jailed in an NDPS case in Nagpur. [@3]

When officers tried to question them, the three fled, but police cordoned off
the area and caught them. [@4] Police registered a case under section 20(b) of
the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act at Ganj police
station, crime number 319/26. [@3] They said they were looking for the suppliers
and potential buyers. [@3]
