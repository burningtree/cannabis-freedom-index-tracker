The provincial court in Salamanca sentenced a man to three years in prison and a fine of 1,790
euros for a cannabis plantation, after he accepted the facts and the prosecution's legal
description. [@1] Prosecutors had first asked for four years and ten months and a fine of 2,580
euros. [@1] The court also imposed a three-month fine, at four euros a day, for stealing
electricity. [@1] Both sides waived an appeal, so the sentence is final. [@2]

The case began with the Salamanca drugs unit. [@1] On 16 August 2023 officers searched a warehouse
on the Peñasolana industrial estate that the man had rented and also used as a home. [@1] They
seized 460 cannabis plants with a net weight of 18,613.13 grams, valued at 119,496.29 euros, and
7,239.48 grams of leaves, valued at 46,477.46 euros. [@1] Salamanca24horas, citing the judgment,
adds 30 buds weighing 1,213.9 grams inside that plant total. [@2]

The grow ran on 20 lamps, 13 fans, an extractor, a deioniser, a carbon filter and irrigation and
lighting controls. [@1] A trailer inside the warehouse held the flowering plants, with air
conditioning and a water supply, set up so the crop could be moved. [@2] A caravan held two
precision scales, two hydraulic presses and two heaters. [@1] Officers also found crystal MDMA, 32
tablets of MDMA and amphetamine, and traces of cocaine and 2C-B that the court treated as
penally irrelevant. [@1]

The man had hooked the lights straight to the grid. [@1] The judgment put the loss to I-DE Redes
Eléctricas Inteligentes, an Iberdrola distributor, at 30,154.69 euros, which he must pay. [@1]
The drugs are to be destroyed. [@2]
