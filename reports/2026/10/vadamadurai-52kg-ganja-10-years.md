The special NDPS court in Madurai sentenced Samathanaprabhu to 10 years'
rigorous imprisonment and a ₹1 lakh fine. [@1] The judge delivered the verdict
on 28 October 2026 after finding the charges proved at the end of the trial.
[@1] The judgment came more than a decade after the seizure in 2015. [@2]

On 14 October 2015, a special team of the Narcotics Intelligence Bureau CID
(NIB-CID) checked vehicles at Vadamadurai junction on the Dindigul–Trichy road
and seized 52 kg of ganja being transported illegally. [@1] Police arrested
Samathanaprabhu, 42, of Kambam village in Theni district. [@1] The 52 kg load
was a commercial quantity meant for illegal distribution, and he was the prime
accused in the case. [@2]

The case was registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act, 1985, and NIB-CID officers filed the charge sheet in the Madurai court
after collecting scientific evidence and witness statements. [@1] The state
police chief, Mahesh Kumar Agarwal, praised the investigating officer and the
NIB-CID team for the investigation, the evidence gathering and the way the case
was presented in court. [@1]
