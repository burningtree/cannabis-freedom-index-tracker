On strict orders from the Superintendent of Police, Bolangir, a special team
under SDPO Sadananda Pujhari was patrolling against drug smuggling when, near
Smart Park in Ghumpali on Monday afternoon, it spotted a Mahindra Xylo
(OR-02-BG-1277) speeding erratically toward Ganga Sagar. [@1] Officers gave
chase; cornered, the driver abandoned the moving vehicle, leapt out and
escaped into the darkness. [@1]

Towed to the station and searched, the Xylo held 16 plastic sacks packed with
ganja, 3 quintals 21 kg, seized formally before Executive Magistrate and
Additional Tehsildar Arjun Majhi. [@1] Police value the load at over Rs 22
lakh; preliminary inquiry traces it from Kandhamal district onward to another
state. [@1] SHO Haribandhu Gadatya confirmed a case was registered, and police
are using the registration and vehicle leads to hunt the absconding driver
and break up the wider interstate network. [@1] The article places the contact
on Monday, describing it as an afternoon patrol stop in one passage and a
night seizure in another. [@1]
