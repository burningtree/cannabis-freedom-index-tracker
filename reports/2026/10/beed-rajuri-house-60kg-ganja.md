Maharashtra police seized more than 60 kilograms of cannabis valued at
Rs 30 lakh at Rajuri in Beed district and arrested a 60-year-old man. [@1]

Police raided a house in Rajuri (Navgan) under Beed (Rural) station limits
on Friday night and recovered ganja packed in four sacks. [@1] The accused,
Ghauskhan alias Babu Umarkhan Pathan, was arrested after it was established
the contraband had been stored for illegal commercial sale. [@1] A case was
registered under sections 8(c) and 20(b)(ii)(C) of the NDPS Act, 1985. [@1]
