Ryazan drug-control officers and Rybnovsky district police detained a
31-year-old Glebkovo resident suspected of illegal drug possession during
operation Mak-2026, AIF Ryazan reported on 22 October 2026. [@1]

During a house search they found a bag of plant material. [@1] Expert
testing identified it as marijuana weighing 543 grams. [@2] According to
police, the man had found wild cannabis near the settlement in summer,
processed it, and kept the drug for personal use. [@1]

Investigators opened a criminal case under part 2 of article 228 of the
Criminal Code for large-scale acquisition, storage and manufacture. [@2]
The charge carries three to ten years in prison. [@1]
