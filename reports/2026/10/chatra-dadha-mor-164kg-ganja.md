Three suspected smugglers left a car carrying 164 kg of ganja and fled police in
Chatra district, Jharkhand, police said on 28 October 2026. [@3] The car was
heading from Chatra towards Bihar, Dainik Bhaskar reported. [@1] Officers were
checking vehicles near Dadha Mor on the Chatra–Jori main road when a white Verna
approached. [@2] The driver ignored the signal to stop and sped away, and the
checking team chased the car. [@2] No one was arrested, and police said they
were trying to identify and arrest the fugitives. [@3]

A search of the abandoned car found 82 packets of ganja weighing 164 kg. [@3]
The vehicle, an Uttar Pradesh-registered Verna with registration UP-14CF-5803,
was seized along with the ganja. [@3] Dainik Bhaskar estimated the value of the
load at about 90 lakh rupees. [@1] Prabhat Khabar put it at about 1 crore
rupees. [@2]

The seizure was made under Operation Clean, a Chatra police campaign against the
drug trade. [@3] Police acted on a tip received by Superintendent of Police
Animesh Naithani, Prabhat Khabar reported. [@2] The tip said a luxury vehicle
was carrying a large consignment of ganja. [@3] A Special Investigation Team was
formed and intensive vehicle checks were carried out. [@3] Sub-divisional Police
Officer Sunny Vardhan gave the details at a press conference on Wednesday. [@3]
