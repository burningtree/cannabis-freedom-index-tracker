On 22 October 2026 investigators from Sopron police went to a family house in the city on criminal information. [@1] They found that a 47-year-old woman living there had been growing a large amount of cannabis in the attic. [@1]

In a tent fitted with ventilation and lights they found several dozen cannabis plants and the nutrient solutions used to grow them. [@1] In the garage they seized several kilograms of packaged dried plant material. [@1]

A 49-year-old local man had been helping her. [@1] At his home police found equipment for making the drug and a mushroom culture. [@1]

Both were taken into custody on suspicion of drug trafficking, and police asked that they be remanded. [@1] The Sopron district court ordered both held. [@1] Kisalföld says that account is the county police's, published on social media. [@1] GlobalHír reported the same search on 27 October. [@2]
