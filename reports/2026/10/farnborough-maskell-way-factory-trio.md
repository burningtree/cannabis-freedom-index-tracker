Three people have been arrested after police uncovered a cannabis factory
at a residence on Maskell Way in Farnborough. [@1] [@2]

Acting on reports of suspicious behaviour, the local Neighbourhood
Enforcement Team entered the property on 8 October, finding approximately
53 cannabis plants in various stages of growth and a substantial amount
of harvested plants. [@1] [@2] A 24-year-old from Farnborough, a
25-year-old from Staines-Upon-Thames and a 32-year-old from Reading were
arrested on suspicion of participating in the production of a Class B
drug and remain in custody. [@1] [@2]
