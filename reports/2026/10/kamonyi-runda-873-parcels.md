On Thursday 1 October 2026, Rwanda National Police in the Southern Province arrested 28-year-old Imanizabayo Landouald in Nyagacaca village, Ruyenzi cell, Runda sector, Kamonyi district. [@1,2] He was carrying 873 parcels of cannabis on a motorcycle. [@1,2,3] Police said he was suspected of taking part in distributing and selling drugs. [@1,2]

Officers took him to Runda police station and handed him to the Rwanda Investigation Bureau for investigation. [@1,2,3] Umunota's English report gives the same place, age, count and handover, and does not print his name. [@3]

Chief Inspector Hassan Kamanzi, the police spokesman for the Southern Province, asked residents to keep reporting people suspected of selling or distributing drugs. [@1,2] He said those who continued would be arrested and prosecuted, and thanked residents whose information had led to arrests. [@1,2]
