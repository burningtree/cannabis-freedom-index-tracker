The Anti-Narcotics Force (ANF) arrested six suspects in eight operations across
Pakistan on Friday 9 October 2026. [@1] Two were held in Karachi, one with 1.2
kg of hashish near a university and another with 750 grams near the DHA area of
Karachi. [@1] Both Karachi suspects confessed to selling drugs to students of
educational institutions, an ANF spokesperson said. [@1]

The force said the operations recovered 91.24 kg of narcotics worth more than Rs
10 million. [@2] Officers seized 66 kg of hashish from a vehicle near the
Motorway Toll Plaza in Rawalpindi and 12 kg from a vehicle near the Hyderabad
Toll Plaza. [@2] Near Sialkot, they recovered 2.4 kg of hashish, 6.75 kg of ice
(methamphetamine) and 190 grams of heroin from a motorcyclist. [@1] Near the
Sargodha Bus Terminal, they seized 1.5 kg of hashish and 1 kg of ice, and near
GT Road in Rawalpindi they recovered 450 grams of weed. [@1] At a courier office
on Ring Road, Peshawar, they found 317 ecstasy tablets weighing 200 grams in a
submersible pump. [@3]

Cases were registered under the Control of Narcotic Substances Act, 1997, and
further investigation was under way, the ANF said. [@1]
