Early on Saturday 10 October 2026, the Fès regional judicial police with the domestic
surveillance directorate (DGST) foiled an international drug-trafficking attempt, seizing 4
tonnes and 100 kilogrammes of chira (cannabis resin) on a farm in a rural area near Fès.
[@1] Officers found the drugs alongside a truck loaded with hashish, four marine engines and
four inflatable boats suspected of serving cross-border runs. [@1]

Three suspected members of the network were arrested, including the main suspect, a woman
already subject to a national wanted notice for a similar drug-trafficking case. [@1] They
were placed under judicial investigation supervised by the competent prosecutor to map the
network's other ramifications. [@1]
