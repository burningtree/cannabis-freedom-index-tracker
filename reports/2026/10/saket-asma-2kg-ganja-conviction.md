The Saket court in New Delhi convicted a woman of possessing more than 2 kg of
ganja. [@2] Special Judge (NDPS) Kumar Rajat convicted Asma under Section 20(b)
of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, which covers
possession, sale, purchase and transport of cannabis. [@1] The order was dated
24 October 2026. [@1] It rejected her claim that police had planted the
contraband on her. [@1] Her counsel had questioned the absence of independent
witnesses, procedural lapses and discrepancies in the weight of the recovered
substance. [@1] The court held that the absence of independent witnesses did not
undermine the prosecution when police testimony was consistent and
corroborative. [@1] It said the difference in weight between the initial seizure
and the later production before a magistrate was minor and did not establish
tampering. [@1] The court listed the matter for arguments on the sentence at a
later date. [@1]

Police received information on 17 February 2022 that Asma was supplying ganja in
the Hazrat Nizamuddin area of New Delhi. [@2] A raiding team caught her near
Neela Gumbad in Khusro Park and recovered a bag containing the contraband. [@1]
The bag held 2.015 kg of ganja. [@2] The Forensic Science Laboratory confirmed
that the recovered material was ganja. [@1] At trial, Asma denied the
allegations and said she had been falsely implicated and that police had planted
the contraband. [@1]
