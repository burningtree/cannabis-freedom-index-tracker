In Edo State, the NDLEA destroyed two cannabis farms in Ugbada Forest, Uzebba,
in Owan West Local Government Area on Sunday 18 October, estimating their
potential yield at 2,023.14 kg. [@1,2] Operatives burnt 450 kg of processed
cannabis at the site and took another 97 kg away as exhibits. [@1,2] In a
related operation at the Ewe community in Owan West, 42-year-old Mary Edokhai
was arrested with 35 kg of cannabis. [@1,2]
