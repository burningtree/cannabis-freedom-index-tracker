Volgograd police detained a 38-year-old local man with a previous conviction,
the regional Interior Ministry's press service said. [@2] A personal search
found three packets of drugs on him. [@3] Expert examination identified the
contents as hashish and methylephedrone. [@1]

The man admitted the offence and told officers he had bought the drugs over the
internet from unknown sellers for his own use. [@1] An investigator opened a
criminal case against him under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal Code,
which covers illegal possession of narcotic drugs in a large amount without
intent to sell. [@1] The offence carries a maximum penalty of 10 years in
prison. [@1]
