Agents of the BLTS specialised unit in the South Department ran a joint operation with the
Haitian Coast Guard at Grand-Barrière, commune of Île-à-Vache. [@1,2]

Officers seized nearly 66 bales of marijuana weighing over 333 kg (333.795 kg);
preliminary tests on the substance came back positive. [@1,2] During the operation, police
arrested 22-year-old Medjine Duborsse, a native of Tiburon commune, and seized a 9mm
Springfield Armory firearm, serial number US 899281. [@1,2]
