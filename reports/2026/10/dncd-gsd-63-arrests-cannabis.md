Agents of the Dirección Nacional de Control de Drogas (DNCD), the Dominican
drug-control agency, working with prosecutors from the Public Ministry and the
National Police, arrested 63 people in simultaneous operations and searches
across Greater Santo Domingo. [@1] The sweep comprised 270 operations and 22
searches, and officers also took firearms, radios and scales. [@1]

Authorities reported seizing 23,374.60 grams of suspected drugs, of which 7,748
grams was cannabis, 15,399.80 grams suspected cocaine and 226 grams a rock-like
material treated as crack. [@2] In the National District, 20 people were
detained in 52 operations and six searches, and officers took 7,091 grams of
cannabis along with cocaine, crack, RD$4,000, seven phones and three scales.
[@2]

In Santo Domingo Oeste, 14 people were arrested in 38 operations and nine
searches, and 321.10 grams of cannabis were seized with cocaine, crack, phones,
scales, radios, a bladed weapon and RD$2,285. [@2] Santo Domingo Este accounted
for 11 arrests and 87.80 grams of cannabis in 49 operations. [@2] Santo Domingo
Norte accounted for seven arrests and 66.30 grams of cannabis in 42 operations,
and officers also took a firearm, two scales and a phone. [@2] In Pedro Brand,
three people were arrested in seven searches and 136.20 grams of cannabis were
seized, along with cocaine, crack, scales, radios and a phone. [@2] Boca Chica
and Ciudad Juan Bosch together accounted for eight arrests and 836.20 grams of
drugs that were not broken down by type, plus radios and cash, in 89 operations.
[@2]

Those detained were handed to the Public Ministry, and the substances were sent
to the National Institute of Forensic Sciences (Inacif) for analysis. [@1]
