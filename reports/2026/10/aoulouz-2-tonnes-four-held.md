Four men were arrested after gendarmes seized more than two tonnes of kif,
Morocco's cannabis product, and taba, a smuggled tobacco leaf, in the Aoulouz
area of Taroudant province. [@1] The suspects tried to flee and were chased
through tracks and alleys before officers surrounded and arrested them. [@1]
They were placed in provisional custody on the public prosecutor's instructions.
[@2] Agadir24 described the four as men nicknamed "Al-Jbala", suspected of
earlier involvement in carrying drugs and supplying dealers. [@1]

The load was travelling in three vehicles: two light cars and a small bus. [@1]
Officers also seized bladed weapons, including swords. [@1] Journal Aswat put
the kif at about two tonnes, with quantities of taba on top of that. [@2]

Officers from the gendarmerie posts at Tafingoult, Aoulouz and Oulad Berhil took
part. [@4] One account placed the arrests at the entrance to Aoulouz town. [@3]

The public prosecutor ordered a judicial investigation into the source and
destination of the shipment and the network's possible extensions. [@1]
