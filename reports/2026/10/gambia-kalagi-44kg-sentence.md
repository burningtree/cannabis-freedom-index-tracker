DLEAG officers stopped white government van registration GG 0848 at the Kalagi Military Checkpoint on 26 August 2026 and found 36 large bundles of suspected cannabis concealed in bags inside the vehicle. [@3] The drivers were Adama Tabally, 29, of the Ministry of Finance, and Yankuba Nyassi, 28, and Musa Jammeh, 30, of the Ministry of Agriculture. [@3]

On Thursday 8 October 2026, Magistrate A. Darboe at the Brikama Magistrates' Court convicted all three after they again pleaded guilty to possessing 44 kg 402 g of cannabis for trafficking under section 43(4)(e) of the Drug Control Act 2003. [@1,2] The prosecution relied on cautionary statements, a weight certificate and an analytical certificate admitted as exhibits. [@1]

The court noted aggravating factors, including trafficking, their status as government employees and use of a government vehicle. [@1] Each man was fined D350,000, with a default sentence of five years at Mile 2 Central Prison. [@1,2] Foroyaa said each must pay D150,000 immediately and the remaining D200,000 in three monthly instalments from 30 October through 30 December 2026, and ordered forfeiture of the van to the Ministry of Finance. [@1] The Standard reported that two accused paid D150,000 each toward the fine with three months to settle the balance under surety, while the third, unable to pay anything, was remanded at Mile 2. [@2]
