Badgonda police seized 210 green cannabis plants from a hillside field at
Naharkhoh's Telanbardi in Mhow on Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1] The plants,
known in Hindi as ganja, were growing among a crop of arhar, or pigeon pea, and
officers walked about a kilometre to reach the field. [@1] Police arrested
Madan, 41, on suspicion of illegal cultivation in the field. [@1] Police said he
had no valid licence to grow cannabis. [@1]

The seized plants weighed 67 kg 400 g, and police estimated their value at about
3 lakh rupees. [@2] Station in-charge inspector Prakash Waskale led the team
that searched the field after a tip-off. [@2] Police registered a case against
Madan under Section 8/20 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act,
India's drug law, and said the investigation was continuing. [@1]

The raid was part of a special campaign against illegal drug cultivation and
trafficking that the narcotics branch of the Bhopal police headquarters began on
27 October. [@1] Naharkhoh is a village in Mhow, in Indore district of Madhya
Pradesh. [@2] The operation was carried out under the direction of the Indore
rural zone inspector general and other senior officers, with deputy
superintendent Lalit Singh Sikarwar supervising. [@2]
