Ridvan Fangaj, a 25-year-old Albanian national living on Swirral Edge in Washington, was caught
looking after cannabis plants when police raided a three-bedroom semi-detached house there on 22
September 2026. [@1,2] He tried to leave through the back door but was arrested, telling officers
he had worn gloves while working in the farm. [@1]

Officers found five growing areas holding 130 plants in total, along with 7.3 kilos of harvested
cannabis in bags and £1,760 in cash. [@1,2] Fangaj, who has no previous convictions, pleaded
guilty to producing cannabis and was jailed for 15 months at Newcastle Crown Court. [@1,2] His
lawyer described him as a low-level "dogsbody" at the bottom of the operation, and he has signed
voluntary deportation papers. [@1,2]
