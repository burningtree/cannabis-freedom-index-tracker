Moemen Al-Seddeik, 37, was sentenced at Lewes Crown Court on 29 October 2026 for
a cannabis farm in the loft of his home in Southview Road, Southwick. [@1]
Recorder David Brock gave him a three-year prison sentence suspended for three
years and 300 hours of unpaid work, the maximum available. [@1] Brock said the
term was at the very top of the range that new sentencing guidelines allowed to
be suspended. [@1] He said he was prepared to suspend it only because Al-Seddeik
had no previous convictions. [@1] He told Al-Seddeik that any offence in the
next three years would mean serving the sentence. [@1]

Police raided the house on 6 September 2023, when Al-Seddeik was at home with
his girlfriend and a housemate. [@1] They found 49 cannabis plants in the loft.
[@1] Officers also seized containers with 25.3g of cocaine, scales, bags, large
bags of cannabis, phones and £9,805 in cash. [@1] The plants were seven to eight
weeks old, and experts estimated they would have been worth £15,200 to £43,660
when fully grown. [@1]

Prosecutor Ava Zoccola said the electricity meter had been bypassed so that
power use was not recorded. [@1] EDF Energy estimated 12,042 hours of unmetered
use at £2,715.18. [@1] Al-Seddeik told police he had used YouTube videos to
learn how to tamper with the meter. [@1]

He said all the plants were his, that he had started growing them three months
earlier, and that he had sold none. [@1] He said he had been trying to fund leg
surgery for his father. [@1] He also said he had sold cocaine for a man called
Ali, who paid him £1,000 to £2,000 a week. [@1] He said Ali had threatened him
after he stopped. [@1] He believed the raid followed a complaint from another
dealer who was angry he no longer wanted to sell cocaine for him. [@1] No order
for costs was made because a proceeds of crime application is pending. [@1]
