Chennai Customs' Air Intelligence Unit intercepted a male passenger arriving from Bangkok on
2 October 2026 after profiling flagged him; he tried to exit through the Green Channel
without declaring anything. [@1,2] Examination of his checked baggage found 26 transparent
airtight packets of dried greenish-brown flowering tops, suspected high-grade hydroponic
cannabis, ingeniously concealed inside Thai food packets. [@1,2]

The 15.02 kg haul tested positive for ganja with the Narcotic Drug Detection Kit and was
valued at about Rs 5.26 crore. [@1,2] Customs seized it under the NDPS Act, arrested the
passenger and had him remanded to judicial custody; investigation continues. [@1,2]
