Sector-126 police in Noida arrested Siddharth Sharma, 26, on 29 October 2026 on
Pushta Road near Garhi Kat in Sector 128. [@2] A search found 2 kg 150 g of
ganja on him, police said. [@2,3] Police gave his address as Gali No. 2 near Japto
Store, Garhi Shahpur, Sector 128. [@2] Amar Ujala put the quantity at “sava do
kilo”, about 2.25 kg. [@1]

Police were patrolling the JP Wishtown police post area when they received a tip
that a young man was standing with ganja near Garhi Kat. [@1] Officers
surrounded him and caught him. [@1] Police said Sharma brought the drug from
Delhi and sold it to labourers and passers-by in small packets. [@1]

Police registered case 192/2026 under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, India's main drug law. [@2] Police also said Sharma had earlier
been booked at Sector-24 station for theft and under the NDPS Act, and at
Sector-126 under the Arms Act. [@2] Sector-126 station house officer Sumneesh
Kumar said a first information report (FIR), the initial police report that
opens a criminal case in India, had been registered and further action was under
way. [@1]

Police named Sharma's father as Bhuvendra. [@3] Police registered a case at Sector-126 station under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act. [@3] The arrest came during a special campaign by the Gautam Buddh Nagar police commissionerate against criminals. [@3]
