Police in Hartebeeskop stopped a Toyota Fortuner with Eswatini registration plates near the Oshoek Port of Entry at about 2.30 p.m. on 20 October 2026. [@1,2] A search uncovered about 272.65 kg of dagga with an estimated street value of R1.1 million, plus a black 9mm Girson pistol with a magazine and 13 rounds, as the police statement spells it. [@1,2]

Two Eswatini men, aged 26 and 38, were arrested for dealing in dagga and possessing an unlicensed firearm with ammunition. [@1,2,3] The vehicle, the dagga, the pistol and the ammunition were seized for further investigation. [@1,2]
