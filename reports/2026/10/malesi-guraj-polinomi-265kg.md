Shkodër police arrested four men and seized 265.4 kilograms of suspected cannabis after an
operation they called Polinomi. [@1,2] Admir Selgjekaj, 38, Elsid Ramci, 25, and brothers Bujar
Kukaj, 56, and Naim Kukaj, 52, were arrested in the act. [@1] Police said all four live in Malësi
e Madhe and have earlier records for the same kind of activity. [@2] Balkanweb added that the
people named are presumed innocent until a final court decision. [@1]

Officers stopped a Volkswagen Passat driven by Ramci in the Guraj neighbourhood of Malësi e Madhe.
[@1,2] Searches of that car and of the homes of Bujar and Naim Kukaj produced 265.4 kilograms of
cannabis packed for sale, together with seven mobile phones and six vehicles. [@1,2] On a plot
owned by Bujar Kukaj they also found two objects they treated as suspected anti-tank mines and
handed them to army engineers. [@1,2]

Police said the inquiry had run for four months with the prosecution. [@1] On their account the
Kukaj relatives stored and packed the cannabis, while Selgjekaj, Ramci and Anarldo Mulleti, 39,
arranged transport and sale inside and outside Albania. [@1,2] Esat Kukaj, 58, Denjan Kukaj, 23,
and Mulleti were declared wanted. [@1] The case sits with the prosecutor at the first-instance
court of general jurisdiction in Shkodër. [@2] Director Dashamir Spaho gave the figures at a press
conference of the Shkodër local police directorate. [@1]
