Hambegamuwa police raided two cannabis plots hidden among fruit trees at Rathbalawewa and burned the plants on the spot. [@1,2] Hiru News reported an estimated 23,000 plants, nearly five and a half feet tall, on about one acre inside a banana plantation, and about 17,000 more on about three-quarters of an acre inside an orange grove. [@1] Ceylonwire reported more than 23,000 plants in the banana field and about 17,000 in the orange grove, and said the raid followed a tip. [@2]

Police put the crop at about 10 million rupees. [@1,2] Two residents of the Hambegamuwa area were arrested and were to be produced before the Wellawaya Magistrate's Court. [@1,2] Neither account names them. [@1,2]
