The Anti-Narcotics Force seized 3,825 kilograms of narcotics worth over
Rs 560 million during an intelligence-based operation in the Jusak area of
Kech district, Balochistan, disrupting an alleged international drug
trafficking network. [@1]

The operation recovered 2,454 kg of hashish, 1,363 kg of methamphetamine
(ice) and eight kg of heroin from a compound allegedly used as a transit
storage facility. [@1] Preliminary investigations indicated the drugs had been
trafficked from Afghanistan into Pakistan and stored at the compound pending
a signal for onward transportation abroad through sea routes; investigations
were underway to identify and apprehend facilitators. [@1]

The ANF had shared intelligence with partner countries about a month earlier,
leading to the seizure of 463 kg of Afghan-origin methamphetamine from a
container at Colombo Port, Sri Lanka, and subsequently arrested an Afghan
national allegedly linked to the network, exposing regional and international
linkages of trafficking networks operating from Afghanistan. [@1]
