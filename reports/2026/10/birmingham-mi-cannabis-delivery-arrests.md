A Clinton Township man and an Eastpointe man were arrested together in
Birmingham, Michigan, on the afternoon of Friday 16 October 2026, and both were
charged with unlawful delivery or sale of marijuana. [@1]

Birmingham police said the two men were driving near Woodward and Oakland that
afternoon when officers noticed a strong marijuana smell. [@1] The officers
checked the car's license plate and found that the Michigan Secretary of State
had canceled it for fraudulent insurance, so they pulled the car over. [@1]
Police said they found large amounts of marijuana and paraphernalia consistent
with marijuana sales inside the vehicle, including fake dispensary packaging.
[@1]

The Clinton Township man was arrested for the marijuana and also charged with
improper vehicle registration. [@1] His passenger, the Eastpointe man, was found
to have two arrest warrants out of Detroit and was also placed under arrest.
[@1] Police did not name either man; both were identified only by their
hometowns. [@1]
