Surat City Police registered 257 narcotics cases and arrested 320 people in the
city in 2026 up to the end of October, data presented to the Narco Coordination
Centre (N-Cord) committee showed. [@1] Ganja, the Indian term for cannabis,
accounted for the largest share: 178 of the 257 cases, in which 219 people were
arrested. [@1]

Police reported seizing 414 kg of ganja and valued it at Rs 1.92 crore. [@2] The
remaining cases involved MD drugs, hybrid ganja, brown sugar, heroin, opium,
poppy husk, codeine syrup and intoxicating tablets. [@1] Across all 257 cases,
the substances seized were worth Rs 3.34 crore, police said. [@1]

The meeting was chaired by Surat Police Commissioner Wabang Jamir. [@1] N-Cord
brings together 25 departments and agencies, among them the Narcotics Control
Bureau, Customs, the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence and the Indian Coast
Guard. [@1] The committee meets every month to review the previous month's
action and plan the next period. [@1] It also reviewed the NDPS squads set up at
every police station in Surat City on 22 April 2025 on the commissioner's
orders. [@1] Police said the squads had improved detection of cases under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, India's main narcotics law. [@1]

Offbeat Stories, a Gujarati news site, put Surat's NDPS cases at 52 in 2024
against 257 in 2026. [@3]
