Uttarakhand's Special Task Force arrested a 22-year-old man with 1.108 kg of
charas, a cannabis resin, near the Asharodi check post in Dehradun on Wednesday.
[@1] The man was identified as Yusuf, who lived in Green View, Doon Gali, Malsi,
in Dehradun and originally comes from Bihar. [@2] The STF said he had brought
the contraband from Kasol in Himachal Pradesh to sell it in Dehradun. [@1]

The STF's Anti-Narcotics Task Force arrested him during a checking operation
following technical surveillance and manual intelligence. [@1] The team stopped
him, searched his belongings and found 1,108 grams of charas in a black bag.
[@1] A drug detection kit confirmed that the substance was charas. [@1] He was
arrested under Sections 8 and 20 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act, and a case was registered at Clement Town police station
with further legal proceedings under way. [@1]

STF senior superintendent of police Ajai Singh appealed to students, parents and
teachers to take part in the force's anti-drug awareness campaigns and urged
young people to stay away from drugs and from trafficking for money. [@1] He
also asked people to share information about traffickers with their nearest
police station or the STF. [@1] The press note gave no market value for the
charas and named no one else in the network. [@3]
