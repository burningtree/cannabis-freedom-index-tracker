Officers of Honduras's National Border Police Service (DNSPF), backed by the tactical GOET unit, arrested a 24-year-old Guatemalan at the El Florido checkpoint in Copán on 26 October 2026. [@1,2]

They found four adhesive-taped packages of dry plant material on him that police treated as suspected marijuana, with a preliminary weight of about seven pounds. [@1,2] Three packages weighed about two pounds each and the fourth about one pound. [@1]

Police said he came from Las Flores in Jocotán, Chiquimula, Guatemala, and would face illicit drug-trafficking charges. [@1] The drugs were secured for chemical analysis and he was transferred to the investigations directorate in Copán Ruinas before referral to prosecutors. [@1]
