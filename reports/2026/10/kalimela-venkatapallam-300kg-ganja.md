The people who had stored the ganja fled before Kalimela police reached the
forest near Venkatapallam village, in Odisha's Malkangiri district, having
received prior warning of police movements. [@1] No accused had been arrested in
the case registered at Kalimela police station. [@2] Inspector-in-Charge
Prabhudatta Biswal confirmed that Case No. 241, dated 3 October 2026, had been
registered under Section 20(b)(ii) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act. [@1] Police were still trying to identify and arrest the people
who fled. [@1]

Police launched a search operation in the forested area near Venkatapalam after
information that cannabis had been stashed there. [@1] They seized 300 kg of
ganja, or cannabis, hidden in the forest. [@1] The contraband was measured on
the spot in the presence of Kalimela Additional Tehsildar Rajeev Kumar Behera,
who acted as the magistrate, and independent witnesses. [@1] Sub-Inspector
Prashant Sabat led the raid team. [@1] Police suspected that inter-state
smuggling networks from neighbouring states were involved in hoarding the
consignment. [@1]
