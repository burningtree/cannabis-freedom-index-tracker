Officers from the Banten regional police's narcotics directorate arrested two
men, identified by their initials as AS and MF, when they collected a package at
a courier warehouse in Gunung Sindur, Bogor Regency, West Java, on Thursday 8
October 2026. [@1] Officers seized 10 packages of cannabis weighing 11,088 grams
(11 kg), which police believe belonged to a fugitive identified as PD. [@1] They
also found plastic bags of methamphetamine weighing 4.56 grams in AS's shoulder
bag. [@1]

The cannabis was found in a suspicious package at a J&T Cargo warehouse on Jalan
Raya Perancis in Jatimulya, Kosambi, Tangerang Regency, on Wednesday 7 October
2026. [@2] Police then used a controlled delivery, coordinating with the courier
company to watch the package until it reached Gunung Sindur. [@2] AS told
officers he had obtained the methamphetamine using map and location directions
from another wanted man, ES. [@1]

Both men were charged under Indonesia's 2009 narcotics law, which carries a
maximum of death or life imprisonment. [@1] Police said they were still looking
for PD and ES. [@2]
