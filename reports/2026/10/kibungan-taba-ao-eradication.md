On 26 October 2026 a joint team destroyed 1,200 fully grown marijuana plants
and 400 seedlings, worth an estimated P256,000, at Sitio Taba-ao, Barangay
Poblacion, Kibungan. [@1] The team brought together the Kibungan municipal
police, the 1st and 2nd Benguet provincial mobile force companies, the
provincial drug enforcement and intelligence units, Regional Intelligence
Unit 14 and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency. [@1]

The Manila Times, which published the account on 28 October, reports the
operation as part of Oplan Grass Cutter. [@1] Its account of the operation
names no arrests. [@1]
