Detectives arrested two persons of interest and seized 493 rolls of bhang
during an anti-narcotics operation in Kibwezi, Makueni County, following a
tip-off about suspected drug storage and distribution at a residential
house. [@1]

Writing on X on Sunday, October 18, 2026, the DCI said National
Anti-Narcotics Investigations Bureau detectives raided the premises and
arrested the two as part of efforts to disrupt suspected trafficking
networks. [@1] A search of the house recovered 493 rolls of bhang, two
brown envelopes and a carrier bag containing cannabis, plus two weighing
scales, Rizla papers and other suspected packaging items; cash believed to be
crime proceeds was also seized. [@1]

The two remained in custody as detectives processed the case ahead of
expected arraignment. [@1]
