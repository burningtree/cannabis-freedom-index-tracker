Anti-narcotics officers of the Hadramout Coast Police detained two men accused
of promoting narcotics after finding hashish and shabu in their possession. [@1]
Police identified the suspects only by initials: one man was 27 years old and
the other was 31. [@1] The force said both were accused of dealing in narcotic
substances and that the drugs were found on them. [@1]

Officers reported 38 grams of hashish, cannabis resin, in the men's possession,
and a further 4 grams of shabu, methamphetamine, seized with the resin. [@1] The
cannabis in this arrest is the hashish. [@1] Shabu is a different drug and is
not part of the cannabis weight. [@1]

The police kept the seized drugs. [@1] They placed both men in detention on the
case so that legal procedures could be completed and the two could be referred
to the competent authorities. [@1] The account names the arresting force as the
anti-narcotics unit of the Hadramout Coast Police. [@1]
