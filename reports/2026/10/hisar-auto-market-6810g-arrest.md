Hisar police arrested one man after finding 6 kilograms 810 grams of cannabis,
known locally as ganja, in a bag he was carrying near gate 4 of the New Auto
Market in Hisar city. [@1] A CIA team stopped him with the bag and registered a
case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1]

Police identified the arrested man as Munna Kumar, son of Sarwan Yadav, a
resident of Tegrah village in Saharsa district, Bihar. [@1] CIA Hisar in-charge
Inspector Karamjeet said the team had been patrolling and watching for drugs.
[@1] Acting on information, officers detained a suspect with a bag in his hand
near gate 4 of the New Auto Market. [@1] A search carried out according to
procedure recovered 6 kilograms 810 grams of ganja from the bag. [@1]

Officers registered an NDPS Act case against him at City police station and
started an investigation. [@1] Police said they would seek remand and question
him about where the ganja had been brought from and who was meant to receive the
supply. [@1] They also said the inquiry would look at whether other people were
involved in the trafficking. [@1] Superintendent of Police Siddhant Jain said
the campaign against people involved in drug trafficking would continue. [@1]
