A 44-year-old German national living in Mallorca was arrested on 21 October 2026 over a shipment
of more than three tonnes of marijuana intercepted the previous March at the port of Valencia
after arriving from Canada. [@1] He is also linked to a separate 80 kg seizure in Germany, and
investigators have charged a second island resident in the same case. [@1]

The man appeared before a judge in Inca the next day, charged with drug trafficking and membership
of a criminal organisation. [@1] He was released without bail even though the prosecutor asked for
him to be remanded in custody; he may not leave the island and must report to the court on the 1st
and 15th of every month. [@1]

The case goes back to 7 March, when the Valencia risk-analysis unit flagged a container in transit
carrying lawful goods. [@2] Customs officers with the National Police and the Civil Guard found
3,289 kilograms of cannabis and six kilograms of Canadian hashish, worth over six million euros
and the largest Canada-origin haul seized in Spain, confirming a new trafficking route into the
European Union. [@1,2] The shipment was disguised as commercial goods bound for a warehouse in
Malaga. [@1]
