Delhi Police's Crime Branch arrested three men in Dwarka and recovered 1.279 kg
of charas, a cannabis resin, from them, officials said. [@1] The arrests were
made on 27 October near Gate No. 10, Yashobhoomi T-Point, on Dabri-Gurugram Marg
in Sector 25. [@1] Police identified them as Navdeep Goyat, alias Chotu, 31,
Bunty, alias Banti, 34, and Sandeep, 30, all residents of Jind district in
Haryana. [@1] They also seized a white Tata Tiago car used to carry the
contraband. [@1] A Northern Range-II Crime Branch team was running an
intelligence-based operation when it saw the three men standing by the car; when
officers approached, the men got into the vehicle and the driver tried to start
it, and the team intervened and apprehended all three. [@2]

A search found 879 g of charas on Navdeep, 231 g on Bunty and 169 g on Sandeep,
1,279 g in all. [@1] A case was registered at the Crime Branch police station
under sections 20(b)(ii)(C) and 25 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act, India's main drug law, and police said the investigation
was continuing. [@1]

Police said the men had bought the charas at Jari in Himachal Pradesh and were
bringing it to Delhi for distribution and sale. [@1] Navdeep told investigators
he had been buying charas in Malana and Jari and selling it near tea stalls in
Jind and Chandigarh, and police said he had resumed after his release from jail
in June 2026 in another NDPS case. [@1] Police said he is reported in 17
criminal cases, including three murders. [@1] Bunty told investigators he had
contacted Navdeep in October to buy and sell charas for money and brought in
Sandeep, who joined for promised financial gain. [@1] The Crime Branch said two
of the men had admitted using charas and "sulfa". [@3] The operation was led by
Inspector Pardeep Kumar under ACP Girish Kaushik, and police said they were
working to trace the source of the charas and other people in the supply
network. [@1]
