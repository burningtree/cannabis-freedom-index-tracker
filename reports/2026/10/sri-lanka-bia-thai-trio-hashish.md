Police Narcotics Bureau officers arrested three Thai nationals at Bandaranaike
International Airport in Katunayake early on 15 October 2026 as they tried to
leave the airport with narcotics. [@1,2] The men, aged 22, 22 and 33 and reportedly
employed as drivers in Thailand, had arrived from Bangkok on Thai Airways flight
TG-307 at about 12.30 am. [@1,2]

Inspection of their three pieces of luggage found 5.96 kilograms of hashish
concealed inside cosmetic product containers, valued at about Rs. 51 million.
[@1,2] The suspects and the seized drugs were to be produced before the Negombo
Magistrate's Court. [@1,2]
