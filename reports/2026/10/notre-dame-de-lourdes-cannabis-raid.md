Sûreté du Québec officers from the MRC de Joliette, a regional county
municipality in Quebec, searched a residence in Notre-Dame-de-Lourdes on 28
October 2026. [@1] The search was carried out with the force's Rive-Nord
organised-crime investigation and coordination division (DECCO Rive-Nord), at a
home on rang Sainte-Rose, a rural road in the municipality. [@2]

During the search, officers seized more than 975 grams of cannabis and more than
450 grams of hashish, a cannabis resin. [@1] The seizure also included two boats
with trailers, two personal watercraft with trailers and more than C$18,800 in
cash. [@1]

The operation followed an investigation that was opened after information
received from the public, and the investigation continues. [@1] The Sûreté du
Québec asked anyone with information about cannabis smuggling or drug
trafficking to contact its criminal information centre confidentially at 1 800
659-4264. [@2]
