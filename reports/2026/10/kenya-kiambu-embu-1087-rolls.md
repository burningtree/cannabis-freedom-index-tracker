In a National Police Service statement shared on 22 October 2026, police said the arrests were
made the previous day in operations against drug distribution. [@1] In Juja, Kiambu county,
officers arrested a woman described as a notorious local bhang peddler and recovered 800 rolls of
suspected cannabis in her possession. [@1,2] She was held pending arraignment as investigations
continue. [@1,2]

In a separate Embu county operation, officers on patrol arrested a man found with 287 rolls of
suspected cannabis, who was likewise detained for arraignment. [@1,2] Together the two seizures
totalled 1,087 rolls. [@1,2] The service says it remains committed to pursuing suspected
traffickers and bringing them before the courts. [@1,2]
