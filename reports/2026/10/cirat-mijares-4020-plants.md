On 15 October 2026 the Guardia Civil's ROCA team from Burriana dismantled two nearby outdoor
"guerrilla" cannabis plantations by the river Mijares at Cirat in Castellón's Alto Mijares
comarca. [@1,2] Agents counted 2,200 plants at one site and 1,820 at the other, 4,020 in all, in
flower and close to harvest, with a gross weight of about eight tonnes and an illicit-market value
estimated near two million euros. [@1,2]

Rudimentary shelters built from local materials had been set up for people watching the crops. [@1,2]
During a device at the second plantation, officers saw four people aged between 23 and 40 cutting
buds to remove them, arrested them for alleged drug trafficking, and seized two raffia sacks holding
40 kilograms of buds. [@1,2]
