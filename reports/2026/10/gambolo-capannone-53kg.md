On the morning of 26 October 2026, officers of the State Police commissariat in Vigevano, Pavia
province, followed a suspicious car to an industrial shed on the edge of Gambolò and stopped three
men who were loading cartons into two vehicles. [@1,2]

Inside they found six large boxes and other containers with more than 53 kilograms of dried cannabis
with flowering tops, plus LED lamps and professional indoor cultivation equipment. [@1,2] The three
Milan residents, aged 43, 46 and 53, were arrested on the spot on cultivation, possession and
trafficking charges for large quantities of drugs. [@1,2]

Searches of their Milan homes turned up 15 grams of hashish, packaging material and lamps matching
those at the shed. [@1,2] The men were held in Pavia prison; the shed and equipment were seized while
inquiries continued into how the cannabis entered local supply chains. [@1,2]
