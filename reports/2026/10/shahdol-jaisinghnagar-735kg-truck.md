During a night patrol on Monday 12 October, personnel of Jaisinghnagar police
station in Shahdol district stopped a suspicious truck and found it loaded
with cannabis packed in 18 sacks and 81 packets, 735 kg in all, according to
an official release announced on Tuesday. [@1] The load was split between
bulk sacks and dozens of smaller packets, and police also seized the truck
itself and three mobile phones, putting the total estimated value of the
confiscated goods at Rs 1.13 crore. [@1]

The truck driver and his assistant were arrested on the spot on Monday; both
are residents of Uttar Pradesh and Jharkhand, and a case was registered
against them under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]
The release, carried by PTI on the night of 13 October, named no source for
the consignment and no destination, and gave no quantities beyond the 735 kg
seized from the truck. [@1]
