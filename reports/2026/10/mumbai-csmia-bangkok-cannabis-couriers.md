In two separate operations, Air Intelligence Unit officers at Mumbai's
Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport arrested two Delhi women who
had arrived from Bangkok allegedly carrying cannabis to smuggle into the
country. [@1] Together the officers seized 1.874 kg. [@1]

In the first case, officers acting on intelligence stopped Neetu of Delhi after
her flight landed and found four clear plastic packets of high-grade cannabis
weighing 910 grams in her check-in and hand baggage. [@1] She admitted the
concealment and seizure in a recorded statement and was arrested. [@1]

In the second, officers stopped Rajni, also of Delhi, after her Bangkok
arrival and found three plastic packets holding about 964 grams in her
luggage. [@1] She likewise admitted failing to declare and concealing the drug
and was arrested under the NDPS Act. [@1] The unit is investigating whether
both women belonged to one coordinated Bangkok-based smuggling network and is
tracing handlers and associates. [@1]
