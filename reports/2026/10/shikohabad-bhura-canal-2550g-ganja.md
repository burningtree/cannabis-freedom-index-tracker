Police seized 2,550 grams of ganja, the Hindi term for cannabis, from the two.
[@1] Officers also took a white auto-rickshaw that police said was used to carry
the drug. [@2]

Sub-inspector Sonu Kumar's team stopped them on the canal bank about 50 metres
from the bridge towards Station Road after a tip-off. [@2] Sameer, son of Banti,
lives in Hindu Nagar, Gihar Colony, in the Kotwali police area of Etah district.
[@2] Sita, daughter of Veersahay, lives in Tehsil Tiraha, Gihar Colony,
Shikohabad. [@2] Police said Sameer told them he brought ganja from Etawah and
sold it to Sita, who bought it and resold it in Shikohabad to earn money. [@2]

The Hindusthan Samachar agency reported that police sent them to jail. [@2] The
arrests came in a district campaign against drug trafficking ordered by senior
superintendent of police Aditya Langhe. [@2]
