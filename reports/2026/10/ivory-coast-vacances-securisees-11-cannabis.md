Ivory Coast's anti-drug units arrested 152 people for drug offences during
Operation Vacances sécurisées 11, the prefect of police reported. [@1] They also
destroyed 131 fumoirs, the rooms where drugs are smoked. [@2] The operation
seized 774.075 kilograms of cannabis. [@1]

The operation was launched on 16 July 2026 at Yopougon, in Abidjan. [@1] Its
results were made public by the prefect of police. [@1] He spoke at Bingerville
on 29 October 2026, at the launch of Épervier 12, the operation that follows
Vacances sécurisées 11. [@1] He said it was right to renew the same policy for
the school year and the end-of-year holidays. [@2]

Épervier 12 is launched simultaneously across the country from Bouaké, with the
police, the gendarmerie, the armed forces (FACI) and the CCDO. [@1] It is to
target crime, armed gangs, fumoirs, illegal bus stations and children in
conflict with the law. [@1] The prefect called on residents to give information
anonymously, saying a police force without information cannot be effective. [@1]
The authorities said the operation should keep public order as schools reopen
and the year-end holidays approach. [@1]
