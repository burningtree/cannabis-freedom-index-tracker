A man in his thirties was arrested in the marina sector of Saint-Ferdinand,
Quebec, early on the evening of Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1] He was carrying
two compressed-air guns, the Sûreté du Québec said. [@1]

Louis-Philippe Ruel, a spokesman for the media relations service of the Sûreté
du Québec (SQ), the provincial police force, said the man had taunted young
people without threatening them or brandishing a weapon. [@1] The young people
reported the presence of a possibly armed man, Ruel said, and officers
intervened and arrested him at the marina. [@1]

A search of the suspect's vehicle then led to the seizure of several narcotics,
notably cannabis and methamphetamine. [@1] Police gave no quantity for either
drug. [@1] Ruel said the man could face charges related to the possession of
narcotics and weapons, and no charges had been announced. [@1] The man was not
named, and police did not say whether he remained in custody. [@1]
