Early on Thursday 29 October 2026, state police and investigative police entered
a house in the Chicahuales I subdivision of Jesús María and detained three
people, including a woman. [@1] The search, on Domingo Gómez street at about
5:00 a.m., had been authorised by a judge as part of an investigation already
under way. [@1] Officers found Xóchitl Jazmyn, Jonathan Antonio and José Pablo
in the house and held them while the search continued. [@1]

In a wardrobe they seized 15 kilograms of marijuana, two kilograms of crystal
and about 20 grams of cocaine. [@1] They also seized about 40 kilograms of
powdered substances in containers and bags. [@1] A pistol, a scale, an electric
sealer and empty plastic bags were also found. [@1]

The three detainees were placed at the disposal of the competent authorities,
and the substances, the gun and the other items were secured for the
investigation. [@1] No other people linked to the alleged activity in the house
had been named. [@1]
