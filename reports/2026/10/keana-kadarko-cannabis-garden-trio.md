The Nasarawa State Police Command arrested three suspects and recovered
quantities of leaves believed to be cannabis, including freshly harvested
leaves from a garden, per a statement by PPRO SP Ramhan Nansel. [@1] [@2]

The men — Aondaver Dominic, Sylvanus Umoor and Peter Tar — were arrested at
about 1 pm on 22 October 2026 by SCID Lafia operatives during an anti-crime
patrol of Sabongida Bature and Gidan Kparev villages in Kadarko. [@1] [@2]
Led to a hideout where marijuana was allegedly sold and consumed, officers
gave chase as the suspects tried to flee and recovered dry leaves suspected
to be Indian hemp. [@1]

Investigation then led operatives to a garden behind the suspects'
residence where marijuana was allegedly cultivated, with freshly harvested
leaves recovered from it. [@1] CP Shetima Jauro-Mohammed ordered a thorough
investigation and the handover of suspects and exhibits to the NDLEA. [@1]
