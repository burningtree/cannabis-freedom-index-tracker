Clark County deputies arrested Kristina Combs on Thursday 29 October 2026 after
she signed for a 37-pound (16.8 kg) package of marijuana delivered to her home.
[@2] The package had been intercepted by Lexington police and was addressed to
the home in Clark County, Kentucky. [@1] Officers from the Gateway Area Drug
Task Force, the Clark County Sheriff's Office and Mount Sterling police
delivered it themselves, and Combs signed for it. [@1] Officers then executed a
search warrant at the address. [@1] She was charged with trafficking marijuana,
cultivation of marijuana and trafficking a hallucinogen. [@1]

The sheriff's office said officers seized 136 pounds (61.7 kg) of marijuana, 36
marijuana plants and more than 5,000 THC vapes. [@1] They also seized 1.4 pounds
(0.64 kg) of hallucinogenic mushrooms, more than $50,000 in cash and a handgun.
[@1]

The Gateway Area Drug Task Force draws detectives from the Kentucky State
Police, Mount Sterling and Winchester police, the Clark County Sheriff's Office
and ATF special agents, and works in the Appalachia High Intensity Drug
Trafficking Area. [@1]
