NCB Mumbai intercepted a commercial goods carrier truck in Nagpur,
Maharashtra, on 4 October 2026 and seized 500 kg of ganja concealed among
steel pipes. [@1] [@2]

Three people — the driver, the Nagpur-based receiver and a close drug
associate — were arrested in connection with an interstate network that
regularly procured ganja from Odisha and moved it into Maharashtra
districts hidden among innocuous goods, using random routes to dodge
enforcement. [@1] The truck had originated in Sambalpur, Odisha, and the
consignment was meant for further distribution in Jalna, Maharashtra, and
Indore, Madhya Pradesh. [@1] Credible information led to the
identification of two West Bengal-based men, and efforts to apprehend other
associates continue. [@1]
