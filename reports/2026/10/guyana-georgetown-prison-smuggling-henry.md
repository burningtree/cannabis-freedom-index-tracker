Tachanna Henry of C Field, Sophia, was detained around 10.15 a.m. on Wednesday 7 October 2026 while visiting the Georgetown Prison. [@1] Officers searched a box she intended to send to Kevon Alfred, a prisoner housed at the Mazaruni facility, and found several plastic bags of leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis. [@1]

Henry was told of the discovery and taken to the Alberttown Police Station along with the suspected cannabis. [@1] Director of Prisons Nicklon Elliot warned that attempts to introduce contraband into prisons carry serious legal consequences and commended the officers for their vigilance. [@1]
