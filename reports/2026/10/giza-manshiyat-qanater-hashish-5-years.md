South Giza criminal court sentenced a hashish trafficker to five years in prison
after convicting him of dealing in cannabis resin in the Manshiyat al-Qanatir
area of Giza governorate. [@1,2]

Security forces had stopped the man on a local road and found four large pieces
of hashish on him, suspecting he was supplying the illicit market. [@1,2] They
took legal action, recorded the seizure, and the public prosecution investigated
and examined the drugs before referring him to the criminal court, which handed
down the five-year term. [@1,2] The opened accounts do not name him or report
any co-defendants. [@1,2]
