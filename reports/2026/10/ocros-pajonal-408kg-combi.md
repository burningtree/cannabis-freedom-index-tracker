Criminal investigators from the Ayacucho police region seized 408 kilograms of cannabis from a
minibus abandoned in the village of Pajonal, in the district of Ocros, on the road between
Ayacucho and Andahuaylas. [@1] The regional police chief, Arturo Valverde Inga, said the occupants
left the vehicle and fled in the dark when they saw the officers. [@1] The work followed
intelligence from the Interior Ministry's intelligence directorate. [@1]

Officers found packages they identified at first as cannabis. [@1] No arrest or identification of
the people who ran was reported. [@1] Police estimated the load at about 408,000 soles on the
Ayacucho market and as much as 714,000 soles if it had reached Lima, and said those figures could
change. [@1] They also said a possible destination was Tacna, and from there Chile, a line of
inquiry still to be checked. [@1]

Infobae reported the same 408 kilograms, the abandoned minibus at Pajonal, the dawn flight and the
Divincri operation, spelling the district Oclos, and said a TV Perú report carried the case. [@2]
It put the Lima estimate above 700,000 soles and the Ayacucho estimate above 400,000 soles. [@2]
