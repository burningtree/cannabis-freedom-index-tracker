Officers seized 76 bales of hashish weighing 3,040 kg. [@1] They also took the
boat, fitted with four powerful outboard engines, and fuel cans still on board.
[@2]

A surveillance system detected the suspicious vessel and the force launched an
interception and boarding operation with the provincial maritime service in
Huelva and an air-service helicopter. [@1] The crew fled at high speed and made
abrupt manoeuvres that the force said put them and the officers at serious risk,
while throwing cans of petrol into the sea. [@1] The Guardia Civil's interceptor
Río Tiétar caught the narcolancha, and officers boarded it and detained all
eight people aboard. [@1]

All eight are accused of drug trafficking, smuggling, a fuel-supply offence
against collective safety known as petaqueo, and serious disobedience to the
authorities. [@2] Horizons, an Algerian daily citing Spanish media, reported
that the boat had set out from Morocco's Atlantic coast. [@3] Horizons said the
case again highlighted the challenges facing Spanish security services on the
sea routes from Morocco to the Iberian peninsula. [@3]
