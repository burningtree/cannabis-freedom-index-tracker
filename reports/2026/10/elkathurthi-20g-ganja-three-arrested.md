Police in Elkathurthi arrested three men with 20 grams of ganja, the local name
for cannabis, during a vehicle check on the outskirts of the village. [@1] The
check was near Kakatiya Vantage, and the three tried to flee when they saw the
officers before being chased down. [@1] Officers seized the ganja and three
mobile phones. [@1] The men were identified as Roddaboyina Sridhar, Matteda
Jaswanth and Puppala Ganesh, all from Damera village. [@1]

The three were going to sell the rest when police caught them, the officer said.
[@1] Police said a preliminary investigation showed the three had been using
ganja for two years. [@1] Police registered a case and sent all three to court
for remand, Narasimha Rao said. [@1]
