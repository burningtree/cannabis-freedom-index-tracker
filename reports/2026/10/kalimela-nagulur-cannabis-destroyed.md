Acting on information about illegal cultivation, a team led by Kalimela police
station officer-in-charge Prabhudatta Biswal raided forest land near Nagulur
village under Papu lur panchayat in Malkangiri district, where more than 2,000
cannabis plants were growing on about two acres. [@1] The team cut the plants
down and burnt them. [@1]

Police arrested 30-year-old Kateya Nainu, a Nagulur resident, and produced him
in court on Sunday. [@1] Officers opened a further investigation to find
others behind the cultivation, including whoever supplied the seeds and
fertilisers or funded it. [@1]
