At about 20:30 on Friday 30 October 2026, personnel from the Philippine Drug
Enforcement Agency's Seaport Interdiction Unit–Batangas inspected parcels at a
courier hub in Barangay San Vicente, San Pedro City, Laguna, with a narcotics
detection dog. [@1,2]

The dog alerted handlers to packages bound for shipment. [@1,2] Agents recovered
28 transparent sachets and 12 resealable sachets of suspected dried marijuana,
a heat-sealed bag of suspected high-grade marijuana known as kush, two suspected
marijuana brownies and a vape cartridge believed to contain marijuana oil. [@1,2]
PDEA put the total at about 250 grams with an estimated value of PHP 30,000. [@1,2]

The seized material was taken to PDEA Regional Office 4A for documentation and
inventory. [@1,2] The agency said investigators were tracing who sent the parcels
and where they were headed, and had not announced any arrests or charges. [@1,2]
