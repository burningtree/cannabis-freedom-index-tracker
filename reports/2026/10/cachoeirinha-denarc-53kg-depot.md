Civil Police officers from the 4th anti-narcotics precinct of Denarc, Rio Grande
do Sul's state anti-drug department, searched a house in Vila Cachoeirinha,
Cachoeirinha, on Wednesday 28 October 2026. [@2] Police found 75 bricks of
marijuana weighing 53 kg. [@1] They said the house was being used as a depot to
supply smaller sales points across the metropolitan region. [@2] No arrest was
reported in the Cachoeirinha case, and investigators said the owner of the load
would answer for drug trafficking. [@2]

The house also held two precision scales, one an industrial model able to weigh
up to 40 kg, material for dividing and packaging the drug, and stickers used in
the trade's tele-entrega, or phone-order delivery. [@2] The operation was
coordinated by delegate Ana Flávia de Melo Leite, head of the 4th anti-narcotics
precinct. [@1]

The search followed intelligence work that mapped the movement of a criminal
faction in the region. [@1] Denarc said the inquiry had entered a new phase
focused on identifying and holding criminally responsible the people behind the
material, and that officers would keep working to dismantle the criminal network
connected to the depot. [@1]
