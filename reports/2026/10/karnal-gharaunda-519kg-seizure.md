Acting on a tip, Karnal Anti-Narcotics Cell sub-inspector Rajan Kumar's team intercepted a Rajasthan-registered truck on the NH-44 service road at Gharaunda late on 27 October 2026. [@1,2]

Officers found 19 sacks with 519.9 kg of cannabis flower and leaves hidden under bags of cashew shells. [@1,2] Police valued the drugs at about ₹1.35 crore. [@1,2]

The driver Bilal and cleaner Yahuda, both from Firozpur Namak in Nuh district, were arrested and a case was registered at Gharaunda police station under the NDPS Act. [@1,2] They told police they had picked up the consignment near the Odisha border before police stopped them en route to delivery. [@1]
