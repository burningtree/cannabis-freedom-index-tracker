Civil police arrested a 47-year-old man and a 51-year-old woman at a house in
the Progresso neighbourhood of São Bento do Sul, in Santa Catarina, on the
afternoon of Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1] The operation was carried out by the
Delegacia de Investigação Criminal (DIC), the city's criminal investigation
unit, with support from the Delegacia de Polícia da Comarca, the police station
for the local court district. [@1,4] The arrests came during the execution of a
search and seizure warrant issued after a police investigation. [@3,4]

Inside the house officers found cannabis plants, cultivation supplies and
portions of the drug ready for use or sale. [@2,4] Officers found three complete
grow tents fitted with running electronic equipment for drying and processing
the plant. [@1,4] They also seized a precision scale and packaging used to portion
the drug. [@2,4] More than R$14,000 in cash was seized. [@2,4] Mobile phones and
notebooks were also seized and sent for forensic examination with the rest of
the material. [@1]

Both were arrested in flagrante, caught in the act, on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@2,4] After a body-of-offence examination, they were to be taken to
the prison system, where they would stay at the disposal of the court awaiting a
custody hearing. [@1]
