Twenty suspects were sent to prison on remand after simultaneous drug raids
centred on Bingöl. [@1] Police detained 27 people. [@1] Three were released once
processing at the station was finished. [@1] Twenty-four were referred to the
courthouse. [@1] Four of them were placed under judicial supervision, and 20
were remanded to prison. [@1]

The Bingöl Chief Public Prosecutor's Office directed the operation, and the
provincial police narcotics branch carried it out. [@1] Teams hit 35 addresses
in four provinces at the same time. [@1] Bingöl Güncel TV reported that the
raids began at 05:30 on 27 October 2026. [@2] Seventy teams, three
special-operations units and 350 personnel took part, with two drones and five
drug-detection dogs. [@2] Twenty-seven suspects were caught during the searches.
[@2]

The finds included 16.19 grams of cannabis, called esrar, 1.28 grams of cannabis
mixed with tobacco, and 43.32 grams of skunk, a high-potency cannabis product
listed separately from ordinary cannabis in Turkish police reports. [@1] Bingöl
Güncel TV reported the same cannabis, skunk, methamphetamine and heroin weights.
[@2] Officers also recorded 74.84 grams of methamphetamine and 0.32 grams of
heroin. [@1] They seized 10 pipes, two precision scales, 2,743 packets of
contraband cigarettes, 170 contraband cigars, 16 electronic cigarettes, two
devices, 62 packets of Terea cartridges, one pistol, one magazine and 935 rounds
of various calibres. [@1]
