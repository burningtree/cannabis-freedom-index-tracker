On Saturday 10 October 2026 the Department of Narcotics Control's Dhaka Metro (South) office sent a
special team led by sub-inspector Ismail Hossain to the Jatrabari area after a tip-off. [@1]

On the Dhaka–Chattogram highway in front of the Royal Host Restaurant, within Jatrabari Police
Station limits, officers searched covered van Dhaka Metro-Na-11-8857. [@1] They arrested Mohammad
Saiful Islam, 46, and seized 50 kilograms of cannabis packed in 24 black polythene bags wrapped
with adhesive tape, along with an Android phone and the van. [@1]

Deputy director Mohammad Anwar Hossain of the Dhaka Metro (South) office described the operation. [@1]
In preliminary questioning, Saiful Islam reportedly said he was transporting the cannabis from a
border area of Cumilla district to supply distribution points across Dhaka. [@1]

A case was filed against him under the Narcotics Control Act at Jatrabari Police Station. [@1]
Duronto Crime News published the same seizure on 11 October 2026 without further figures. [@2]
