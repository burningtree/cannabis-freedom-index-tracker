Two men, aged 28 and 30, were arrested for drug trafficking on the afternoon of
Wednesday 28 October 2026 in Fátima do Sul, Mato Grosso do Sul. [@1] A team from
the Força Tática, a tactical unit of the 14th Military Police Battalion (14º
BPM), carried out the operation. [@1] Military police said the incident was
recorded at about 16:15 during a police blockade on Avenida 9 de Julho. [@1]
Officers stopped a vehicle occupied by the two men and, during the search, found
a portion of a substance analogous to hashish. [@1]

After the stop, the team searched addresses linked to the suspects and found
further portions of drugs and a precision scale. [@1] Police described the scale
as equipment commonly used to weigh drugs. [@1] In all, officers seized about
155 grams of hashish, 5.6 grams of marijuana (maconha) and 6.2 grams of skunk, a
cannabis product sold under that name — about 167 grams of cannabis — together
with the scale, a mobile phone and the vehicle. [@1]

The two men were placed under arrest and taken, with the seized material, to the
Civil Police station for the legal steps that follow. [@1] The military police
said the action was part of its commitment to confronting drug trafficking and
protecting public safety. [@2]
