On local intelligence, a joint Anti-Narcotics and Badalpur police check
stopped a truck at the Kudi Kheda turn on the Dadri-Lalkuan road on the
night of 13 October. [@1,2] A secret cavity built into the truck two months
earlier to dodge checks held about 1.66 quintals of ganja, 165.7 kg worth
roughly Rs 1 crore; the truck and an accompanying scout car were seized, along
with four phones, cash and bank cards. [@1,2]

Two men rode in the truck while the third drove ahead scouting the route.
[@1] All three — Dinesh Pal, Sameer Kumar and Ravi Kumar Sahni, Bihar
natives living in Delhi and Bihar — were arrested and booked under NDPS
sections 8, 20, 29 and 60. [@1,2] The gang bought the drug in Bhubaneswar
for about Rs 15,000 a kilo and sold it at four times the price to Delhi
wholesalers and retailers, spending proceeds on an SUV, the truck, a taxi and a
Mustafabad house. [@2] Ringleader Pal had served about 15 months in jail
after a 250 kg seizure in 2021. [@1,2] DCP Central Praveen Ranjan Singh said
the Delhi receivers and the transport company behind the truck are under
investigation. [@1]
