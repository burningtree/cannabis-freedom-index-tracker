On 19 October 2026, Ottawa police executed a search warrant at a cannabis and nicotine retail
establishment in the 300 block of Catherine Street, an unlicensed shop operating as a My Legacy
location. [@1,2] Officers seized about 13 kilograms of cannabis flower, 393 grams of hash,
49,244 milligrams of THC-infused edibles and 80 THC vape cartridges, alongside 72,720
cigarettes, nicotine pouches, cigars, vape devices and more than $10,000 in cash. [@1,2]

A 32-year-old Ottawa woman was charged under the Cannabis Control Act with selling and possessing
illicit cannabis, and under the Criminal Code with selling unstamped tobacco and possessing
proceeds of crime. [@1,2] The investigation is continuing. [@1]
