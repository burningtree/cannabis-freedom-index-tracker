Police in Pelalawan regency, Riau province, detained a 28-year-old man known by
the initial S in a raid on a house in Beringin Indah village, Pangkalan Kuras
district, on Wednesday 28 October 2026. [@1] The operation followed reports from
residents that the house was being used for methamphetamine deals. [@1] S is a
farmer who lives in the village. [@1]

Officers found one packet of suspected methamphetamine in a Climax cigarette
box, a second packet in his trouser pocket, and a packet of suspected dried
cannabis in a black shoulder bag. [@1] Police seized 0.47 grams of suspected
methamphetamine and 3.86 grams of suspected dried cannabis. [@1] They also took
a black Samsung Android phone. [@1]

S told officers the drugs were his and that he had got them from a person whose
identity is still under investigation. [@1] A urine test found methamphetamine
in his system. [@1] The case was recorded as police report
LP/A/132/X/2026/RIAU/Res Pelalawan, dated 29 October 2026. [@1] Investigators
cited trafficking and possession provisions of Indonesia's 2009 narcotics law,
together with an article of the 2023 criminal code. [@1] The head of the
narcotics unit, Iptu Rudi Syahputra, said the suspect and the evidence were
being held at the Pelalawan police station for further investigation. [@2]
