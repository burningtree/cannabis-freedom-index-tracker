In a statement dated Monday 26 October 2026, the Lebanese Army command said a unit destroyed
about 180 dunums (18 hectares) of cannabis at Jabab al-Hamr in Hermel and seized a large
quantity of the crop estimated at about 60 tonnes. [@1]

In parallel raids, a unit that had been watching a wanted man arrested D.S. at his home in
Brital, Baalbek, for leading an armed kidnapping and robbery gang and dealing drugs; a rocket
shell and drugs were found in the house. [@1] Other units arrested 25 more people: three
citizens held in Bani Sakhr, Akkar, for smuggling, with military weapons and ammunition seized
from them, and 22 Syrians detained in Ras Baalbek, Arsal, al-Sharawneh and Qaa projects for
illegal entry and moving without legal papers. [@1] The seized items were handed over and the
detainees questioned under the supervision of the competent judiciary. [@1]
