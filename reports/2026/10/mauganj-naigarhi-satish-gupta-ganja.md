Naigarhi police in Mauganj district, Madhya Pradesh, arrested Satish Gupta after
he had been missing for six months in a ganja-smuggling case. [@1] Ganja is the
name used locally for cannabis. [@1] Officers took him from his hardware shop in
Pahadi village, inside the Shahpur police station area. [@1] He is 38, the son
of Rajbahor Gupta, and a resident of Pahadi. [@2] Station house officer Anil
Kakde's team carried out the arrest under Superintendent of Police Manohar Singh
Mandloi. [@2] Additional Superintendent Shashikant Saryam and subdivisional
police officer Sachi Pathak were also named as supervising the search. [@2]

The arrest followed a raid on 11 April 2026 at a poultry farm in Dihiya, where
police caught Kuldeep Jaiswal, also known as Vishnu, of ward 13 in Naigarhi,
with 2 kilograms 340 grams of ganja. [@2] They valued it at 22,400 rupees. [@2]
During questioning Kuldeep said he had obtained the ganja from Satish Gupta.
[@2] A case was then registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, Kuldeep was sent to jail, and Satish was recorded as absconding.
[@2] Dainik Bhaskar's headline later described that poultry-farm seizure as 2 kg
of ganja. [@1]

Police had kept an informer network looking for the missing accused, and
arrested him at the shop after a tip. [@2] The officers credited with the arrest
were inspector Anil Kakde, sub-inspector Anantvijay Singh, R.P. Verma, head
constable Ramkumar Bhaskar, Rashiraman Sahu, and constables Avinash Singh and
Prakash Kushwaha. [@2]
