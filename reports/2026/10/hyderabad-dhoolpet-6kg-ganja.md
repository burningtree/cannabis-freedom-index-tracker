The arrested men were Suresh Singh, alias Tinku Singh, and Pramod More. [@2]
According to Excise Circle Inspector Praveen Kumar, the two had allegedly
procured the cannabis in Odisha and were transporting it on an electric scooter
to sell it in Dhoolpet. [@3] Officers intercepted them near the Shiva temple in
the Jiyaguda area of Dhoolpet. [@3]

Besides the cannabis, the team seized Rs 10,000 in cash and three mobile phones
from the pair, the inspector said. [@1]

The team involved in the seizure included CI Praveen Kumar, Viplav Reddy and
Venkanna, along with constables Janardhan, Ravi, Krishna Prasad, Surya,
Nagaraju, Beerappa and Hema. [@3] The two accused were arrested and the seized
material was taken into custody. [@3]
