Acting on operational information, officers from the drug control department of
South Ossetia's criminal police searched the home of a 56-year-old Tskhinvali
resident, the Interior Ministry said. [@1] They seized 37.73 grams of marijuana
during the search. [@1] A medical examination found traces of marijuana in his
body, the ministry said. [@2]

The Tskhinvali city court fined him 8,000 rubles for illegal drug use. [@1] The
fine was an administrative penalty, not a criminal sentence. [@1] The Interior
Ministry said the man also faces criminal punishment for storing the marijuana
found in his home. [@1] The drug control department of the criminal police,
known by the Russian initials UKON KM, is conducting the inquiry into the
storage. [@1]

Tskhinvali is the capital of South Ossetia, whose Interior Ministry announced
the case. [@2] The ministry said the resident would be held liable for both
illegal use and storage of narcotic drugs. [@1]
