After about two months of surveillance following neighbour complaints about cannabis odour,
Thailand's immigration bureau and officials of the Department of Thai Traditional and Alternative
Medicine searched a three-storey rented house in Soi Khu Bon 27, Bang Khen district, Bangkok, on
Monday 19 October 2026 under a Min Buri Criminal Court warrant. [@1,2]

Officers arrested one Vietnamese man and four Myanmar nationals who were sorting and packing
cannabis buds for customers in Bangkok; black bags of buds were stacked throughout the house,
totalling more than five tonnes. [@1,2] Immigration Bureau chief Pol Lt Gen Noppasilp Poonsawat
said the house had ventilation piping to discharge smell outdoors, and that Myanmar workers were
paid about 200–300 baht a day while the Vietnamese suspect appeared to supervise the work.
[@1,2]

Investigators said a Thai tenant had leased the property more than a year earlier before the
foreign workers moved in. [@1,2] A licence linked to the operation was issued in Nong Bua Lam Phu
province and did not cover processing or distribution in Bangkok; a department legal officer said
that breach of the Thai Traditional Medical Knowledge Protection and Promotion Act carries up to
one year in prison and/or a 20,000-baht fine. [@1,2] Police said the buds would be sent for
laboratory tests and that inquiries into the renter and suppliers were continuing. [@1,2]
