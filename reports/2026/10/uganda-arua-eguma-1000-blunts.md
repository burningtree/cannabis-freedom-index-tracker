Police in Arua City's central business division arrested Godfrey Eguma at the Link Bus Terminal
on Thursday 22 October 2026 at about 8:30 am during routine checks as he prepared to board a bus
to Masindi District. [@1,2]

West Nile regional police spokesperson Josephine Angucia said a search of his luggage recovered
more than 1,000 rolled sticks of suspected marijuana. [@1,2] Under questioning, Eguma allegedly
said the drugs were meant for sale to workers at the Kinyara sugar plantation in Masindi. [@1,2]

Officers photographed the exhibits, recorded statements and held Eguma at Arua CBD police station
while trying to trace the source. [@1,2] Angucia said he would be charged under Uganda's Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (Control) Act once investigations allowed. [@1,2]
