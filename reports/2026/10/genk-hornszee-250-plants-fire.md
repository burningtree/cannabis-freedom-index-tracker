On Wednesday evening, 7 October 2026, emergency crews were called to a smouldering fire in a shed
behind a house on Hornszee in Genk. [@1] Police zone Carma found a cannabis plantation of about
250 plants, spread over several rooms. [@1]

On the Limburg prosecutor's orders, officers arrested two Genk residents, a 64-year-old woman and
a 74-year-old man. [@1] A search of their home found several firearms, which were seized. [@1] The
plantation was dismantled and the plants destroyed. [@1] Grid operator Fluvius found signs of
electricity theft and a separate report was drawn up. [@1]

The prosecutor brought the man before an investigating judge on suspicion of cultivating drugs.
[@1] The judge ordered him held and taken to prison. [@1] The woman was released after
questioning. [@1] The inquiry continues under the investigating judge in Tongeren. [@1]

VRT NWS reported the same remand: the man in his seventies was sent to prison, the 64-year-old
woman was released, firearms were found in the searches, the plants were destroyed, and Fluvius
recorded signs of electricity theft. [@2]
