A narcotics dog working for Toldstyrelsen, the Danish customs agency, marked three parcels during a check at a parcel centre in the Copenhagen capital area on Friday 2 October 2026. [@1,2] Each parcel held more than 20 kilograms of cannabis, more than 60 kilograms in all. [@1,2]

Customs tested the contents and found cannabis with a THC level above what is legal. [@1] The case was passed to Copenhagen Police, which is responsible for the investigation. [@1,2] Control director Jeppe Kjærgaard said it was worrying that such quantities were being smuggled into Denmark in parcels, and that the stop kept the cannabis off the street for resale or use. [@1,2] The statements name no one arrested. [@1,2]
