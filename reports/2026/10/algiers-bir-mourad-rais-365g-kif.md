Police in the 8th urban security unit of Bir Mourad Raïs, an administrative
district of Algiers, dismantled an organised network of three people trafficking
drugs and psychotropic substances. [@1] The unit operates under the Algiers
wilaya security services, whose communication unit announced the operation in a
statement. [@1] It followed reliable information about the group's suspicious
activity, and investigators identified the suspects and located them before
arresting them. [@1] The network sold drugs and psychotropic pills among young
people. [@2]

The operation ended with the seizure of 365 grams of treated kif, processed
cannabis, a quantity of psychotropic substances and an electronic scale. [@2]
The seizure also included two maritime signal flares, prohibited bladed weapons
and 44,000 dinars in cash. [@1] Police consider the sum to be proceeds of the
group's criminal activity. [@1]

After the legal procedures were completed, the three suspects were presented to
the public prosecutor at the Boufarik court. [@2]
