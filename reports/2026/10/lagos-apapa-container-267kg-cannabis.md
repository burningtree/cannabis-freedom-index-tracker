Police and other security agencies examining a 40-foot container at Apapa, the
district that holds Lagos's port, found 275 sachets of a substance suspected to
be Canadian Cannabis Indica concealed inside four chest freezers. [@1] The
Nigeria Police Force put the weight of the suspected cannabis at 267 kg. [@2]

The force listed the find among its top 10 state-by-state operational successes
recorded on 6 October 2026. [@1] The summary was signed by Force Public
Relations Officer CSP Ani Iniedu at Force Headquarters in Abuja. [@2] It covered
operations in Lagos, Oyo, Plateau, Borno, Delta, Bauchi and Kogi states. [@2]

The container was examined jointly by police operatives and other security
agencies. [@1] The recovered exhibits were taken into custody for further
investigation, with the State Criminal Investigation Department (SCID) in Panti,
Lagos, handling the case. [@1] The SCID is the force's state-level criminal
investigation unit. [@1] The statement named no suspect in the container case.
