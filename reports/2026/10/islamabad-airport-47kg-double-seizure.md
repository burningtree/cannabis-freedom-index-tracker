Customs authorities at Islamabad International Airport seized a total of 47 kilograms of
suspected drugs in two separate operations against drug smuggling attempts over the past
week, arresting passengers and initiating legal proceedings. [@1]

Passengers arriving from abroad on Thai Airways flight TG-349 were put under strict
surveillance on 2 October 2026, when Thai national Naeehaphong Pueehykosai was stopped and
searched; officers recovered 23 kilograms of suspected cannabis worth around Rs 31 million
from his possession. [@1] A special vigilance team formed on credible information under the
Assistant Collector Customs (Airport) made the seizure; the passenger was detained and a
case registered against him. [@1]

In the other operation, officers stopped an incoming passenger on a tip-off and recovered 24
kilograms of hashish valued at around Rs 310 million on the international market. [@1,2]
Legal proceedings against the arrested passengers are underway. [@1]
