Acting on a tip-off, a joint team from the Ambua, Udaygarh, Azad Nagar and
Jobat police stations raided two farms at Dudwa Faliya in Chhota Itara
village, Alirajpur district, at about 7 am on Sunday 11 October. [@1] In the
first field officers found 1,110 plants weighing 1,530 kg and arrested Mukam
Chauhan, 44, on the spot. [@2] In the second they found 930 plants weighing
549 kg and arrested Jagan, 50. [@2]

Police put the total seizure at 2,079 kg of ganja, which the authorities
valued at about one crore rupees. [@1,2] Cases 299/26 and 300/26 were
registered against the two men under sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1,2]
The two outlets differ slightly on the plant count: Bhaskar English reports
2,070 plants against the Free Press Journal's itemised 2,040. [@1,2]

Superintendent of Police Raghuvansh Singh told a press conference the
operation ran under the chief minister's drug de-addiction campaign, with
officers from four stations and the cyber team taking part. [@2]
