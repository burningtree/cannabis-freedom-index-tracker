Officers of the Aspropyrgos crimes division arrested three people aged 30,
33 and 36 — a couple and a relative — accused of drug dealing after an
operation in Drapetsona. [@1] [@2]

Acting on information and aided by a trained police dog, officers searched
two apartments used as dealing bases and seized 907 grammes of raw cannabis
and 206 grammes of processed cannabis, plus 75 g of pink cocaine, 16.36 g
of cocaine, 13 g of MDMA and two MDMA tablets. [@1] They also seized a
ready-to-deal cocaine packet, two precision scales, containers, a pistol
with two magazines, 14 rounds, a knuckle-duster and seven mobile phones.
[@1] A case file for drug and weapons-law violations was filed and the
three were led to the competent prosecutor. [@1]
