An 18-year-old man was arrested after police seized more than P4.9 million worth of suspected
marijuana, kush and liquid marijuana during a buy-bust operation in Urdaneta City, Pangasinan,
on 2 October 2026. [@1,2] The Urdaneta City Police Station led the operation with the
Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency Region 1 and police drug enforcement and intelligence units.
[@1,2]

The suspect, single and jobless, is a native of Bacnotan, La Union, temporarily living in
Urdaneta City. [@1,2] Officers recovered 27,730 grams of dried marijuana leaves, 511 grams of
kush and 240 millilitres of liquid marijuana worth P4,913,400 in total, packed in
vacuum-sealed sachets and compressed bricks, plus vape cartridges with marijuana oil. [@1,2]
They also found a .22-calibre revolver with no serial number loaded with six rounds, a black
iPhone 15, a genuine P500 bill used as buy money and boodle money. [@1,2]
