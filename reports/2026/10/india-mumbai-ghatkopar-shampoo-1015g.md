The Air Intelligence Unit of Mumbai Customs arrested a 30-year-old Ghatkopar
resident for allegedly attempting to smuggle high-grade hydroponic weed
sourced from Bangkok into the country. [@1][@2]

A.A.R. Miyan was intercepted on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, based on spot
profiling shortly after landing at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International
Airport on a flight from Bangkok; officers stopped him near the arrival
hall's exit gate. [@1][@2]

A search of his trolley bag found clothes alongside three shampoo bottles
hiding transparent plastic packets of hydroponic weed totalling 1,015 grams.
[@1][@2] Miyan admitted he took the risk to earn quick money despite knowing
the penalties, was served a summons, had his statement recorded and was
arrested as the AIU began tracing his suspected associates. [@1][@2]
