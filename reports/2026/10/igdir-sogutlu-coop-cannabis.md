Police identified three suspects in Söğütlü Mahallesi, Iğdır, only as M.C.Ş.,
E.V. and M.S.B., born in 2007, 2008 and 2009. [@1] Officers detained all three
after finding cannabis and methamphetamine in a chicken coop they assessed the
suspects were using to hide drugs for sale. [@1]

Narcotics teams of the Iğdır Provincial Police Directorate carried out the
Söğütlü work under the Iğdır Chief Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1] On 25
October 2026 they ran inquiries and technical surveillance in the neighbourhood
aimed at drug trafficking. [@1] In the coop they seized 1.37 grams of cannabis,
recorded as esrar, in one piece, and 15.38 grams of methamphetamine in five
pieces. [@1]

After processing at the police station, the three were referred to the judicial
authorities on 26 October, and all three were remanded. [@1] The inquiry
continues on drug-trafficking allegations, and the proceedings against the
suspects are still open. [@1]
