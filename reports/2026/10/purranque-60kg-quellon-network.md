The case began after investigators noticed marijuana sales in Quellón and the local prosecutor asked
OS-7 to trace the supply chain. [@1,2] On 13 October 2026 officers intercepted the group on Route 5
Sur at the Purranque toll plaza while they were driving south from Santiago. [@1,2]

Five Chilean men travelling in two vehicles were detained. [@1,2] More than 60 kilograms of marijuana
was found in one of the cars, intended for distribution in Quellón and Puerto Montt. [@1,2] Police
also seized both vehicles, mobile phones and cash; Cooperativa says no weapons were found. [@2]

Four of the five had prior criminal records, some under Chile's drug law. [@1,2] Regional prosecutor
Carmen Gloria Wittwer called the haul one of the largest linked to Chiloé supply investigations in
2026. [@2] Results were presented at a press briefing at the Carabineros training school in Puerto
Montt the same morning. [@2]

The five were formalised and ordered into pre-trial detention while the investigation continues. [@1,2]
La Tercera notes that police warned about mobile apps used to alert drivers to checkpoints on the
highway. [@1]
