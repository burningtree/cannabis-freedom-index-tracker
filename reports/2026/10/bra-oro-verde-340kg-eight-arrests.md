Carabinieri of the Bra company, in Cuneo province, dismantled a network of indoor cannabis
greenhouses in Sanfrè, Castellinaldo and Bene Vagienna in the Cuneese and in Ceres in the Lanzo
valleys of Turin province. [@1,2,3] They announced the results on 13 October 2026 as operation Oro
verde. [@1]

Eight Albanian nationals were arrested — five in the act and three under custody orders — and five
other people were reported while remaining at liberty. [@1,2,3] The investigation, begun in January
2026 under the Asti prosecutor's office, had mapped what the Carabinieri called a specialised indoor
cultivation group that also sold advice on grow setups to other crews. [@1,3]

Searches recovered more than 340 kg of cannabis, as flowering plants ready to cut and as dried
marijuana packed in heat-sealed bags, plus 200 grams of hashish and large sums of cash the force
treated as proceeds. [@1,2,3] The Carabinieri put the retail value of the drugs at about €500,000.
[@1,3] The suspects were also accused of aggravated theft of electricity through an illegal bypass of
the E-Distribuzione meter that powered the lamps and fans. [@1,3]
