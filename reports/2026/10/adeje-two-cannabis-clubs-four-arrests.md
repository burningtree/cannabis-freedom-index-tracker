National Police officers in Adeje, in the south of Tenerife, arrested four
people and closed two cannabis clubs in an operation against suspected drug
trafficking. [@1] The two associations were sealed by court order for six
months. [@1]

Some of the products were prepared for consumption. [@2] More than €50,000 in
cash and more than £2,000 were also confiscated. [@1]

The locations of the two clubs and the date of the operation have not been made
public. [@1] The four arrests relate to this investigation and do not mean that
every cannabis association is involved in illegal activity. [@1]
