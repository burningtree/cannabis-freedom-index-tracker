Armed police arrested three people with 110 kg of ganja in Siraha: those
arrested are 43-year-old Mishrilal Saday and 31-year-old Maniklal Saday of
Aurahi rural municipality-2 and 33-year-old Shyamsundar Yadav of
Dhangadhimai municipality-5. [@1]

According to the Armed Police, the ganja in six sacks was recovered while
they were carrying it on their heads; three mobile phones were also recovered
from them, Armed Police Force Nepal No. 7 Battalion headquarters Siraha chief
SP Sumanjung Thapa informed. [@1]

Investigators found smugglers buying ganja from various places in districts
including Udayapur and Sindhuli and trafficking it toward India through
Siraha's Balan, Kamala, Gagan and Ghurmi rivers and border areas. [@1] SP
Thapa said the Armed Police had stepped up surveillance to control drug
smuggling toward India through Siraha's rivers and border zones. [@1]
