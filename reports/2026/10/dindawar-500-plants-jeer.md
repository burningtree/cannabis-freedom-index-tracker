On 18 October 2026 the special narcotics vigilance cell of Basavana Bagewadi police station raided a
farm in Dindawar village in Basavana Bagewadi taluk of Vijayapura district, Karnataka. [@1,2]
Officers said the farmer had grown red gram (toor) on the edges of the plot and more than 500
cannabis plants in the middle. [@1,2]

They arrested Sharanappa Jeer, 56, also named in local coverage as Shivashankar Sharanappa Jeer,
registered a case under the NDPS Act, and seized cannabis they weighed at 202.75 kilograms, valued
at ₹1,01,37,500. [@1,2] Superintendent of Police Laxman Nimbargi credited officers including
Chandrakant Nandareddy and Gurushanth Dashyal. [@1,2]
