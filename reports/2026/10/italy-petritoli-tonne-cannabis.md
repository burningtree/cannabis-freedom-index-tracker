The Guardia di Finanza seized about a tonne of cannabis in a warehouse in Petritoli, in the province
of Fermo, the force said on 15 October 2026. [@1,2] Officers found thousands of plants hanging to
dry, containers of buds and leaves, grinding and separating machines, a forced ventilation and
humidification system, and a scale. [@2]

The cannabis came from a large plantation found earlier on land in a neighbouring municipality that
was available to an agricultural company. [@1,2] Seven people were working in the warehouse,
including the company's legal representative; one worker was undeclared. [@2]

The company's administrator could not show where the finished and semi-finished products were going,
and the agricultural entrepreneur was reported to the Procura of Fermo under the hemp law. [@2] The
reports did not mention any arrests. [@1,2]
