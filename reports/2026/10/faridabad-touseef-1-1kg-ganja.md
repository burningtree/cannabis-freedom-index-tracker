Police in Faridabad arrested Touseef, a tailor who lives in Sanjay Colony, near
Rajiv Colony on 29 October 2026 and recovered 1.1 kilograms of ganja (cannabis)
from him. [@2] The Crime Branch's Uncha Gaon team made the arrest. [@1] The team
acted on a tip-off that a young man would arrive near Rajiv Colony with illegal
drugs, and officers surrounded him there. [@1] They found the ganja when they
searched him. [@1]

According to the police, Touseef uses drugs himself and had bought 1.5 kilograms
of ganja in Hasanpur, in Palwal district, for 15,000 rupees. [@2] The quantity
recovered was less than the 1.5 kilograms police said he had bought. [@2] A case
was registered against him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act at Sector 58 police station. [@1]

Police said they were trying to identify the main supplier and investigate his
wider network. [@1] They described the arrested man as a ganja smuggler. [@1]
