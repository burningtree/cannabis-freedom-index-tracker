National Police officers in the Dominican Republic arrested two men and a woman
during an operation in the Capotillo sector of the Distrito Nacional, Santo
Domingo. [@1] Police spokesman Diego Pesqueira said the search took place on
Calle Duarte, in the alley known as callejón Sibanito. [@1] It formed part of
the hunt for Vladimir Bisamal Yanki, alias “Boca 30”, an 18-year-old wanted by
the authorities. [@1] The detainees were identified as Marcos Augusto Rivera
Díaz, Emilio Lucas and Esperanza Cuevas María, whom police described as the
fugitive's partner. [@1] Rivera Díaz was to be sent to the National Drug Control
Directorate (DNCD), while the three remained in custody for legal proceedings.
[@2]

Agents of the Central Investigation Directorate (DICRIM) found nine packages of
a vegetable believed to be marijuana, 10 plastic containers with a similar
substance and 82 ziplock bags of suspected marijuana. [@1] They also seized a
scale and seven machines in a deteriorated state. [@1] The operation was carried
out by the Operational Department II of the Central Unit for the Search and
Capture of Fugitives, Rebels and Convicted Persons, with the investigation
sub-directorate of Santo Domingo Norte. [@1]

The police said their operations against the sale and distribution of controlled
substances in the country remained active. [@1] The search for Bisamal Yanki
continued. [@2]
