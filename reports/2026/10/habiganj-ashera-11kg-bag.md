Special Response Battalion 9 recovered 11 kilograms of cannabis in an operation
in the Ashera area of Lokra Union, Habiganj Sadar Upazila. [@1] The unit is the
Special Response Battalion, known as SRB-9. [@1] No one could be detained over
the recovery. [@1]

Officers carried out the operation on Friday 30 October at about 11:30 in the
morning. [@1] They acted on secret information. [@1] The cannabis was recovered
from an abandoned black school bag on the bank of a pond. [@1] Inside the bag,
the cannabis was wrapped in khaki-coloured Scotch tape. [@1] The bag had been
left on the pond bank, and the cannabis was found inside it in that wrapping.
[@1]

The recovered cannabis was handed to Habiganj Sadar Model Police Station for
further legal action. [@1] Special Response Battalion 9 said its investigation
into the incident was continuing. [@1] The station was to take the next legal
steps with the cannabis that SRB-9 had recovered. [@1]
