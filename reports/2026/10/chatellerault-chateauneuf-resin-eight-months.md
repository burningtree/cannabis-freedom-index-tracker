On Sunday 25 October 2026, police in Châtellerault made an unexpected drug find
while intervening for another reason, and a man's clumsiness when surprised by
them led to his arrest for drug possession. [@1] The officers had gone to a home
in the Châteauneuf neighbourhood of the town for a reason other than a search
for drugs. [@1] As soon as the door opened, the occupant, faced with the
uniforms, let blocks of cannabis resin fall to the floor in front of them. [@1]
They seized one and a half plaques of the illicit product without effort. [@1]
The suspect, a man in his thirties, was arrested and placed in police custody.
[@1]

He was sent before the court in Poitiers and tried the following Tuesday under a
comparution sur reconnaissance préalable de culpabilité, or CRPC, a guilty-plea
procedure in which the defendant accepts the charge before a sentence is set.
[@1] The court sentenced him to eight months in prison, which will be the
subject of an aménagement, the French term for an adjustment of how a prison
sentence is carried out. [@1]
