A mob attacked a police team investigating a self-styled godman's cannabis crop
at Vaipet (Vayupet) village in Sirikonda mandal, Adilabad district, on Saturday
10 October 2026. [@1] Around 50 residents, suspected to be followers of the
godman, attacked the officers with sticks, police said. [@1] Villagers said
police had abused them with objectionable language, and pelted stones and
attacked with logs. [@2] Telangana Today reported four policemen injured,
including an inspector, an ASI and two constables. [@1] They were taken to
RIMS-Adilabad, where their condition was said to be stable. [@1] Inspector
Ramesh suffered head injuries and constable Shivaprasad a fractured hand, Deccan
Chronicle reported. [@2]

Police had arrested Chahakati Ramu, a self-styled godman, a day earlier. [@1]
Ramu, 62, is known in his village as the maharaj. [@2] Police recovered 18
plants worth Rs 1.80 lakh from his possession. [@1] He was taken into custody
while trying to flee during the raid. [@1] Police said he grew the plants for
his own consumption and to sell them. [@2]

Additional police were sent to Vaipet and prohibitory orders were imposed in the
village. [@2] Police registered an attempt-to-murder case against around 20
villagers. [@1] Superintendent of Police Ankhil Mahajan named five injured
officers, including two armed reserve constables. [@3] On Tuesday 13 October,
authorities announced the arrest of 20 villagers over the attack. [@3] Mahajan
said they were held on charges of obstructing police from discharging their
duties. [@3] The 20 included Ramu, the police said. [@3] Villagers conspired to
attack the police after Ramu's arrest, damaged the police vehicle and stole a
policeman's cell phone. [@3]
