The Anti-Narcotics Force foiled a Mastung-to-Quetta drug run in Quetta,
Balochistan, intercepting an ambulance near Sona Khan Chowk on intelligence that
a large narcotics consignment would move under ambulance cover. [@1] Under the
floor officers found a secret compartment holding 60 kg of hashish. [@1]

A suspected key member of the trafficking network was arrested, and a case
was registered under the Control of Narcotic Substances Act, 1997, with more
arrests expected as the network is mapped. [@1] The ANF noted traffickers'
use of emergency-service vehicles to dodge checks, citing a recent similar
ambulance seizure in Shikarpur, Sindh. [@1]
