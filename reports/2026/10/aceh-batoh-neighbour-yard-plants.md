Lueng Bata police arrested a 43-year-old man, identified only as AD, for
secretly growing dozens of cannabis plants in his neighbour's yard in Gampong
Batoh, Banda Aceh. [@1] AD, who had tended the narcotic crop since August 2026,
was caught red-handed watering the forbidden plants on Tuesday 13 October.
[@1]

Police seized 21 suspected cannabis plants from the yard: 16 in polybags and
five planted directly in the ground. [@1] The case broke when the yard's owner,
52-year-old Edi Mareansyah, grew suspicious of what was growing in his yard.
[@1] At first Edi did not know what AD had planted, but as the plants grew he
suspected cannabis. [@1]

Edi reported the find to local village officials, who came to the yard around
2 p.m. the same day and found AD watering the suspected cannabis. [@1] AD
was arrested and taken to Lueng Bata station around 3 p.m.; police processed
the scene, secured the suspect and the evidence, and are gathering statements for
the investigation. [@1] Kapolsek AKP Jufri spoke for the operation under city
police chief Kombes Teguh Priyambodo Nugroho. [@1]
