The Fifth Sentencing Court of San Salvador sentenced Daniel Eduardo Ramírez Carbajal, 24, to eight
years in prison for possession and holding of marijuana for the purpose of trafficking, an offence
against public health. [@1] La Noticia SV reported the sentence on 6 October 2026. [@1]

The court said the events were on the night of 8 December 2025, on the main Las Palmas street in
the Reparto de Apopa neighbourhood, San Salvador Oeste. [@1] Soldiers on patrol saw him in a
vehicle and found drugs on the passenger side, then called the police. [@1] Officers seized seven
medium portions of plant material. [@1] An expert examination found 188.2 grams of marijuana,
valued at 664.34 dollars on the illicit market, enough for about 376.4 cigarettes. [@1] An
extraction from his phone found messages using language associated with drug dealing and
photographs linked to drugs. [@1] On that evidence the court imposed the eight-year term. [@1]
