Bhojpur police arrested 19-year-old Ekraj Magar, of Aamchok Rural
Municipality-7, with 244 kilograms and 770 grams of cannabis. [@1] He was taken
from the riverbed of Dhiring Khola in Hatuwagadhi Rural Municipality-9, in
Bhojpur district of Koshi Province. [@1] District Police Office information
officer Inspector Rakesh Rai said the arrest was on Tuesday afternoon, the day
before the 7 October briefing. [@1] Naya Patrika, also citing the district
police office, said officers took the same 19-year-old into custody at 3:30 in
the morning on 20 Ashoj 2083 with 244.770 kilograms of cannabis. [@2]

At a press meeting the next day at the District Police Office, Inspector Rai
said police recovered six sacks of cannabis, a motorcycle registered Ko.Pra.
01-02-046 Pa 8651 and a mobile phone from Magar. [@1] Naya Patrika reported the
same motorcycle number and a mobile phone, and wrote that the cannabis was in 96
sacks. [@2] Daksu Media also reported six sacks, a motorcycle and a mobile
phone, and gave the weight as 244 kilograms and 700 grams. [@3]

A team of six from Area Police Office Ghoretar, led by Inspector Uday Kumar Rai,
and two officers from the district police office, led by Assistant Sub-Inspector
Pravin Rai, made the arrest. [@1] The Bhojpur District Court extended custody by
20 days so the cannabis investigation could continue. [@1] Daksu Media said the
arrest followed a special tip and that the 20-day court extension was already in
force. [@3] Police said they were stepping up the district campaign against
cannabis and other drugs. [@1]
