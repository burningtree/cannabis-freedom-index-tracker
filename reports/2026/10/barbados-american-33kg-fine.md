The Barbados Police Service Narcotics Unit arrested and charged 51-year-old
American national Tykeesha Genen Scott-Dickens of Rosedale, New York, with
importation, possession and trafficking of cannabis and intent to supply, for
offences committed on 13 October 2026. [@1]

The suspected cannabis weighed 3.3 kilogrammes with an estimated street value of
BDS$52,800. [@1] Scott-Dickens appeared before Magistrate Keitha Ellis in the
District 'B' Magistrates' Court on 14 October, pleaded guilty to the offences,
and was fined BDS$13,000 payable forthwith, or nine months' imprisonment at Dodds
Prison. [@1]
