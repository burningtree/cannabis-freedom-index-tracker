Police in Cauca arrested a 34-year-old driver, identified only as ESUA, in the
act of moving more than 2.6 tonnes of marijuana described as the creepy type,
hidden in a turbo cargo truck. [@1] Preliminary investigations said the load was
bound for illegal markets in Peru and Chile. [@1] He was the driver of a 2007
Hyundai HD 65 turbo truck, plates UPQ254, in which the load was found. [@1]

Units of the criminal investigation section, Sijín, and the National Police
antinarcotics directorate seized 1,186 packages weighing 2,663 kilograms. [@1]
The organised-crime line of Sijín, attached to the Cauca police department,
reported 2,563 kilograms, and the antinarcotics directorate another 100
kilograms. [@1] Authorities estimated the marijuana's commercial value at
266,300,000 pesos and also seized the truck, which they valued at about 95
million pesos. [@1] The search was on the Pan-American Highway in the stretch
through Cauca department. [@1]

A guarantee-control judge declared the arrest legal, and the driver was left
with Specialised Prosecutor's Office 52 of the directorate against drug
trafficking. [@1] He was to remain in jail while answering a charge of
trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics under article 376 of the Penal
Code. [@1] The same account says he will stay in jail while the court decides
his legal situation. [@1] Authorities said they still had to establish where the
marijuana came from, who sent it, and whether the driver acted alone or for a
network able to move large loads into other South American countries. [@1]
