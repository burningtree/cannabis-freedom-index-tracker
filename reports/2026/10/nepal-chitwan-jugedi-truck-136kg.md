Police arrested Ranjit Sunar, 27, a resident of Pandraang in Bhimfedi
Rural Municipality-4, Makwanpur, on Wednesday morning at Jugedi of Bharatpur
Metropolitan City-29 with approximately 136 kg 500 grams of marijuana. [@1]
A District Police Office Chitwan team searching his truck, registration Na.5
Kha 8676, travelling from Kathmandu to Pathlaiya, found the drug packed in ten
sacks and arrested the driver. [@1] Police say further investigation is
underway. [@1]
