Police in Kiambu County arrested three suspects and seized 483 rolls of
cannabis sativa in two intelligence-led operations targeting distribution
and trafficking. [@1]

In Wangige Town, officers from Kibiku Police Station acted on a tip-off and
arrested two suspects allegedly distributing cannabis sativa; a search after
the arrests recovered 73 rolls of bhang from the suspects, and a hideout
search found 307 more rolls — 380 rolls plus about 10 g of unprocessed
cannabis in all. [@1]

In a separate operation at Corner One, officers from Kimuchu Police Station
arrested another suspect found with 103 rolls of cannabis sativa. [@1] The
three suspects were placed in lawful custody pending arraignment, and the
drugs secured as exhibits. [@1]
