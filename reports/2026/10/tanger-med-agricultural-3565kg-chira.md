Morocco’s National Police said officers at Tanger Med port, working with customs, intercepted more
than 3.5 tonnes of cannabis resin on the morning of Sunday 11 October 2026. [@1,2]

The resin, known locally as chira, was concealed inside crates of agricultural produce on a
Moroccan-registered international freight truck during a joint inspection. [@1,2] Le Brief, citing
the General Directorate of National Security (DGSN), said the packing was meant to facilitate
smuggling abroad. [@2]

Police put the weight at 3 tonnes and 565 kilograms. [@1,2] The driver, a 35-year-old Moroccan, was
placed under a judicial investigation supervised by the competent prosecutor while detectives looked
for other domestic or international links. [@1,2]

Le360 described the case as part of wider border efforts against trafficking networks; neither
outlet reported further arrests. [@1,2]
