Border Guard Bangladesh detained one person on Thursday 29 October during
separate anti-drug and anti-smuggling drives on the Cumilla border. [@1]
Jugantor, citing a press release from Cumilla Battalion (10 BGB) issued that
day, said the person was held on suspicion of involvement in smuggling. [@2] The
detainee and the drugs were handed to Cumilla Kotwali Model Police Station for
legal action. [@2]

Patrol teams from the Bibirbazar and Bauyara Bazar border outposts of Cumilla
Battalion carried out two drives in Adarsha Sadar upazila. [@1] The searches
were at Bibirbazar land port and Dakshin Suryanagar, about 300 and 800 yards
inside Bangladesh from the border. [@2] BGB is Bangladesh's border force, and a
BOP is one of its border outposts. [@1]

Daily Naya Diganta reported that the battalion seized 15 kg of cannabis, 117
bottles of Escaf syrup and 25,000 tapentadol tablets, and recovered a further 9
kg of cannabis from the person detained. [@1] Jugantor, citing the same
battalion's press release, reported the cannabis as 24 kg together with the same
counts of syrup and tablets. [@2] Escaf is a codeine cough syrup commonly
trafficked on this border, and tapentadol is an opioid painkiller sold as
tablets. [@1]

BGB estimated the market value of the whole mixed seizure at Tk 51,30,800. [@1]
Lieutenant Colonel Mir Ali Ejaz, commanding officer of the Cumilla battalion,
said BGB operations to stop smuggling and cross-border crime were continuing.
