Police in Anantnag district, Jammu and Kashmir, arrested eight people in
separate operations across the district in a single day, a police spokesman said
on 29 October 2026. [@3] Police recovered charas, a cannabis resin, and heroin,
and registered eight cases under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act. [@1] Police said investigations in all the cases were continuing.
[@2]

At Pehru Crossing, a team from the Sherbagh police post stopped Abid Ahmad Mir
of Chak Hangulgund, Kokernag, and seized about 118 grams of charas. [@2]
Kokernag police arrested Mohammad Arif Naikoo of Gadool on the link road between
Bidder and Irkumoo with about 12 grams of charas, in a case registered as FIR
No. 180/2026. [@2] Near Kirkadal in Bijbehara, Tajdar Amin Khan of Sitari
Mohalla was arrested with about 20.6 grams of heroin, the only heroin seizure of
the day, under FIR No. 329/2026. [@2] Anantnag police station officers arrested
Amir Hussain Sheikh of Donipawa and Bilal Ahmad Dar of Halpora, Kokernag, with
about 8 grams and 17 grams of charas respectively. [@2]

At Sangam, a police post team arrested Mehboob Hussain Shah of Surankote,
Poonch, with about 19 grams of charas. [@2] At Link Road Shehlipora, opposite
NH-244 and a stone quarry in Achabal, police arrested Moomin Nazir Wagay of
Kanganhal with about 19 grams of charas including packing material, in FIR No.
117/2026. [@2] A Khanabal police post team arrested Rahil Mushtaq Bhat of Aung
Matipora, Anantnag, at Harnag with about 150 grams, the largest seizure of the
day, in FIR No. 401/2026. [@2]

Police said they would keep up action against trafficking and appealed to the
public to help work towards a drug-free society. [@3]
