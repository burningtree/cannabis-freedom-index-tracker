Customs officers found 407 kg of cannabis resin on the morning of Thursday 22 October
2026 in the trailer of a Romanian lorry parked at the Adrets-de-l'Estérel service area on
motorway A8 in the Var. [@1,2] The resin was hidden in fourteen cartons mixed into a load of
Christmas goods bound for Italy. [@1]

The 39-year-old driver was sentenced on Monday 26 October 2026 at the Draguignan criminal
court to four years in prison, a permanent ban from French territory and a customs fine of
3.26 million euros. [@1,2] In custody he first denied the shipment, then said he had agreed in
Barcelona to drive it to Rome because of financial pressure. [@1]
