In Saturday raids across the state, the Anti-Narcotics Task Force of the West
Bengal Special Task Force arrested five people from an area under Nimta
police station in North 24 Parganas and seized around 90 kg of cannabis
valued at about Rs 44.5 lakh. [@1] Officers also confiscated Rs 5 lakh in
cash and three vehicles. [@1]

Preliminary investigation suggests two of the arrested men are Odisha
residents who had allegedly come to supply the cannabis, while the other
three are accused of possessing and consuming narcotics, police sources said.
[@1] The STF has opened investigations to trace the source and destination of
the load and identify others in the network. [@1] The same article pairs this
case with a separate Bagdogra seizure of 1 kg of brown sugar, a heroin-class
drug outside this tracker's scope. [@1] The article's photograph is credited
IANS/representative rather than to the scene, so no image is listed. [@1]
