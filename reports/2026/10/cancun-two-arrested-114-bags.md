Alejandro “N” and Alfonso Daniel “N” were arrested on Avenida 127 in
Supermanzana 101, Cancún, by officers of the state Citizen Security Secretariat
(SSC). [@2] According to the police account, the two men were beside the boot of
a car, exchanging transparent plastic bags of dried herb that resembled
marijuana. [@2] One of the men moved towards the vehicle as police approached,
while the other stayed where he was. [@1] The officers intervened and carried
out a preventive inspection of both men. [@1]

The inspection found 114 transparent bags of dried herb resembling marijuana and
31 bags of solid fragments resembling cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized two
waist bags, two mobile phones and a red Mazda 3 i Touring with Quintana Roo
plates. [@3] The men and the seized items were handed to the state prosecutor's
office and its unit specialised in narcomenudeo, small-scale drug dealing, to
determine their legal situation. [@2]

The police report said officers from the SSC's investigation directorate were on
prevention and surveillance patrols. [@3] Turquesa News noted that both men must
be presumed innocent until their legal situation is resolved. [@2]
