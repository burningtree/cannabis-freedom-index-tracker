Police in Fuengirola arrested two people in separate operations involving
an electric-scooter rider and a suspected unlicensed pub at the marina.
[@1]

On 3 October, officers following up building-noise and a strong drug
smell inspected premises at the marina operating as an unlicensed pub,
seizing 5.09 kg of marijuana, 1.35 kg of hashish, EUR 40, a mobile phone
and other items; the arrested man resisted and needed medical attention.
[@1] Separately on 13 October, a scooter rider stopped on Avenida Jesus
Santos Rein for phone and helmet offences was found with 18 bags of
cocaine (15.2 g), eight bags of ketamine (9 g), 11 Viagra and 19
alprazolam tablets plus EUR 305, and was taken to the Policia Nacional
station. [@1]
