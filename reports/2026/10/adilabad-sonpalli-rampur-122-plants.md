As part of the continued crackdown on ganja cultivation, police seized 122
ganja plants during two separate raids in Adilabad district on Friday,
estimating their value at around Rs 12.20 lakh. [@1]

A raid at Sonpalli village in Ichoda mandal led to the seizure of 98 cannabis
plants grown in the backyard of a house belonging to Todasam Muyya, 78, who
was booked under the NDPS Act. [@1] In the other case, police seized 24
cannabis plants grown in a cotton field at Rampur village in Gadiguda mandal
and registered an NDPS case against the cultivator, Gedam Rajeshwar, 38, of
Rampur. [@1]

Police said they would recommend to the departments concerned that benefits
under government schemes be discontinued to the cultivator of the prohibited
crop. [@1]
