On 8 October 2026 at about 1:30 p.m., Shreveport City Marshal’s Office and police
warrant officers went to a home in the 2900 block of Grassmere Street to arrest a
fugitive. [@1] When someone ran from the house, the Violent Crimes Abatement Team
obtained a search warrant. [@1]

The search recovered about 10 pounds of marijuana, $12,395 in cash and three firearms,
the city said. [@1,2] Robert Henderson was booked on possession of marijuana with intent
to distribute, possession of a firearm by a convicted felon and illegal carrying of
a weapon with a controlled dangerous substance. [@1] Semja Henderson was booked on
possession of marijuana with intent to distribute. [@1]
