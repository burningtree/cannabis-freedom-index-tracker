Two 20-year-old men were arrested in Rincón de Centeno, Juventino Rosas, during
a security operation involving the Fuerzas de Seguridad Pública del Estado
(FSPE). [@2] They were identified as Juan Pablo “N” and Alejandro “N”, who lived
in San José de Yustis, Celaya. [@1] The stop happened on 5 October 2026 on Calle
Corregidora, during preventive patrols, when officers saw a Nissan whose driver
was making manoeuvres that endangered traffic. [@3] Under the rear seat,
officers found a bag with 22 wrappers of green, dry herb with the
characteristics of marijuana. [@1]

The seized herb weighed about 687 grams, which the authority said was equivalent
to about 687 doses. [@1] A magazine loaded with 26 rounds was also found inside
the car. [@1] The car was a grey sedan. [@2]

The two men, the suspected marijuana, the magazine, the cartridges and the car
were handed over to the federal prosecutor's office in Celaya, which will
continue the investigation and decide their legal status. [@1]
