In a coordinated and ongoing crackdown on illicit brews and drugs, police
arrested two suspected traffickers and seized over 25 kg of cannabis sativa
in separate operations in Kiambu and Bungoma counties, the NPS said. [@1]

In Juja Town, officers from Juja Police Station intercepted a motor vehicle
in the KK Towers area; vehicle registration KDV 137V was stopped and
searched, recovering approximately 20 kg of cannabis sativa. [@1] The driver
was taken into custody and the narcotics secured as exhibits; the statement
did not identify the driver or say where the drugs were headed. [@1]

In Bungoma County, officers acting on a member of the public's information
searched a rental residence at Kamukuywa Market in Kimilili Sub-County,
recovering bhang stored in two green sacks and a blue basin, plus four
packets of rolling papers and a rolling stick. [@1] The suspect was arrested
and escorted to Bungoma Police Station for processing and arraignment; the
NPS did not name the suspect or specify the house quantity. [@1]
