Police in Regional Division 6 (East Berbice-Corentyne) found about 23.854 pounds of suspected cannabis during an operation at Sheet Anchor Village, East Canje, Berbice, between 5.50 p.m. and 6.10 p.m. on Saturday 17 October 2026. [@1,2]

Ranks searched a bushy area on an unoccupied plot and found six five-gallon buckets, five of which held black bags wrapped in transparent plastic containing leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis. [@1,2] The cannabis was taken to the Central Police Station, weighed at 23.854 pounds, and lodged. [@1,2]

No arrests were made and investigations are continuing. [@1,2]
