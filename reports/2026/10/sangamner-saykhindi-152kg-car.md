The Local Crime Branch of Ahilyanagar Police, in a joint operation with
Sangamner city police, seized 152.61 kg of ganja worth Rs 76.3 lakh being
smuggled to Pune. [@1] On directions from Ahilyanagar SP Mummaka Sudarshan,
LCB PI Santosh Mutkule formed a special task force led by PSI Vikas Jadhav;
on Monday, while collecting criminal intelligence in Sangamner city, the team
tipped that a large drug consignment was moving from Nashik toward
Sangamner in a car of a specific registration number. [@1]

Police laid a trap near Saykhindi Phata along the Nashik-Pune highway,
intercepted the vehicle and searched it before witnesses, seizing the ganja;
occupants Dattatray Ichake, 33, and Shrihari Midgule, 35, both from Shirur
in Pune district, were taken into custody. [@1] Police also seized the car
valued at Rs 12 lakh, two fake high-security registration plates, mobile
phones, Rs 17,970 in cash and empty plastic packaging material. [@1]
