Gardaí seized over €3 million worth of illegal vape and cannabis products in
searches across five counties as part of Operation Tara. [@1][@2]

As part of an ongoing operation targeting an organised crime group, more than
100 officers attached to the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau
and other national units searched properties in Longford, Mayo, Roscommon,
Wexford and Carlow on Wednesday, October 14, 2026. [@1][@2]

Alongside the products, officers seized €235,000 in cash, £3,000 sterling,
$3,500 and several high-value vehicles. [@1][@2] The seized products are
being sent to Forensic Science Ireland for analysis, and no arrests had been
made as investigations continued. [@1][@2]
