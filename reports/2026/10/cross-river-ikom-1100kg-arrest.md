The Cross River State Command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) raided Yala Nkum
(Iyala Nkun) village in Ikom Local Government Area on 4 October 2026 after surveillance, and
arrested 46-year-old Adama Inuwa, who said she was from Ogoja Local Government Area. [@1,2]

The operation recovered 181 bags of suspected cannabis sativa weighing about 1,100 kilograms,
along with 17.15 kilograms of cannabis seeds. [@1,2] The command's media and advocacy officer,
Sebastian Lebo, signed the statement announcing the arrest. [@1]

Inuwa has been taken into custody, and the agency says investigations are continuing to identify
possible networks behind the drugs; she will be charged in court once investigations and legal
procedures are complete. [@1] The command described the seizure as part of its effort to disrupt
the supply of drugs in the state and keep them from reaching the public, especially young people.
