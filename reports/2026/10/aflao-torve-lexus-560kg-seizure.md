The Customs Division of the Ghana Revenue Authority intercepted more than
half a tonne of suspected cannabis at Torve, near Akatsi in the Volta
Region. [@1] [@2]

The Preventive Monitoring Unit of the Aflao Sector Command, acting on
intelligence on the night of 15 October 2026, tracked a black Lexus
believed to be carrying narcotics; the driver tried to evade arrest but
abandoned the vehicle after it developed a mechanical fault. [@1] [@2] A
search found 1,065 compressed parcels weighing 560.4 kg, valued at about
GH¢11.2 million, plus a Ghana Card bearing the name Kunuhor Gidsway in a
vehicle fitted with falsified plates. [@1] [@2] The haul was jointly
authenticated by the NIB, Defence Intelligence, NACOC, the FDA and
National Security and handed to NACOC for investigation. [@1] [@2]
