At Enhere Junction in Warri, Delta State, NDLEA officers arrested 46-year-old Helen
Bakporo Samson on Monday 19 October 2026 and recovered 6.0 kg of skunk. [@1,2]

Director of Media and Advocacy Femi Babafemi included the arrest in a Sunday statement
summarising recoveries across nine states; Citizens Compass identified Samson as physically
challenged and said she was held with the cannabis at the junction. [@1,2] The reports
name no accomplices and give no court outcome. [@1,2]
