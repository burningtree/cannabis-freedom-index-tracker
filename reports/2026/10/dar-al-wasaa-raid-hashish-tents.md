The Lebanese Army Command's Directorate of Orientation said in a statement
dated 13 October 2026 that a unit raided the homes of wanted men in Dar
al-Wasaa, Baalbek. [@1] Troops seized about 22 kg of hashish and about 22,000
Captagon pills. [@1]

They destroyed 14 tents used for growing marijuana and arrested two Syrians,
identified only as M.H. and A.B., for working with a wanted man. [@1] The
army said the seized drugs were handed to the competent authorities and an
investigation was opened under judicial supervision. [@1]

The same statement says other army units arrested 134 Syrians in
al-Mushrifah–Hermel, Madfoun–Batroun, Chadra, Dabbabiyeh and Wadi
Khaled–Akkar for being in the country illegally. [@1]
