Valsad district police seized 2,535.1 kg of intoxicating tablets
containing cannabis, valued at Rs 12.67 crore, and arrested two men in
raids at Vapi and Dadra, with two other alleged network members wanted.
[@1] [@2]

The district's Anti-Narcotics Wing and Special Operation Group first
searched the Vapi house of Mohammad Amin Mohammad Basu Khan, 46, in Raza
Gali, Ahmednagar, seizing 387.75 kg of tablets worth Rs 1.93 crore. [@1]
Tracing the supply led to Pradip alias Mannu Verma, 28, whose shop near
the Lavachha border in Dadra yielded another 2,147.35 kg worth Rs 10.73
crore and his arrest; two mobile phones and electricity bills were also
taken. [@1] [@2] Khan and Verma allegedly acted with Anurag Gupta of
Kanpur and Salman Iqbal Siddiqui of Mumbai, both wanted. [@1]
