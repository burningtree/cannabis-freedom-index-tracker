The Government Railway Police recovered 48.9 kg of ganja in two separate
incidents at Ferozepur railway station and Ferozepur Cantonment railway station
in Punjab. [@1] In the first, a team led by ASI Jatinder Singh stopped two
suspicious men carrying a plastic bag at Ferozepur station and found 18.4 kg of
ganja on them. [@1]

The two men were identified as Shivcharan of Sunder Nagar in Faridkot and
Shivraj Singh of Shahdeen Wala village in Ferozepur district. [@1] A case was
registered against them under Sections 20, 61 and 85 of the NDPS Act. [@1]

In the second incident, a team led by ASI Surjit Singh was at Ferozepur
Cantonment station for anti-sabotage checks when a Railway Protection Force
constable tipped them about an unattended bag on a seat in a general coach of
the Tripura Sundari Express, standing on the washing line. [@1] The bag held
30.5 kg of ganja; the case was registered against an unidentified person, whom
police were trying to trace. [@1] Both first information reports were dated 25
October. [@1]
