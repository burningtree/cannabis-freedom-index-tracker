A three-month operation against drug supply and criminal proceeds in Taranaki ended with search
warrants at two New Plymouth properties last week, six arrests and about $15,000 seized. [@1]
Officers found cash, cannabis, methamphetamine, a .308 rifle, shotgun ammunition and a homemade
taser, plus stolen goods linked to old burglaries. [@1]

A 43-year-old man faces drugs, weapons and receiving charges and was due in New Plymouth District
Court on 15 October; a 30-year-old gang member was due on 20 and 22 October on weapons, drugs and
receiving charges. [@1] A 27-year-old New Plymouth woman faces cannabis possession and supply
charges as well as methamphetamine and utensil charges; she was remanded in custody to reappear
on 29 October. [@1] Two more 31-year-old men were also due before the court. [@1]
