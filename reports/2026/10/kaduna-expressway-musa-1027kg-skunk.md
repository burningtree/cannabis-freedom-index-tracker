Officers of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency on patrol along the
Zaria-Kaduna Expressway in Kaduna State arrested Mohammed Musa, 29, with 31
jumbo bags of skunk and 775 blocks of the same substance, together weighing
1,027 kilogrammes. [@1,2] The seizure was announced by NDLEA spokesman Femi
Babafemi in the agency's Sunday 11 October statement covering a nationwide
sweep, reported by TVC News that morning and by Voice of Nigeria's Charles
Ogba the same day. [@1,2]

The Kaduna interception was one item in a roundup headlined by the
rearrest of an 83-year-old grandmother in Abia State. [@1] The statement
places it among operations in Lagos, Rivers, Taraba, Kano, Edo, Cross
River, Ondo and Plateau states, all of them credited to intelligence-led
work and praised by NDLEA chairman Brig Gen Mohamed Buba Marwa (retd).
[@1,2] No separate date was given for the expressway arrest within the
sweep. [@1] Both outlets give the combined weight identically as 1,027
kilogrammes. [@1,2]
