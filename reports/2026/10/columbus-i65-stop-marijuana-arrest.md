Benjamin Bracewell, 49, and Sylvia Flowers, 37, both of Norman, Indiana, were in
an SUV that Columbus police pulled over for speeding on Interstate 65 near the
64-mile marker at about 8:30 p.m. on 28 October 2026. [@1] The stop was near the
Walesboro exit. [@2] A police dog alerted to the smell of drugs, and a search of
the SUV found marijuana, police said. [@2] Officers also reportedly found a
round object wrapped in plastic near Flowers that they believe she tried to
discard. [@1] Both were arrested for the preliminary charge of possession of
marijuana. [@1] More marijuana and methamphetamine were found in Flowers'
clothing at the jail, according to police. [@2]

Police said the object held two bags: one with 449 grams (almost a pound) of
suspected methamphetamine and another almost 27 grams of suspected fentanyl.
[@1] After further investigation, Bracewell and Flowers were additionally
charged with dealing a narcotic drug and dealing methamphetamine, both level-2
felonies. [@1] All charges are merely accusations, and the defendants are
presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty. [@1]
