Valsad district police seized 243.67 kg of cannabis-based narcotic
substance and arrested a 32-year-old man in a raid at a residential flat
in Vapi, Gujarat, on 25 October 2026. [@1] [@2]

Acting on Dungra police tip-off information received by Sub-Inspector
K.R. Chotaliya, a team raided Room G/8 in Building E/4 of Lakeview
Residency, where occupant Kartik alias Pappu Biswal, employed and
resident in Dungra and originally of Balasore, Odisha, was allegedly
storing narcotics for retail sale; he was apprehended on the spot. [@1]
[@2] The haul was valued at Rs 1,21,83,500 with a Rs 5,000 mobile phone
also seized; Biswal was booked under the NDPS Act. [@2] Co-resident
Banmali Patra was named as a wanted accused. [@1] [@2]
