The Vapi town police's SOG and LCB teams raided a rented first-floor
flat on Charwada Road on 25 October 2026 on a tip-off that a large
consignment of narcotics had been delivered. [@1] [@2]

Officers recovered 243.67 kg of cannabis, valued at Rs 1.46 crore, hidden
in multiple bags inside the flat; occupant Kartik Biswal, who was
present, was arrested. [@1] [@2] Investigation found Banmali Patra of
Odisha had rented the house and arranged delivery of the Odisha-sourced
cannabis for distribution. [@1] [@2]
