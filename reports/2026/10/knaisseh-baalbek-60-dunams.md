The Lebanese Army said on Saturday 10 October 2026 that one of its units had destroyed about 60 dunams of land planted with cannabis in the town of Knaisseh, in the Baalbek district. [@1,2] That is about 6 hectares. [@1] The same unit arrested 17 Syrian workers who had no legal papers and seized equipment they had for harvesting the crop. [@1,2] The seized items were handed over, and an investigation of the people arrested began under the competent judicial authorities. [@1,2]

The English communiqué calls the crop hashish as well as cannabis plants. [@1] The Arabic communiqué calls it hashish hemp, حشيشة الكيف. [@2] Neither text names the workers, gives a weight, or says whether anyone else was sought. [@1,2]

The army's Arabic statement includes four photographs of soldiers cutting plants, a tractor in the field, trucks in the crop, and smoke over cut piles. [@2]
