Following Hawks intelligence that dagga was being cultivated and possessed at a farm in the
Nelspruit area, a multidisciplinary team searched the premises on Wednesday 7 October 2026.
[@1,2] The intervention brought together serious organised crime investigators with the White
River K9 unit, the National Intervention Unit, the Tactical Response Team and the local criminal
record centre. [@1]

Officers uncovered an established dagga plantation and processed dagga extract, along with two
rifles, two handguns and a substantial quantity of live ammunition held on expired firearm
licences. [@1,2] A 71-year-old man and a 32-year-old woman were arrested on charges under the
Firearms Control Act and the Drugs and Drug Trafficking Act, including dagga cultivation and
possession, and appeared before the Nelspruit District Court on 8 October. [@1,2] Hawks
provincial head Major General Nico Gerber said the operation showed the value of intelligence-led,
multidisciplinary policing. [@1,2]
