Drug-control officers in Ishim detained a man born in 1977 suspected of
illegal drug possession after checking operational information, Tyumen
Seichas reported on 30 October 2026. [@1] With witnesses present, police
searched his home and found five jars of plant material. [@1]

Expert testing identified the contents as marijuana weighing 2,622 grams. [@1,3]
The man told investigators he had bought the drug for personal use. [@2,3] An
Ishim district investigator opened a criminal case under part 2 of article 228
of the Criminal Code for large-scale possession. [@2,3] A judge ordered him not
to leave his place of residence pending investigation. [@1]

Each of the five jars was a five-litre container. [@3]
