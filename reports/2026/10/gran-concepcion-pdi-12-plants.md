Four people were detained in Gran Concepción after the Antinarcotics Brigade of
the Policía de Investigaciones (PDI), Chile's investigative police, intercepted
a vehicle and carried out three searches. [@1] The four are two men, aged 40 and
29, and two women, aged 34 and 29. [@1] Three have criminal records and had
previously been convicted of drug trafficking, and one was serving a
supervised-release sentence when the operation took place. [@1] The brigade said
they belonged to a criminal structure linked to drug trafficking that was moving
illicit substances into the Biobío region from other parts of the country. [@2]

Subprefect Germán De la Hera, the brigade's acting chief, said they were
travelling to San Pedro de la Paz in a vehicle carrying 17 kilograms of cocaine
base and 1.5 kilograms of ketamine. [@1] Officers then executed three
entry-and-search warrants in San Pedro de la Paz, Coronel and Concepción,
seizing a further 1.4 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride and cannabis plants.
[@1] In one of the properties they found an indoor grow with 12 cannabis plants.
[@1] The operation also took 500 rounds of .40-calibre and 9 mm ammunition. [@1]
Officers also seized a Bersa firearm whose serial number had been erased, along
with mobile phones and cash. [@1] Mayor Juan Pablo Spoerer of San Pedro de la
Paz said the main finds were concentrated there and that the property had every
characteristic of what is known as a casa narco, a house used for dealing drugs.
[@1]

Prosecutor Carla Hernández said the Public Prosecutor's Office will ask that the
accused be held in preventive detention. [@1] All four are to be formally
charged under Law 20.000, Chile's drug law, and under the firearms and
ammunition law. [@1] For drug trafficking they face a minimum sentence of three
years and one day. [@1]
