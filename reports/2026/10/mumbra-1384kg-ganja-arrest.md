An anti-narcotics squad of Mumbra police was on patrol at Harani Vasti, Mumbra,
when officers saw a man walking and behaving suspiciously. [@1] The officers
stopped him before he could run and a search found narcotics on him. [@1] Police
took 18-year-old Sahil Siraj Shaikh, a resident of Mumbra, into custody and
seized 1.384 kg of ganja from him. [@1]

Police valued the ganja at ₹28,229. [@1] The operation was led by Assistant
Police Inspector Dhage, and officers searched him after his answers to their
questions seemed inconsistent. [@2] A case was registered against Shaikh at
Mumbra police station under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act,
and he was arrested. [@1]

The arrest came during a strict campaign against people who sell and use
narcotics in the Mumbra police station area, ordered by the Thane city police
commissioner. [@1] Police described the arrest as part of a sustained effort to
curb the distribution and consumption of illicit substances in Mumbra. [@2]
