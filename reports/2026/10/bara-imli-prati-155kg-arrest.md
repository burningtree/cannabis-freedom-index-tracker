Police arrested a car driver with 155.635 kg of marijuana in Jitpur-Simara
Sub-Metropolitan City-8, Bara, on Friday 2 October 2026. [@1]

Acting on a tip-off, police stopped a car with registration number Ba 13 Cha 4734 at Imli
Prati at around 9 am while it was travelling from Manahari toward Birgunj. [@1] Officers
found 155.635 kg of marijuana concealed inside the car and wrapped in red plastic. [@1]

Police arrested 28-year-old Ambir Dimdung of Raksirang Rural Municipality-1, Makwanpur,
who was driving the car. [@1] The car, seized marijuana and driver are kept at the Area
Police Office, Simara, as the investigation continues. [@1]
