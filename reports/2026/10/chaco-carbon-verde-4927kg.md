Chaco provincial police stopped a truck on National Route 11 at kilometre 999 during the night
and found that it was already listed for seizure in a theft case. [@1,2] With federal judicial
authorities involved, officers took the vehicle and its two occupants to the provincial police
school for a witnessed search. [@1,2]

A narcotics dog indicated drugs, and the search recovered 4,927 kilograms and 249 grams of
marijuana, plus an Honor mobile phone. [@1,2] The two men on the truck were detained for a
suspected breach of national drug law 23.737. [@1,2]

Security minister Hugo Matkovich presented the haul on 15 October 2026 as operation Carbón Verde
and called it historic for the provincial force. [@1,2] Officials valued the load at about ARS 1.5
billion wholesale (about USD 1 million) and up to ARS 7.5 billion at retail. [@1,2] Matkovich said
the inquiry into origin and destination was continuing and would not be detailed yet. [@1,2]
