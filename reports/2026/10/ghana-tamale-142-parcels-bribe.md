Acting on intelligence about narcotics moving along the Bimbilla–Tamale road, the Northern Regional
Police Command's Special Operations Team, codenamed Red Maria, intercepted a mini commercial van,
registration GW 7800-14, at about 6:30 pm on 15 October 2026. [@1,2] A search found 142 compressed
parcels of suspected Indian hemp concealed in four jute sacks of the kind traders call Ghana Must
Go. [@1,2]

The driver, 46-year-old Alhassan Zeblim, told investigators he was carrying the consignment to
34-year-old tricycle rider Ibrahim Yussif in Tamale; Yussif arrived at the meeting point with his
brother Bawa Yussif, 56, and both were arrested, while 32-year-old Mohammed Haruna was later
identified as the alleged owner and also detained. [@1] During the operation the suspects allegedly
offered the officers GH₵50,000 to let the shipment pass unreported; the team refused and proceeded
with the arrests and seizure. [@1,2]

The suspects, the hemp, the van and the tricycle were handed to the Regional Drug Law Enforcement
Unit for further investigation; no court date has been disclosed. [@1]
