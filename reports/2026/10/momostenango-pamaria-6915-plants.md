Agents of the Subdirección General de Análisis de Información Antinarcótica, part of Guatemala's National Civil Police, found and destroyed 6,915 marijuana plants in a mountain area of Momostenango, Totonicapán, on 23 October 2026. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry said the plants were at the harvest stage, in aldea Pamaría, and put their value at 3,457,500 quetzales. [@1] Prensa Libre, reporting the police account the same day, said the plants were eradicated and burned at caserío Paraje Racaná and that the crop could be worth about 3.4 million quetzales. [@2]

The ministry said the plants were destroyed under its usual protocol. [@1] Neither account names an arrest. [@1,2]
