Officers of Kota's Railway Colony police station arrested Hariram Meena, 50, at
Tirath village in Keshoraipatan. [@1] Meena, a ganja (cannabis) smuggler, had
been evading arrest for a year. [@1] Police had announced a reward of 15,000
rupees for him. [@1]

Superintendent of Police Tejaswani Gautam said Meena was the accused in a ganja
smuggling case registered in August 2025. [@1]
