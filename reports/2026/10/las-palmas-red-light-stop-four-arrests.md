Four middle-aged men were arrested after the Local Police of Las Palmas de Gran
Canaria found them carrying 700 grams of marijuana and 27 grams of hashish
pollen. [@1] The events occurred during the night of Thursday to Friday, 29 to
30 October 2026, when officers saw a vehicle run a red light on Calle León y
Castillo. [@1]

Officers ordered the car to stop and it pulled over in the Plaza de la Feria.
[@1] As they approached, they saw that the occupants were nervous and that the
driver was breaking up a substance and putting pieces of it among his clothes.
[@1] A search of the vehicle found the marijuana under the driver's seat. [@1]
Several pieces of hashish pollen were also seized from the driver's clothing.
[@1]

Questioned by the Local Police, two of the men said the driver owned the bag of
marijuana, while a third chose to stay silent. [@1] Police said the amounts
seized exceeded those presumed to be intended for drug trafficking. [@1] The
driver also tested positive for cocaine in a drug and alcohol test and was given
an administrative penalty. [@1] The driver's partner took charge of the vehicle.
[@1]
