Anti-narcotics units of Senafront, Panama's National Border Service, carried out
two raids in the Pueblo Nuevo sector of Darién province with the Judicial
Investigation Directorate of the National Police and the Darién drug
prosecutor's office. [@2] During the operation one citizen was detained, and
officers seized presumed illicit substances, ammunition, technological equipment
and cash. [@2] The raids were part of Plan Firmeza and Operación Escudo de
Acero, strategies through which Senafront says it seeks to fight
microtrafficking and other drug-related offences nationwide. [@1]

Among the items were packages of plant material presumed to be marijuana, and
carrizos, small straws, of white powder presumed to be cocaine. [@2] Officers
also found unfired .38-calibre ammunition, several mobile phones and a metal
grinder that still held residues of presumed drugs. [@1] Cash was seized, though
the authorities did not state the amount. [@1] The detained person and the
evidence collected were placed at the disposal of the competent authorities for
the corresponding legal process. [@1] Senafront said the action was meant to
fight the drug trade and reinforce security in national territory. [@2]
