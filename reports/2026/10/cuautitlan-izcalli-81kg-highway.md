While on patrol on 13 October 2026, officers of the State of Mexico's Grupo de Intervención
Policial and the National Guard received a report of a vehicle speeding in Tlalnepantla. [@1]
They tracked it to the México–Querétaro autopista at Ampliación La Quebrada in Cuautitlán
Izcalli, where they stopped the car for a preventive inspection. [@1,2]

In the trunk and on the rear seat they found plastic bags holding about 81 kilograms of green and
dry plant material consistent with marijuana. [@1,2,3] The officers arrested Luis “N”, 28, and
Eduardo “N”, 59, after informing them that possessing suspected illicit drugs is a crime. [@1,2,3]

According to preliminary inquiries cited by state authorities, the pair had travelled from Zapopan,
Jalisco, toward San Andrés Totoltepec in Mexico City's Tlalpan borough and expected a cash payment
for moving the load, which investigators said might belong to a Jalisco-origin criminal group. [@1,2]
Quadratin adds that officials are looking at a possible link to the Cártel Jalisco Nueva Generación.
[@2]

The suspects, the drugs and the seized vehicle were presented to federal prosecutors in
Tlalnepantla to open an investigation. [@1,2]
