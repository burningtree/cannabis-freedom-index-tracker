Mohanpur sub-divisional police, with central armed police, cut down about 45,000 cannabis plants on Wednesday 28 October 2026 at Barkathal, under Sidhai police station in Mohanpur subdivision, West Tripura. [@1,2] Police said the crop stood on 16 plots covering about six acres (about 2.4 hectares). [@1,2]

The drive was led by Vinod Kumar, assistant commandant of the 124th battalion of the Central Reserve Police Force, and Mohanpur sub-divisional police officer Ashish Dasgupta. [@1,2] Officers from Sidhai police station, Sundartilla police outpost and the Tripura State Rifles took part. [@1,2]

Dasgupta told reporters that the campaign would continue and that police were still identifying who had grown the plants. [@1,2] Neither report names an arrest. [@1,2]
