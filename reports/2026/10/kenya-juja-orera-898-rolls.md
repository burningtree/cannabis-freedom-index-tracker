Detectives arrested two people and seized 898 rolls of bhang and three
litres of illicit alcohol in separate anti-narcotics operations in Kiambu and
Homa Bay counties on October 22, 2026. [@1][@2]

In Juja, Kiambu County, detectives attached to Juja Police Station arrested
24-year-old Ann Wanjiku Njoki at Juja Matatu Stage during an operation
targeting suspected drug dealers; she was found carrying 800 rolls of bhang,
which officers seized and secured as evidence. [@1][@2]

In Orera, Rachuonyo East Sub-County, Homa Bay County, officers from the
National Anti-Narcotics Investigations Bureau raided the home of 33-year-old
Fredrick Dunga, also known as Ofisa or Babu, after residents complained about
suspected drug trafficking near Orera Comprehensive School. [@1][@2] A search
of the premises yielded 98 rolls of bhang tucked away in a cup, alongside
three litres of chang'aa. [@2]

Both suspects were taken into custody for processing ahead of their expected
arraignment as investigations into the suspected trafficking continued. [@1][@2]
