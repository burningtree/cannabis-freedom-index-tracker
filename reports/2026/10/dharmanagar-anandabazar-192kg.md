Acting on a tip-off, police conducted an operation at Anandabazar Naka Point
under the Dharmanagar subdivision and seized a large quantity of dried
cannabis. [@1] On Wednesday afternoon, Dharmanagar Police and Anandabazar
Police jointly carried out the operation and intercepted a vehicle; searching
it led to the recovery of approximately 192 kg of dried cannabis, and the two
people travelling in it were detained. [@1]

According to police sources, at around 1 p.m. on Wednesday, acting on
confidential information, police launched a checking operation at
Anandabazar Naka Point and intercepted a vehicle bearing registration number
TR 01 BC 1518 on suspicion; the subsequent search uncovered the cannabis,
which weighed approximately 192 kg. [@1]
