Personnel of the Mexican navy, through the Armada de México, and of the Secretaría de la Defensa Nacional found eight marijuana plots near the municipality of La Yesca, Nayarit, on patrols on 20, 21 and 22 October 2026. [@1,2] The plots covered about 14,880 square metres, about 1.5 hectares. [@1,2] About 122,400 plants were destroyed where they stood. [@1,2]

Both departments said the work was part of joint action against drug production and that the statements did not report arrests. [@1,2] D8, citing the Secretaría de Marina, published the same area, plant count and dates on 23 October. [@2]
