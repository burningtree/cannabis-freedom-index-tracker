After learning in mid-October that a 47-year-old was running an illegal cannabis operation, police
from Sisak-Moslavina county searched three properties he used in Kutina, Popovača and Velika
Ludina. [@1,2] At a press conference on 22 October 2026 they said they seized 2,753 grammes of
cannabis, indoor grow tents and drying gear, and an automatic rifle described as a Kalashnikov with
514 rounds of ammunition. [@1,2]

The man, previously unknown to police, was arrested without resistance and given a one-month
detention order while investigators filed charges for drug production and trafficking and illegal
weapons possession. [@1,2] Criminal police leaders warned other growers they would keep targeting
production sites, noting that drug raids increasingly find firearms. [@1,2]
