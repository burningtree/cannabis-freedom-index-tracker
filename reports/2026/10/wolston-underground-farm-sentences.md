On 19 August 2026 Warwickshire Police searched a shipping container in countryside near Wolston and
found a hatch leading to an underground cannabis farm. [@2] Five people on the site were arrested;
two were later charged and three bailed. [@2]

Gjergivatal Pjeter, 33, of New Market Street, Birmingham, and Eldoh Sylaj, 31, of Upper Stone
Street, Maidstone, admitted being concerned in producing Class B cannabis. [@1] At Warwick Crown Court
on Monday 26 October 2026 they were each sentenced to 22 months in prison. [@1]

Police estimated the crop at about £500,000. [@1,2] The court ordered the cannabis destroyed and each
man to pay a £187 victim surcharge. [@1]
