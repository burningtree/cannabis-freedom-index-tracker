Judge Natasha Galea Sciberras sentenced Leo Galea, a 48-year-old man from Mosta, to eight years in prison after he admitted trafficking 14.5 kilograms of cannabis resin instead of standing trial. [@1] The prosecution and the defence had agreed that sentence, and a fine of 15,000 euros, if he admitted the charges. [@1] The judge accepted the agreement and also ordered him to pay 2,327 euros in court expenses. [@1] Analysis found a purity of about 16 percent and an estimated value of 328,000 euros. [@1]

TVM says that on 11 April 2019 Galea was seen driving in Birkirkara and handing a bag to the driver of another car. [@1] Police pursued that man and stopped him, but did not find the bag. [@1] The bag was found in the area and held cannabis resin. [@1] Police followed Galea and stopped him between Mġarr and Mosta. [@1] A raid on his farm found several bags containing a considerable amount of cannabis. [@1] He had been due to stand trial for trafficking and for associating with others to traffic drugs. [@1]

Times of Malta reported the arraignment that followed those stops. [@2] James Micallef, then 25 and from Birkirkara, was found with about five kilograms of cannabis resin, which police said he had just collected from another man. [@2] At a farmhouse in the limits of Mosta, police arrested Leo Galea, then 40, with about 10 kilograms of cannabis resin and two unlicensed revolvers. [@2] Both men pleaded not guilty before magistrate Natasha Galea Sciberras, who refused bail and froze their assets. [@2] Galea was also charged with keeping the revolvers without a licence. [@2]

TVM published a photograph of resin slabs in clear wrapping with the sentence report. [@1]
