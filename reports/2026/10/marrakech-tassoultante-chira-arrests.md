Marrakech judicial police arrested two young men on Tuesday 27 October 2026 on
suspicion of possessing and trafficking drugs, in two separate operations. [@1]
The first was caught in flagrante delicto; a search of his home in the territory
of the Royal Gendarmerie of Tassoultante found 450 g of cocaine, six slabs of
chira (cannabis resin), a motorcycle and an electronic scale. [@1] The second
was found with 546 g of cocaine, 270 g of chira, a motorcycle, an electronic
scale and cash from the trade. [@1]

The Marrakech police prefecture's judicial police ran the two operations. [@2]
Investigators said they were trying to establish the circumstances of both cases
and their possible extensions before bringing the men to court. [@2]
