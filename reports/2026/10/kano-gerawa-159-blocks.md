In Kano State, NDLEA operatives arrested 30-year-old Murtala Lawan in the
Gerawa area on Thursday 22 October. [@1,2] The operation recovered 159 blocks
of cannabis weighing 97.2 kg. [@1,2] The arrest was one of a string of NDLEA
operations across nine states announced by spokesman Femi Babafemi on Sunday 25
October, whose chairman commended the officers and urged them to sustain the
campaign alongside school and community sensitisation. [@1,2]
