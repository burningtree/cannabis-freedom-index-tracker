Jharada police said they caught Kripal Singh beside Bamnai road, near the
underbridge on the Ujjain–Garoth road, and seized 637 grams of ganja, the Hindi
word for cannabis, from him. [@1]

In a second stop, Tarana police seized 240 grams of ganja from another smuggler
near a BSNL tower. [@1] Police put the two seizures together at more than 800
grams of ganja recovered from two smugglers, and the amounts they listed add up
to 877 grams. [@1]

Both stops were in Ujjain district in Madhya Pradesh. [@1] Police said they had
taken action against both men under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1]
