Police in the Nadi police-station area of Patna district arrested a father and
son with 10.318 kilograms of ganja, the local name for cannabis, and seized the
CNG auto used to carry it. [@1] The two are Sanjeev Kumar, 21, and his father
Amarnath Rai, 50, of Saidpur in the Rustampur police-station area of Vaishali
district. [@3] A press note from the Fatuha subdivisional police office,
reported by Prabhat Khabar, dated the action 28 October 2026. [@3]

A search with a magistrate present found the 10.318 kilograms hidden under the
driver's seat. [@1] Officers seized the ganja and the auto, registration
BR-01PP-5234, and arrested both the driver and the owner. [@2] Police put the
estimated price at about 2 lakh rupees. [@1]

Fatuha subdivisional police officer Awadhesh Kumar said at a Thursday press
conference that a Wednesday tip described ganja moving by auto from Rustampur
via the Tinmuhani bridge toward Patna city. [@1] Led by the station house
officer, the team and a deputed magistrate waited under the bridge; when the
driver saw police he turned and fled, and officers chased and caught the auto.
[@2] The tip reached Nadi police at about 10:05 a.m. on 28 October that a CNG
tempo was bringing a consignment from Rustampur to be taken toward Patna by the
Pipa Pul route. [@3] The team then cordoned the road under Pipa Pul toward
Kharbandi, where the driver of a suspicious CNG tempo tried to flee. [@3]

Nadi police station registered case 426/2026 on 28 October 2026 and opened a
case under the NDPS Act. [@3] Station house officer Saddam Hussain led the raid,
with additional station house officer Devkaran Bunty, Santosh Kumar, Manoj Kumar
and other Nadi officers. [@3]
