The Khust District Court sentenced a 33-year-old resident of Khust to five years in prison for growing cannabis at home. [@2,3] He remains in custody until the verdict takes legal effect. [@2,3] Prosecutors from the Khust District Prosecutor's Office brought the case. [@1,2,3] РІО-Новини and Банош carry the account published by the Zakarpattia regional prosecutor's office. [@1,2]

Police arrested him in July 2026. [@1,2,3] In court, prosecutors proved that in the spring of 2026 he had illegally sown and grown cannabis. [@1,2,3] He fitted a greenhouse with irrigation and ventilation and tended the plants. [@1,2,3] He grew some of them in pots in the yard. [@1,2,3]

Searches found and seized about 240 cannabis plants. [@1,2,3] An expert examination confirmed that the plants contained narcotic substances. [@1,2,3] In the house, officers also found dried cannabis, electronic scales, packaging bags and devices for smoking. [@1,2,3] The court found him guilty of illegally sowing and growing 50 or more cannabis plants, and of illegally making and keeping narcotics without intent to sell. [@1,2,3]

Банош published a prosecutor's office photograph of tall flowering plants in pots inside the greenhouse. [@2]
