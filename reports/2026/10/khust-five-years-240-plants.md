The Khust District Court sentenced a 33-year-old resident of Khust to five years in prison for growing cannabis at home. [@2] He remains in custody until the verdict takes legal effect. [@2] Prosecutors from the Khust District Prosecutor's Office brought the case. [@1,2] Both reports carry the account published by the Zakarpattia regional prosecutor's office. [@1,2]

Police arrested him in July 2026. [@1,2] In court, prosecutors proved that in the spring of 2026 he had illegally sown and grown cannabis. [@1,2] He fitted a greenhouse with irrigation and ventilation and tended the plants. [@1,2] He grew some of them in pots in the yard. [@1,2]

Searches found and seized about 240 cannabis plants. [@1,2] An expert examination confirmed that the plants contained narcotic substances. [@1,2] In the house, officers also found dried cannabis, electronic scales, packaging bags and devices for smoking. [@1,2] The court found him guilty of illegally sowing and growing 50 or more cannabis plants, and of illegally making and keeping narcotics without intent to sell. [@1,2]

Банош published a prosecutor's office photograph of tall flowering plants in pots inside the greenhouse. [@2]

## Update

A 33-year-old resident of Khust was sentenced to five years in prison for
illegally growing hemp and possessing cannabis. [@3] He was arrested in July
2026, and prosecutors proved in court that he had illegally planted and grown
cannabis as early as the spring. [@3] The Khust District Court issued the
verdict, and he will remain in custody until it becomes final. [@3]

To grow the plants, he set up a greenhouse with irrigation and ventilation
systems and tended to them. [@3] He placed some of the cannabis plants in pots
in his yard. [@3] During searches, law enforcement officers discovered and
seized approximately 240 cannabis plants, and an expert analysis confirmed that
they contained narcotic substances. [@3] Dried cannabis, electronic scales,
packaging bags and smoking devices were found in his home. [@3]

He was found guilty of illegally planting and growing 50 or more cannabis
plants, as well as illegally manufacturing and possessing narcotic substances
without the intent to sell. [@3] Prosecutors from the Khust District
Prosecutor’s Office represented the prosecution in the case. [@3]
