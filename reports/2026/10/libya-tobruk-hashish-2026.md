Eastern Libyan anti-drug officers found about 400 kg of hashish at a house in an agricultural area southeast of Tobruk. Officials valued it at about 6 million dinars and said it had crossed the Egyptian border for sale in Libya. The detainees were Libyan and Egyptian nationals. [@1]
