On 21 October 2026 the Public Ministry and Guatemala's national civil police stopped a
vehicle at the inter-agency checkpoint at kilometre 185.3 of the CA-2 highway in Nuevo San
Carlos, Retalhuleu department. [@1,2]

Officers found 15 plastic bags that field-tested as marijuana, prefilled cannabis
cigarettes, e-cigarettes and mobile phones, and arrested three men identified in official
posts as Carlos O., Kevin O. and Víctor M. [@1] They were handed to the competent court. [@1]

A local report from the same day named the detainees as Carlos Humberto Orozco Salazar, 39,
Kevin Junior Eduardo Oliva Morales, 33, and Víctor René Morales Hernández, 42, all
Guatemalans travelling in a pickup registered P-289KLW. [@2] That account also described 15
packages of marijuana and additional cannabis cigarettes but did not give a total weight. [@2]

The checkpoint sits on the main Pacific corridor toward Guatemala City; neither source said
who was meant to receive the drugs. [@1,2]
