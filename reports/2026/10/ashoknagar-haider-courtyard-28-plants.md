Dehat police in Ashoknagar district, Madhya Pradesh, found 28 cannabis plants,
known in Hindi as ganja, in the courtyard of a house in Haider village and
beside two rooms under construction. [@1] They arrested Devi Singh Yadav, 60, a
resident of the village. [@1] Officers uprooted all the plants with their roots
and seized them. [@1] The seized plants weighed 9 kg 50 g in total, police said.
[@2]

Police said a tip-off on Friday reported that Yadav was growing cannabis in his
courtyard. [@1] Officers informed senior officers and formed a team after the
tip-off. [@1] They searched the courtyard and the two unfinished rooms, where
the 28 green plants were found. [@1] A case was registered against him at the
Dehat police station late that night under the NDPS Act, India's main drug law.
[@1] He was produced before a court. [@1]

The operation was carried out under district police chief Rajeev Kumar Mishra,
with additional superintendent Antar Singh Kanesh and sub-divisional police
officer Om Prakash Mishra supervising. [@1] Mishra said the campaign against the
production, storage and sale of illegal drugs would continue across the
district. [@1]
