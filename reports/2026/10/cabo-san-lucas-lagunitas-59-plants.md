State investigators arrested Erik “N” and Shawna “N” during a search of a house
in the Lagunitas 2 neighbourhood of Cabo San Lucas, and a federal judge later
bound both over for trial. [@1] Mexico’s Fiscalía General de la República (FGR),
the federal prosecutor’s office, said its Baja California Sur office obtained
that vinculación a proceso for probable drug offences and possession of
cartridges reserved for the armed forces. [@2] The Agencia Estatal de
Investigación Criminal (AEIC), the state criminal investigation agency, made the
arrests during the search and handed the two people and the seized items to the
federal public prosecutor. [@1]

The FGR said the search found 59 plants with the characteristics of marijuana,
231.9 grams of cannabis sativa L., 11.9 grams of seeds of the same plant, and a
liquid containing delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC). [@1] Officers also seized
39 boxes of vaporizers, lamps and other cultivation equipment, and 79 usable
cartridges of various calibres. [@1] Palabra BCS, reporting the same account,
described the 231.9 grams as green and dried herb that tested as cannabis sativa
L., and said the plants had green leaves of different sizes. [@2]

A judge found the detention lawful and issued an auto de vinculación a proceso,
an order binding both people over for trial, for sowing and cultivating
marijuana, possessing cannabis for trade or supply, and possessing cartridges
reserved for the armed forces. [@1] The court imposed prisión preventiva
oficiosa, mandatory pretrial detention, and set three months to close the
complementary investigation. [@3] The published FGR account says the two are
presumed innocent until a court conviction. [@2]
