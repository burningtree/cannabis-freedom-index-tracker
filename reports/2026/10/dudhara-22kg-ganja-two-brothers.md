Police in the Dudhara police station area of Sant Kabir Nagar district, Uttar
Pradesh, arrested two brothers and seized 22 kg 300 g of ganja, or cannabis.
[@1] The arrests took place on 30 October 2026. [@3] The two were Avnish Singh
alias Tony and Avinash Singh alias Tinku, residents of Bharwalia Babu village.
[@1] Officers caught them at a tin shed on a fish farm near the Bharwalia Babu
canal bridge. [@2]

The ganja was packed in 20 packets and kept in five sacks. [@1] Station house
officer Surendra Kumar Singh said officers were checking suspicious people and
vehicles when they found the stash. [@2] Police registered case 431/2026 against
both at Dudhara police station under sections 8 and 20 of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act. [@2] Both were sent to court. [@3]

Police described the arrest as part of a campaign against the illegal narcotics
trade in the district. [@3] The operation was led by station house officer
Surendra Kumar Singh under Superintendent of Police Sandeep Kumar Meena. [@3]
