Chile's investigative police, the PDI, detained six people in the Población
Norte Grande, a neighbourhood of Valdivia, on 13 October 2026 and dismantled a
group that stored, bulked up and distributed drugs there. [@2] The six were
three men and three women, one of them a 15-year-old girl, all of them Chilean.
[@2] One of those detained, according to the investigation, received drugs and
sold them in small quantities in the commune of Máfil. [@2] Four were to be
brought before the Juzgado de Garantía de Valdivia, the city's guarantee court,
for a detention hearing. [@1] The 15-year-old girl and another woman were
released on the prosecutor's instructions. [@2]

Detectives seized 84.2 grams of cannabis, 2 kg 49.88 g of cocaine base and
765.49 g of cocaine hydrochloride. [@2] They also seized seven mobile phones, a
vehicle, two scales, chemicals used to bulk up the drugs, two 9 mm cartridges
and a pistol magazine. [@2] Police valued the drugs and seized items at more
than 40 million Chilean pesos. [@3]

The head of the Valdivia anti-drug brigade, subprefect Gabriel Cornejo, said the
group's members had separate roles in storing, bulking up and selling the drugs.
[@2] The investigation was directed by the Los Ríos regional prosecutor's office
through its SAC-ECOH criminal analysis unit. [@1]
