Police entered a building on the Molenwerf in Uitgeest at about 8.30 on Wednesday morning, 28 October 2026, after an investigation. [@1] They found more than 200 cannabis plants and more than 60 cuttings. [@1] No one was arrested. [@1]

The national police said the plants would be destroyed. [@1] NH Nieuws, reporting the same day, said the plants and cuttings had been seized and that the growing equipment had been removed. [@1,2] Both say electricity had been taken illegally. [@1,2] NH Nieuws also says water had been tapped illegally. [@2] Police warn that an illegal connection can short-circuit or overheat and start a fire. [@1]

The police notice does not name a suspect or give a street number. [@1]
