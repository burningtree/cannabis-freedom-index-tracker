The Marine Police arrested 31-year-old Vincent Lotsu for alleged unlawful cultivation and
possession of suspected cannabis in the Volta Region. [@1,2]

The arrest followed a routine patrol along the Volta Lake on Tuesday 13 October, when
officers of the Kpando-based Marine Police unit discovered a fenced wooden structure at
Mangoase in the Central Tongu District; a search uncovered a plantation of suspected Indian
hemp behind the fence and 16 sacks of dried leaves believed to be cannabis in Lotsu's
possession. [@1,2]

Police handed the suspect and exhibits to the Kpando Police Divisional Investigation
Department, and sent the suspected narcotics to the crime laboratory for examination.
[@1,2] Lotsu remained in custody assisting investigations, with arraignment before court
planned, according to Volta North PRO Inspector Felix Kwao. [@1,2]
