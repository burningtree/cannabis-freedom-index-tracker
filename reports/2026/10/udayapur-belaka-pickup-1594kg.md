During a vehicle inspection at Deurali in Belaka Municipality-5 on Monday
evening, police stopped a pickup truck and found 159.4 kg of marijuana packed
in six sacks and concealed inside sacks of rice husk. [@1,2] The truck bore
registration Province 1-02-002 Cha 5290. [@2]

Driver Pashupati Safi, 28, of Saptakoshi Municipality-8 in Saptari district,
was arrested on the spot and taken into custody for investigation. [@1,2] The
Belaka interception was the latest of three Udayapur operations in about 36
hours: an abandoned pickup with 940.44 kg had been found that morning on the
Ranitaar-Beltar road in Chaudandigadhi, and 22-year-old Bishwaraj Rai had been
caught a day earlier near Katari allegedly carrying marijuana toward Siraha.
[@1,2] Together the three busts netted a combined 1,209.84 kg, and police say
the hunt for the wider network continues. [@1] Across the operations officers
also impounded two Bolero vehicles; the drugs and vehicles were transferred
to local police offices for further investigation. [@2]
