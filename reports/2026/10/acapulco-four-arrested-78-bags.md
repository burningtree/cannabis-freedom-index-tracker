State police and federal troops arrested four people in two operations in
Acapulco, Guerrero, on 29 October 2026. [@1] Three were detained during
prevention patrols in Tuncingo, a locality of the port. [@1] A fourth person was
detained in the Lucio Cabañas neighbourhood. [@1]

In Tuncingo, officers seized 70 small bags of suspected marijuana and 30 doses
of a substance similar to crystal, the methamphetamine known in Mexico as
cristal. [@1] In Lucio Cabañas they seized eight small bags of a herb resembling
marijuana. [@1] In all, the state police counted 78 small bags of suspected
marijuana and 30 doses of a substance similar to crystal. [@1] Quadratín
Guerrero reported a total of 108 doses of drugs across the two operations. [@2]

A press bulletin said the operations involved the State Police, the Mexican
Army, the Navy, the National Guard and the state Attorney General's Office. [@2]
The operations were part of inter-institutional crime-prevention patrols
maintained across the municipality. [@1] Those detained are presumed innocent
until a court rules on their responsibility. [@1] The Public Security
Secretariat of Guerrero urged residents to use the 911 emergency number and the
089 anonymous tip line. [@1]
