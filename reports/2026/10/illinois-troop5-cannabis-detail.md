From 29 September through 3 October 2026, Illinois State Police ran a violent-crime
suppression detail across Iroquois, Kankakee and McLean counties. [@1,2] The operation
produced 22 arrests, 23 felony charges, one recovered firearm, 22,371 grams of cannabis
and nine grams of other controlled substances, the department said in a 7 October release.
[@1,2]

On 30 September a trooper stopped a driver in McLean County, smelled raw cannabis and
found two large bags of flower and cannabis products plus a loaded illegal firearm; the
driver went to McLean County Jail. [@1] On 1 October another McLean stop of four occupants
yielded three bags with one-pound cannabis packages, other cannabis products, cocaine and
methamphetamine; all four were arrested. [@1]

Patrol troops, investigators, air operations and interdiction teams from several regions
supported the detail, ISP said. [@1]
