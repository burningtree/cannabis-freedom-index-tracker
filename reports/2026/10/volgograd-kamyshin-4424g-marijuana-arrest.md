Police in Kamyshin district of Volgograd Oblast searched a 63-year-old
local man’s home during operational work and seized plant material in two
bags and a cardboard box, Vesti.ru reported on 19 October 2026 from the
regional Interior Ministry. [@1]

Expert testing identified cannabis weighing 4,424.7 grams. [@2] The man told
officers he had gathered wild plants in one of the district’s rural
settlements and kept the marijuana for personal use without intending to
sell it. [@2]

Investigators opened a large-scale possession case under part 2 of article
228 of the Criminal Code and detained him under article 91 of the Criminal
Procedure Code. [@2] Volgogradskaya Pravda said on 20 October that a court
was still deciding whether to hold him in pretrial custody. [@2]
