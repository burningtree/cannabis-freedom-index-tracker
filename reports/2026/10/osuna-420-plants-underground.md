On 16 October 2026 the Operational Unit of the Tax Agency's Customs Surveillance Service (Servicio
de Vigilancia Aduanera) in Seville and the Civil Guard's Osuna judicial police team, under a court
order, carried out three house searches in the town of Osuna in Seville province. [@1,2]

They seized 420 cannabis plants at different stages of growth, cash and a prohibited taser-type
weapon, and arrested three people accused of drug trafficking and electricity fraud. [@1,2]

Agents said some of the indoor set-ups were built to hide the crops. In one dwelling, underground
structures and spaces had been created inside the building to house a plantation, works they said
could have compromised the structure of the building itself. [@1,2]
