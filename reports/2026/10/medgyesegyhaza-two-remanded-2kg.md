An investigating judge at the Gyula District Court ordered a 43-year-old man and a 58-year-old man,
both from Medgyesegyháza in Békés County, held for one month. [@1] The Battonya District
Prosecutor's Office had asked for the remand on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1,2]

The prosecutor's account, published by Beol, says the 43-year-old decided at the start of 2025 to
grow and sell cannabis, using equipment from his wife's former licensed medical-hemp business and
seeds bought in the Netherlands. [@1] He first grew plants in pots in a room of his home, dried
what he could use and consumed it. [@1] In December 2025 he sowed again from those seeds and by
March 2026 had raised 100 plants about 150 centimetres tall. [@1] He cut them, dried the usable
parts into bags and buried at least 2 kilograms in the yard of his house. [@1]

He also sold some of what he had grown. [@1] One buyer was the 58-year-old man, who took it partly
to use and partly to sell on. [@1] The judge agreed there was a risk they would flee, offend again
or interfere with the evidence, given the sentence they could face. [@1]

Körös Hírcentrum said it was reprinting the Békés County Chief Prosecutor's Office statement
unchanged. [@2] That text matches the growing, the buried 2 kilograms and the one-month remand,
and its headline also gives the second man's age as 58. [@2]
