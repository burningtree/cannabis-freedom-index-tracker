The Department of Narcotics Control arrested 40-year-old Indian national
Abhijit Das with 4.28 kg of kush, a cannabis-based drug, at Dhaka's
Shahjalal International Airport. [@1] He was detained at the Green Channel in
the ground-floor arrivals hall on Wednesday afternoon. [@1]

Abhijit, from North 24 Parganas in West Bengal, was caught after a DNC team
led by assistant director Md Abu Sayeed Mia of the Dhaka Metro North airport
office took position at the Green Channel around 1 p.m. on a tip-off. [@1]
Searched before witnesses and airport agency staff, his trolley bag yielded the
kush stashed beneath three Indian sarees. [@1] He had arrived on Thai Airways
flight TG-321 from Bangkok. [@1] He told interrogators he works
as a porter at Bongaon Railway Station in India, and the DNC says a syndicate
uses such couriers to plant a new kush market in Bangladesh. [@1] He had
planned to stay two to three days, deliver the consignment and return to
India through the Benapole land port. [@1] Police were preparing to produce
him in court. [@1]
