Police arrested a 23-year-old man on the morning of Thursday 29 October 2026
while serving an arrest warrant and a home-search warrant in the district of
Morro Alto, in Guarapuava. [@1] Officers found him at the back of the house,
where he was with his wife and their children. [@1] During the stop he told the
team that he had drugs in the property. [@1]

In a bag, officers found 405.9 grams of a substance they described as analogous
to crack, divided into portions, and 13.1 grams of cannabis, recorded in the
report as maconha. [@1] They also seized a precision scale and a black airsoft
replica firearm with a magazine. [@1] Asked about the materials, the man said
they were his. [@1]

He was arrested and taken, with the seized items, to the 14th Police Subdivision
(14ª Subdivisão Policial) for the legal procedures. [@1] The image distributed
with the account is credited to the Military Police of Paraná (PMPR). [@1]
