Moroccan authorities intercepted more than 3.5 tonnes of cannabis resin concealed inside a freight
truck at Tanger Med port during a joint border inspection early on Sunday 11 October 2026. [@1,2]
Coordinated checks by port security and customs targeted a Moroccan-registered articulated truck
used for international freight; the resin, 3,565 kilograms in all, had been hidden in crates of
agricultural produce arranged to disguise it for shipment abroad. [@1,2]

The truck driver, a 35-year-old Moroccan national, was taken into custody. [@1] A judicial
investigation was opened under the supervising prosecutor to map the suspected network; the
intended destination was not disclosed. [@1,2]
