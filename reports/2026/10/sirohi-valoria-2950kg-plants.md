Acting on a tip-off, teams under Sirohi superintendent Dr Ajeya Singh Rathore
raided fields at Pagara Fali in Valoria village under Rohida police station,
where ganja was allegedly being grown behind maize and fennel crops. [@1]
Because the plots belonged to different account holders, teams from the
Rohida, Pindwara, Swarupganj and Abu Road Sadar stations moved in coordination
under additional superintendent Narendra Kumar and Pindwara circle officer
Rahul Meena. [@1]

Police seized green ganja plants weighing 2,950.84 kg in total and arrested
Hosiyaram, Jepuram, Lasmaram and Anil Kumar, registering five cases under the
NDPS Act. [@1] Questioning of the four continues as officers trace the other
links of the illegal cultivation. [@1]
