In early October, Marijampolė detectives detained a Kaunas-district
resident in Raseiniai district on suspicion of holding over 112 kilograms
of assorted narcotics. [@1] [@2]

On 5 October, officers of the organised-crime unit searched the suspect's
car, home and other places, finding about 97 kg of cannabis, 10 kg of
cocaine and 5 kg of hashish. [@1] [@2] The man was charged with illegally
possessing narcotics for distribution or in a very large quantity, and was
remanded in custody; the Marijampolė unit leads the pre-trial
investigation. [@1]
