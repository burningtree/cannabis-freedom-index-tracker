Police in Makthal, Narayanpet district, Telangana, arrested three men with 1.4
kg of ganja, the local name for cannabis, during a check at the Ramlila Maidan
ground on Saturday 17 October 2026. [@2] Officers seized the scooter they used,
three mobile phones and Rs 7,000 in cash. [@2] Superintendent of Police G.
Vineeth said the ganja was worth about Rs 60,000. [@2]

Police named them as Om Ashok Koradi, of Raichur in Karnataka, Rajasekhar
Ramchandar Huneravu, of southern Solapur, and Md Yaseen Bande Nawaz Makhanandar,
of Solapur in Maharashtra. [@2] They bought the cannabis in Maharashtra and
brought it through Raichur to sell in Makthal and nearby areas, according to the
preliminary investigation. [@1] The three were remanded in custody. [@3]

Vineeth said police were investigating who else was involved, where the ganja
was supplied from and how the financial dealings worked. [@2] The arrest came
during a district police drive against illegal liquor, sand transport, gambling,
ganja and gutka sales. [@1]
