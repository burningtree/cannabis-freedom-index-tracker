A van driver was arrested after testing positive for cannabis during a roadside
stop for not wearing a seatbelt. [@1] The driver was spotted by an officer from
Hampshire Constabulary's roads policing tasking team while the unit was carrying
out patrols targeting the 'fatal 5' offences. [@1] The force describes those
five key road safety offences as speeding, drink or drug driving, using a mobile
phone, not wearing a seatbelt, and careless or dangerous driving. [@1]

The force said officers who stop vehicles for moving traffic offences can also
require drivers to undergo roadside impairment tests. [@1] The driver was then
undergoing a blood specimen procedure in custody, police said. [@1]

The Roads Policing Tasking Team urged motorists to wear seatbelts and never
drive while impaired by drink or drugs. [@1] A positive roadside result is not
by itself a court finding of guilt. [@2] No result from the blood test, charging
decision or court date has been announced. [@2]
