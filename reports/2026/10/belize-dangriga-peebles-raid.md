Dangriga police conducted a joint search at a residence on Magoon Street in
Dangriga Town at around 4:00am on Monday, October 20, 2026, uncovering a
nine-millimetre pistol with 45 rounds of ammunition, 462 grams of cannabis,
41 grams of suspected crack cocaine and 787 grams of cocaine hidden in
different spots around the house. [@1][@2]

Charles Peebles, a 56-year-old resident of Magoon Street, was arrested and
charged with keeping a firearm without a gun licence, keeping ammunition
without a gun licence and several counts of possession of controlled drugs
with intent to supply. [@1][@2]
