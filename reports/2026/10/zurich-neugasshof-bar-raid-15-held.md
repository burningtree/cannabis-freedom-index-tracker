City police stormed the well-known Neugasshof milieu bar in Zurich's
Kreis 5 late on Wednesday evening on the prosecutor's orders, using
special forces with a battering ram, over suspected large-scale drug
dealing. [@1] [@2]

Fifteen people — twelve men and three women aged 25 to 71 with Swiss,
Hungarian, Italian, Serbian and Croatian citizenship — were arrested;
fourteen stayed in provisional custody, including bar owner and operator
Roland Gisler, convicted of drug dealing in 2021. [@1] [@2] Searching the
building, including its opened floors, officers found several hundred
grammes of cocaine, several kilos of cannabis flowers, packed hashish
portions, a large sum of cash, a pistol with two loaded magazines, a
baseball bat and data carriers. [@1] [@2]
