Police in Biramitrapur, in Sundargarh district, arrested a 35-year-old man for
allegedly possessing 1 kg of ganja. [@2] Police identified him as Mathura Singh
and said he was intercepted at Talasera Chowk on Tuesday afternoon after a
tip-off. [@2] The additional tehsildar of Biramitrapur, a revenue official,
reached the spot, and the search was carried out in the presence of witnesses
and police personnel. [@2] About 1 kg of ganja, the local term for cannabis, was
recovered from a plastic bag Singh was carrying, and the contraband was seized.
[@2] He was taken to the police station for questioning and arrested the next
day, on Wednesday 28 October 2026. [@2] Dainik Bhaskar said Singh was a resident
of Vedvyas. [@1]
