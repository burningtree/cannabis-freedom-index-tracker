Police in Mahuawa, East Champaran, searched a milk drum loaded on a motorcycle
parked beside the Dharmnagar border road during a patrol. [@1] The rider noticed
the patrol, left the motorcycle and escaped towards the Nepal border under cover
of darkness. [@1] Officers seized the motorcycle and the narcotics and were
trying to identify the fugitive from the bike, with raids under way to arrest
him; no arrest was reported. [@1]

Inside the drum, officers found 180 bottles of Nepali Kasturi liquor and, hidden
beneath them, 509 grams of a charas-like narcotic substance, station house
officer Manoj Kumar said. [@1] Motihari police put the haul at 509 grams of
charas, a cannabis resin, and 54 litres of Nepali liquor. [@2]

Police patrol the India-Nepal border area to curb the illegal narcotics and
liquor trade, but traffickers keep adopting new methods, including hiding
contraband in milk drums. [@1] The patrol team included Sikandar Kumar, Rupesh
Kumar, Vijay Rai and Pramod Singh, Kumar said. [@1]

A joint team of Mahuawa police station and the SSB searched the milk container and recovered Nepali liquor and charas. [@3] Police had been told that a youth was smuggling liquor while pretending to sell milk. [@3] Officers seized 54 litres of Nepali liquor from the container, along with a consignment of charas.
