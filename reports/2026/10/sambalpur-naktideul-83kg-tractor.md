Naktideul police in Sambalpur district, Odisha, seized about 83 kg of ganja, the
local term for cannabis, hidden beneath the trolley of an unregistered tractor.
[@3] On 30 October 2026, a special police team chased the tractor around 1.40
pm. [@3] The tractor was going from Mushakani village towards Batgaon when
police started following it. [@1] The driver abandoned the tractor in a forest
near Telisingha chowk and fled, and police searched the area but could not find
him. [@1]

Officers found the cannabis packed in a compartment under the tractor trolley.
[@3] Sambalpur's superintendent of police put the weight at 83.009 kg and
described the vehicle as a Mahindra tractor with trailer. [@2] Police said the
tractor and trailer were seized. [@2] A case was registered under the NDPS Act.
[@2] Police were checking the chassis and engine numbers to identify the owner
and trace the driver. [@3] The market value of the seized ganja had not been
assessed, the Times of India reported. [@1]

Kanak News reported that the tractor was carrying the cannabis from the Boudh
area towards Chhendipada. [@4] The outlet said police were also examining
allegations of a larger trafficking network. [@4] Sambalpur police said they
remained resolute in their fight against drug trafficking. [@2]
