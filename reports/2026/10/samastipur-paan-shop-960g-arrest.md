Muffasil police in Samastipur, Bihar, arrested a paan shopkeeper near Shivnandan
Chowk and recovered 960 grams of ganja from his shop. [@1]

The raid followed a tip-off that ganja was being sold near Shivnandan Chowk,
station house officer Ajit Prasad Singh said. [@1] Officers raided the paan shop
beside the bypass embankment. [@1] Kumar ran the shop beside the chowk and used
it as cover to sell ganja. [@1] Police were investigating how long Kumar had
been selling ganja and where he obtained it. [@1]

Singh said the seized ganja would be sent to Assam for testing. [@1] He said the
ganja was likely worth lakhs of rupees. [@1] Kumar was being processed to be
sent to judicial custody. [@1]
