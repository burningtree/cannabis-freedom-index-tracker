Customs officers at Hurghada International Airport, led by customs director
Mohand Sami, made the seizure while inspecting passengers arriving on a flight
from a European country. [@1] A search of one passenger's luggage found a
plastic roll of green pieces and two plastic bottles, one holding a green plant,
all suspected to be marijuana. [@3] The Hurghada prosecution is investigating
the seizure of a foreign passenger at the airport. [@1]

The passenger was leaving the customs gate when Ahmed Waheed, head of passenger
inspection, suspected him. [@2] His bags were passed through an X-ray machine,
which showed irregular densities. [@2] Hall supervisor Mohamed Abu Bakr was then
tasked with searching the bags. [@2] Customs officers Amira Ismail and Wael Ali
inventoried and sealed the find in the presence of customs security officials.
[@1]

Atef El-Sayed, head of the Central Administration of Customs for the Red Sea and
Southern Region, ordered legal measures and the filing of customs seizure report
No. 12 of 2026. [@2] The seizure followed instructions from Ahmed Amawy, head of
the Egyptian Customs Authority, to tighten controls at airports and border
posts. [@1]
