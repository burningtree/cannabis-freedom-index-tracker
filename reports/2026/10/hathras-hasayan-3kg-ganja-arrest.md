Hasayan police in Uttar Pradesh's Hathras district arrested a man with 3 kg 300
g of ganja, the Hindi term for cannabis, on Friday 30 October 2026. [@1] The man
was named as Chandraprakash, 68, son of Bishan Swarup and a resident of
Sichawali Sani village in the Hasayan police station area. [@2] Sub-Inspector
Manu Yadav was leading a patrol with a team of constables. [@2] They caught him
on the road towards Sichawali Sani village, about 200 metres in from the
highway. [@2] Chandraprakash told police he sold ganja at his shop in Nagla Rati
to meet his expenses. [@2]

Police said the bag and ganja together weighed 3,300 g. [@1] They took 300 g as
a sample and sealed the remaining 3,000 g, the police account says. [@1]

A case numbered 205/26 was registered at Hasayan police station. [@2] It was
registered under section 8/20 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act, the country's main drug law. [@2] Dainik Bhaskar reported that the
arrest came in a campaign against illegal drug trafficking ordered by Hathras
superintendent of police Chirangiv Nath Sinha. [@3] News Darpan reported that
Hasayan station house officer Manish Chikara carried out the operation with his
team. [@4] Police said further legal action was under way. [@4]
