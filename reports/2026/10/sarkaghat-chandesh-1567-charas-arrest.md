Sarkaghat police stopped a pickup coming from the Patrighaat side during a
vehicle check at Chandesh, in Mandi district, on the night of Saturday 24
October 2026. [@2] They arrested the driver, Amar Singh, a resident of Padhar
tehsil in Mandi district. [@3] Mandi district police put the recovery at 1.567
kg and said a case had been registered. [@1]

The case was registered against Singh under the Narcotics, Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act at Sarkaghat police station. [@3] Police were
preparing to produce him in court to seek remand for questioning. [@3]

Police said they were trying to establish where the charas came from and where
it was to be delivered. [@2] They were using technical investigation to reach
others connected to the case. [@2] More arrests were possible, police said. [@3]
Mandi superintendent of police Vinod Kumar confirmed the seizure and said
several aspects of it were being examined. [@2]
