Cluj organised-crime police and DIICOT prosecutors documented an outdoor cannabis cultivation
in Florești during 2026 and detained two local men, aged 49 and 51, on 22 October. [@1,2]
Investigators seized 20 plants between one and three metres tall and additional cannabis in
bags and containers found during house searches, along with grinders and other evidence. [@1,2]

Both were held for risk-drug trafficking and a Cluj rights judge ordered 30-day preventive
arrest on 23 October, AGERPRES reported in the national police communiqué. [@1,2]
