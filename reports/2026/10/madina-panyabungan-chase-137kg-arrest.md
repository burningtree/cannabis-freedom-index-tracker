Acting on a public tip about a drug deal in Panyabungan, the Mandailing Natal
drug squad chief ordered a joint police-BNNK stakeout on the evening of 13
October. [@1] Near the Darussalam sluice gate in East Panyabungan, the team
spotted two men riding one motorbike toward Panyabungan town; when the bike
did not stop, one rider threw a large black plastic bag onto the road and the
chase was on. [@1]

The pursuit ended in front of the Aek Galoga petrol station near Dr Epta
hospital in Pidoli Lombang village around 19:30, where officers seized the
rider while his companion fled into a rubber plantation and remains sought.
[@1] The discarded bag held 14 brown-wrapped balls of ganja weighing 13.7 kg
gross; officers also took a black-red Honda Beat (BA 6683 AAU), its key and
another large black plastic bag. [@1]

The pair had reportedly picked up 22 balls in two bags from Huta Tua in East
Panyabungan, so part of the load escaped with the fugitive. [@1] The arrested
man, 27-year-old Padang resident Randa Kurnia Putra, tested positive for
amphetamine and methamphetamine and was taken to the Madina drug squad command
for further process. [@1]
