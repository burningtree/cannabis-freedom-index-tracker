Hariharganj police in Palamu district stopped a CNG autorickshaw on National
Highway 139 on Monday 12 October and seized 18 kg 470 g of ganja concealed in
a specially built box beneath the driver's and passengers' seats. [@1]
Station officer-in-charge Deobrat Poddar said the drug was packed in 18
packets of different weights, hidden to avoid detection. [@1]

The driver, 52-year-old Md Nayeem, was arrested along with the autorickshaw;
he allegedly told investigators a local person had entrusted him with the
consignment and claimed he did not know the packets held contraband. [@1]
Because Hariharganj sits close to the Jharkhand-Bihar border, police suspect
the load was meant for transport into Bihar, and they are tracing its source
and destination. [@1] Nayeem was forwarded to judicial custody on Tuesday 13
October under the NDPS Act; at 18.47 kg the seizure counts as an intermediate
quantity, below the 20 kg commercial threshold. [@1]
