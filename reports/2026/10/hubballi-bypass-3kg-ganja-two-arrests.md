The Central Crime Branch (CCB) police in Hubballi arrested two men and seized
3.213 kg of ganja, or cannabis, from them near Thimmasagar village on the
Hubballi-Dharwad bypass on the morning of Monday 5 October 2026. [@1] Inspector
Ramesh Kambale and other CCB personnel took part in the operation. [@1]

Police named the arrested men as Tejesh Ramageri, 26, a resident of Banagitti
Gudihal Cross in Kalghatgi taluk, and Joseph Das, 26, of Anand Nagar in Old
Hubballi. [@2] Officers recovered 3.213 kg of ganja, two mobile phones and
₹1,050 in cash, and valued the cannabis at ₹1,60,650, about ₹1.6 lakh. [@1]

A case was registered at Old Hubballi police station, and police said further
investigation was under way. [@1] Karnataka TV reported that investigators were
looking into where the cannabis came from and to whom it was being sold. [@2]
