A 30-year-old Chilean man was arrested when detectives of the PDI, Chile's
investigative police, searched two homes in the La Vara and Alerce sectors of
Puerto Montt on 28 October 2026 under two court warrants. [@1] The man already
had a record for drug trafficking. [@1] He was placed at the disposal of the
courts for his control de detención, the hearing at which a judge reviews the
arrest. [@1]

In the yard of one of the homes, officers found a bag holding a large number of
bags of cannabis sativa. [@1] The head of the Puerto Montt anti-narcotics
brigade, Subprefect Pedro Torres, said the load was 26 packages weighing about
27 kg. [@1] The PDI valued the marijuana at 217 million pesos. [@1] Officers
also seized a press for making cannabis resin and a vehicle used in the crime.
[@1]

Torres said the investigation was run jointly with the Sacfi unit of the Los
Lagos prosecutor's office. [@1] He said the inquiry remained sealed because
further steps were pending. [@1] He said the man took delivery of the drug in
his own vehicle and distributed it to different parts of Puerto Montt. [@1] The
PDI said the operation, named Reflejo, followed investigative work that
identified a man who bought consignments of illegal drugs, stored them and sold
them in the area. [@2]
