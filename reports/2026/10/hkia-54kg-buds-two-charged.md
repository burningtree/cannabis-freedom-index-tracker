Hong Kong Customs detected a drug trafficking case involving incoming air
passengers at Hong Kong International Airport on 23 October, seizing
about 5.4 kilograms of suspected cannabis buds worth about HK$1.1
million. [@1] [@2]

A 58-year-old mainland man arriving from Phu Quoc, Vietnam, was found
with the buds in his carry-on suitcase and arrested; follow-up
investigation led to the arrest of a 36-year-old mainland man arriving
from Bangkok, Thailand, suspected to be connected. [@1] [@2] The two were
jointly charged with one count of trafficking in a dangerous drug, with
the case to be brought at West Kowloon Magistrates' Courts on 24
October. [@1] [@2]
