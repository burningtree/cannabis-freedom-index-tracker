Mossos d'Esquadra officers searched a detached house in Sant Just Desvern, in the Baix Llobregat, on
20 October 2026 and seized 715 cannabis plants about a metre and a half tall and in an advanced stage
of flowering, grown in three separate spaces in the basement. [@1,2]

Upstairs they found two large rooms fitted with a sophisticated electrical system and numerous
heating mats for drying buds, and they also took 2,800 euros in cash, an electric pistol and large
precision scales. [@1,2] The house had a fraudulent connection to the electricity grid that carried
a serious risk of fire; workers removed the installation and restored a safe supply once the court
diligences were finished. [@1,2]

Two people in the house were arrested on suspicion of a crime against public health, and a
92-year-old woman who lived there and is related to them was identified. [@1,2] Both arrestees were
brought before the duty court at Esplugues de Llobregat; one arrest was then lifted so that person
could care for the elderly woman. [@1,2]

The search was authorised by court no. 3 of Esplugues de Llobregat and grew out of a local police
intervention near the house months earlier; the Mossos investigation unit at Esplugues ran the case
with support from security officers and the ARRO operational resources group. [@1,2]
