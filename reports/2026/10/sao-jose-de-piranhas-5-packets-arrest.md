Military Police in São José de Piranhas, in the Sertão of Paraíba, arrested a
man identified by his initials, J. H. B. R., on the afternoon of Thursday 29
October 2026 on suspicion of involvement in drug trafficking. [@1] Officers on
an ostensive patrol along Rua Gilberto Furtado, in the São Sebastião
neighbourhood, said he behaved suspiciously when he saw the police car
approaching. [@1]

He ran towards a caçamba, a tipper truck, parked nearby, and during the attempt
to escape dropped material on the ground. [@1] Police recovered five trouxinhas
— small paper packets — of a substance similar to cannabis. [@1] They also
recovered five rocks of a substance similar to crack. [@1] A search of his
person turned up 40 reais in cash, and he told the officers the drugs were for
his own consumption. [@1]

The man and all the material seized were taken to the Civil Police station for
the region for the legal procedures and investigation of the case. [@1]
