Two Colombian men were arrested in Maó, also called Mahón, in Menorca, on
suspicion of a crime against public health after National Police officers
stopped their car at a selective vehicle check. [@1] Officers saw the front-seat
passenger throw an object out of the window as they approached, then stopped the
car and identified its three occupants. [@1]

The search found about 25 grams of cocaine, around 2.5 grams of MDMA (ecstasy),
almost half a gram of tusi, just over half a gram of methamphetamine, about 0.40
grams of marijuana and two doses of LSD. [@1] Tusi is also known as pink
cocaine. [@3] Officers also seized more than €100 in cash, a precision scale and
clear plastic bags that police said were intended for distributing the drugs.
[@1]

The two men were taken to the National Police station in Maó, where the case
file was drawn up. [@3] The National Police announced the arrests in a statement
on Thursday. [@2] The arrests were part of a police operation of selective
vehicle checks in Menorca. [@1]
