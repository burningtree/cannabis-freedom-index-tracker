On Tuesday 27 October 2026, Federal Highway Police officers stopped a truck
during checks on the BR-262 in Corumbá, Mato Grosso do Sul. [@1] Asked about the
trip, the driver said the vehicle was empty because a mechanical failure had
made loading impossible, but he contradicted himself when officers asked for
more detail. [@1] Suspecting the account, the team searched further and, on
opening the cargo compartment, found several black bags of drugs. [@1]

In all they seized 418 kilograms of skunk, cannabis flower, and 13.2 kilograms
of hashish, cannabis resin, together with 726 kilograms of cocaine and two 9mm
pistols. [@1] Of the cocaine, 347.9 kilograms was base paste and 378.9 kilograms
was hydrochloride. [@1] The Federal Highway Police headlined the cocaine haul as
the third-largest cocaine seizure the force had made in 2026. [@1]

The driver said he had received the illicit material in Corumbá and that the
delivery was meant for Miranda, also in Mato Grosso do Sul. [@3] He was arrested
in the act and taken to the Federal Police station in Corumbá. [@1] g1, citing
the force, likewise reported 418 kilograms of skunk and 13.2 kilograms of
hashish from the same stop. [@2]
