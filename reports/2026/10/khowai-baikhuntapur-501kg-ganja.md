Troops of the Assam Rifles, a paramilitary force, and Customs officers seized
501 kg of ganja, the cannabis preparation sold in India, in a joint operation on
8 October 2026. [@2] The smugglers abandoned the contraband at an isolated
location near Baikhuntapur village, Khowai, Tripura, after a pursuit that
started at West Sonatala. [@2] The driver of the vehicle escaped from the spot.
[@3] No arrest was reported in connection with the seizure. [@3]

The chase ended about 18 km short of Hezamara block, on the boundary between
West Tripura and Khowai districts. [@1] A press release put the estimated value
of the seized ganja at Rs 2.50 crore. [@1] The operation was based on
intelligence inputs and continuous surveillance. [@1] The recovered contraband
was handed over to Customs for further investigation and legal proceedings. [@1]

Assam Rifles said the recovery highlighted continued efforts with partner
agencies to disrupt trafficking networks operating through the Northeast. [@1] A
police official said dry ganja produced in Tripura is not consumed locally and
is smuggled to Bihar and Uttar Pradesh, where it fetches higher prices. [@4]
Under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, cultivating,
possessing or selling cannabis is illegal and can attract up to 20 years in
prison. [@4]
