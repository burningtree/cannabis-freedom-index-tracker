Rowan Webster, 26, and Ollie Wilson, 22, entered guilty pleas at Mold Crown
Court on Friday, 30 October 2026. [@1] They admitted possessing 140 grams of
cannabis and 27.2 grams of cocaine with intent to supply. [@1] The charges
relate to their arrest at The Garage in Forden, near Welshpool, on 8 February
2024. [@1] The court heard that both men were acting as drug couriers. [@1]
Recorder Robert Philpotts adjourned sentencing until 26 November and ordered
pre-sentence reports on both defendants. [@1] He made their cooperation with the
reports a condition of bail and said it was “in your interests”. [@1] “You will
receive substantial credit for your pleas of guilty,” the recorder told them.
[@1]

They gave no indication of plea at that hearing. [@2] Prosecutor Helen Tench
told the magistrates that the offences were not suitable for trial at the lower
court. [@2]
