Police in Thika netted over 265 kg of bhang valued at over Sh5 million in
Kiganjo Estate during the weekend. [@1] Working with community policing,
Thika Sub County Police Commander Lawrence Muchangi said officers seized the
narcotic packed in eight gunny bags and loaded in a personal vehicle on
Friday night. [@1]

Three suspects aboard the vehicle escaped after being confronted: patrol
police spotted the vehicle parked roadside in the estate while gunny bags
were being dropped, and on approach the occupants fled, abandoning the
consignment. [@1] The vehicle and drugs were impounded and moved to the
nearby Kimuchu police post. [@1]

Muchangi said police suspected a well-known bhang trafficker under police
watch who had repeatedly secured bond releases to continue supplying the
region; a manhunt was launched and the public urged to share information,
with an appeal to courts against lenient bail for such criminals. [@1]
