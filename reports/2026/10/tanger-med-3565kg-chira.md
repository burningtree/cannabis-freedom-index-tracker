Port police at Tanger Med, working with customs, thwarted an international drug
trafficking attempt in the early hours of Sunday 11 October, seizing more than three and
a half tonnes of cannabis resin. [@1,2]

Border control of a Moroccan-registered international freight truck uncovered the drugs
hidden inside crates of agricultural produce, carefully packed to facilitate the
trafficking. [@1,2]

The truck driver, a 35-year-old Moroccan national, was placed under the judicial
investigation supervised by the competent prosecutor to clarify the case and dismantle any
national or international ramifications of the network. [@1,2] The operation was part of
sustained border-post efforts against international drug trafficking networks. [@1]
