Desiree Shear, 38, of Gordon, Wisconsin, was sentenced in Washburn County
Circuit Court. [@1] The sentencing hearing was held on 28 October 2026. [@1] She
had pleaded no contest to operating a motor vehicle with a controlled substance
while a minor passenger was in the car. [@1] The case came from a November 2025
traffic stop in which a Wisconsin State Patrol trooper reported smelling
marijuana coming from her vehicle, where her two teenage daughters were sitting.
[@1]

The trooper had stopped her for a traffic violation on Highway 53 near Pierce
Road. [@3] She showed signs of impairment during the stop. [@3] Field sobriety
tests showed she was under the influence of a restricted controlled substance.
[@3] She was arrested and charged with operating while under the influence with
a passenger under 16, possession of THC and possession of drug paraphernalia.
[@3] A blood sample was taken at a local hospital before she was taken to the
Washburn County Jail. [@3] Her daughters, 15 and 17, were left in the care of a
family member. [@3]

The criminal complaint filed in Washburn County Circuit Court said the stop
turned up marijuana and drug paraphernalia in the vehicle. [@2] She faced felony
and misdemeanor charges in the case. [@2]
