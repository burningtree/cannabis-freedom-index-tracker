Police confiscated 574.87 kilos of cannabis from an ambulance on Monday
morning. [@1] [@2] On a tip-off, a joint team of Duhabi Area Police Office
and the Central Response Vehicle tracked the ambulance, suspected of moving
drugs from Itahari to Biratnagar on the Koshi Highway, and found it near the
banks of the Budhi River in Duhabi-6. [@2]

The cannabis lay inside ambulance Na 4 Cha 6063, which carried a 'Nepal India
cooperation' sticker. [@1] [@2] Police impounded the vehicle and are
searching for the driver, who fled during the intervention; further
investigation is underway. [@1]
