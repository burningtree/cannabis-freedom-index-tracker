Simferopol drug-control officers detained a 20-year-old local man suspected
of illegal drug possession during operation Mak, the Crimea Interior Ministry
said on 11 September 2026. [@1] A search of his flat found two bags and a
cardboard box containing cannabis weighing about five kilograms in total. [@1]

The man admitted guilt and told police he had worked as a labourer on a
building site near a village in Simferopol district, noticed wild cannabis
there, returned at night to cut it and planned to dry it for personal use. [@1]
Officers opened a criminal case for large-scale drug possession and ordered
him not to leave his place of residence pending investigation. [@2]

Crimea-news published the same ministry account on the day of the announcement. [@2]
