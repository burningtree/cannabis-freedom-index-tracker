Gendarmes from Brossac and Châteauneuf-sur-Charente stopped a driver at a
coordinated roadside check in Châteauneuf-sur-Charente, in the Charente
department, on 23 September 2026. [@2] His drug test came back positive. [@2]
The officers then searched his home, where they found about 3 kg of cannabis,
five plants and an indoor grow room with equipment for producing the drug. [@1]
His car was immobilised and his driving licence suspended. [@1]

The gendarmes described the case in a Facebook post, which Actu Charente
relayed. [@3] He is due before the tribunal correctionnel — the criminal court
that handles mid-level offences — in early 2027. [@3] Actu Charente wrote that
the quantity, the plants and the grow room moved the case beyond simple
possession and use, already an offence, towards drug trafficking or unlawful
production. [@2] The outlet reported that the maximum for those offences is 10
years in prison and a €7.5 million fine. [@2] Driving under the influence
carries up to two years in prison and a €4,500 fine. [@2]
