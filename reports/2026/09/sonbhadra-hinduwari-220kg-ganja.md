Police in Sonbhadra district, Uttar Pradesh, arrested two alleged cannabis
smugglers, Bires, 45, and Govind, 25, both from Budaun district. [@2] The
arrests came during a joint operation by Robertsganj police and the SOG (Special
Operations Group) under Operation Shuddhi. [@2] Officers were checking vehicles
near Gangwar village late on Sunday, in the Robertsganj police station area,
when they were alerted to a truck parked near Hinduwari Tiraha. [@1] The two
jumped from the truck and tried to flee, and were caught beside the road near
the bridge railing. [@3] Police recovered 220 kg of ganja, or cannabis, from the
two men. [@1] The cannabis was packed in seven sacks hidden in a secret
compartment welded into the front of the cargo area. [@3] Police valued the
cannabis at about Rs 1.10 crore and the total seizure, including the six-wheeler
truck, two mobile phones and Rs 4,000 in cash, at about Rs 1.20 crore. [@2]

During questioning, the men told police they were in contact with people in
Delhi and worked bringing them cannabis. [@3] They said they collected the
consignment in the Sundargarh-Kalahandi area of Odisha through a local contact.
[@3] A case was registered against both at Robertsganj police station under the
NDPS Act, India's main drug law. [@2] Investigators were examining the seized
phones to identify financiers, handlers and local contacts. [@2]

The consignment was being transported from Odisha to Delhi, police said. [@1]
Superintendent of Police Abhishek Verma said the seizure was a major strike
against organised drug trafficking and that further action would target every
link in the Odisha-Delhi supply chain. [@2]
