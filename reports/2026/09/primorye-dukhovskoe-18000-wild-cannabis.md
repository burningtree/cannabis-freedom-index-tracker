Drug-control officers and criminal detectives from the Primorye Border
district Interior Ministry found two wild Manchurian cannabis sites covering
0.8 hectares near Dukhovskoye during operation Mak-2026, the Primorye
police press service said on 28 September 2026. [@1] The plants showed no
signs of cultivation. [@2]

Officers treated the sites with herbicides on the spot and destroyed more
than 18,000 cannabis plants in total. [@1] Vladivostok’s news wire carried
the same figures from the regional police account on 28 September. [@2]
The release named no suspects or landowners. [@1,2]
