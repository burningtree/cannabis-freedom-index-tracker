After a tip to the National Police's specialised anti-drug department, regional anti-narcotics
agents from Concepción ran Operation Aquidabán I on 8 September 2026 at Estancia Sanguina
in Yby Yaú, Concepción department. [@1,2] Concepción police and the tactical police unit
supported the work, directed by prosecutor Pablo René Zárate. [@1,2]

Officers cut and burned eight plots: five in harvest stage totalling about 10 hectares and
three still growing totalling about five hectares, for roughly 15 hectares in all. [@1,2]
They also destroyed about 300 kilograms of chopped marijuana spread on a black tarp, seven
precarious camps and assorted camp gear. [@1,2] Police seized a six-metre aluminium boat
without an engine but with homemade oars, which they suspect was used to move drugs and
people on the Aquidabán river. [@1,2]

ABC Color said police put the economic loss to traffickers at G. 6.795 billion. [@1]
Concepción al Día said the action was part of the SUMAR anti-drug plan. [@2]
