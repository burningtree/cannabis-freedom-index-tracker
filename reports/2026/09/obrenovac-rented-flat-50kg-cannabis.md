On the order of the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office in Belgrade, organised-crime police searched a
rented apartment in Obrenovac and arrested two men on suspicion of unauthorised production and supply
of narcotics. [@1,2] Interior minister Ivica Dačić said officers found multiple PVC packages holding
about 50 kilogrammes of plant material believed to be cannabis. [@1]

The prosecutor's office identified the suspects as D.K., 32, and L.J., 30, and said they were believed
to have kept the cannabis in the flat for sale without authorisation. [@2] Police ordered 48-hour
detention before handing them to prosecutors with a criminal complaint. [@1,2]

Dačić said the seizure was part of continued work against drug trafficking channels. [@1] The charge
named in the prosecutor's notice was unauthorised production and placing narcotics on the market. [@2]
