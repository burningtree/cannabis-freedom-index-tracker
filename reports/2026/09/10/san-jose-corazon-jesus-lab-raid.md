Public force officers responding to a 911 call about a domestic dispute entered a home in
Corazón de Jesús, in San José’s Hospital district, and saw a large quantity of suspected
drugs on a table. [@1,2] They alerted the public security ministry, and prosecutors assigned
the case to the Drug Control Police (PCD), which guarded the house until a search warrant
could be executed. [@1,2]

On Thursday 10 September 2026 the PCD search confirmed a controlled-environment marijuana
laboratory. [@1] Officers detained three men surnamed Chávez, Abarca and Fallas and two women
surnamed Araya and Muñoz; the ministry said most had previous drug possession records. [@1,2]

They seized 53 plants between about 20 centimetres and 1.5 metres tall, 2.262 kg of buds,
1.205 kg of processed marijuana, 249 g of powder and 85 g of compacted powder, plus 35 bags
of seeds and cultivation equipment including lamps, automated irrigation and climate controls. [@1,2]
The processed forms alone exceeded 3.8 kg, excluding the live plants. [@1]

The five were taken to the San José First Judicial Circuit while prosecutors prepared
custody requests. [@1,2]
