On Tuesday 30 September 2026 agents from Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat
(SENAD), backed by the internal defence command's joint task force (CODI-FTC), eradicated
16 hectares of cannabis at rural Estancia La Niña in Bella Vista Norte, Amambay department.
[@1,2] The work continued a search that had begun on 25 September under anti-narcotics
prosecutor Sarita Bonssi. [@1,2]

La Nación said the teams destroyed seven precarious camps and eliminated about 49 tonnes
of marijuana, made up of an estimated 48,000 kg still in the fields, 800 kg of branches
drying and 200 kg already chopped, and seized four bags of seeds. [@1] La Tribuna carried
the same hectare, tonnage and camp figures. [@2]

Investigators put the traffickers' loss at USD 7.3 million using expected Brazilian market
values. [@1,2] Neither account reported an arrest. [@1,2]

Photographs published with the stories show destroyed camps and SENAD personnel at La Niña
during the September intervention. [@1,2]
