A man was fined Sh24 million after being convicted of trafficking 266.2 kg
of bhang valued at Sh7.98 million in Elgeyo Marakwet County. [@1] Vincent
Odhiambo was sentenced to pay the fine or serve 10 years in prison in
default, following a conviction secured by the ODPP under Section 4(a)(ii) of
the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (Control) Amendment Act,
2022. [@1]

The prosecution told the court that on January 18, 2026, Odhiambo, jointly
with others, was found trafficking 266.2 kg of cannabis sativa transported
in a Toyota Fielder along the Kapchorwa-Torongo murram road in Kamwosor
Location, Keiyo South Sub-County. [@1] Principal Prosecution Counsel Felix
Namasake led the prosecution, calling six witnesses. [@1]

The court treated the quantity as a significant aggravating factor and
imposed a deterrent sentence, noting its duty to protect the public from
narcotics offences. [@1]
