On Monday 14 September 2026 a joint team uprooted and burned about 36,000 fully grown marijuana plants on about 6,000 square metres of public land along the boundary of Barangay Saclit in Sadanga, Mountain Province, and Tinglayan, Kalinga. [@1,2] The Daily Tribune put the plants at about 7.2 million pesos. [@1]

The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency's Mountain Province office led the work, with the Sadanga municipal police, the provincial intelligence and drug-enforcement units, and the 2nd provincial mobile force company. [@1,2] No cultivator was caught at the site. [@1,2] Abante reported that the caretakers may have been tipped off and left. [@2] The inquiry into who planted the crop was still open. [@1,2]
