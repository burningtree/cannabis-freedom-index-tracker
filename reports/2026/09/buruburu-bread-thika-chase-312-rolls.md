Two persons of interest were arrested in separate Nairobi and Thika operations
after police recovered a combined 312 rolls of bhang and 3 kg of the
narcotic, the DCI announced on Monday, September 21, 2026. [@1] [@2]

At Buruburu Police Station, a 26-year-old woman visiting her detained
boyfriend asked desk officers to pass him a loaf of bread; the unsealed
package aroused suspicion and a search found five rolls of bhang and a
matchbox buried deep in the loaf. [@1] A further jacket search recovered
seven more rolls, for 12 in all, secured as exhibits pending arraignment.
[@1] [@2]

In Thika West, a motorcycle rider and his female pillion passenger met a
police roadblock at Kairi and fled; the chase ended near Ree Supermarket
when the rider lost control at a speed bump. [@1] The passenger melted into
the crowd while rider John Mwangi alias Bombay was held; a search recovered
300 rolls plus 3 kg wrapped in yellow polythene strapped to the seat, and he
was taken to Kimuchu Police Station pending arraignment. [@1] [@2]
