DIICOT prosecutors from Caraș-Severin and organised-crime police carried out five house searches on
17 September 2026 after finding a cannabis plantation in an isolated area outside Berzovia. [@1,2]

Investigators said four suspects aged between 19 and 53 set up and maintained the plantation during
2026 to sell the production. [@1,2] They bought cannabis seeds from Spain through an online platform
and chose land surrounded by dense vegetation, equipped with support and irrigation systems. [@1,2]

The four were taken to the DIICOT office for questioning; the reports did not say whether any were
arrested or how much cannabis was involved. [@1,2]
