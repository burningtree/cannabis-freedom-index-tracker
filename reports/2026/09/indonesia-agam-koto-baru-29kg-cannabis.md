Officers of West Sumatra's provincial narcotics agency, BNNP Sumatera Barat,
stopped a car at the Koto Baru market junction in Agam regency at about 04:50 on
Tuesday 15 September 2026. [@1] The driver rammed the blockade car, badly
damaging it, the agency said. [@2] A search found a large blue sack holding 20
large packages of dried cannabis and 10 more packages wrapped in brown tape.
[@1] The officers detained the driver, a man identified only by the initial S.
[@1] A second man, initial A, was detained at the Ganting petrol station in
Padang Panjang. [@1]

The agency put the total net weight at 29,853.32 grams, about 29.85 kg. [@1]
Chief Toton Rasyid said the blue sack held 19,494.69 grams and the taped
packages 10,358.63 grams. [@1] He said officers had acted on intelligence about
a planned road shipment of dried cannabis from Mandailing Natal, in North
Sumatra, to Padang Panjang. [@1]

The two men were held as suspects and charged under Indonesia's 2009 narcotics
law. [@1] A told investigators he had been ordered by Solihin, an inmate at
Bukittinggi prison, to take delivery of the 30 packages. [@1] The agency said
Solihin was arrested by BNNP Sumbar in 2025 in a cannabis case. [@1]
