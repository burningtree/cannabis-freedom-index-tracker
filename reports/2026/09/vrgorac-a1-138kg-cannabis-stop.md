Border police from the Vrgorac station stopped a 26-year-old Croatian man driving on the A1 motorway
on Tuesday 22 September 2026 after spotting two suspicious sports bags in the boot of his car. [@1,2]
They moved the vehicle to the station, obtained a search warrant and found 20 packages of cannabis
weighing 13 kilogrammes and 848 grammes. [@1,2]

Police said the drug was intended for further unauthorised sale. [@1,2] The man was arrested,
questioned and handed to a custody officer with a criminal complaint for unauthorised production
and trafficking in drugs. [@1,2]
