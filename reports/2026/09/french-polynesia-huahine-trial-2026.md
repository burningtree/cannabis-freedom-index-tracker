The Papeete court sentenced Huahine producers to three years, two suspended, and a 2.5 million CFP fine; distributors received two years suspended and a 500,000 CFP fine. Police had seized 2 kg of cannabis, 177 sticks and 4.5 million CFP in cash. The case followed the August seizure of 513 kg of plants and cuttings on the island. [@1]
