Algeria's army carried out operations across several regions of the country
between 2 and 8 September 2026. [@1] Its combined detachments, working with the
different security services, intercepted 35 narcotrafiquants, or drug
traffickers. [@1] The detachments also foiled attempts to bring 3 quintals and
29 kilograms (329 kg) of kif traité into the country from the Moroccan border.
[@1]

The same operations led to the seizure of 210,986 psychotropic tablets. [@1] The
army described the operations as part of efforts to prevent narcotics from
entering national territory. [@1] The figures cover a week of operations across
several military regions rather than a single raid. [@1]

The army presented the operations as part of its fight against drug trafficking.
[@1] The army's operations that week also covered counterterrorism, illegal gold
mining, smuggling and migration. [@1]
