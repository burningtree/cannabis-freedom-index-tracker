Yakutia police reported the second stage of operation Mak-2026 on
29 September 2026, saying officers had found 37 wild-cannabis sites in
ten districts including Amginsky, Lensky and Namsky. [@1] They destroyed
eight tonnes of wild cannabis covering 14 hectares. [@1]

The republic Interior Ministry said investigators opened nine criminal
cases linked to natural-origin drugs during the stage and issued 24 orders
requiring the destruction of narcotic plants still growing on private land. [@1]
360.ru quoted the same totals from the Yakutia police press office. [@2]
Neither outlet named anyone arrested during the eradication work itself. [@1,2]
