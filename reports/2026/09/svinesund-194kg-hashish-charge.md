Prosecutors in Øst have charged a 41-year-old Polish lorry driver with a gross drug offence after customs stopped him at Svinesund. [@1] TV 2 reported the charge on 17 September 2026. [@1]

The stop was on the afternoon of 23 April 2026, as he entered Norway. [@1] The indictment says he tried to bring in 194.8 kg of cannabis, hidden in cavities around the lorry's refrigeration unit. [@1] Police lawyer Anders Svarholt told TV 2 the find was the work of Customs at the border. [@1]

The driver has admitted importing cannabis. [@1] Svarholt said the admission does not cover the whole amount: the driver said he had been told it was about 80 to 100 kg, not nearly 200 kg. [@1]

One day has been set aside for the case at Halden courthouse. [@1] TV 2 does not give that date. [@1]
