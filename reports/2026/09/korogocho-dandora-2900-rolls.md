Police officers recovered approximately 2,900 rolls of cannabis after intercepting a
suspected narcotics trafficker during a patrol in the Highridge area of Nairobi County.
[@1,2]

According to a National Police Service statement issued on 23 September 2026, officers saw
a man riding a motorcycle while carrying a brown sack and pursued him along Korogocho Road
toward the Dandora Dumpsite. [@1,2] Realising he was followed, the suspect abandoned the
sack and accelerated toward the dumpsite, evading arrest. [@1,2]

The sack was found to contain about 2,900 rolls of cannabis, commonly known as bhang.
[@1,2] The exhibit was secured in safe custody for further investigation, and efforts to
trace and apprehend the suspect continue. [@1]
