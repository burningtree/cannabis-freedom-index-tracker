Three people were arrested in a multi-agency crackdown on narcotics
trafficking in Kiambu and Busia counties, with cannabis sativa recovered in
separate coordinated operations targeting cultivation, possession and
transportation. [@1] The DCI announced the operations in a statement on its
X account on Wednesday, September 16, 2026. [@1]

In Kiambu East, detectives acting on intelligence raided a residential
property at Runda Palm Garden, Thindigua, where a cannabis sativa plantation
was found flourishing in an otherwise quiet neighbourhood; one person was
arrested as the plants were secured as exhibits. [@1]

In Ruiru, Gatong'ora station officers stinging narcotics in the Rainbow area
arrested another person found with 80 rolls and a sachet of bhang, seized as
exhibits while the source and distribution chain were investigated. [@1] In
Busia, officers intercepted a red TVS motorcycle at the Kenya-Uganda border
carrying what looked like a sack of potatoes, inside which two cannabis
consignments were concealed; the rider was arrested with bike and drugs
secured. [@1]

The three were being processed pending arraignment with the cannabis in
police custody, as the DCI said operations continue countrywide to dismantle
cultivation, trafficking and distribution networks. [@1]
