Le Dauphiné Libéré reported that gendarmes from the community brigade at Rémuzat acted on a
tip about a cannabis plantation in the Baronnies provençales hills. [@1]

On site they smelled cannabis, seized nearly 450 plants and destroyed them, the paper said.
[@1] A 39-year-old man from the Drôme, caught watering the crop, was arrested. [@1]

According to the report he admitted the offence and said he used the harvest for barter, and
was expected to go before a court under a comparution sur reconnaissance préalable de
culpabilité procedure after custody. [@1]

The newspaper published a photograph of the plantation with its account. [@1]
