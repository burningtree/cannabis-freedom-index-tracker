The Bureau of Customs-NAIA turned over to PDEA eight abandoned checked-in bags holding
about 94.597 kg of suspected high-grade kush valued at P141.895 million. [@1,2]

Acting on PDEA intelligence, NAIA Inter-Agency Drug Interdiction Task Group operatives
inspected the bags, left behind at the baggage conveyor and moved to the interline baggage
room of Terminal 3 on 1 September; X-ray re-screening showed suspicious images and a
narcotics dog gave a positive signal. [@1,2] Investigators linked the bags to three
arriving passengers from Bangkok. [@2]

The turnover was reported belatedly on 4 September, with the district collector and the
Customs commissioner pledging tighter border controls. [@1]
