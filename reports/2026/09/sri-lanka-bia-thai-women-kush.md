Police Narcotics Bureau officers arrested three Thai women at the arrival terminal
of Bandaranaike International Airport in the early hours of 20 September 2026
after they arrived from Bangkok on Thai Airways flight TG-307, which landed at
about 12.50 am. [@1,2]

In the first operation a 38-year-old woman was found with 3.22 kg of kush and
1.852 kg of hashish; in the second a 31-year-old woman was found with 1.994 kg of
kush and 620 g of hashish. [@1,2] A 36-year-old woman on the same flight was
detained for allegedly aiding and abetting the second suspect. [@1,2] The drugs
were sealed in airtight packaging made to resemble nutritional supplements and
collagen products and bars of soap, with a combined 5.214 kg of kush and 2.472 kg
of hashish valued at about Rs. 85 million. [@1,2] The bureau was still establishing
whether the two seizures were connected. [@1,2]
