cannabis and more than EC$10,000 in cash at Creekside. [@1] The stop happened at
about 7:45 a.m. on 14 September 2026, when officers of the Criminal
Investigations Department Task Force intercepted a white Toyota Ractis carrying
three men. [@1] A search of the vehicle found plant material resembling cannabis
and sums of Eastern Caribbean and United States currency. [@2] The three were
arrested on suspicion of unlawful possession of cannabis and taken into custody
for questioning. [@1]

WIC News put the cash at more than EC$1,000. [@3] WIC News described the stop as
part of a surveillance operation. [@3] WIC News said the car, drugs and cash
