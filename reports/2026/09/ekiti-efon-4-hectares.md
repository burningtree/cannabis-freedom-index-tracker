In early September 2026, as part of coordinated NDLEA raids across nine states, operatives
destroyed a four-hectare cannabis farm in the Efon-Alaaye forest area of Ekiti State and
recovered 117 kilograms of harvested skunk, Dateline Nigeria and Channels Television reported
in statements issued on 6 September. [@1,2]

The outlets said officers arrested Chuks Alex, 50, and Asafa Adekunle, 35, in connection with
the Ekiti operation. [@1,2] The same NDLEA release described separate seizures from Lagos
ports, Adamawa, Kogi and other states but treated the Ekiti farm destruction as one distinct
field action. [@1,2]

Neither account published photographs of the eradication. [@1,2]
