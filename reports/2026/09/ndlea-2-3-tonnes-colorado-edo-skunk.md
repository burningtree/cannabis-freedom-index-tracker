A real estate agent, Abideen Jide Salami, was arrested on 9 September 2026 after
arriving with a collection code sent by his brother in the UK to collect a
luggage holding Colorado, a synthetic cannabis. [@1] Officers at Murtala
Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja, Lagos, found 27 parcels of Colorado
weighing 32.20 kg in the luggage. [@1] Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement
Agency, NDLEA, said the operations intercepted more than 2.3 tonnes of assorted
illicit drugs, including synthetic cannabis, opioids and other controlled
substances. [@1] Spokesman Femi Babafemi announced the results in a statement on
Sunday. [@1]

A follow-up operation led to the arrest of Nurudeen Abiodun Kadiri, 44, who told
investigators his brother in the UK sent him the 12.25 kg Colorado consignment.
[@1] At Maza Maza motor park in Lagos, officers found three packs of Colorado in
a music speaker bound for Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire, and then Liberia. [@1] Tour
agent Ifeanyi Obed Augustine, 41, and the alleged owner of the consignment,
Obasi Ugochukwu, 46, were arrested at a nearby eatery. [@1] Ifeanyi Nwokolo
Henry was arrested at Iddo Park on 11 September while trying to dispatch 2.1 kg
of Colorado to Kano. [@1]

In Edo State, officers destroyed 4,832.56 kg of cannabis, known locally as
skunk, on farms in Usen, Igwe-Oke and Ogboha forests. [@1] The operations
covered Ovia South-West, Owan East and Owan West local government areas, and
Sunday Segun, 39, was arrested on 11 September. [@1] In Akure, Ondo State,
officers raided a house on Oke-Iya, Ijoka Road on 9 September and found 186 kg
of skunk in the boot of a Toyota Avalon. [@1] Paul Ogbuwala, 58, was arrested in
connection with the seizure. [@1] Hannah Michael was arrested at Gonin Gora in
Kaduna State with 369.6 kg of cannabis. [@2] Four suspects, including
70-year-old Godwoman Gboriye, were arrested at Ovom in Yenagoa, Bayelsa State,
with 110 bags weighing 876 kg. [@1]

The statement also reported 785,000 pregabalin capsules weighing 669.425 kg
found with Saidu Adamu, 20, in Sabon Gari, Kano. [@1] In Plateau State, Chinedu
Uzor, 51, was arrested at Katako Market in Jos with 157,100 tramadol capsules.
[@1] In Delta State, raids in Ughelli and Asaba recovered pentazocine
injections, codeine syrup, tramadol, diazepam, flunitrazepam and MDMA. [@1]
Emeka Oji and Chukwuebuka Anedi were arrested. [@1] Habibu Sabitu, 28, was
arrested with 1,700 diazepam tablets concealed in loaves of bread. [@1]
