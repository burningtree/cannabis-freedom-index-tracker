On 15 September 2026, Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA) officers at Tuas
Checkpoint referred a Malaysia-registered motorcycle for extra checks after risk information from
the Integrated Targeting Centre. [@1,2] They found four bundles of vegetable matter under the seat
and, after Central Narcotics Bureau (CNB) officers joined the search, six more bundles in a black
haversack on the handlebar. [@1,2]

The bundles contained about 10.164 kg of cannabis in total. [@1,2] A 36-year-old Malaysian man
and a 35-year-old Malaysian woman were arrested. [@1,2] The agencies said the seizure was worth
more than S$203,000 and could supply about 1,450 users for a week. [@1,2] Under the Misuse of
Drugs Act, importing more than 500 g of cannabis may attract the death penalty. [@1]
