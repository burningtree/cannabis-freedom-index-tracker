Customs officers at Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport seized 906.52 g of
cannabis from a transit express shipment, according to a Guangzhou Customs
statement carried by Chinanews. [@1] The parcel was declared as children's
bowling toys. [@1]

Officers said an X-ray image looked abnormal, so they opened the shipment and
found a toy box containing eight packages of green plant material wrapped in
silver plastic with a strong smell. [@1] Laboratory testing confirmed cannabis
weighing 906.52 g. [@1] Customs reminded the public that smuggling, selling,
transporting or manufacturing drugs is a criminal offence in China regardless of
quantity. [@1]
