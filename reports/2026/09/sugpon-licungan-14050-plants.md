From 5 a.m. on 8 September until 10.20 p.m. on 9 September 2026, Ilocos Sur police burned marijuana on seven sites along the border of Barangay Licungan in Sugpon and Barangay Tacadang in Kibungan, Benguet. [@1,2] The sites covered about 1,960 square metres and held about 11,050 fully grown plants and 3,000 seedlings. [@1] The Daily Tribune valued plants and seedlings together at about 2.33 million pesos. [@1] RMN, citing the Dangerous Drugs Board price, put the grown plants at 2.21 million pesos and the seedlings at 120,000 pesos. [@2]

The largest site was about 800 square metres, with about 4,000 fully grown plants valued at 800,000 pesos. [@1] Another, of about 450 square metres, held about 2,700 plants valued at 540,000 pesos. [@1] Officers uprooted and burned the plants after an inventory and kept samples for a laboratory. [@1]

The Ilocos Sur provincial drug enforcement unit led the work, with the regional mobile force, two provincial mobile companies, the provincial intelligence unit and Sugpon police. [@1] No cultivator was arrested. [@1]
