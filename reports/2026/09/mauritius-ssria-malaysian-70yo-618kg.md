Officers of the Mauritius Revenue Authority's Customs Anti-Narcotics Section intercepted 6.18 kg of cannabis at the Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport. [@1] The seizure was made on 15 September 2026 after the arrival of flight MK647 from Malaysia, when a 70-year-old male Malaysian passenger was stopped following information received, profiling and questioning. [@1]

A search of his registered luggage found the cannabis concealed among items of clothing, with an estimated street value of Rs 9.27 million. [@1] The passenger and the exhibits were handed to the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit for further enquiry. [@1]
