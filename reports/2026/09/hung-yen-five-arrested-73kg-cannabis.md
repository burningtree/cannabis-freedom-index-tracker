Police in Hưng Yên province arrested five people accused of growing cannabis
plants and selling the drug online. [@1] The provincial police's Drug Crime
Investigation Division, working with the Ministry of Public Security's drug
crime department and Phụng Công commune police, inspected locations in Hạ
Hamlet, Phụng Công Commune. [@1]

Between February and June 2026 the group allegedly sold 59.19 kg of cannabis and
kept another 14.508 kg for sale, a total of 73.698 kg. [@1] Police said it was
the largest cannabis case recorded in Hưng Yên to date. [@2]

Investigators said the suspects rented several locations in Hưng Yên and Hà Nội
to grow, tend and harvest cannabis. [@1] They allegedly used social media to buy
seeds and to organise the cultivation and sale of the drug. [@1] After
harvesting, the cannabis was dried, cut into smaller pieces, packaged and
distributed through online channels and delivery services. [@1] Police also
found a closed cultivation and care system installed at Phụng Công Commune. [@1]

The investigation agency opened a criminal case and prosecuted several suspects
for illegally trading narcotic substances and growing plants containing narcotic
substances. [@1] In Việt Nam, cannabis is classed as a narcotic, and
cultivating, possessing, trafficking or using it is prohibited under
drug-control laws. [@1]
