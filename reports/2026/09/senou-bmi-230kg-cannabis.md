Around 22:00 on Thursday 17 September 2026, Bamako’s mobile intervention brigade of customs
stopped a goods truck at Sénou, on the edge of the capital, after a customs tip. [@1,2] The truck
had come from a neighbouring country. [@1,2] Under a load of plastic waste officers found 230
kilograms of cannabis: 282 bricks of skunk, 29 balls of kush and nine moulds of cannabis resin.
[@1,2] Customs estimated the value at 312 million CFA francs. [@1,2]

The driver and his apprentice abandoned the vehicle and ran. [@1,2] Director-general Cheickna Amala
Diallo, an inspector-general, was at the scene and praised the brigade. [@2] He said the point of
the seizure was the protection of the population, especially young people, beyond the value of the
goods. [@2] Malijet reported that the inquiry was still trying to identify the two men and the
supply network. [@1]
