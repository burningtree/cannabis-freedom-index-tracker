Latvian police said they broke up a six-person organised drug-dealing group in Rīga and nearby
Ādaži, seizing about 2 kilograms of cannabis and smaller amounts of other narcotics. [@1,2]

On 14 September 2026, in an existing investigation, officers from the Rīga criminal police, the
Special Tasks Battalion and support units arrested five people in Ķengaraga and Ādaži. [@1,2] They
were born in 1994, 2000, 2003, 2004 and 2006. [@1,2] One flat held a stash with several different
drugs. [@1,2]

On 17 September police arrested a sixth member, a man born in 1984, on suspicion of large-scale
amphetamine distribution, and seized more drugs at linked addresses. [@1,2]

Eleven searches and a cryptocurrency-wallet search recovered about 2 kilograms of cannabis, about
15 grams of cocaine, about 700 grams of amphetamine, about 20 grams of ketamine, tablets, scales
and other items; exact weights await laboratory tests. [@1,2] Police also took four Mercedes and BMW
cars and more than €5,500 in cash they said was probably criminal proceeds. [@1,2]

Three suspects were jailed pending trial and others received non-custodial measures. [@1,2] Proceedings
were opened for organised large-scale drug dealing, which carries five to fifteen years in prison
under Latvian law. [@1,2]
