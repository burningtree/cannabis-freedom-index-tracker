On Friday 5 September 2026 the National Anti-Drug Police Directorate (DNPA), with the
National Special Forces Directorate (DNFE), eradicated and incinerated two marijuana
plots at Los Zorros aldea in Marale municipality, Francisco Morazán department. [@1]

La Tribuna said the fields covered about three manzanas and held roughly 12,000 plants
between 1.5 and 2 metres tall, and that officers also destroyed a rustic structure they
believed was used to process and store marijuana. [@1] The micro-trafficking prosecutor's
office accompanied the operation. [@1]

The same newspaper had reported on 6 August that DNPA teams first located and secured the
two plots after intelligence work, counting about 12,000 plants in the same area and
seizing 20 pounds of dry marijuana in two sacks. [@2] The September burn followed that
August find; neither story reports an arrest. [@1,2]

La Tribuna published photographs of anti-drug police at the secured plots in August and
during the September eradication. [@1,2]

No cultivator was identified in either account. [@1,2]
