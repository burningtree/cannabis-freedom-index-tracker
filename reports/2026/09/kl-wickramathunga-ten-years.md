Dasham Novada Wickramathunga, a 33-year-old Sri Lankan barber, pleaded guilty in the Kuala Lumpur
High Court on 29 September 2026. [@1,2] Judge Datuk Aslam Zainuddin sentenced him to 10 years in
prison and 12 strokes of the cane, with the prison term running from his arrest on 6 April 2024.
[@1,2]

Harian Metro says he faced an alternative charge of trafficking about 2.569 kg of cannabis, jointly
with two Malaysians, at a residence on Jalan Ipoh in Sentul at 11.30 pm on 6 April 2024, under
section 39A(2) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952 read with section 34 of the Penal Code. [@1] Utusan
Malaysia describes the charge as possession of 2.57 kg at a condominium unit on Jalan Ipoh. [@2] The
prosecution told the court the original charge had been under section 39B, which can carry life
imprisonment and at least 15 strokes, before a defence representation led to the alternative charge.
[@1]

Defence counsel Zulkifli Awang said Wickramathunga was a first offender who had earned about RM1,700
a month as a barber and supported elderly parents in Sri Lanka. [@1,2] In the same hearing the court
discharged a Malaysian woman aged 25 and a Malaysian man aged 29 after the Attorney General's
Chambers gave no instruction to continue against them. [@1]
