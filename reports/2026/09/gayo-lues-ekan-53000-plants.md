From Thursday 17 September through Sunday 20 September 2026 a Gayo Lues police
team destroyed about 53,000 cannabis plants spread over roughly 26.5 hectares
in mountains between Ekan and Pepelah villages, Pining subdistrict, Aceh’s Gayo
Lues regency, during Operasi Antik Seulawah 2026. [@1,2]

News of Aceh and Liputan 1 reported that Gayo Lues police chief Cakra Donya
led the group, which left Ekan at 08:00 on 17 September and walked about ten
hours through steep forest to reach the mapped plots. [@1,2] Over the following
days officers burned the standing crop on the spot, found huts and three presses
they believed were used to pack cannabis, and seized about 180 kilograms of dried
cannabis in three stashes. [@1,2]

Police kept ten plants as evidence at district headquarters and burned the rest.
[@2] No arrests were reported. [@1,2]

Metropolis.id published a police photograph of dried cannabis from the site.
