On 22 May 2026, police on patrol in the Sanlaet area of Luganville, Santo, stopped a
black taxi that was blocking traffic and searched it. [@1] They found marijuana in a
black basket belonging to Bakon Etul, also known as Bakon Embulu, and arrested him.
[@1] Forensic tests on 9 June 2026 confirmed 135 g of marijuana. [@1]

Etul pleaded guilty on 18 September 2026 before Justice Dudley Aru. [@1] The court
set a 15-month starting point, cut it for his early guilty plea and time on remand,
then added six months because he was a fourth-time offender, including a 2008
cannabis cultivation conviction. [@1]

The end sentence was eight months in prison to be served immediately. [@1]
