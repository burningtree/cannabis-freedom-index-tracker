Special agents of Paraguay's anti-drug agency SENAD, from the regional office at Salto del Guairá,
searched a rural property at Maracaná, in the department of Canindeyú, and seized 1,753 kilograms of
cannabis, local outlets reported on 30 September 2026. [@1,2,3]

Hidden in a wooded part of the property were 478 kilograms of marijuana pressed into brick-shaped
bales ready for sale, together with 1,275 kilograms of chopped marijuana in 51 large sacks, showing
the site worked as a collection and pre-processing point; under the direction of anti-narcotics
prosecutor Juan Benegas, the fiscal-police team weighed samples in the field and moved the evidence
to the regional base for its definitive weighing and custody. [@2,3]

Those responsible for the stash fled when the agents arrived and nobody was arrested; the seizure is
suspected to have hit a supply point feeding the drug market across the border in Brazil, and the
investigation continues to identify the property's owners and the logistics network behind the load.
[@1,2]
