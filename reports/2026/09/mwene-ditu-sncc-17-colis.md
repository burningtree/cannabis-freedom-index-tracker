Security services in the railway town of Mwene-Ditu, Lomami province, seized 17 packages of
cannabis at the yard of the Société nationale des chemins de fer du Congo (SNCC) while the
drugs were on a freight train preparing to head toward Katanga. [@1,2]

On Friday 4 September 2026 the packages were burned on the grounds of Mwene-Ditu city hall
in the presence of mayor Gérard Tshibanda Kabwe, members of the urban security council and
local SNCC managers. [@1,2] Express Medias noted that trading the substance is banned under
Congolese law. [@2]

7sur7 said the incineration formed part of municipal efforts against cannabis circulation,
sale and use, which Kabwe has treated as a priority since taking office because of the
drug's impact on youth. [@1] The reports name no suspects or charges. [@1,2]
