Police in Kitui arrested two suspected traffickers in separate cannabis
interceptions reported by the Directorate of Criminal Investigations on
September 26, 2026. [@1][@2]

In the first incident, 24-year-old Dickens Tito arrived at Kitui Police
Station carrying a parcel of rice said to be a meal for a remandee; officers
on duty grew suspicious and inspected it before it could reach the inmate,
peeling back the rice to expose 69 rolls of bhang together with 33
matchsticks hidden inside. [@1][@2][@3] Tito was arrested at the station and
the items were secured as exhibits. [@1][@3]

In the second incident, a multi-agency security team stopped a Public Service
Vehicle travelling from Kitui towards Embu at a roadblock in Kanyonyoo along
the Thika–Garissa Highway and found a pink briefcase in the rear boot holding
13 bales of cannabis sativa tightly wrapped in yellow sealing tape. [@1][@3]
The briefcase was linked to 29-year-old Justice Kabila Njanja, who was
arrested at the scene and taken into custody. [@3]

Both suspects were kept in custody as investigators worked to establish where
the narcotics came from, their intended destinations and whether other people
were involved. [@3]
