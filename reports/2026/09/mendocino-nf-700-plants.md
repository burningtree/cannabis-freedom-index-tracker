On Tuesday 16 September 2026 law enforcement from the U.S. Forest Service and the Glenn County
Major Crimes Task Force, with helicopter Falcon 1, eradicated about 700 marijuana plants from
an illegal cultivation site in a remote part of Mendocino National Forest, California. [@1,2]

The Forest Service said the September 22 release described a single grow in isolated terrain
where trespass cultivation often damages watersheds and wildlife habitat. [@1] Action News Now
reported the same plant count and agencies from Willows, California. [@2]

Both sources advised visitors who stumble on grow sites to leave immediately and report to
local law enforcement. [@1,2] Neither reported any arrests. [@1,2]
