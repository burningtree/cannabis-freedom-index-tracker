On 11 September 2026 the Bayi district criminal-investigation detachment of
Nyingchi police, with a local police station and a village work team, confirmed
wild cannabis after a public tip about plants near Baji Village in the Tibet
Autonomous Region. [@1]

Officers uprooted more than 100 plants root and stem, registered them, and sent
the material for regulated destruction, the Linzhi public security bureau said.
[@1] They then searched nearby slopes, farmland and ditches and reported finding
no further plants. [@1] The notice warned that even wild cannabis may not be
picked or kept. [@1]

A later Linzhi municipal release on 30 September said three schoolchildren in
Baji Village spotted wild cannabis, reported it to police and the village work
team, and joined a community uprooting before being awarded “little anti-drug
guardian” certificates. [@2] That piece does not repeat the 11 September count
or say whether it describes the same patch. [@2]

No cultivator was identified and no arrests were announced. [@1,2]
