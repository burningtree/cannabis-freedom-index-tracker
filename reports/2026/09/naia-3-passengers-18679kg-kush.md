The Bureau of Customs-NAIA seized an estimated P18.679 million worth of suspected high-grade
marijuana, or kush, from the luggage of three Filipino passengers who arrived at NAIA
Terminal 3 from Bangkok on 24 September 2026. [@1,2]

The three inbound passengers carried six pieces of luggage that were put through physical
examination after X-ray images looked suspicious; the bags appeared to hold foodstuff, but
examination found about 18,679 grams of suspected kush. [@1,2]

Customs' initial report noted examination and verification were still ongoing at
announcement time. [@1] In the follow-up, the three suspects were transferred to PDEA
custody and face criminal charges for violating the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of
2002 in relation to the Customs Modernization and Tariff Act. [@2]
