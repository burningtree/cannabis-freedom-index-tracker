On Monday 10 September 2026 the Criminal Court sentenced Mohamed Shareef of Asrafeege, GDh. Vaadhoo,
to 25 years in prison and a fine of MVR 100,000 for trafficking cannabis. [@1] After counting time
already spent on remand, the court said he had 23 years and six months left to serve. [@1]

Police Intelligence and the Drug Enforcement Department had searched an Ensis Fisheries
accommodation and maintenance block in Hulhumalé on 14 June 2020 and arrested Shareef and
Afsal Rasheed of GA. Kolamaafushi. [@1,2] Edition.mv said officers found 6.5 kg of drugs hidden in
various places; an earlier Atoll Times report on the same case put the cannabis at 6.8 kg. [@1,2]

The court said fingerprints on a pipe where drugs were hidden and on a digital scale matched
Shareef's, and that video showed him buying bottles of the same brand as containers used at the
site, while finding no evidence linking him to the other drugs found there. [@1] Shareef had been
released from remand in 2021 and was sought by police in April 2026 after missing sentencing
hearings; Afsal's case had not been decided when Shareef was sentenced. [@1,2]
