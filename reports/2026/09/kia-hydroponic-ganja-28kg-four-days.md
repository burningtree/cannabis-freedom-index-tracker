Shubham Rathod, a resident of Uttar Pradesh, was caught at Kempegowda
International Airport on 15 September after arriving from Bangkok with 16 kg of
hydroponic ganja, valued at Rs 5.60 crore, concealed in his check-in baggage.
[@1] A day earlier, on 14 September, another passenger, Abdul Lateef, was caught
smuggling 11.96 kg of hydroponic ganja worth Rs 4.19 crore. [@1] The third case
was intercepted on 12 September, when two passengers arriving from Bangkok were
found carrying 990 grams of ganja concealed in duty-free bags. [@1] The
contraband was valued at Rs 34.65 lakh, a senior customs official said. [@1]

All four accused were arrested under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, 1985. [@1] Customs officials at Kempegowda International Airport
intercepted the three cases in the past four days, seizing 28.95 kg of the
contraband valued at more than Rs 10 crore. [@1] The 16-kg seizure was the
largest of the three cases. [@1]
