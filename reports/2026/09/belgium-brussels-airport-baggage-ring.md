After sports bags holding 30 kilogrammes of cocaine were left behind at Brussels Airport in
February 2025, investigators traced the passenger and uncovered the network behind the cocaine
trafficked into Belgium. [@1,2] Couriers flew in from Africa or the Americas with narcotics
luggage, while recruits hired over social media bought cheap last-minute tickets to enter the
baggage halls, collect the bags and hand them to network couriers at agreed spots. [@1,2]

During the investigation Belgian police arrested two suspects caught with 55 kilogrammes of
cocaine and 30 kilogrammes of cannabis, and traced the network's links to France and the
Netherlands, where members travelled between the three countries. [@1,2] A joint investigation
team at Eurojust coordinated the next steps, the financial trails and the suspect
identifications. [@1,2]

On 30 September 2026, a joint action day across Belgium, France and the Netherlands arrested 21
suspects and carried out 27 searches, seizing more than €57,000 in cash, crypto wallets, eight
vehicles and two firearms; analysis of the seized evidence continues. [@1,2]
