High Court judge Datuk Mohd Arief Emran Arifin sentenced S. Mohan, 38, and Ahmad Shamil Abdul
Rashid, 28, to 30 years in prison and 12 strokes of the cane for trafficking 139 kilograms of
cannabis four years ago. [@1,2] The terms run from their arrest on 31 May 2022. [@1,2]

The men were caught after police surveillance watched them move bags and plastic packets from a
Toyota Vellfire into a Proton Preve at a shopping centre car park in Bangsar at 2pm that day;
Ahmad Shamil tried to flee in the Vellfire but was caught nearby, and the cannabis, 139,019.3
grams, lay in the Preve's rear passenger area. [@1,2] They were charged under section 39B(1)(a)
of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, which allows the death penalty or life imprisonment, with at
least 12 strokes of the cane where death is not imposed. [@1,2]

Deputy Public Prosecutor Zaileen Nadia Zubir asked for 30 years and caning, pointing to the
quantity, while the defence sought the minimum, citing remorse and over four years already spent
in custody. [@1,2]
