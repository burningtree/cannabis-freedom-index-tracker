Royal Eswatini Police Service (REPS) officers arrested four people and seized
nearly 90 kg of dagga in three separate operations across the country on
Saturday. [@1] The suspects were charged with contravening the Pharmacy Act by
possessing dagga without a licence or permit. [@1] The suspects were expected to
appear in court soon. [@1]

At about 11am, Buhleni police arrested two men, aged 28 and 32, from
Mgungundlovu and Bulandzeni at Mzinene after finding 66.7 kg of dagga. [@1] The
men were reportedly travelling in a Honda Fit when police intercepted them. [@1]
In a separate operation, Lavumisa police arrested a suspect at about 1.30am at
Nsubane after finding 11.2 kg of dagga hidden in the boot of a white Nissan
sedan. [@1] Later at about 9.30am, officers arrested a 28-year-old man from
Sigwe, Tidulini, at They after finding 11.92 kg concealed in the boot of a
Toyota Quantum. [@1]

The three seizures brought the total amount of dagga recovered to 89.82 kg. [@1]
Police said dagga trafficking and dealing remain illegal in Eswatini and
reiterated their commitment to clamping down on both. [@1]
