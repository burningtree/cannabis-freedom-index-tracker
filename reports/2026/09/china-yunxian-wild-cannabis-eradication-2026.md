Yun County police in Lincang prefecture, Yunnan, uprooted 54 wild cannabis
plants in several recent township operations, according to a Lincang anti-drug
publicity piece republished on 16 September 2026. [@1]

Officers in Xiaojie used a drone and foot patrols to pull seven plants from
hills and abandoned plots. [@1] In Yongbao, two students who had attended
school anti-drug classes reported plants they recognised, leading police to
clear 19. [@1] Aihua patrols found three plants near suburbs and ditches, and
Maolan officers found 25 while visiting households and fields. [@1] Each batch
was uprooted and destroyed immediately, the account said. [@1]

The post gave no individual dates, sentences or cultivators. [@1]
