Police from the Parkes Rural Crime Prevention Team carried out a firearm and weapon-prohibition
compliance check at a property on Cooka Hills Road, Cookamidgera, about 17 km south of Parkes in the
Central West of New South Wales, around midday on Wednesday, 16 September 2026. [@1,2]

During the search officers allegedly found and seized four firearms — including a single-barrel
shotgun and a gel-blaster rifle — ammunition, a hydroponic setup and one cannabis plant. A
41-year-old man was arrested at Parkes Police Station and charged with possessing a shortened firearm
without authority, a firearm found at premises subject to a prohibition order, and possessing
ammunition without a licence or permit. He was remanded in custody to appear before Bail Division
Court 4 on Thursday, 17 September 2026. [@1,2]
