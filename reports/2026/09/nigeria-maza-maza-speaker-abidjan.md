NDLEA Directorate of Operations and General Investigation officers intercepted a
music speaker loaded with three packs of Colorado at a motor park in Maza Maza,
Lagos, heading for Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire, and from there to Liberia. [@1,2]

Tour agent Ifeanyi Obed Augustine, 41, and the consignment's owner Obasi
Ugochuckwu, 46, were nabbed in a quick sting operation at a neighbouring cafe.
