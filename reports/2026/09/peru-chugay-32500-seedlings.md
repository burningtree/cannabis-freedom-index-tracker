The National Police eradicated and burned 32,500 marijuana seedlings in Yacopampa, Chugay district,
Sánchez Carrión province, La Libertad, according to a police report cited by Macronorte on
23 September 2026. [@1]

Officers seized 63 sacks holding about 621.7 kg of marijuana and disabled two centres used to
collect and dry the drug. [@1] The report did not say whether anyone was arrested. [@1]

Police said they were still working to identify who was behind the cultivation and to establish
responsibility for drug trafficking. [@1]
