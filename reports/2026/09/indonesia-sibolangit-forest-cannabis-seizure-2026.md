Police found hundreds of harvested cannabis plants, known locally as ganja, on a
plot in forest at Dusun II Sarang Kulit, Desa Ketangkuhen, Sibolangit, Deli
Serdang, North Sumatra, on Tuesday 8 September 2026. [@1] The harvested plants
were still standing on the plot when two residents hunting in the forest saw
them. [@1] They reported the find to village head Jois Bangun. [@1] He passed
the information to the Pancurbatu police chief. [@1] A team from the Pancurbatu
police precinct led by precinct chief Kompol Junaidi, with criminal
investigation operations officer Ipda Elman and the intelligence unit, carried
out the operation. [@1] The officers walked more than an hour through steep
terrain and forest to reach the site. [@1]

Near the plot the officers found a bamboo hut with a tarpaulin roof. [@1] The
hut was empty and officers found no one there, Junaidi said. [@1] Inside, police
found dried cannabis leaves kept in buckets and sacks. [@1] They also found
cannabis seeds that police believed would be used for replanting. [@1] Junaidi
said police were still investigating who owned and managed the plot. [@1]
