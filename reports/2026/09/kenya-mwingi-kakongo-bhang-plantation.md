The suspected cultivators of a three-acre bhang (cannabis) plantation in Mwingi
Central fled before security officers could apprehend them. [@1] The plantation
lay in Kakongo Village, Kairungu Location, Kiomo Kyethani Ward, along the Tana
River. [@1] Police said the bhang had been planted among assorted food crops in
an apparent attempt to conceal it. [@1] Detectives from the National
Anti-Narcotics Investigations Bureau were pursuing leads to establish who owned
the plantation and to trace the wider network. [@2]

The plantation covered about three acres, or 1.2 hectares. [@1] Officers from
the National Police Service and National Government Administration Officers,
helped by members of the public, uprooted the crop and took it to Mwingi Police
Station. [@1] The crop weighed 2,000 kg there. [@2] The Directorate of Criminal
Investigations estimated its street value at Sh60 million. [@2]

Officers also recovered seven solar panels, six jembes (hand hoes), four pangas
(machetes), two weighing scales, two fork jembes, two hacksaws, a solar control
unit, assorted solar accessories, five petrol-powered water-pump generators and
30 kg of CAN fertiliser. [@1] Personal documents believed to belong to the
cultivators were found at the scene. [@2] The DCI said the solar equipment and
water pumps pointed to an organised operation equipped to sustain the
plantation. [@2]
