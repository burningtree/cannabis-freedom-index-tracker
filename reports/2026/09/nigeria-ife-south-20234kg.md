The Osun State command of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) destroyed 20,234 kg
of skunk on a cannabis farm in Area 4, Ife South Local Government Area, on 30 September 2026. [@1]
The farm covered 8.0936 hectares, the agency said in a statement published on 4 October. [@1,2]

NDLEA spokesperson Femi Babafemi said operatives destroyed the farm on 30 September. [@1] The
statement announced other operations across the country, but gave no count of the plants destroyed
at Ife South and no estimate of their value. [@1,2] It reported no arrests at the farm. [@1,2]

The same statement announced unrelated seizures and arrests in Plateau, Lagos, Kogi and Edo states,
including 385 kg of skunk seized in Jos North and a 47-year-old man arrested six years after he
failed to appear in a 198 kg skunk case. [@1,2] Those are separate events, not part of the Osun
destruction. [@1,2]

The agency did not say who cultivated the Osun farm or what happened to the land after the
burning. [@1,2]
