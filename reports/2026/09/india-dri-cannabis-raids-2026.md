Between late August and 9 September 2026 the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence seized about 740 kg of cannabis and charas in eight operations and arrested 13 people. The largest haul was 375.95 kg of ganja from two trucks near Raipur; officers also found 140.5 kg near Patna, 69 kg near Ludhiana and 5 kg of charas smuggled from Nepal. Three airport cases yielded 24.6 kg of high-potency cannabis from Hanoi and Bangkok. [@1]
