Maldives Customs Service, acting on intelligence, searched the luggage of two Indian
passengers arriving at Gan International Airport in Addu City on Tuesday and found
3,345 grams of cannabis concealed inside. [@1,2] Police identified the pair as
24-year-old Vivek Amban and 32-year-old Shahmil Pali Parambath, both Indian
nationals. [@1]

The Maldivian suspect, 36-year-old Abdulla Ibrahim of Fuvahmulah, was arrested
later during a controlled delivery operation run jointly by the South Police Serious
Crime Investigation unit, Counter Narcotics Operations and police intelligence. [@1]
The two couriers were taken into custody at the airport and handed to the Maldives
Police Service for investigation. [@2]
