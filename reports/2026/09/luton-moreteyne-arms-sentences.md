On 23 September 2026 Luton Crown Court sentenced three Luton men after they pleaded guilty to
conspiracy to produce cannabis over a large indoor grow. [@1,2] Shane Porter, of Yeovil Road, was
jailed for seven years and 10 months; Michael Murphy (spelled Micheal in the police release), of
Tower Road, for seven years; and John Miller, of Turners Road North, for four years and one
month. [@2] Bedfordshire Police summarised the terms as a combined 18 years. [@1]

The case began in October 2024 when officers stopped a car in Luton, found 18 bags of cannabis and
cash, and arrested Murphy and Porter. [@1,2] Miller was later stopped and searched; police then
linked the three men. [@1,2] Operation Costello warrants at properties in Luton and Millbrook found
13 cultivation rooms with more than 1,200 mature plants ready for harvest, valued by drug experts
at up to £1.1 million. [@1,2] BBC News identified the site as the former Moreteyne Arms opposite
Millbrook Station. [@2] Further warrants in Luton and Dunstable recovered more cannabis, about
£20,000 in cash and high-value designer goods. [@1,2]

BBC reported that Porter and Miller also pleaded guilty to conspiracy to supply cannabis; the police
statement said all three pleaded to both conspiracy offences. [@1,2]
