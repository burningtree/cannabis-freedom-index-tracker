Thang Quoc Bui, 49, a Vietnamese citizen, was sentenced in Melbourne County Court
on 11 September 2026 to 22 months in prison after pleading guilty to cultivating a
commercial quantity of cannabis. [@1,2]

Victoria Police had found 269 cannabis plants weighing 189.38 kg in six rooms and the
garage of a Marungi St house in central Shepparton on 26 November 2025, in a setup
the judge called sophisticated, with lighting, irrigation, vents and a power bypass.
[@1] Officers found Bui in a car in the driveway with a key to the house and live
CCTV alerts on his phone; the defence said he was a paid "crop sitter" watering
plants he did not own. [@1]

Judge Peter Rozen said Bui's role appeared relatively minor but general deterrence
required imprisonment; Bui must serve 12 months before parole, with 241 days of
pre-sentence detention credited. [@1] The court heard he would be deported to Vietnam
after any term served. [@1] Police later alleged he and his wife were stopped at an
airport on 5 February 2026 trying to leave Australia in breach of bail. [@2]
