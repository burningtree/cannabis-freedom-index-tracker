On Thursday 3 September 2026 a Padwa police team destroyed about 12,000 cannabis plants on nearly 10 acres (about 4 hectares) of hill forest near Disariguda and Lenjiiguda, under Padwa police station in Koraput district. [@1,2] Koraput superintendent Rohit Verma said the plants were about four to five feet tall and were uprooted on the spot in the presence of an executive magistrate. [@1]

Police arrested three men they said were planting and tending the crop: Jayaram Gemel, 30, of Disariguda, and Hasya Khara, 45, and Ghasi Gemel, 42, both of Lenjiiguda. [@1,2] They seized two plastic buckets, two spades and a partly used sack of urea. [@1,2] Sub-inspector Umesh Dharua led the raid. [@2] OMMCOM News said Padwa registered Case No. 88, dated 3 September 2026. [@2]
