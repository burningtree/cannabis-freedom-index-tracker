The Guardia Civil said on 26 September 2026 that its operation Garnata Silo had dismantled a
cannabis farm hidden in five sunken, interconnected sea containers at Iznalloz, Granada, and
arrested four men aged 31 to 51 on suspicion of drug trafficking and belonging to a criminal
group. [@1,2]

Agents carried out five court-authorised searches, at a property where the buried containers proved
almost invisible from the surface even on visual inspection; inside they found lighting,
air-conditioning, ventilation and air-filtering running a grow and drying space that held 997 plants,
some drying and some in pots, and with other plants found at a home and a second estate the operation
seized 1,357 cannabis plants in different growth phases and 600 grams of shredded cannabis. [@1,2]
The registrations also yielded a semi-automatic pistol, a revolver, an imitation shotgun and an
air-soft replica, 167 cartridges of various calibres, three large blades, a precision bow with
arrows, more than 26,600 euros in cash, a bulletproof vest, three tracking devices and three
vehicles, one suspect also facing a charge of illegal possession of weapons. [@1,2]

Endesa technicians cut illegal grid connections at three of the five properties and the Seprona
nature-protection service checked the estates for planning offences and the welfare of animals found
there; the four arrests were made over to Granada's investigating court of duty and the operation
stayed open. [@1,2]
