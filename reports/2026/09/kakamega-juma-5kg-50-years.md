A Kakamega court sentenced a woman to 50 years in prison after finding her
guilty of trafficking five kilogrammes of cannabis sativa, with the
alternative of a Sh50 million fine. [@1] [@2]

The sentence followed evidence that police recovered the drugs from Rachael
Akinyi Juma's rented house in the Murram area of Kakamega Central
Sub-County in a raid on June 12, 2019: 22 brooms held five kilogrammes of
bhang valued at about Sh16,600. [@1] [@2] She was charged under Section 4(a)
of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (Control) Act, 1994. [@1]

The prosecution team of Assistant DPP Lazarus Sirtuy and Prosecution Counsel
Stephanie Akinyi linked Juma to the narcotics beyond reasonable doubt, and
Hon. D.K. Wambua convicted and sentenced her, closing a case in court since
2019. [@2]
