The Trinidad and Tobago Coast Guard intercepted a pirogue bound for Trinidad
in the Gulf of Paria in the early hours of Monday, September 28, 2026. [@1]

The vessel carried three foreign nationals, later identified as Venezuelans,
with compressed plant-like material in 411 sealed packages, confirmed as
marijuana by the TTPS Special Investigations Unit: 231.42 kilograms with an
estimated street value of TT$25,456,200. [@1][@2]

The vessel, crew and narcotics were brought to Staubles Bay and handed to the
authorities for processing under established procedures, with investigations
continuing. [@1][@2]
