In a joint late-evening operation on Tuesday September 22, the National
Judicial Police Brigade intercepted a huge drug cargo bound for international
trafficking: 4,631 kg of cannabis resin (chira) were seized and one person
arrested on the outskirts of El Jadida. [@1] [@2]

The BNPJ acted on extremely precise intelligence from the DGST, in field
coordination with the Royal Gendarmerie, locating a utility vehicle in a
rural area under El Jadida carrying 118 bales of chira ready for
international dispatch. [@1] [@2] As the main suspect tried to flee during
the intervention, forces deployed surveillance drones for aerial combing
backed by canine brigades on the ground, quickly tracking and arresting the
fugitive. [@1]

The suspect was placed in custody pending the judicial inquiry under the
competent prosecutor's supervision; investigations continue to map the
network's national and international ramifications and arrest all
accomplices. [@1] [@2]
