Mohanpur sub-divisional police and the Border Security Force destroyed about 80,000 cannabis plants on Tuesday 15 September 2026 at Urabari, under the Noagaon village committee and Sidhai police station in West Tripura. [@1,2] About 50,000 plants were on two plots and about 30,000 on eight further plots. [@1,2]

Sub-divisional police officer Ashish Dasgupta led the team after a tip-off. [@1,2] He said the drive was part of work toward a drug-free Tripura and that similar operations would continue. [@1,2] Neither report names an arrest. [@1,2]
