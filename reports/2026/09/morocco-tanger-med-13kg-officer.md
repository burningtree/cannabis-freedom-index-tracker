Two people, a utility-vehicle driver and a police officer posted at Tanger Med,
were held after a port check found 13 kilograms of chira as the driver prepared
to leave for Europe. [@1] The Tangier regional judicial police opened an inquiry
under the competent public prosecutor's office into suspected international drug
trafficking involving the two men. [@1] A security source told MCG24 that the
judicial inquiry was opened on Saturday 19 September 2026. [@2]

According to the investigation, the case broke on 17 September 2026, when a
check at Tanger Med seized 13 kilograms of chira, Moroccan cannabis resin, from
the driver of a utility vehicle who had already cleared border controls and was
preparing to sail for Europe. [@1] MCG24, citing a security source, reported the
same 17 September seizure of 13 kilograms of chira from a utility-vehicle driver
about to leave Moroccan territory by ferry for Europe. [@2] CCTV showed that,
after passing the security inspection and reaching the ferry quay, the driver
received the consignment from a port security employee of the rank of mokkadem,
a Moroccan police warrant-officer grade, who was then arrested on suspicion of
involvement. [@1] MCG24 reported that the person who handed over the drugs was a
security employee of mokkadem rank working at Tanger Med, and that he was
arrested in the case. [@2]

Both suspects were placed in garde à vue, police custody, for the
prosecutor-supervised inquiry, which was meant to establish the circumstances
and any national or international extensions. [@1] Inquiries were continuing in
order to arrest any further suspects and to seize proceeds of the suspected
criminal activity. [@1] Alongside the judicial procedure, the Direction Générale
de la Sûreté Nationale, Morocco's national police directorate, ordered the
suspected officer temporarily suspended from duty until the case ended and
disciplinary responsibility could be decided. [@1]
