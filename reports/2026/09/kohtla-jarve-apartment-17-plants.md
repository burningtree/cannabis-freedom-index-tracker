Police in Estonia's Ida Prefecture arrested a 48-year-old man in Kohtla-Järve during the
week before their 18 September announcement, suspecting him of illegal cannabis cultivation
and repeat large-scale handling of narcotic or psychotropic substances. [@1] [@2]

Officers had followed up a tip, linked the activity to a man previously convicted of drug
offences, and searched his flat after securing a warrant. [@1] [@2] Inside they found a tent
holding 17 cannabis plants with lighting, ventilation and a humidifier, plus fertiliser,
mushroom spores and a cannabis–tobacco mix. [@1] [@2]

Prosecutor Sofja Hristoforova said the same man had been arrested in August 2025 for a
similar offence and given three years and six months in prison, of which he served four
months before the rest was suspended on four years' probation. [@1] [@2] Because he was
still on probation, prosecutors asked the court to remand him in custody for at least two
months while the new case is investigated. [@1] [@2] The Viru district prosecutor's office
is leading the file with Ida Prefecture detectives. [@1]
