Security forces in Giza Governorate arrested a 40-year-old unemployed man on
9 September 2026 within the jurisdiction of Al-Manira Al-Gharbeya police station,
according to Egyptian news reports. [@1,2] Officers seized 325 bundles of hashish,
a sum of money and a sharp weapon during the operation led by Captain Taha Salah,
head of investigations at the station. [@2]

Confronted with the seizure, the man admitted possessing the hashish to sell for
profit and said the cash came from drug sales, Al-Wafd reported. [@2] He said he
kept the weapon for self-defence. [@2] Legal steps were taken and the public
prosecution opened an investigation; Jarayid reported a four-day detention order
pending inquiries. [@1,2]

Jarayid’s English brief gave the same bundle count but did not repeat the officer’s
name or the confession details spelled out in Arabic. [@1]
