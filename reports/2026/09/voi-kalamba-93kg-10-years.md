A Voi court sentenced a man to 10 years in prison after finding him guilty of
trafficking 9.3 kg of cannabis sativa valued at Sh279,000 in Taita Taveta
County. [@1] [@2]

Daudi Kalamba was sentenced by Principal Magistrate Hosea Mwangi with the
option of a Sh1 million fine, convicted under Section 4(a)(ii) of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (Control) Act. [@1] [@2] The case
arose on June 15, 2026, after Kamtonga Police Station officers received
information that Kalamba was allegedly cultivating suspect plants; the
prosecution led by Counsel Nelson Ogeto proved the case beyond reasonable
doubt. [@2]
