Between 8 and 12 September 2026 the Federal Police ran another phase of Operation Carcará on
the Alto Turiaçu indigenous land in western Maranhão and eradicated more than 300,000 cannabis
plants. [@1,2]

The force said the goal was to curb illegal cultivation in the area. [@1,2] Federal agents
worked with the Maranhão military firefighters. [@1,2] g1 reported that investigations
continued to identify those responsible for the plots. [@2]

Neither the Federal Police note nor g1 reported arrests or a hectare figure. [@1,2]

The Federal Police published a photograph from the operation on its website. [@1]
