Regional anti-narcotics agents from Concepción ran Operation Aquidabán II on Tuesday
29 September 2026 on the Estancia Sanguina Cue property in rural Yby Yaú, with support
from the departmental police command. [@1,2]

Concepción al Día said work ran from about 07:00 to 17:00 under prosecutor Arnaldo
Argüello of the specialised anti-drug unit. [@1] Officers found four plots of about
three hectares each, all in harvest stage, totalling about 12 hectares of marijuana. [@1,2]
They also found about 2,000 kilograms of dry branches and two precarious camps with
living gear. [@1] Samples were taken, then plants and other evidence were destroyed by
controlled burning. [@1]

The official estimate put production from the 12 hectares at about 36,000 kilograms,
with an economic loss of about G. 5.4 billion (roughly USD 93,750 at local prices). [@1]
ABC Color gave the same hectarage and 36,000 kg figure without the branch weight or
schedule. [@2] Both accounts said the action was part of the SUMAR anti-drug plan. [@1,2]

Concepción al Día published a photograph of officers cutting plants during the raid. [@1]
