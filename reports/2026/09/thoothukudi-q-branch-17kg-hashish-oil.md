Four men were arrested in the early hours of Monday 28 September 2026 when
Thoothukudi Q branch police intercepted their car and found packets of hashish
oil inside. [@1] Police identified them as J. Rexton, 53, C. Rosario, 40 and S.
Antony Libitas, 36, all of Thoothukudi, and M. Sermadurai, 46, of Siluvaipatti.
[@1] The men were allegedly taking the contraband by car for smuggling to the
Maldives. [@1]

Police said they acted on a tip-off and intensified surveillance across the
district before intercepting the vehicle. [@1] Officers seized 17 kg of hashish
oil. [@1] PTI put the value at about Rs 17 crore (170 million rupees) on the
international market. [@2] PTI reported that the suspects had planned to launch
a boat to take it across the sea to the Maldives. [@3]

The Q branch handed the men and the seized oil to the Narcotics Intelligence
Bureau for further investigation. [@1] DT Next reported that a case had been
registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@3] Police
were questioning the men to trace the source of the oil and the syndicate
members waiting for the consignment in the Maldives, the report said. [@3]
Hashish oil is a concentrated extract of the cannabis plant, high in THC, the
compound responsible for its intoxicating effects. [@3]
