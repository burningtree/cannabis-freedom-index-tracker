The Trinidad and Tobago Police Service destroyed more than 8.3 tonnes of
illegal narcotics at the Caroni Cremation Site on Tuesday, September 29,
2026, with an estimated street value of $1.6 billion. [@1][@2]

The destroyed stockpile, seized in operations by the Southern, Eastern and
North Eastern Divisions and the Special Investigations Unit, some dating back
to 2005, comprised 3.08 tonnes of cocaine worth about $1.431 billion, 5.22
tonnes of marijuana worth about $156 million, and 4.87 kilograms of ecstasy
and heroin worth about $13 million. [@1]

Commissioner of Police Allister Guevarro called it a landmark day for
accountability and transparency, while Prime Minister Kamla Persad-Bissessar
said the haul equalled roughly one percent of annual GDP seized from the
local drug mafia. [@1][@2]
