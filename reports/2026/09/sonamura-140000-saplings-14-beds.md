Sepahijala district police carried out a crackdown on illicit ganja (cannabis)
cultivation at several places in the district on Sunday 13 September 2026. [@1]
Fourteen nursery beds holding about 140,000 saplings were found and destroyed on
the spot after legal formalities. [@1] The beds were at Mainamura, Anandapur,
Ghatighar and Baga Lunga, under Sonamura police station. [@1] The drive ran from
8:20 am to 11:30 am. [@1] It was led by Inspector Tapas Das, the officer in
charge of Sonamura police station. [@1]

Police said they acted on secret information. [@1] The operation was carried out
jointly with the BSF, TSR battalions, Forest Department officials and other
agencies. [@1] The units named were the 81st Battalion of the BSF and the 11th
Battalion of the TSR, with other sister agencies. [@1] Police put the
destination market value of the seized ganja at about 2.8 million rupees (28
lakh). [@1]

Kailashahar News, a Bengali-language outlet, reported the same operation, with
14 nursery beds destroyed. [@2] It put the number of saplings destroyed at about
140,000, with a market value of about 28 lakh rupees. [@2] Das told reporters
that operations against illegal contraband would continue the next day. [@1]
