The Bureau of Customs and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency intercepted
fifteen boxes declared as personal effects from Canada at a warehouse in Pasay
City after X-ray screening flagged suspicious images. [@1,2,3] During a full
examination, officers found six boxes with 28 cans holding about 28,324 grams of
suspected methamphetamine and, in another box, about 4,746 grams of suspected
high-grade marijuana, which authorities called kush, valued at about ₱7.119
million on the standard drug price. [@1,2,3]

They also found twenty towels and twenty duvets suspected of carrying liquid
methamphetamine. [@1,2,3] PDEA laboratory tests on samples from the marijuana
and the other substances returned positive for cannabis and methamphetamine
hydrochloride respectively. [@1,2,3] Customs commissioner Ariel Nepomuceno said
the case showed intelligence-led profiling and coordination at the country’s
main entry points. [@1,2]
