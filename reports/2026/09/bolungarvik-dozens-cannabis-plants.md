On Wednesday 23 September 2026, Westfjords police searched a home in Bolungarvík after a suspicion that cannabis was being grown there. [@1,2] They seized dozens of fully grown plants and the equipment that went with the grow. [@1,2]

The occupant was arrested and questioned. [@1,2] Police said the quantity suggested the cannabis was meant for distribution and sale. [@1,2] After the scene was examined and the man had been questioned, he was released, and police treated the case as solved. [@1,2] They said it was the third cannabis grow found in the Westfjords district that year. [@1,2]
