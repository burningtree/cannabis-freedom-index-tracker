On 9 September 2026, inland surveillance customs officers at the Le Boulou toll on the A9, on the
Spain-to-France carriageway, checked a French-plated van marked for wheelchair passenger transport. [@1,2]

The driver, alone in the vehicle, said he did not know what he was carrying. [@1,2] Officers found
large sports bags at the rear containing 263 kilograms of cannabis pollen; the Perpignan customs
directorate valued it at more than two million euros on the illicit market. [@1,2]

The man was held by customs, then handed to the Perpignan OFAST anti-narcotics office on prosecutors'
instructions. [@1,2]

On 14 September 2026 the Perpignan judicial court tried him in immediate appearance and sentenced
him to three years in prison and a €2,630,000 customs fine. [@1,2] Customs said the case showed
traffickers misusing disability-access vehicles to move drugs. [@1]
