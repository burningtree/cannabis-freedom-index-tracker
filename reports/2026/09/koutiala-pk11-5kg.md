On Sunday 6 September 2026, at about 10:30, officers at the PK 11 customs post in Koutiala searched a local company's bus travelling from Yorosso to Bamako. [@1] A passenger was keeping a backpack close beside him. [@1] In the customs yard, with the passengers present, officers found 10 balls of cannabis weighing 5.60 kg in all, and 400 blister packs of tramadol tablets, Tapentadol 250 mg, which is 4,000 tablets. [@1,2]

Fasso Actu said the Directorate General of Customs reported that the drugs and the tablets were seized and that the passenger, described as a pupil, was arrested for whatever followed. [@2] Mali 24 gives the same weight, the same count of tablets and the same arrest. [@1]

The next day customs seized 1,530 kg of Turkish spaghetti at a Bamako bus station. [@1,2] That was a separate stop. [@1]
