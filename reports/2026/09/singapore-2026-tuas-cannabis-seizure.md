ICA officers stopped a Malaysia-registered motorcycle at Tuas Checkpoint on 15 September 2026 and found about 10,164 g of cannabis in bundles under the seat and in a haversack on the handlebar.[@1] A 36-year-old Malaysian man and a 35-year-old Malaysian woman were arrested.[@1] The drugs were worth more than S$203,000 and could feed about 1,450 abusers for a week, and the amount was twenty times the 500 g capital threshold.[@1] [@1]
