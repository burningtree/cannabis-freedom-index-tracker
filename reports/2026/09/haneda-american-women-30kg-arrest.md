Tokyo police arrested two American women for allegedly smuggling about 30 kg of cannabis
worth around ¥150 million into the country through Haneda Airport. [@1,2]

The two 25-year-olds, identified as Cartron Jamere Bourbon Castillo and Jocelyn Dizon
Natividad Patrin, arrived from New York on 5 September with two suitcases containing what
police described as cannabis-like dried plants; customs officers found the drugs on
inspection. [@1,2] A Tokyo police spokesperson said the women allegedly worked with an
unidentified third party. [@1]

Both suspects accepted the job after being promised covered flights and lodging plus a
monetary payout; Bourbon told police she took it for the payment and the chance to travel
to Japan for free, while the other suspect denies knowing the suitcase contained
cannabis. [@1] Police arrested both on suspicion of violating Japan's drugs and
psychotropics control law, including smuggling narcotics for profit, which can bring up to
10 years' imprisonment. [@1,2]
