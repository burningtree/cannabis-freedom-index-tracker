Police searched 15 properties across the region in one week, seizing about 70 kilograms of dried
cannabis, 1,586 cannabis plants, $86,000 in cash and six vehicles. [@1] Seven people aged 33 to 55
were arrested on Monday and two more, aged 34 and 44, on Tuesday; all nine were charged with
participating in a criminal group and various drug cultivation, trafficking and other offences.
[@1] Five of those arrested were unlawful non-citizens. [@1]

The operation brought together ACT Policing with Australian Federal Police and Australian Border
Force personnel, and information from the investigation led NSW police to a grow house in
Jerrabomberra. [@1] Detective Acting Inspector Mark Battye, of the drugs and organised crime team,
said the raids had hit an organised crime group behind large-scale commercial cannabis
cultivation. [@1] "This is the largest operation on grow houses we've ever conducted," he said.
[@1]

Police said most of the homes used as grow houses were privately rented through online platforms
without property managers. [@1] They urged landlords to check identity documents and inspect rental
properties regularly, pointing to warning signs such as tenants avoiding inspections, paying months
of rent in advance or offering above the asking price. [@1]
