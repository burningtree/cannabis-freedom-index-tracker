Seles Stephen Rokasia pleaded guilty to unlawful possession of cannabis and
accepted the prosecution summary when he was sentenced at Luganville on 18
September 2026. [@1]

Police searched his house in the Chapuis area of Luganville on 6 March 2026 under
a warrant and found cannabis that tested positive, weighing 45.34 g in total. [@1]
He said he intended to keep it for personal use. [@1] He had spent about a month
in custody from 7 March to 7 April 2026 before bail. [@1]

Justice Dudley Aru treated him as a first offender from Makam village on North
Ambrym, employed as a builder. [@1] Following submissions modelled on earlier
Vanuatu cannabis cases, the court sentenced him to twelve months’ supervision with
a condition that he attend a drug awareness and rehabilitation programme, plus 100
hours of community work. [@1] The 45.34 g of cannabis was ordered forfeited and
destroyed within seven days. [@1]
