On Wednesday 30 September 2026, Ecuadorian soldiers seized about 1.3 tonnes of marijuana from a pickup abandoned after a chase in Santa Martha de Cuba, in Carchi province, Defence Minister Gian Carlo Loffredo said. [@1] The people in the pickup skipped a military checkpoint. [@1] Soldiers followed them. [@1] Kilometres later the occupants left the vehicle and escaped. [@1] The army's own post, quoted by Primicias, said the soldiers had seized 1,300 kg in Carchi. [@1]

When the army searched the pickup it found 24 bundles holding about 960 packages of suspected marijuana, Loffredo wrote. [@1] He valued the load at 6.5 million US dollars and called the seizure a blow to the logistics and illicit economies of trafficking groups on the northern border. [@1]

El Universo, citing the same post, reported that the occupants were not arrested. [@2] It said the operation was carried out by the security bloc through the armed forces, using information from the National Intelligence Centre. [@2]
