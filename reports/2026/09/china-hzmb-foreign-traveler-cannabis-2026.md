A foreign male traveler was stopped at the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Zhuhai
port on 13 September 2026 after customs officers saw him act nervously in the
green channel arrival hall, The Macau Post Daily reported on 14 October 2026,
quoting Gongbei Customs. [@1]

Officers found a sealed bag in his right trouser pocket containing a black
resinous mixture with leaf fragments weighing 2.8 g gross. [@1] A field reagent
test reacted positive for cannabis, and laboratory analysis confirmed
tetrahydrocannabinol with a net weight of 2.51 g. [@1] Gongbei Customs
transferred the case to its anti-smuggling department. [@1]

The statement did not identify the traveler’s nationality. [@1] Customs
reiterated that smuggling drugs is a criminal offence regardless of quantity.
