Kerala excise officials destroyed 1,566 fully grown cannabis plants in six plantations about four kilometres inside forest near the Kadugumanna settlement in Attappady, Palakkad. [@1] They collected samples, cut the plants and burned them. [@1] The Hindu said the team set out on Saturday morning and that the plants were ready for harvest. [@1]

Mathrubhumi reported the same count of 1,566 plants as a joint excise and forest operation, Operation Thandari, on Achchanvettumala in Padavayal village, four kilometres north of the Kadugumanna settlement and inside Mukkali forest station's second block. [@2] It said assistant excise inspector M. B. Rajesh of the Agali range led the party, which walked in through a steep ravine. [@2]

Excise said the growers had not been identified and that the inquiry would look at cultivation in the Attappady forest and along the Tamil Nadu border. [@1,2]
