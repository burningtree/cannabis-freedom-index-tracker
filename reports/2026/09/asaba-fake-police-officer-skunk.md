Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) arrested 49-year-old
Innocent Ekpe, a suspected fake police officer, on the Benin-Onitsha expressway
in Asaba, Delta State, on Wednesday 16 September 2026. [@1] Officers found
292.48 kg of skunk concealed in his silver Toyota Sienna bus. [@2] His alleged
accomplice, 39-year-old Nduka Joseph, was arrested with him. [@3]

The NDLEA said Ekpe had long posed as Godwin Emeka, a Deputy Superintendent of
Police, using a forged identity card to pass checkpoints while transporting
illicit drugs. [@2] The agency announced the arrests in a statement by its
spokesman, Femi Babafemi. [@2]

The Asaba arrest was one of a string of operations the agency reported across
Lagos, Edo, Niger, Delta, Ondo, Gombe, Imo and the Federal Capital Territory
(FCT). [@1] In the same statement, the agency reported seizing about 6,000 kg of
cannabis and more than 1.3 million pills and capsules of tramadol and other
opioids, with a combined street value above N3.4 billion. [@3]
