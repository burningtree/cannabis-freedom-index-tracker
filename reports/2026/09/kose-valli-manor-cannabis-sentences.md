Police arrested 38-year-old Meelis Pähnat and 34-year-old Serafim Gerzuni in March 2026 at a
cannabis grow they had run in rented rooms of a former distillery at Valli manor in Kose Parish,
Harju County. [@1,2,3] Officers seized about 30 kilograms of cannabis and buds plus grow lights,
ventilation, irrigation and packaging equipment. [@1,2,3]

Prosecutors said the operation was commercial: the quantity could intoxicate at least 44,500
people, and both men admitted guilt in a plea procedure. [@1,2,3] The court also ordered each to
pay €10,000 in lieu of confiscating further assets. [@1,2,3]

Harju County Court on 2 September 2026 sentenced Pähnat to four and a half years in prison and
Gerzuni to five years, of which he must serve nine months immediately with the remainder
suspended on probation if he completes supervision and a social programme. [@1,2,3] The judgment
had not yet become final. [@1,2,3]
