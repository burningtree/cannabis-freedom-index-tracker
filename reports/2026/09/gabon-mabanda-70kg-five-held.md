The Mabanda territorial gendarmerie brigade arrested five Gabonese nationals on 11
September 2026 in possession of nearly 70 kg of cannabis and 1,373 tramadol
tablets, according to judicial sources. [@1,2] The suspects, described as gold
miners based in Mouila, were carrying seven bags of cannabis plus the tablets,
with an estimated market value of 10.052 million CFA francs. [@1,2]

The five were named as Donnevy Massandé, Wensley Mbena-Mbena, Wilfried London
Marat, Omer Brice Chevignon Mandoh and Ndho Mateba. [@2] They were presented to
the Tchibanga prosecutor's office and face charges of drug trafficking and
laundering its proceeds under Articles 597 to 603 of the Penal Code. [@1]
Prosecutor Guychard Ndong Mebalé said the case falls under the exclusive
jurisdiction of the Libreville court of first instance, to which the suspects were
to be transferred. [@1,2]

Investigators believe the drugs may have come from the neighbouring Republic of
Congo; that assessment remains part of the ongoing investigation. [@1,2]
