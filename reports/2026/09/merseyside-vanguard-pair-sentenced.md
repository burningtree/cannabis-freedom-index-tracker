Two men have been sentenced to a combined seven years in prison after
running a sophisticated cannabis and cocaine distribution network via
social media. [@1] [@2]

Jake Lowe, 28, was spotted naked perched on a fence near his Wigan home
after fleeing through a window during the 23 July Operation Vanguard
raids; Joseph Knockton, 30, was arrested at his Rainhill address. [@1]
[@2] Project Medusa officers found the pair selling over nine kilograms
of cannabis in 26 days through WhatsApp and Instagram in branded
packaging, turning over about £150,000 a month; raids recovered over 4 kg
of cannabis worth £66,000, cocaine, £30,000 cash, a Rolex, a knuckle
duster and a Taser. [@1] [@2] Lowe pleaded guilty to cannabis-supply,
cannabis-possession, cocaine-possession and criminal-property counts;
Knockton to cannabis-supply and criminal-property counts. [@2] At
Liverpool Crown Court, Lowe received four years and Knockton three years.
