After an eight-hour trek, a team led by the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA)
uprooted 129 fully grown marijuana plants around 11:08 on Thursday 3 September 2026 at
Sitio Guincamote, Barangay Captain Claudio, Toledo City, Cebu. [@1,2]

SunStar Super Balita said the plants were worth about 25,800 pesos and that PDEA-7
identified the cultivator as alias Per, 30, who was not there when officers arrived. [@1]
Daily Tribune put the same value on the plants and said regional director Joel B.
Plaza reported that alias Gene had fled; representative samples went for laboratory
testing and the rest were burned on site. [@2]

The force included Toledo City police, Naval Forces Central, the provincial intelligence
unit and the 2nd provincial mobile force company. [@1] SunStar said police were still
looking for alias Per to face cultivation charges under the Comprehensive Dangerous
Drugs Act. [@1]

The published photographs show PDEA officers with the uprooted plants in forest at
Guincamote on the operation day. [@1,2]
