Police in Jumgal district, Naryn Region, have completed a campaign to destroy
plants containing narcotic substances. [@1] The Naryn regional police department
said the operations found and destroyed about 2 tonnes of wild-growing cannabis.
[@1]

Its Kyrgyz-language report called the plant kara kurai, the Kyrgyz name for wild
hemp. [@2]

The police department said the destruction was aimed at fighting the illegal
drug trade and at ensuring the safety of citizens and maintaining public order.
[@1] It said similar operations are carried out regularly and stressed the
importance of preventive measures. [@1]

Kabar said the work was carried out on the territory of Jumgal district. [@2]
The police account named no suspects and reported no arrests, and it gave no
precise site within the district or area covered. [@1]
