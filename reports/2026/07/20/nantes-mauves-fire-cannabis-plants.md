On Monday 20 July 2026 a major fire broke out near the Prairie de Mauves at chemin du
Moulin-des-Marais in eastern Nantes, destroying about ten hectares of vegetation and part of
nearby informal settlements. [@1,2] About 150 firefighters evacuated residents; police who
helped clear the camp discovered a cannabis plantation of roughly 150 to 200 plants, 20 Minutes
reported, citing a police source. [@1]

Officers left the plants in place while evacuation continued, Ouest-France said. [@2] Once the
fire was under control on Tuesday 21 July 2026, police returned and seized the plants that had
not burned. [@1,2] A judicial investigation was opened. [@1]

The fire mobilised about 150 firefighters and led to the preventive evacuation of roughly 200
people, according to 20 Minutes; no casualties were reported. [@1]
