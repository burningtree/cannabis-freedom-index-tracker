In an operation by the State Security Service with internal affairs officers, a
man born in 1988 was found growing 423 cannabis plants for sale on land he owned
in Yangiqorgon district of Namangan region. [@1] The plants were seized as the
case was announced together with a Khorezm hashish interception totalling nearly
a kilogram of hashish and the 423 plants. [@1]
