Rota Tari pleaded guilty at Port Vila on 19 May 2026 to unlawful possession of
cannabis contrary to section 2(62) of the Dangerous Drugs Act and was sentenced
on 3 July 2026. [@1]

Police executed a search warrant on 25 September 2021 at the Blacksands home on
Efate where she lived with her aunt. [@1] They found cannabis in her room; a
presumptive test was positive and the net weight was 119 g. [@1] She admitted
the drugs in a recorded interview on 26 September 2021. [@1]

Justice Josaia Naigulevu set a twelve-month starting point, discounted it for
her first conviction and guilty plea, and further reduced the term because of
the years-long delay and personal circumstances. [@1] The end sentence is three
months' imprisonment, suspended in full for six months. [@1] The cannabis is to
be destroyed. [@1]
