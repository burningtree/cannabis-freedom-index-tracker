In the early hours of Tuesday 7 July 2026, Central Narcotics Bureau officers
intercepted a car on Kim Keat Road and arrested a 25-year-old foreign national
for suspected drug trafficking. [@1,2] A search of the rear passenger floorboard
found about 3,129 grams of cannabis. [@1,2]

Officers took him to a condominium near Geylang East, where a search of his unit
recovered about 200 grams more cannabis, 121 grams of methamphetamine, 55 grams
of Ecstasy, and S$1,259.90 in cash. [@1,2] The bureau put the total seizure at
about 3,329 grams of cannabis and estimated the drugs at more than S$81,000,
enough to supply about 546 abusers for a week. [@1,2] Trafficking more than 500
grams of cannabis can carry a mandatory death sentence under Singapore law. [@1,2]
