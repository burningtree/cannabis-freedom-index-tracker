During operation Mak-2026, drug-control officers of the Khabarovsk Krai
Interior Ministry, together with Bikin district police and the regional FSB
border directorate, found and cleared a large patch of wild cannabis, the
regional police said. [@1] The plants grew in a forest belt covering about one
hectare next to a village in Bikin municipal district. [@1]

Officers destroyed more than 4,700 cannabis plants weighing about 320
kilograms in total. [@1] They pulled stems with the roots, treated the ground
with herbicides and burned the material on the spot. [@2] Vestidv reported that
similar eradication work was continuing in other parts of the krai. [@2]
