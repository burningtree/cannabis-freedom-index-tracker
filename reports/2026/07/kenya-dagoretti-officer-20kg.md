Officers from Muthangari Police Station arrested a fellow officer after raiding
his residence in Dagoretti sub-county and recovering two bags containing six
bales of cannabis sativa wrapped in yellow tape, weighing about 20 kilograms.
[@1,2] The raid followed a tip-off from members of the public about the officer's
alleged illegal activities. [@1,2]

The officer was taken into custody pending processing and arraignment, with the
drugs secured as exhibits. [@1,2] The National Police Service said it enforces
the law without fear or favour and that no individual receives preferential
treatment because of position or status. [@1,2]
