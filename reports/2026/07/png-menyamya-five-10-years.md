Five men caught with 56.5 kilograms of marijuana at Menyake village in Menyamya,
Morobe Province, were jailed for ten years each by a district court in Lae on
Wednesday 30 July 2026. [@1] Four were from Tari in Hela Province and were
travelling toward Port Moresby on the Gulf Highway when they stopped for the
night at a local house; the fifth was from Morobe. [@1]

Police raided the house at about 4 a.m. on 21 July after a tip-off and found
nine bags of marijuana. [@1] Two of the men tried to attack officers with bush
knives and were subdued; the account says they were taken to Angau General
Hospital in Lae while five others were arrested. [@1] Magistrate Nasiling
Bingtau convicted all five of possessing a dangerous drug and warned that the
Controlled Substance Act allows fines up to K1 million and terms up to forty
years. [@1] Four will serve their sentences at Hawa Correctional Institute in
Tari and the Morobe man at Menyamya district cell because Buimo prison in Lae
had no space. [@1]
