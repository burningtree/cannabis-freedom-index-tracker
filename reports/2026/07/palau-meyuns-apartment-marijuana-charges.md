Officers from Palau's Division of Transnational Crime and Division of Juvenile Justice searched Jeff Apartment in Meyuns and a silver Toyota Vanguard registered to Loliza Lalii Takawo at about 5:50 a.m. on 29 July 2026. [@1]

They seized two glass jars of suspected marijuana weighing 11.52 g combined from a living-room rack, along with suspected methamphetamine, paraphernalia and USD 25,551.89 in cash from the apartment and vehicle. [@1] Courtney Olidang Saburo was seen allegedly trying to flush evidence in the bathroom during the search. [@1]

Takawo and Saburo were charged with trafficking and possession of controlled substances and Saburo with an additional meth possession count. [@1] Investigators cited the drug quantities, cash, packaging materials and Takawo's statements as grounds to believe meth was held for distribution. [@1] Both women had been held at the Division of Corrections since 11:30 a.m. on 29 July. [@1] Palau law provides 15 to 35 years for possession of a controlled substance and 25 to 50 years for trafficking. [@1]
