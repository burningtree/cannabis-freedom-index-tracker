More than $17 million in illicit cannabis, concentrates and processing
equipment was seized during a large enforcement operation against illegal
cannabis businesses in Greece, New York — the largest seizure in the history
of the State Office of Cannabis Management. [@1]

The months-long investigation by the Greece Police Department with the DEA,
New York State Police and OCM ended July 28, 2026, with raids at two homes
and two warehouses, confiscating hundreds of pounds of flower and thousands
of pounds of products including edibles and concentrates slated for
distribution across Central New York; some gummies were labelled 300 mg THC
each against a 10 mg legal limit. [@1][@2]

Joseph Grant, 31, of Webster, and Jason Grant, 29, of Rochester, were each
charged with four felony counts of first-degree criminal possession of
cannabis. [@2] Officers also recovered a ghost gun, more than $219,000 in
cash and six vehicles. [@2]
