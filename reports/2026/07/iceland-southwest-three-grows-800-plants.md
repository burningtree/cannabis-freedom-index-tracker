Capital-area police said on 6 July 2026 that they had stopped three cannabis grows at different places in southwest Iceland. [@1,2] Eight searches were carried out, with help from police in Suðurnes and South Iceland. [@1,2]

Officers seized just under 800 cannabis plants, some other drugs, money and growing equipment. [@1,2] Police judged the grows fairly large and said that in every case the cannabis was meant for sale and distribution. [@1,2] Eight people were arrested and questioned. [@1,2] The investigation was continuing, and police said they would give no further details for the time being. [@1,2]
