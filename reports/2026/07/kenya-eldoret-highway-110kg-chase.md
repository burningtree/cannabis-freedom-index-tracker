DCI Anti-Narcotics Unit detectives, acting on intelligence, flagged down a blue
Nissan Note (KDN 836H) at the KFA roundabout on the Eldoret-Nakuru highway, but
the driver accelerated, made a dangerous U-turn and raced toward the Eveready
area on a punctured front rim. [@1,2]

The two occupants abandoned the car and fled on foot before detectives caught
them; Capital FM names them as Emmanuel Wesonga and Stephen Daria. [@1,2] A
search of the vehicle found five sacks holding 110 kilograms of cannabis sativa
worth about KSh 3.3 million. [@1,2] The suspects were held pending arraignment
with the drugs secured as exhibits; the DCI disclosed neither origin nor
destination. [@1,2]
