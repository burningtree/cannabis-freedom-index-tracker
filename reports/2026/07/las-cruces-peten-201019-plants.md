The Guatemalan army, the public prosecutor's office and SGAIA, the
anti-narcotics analysis unit of the national civil police, eradicated and
burned cannabis in the municipality of Las Cruces, Petén. [@1,2] The defence
ministry said the work was done through CICNAG, the inter-institutional
centre against drug activity. [@1]

The army said officers found 12 fields of cannabis ready for harvest,
covering 13,712.6 square metres, about 1.37 hectares, and incinerated 201,019
plants. [@1] It valued them at 100,509,000 quetzales, about 13.1 million
dollars. [@1] The interior ministry also reported 201,019 plants eradicated
in Las Cruces and said they were worth more than 100 million quetzales on
the illegal market. [@2] It said prosecutors and the army took part with
SGAIA. [@2]

The army said the plants were burned under the procedures set by the competent authorities, and that it would keep running joint operations with the civilian security institutions. [@1] The announcements name no one arrested. [@1,2]
