Special agents of Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (SENAD), with prosecutor Celso Morales,
searched a rural property in the Cerro Corá district of Amambay, about 40 km from Pedro Juan
Caballero. [@1,2] They eradicated seven hectares of cannabis in germination, growth and harvest
stages, destroyed 300 kg of drying material and 50 kg of seeds, and burned three production camps.
[@1,2]

The agency also seized a 150 cc motorcycle it said was used for logistics on the site. [@1,2] SENAD
estimated that more than 21 tonnes of cannabis were kept from reaching illicit markets, mainly in
Brazil, and put the economic loss to traffickers above USD 3 million. [@1,2]

No one was detained; Morales said investigators would try to identify those responsible. [@2] ABC
Color reported that four drying racks were destroyed along with the camps. [@2]
