Detectives seized 271.3 kg of cannabis from a car on the Chavakali–Kapsabet road in Kaimosi, Nandi
Central sub-county, in operations the Directorate of Criminal Investigations reported on 24 July
2026. [@1,2] Acting on a tip-off, officers ambushed a sky-blue Mazda CX-5 and found seven sacks of
cannabis, which the DCI valued at about Sh13.4 million. [@1,2] The driver and a passenger abandoned
the vehicle and escaped, and police said they were pursuing them. [@1,2]

In a separate operation at Ngariga Junction on the Nairobi–Nakuru highway in Kiambu county,
Anti-Narcotics Unit and Transnational Organised Crime Unit detectives stopped a public service
vehicle and arrested 32-year-old Francis Oteba after finding five bales of cannabis in a black bag.
[@1,2]

In Kisii county, detectives arrested Nancy Moraa, Phylis Matundura Mogaka and Andrew Makokha
Cheng'oli and recovered 10 kg of cannabis and Sh63,335 in cash suspected to be proceeds of
trafficking. [@1,2] Four people were arrested across the three counties in total. [@1,2] The DCI said
the operations were part of efforts to dismantle trafficking networks and that investigations were
continuing. [@1,2]
