Gendarmes stopped a cannabis transfer on the seafront at Bras-Panon, Réunion, on
Sunday 26 July 2026. [@2] Witnesses alerted officers after seeing two men moving
suitcases towards a boat on the shore. [@1] The transfer was taking place near
the Rivière du Mât on the Bras-Panon seafront. [@2] The gendarmes searched the
area after the alert. [@1] Officers found 10 sacks that had been carefully
hidden. [@1] They held 156 kg of cannabis herb in total. [@2] Officers arrested
the two suspects and placed them in custody. [@2] The gendarmerie said the cargo
was meant to leave by sea. [@1]

After custody, investigators established that the cannabis was to be transferred
to Mauritius. [@2] Both men were brought before the public prosecutor. [@1] A
judicial investigation was opened to trace the network and identify its
branches. [@1] The gendarmerie said it had deployed a large operation involving
multiple units. [@1] It thanked the witnesses, whose alert it called decisive.
[@2] It said the case showed how a simple report can take large quantities of
drugs off the market. [@1]
