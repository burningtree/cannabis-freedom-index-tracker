Polish border guards seized 1,194 kg of cannabis hidden in shipments of laterite bricks sent from
Thailand through the German port of Hamburg to Poland, officials said on 2 July 2026. [@1,2] The
Border Guard estimated the haul's street value at PLN 48 million, about €11.2 million. [@1]

Officers from the Border Guard's maritime unit in Gdańsk found 815 kg in a first container inspected
in Hamburg and another 379 kg in a second container destined for the same recipient in Poland. [@1]
The cannabis was concealed inside specially modified bricks. [@1]

Four men, including the owner of the company that was to receive the shipment, were detained in
Szczecin on 6 May during an attempted unloading; four more were detained near Poznań on 15 June. [@1]
Six were placed in pre-trial detention for three months, one was released after questioning and one
was placed under police supervision. [@1]

Prosecutors charged the suspects with importing and smuggling large quantities of narcotics in the
European Union; three also face charges of participating in an organised criminal group, offences
that carry up to 20 years in prison. [@1] The investigation, supervised by the National Public
Prosecutor's Office's organised crime and corruption unit in Gdańsk, involved German customs in
Hanover, a joint anti-drug task force in Oldenburg, Europol and Eurojust. [@1] Interior Minister
Marcin Kierwiński described the operation as the Border Guard's largest anti-drug seizure in recent
years. [@1]
