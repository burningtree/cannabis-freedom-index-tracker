A tip reached the Federal Criminal Police Office on 12 July 2026 that a Bulgarian articulated
truck would carry a large amount of cannabis herb from Spain through France and Italy or Slovenia
into Austria. [@1] The next day, 13 July, officers saw the truck enter from Slovenia at the
Spielfeld crossing and followed it to the planned unloading point in Traiskirchen, in the Baden
district. [@1]

They arrested the 58-year-old Bulgarian driver and the man who was to take the drugs, a
30-year-old Slovenian living in Vienna. [@1] Both partly admitted the facts. [@1] On the orders of
the Graz prosecutor they were taken to St. Pölten prison. [@1]

Of the 200 kilograms of cannabis herb seized, 100 kilograms were for the 30-year-old, who was to
receive them for an international group. [@1] The other 100 kilograms, still in the trailer, were
to go on to Greece after the handover in Traiskirchen. [@1] Another 6 kilograms that looked like
cocaine tested as a dummy with no active ingredient. [@1] Police put the street value of the
cannabis above 2 million euros. [@1] NÖN reported the same weights, arrests and route from the
police press conference in St. Pölten on 22 October. [@2]
