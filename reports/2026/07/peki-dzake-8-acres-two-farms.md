On Thursday 16 July 2026 about 60 police officers from Ho entered the Peki Dzake forest in
South Dayi district and destroyed two marijuana plantations covering eight acres in total.
[@1] Graphic Online said the raid followed a tip-off described by Volta Regional Police
Commander DCOP Wisdom Akorli. [@1]

Officers armed with machetes cut the plants and burned the piles with lorry tyres. [@1]
Occupants fled deeper into the forest before police reached the plots; Graphic reported three
men and a woman running from one farmstead. [@1] Police seized a hunting gun from a man spotted
at a distance in the forest. [@1]

Akoli said a mop-up would soon target remaining plants and seedlings in the Peki-Dzake enclave
and that the campaign would extend across the Volta Region. [@1] He appealed for confidential
tips on cultivators. [@1]

Graphic published a photograph of officers destroying plants during the operation. [@1]
Modern Ghana had reported a separate 9 July 2026 operation that destroyed eleven farms in
the same Peki-Dzake enclave. [@2]
