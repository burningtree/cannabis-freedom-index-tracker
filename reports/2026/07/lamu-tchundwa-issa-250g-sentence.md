A Lamu court ordered a man to pay a Sh50 million fine or serve 10 years in
prison after convicting him of trafficking narcotic drugs. [@1] [@2]

Resident Magistrate Flavian Mulama found Abdulrahman Issa guilty of
trafficking cannabis sativa and diazepam by storing the drugs, committed
with others not before the court on March 12, 2026, at Tchundwa Village in
Lamu East Sub-county. [@1] [@2] Patrol officers found Issa carrying an orange
bag holding 250 g of cannabis, 10 diazepam tablets, a partially smoked bhang
roll, a razor blade, a metallic spoon, a transparent side plate, a broken
tile and a matchbox. [@1] [@2]

Issa denied the charge, claiming the items came from his father's house, but
called no witnesses; the magistrate credited three consistent prosecution
witnesses led by Counsel Idris Kipruto Tomno and ruled the paraphernalia
showed trafficking rather than personal consumption. [@1]
