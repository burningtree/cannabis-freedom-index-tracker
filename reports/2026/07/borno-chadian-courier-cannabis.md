Troops of Sector 3, Joint Task Force North East under Operation Hadin Kai, intercepted a
suspected foreign drug courier and recovered illicit drugs, alcohol and other prohibited
substances allegedly heading to terrorists in the Lake Chad Islands. [@1,2]

The suspect, 48-year-old Rachael Samuel, a Chadian national from Kelo in Chari-Baguirmi
Province, was arrested on Thursday 9 July during intensive search operations along the
Maiduguri–Monguno axis while travelling toward Kwatan Doron Baga in Kukawa Local
Government Area of Borno State. [@1,2] Acting Military Information Officer Captain
Mohammed Goni disclosed the arrest in a statement on the Army's official channel. [@1,2]

Recovered from her were seven compressed bundles of suspected cannabis sativa weighing
about 30 kilogrammes, two 10-litre containers of hydromercuric chloride ("Suck and Die"),
a carton of 48 bottles of dry gin and 61 bottles of herbal alcoholic drinks, plus a phone,
two ATM cards and cash, valued in total at millions of naira. [@1,2] Preliminary findings
indicate the consignment was bound for the Lake Chad Islands, where terrorist groups use
drugs and intoxicants to stimulate fighters and suppress fear. [@1,2] She remains in
military custody with exhibits secured pending transfer to the appropriate agency. [@1,2]
