On 16 July 2026 at about 23:30 Western Indonesian Time, BNN officers at Terminal 3 Soekarno-Hatta
checked the suitcase of a Chinese passenger, ZG alias A, after a public tip about cannabis on a
Bangkok–Hong Kong flight transiting Jakarta. [@1,2] Customs found 21 leaf packages; a Narcotest and
BNN laboratory analysis confirmed 14,616.5 g of cannabis. [@1,2]

Investigators questioned the suspect and five witnesses from customs and aviation security, and
notified the Chinese embassy. [@1,2] He was charged under Article 114 paragraph (2) and/or Article 111
paragraph (2) of Law 35 of 2009 on narcotics. [@1,2] BNN later destroyed the drugs on 17 September
2026 as part of the criminal case. [@1,2]
