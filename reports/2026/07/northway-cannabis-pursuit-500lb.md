Torrey R. Jackson, 39, and Tori L. Rushlow, 29, both of Massena, were arrested
on the afternoon of Wednesday 16 July 2026 after a pursuit on Interstate 87
that began in Queensbury and crossed Saratoga County. [@1,2,3] Troopers had
tried to stop Jackson for a traffic violation around 3:24 p.m.; he did not pull
over and drove through fields and residential yards before re-entering the
highway the wrong way, striking a marked patrol car and allegedly trying to ram
other police vehicles. [@1,2,3]

Rushlow threw garbage bags of vacuum-sealed cannabis onto the roadway during the
chase, and officers found more cannabis in the vehicle when it stopped in the
median around 4:15 p.m. [@1,2,3] The total seized, including bags thrown from
the car, was more than 500 pounds. [@1,2,3] Both tried to run and were arrested
while resisting; they were arraigned in Wilton Town Court and remanded to
Saratoga County jail without bail on charges including first-degree criminal
possession of cannabis and attempted aggravated assault on an officer. [@1,3]
