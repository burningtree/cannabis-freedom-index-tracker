On Friday 17 July 2026 the Brussels criminal court convicted thirteen members of an Albanian
criminal organisation of drug offences linked to at least five cannabis plantations found in
2025, La DH and Bruxelles Today reported. [@1,2] The thirteen received prison sentences of
between three and eight years; a fourteenth defendant was sentenced to 100 hours of
community work. [@1,2]

The case began in January 2025 when police called to two armed men trying to enter a garage
in Jette found more than a thousand cannabis plants; one suspect said he had come from Albania
to tend the crop. [@1,2] Phone analysis, wiretaps and surveillance then exposed four further
plantations in Auderghem, Schaerbeek, Marchienne-au-Pont and Ans, rented under false identities. [@1,2]

Press reports said the group paid Albanian workers about 5,000 to 8,000 euros for roughly two
months of cultivation, stored harvests in several Brussels municipalities and earned about
seven million euros, partly from shipments to the United Kingdom. [@1,2] Investigators alleged
the suspected leader continued to organise plantations by phone from detention and even moved
cannabis into Merksplas removal centre while awaiting expulsion. [@1,2] The prosecutor described
Brussels as a hub for Albanian organised crime. [@1,2]

Several defendants had prior drug convictions in Belgium or abroad, the reports said. [@1,2]
