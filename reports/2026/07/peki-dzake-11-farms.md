On 9 July 2026 the Volta Regional Police Command, working with the national Drug Law
Enforcement Unit (DLEU), raided the Peki-Dzake enclave in South Dayi district after
intelligence on cannabis grown at remote sites. [@1]

About 60 officers from the DLEU, Police Intelligence Directorate, SWAT, regional defence
force, highway patrol and criminal investigations took part under Chief Superintendent Mick
Omari Boakye and DLEU director DSP Richard Dotsey Gablah. [@1] They found and destroyed
11 large farms; mature plants were cut and burned in a controlled burn so fire would not
spread to nearby bush. [@1]

Volta police public affairs officer Chief Inspector Francis Gomado said no one was arrested
because people tending the farms spotted the team on the difficult approach and escaped
before officers reached them. [@1] DSP Gablah told the Ghana News Agency the command had
destroyed about eight acres (roughly 3.2 hectares) of suspected Indian hemp at Peki Dzake
and would keep up intelligence-led work against cultivation and trafficking. [@2]

Modern Ghana published a photograph of officers at one of the cleared farms with its
report. [@1]
