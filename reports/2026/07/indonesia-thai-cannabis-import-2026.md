A joint BNN, customs and East Java police operation from 29 June to 1 July 2026 found 3.37 tonnes of cannabis buds from Thailand in 500 suitcases and 80 cartons at a warehouse in Gresik. Twelve people were arrested, including a Chinese national; BNN said the buds were intended to be turned into THC vape liquid. [@1]
