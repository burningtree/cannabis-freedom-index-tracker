Taho Bong pleaded guilty at Port Vila on 19 May 2026 to unlawful possession of
cannabis and was sentenced on 9 July 2026. [@1]

Police patrolling Fresh Wota 2 after reports of disorderly drinking on 25 December
2021 searched him and seized 79.89 g of cannabis that tested positive. [@1] He told
investigators he would only speak in court. [@1]

Justice Josaia Naigulevu set a 16-month starting point, reduced it for his guilty
plea, first conviction, remorse and the long delay since 2021, and imposed five
months in prison suspended for twelve months. [@1] Bong, aged 34 with four children,
must also complete fifty hours of community work and six months of supervision. [@1]
The cannabis is to be destroyed. [@1]
