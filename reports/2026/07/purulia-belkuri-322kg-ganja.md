Police stopped a truck on National Highway 18 near the Belkuri toll plaza, under
the Purulia Mufassal police station in West Bengal's Purulia district, late on
the night of 27 July 2026. [@2] Officers found 322 kg of cannabis in the
vehicle. [@3] Two escort vehicles travelling ahead of and behind the truck were
stopped as well. [@3]

Seven men were arrested. [@1] They were named as Jibon Dey, Raju Pal and Utpal
Kumar Sinha from West Bengal, Dipak Kumar Singh, Ajeet Kumar Singh and Ranjit
Kumar Prasad from Bihar, and Kalia Lanka from Odisha. [@1] A mechanic opened the
hidden compartment in front of an executive magistrate and independent
witnesses. [@4]

Police seized the three vehicles used in the run and 10 mobile phones. [@4]
Police said the consignment had come from Ganjam district in Odisha and was
being taken through the Purulia-Ranchi corridor to Dhanbad in Jharkhand. [@1]
Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari said the Kolkata Police Special Task Force, with
the Purulia district police, had made the interception. [@2] He commended the
"swift, coordinated and resolute action" of the agencies. [@2] Police said the
seven were being questioned to trace the rest of the network. [@1]
