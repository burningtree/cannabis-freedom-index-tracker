Sarajevo canton drug police ran operation “Šibica” in Ilidža on 24 July 2026 under the cantonal
prosecutor’s supervision. [@1,2] With a Sarajevo municipal court warrant they searched a house, a
flat and two cars used by J.B. from Sarajevo. [@1,2]

Officers said they found three active improvised indoor laboratories for growing and processing
marijuana, with more than 280 plants and over 3.3 kilogrammes of dried plant material that looked
like marijuana, plus lab equipment, cash in several currencies and two mobile phones. [@1,2] J.B.,
born in 1987 according to the ministry, was jailed on suspicion of unauthorised production and
trafficking in narcotics. [@1,2] Police said he would be sent to the cantonal prosecutor after
processing. [@1,2]
