A court in Macará, Loja province, sentenced Eduardo V. to 22 years in prison for large-scale drug
trafficking after police found 1,299.65 kg of marijuana hidden in a truck, the prosecutor's office
said on 20 July 2026. [@1,2]

The truck was stopped on the Panamericana in Macará on 9 November 2025. [@1] Officers found 2,688
blocks of marijuana inside 40 metal beams, and the driver was arrested. [@1,2] Prosecutors said the
load was bound for Peru and then Chile. [@1]

The court also heard evidence from the driver's phone, including photographs of him holding the
blocks. [@1] Large-scale trafficking carries 22 to 26 years in prison under Ecuador's criminal
code. [@1,2]
