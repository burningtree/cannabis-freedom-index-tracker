On the evening of Thursday 14 May 2026, motorcycle units of the Royal Gendarmerie company in Sidi
Ifni, under the Guelmim regional command, stopped a truck coming from northern Morocco at a
checkpoint on the Tiznit–Dakhla highway in the commune of Lakhsas. [@1,2]

They seized 190 packages of cannabis resin (chira) weighing about 6.5 tonnes, an inflatable boat and
two jet engines that officials said appeared intended for maritime trafficking. [@1,2] The driver, a
30-year-old man, was placed in custody under the public prosecutor's office while investigators
sought other members of the network and any national or international links. [@1,2]
