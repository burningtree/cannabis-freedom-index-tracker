On 31 May 2026 the National Anti-Drug Police Directorate (DNPA), with the Directorate of
Police Investigations and community-security police, raided a mountainside site at El Chelón
No. 2 aldea in Guaimaca municipality, Francisco Morazán department. [@1,2]

La Tribuna reported that officers detained a 29-year-old man from the area and found about
1,500 seedlings roughly 30 centimetres tall and about 1,000 plants between 1.5 and 2 metres
high on roughly half a manzana where maize and beans had been grown before. [@1] The paper said
40 plastic bags totalling about 80 pounds of marijuana were buried in a blue barrel. [@1]

Tiempo gave the same plant counts and said more than 2,500 suspected marijuana plants were
secured together with the 80 pounds in bags, and that the drugs would be destroyed by the
authorities. [@2] Neither outlet named the suspect. [@1,2]

Both reports said DNPA, DPI and DNPSC took part after surveillance work, and Tiempo added
that a pistol, ammunition and a phone were seized for investigation. [@1,2]
