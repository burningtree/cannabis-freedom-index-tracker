Railway Protection Force (RPF) officers from Hijli post and the force's crime
intelligence branch in Kharagpur stopped a man carrying ganja at Hijli railway
station on 14 May 2026. [@1] Officers identified the man as Anil Singh, aged
about 33, a resident of Fatehpur district in Uttar Pradesh. [@1] The Nandankanan
Express had arrived at platform 1 at about 17:30, and they picked him out near
the general coach with a blue travel bag. [@1] A search of the bag found four
packets wrapped in brown cello tape, and the RPF said the ganja weighed about 4
kg 100 g and had a market value of about Rs 2,05,000. [@1] Singh told officers
he had collected the ganja near Bhubaneswar railway station and was taking it to
Fatehpur for illegal sale, and that he had no authority or documents for
carrying it. [@1]

The search was carried out before a gazetted officer, Jitendra Kumar Singh,
after the detained man chose that option under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, India's drug law. [@1] He was arrested at
about 00:45 on 15 May under the NDPS Act. [@1] A personal search found two
mobile phones and SIM cards on him, and his family was told of the arrest. [@1]
He and the seized articles were then handed to the Government Railway Police
(GRP) in Kharagpur. [@1] The GRP registered case No. 58/2026 on 15 May under
Section 20(b)(ii)(B) of the NDPS Act. [@1]
