The Specialized Criminal Court in Sana'a sentenced four men to 25 years for possessing and distributing hashish with intent to traffic and for preparing a place for its use. Three others got five years for possessing hashish for use, five got one year for preparing drug-use premises, and one was acquitted. The same court opened a new case against 25 defendants accused of trading hashish, methamphetamine and Captagon and of reprocessing hashish. [@1]
