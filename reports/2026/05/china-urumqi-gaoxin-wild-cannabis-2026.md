On 21 May 2026 a community police officer on patrol in Urumqi’s high-tech
district noticed unusual plants beside the road at the south gate of the Dacheng
 neighbourhood complex on Xincheng Lane, China Daily reported. [@1]

The officer alerted superiors and anti-drug specialists, who confirmed wild
cannabis. [@1] Officers from the Gaoxin street sub-station and a nearby police
station then uprooted 349 plants root and stem, China Daily said. [@1] A 26 May
roundup in Legal Daily described the same discovery date and location and said
teams cleared cannabis seedlings during a wider search before destroying the
material, without giving a new total. [@2]

Both pieces said officers warned residents to report suspicious plants. [@1,2]
No arrests were announced. [@1,2]
