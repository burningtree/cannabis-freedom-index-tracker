Personnel of the Mile 38 Police Checkpoint, under Superintendent Foday Sorie
Fofanah, intercepted Mohamed Sheriff, a driver residing in Kissy, Freetown, in
control of vehicle AXO 085 on Saturday 9 May 2026. [@1]

A thorough search found a concealed compartment beneath the rear seat holding
eleven parcels of dry leaves suspected to be cannabis sativa. [@1] The suspect
and the recovered substances are in police custody while investigations
continue. [@1]
