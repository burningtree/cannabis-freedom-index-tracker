On 10 May 2026 Libreville prosecutors burned three truckloads of seized drugs,
including cannabis, in the presence of Justice Minister Augustin Emane.[@1]

The ministry valued the combined haul at nearly five billion CFA francs and said
the aim was to keep seized drugs from leaking back into sale.[@1] The code
requires confiscation and destruction of seized drugs — this was that order
carried out in public.[@2]
