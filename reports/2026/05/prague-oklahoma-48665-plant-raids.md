On Wednesday 6 May 2026, Attorney General Gentner Drummond's Organized Crime Task Force raided
two unlicensed grow sites in Prague, Benson Kush and Grand Growth. [@1,2] Agents detained 36
suspects and seized 48,665 marijuana plants plus 1,803 pounds (about 818 kg) of processed
marijuana. [@1,2]

The attorney general's office said the processed cannabis alone had an estimated street value of
more than $3 million. [@1,2] Three people identified as illegal immigrants were taken into
Immigration and Customs Enforcement custody and deported. [@1,2]

The task force worked with the FBI, Homeland Security Investigations, Customs and Border
Protection, ICE, Oklahoma City police, the Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, the agriculture
department, Pottawatomie County agencies and the Oklahoma National Guard civil support team. [@1,2]
Drummond said fewer than 1,200 licensed grows remained in the state compared with more than 9,000
when he took office. [@1,2]
