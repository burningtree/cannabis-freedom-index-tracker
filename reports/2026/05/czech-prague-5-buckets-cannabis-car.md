Prague police detained a driver after finding an estimated five buckets of
cannabis in his car in Xaveriova Street in Prague 5 on Friday afternoon, 15 May
2026. [@1] Officers from the Prague police's standby service stopped the vehicle
in the course of their duty. [@1] Criminal proceedings were opened in the case.
[@1]

Police spokesman Jan Rybanský said officers found several buckets of dried plant
material and established that some of it was marijuana. [@1] He said there were
more containers of dried material and that it was not yet clear what proportion
of it was the drug. [@1] The buckets were to be sent for expert examination to
establish exactly how much marijuana they contained. [@1]

The man was secured at the scene and taken to a police station for questioning
so that he could explain the situation. [@1] The case was still being
investigated on suspicion of the crime of unauthorised production and other
handling of cannabis. [@1]
