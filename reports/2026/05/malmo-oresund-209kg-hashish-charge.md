Swedish Customs have charged a 37-year-old man with aggravated drug smuggling after officers found 209.9 kg of cannabis resin in a truck at the Öresund Bridge in Malmö. [@1] The agency announced the charge on 22 May 2026 and said the trial at Malmö District Court would start on 1 June. [@1] If convicted, Customs said, he risks five to six years in prison and expulsion. [@1]

On 12 February an inspector checked a Slovenian-registered truck and trailer. [@1] The driver answered questions in a way the inspector found evasive, and the seal number on the trailer did not match the freight papers. [@1] A closer search found 209.9 kg of cannabis resin among the legal goods. [@1] Customs estimate the street value at about 25 million kronor. [@1]

Ingrid Lamberth, of Customs' criminal department in the south, said investigators used the driver's phone to obtain surveillance film from a place in the Netherlands. [@1] The film, she said, showed him stopping and taking boxes from another person. [@1] The man, a citizen of Bosnia and Herzegovina, told investigators he met people he did not know because his contractor told him to, took the boxes, and mixed them with the legal cargo. [@1] He said he did it because he needed money. [@1] Trailer published the same account on 27 May. [@2]
