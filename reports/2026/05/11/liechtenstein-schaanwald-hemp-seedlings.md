At about 10:45 on Monday 11 May 2026 a man crossed from Austria into Liechtenstein at
the Schaanwald border post. [@1,2] During a check, officers of the Office for Customs
and Border Security (BAZG) found hemp seedlings on him. [@1,2]

The Liechtenstein national police seized the plants and reported the man for an offence
under the Narcotics Act. [@1,2] Liechtenstein’s police bulletin and local press published
the case on 12 and 13 May 2026. [@1,2]
