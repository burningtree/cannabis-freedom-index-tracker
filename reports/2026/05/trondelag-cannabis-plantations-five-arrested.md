On Wednesday 20 May 2026, police in Trøndelag arrested five Lithuanian citizens
in a coordinated action against eight addresses in the county. [@1] The five are
men in their twenties to forties. [@1] They are charged with a serious narcotics
offence for producing and/or distributing drugs. [@1] One person was arrested in
Namdalen, two in Gauldalen and two in the Trondheim area. [@1]

Officers found three cannabis plantations in operation and a home that had
probably been used for cannabis production. [@1] The plantations were in
Namdalen and Gauldalen. [@1] With help from Vest police district, officers also
searched an address south of Bergen and found what appeared to be a production
premises. [@1] Police seized production equipment and cannabis plants, and made
a larger seizure of packaged cannabis. [@1]

Police lawyer John R. Sødahl Furunes, the prosecutor in charge, said the action
followed a systematic investigation and that the production and distribution
appeared organised across several locations. [@1] Several units of the police
district took part in an extensive investigation that began in the autumn of
2025. [@1] Kripos and Oslo police district also assisted, and Vest police
district in particular gave substantial help. [@1] The five men were to be
brought before Trøndelag District Court in Trondheim for remand hearings on
Thursday 21 May and Friday 22 May. [@1]

At the Thursday hearing, the first of the men told the court he was arrested at
the place where he worked looking after plants. [@2] He admitted guilt and said
he deeply regretted it. [@2] Prosecutors asked that he be held on remand for
four weeks. [@2] A man in his thirties also admitted growing plants but said he
had acted alone. [@2] Police told the court they believed that production, and
the accused, were part of a larger organised network. [@2] They also sought four
weeks' remand for him, with bans on letters and visits. [@2] NRK reported that
the court was expected to decide that afternoon whether the two men could be
held. [@2] The other three were to be brought before the court for remand on
Friday. [@2]
