Federal agents closed three unlicensed cannabis shops in Massena on 20 May 2026
during Operation Weed Out along New York's northern border. [@1,2]

The Drug Enforcement Administration said Uncle Crandy's, Vape Bank/All the Smoke
Massena (formerly 1 Willow Cannabis Co.) and Famous A's had sold marijuana and
THC products without state licences and offered items banned under New York law,
including candy-bar-style THC, high-potency gummies and cartoon-labelled packages.
[@1,2] Civil forfeiture complaints in the Northern District of New York allege
Uncle Crandy's and Famous A's also sold marijuana to an underage undercover
officer. [@1,2]

Special Agent in Charge Farhana Islam said the operation seized more than
1,000 pounds (about 454 kg) of marijuana and THC-related products, 40 pounds of
suspected methamphetamine and about 20 illegal firearms, including an AK-47,
five AR-15-style rifles and weapons with extended magazines. [@1,2] WWNY reported
that agents also searched homes and a warehouse in Hogansburg the same week and
that a Plattsburgh news conference on 21 May displayed seized goods in trucks.
[@2]

The DEA, FBI, HSI, IRS Criminal Investigation, Massena police, St. Lawrence
County Sheriff's Office and Plattsburgh police are investigating with the U.S.
Marshals Service. [@1] Massena Police Chief Cody Wilson and local sheriffs said
Massena intelligence had fed the federal case. [@1,2]
