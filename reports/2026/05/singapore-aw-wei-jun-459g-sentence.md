Alger Aw Wei Jun, a 25-year-old Singaporean repeat drug offender, was sentenced on 26 May 2026 to
16 years and 10 months' jail and 12 strokes of the cane for possessing cannabis for the purpose
of trafficking, after also admitting to methamphetamine possession and consumption. [@1,2]

On the night of 5 August 2024 his friend Hidayatullah Taruna Jaya Taruna asked him via WhatsApp to
collect a "book"—cannabis—and deliver it for cash. [@1,2] Aw took the bag from a staircase landing
in an Ang Mo Kio public housing block on the morning of 6 August and waited for further
instructions. [@1,2] At about 2.20 p.m. that day Central Narcotics Bureau officers raided the flat
where he was and arrested him with a block of at least 459.2 g of cannabis, plus methamphetamine
whose use his urine confirmed. [@1,2]

Aw had been convicted in March 2021 of meth consumption and trafficking, so the mandatory minimum
for this cannabis charge was 10 years' jail and 10 strokes rather than the lower first-offender
threshold. [@1,2] The prosecution had sought 16 years 10 months to 19 years and 12 to 13 strokes;
the judge imposed the bottom of that range. [@1,2] Aw had been remanded since August 2024 because
no one could stand bail for him. [@1,2]
