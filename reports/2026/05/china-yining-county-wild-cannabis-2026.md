Yining County's anti-drug office and the county public security bureau's
anti-drug brigade organised a “ban cultivation, eradicate drugs” (禁种铲毒)
special operation aimed at zero cultivation and zero yield, the county media
centre reported on 18 May 2026. [@1] Teams searched remote fields, wooded hills,
abandoned courtyards and tree belts under a standard of missing no corner. [@1]
Wild cannabis and other drug-source plants they found were uprooted on the spot
and destroyed, the report said. [@1]

A township anti-drug officer told the county media centre that, following
superior orders, staff had swept wild-cannabis-prone areas such as abandoned
yards and field margins and had uprooted more than 10,000 wild cannabis plants
so far. [@1] A village police officer said the work was meant to stop wild or
exploited drug plants from becoming a supply route. [@1] The article names no
arrests or prosecutions. [@1]
