Delta State NDLEA Commander Halilu Hamidi told reporters in Ogwashi-Uku on 15 September 2026
that operatives had destroyed 11 hectares of cannabis farmland at Obiaruku in Ukwani local
government area on 10 May 2026. [@1,2]

Hamidi grouped the raid with two other clearances—10.2 hectares at Emu-Obiogo in August 2025
and 15 hectares at Orogun on 12 May 2026—for a total of 36.2 hectares destroyed while he had
led the command. [@1,2] Stone Reporters News said the farm destructions were part of efforts
to keep Delta from becoming a cultivation and trafficking centre. [@2]

Neither briefing account names arrests or crop weights for the Obiaruku farm itself. [@1,2]
