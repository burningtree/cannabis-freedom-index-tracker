On Sunday 24 May 2026, French gendarmes intercepted a vehicle as it left the ferry at the port
of Sète and found 2,692 kilograms of narcotics, mostly cannabis resin. [@1,2] Officers from the
Lille research section, backed by the GIGN and gendarmerie units from Nord, Hérault and Nîmes,
had tracked the vehicle before it could deliver its load to the Lille area. [@1,2]

The driver was arrested on the spot, and a second raid near Paris netted the network's suspected
logistics coordinator, at whose home officers found €34,000 in cash and another vehicle. [@1,2]
The case began on 13 April, when the Lille prosecutor opened a preliminary inquiry into a ring
moving Moroccan cannabis by sea and land; Moroccan intelligence helped map the route and
anticipate the shipment. [@1,2]

On 28 May the Lille prosecutor opened a formal judicial investigation for organised importation
and criminal conspiracy, and both suspects were charged, one being remanded in custody. [@1,2]
