On 27 May 2026 the Subdirección General de Análisis de Información Antinarcótica (SGAIA) of
Guatemala's National Civil Police, working with army troops and public prosecutors, eradicated
cannabis plantations in mountain areas around the hamlet of Ixmucané, in Las Cruces, Petén. [@1,2]

The Interior Ministry said 252,888 plants in the harvesting stage were destroyed, valued at about
126,444,000 quetzales (about 16.2 million US dollars at the ministry's conversion). [@1] La Hora,
citing the same anti-narcotics report, said the plants were spread across five clandestine fields.
[@2] No arrests are named. [@1,2]
