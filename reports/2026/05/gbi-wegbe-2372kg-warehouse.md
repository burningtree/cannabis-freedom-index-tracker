In the early hours of 31 May 2026, officers of Ghana's Narcotics Control Commission (NACOC) searched
a farmhouse and warehouse at Gbi-Wegbe, near Hohoe in the Volta Region, after intelligence that the
site was storing illegal drugs. [@1,2]

They seized 2,372.38 kilograms of a substance suspected to be cannabis. [@1,2] NACOC put its
estimated street value at GH¢1,186,190. [@1,2]

The commission said no arrests were made during the raid and that inquiries were under way to find
those behind the operation. [@2] The seizure was part of wider enforcement against production,
trafficking and distribution of narcotics. [@1,2]
