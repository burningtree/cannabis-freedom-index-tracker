Late on Sunday night, Dalkhola police received word that two women were moving
suspiciously below the underpass of the National Highway 12 bypass toward the
college, and officers reached the scene. [@1] A search of the bags the women
carried produced 11 kilograms of ganja. [@1]

The arrested women were named as Nitu Ray and Artika Kapur, residents of the
Matigara area of Siliguri. [@1] Inspector-in-charge Sankha Chatterjee said a
case was registered under BNS sections; produced before the Raiganj special
court on Monday, both were remanded to 14 days' judicial custody. [@1] Police
began questioning them on who was sending consignments on the Matigara to
Dalkhola route and whether a larger ring is involved. [@1]
