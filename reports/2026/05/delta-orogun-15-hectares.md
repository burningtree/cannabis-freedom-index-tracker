On Tuesday 12 May 2026 National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) operatives, backed by
soldiers, raided a cannabis farm at Orogun village in Ughelli North local government area,
Delta State. [@1,2]

Agency spokesman Femi Babafemi said in a statement reported by Punch and National Post that
the teams destroyed 37,500 kilograms of skunk cultivated on 15 hectares, recovered another
55.4 kilograms from the site and arrested 30-year-old Godwin Vincent Osadera. [@1,2]

The same weekly release also described a separate farm clearance at Ejeonu on 10 May; those
figures are not included here. [@1,2]

Neither news account published a field photograph of the Orogun raid. [@1,2]

Osadera was named as the suspect arrested in connection with the Orogun farm. [@1,2]
