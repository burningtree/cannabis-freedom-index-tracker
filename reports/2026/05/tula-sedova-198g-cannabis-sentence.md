Privokzalny district court in Tula convicted 40-year-old Tula resident Remizov V.S. of
illegal storage of a large amount of cannabis without intent to sell under part 2 of article 228
of the Criminal Code, MK Tula reported on 18 May 2026. [@1] The court found that from February
2025 he had been growing cannabis plants in his flat on Sedova Street alongside ordinary
houseplants. [@1,2]

In February 2026 he dried and ground the crop and kept 198 grams of marijuana at home for
personal use until police discovered it. [@1,2] The court sentenced him to three years and three
months' imprisonment, suspended, with a probation period and additional obligations, while
leaving earlier travel restrictions in place. [@1] The 198 grams of cannabis were ordered
destroyed. [@1,2]
