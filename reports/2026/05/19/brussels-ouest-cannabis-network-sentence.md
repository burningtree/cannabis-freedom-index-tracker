The Brussels criminal court convicted eighteen people on 19 May 2026 for large-scale drug production
by a criminal organisation after an inquiry by the Bruxelles-Ouest police zone, Le Soir and
Bruxelles Today reported on 1 June. [@1,2] The combined prison sentences amounted to eighty-seven
years, with €110,900 in fines imposed on seventeen defendants. [@1,2]

At the start of 2025 investigators carried out twenty-one searches that dismantled eleven cannabis
plantations with a total capacity of 7,380 plants, the police summary quoted by both outlets said.
[@1,2] They seized twenty-three kilograms of cannabis along with cocaine, cash, vehicles, firearms
and forged papers, valued at about €5,659,944 in confiscations. [@1,2]

The network used thirteen buildings in Brussels, Wallonia and Flanders, including sites named in
Rhode-Saint-Genèse, Lasne, Watermael-Boitsfort, Woluwe-Saint-Pierre, Mons, Fontaine-l’Évêque,
Herent, Gosselies, Rixensart, Bertrix, Soignies and Jette. [@1,2]
