Jhargram district police scored a major success after an all-night watch on the
Kharagpur–Chichira National Highway 49. [@1] Led by SDPO Shamim Biswas, the
Special Operations Group with Jhargram police station began the watch late on
Saturday night; in the early hours of Sunday a suspicious white pickup van
running from Jharkhand toward Kolkata was stopped at Guptamani. [@1]

The van held 156.2 kilograms of ganja packed in sacks. [@1] Police arrested
driver Himanshu Singh of Jaunpur district in Uttar Pradesh and helper Ajmal
Hossain of Kashipur in Kolkata, and registered an NDPS Act case. [@1] Produced
before the Jhargram court on Sunday, both were remanded to seven days' police
custody for the investigation. [@1]

Police say the Kharagpur–Chichira highway has long served traffickers, with
earlier SOG-assisted raids in Jhargram and Jambani seizing large quantities;
investigators believe Odisha-grown ganja is moved across the country in
long-haul vehicles by luring drivers and helpers with money. [@1]
