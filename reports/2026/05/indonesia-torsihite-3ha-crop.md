Mandailing Natal police chief AKBP Bagus Priandy said officers found a cannabis
field of about three hectares on Tor Sihite hill at Rao Rao Dolok village,
Tambangan district, on Saturday 30 May 2026 at about 5:15 am. [@1]

The field held about 3,000 cannabis plants estimated at three to four months
old and one to two metres tall. [@1] Officers found no owner or suspected
grower at the site, which sits in steep hills far from settlements and took
about eight hours to reach on foot. [@1] Plant exhibits were taken to the Polres
for further investigation. [@1]
