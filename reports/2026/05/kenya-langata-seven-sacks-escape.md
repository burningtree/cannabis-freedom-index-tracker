Acting on intelligence linking people to drug trafficking in the area, DCI
Anti-Narcotics Unit detectives stormed a house along Kitengela Road in Maasai
Estate, Langata, on Friday 8 May 2026. [@1,2]

Officers were met by a calm, cooperative man who claimed to be fetching keys and
then slipped out through a back window, evading arrest; a woman then admitted
the team. [@1,2] The search recovered seven sacks of cannabis sativa hidden in
the servant quarters, two weighing scales, a roll of yellow cellotape believed
used for packaging, two cartons of Rizla rolling papers, a mobile phone and
assorted documents pointing to an organised distribution operation. [@1,2]

The exhibits went to the DCI headquarters store; a manhunt is on for the
escapee, and the DCI appealed for tips through its Fichua Kwa DCI initiative.
[@1,2]
