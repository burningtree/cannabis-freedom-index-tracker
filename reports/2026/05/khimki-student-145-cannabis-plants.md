South-Western Moscow district drug officers and federal drug-control staff stopped a
22-year-old Moscow Oblast man who police said was a student at a capital university and was
involved in illegal drug trafficking. [@1] Supported by SOBR Mangust, they detained him at his
home on Sovkhoznaya Street in Khimki and found a bag of plant material in his clothing. [@1,2]

A search of that flat recovered two more wraps, five plastic containers of similar material,
scales, vacuum bags and seven bottles of fertiliser. [@1] Police then searched a second flat he
rented in a neighbouring building, where they found six containers, two bags, a container of
chopped plant matter, 145 cannabis plants, growing equipment, scales and a vacuum packer. [@1,2]

Laboratory tests identified the seized material as more than 700 grams of marijuana. [@1,2] The
Lomonosovsky district police investigative unit opened a case for attempted large-scale drug
trafficking, and a court remanded the student in custody. [@1]
