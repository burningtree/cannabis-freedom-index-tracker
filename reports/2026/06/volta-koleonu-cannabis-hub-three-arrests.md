Acting on intelligence, NACOC’s tactical team carried out an overnight raid on a stash house
in Koleonu, Wegbe and Akplamafu in the Volta Region and arrested three people the
commission described as principal suspects in an illegal drug network. [@1,2]

NACOC said the facility was used to cultivate cannabis, produce hashish oil and distribute
drugs from one location. [@1,2] The commission called the recovery of hashish oil a sign of
processing beyond dried cannabis alone and said earlier Volta seizures this year had
involved cannabis without reported hashish oil or arrests at cultivation sites. [@2]

The three remained in custody assisting investigations; NACOC said they were expected to
face prosecution and that officers were tracing collaborators and supply routes. [@1,2]
