Kazan drug-control officers stopped a 36-year-old unemployed man from Pestrechinsky district
who they said cultivated cannabis and made marijuana for sale. [@1] They detained him in his
Volkswagen Tiguan on a city car park while he waited for a buyer, and under the driver's seat
found two containers with 15 grams of marijuana confirmed by testing. [@1]

At his home in Kamysh village officers seized five more glass jars of marijuana, 30 cannabis
plants from a greenhouse in the yard, and equipment including seeds, fertiliser, LED lamps,
ventilation, scales and packing materials. [@1,2] Expert examination put the total amount of
marijuana at more than three kilograms. [@1,2]

City police investigators opened cases for attempted large-scale trafficking and illegal
cultivation of narcotic plants, and a court ordered the man held in custody. [@1,2]
