District Police Chief Raju Lama told a programme in Pouwadungma Rural Municipality on 21 June 2026
that Bhojpur's anti-drug campaign in fiscal year 2082/83 had destroyed 130,485 mature cannabis
plants and 8,550 saplings in hidden plantations across the district. [@1]

Officers also seized 1,556 kilograms of processed marijuana and arrested 14 people on drug charges,
Lama said. [@1] Three were accused of producing, packing and selling cannabis and 11 of helping
move and store it; all were handed to the courts. [@2]

Lama said police would keep uprooting illegal plots, with particular attention to Hatuwagadhi and
Aamchok rural municipalities, and urged farmers toward legal crops. [@2] A district awareness drive
was due to run until 26 June ahead of the International Day Against Drug Abuse. [@1]
