Latvian police say four members of a group selling narcotics at scale were detained this week after
months of work. [@1,2] On 3 June 2026 officers arrested three men born in 1975, 1977 and 1999, and
on 4 June they detained a fourth person. [@1,2]

The same day teams from the Latgale criminal police bureau and the special assignment battalion
carried out ten sanctioned searches in Rēzekne city and in Rēzekne and Madona municipalities. [@1]
Inside a fitted-out house they found two marijuana grows and took 835 live plants, about 24
kilograms of dried cannabis and specialised growing kit, which police called a record Latgale
seizure for the year. [@1,2]

Further searches yielded 13 firearms, about 100 weapon parts and €3,145 in cash, which officers
planned to freeze. [@1] Prosecutors asked the court to jail two of the detainees. [@1] Police
stress that none of the suspects has been proven guilty yet. [@1]
