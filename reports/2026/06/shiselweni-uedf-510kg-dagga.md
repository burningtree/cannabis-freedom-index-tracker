The Umbutfo Eswatini Defence Force seized more than 510 kilograms of dagga worth over
E510,000 and arrested three Eswatini nationals in operations in the Shiselweni Region
between 23 and 25 June 2026. [@1,2]

On 23 June at about 6am, troops at a mounted checkpoint at Mashayineni in the
Lushikishini area stopped an orange Nissan Murano carrying 107.1 kg of dagga valued at
E107,100; Thabiso Myolwa (31) and Patrick S. Mbatha (34), both of Luve in Mbelebeleni,
were arrested with the vehicle and consignment. [@1,2] Barely an hour later at the same
checkpoint, soldiers stopped a white Toyota Hilux GD-6 bakkie holding 403.2 kg valued at
E403,200; driver Mxolisi Mnisi (44) of Maguga, Hhohho Region, was arrested with the
vehicle and drugs. [@1,2]

On 25 June at about 5:37pm, troops patrolling at Sivule in the Lubombo Region intercepted
four Zimbabwean nationals attempting to enter Eswatini illegally from South Africa and
handed them to law enforcement. [@1,2]
