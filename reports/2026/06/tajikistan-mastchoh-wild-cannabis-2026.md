Officers of the Interior Ministry's district department in Mastchoh, working
with the district youth and sports department, carried out an operation on
vacant land in Buston. [@1] They found and destroyed more than 4,000 wild
cannabis plants there. [@1] The plants weighed 48 kg in total. [@1]

The operation took place on abandoned land in Buston. [@2] It was part of
Operation Kuknor-2026 (Poppy-2026), a republican (nationwide) campaign. [@2]

Police said inspection and raid work continues across the district, with further
measures to find and destroy drug plants and prevent their illegal cultivation.
[@1] They urged residents to report illegal cultivation, storage or trafficking
of drugs immediately. [@1] Experts cited by the ministry said public involvement
in preventing violations and close cooperation with law enforcement are
important in stopping the spread of drugs. [@1]
