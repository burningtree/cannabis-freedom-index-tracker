The Borgerhout furniture business allegedly served as a front for hashish
trafficking between Morocco and Belgium: hashish hidden in furniture was moved
from Morocco to Belgium while part of the proceeds was reinvested in Morocco.
[@1,2] The judicial investigation opened in 2025 over suspected criminal use of
the business for drug imports. [@1,2]

On 30 June 2026 the investigating judge ordered searches; the 61-year-old shop
owner was arrested and remanded the next day on suspicion of narcotics
trafficking in association. [@1,2] The council chamber confirmed his continued
detention, with a further hearing set for 19 August. [@1,2] The business, which
had filed no tax returns since 2019 and held no VAT number yet kept receiving
payments including cash deposits, was sealed. [@1,2] The investigation under an
investigating judge with the Antwerp local detective branch continues. [@1,2]
