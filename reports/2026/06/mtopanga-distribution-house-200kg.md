Detectives from the Anti-Narcotics Unit arrested three suspects in Mombasa
following an intelligence-led raid in Mtopanga, where cannabis sativa valued
at approximately Sh6 million was recovered. [@1]

Acting on a tip-off linking the premises to drug trafficking, officers
stormed a two-bedroom house believed used as a distribution base and found
three sacks packed with cannabis sativa weighing about 200 kg. [@1] Police
also impounded a Honda motorcycle, registration KMGA 080F, suspected of
moving narcotics around the area. [@1]

The three were taken into custody for processing ahead of expected court
appearance on drug-related charges; the cannabis was secured as court
evidence. [@1]
