Police in Rachuonyo North Sub-County, Homa Bay County, seized cannabis sativa
worth Ksh1.2 million while arresting three suspects. [@1]

Per the National Police Service, a multi-agency team — Kendu Bay Police
Station officers with National Government Administrative Officers including
the Rambira Location chief and the Kamser Seka assistant chief — operated in
Rachuonyo North, arresting three suspected traffickers and a large
consignment. [@1]

The raid recovered 636 rolls of cannabis sativa in two green sacks and one
white carrier bag; the suspects were held at Kendu Bay Police Station pending
processing and arraignment, with the narcotics kept as exhibits. [@1]
