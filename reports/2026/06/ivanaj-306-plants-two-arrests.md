On 20 June 2026 the Malësi e Madhe police commissariat finished an operation it called Flagranca in the village of Ivanaj. [@1,2] The Albanian Telegraphic Agency published the police account that day from Tirana. [@1] Euronews Albania published the same account the same afternoon. [@2]

After proactive inquiries, officers found 306 suspected cannabis sativa plants under cultivation on a plot in Ivanaj and destroyed them by burning. [@1,2] At the place where the plants were growing they caught two men in the act and arrested them. [@1] They are N. Z., 40, who lives in the village of Guci e Re, and K. P., 43, who lives in the village of Grudë e Re. [@1,2] Police suspect both of cultivating the plants. [@1,2]

The file went to the prosecutor at the Court of First Instance of General Jurisdiction in Shkodër for further action. [@1,2] Police said checks would continue across Shkodër county, to find and stop further cultivation. [@1] Neither account gives a weight, an area, or the men's full names. [@1,2]

Euronews Albania published a still with the report. It shows officers in Policia vests walking a person along a track, with a Malësi e Madhe label and a police emblem. [@2] The still does not name the person. [@2]
