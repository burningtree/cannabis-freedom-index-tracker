Gardaí found a cannabis grow house at a home in Ballymakeera, Co Cork, on 8 June 2026 and seized 122
mature plants and about 1 kg of loose cannabis. [@1,2] The plants were valued at €97,600 and the loose
cannabis at €20,000, for a total of €117,600. [@1,2]

The search was carried out under Operation Tara by the Cork North West Divisional Drugs Unit,
supported by the Cork South West unit. [@1,2] A man in his 40s was arrested and detained under
Section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996 at a Garda station in the Southern
Region. [@1,2]

The drugs were sent to Forensic Science Ireland for analysis, and investigations are ongoing. [@1]
