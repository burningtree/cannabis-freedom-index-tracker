Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport customs seized 47.52264 kg of cannabis
from a transit express shipment, according to a Guangzhou Customs notice
reposted by Beijing Daily and Sina on 10 June 2026. [@1,2] The parcel was
declared as low-allergy memory-foam pillows. [@1,2]

Officers said an X-ray image looked abnormal, so they opened the shipment and
found many identical pillows, 48 of them with opened seams hiding green plant
material in plastic or foil with a strong smell. [@1,2] Laboratory testing
confirmed cannabis weighing 47.52264 kg. [@1,2] Customs reminded the public
that smuggling or trafficking drugs is a criminal offence in China regardless of
quantity. [@1,2]

The notices did not name any suspect or say whether the parcel was referred to
criminal investigation. [@1,2]
