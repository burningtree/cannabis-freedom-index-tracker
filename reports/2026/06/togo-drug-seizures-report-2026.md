In June 2026 Togo reported that its security forces had seized more than 50
tonnes of drugs in 2025, mainly cannabis, alongside 52 kg of cocaine and
psychotropic drugs.[@1]

Security minister Calixte Madjoulba pointed to the seizures as evidence of
anti-trafficking work.[@1] The report described Togo's port and its position on
the Abidjan–Lagos corridor as routes used by traffickers, following the January
destruction of the cannabis seized in 2024.[@1]

The figure marked Togo as a transit platform with local consumption on the
side — and made the 2024 bonfire look like a prelude.[@1]
