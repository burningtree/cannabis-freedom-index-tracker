Cooktown police charged two men with drug offences after executing a search
warrant in Wujal Wujal on Friday 12 June 2026. [@1] It will be alleged
officers attended a Bloodwood Road property and located 575 cannabis plants
and more than 10 kilograms of dried cannabis. [@1,2]

A 63-year-old Bloomfield man was charged with possessing dangerous drugs in a
Schedule 2 quantity of or exceeding Schedule 3, producing dangerous drugs in
the same quantity category, and possessing anything used in the commission of
a crime. [@1] A 38-year-old Bloomfield man was charged with the same three
counts plus possessing utensils or pipes and an offence of buying or possessing
Schedule 4 or Schedule 8 medicines. [@1] Both were issued a notice to appear
in Cooktown Magistrates Court on 15 July 2026. [@1]
