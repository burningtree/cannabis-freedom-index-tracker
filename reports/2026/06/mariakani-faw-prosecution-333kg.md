Two people charged with trafficking cannabis worth Sh10 million appeared
before a Mariakani court, pleading not guilty. [@1] Paul Ogolla Ogutu, alias
Ogola, and Emmanuel Ndoro Chaka, alias Ndoro, appeared before Resident
Magistrate Jamlick Muriithi Mwenda. [@1]

Per the prosecution, ANU officers intercepted the white FAW JH8 lorry,
registration KDL 220P, towing trailer ZE 6680 with container MSMU 6177520,
in Mariakani, Kaloleni Sub-County, on 19 June 2026, recovering nine sacks of
dry plant material later confirmed at 333.35 kg valued at Sh10,000,500. [@1]
The lorry belonged to Sayusa Company; Ndoro drove while Ogutu rode as
passenger. [@1] Exhibits were photographed and sent to the Government
Chemist for analysis, sampling and weighing. [@1]

Prosecutor Martin Mbote opposed bail and sought a pre-bail report, arguing
both men live near the Kenya-Tanzania border and inquiries including forensic
and cyber analysis were ongoing. [@1] The court set 2 July for pre-bail
report and pre-trial directions. [@1]
