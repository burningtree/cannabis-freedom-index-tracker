Shenzhen Post Office Customs seized cannabis with a gross weight of 973.6 g in
transit mail, according to a “Customs Release” WeChat notice reported by
Chinanews on 3 June 2026. [@1] The parcel was declared as a baby blanket. [@1]

Officers screening transit cargo said the X-ray image looked wrong and opened the
shipment. [@1] They found plant material hidden inside the blanket in transparent
plastic bags. [@1] A cannabis field test was positive and laboratory analysis
detected tetrahydrocannabinol. [@1] Customs said the case was transferred to
anti-smuggling investigators. [@1] It reminded the public that drug smuggling
and trafficking are criminal offences regardless of quantity. [@1]
