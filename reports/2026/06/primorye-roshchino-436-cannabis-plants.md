Drug officers from the Dalnerechensk Interior Ministry office searched the
homestead of a 38-year-old Roshchino resident after a tip about illegal
cultivation, PrimaMedia reported on 29 June 2026. [@1]

On the garden plot they uprooted 436 cannabis plants. [@2] Forensic testing
confirmed the plants contained controlled narcotic substances. [@1]

Investigators opened a criminal case under point "v" of part 2 of article
231 of the Criminal Code for large-scale illegal cultivation, which can
carry up to eight years in prison. [@2]
