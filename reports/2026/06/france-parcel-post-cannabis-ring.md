During an action week between 15 and 19 June 2026, French and Spanish police arrested 10 suspects
and carried out 24 searches across both countries against a ring that moved cannabis from the
Barcelona area to the Rennes region by parcel post. [@1,2] The searches seized over €278,000 in
cash and €111,000 in bank accounts, as well as cannabis and other drugs, firearms, phones and
cars. [@1,2]

Three suspects ran the operation: two based in Spain who coordinated supply and shipments, and one
based in France who handled distribution on arrival. [@1,2] Since September 2024 they sent at
least 114 parcels holding around 1.4 tonnes of cannabis through a courier firm, using forged
identities; drug money travelled back to Spain the same way, in parcels fitted with GPS
trackers. [@1,2] Authorities put the network's assets, including property, at about €4
million. [@1,2]

Eurojust coordinated the investigation from The Hague, executing the European arrest and
investigation orders, while Europol exchanged intelligence and stationed experts in both
countries, even funding the deployment of French investigators to Spain. [@1,2]
