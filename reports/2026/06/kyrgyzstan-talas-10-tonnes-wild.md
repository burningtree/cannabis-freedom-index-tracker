Police in Talas district destroyed about 10 tonnes of wild-growing cannabis as
part of anti-drug operations, the Talas regional police department said. [@1]

Preventive raids were run jointly with local government representatives to find
and destroy narcotic plants and prevent drug crime. [@1] Surveying the district,
officers found plots of wild cannabis, after which police and local officials
mowed and burned about 10 tonnes of narcotic plants. [@1] Police said prevention
and outreach work among residents against drug trafficking continues. [@1]
