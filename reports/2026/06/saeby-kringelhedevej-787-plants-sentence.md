A 26-year-old man from Lithuania was sentenced on 15 June 2026 to nine months in prison, deportation from Denmark and a six-year ban on returning. [@1,2] North Jutland police had found him the day before, a Sunday, at a farm on Kringelhedevej between Voerså and Præstbro, south of Sæby, after a tip about suspicious activity. [@1,3]

In outbuildings, officers found a greenhouse they described as professionally fitted, with lamps, insulation, ventilation and heat. [@1,3] They counted 787 cannabis plants, which they said came to a minimum of about 22 kg of marijuana. [@1,2,3] He was convicted the same day at a custody hearing at the court in Hjørring. [@1,2,3] Peter Skovbak, head of the Frederikshavn investigation centre, told TV2 Nord that the man was sentenced under the aggravated drugs provision. [@1]
