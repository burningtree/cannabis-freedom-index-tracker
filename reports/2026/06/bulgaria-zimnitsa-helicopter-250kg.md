More than 250 kilograms of marijuana worth around €2 million and a helicopter
believed to have been used to smuggle narcotics were seized in a special
police operation in the Yambol region. [@1]

The aircraft was discovered at a former grain storage facility in the village
of Zimnitsa near Straldzha during a joint operation by the State Agency for
National Security (DANS) and the anti-organised crime directorate GDBOP. [@1]

Four people were placed in pre-trial detention, officials said at a joint
press conference quoted by BTA. [@1] Investigators said the helicopter's
exact role in the suspected trafficking network was still being clarified. [@1]
