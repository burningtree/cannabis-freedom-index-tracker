Infantry Battalion 753/ATV border task force personnel secured a cannabis
plantation at Tomka village, Oksamol district, Pegunungan Bintang regency, on 23
June 2026, finding 169 harvest-ready plants believed grown in the forest around
the village. [@1]

Troops immediately uprooted the plants to hand them to the nearest police post
for destruction. [@1] Joint Regional Defence Command III chief Lieutenant
General Lucky Avianto said intelligence indicated the crop was a logistics
source for an armed criminal group (KKB) and would have been sold to the public
in Jayapura after harvest; that attribution comes solely from the military. [@1]
