The Republican Police executed a large-scale operation in the Vodjè district of
Cotonou, dismantling a notorious ghetto that secretly harboured an Indian-hemp
(cannabis) trafficking network serving as both a transit point and a consumption
hub. [@1,2]

The raid led to the arrest of several people on site and the seizure of
substantial equipment linked to the trade; investigations continue into the
network's branches. [@1,2] Police are actively searching for a Béninese armed
forces officer nicknamed "Alòtrò" (gecko), suspected of direct complicity. [@1,2]

According to investigators, the soldier did not frequent the ghetto to consume
but ran a racketeering system, regularly extorting payments from dealers in
exchange for supposed protection and immunity from law-enforcement raids. [@1,2]
