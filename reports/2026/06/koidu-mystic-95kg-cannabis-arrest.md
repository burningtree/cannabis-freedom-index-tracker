Sierra Leone Police said an intelligence-led raid led by Tankoro division commander CSP Dr Sahr Nyuma arrested Sheriff Koroma, nicknamed Mystic, at his hideout in Tankoro Camp, Koidu City, on 25 June 2026. [@1,2]

Officers recovered about 95 kilograms of suspected cannabis sativa, additional dried leaves believed to be cannabis, two packets and 370 tablets of suspected tramadol. [@1,2]

Police called the operation one of the largest anti-drug actions in the Tankoro division and said enquiries continued to trace others in the network. [@1,2]
