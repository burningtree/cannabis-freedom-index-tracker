Rostov drug-control officers detained a 44-year-old unemployed city
resident after searching his homestead in the Pervomaysky district on
17 June 2026, DONNEWS.RU reported. [@1]

Officers found cannabis planted along garden paths and, in the basement,
a grow room with artificial lighting, ventilation and plants in buckets,
plus dried plant material. [@1] They seized 419 live plants and 200 grams
of marijuana. [@2]

The man told investigators he had bought seeds online about a year earlier
and grown the crop for personal use. [@2] Investigators opened criminal
cases under parts 2 of articles 228 and 231 of the Criminal Code and a
court jailed him pending trial. [@1]
