Drug Law Enforcement Unit officers from the Eastern Region CID, with the Police Intelligence
Directorate and other units, destroyed cannabis covering six acres at Akyeremanteng in Yilo
Krobo municipality. [@1,2] The work followed the arrest of one suspect on Monday 15 June 2026;
he led officers to the farms on 16 June, Bryt FM reported. [@2]

On two plots of two and four acres, police burned the crop. [@1,2] Investigators found illegal
underground connections to the national electricity grid and irrigation systems. [@1,2] Other
people suspected of cultivating the drug escaped into nearby bush after residents alerted them
to the police approach. [@1,2]

Dr Samuel Antwi Akomea, director of the Ghana Police High Risk Operations Training Centre,
told Asaase Radio the area was a major cultivation hub and that similar operations would
continue. [@1] Eastern Regional Operations Commander Agyare Okyere said intelligence work
against the network would go on. [@1] The suspect was expected to face prosecution in court.
[@1]

Asaase Radio published a photograph of officers at one of the sites. [@1]
