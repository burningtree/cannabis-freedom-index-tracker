Acting on a tip-off, Mekhliganj police searched a vehicle at the Changrabandha
bypass checkpost on Friday morning. [@1] Officers stopped a Honda Civic (WB 06 H
2859) travelling from Cooch Behar toward Siliguri and began their inspection. [@1]

Sensing the search, driver Ranbir Shikari, a trafficker from Ward 20 of
Subhashpalli in Siliguri, abandoned the car and jumped into a nearby pond to
flee, but police immediately dragged him out of the water and arrested him. [@1]

Built-in compartments in various parts of the car held 37 packets containing
about 57.65 kilograms of ganja, with a market value of several lakh rupees, and
the car was seized. [@1] Block development officer-cum-executive magistrate
Nareshraj Rai and Mekhliganj officer-in-charge Mohammed Shahbaz attended the
operation; a preliminary probe suggested wider involvement, which police said
they were investigating. [@1]
