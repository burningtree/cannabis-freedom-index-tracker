On the night of 16 June 2026, a team from RAB-13 stopped a pickup at a
checkpoint on the Darapara culvert, on the road from Bhulli Bazar toward
Farabari in Madarganj village, ward 3 of Auliapur union, under Bhulli police
station in Thakurgaon district. [@1] The team belonged to Crime Prevention
Company-1 at the Dinajpur camp of the Rapid Action Battalion, which the note
said was running regular operations against drugs in its Rangpur area. [@1]
Officers arrested 45-year-old Khorshed, of Ramchandrapur Bhollak in Brahmanpara,
Cumilla, and 40-year-old Md Siraj Mia, of Ramchandragarh in Gouripur,
Mymensingh. [@1]

The search at 8.45 p.m. recovered 64 kilograms of cannabis, the pickup and
55,000 taka in cash. [@1] RAB described the two as professional drug traders who
collected drugs from districts across Rangpur Division under cover of driving
the pickup and supplied them elsewhere in the country. [@1]

A case was filed against the accused under the Narcotics Control Act, and they
were handed to the local police station with the seized items. [@1] The note was
issued for the battalion commander by Biplab Kumar Goswami, an additional
superintendent of police and senior assistant director for media, and said
RAB-13's intelligence work would continue under a zero-tolerance policy on
drugs. [@1]
