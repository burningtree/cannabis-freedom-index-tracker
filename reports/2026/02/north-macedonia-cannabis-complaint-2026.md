The Interior Ministry reported a criminal complaint against ten people suspected of unlawful drug production and distribution under Article 215. Two suspects were in 48-hour custody, and investigators were examining links to cannabis seized in Serbia. The ministry said some inspected companies operated lawfully; the complaint was not a conviction. [@1]
