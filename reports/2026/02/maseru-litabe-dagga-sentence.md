Magistrate Puseletso McPherson on Monday sentenced 'Matsepang Litabe (54) after finding
her guilty of unlawful possession of dagga. [@1]

Litabe was ordered to pay a fine of M4,000 or serve four years' imprisonment, half of
which was suspended, for contravening Section 91(2) of the Penal Code Act No. 6 of 2010.
[@1] Detective Woman Police Constable Chabana of the Maseru Rural Headquarters testified
that on 6 February 2026 she received a tip-off that the accused was holding dagga; at the
accused's residence she found 115 packets, and asked her to produce a licence for them,
which she failed to do, leading to her arrest. [@1]
