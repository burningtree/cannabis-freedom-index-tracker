The government withdrew cultivation approvals from six companies after thirteen inspections, citing storage, reporting, documentation and stock discrepancies. The Health Ministry announced checks across 43 licensed entities. It also promised stronger legislation after the inspections, rather than announcing that a new law was already in force. [@1]
