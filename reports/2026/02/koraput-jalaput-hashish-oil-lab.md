Koraput district police in Odisha arrested six people within 30 hours of raiding a hashish oil laboratory on an island in the Jolaput reservoir. [@1] Police named Allen Jayaraj, 33, of Thiruvananthapuram as the alleged kingpin and main cook; Kiran D, 33, Anand Raj, 28, and Bittu B, 34, also from Kerala; and local facilitators Lalchan Khilla, 25, of Malkangiri and Sadhu Pangi, 45, of Koraput. [@1]

Acting on intelligence, officers approached by boat on 26 February 2026 and trekked through forest to the extraction unit under Padwa police station limits, along the Odisha-Andhra Pradesh border. [@1] SP Rohit Verma said the unit had been set up about 15 days earlier. [@1]

Police reported seizing 1,800.5 litres of hashish oil, which they valued at over Rs 225 crore, and 1,000 kg of ganja, dried cannabis, valued at around Rs 5 crore. [@1] They recovered a 28 KV generator, modified pressure cookers with custom piping, metal connectors, storage drums and nearly 80 litres of cleaning solvents. [@1]

Devdiscourse's initial account on 26 February said the suspects had escaped and efforts to apprehend them continued. [@2] The later police account said one accused was caught at the site while the alleged mastermind and associates fled. [@1] Police sealed the island, blocked ferry points and escape routes, patrolled by boat, and deployed six ground teams and drones in a 30-hour search. [@1]

Police said Jayaraj had prior cases under the NDPS narcotics law and used his contacts to establish the unit. [@1] They alleged that the group processed bulk-purchased cannabis into hashish oil using specialised chemicals and controlled heating; the Kerala-based members handled production and distribution, while local facilitators sourced raw material. [@1]

A special investigation team was formed under the SP's supervision, with forensic examinations and financial inquiries under way and teams being sent to other states to trace the network. [@1] Verma said police would seek Jayaraj's remand for further questioning. [@1] The DIG declined to release further details during the investigation and appealed for public assistance under the Nasha Mukt Odisha campaign. [@1]
