Three suspects were arrested early Sunday morning along the Nairobi-Mombasa
highway in possession of 203.4 kg of cannabis valued at Sh6.1 million. [@1]
[@2] Mohamed Omondi, 49, Lilian Achieng, 36, and Patrick Wambua, 30, were
intercepted at Bonje in Jomvu Sub-County at 6:45 am aboard trailer KDL 915L.
[@1] [@2]

Jomvu Sub-County Police Commander Celina Chirchir said a multi-agency team
led by the Anti-Narcotics Unit acted on a public tip-off, laying a trap from
4 am; searching the trailer found three unauthorised occupants and three
sacks of cannabis. [@1] [@2] The lorry had been under surveillance from
Uganda, entering Kenya through Malaba before diverting to Thika — where the
driver claimed to deliver chicken feed — and on toward Mombasa. [@1] [@2]

Chirchir said the woman suspect had long been on police radar, evading
arrest under multiple false identities; officers recovered her genuine
national ID in the operation. [@1] [@2]
