On 26 February 2026, Colombia's traffic police seized 2,140 kg of marijuana from a cargo van
stopped on the Cali–Andalucía highway at Buga in Valle del Cauca. [@1,2] Officers found 100
packages of various sizes in the load bed and arrested the 39-year-old driver, who was travelling
the Corinto–Bogotá route. [@1,2] Police also immobilised the van and seized a mobile phone. [@1,2]

The National Police valued the marijuana at about 4.28 billion pesos and said it prevented roughly
4.14 million doses from reaching the illegal market. [@1] El Tiempo reported the same weight and
arrest but put the averted doses at about 2.14 million. [@2] The driver was handed to prosecutors
at Buga's URI office. [@1,2]
