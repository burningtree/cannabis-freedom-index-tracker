Belgrade’s Higher Public Prosecutor said the Konjuh seizure followed investigative work in its earlier two-tonne Zemun case. The office publicly questioned when the organised-crime prosecutor became involved and why inspectors had notified it. The statement exposed a dispute over the investigation’s handling while the cases continued. [@1]
