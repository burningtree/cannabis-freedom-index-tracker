On Friday 20 February 2026 integrated military and federal police raided a farm at Brotas de
Macaúbas in Bahia's Chapada Diamantina region. [@1,2] g1 said nobody was arrested. [@1]

The plantation covered about six hectares—roughly eight football pitches—with irrigation,
solar power and fertigation, according to g1 and Folha do Vale. [@1,2] Officers seized one tonne of cannabis and incinerated about
200,000 plants on the spot. [@1,2] The Military Police estimated the destroyed crop could
have produced about 66 tonnes of marijuana. [@1,2]

g1 published a Military Police photograph of the plantation with its report. [@1]
