On Friday 13 February 2026, narcotics detectives from Empat Lawang district police, led by
district police chief Abdul Aziz Septiadi, reached a hidden cannabis plantation of roughly
three hectares in coffee-growing hills at Batu Jungul village, Muara Pinang district,
Empat Lawang regency, South Sumatra. [@1,2] The field was split across two plots on a
mountaintop reached after a six-to-eight-hour walk from the village. [@1]

Officers uprooted plants and seized eight sacks of dried cannabis ready for sale, weighing
about 200 kilograms, which police valued at about one billion rupiah. [@1,2] They also
reported about 6,000 wet cannabis stalks at the site. [@1]

At a guard hut they briefly detained a couple with initials R (22) and A (18), whom police
described as plantation minders rather than the owner. [@1] While evacuating the pair and
evidence, an crowd that grew to about 50 people with machetes blocked the track and forced
police to release them for officers’ safety. [@1] Polda Sumsel spokesperson Commissioner
Nandang Mu’min Wijaya said investigators had already recorded their statements and identified
the alleged owner as Pen, with proceeds said to go to a man with initial E, R’s father. [@1,2]

The report names no successful arrest of Pen or E at the scene. [@1,2]
