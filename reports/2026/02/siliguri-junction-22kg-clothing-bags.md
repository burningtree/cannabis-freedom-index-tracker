Acting on secret-source information, Pradhannagar station police scored a big
success: about 22 kg of ganja was seized near Siliguri's Junction area and a
youth named Nikhil Kumar was arrested; the detainee is a resident of Bihar.
[@1] Kumar had collected a large quantity of ganja in Cooch Behar and set
out to smuggle it to Bihar; on Wednesday, while waiting for a bus in the
Siliguri Junction area, a special police team raided and caught him. [@1]

Kumar carried several bags full of clothes, and searching them stunned
police: packet after packet of ganja lay hidden with great cunning among the
clothes, weighing about 22 kg in all. [@1] He was to be produced in Siliguri
subdivision court on Thursday, and Pradhannagar police began investigating
who else was involved in the ring. [@1]
