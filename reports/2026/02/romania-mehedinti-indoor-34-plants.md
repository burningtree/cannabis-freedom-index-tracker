Police in Mehedinți and DIICOT prosecutors carried out nine home searches across the county on
23 February 2026 against two men, 33 and 38, suspected of buying, holding and selling cannabis
to users in Drobeta-Turnu Severin since 2025. [@1,2] One was also accused of running an indoor
grow at his home for sale. [@1,2]

Officers seized 34 cannabis plants at various stages of growth, about 80 grams of cannabis
portioned for sale, precision scales, grinders, items with drug traces and digital storage
devices. [@1,2] Prosecutors ordered both men detained on 23 February and on 24 February asked a
judge for 30 days' preventive arrest. [@1,2] Gendarmes supported the searches. [@1,2]

The communiqué reminded readers that suspects remain presumed innocent through the criminal
process. [@1,2]
