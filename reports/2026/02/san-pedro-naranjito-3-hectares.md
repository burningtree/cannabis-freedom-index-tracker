On 17 February 2026 agents of Paraguay's Secretaría Nacional Antidrogas (SENAD) from regional
department No. 7 in Concepción, with special forces and prosecutor Abg. Juan Daniel Benítez
of the Public Ministry, carried out a rural search at Colonia Naranjito in General Resquín
district, San Pedro department. [@1]

Concepción al Día reported that they eradicated about three hectares of suspected marijuana
in the growth stage and destroyed the crop on site. [@1] The outlet said SENAD estimated the
intervention removed about 9,000 kilograms (nine tonnes) of marijuana from circulation and
published an aerial photograph credited to the agency showing the plots detected before the
clearance. [@1]

The report named no arrests. [@1]
