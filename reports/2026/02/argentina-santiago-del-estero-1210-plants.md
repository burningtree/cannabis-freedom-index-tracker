Gendarmerie officers searched a property at Monte Encantado, in the La Banda department of Santiago
del Estero, and seized 1,210 cannabis plants, together with an irrigation system, an electrical and
lighting installation, clearing tools, a mobile phone, a car and other items used for illegal
cultivation. [@1,2] The searches were announced by the Gendarmerie on 10 February as having taken
place the previous day. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the province's unit for investigating complex crimes with a
reconnaissance unit, and dismantled the plantation at the finca. [@1] Officers searched a second
address, a house in the Misky Mayo neighbourhood of the provincial capital, where they found 2.17
kilograms of plant material in its natural state, residues in containers, precision scales, a
grinder, a bag sealer and another mobile phone. [@1,2]

Federal Court No. 2 of Santiago del Estero and Federal Prosecution No. 1, which handle the case,
ordered the detention of the only adult involved and the seizure of everything found at the two
addresses. [@1,2] No other suspect was named in either account. [@1,2]
