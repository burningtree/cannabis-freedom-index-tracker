Police in Amdanga of North 24 Parganas foiled an ingenious ganja smuggling
attempt: searching on NH12 near the old registry office, police stopped an
engine van whose driver claimed it carried only empty plastic bottles — and
so it appeared at first glance. [@1] But searching the vehicle, police found
sack after sack of ganja under the bottles; the haul was about 200 kg with
an estimated market value of Rs 20 lakh. [@1]

Engine van driver Sheikh Farid was arrested; the 42-year-old's home is in
Rajasthan, and he said the ganja-laden van had been concealed behind the
empty bottles. [@1] Police believe others besides Farid may be involved in
the trafficking ring, took him into custody, and began hunting the ring's
other members. [@1] Such novel-method smuggling is far from rare, but
Amdanga station police's alert operation stopped a major attempt. [@1]
