On 18 February 2026 the Directorate for the Fight Against Drug Trafficking (DLCN), with the
First Naval Special Forces Battalion and the Armed Forces strategic intelligence directorate
C2, located and eradicated a marijuana plantation at Las Minas aldea in Tocoa municipality,
Colón department. [@1,2]

La Tribuna said the plot covered about two manzanas and that officers uprooted and incinerated
10,097 marijuana plants to keep them from being processed or sold. [@1]

Prensa Latina, citing a prosecution note, reported more than 10,000 bushes burned on roughly
1.4 hectares in the same Las Minas operation in mountainous Colón. [@2]

Both accounts said the authorities are continuing operations against cultivation and trafficking
networks; neither named any arrests. [@1,2]

The prosecution's note, as relayed by Prensa Latina, also recalled an earlier coca eradication
at Icoteas in Limón; that earlier case is not covered here. [@2]
