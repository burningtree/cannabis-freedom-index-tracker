Peru's Ministry of Defence said that on 12 February 2026 the army, working with the National Police,
eradicated more than 119,000 marijuana seedlings at Sarabamba in Anco district, La Mar province,
Ayacucho, in the valley of the Apurímac, Ene and Mantaro rivers (VRAEM). [@1,2]

Personnel from the 2nd Wari Infantry Brigade took part through a coordinated incursion with
Counterterrorism Battalion No. 34 and specialised police units. [@1,2] The seedlings were spread
across three plots. [@1,2]

Officers also dismantled two clandestine storage sites and seized seven sacks of illicit material.
[@1,2] The ministry linked the operation to wider pacification work in the VRAEM and did not report
arrests. [@1,2]
