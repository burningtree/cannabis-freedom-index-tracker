Police arrested a 58-year-old farmer from Central Aceh, AW, carrying four sacks of ganja
in a Toyota Avanza. [@1]

AW had obtained the drugs in Beutong, Nagan Raya, for delivery to North Aceh; the case
broke when the Aceh Police narcotics team learned of a ganja deal in Nagan Raya on Friday
13 February, then tracked the suspect's departure toward Bireuen the next day. [@1]
Officers found the car on the Banda Aceh–Medan road at Kulu village, Kuta Blang,
Bireuen, in the early hours of Sunday 15 February, and discovered sacks covered with
black tarpaulin. [@1]

AW admitted the sacks held ganja taken from a man identified as Z in Beutong Ateuh: three
large white sacks and one small one totalling about 50 kilograms, meant for a man
identified as G in Geudong village, Geureudong Pase, North Aceh. [@1] Police went after G
but he escaped and is still sought, while AW is detained at Polda Aceh. [@1]
