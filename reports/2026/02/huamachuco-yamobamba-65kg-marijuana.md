During a road patrol on 1 February 2026, officers of the highway police drug division in Huamachuco
intervened a Toyota Hilux in the Yamobamba sector of Sánchez Carrión province, La Libertad region.
[@1,2]

They found marijuana divided into plastic-wrapped packages hidden in the vehicle, weighing 65 kg in
Caretas and described as more than 60 kg in Sol TV. [@1,2] The driver, Rubén Carbajal, 29, was
detained at the scene. [@1]

The Division of State and Drug Security of the Highway Police (DESDPC) said the stop was part of
efforts to block sierra routes used to move drugs toward the north coast. [@1]

Caretas reports that the case went to the La Libertad specialised drug-trafficking prosecutor and
that the detainee and drugs were sent to DEPANDRO in Trujillo for official weighing, sealing and
statements. [@1] Sol TV says Carbajal was handed to DEPANDRO Trujillo without explaining the cargo.
[@2]
