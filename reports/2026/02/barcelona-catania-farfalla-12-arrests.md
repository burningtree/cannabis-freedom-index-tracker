Spanish National Police and Italy’s Guardia di Finanza dismantled a criminal
group producing cannabis in Spain and sending marijuana and hashish to eastern
Sicily, in a joint operation coordinated by Eurojust and Europol. [@1,2]

On the action day of 10 February 2026, officers arrested twelve people—six in
Spain and six in Italy—and found three indoor cannabis plantations with 1,670
plants between Barcelona and Catania, along with growing equipment. [@1,2] They
also seized two rifles and two pistols, more than 40,000 euros in cash, and
blocked movable and immovable property worth about one million euros, including a
luxury home in Barcelona and several vehicles. [@1] Eurojust said six suspects
were placed under house arrest in Italy and two were held in pretrial detention
in Spain. [@2]

Investigators said the Barcelona-based leadership ran indoor production in Spain
and moved drugs to Italy hidden in fruit and vegetable pallets, later in animal-
feed sacks, and sometimes by sea. [@1,2] One hundred and forty-five officers took
part in the raids. [@1] Earlier stages of the same inquiry had already led to
thirteen other arrests and more than 900 kilograms of hashish and marijuana
seized, which the ministries describe separately from this February sweep. [@1,2]
