On Monday 19 January 2026 Macclesfield officers executed a warrant at a boarded-up former registry
office on Park Green. [@1,2] Four ground-floor rooms held fully grown cannabis plants. [@1,2] The
building had living quarters with fresh food, beds, a washing machine and drying clothes; a radiator
was still warm. [@1]

Around 5pm a thermal search found Viktor Ymeralilaj, 31, of no fixed address, crouched behind a roof
beam in the loft after about eight hours. [@1,2] He told police he had been held against his will by
two men who employed him to water the plants; he was nonetheless charged. [@1,2]

Police Constable David Noon estimated the street value of the cannabis at upwards of £175,000 and
said it had been destroyed. [@1,2] On Wednesday 18 February 2026 at Chester Crown Court Ymeralilaj
pleaded guilty to cultivation at the first opportunity and was jailed for 16 months. [@1,2]
