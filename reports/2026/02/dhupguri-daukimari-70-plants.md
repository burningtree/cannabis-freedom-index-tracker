Dhupguri police achieved notable success in an anti-narcotics drive, destroying
a large quantity of ganja plants in a sweeping Wednesday operation based on
secret-source information. [@1] A large police force took part under Dhupguri
station in-charge Anindya Bhattacharya and Daukimari outpost officer-in-charge
Ramen Laskar. [@1]

Police first raided Purba Daukimari and Madhya Khattimari, where about 70
mature ganja plants illegally grown across several plots were found; the
plants were seized and destroyed on the spot. [@1] Complaints of secret ganja
cultivation in these areas had been reaching the police for a long time, and
the operation followed on that basis, officials said. [@1]

Sensing the police presence, the landowners and accused fled the area; police
began a search for them, stepped up surveillance, and set about identifying
the accused and their network links. [@1]

The police team then raided Kalar Khas under Jhar Alta 2 gram panchayat,
where further evidence of illegal ganja cultivation was found and the plants
were likewise cut and burnt on the spot, spreading a stir across the area with
the back-to-back operations. [@1] Local residents largely welcomed the drive,
and police said such operations would continue with no compromise in the
anti-narcotics fight. [@1]
