Guinea's national police said anti-drug operations continued across the country under
Director General Djeneba Sory Camara. [@1] In Mandiana prefecture, Brigade
Anti-Criminalité No. 26 seized 37 kg of cannabis on Sunday 15 February 2026 in the
Hérémako quarter in the town centre. [@1,2]

Officers arrested a 51-year-old woman in possession of the drugs and took her to Mandiana
central commissariat for further investigation. [@1,2] Police said they were working to
establish where the cannabis came from and whether accomplices were involved. [@2]

Guinee7 reported the seizure under the national anti-narcotics campaign and said police
would pursue traffickers "to their last hideouts." [@1] Mediaguinee quoted local commanders
reaffirming intensified controls and reminded citizens to call the free 117 hotline.
[@2]
