Gladstone Criminal Investigation Branch, supported by the State Drug Squad,
charged 25 people with 235 offences and seized more than $3 million worth of
dangerous drugs as part of Operation Promontory, a nine-month operation that
closed on 19 February 2026. [@1] Crime scenes were established at two
residential properties at Toolooa and Glen Eden, and police conducted an
extensive search of bushland. [@1]

Police allege the searches yielded more than 7.5 kilograms of
methylamphetamine, 300 grams of cocaine, 360 pounds (about 163 kilograms) of
cannabis, and other illicit substances. [@1] Officers also allegedly seized
more than $1 million in cash plus multiple vehicles and other assets used in
crime. [@1] Eleven of the 25 people charged face trafficking dangerous drugs
counts covering methylamphetamine and cannabis. [@1]

Gladstone CIB officer in charge Detective Senior Sergeant Brett Richard said
police were confident Operation Promontory had dismantled a deeply entrenched
drug syndicate and disrupted supply in the Gladstone region. [@1] ABC reported
Detective Acting Superintendent Luke Peachey told media the syndicate operated
like a business, and that police would allege drugs, cash, firearms and
ammunition were buried near target addresses. [@2] Investigations were
continuing to establish where the alleged drugs originated. [@1,2]
