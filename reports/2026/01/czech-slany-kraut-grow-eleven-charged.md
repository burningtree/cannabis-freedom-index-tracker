Police detained and charged 11 people with drug offences in raids on 26 January
2026, mostly in the Central Bohemian Region and also in Prague. [@2] The
operation was run by the National Drug Headquarters, the Czech national drug
squad. [@1] The Slaný arrests were made in the Kladno district, near the ČSAD
bus depot. [@1] There, in the basement of a multi-storey building, investigators
found a large cannabis grow. [@2] Firefighters were also called to the building.
[@2]

Police seized hundreds of cannabis plants containing THC and other evidence.
[@1] Six of the accused were remanded in custody and five are being prosecuted
at liberty. [@2] All 11 are Czech citizens. [@2]

Police said the group not only grew the drug but also distributed it abroad.
[@1] Most of the accused face eight to 18 years in prison if convicted, under
paragraph 283a of the criminal code, which covers offences committed for an
organised criminal group. [@2] The case is code-named Kraut. [@2] The
investigation is at an early stage and police said they would not release
further details for now. [@2]
