The National Police Service confiscated about 90 kg of unprocessed cannabis
sativa in a targeted operation in Nairobi's Nyamakima area. [@1] Officers
deployed across the CBD acted on intelligence pointing to illegal activity
in Nyamakima, intercepting a lorry suspected of transporting illicit goods
and escorting it to the Nairobi Area Traffic yard for inspection. [@1]

A thorough search found 14 sacks hidden in the vehicle holding the 90 kg of
bhang; the driver and his assistant were arrested on the spot and placed in
custody pending investigation and arraignment. [@1] The NPS called it a
significant blow to trafficking networks in the capital. [@1]
