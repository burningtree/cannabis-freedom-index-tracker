Acting on a tip-off, a large police team uncovered a dense field of mature dagga plants in a
secluded part of Buffelspruit Farm near Schoemansdal, just outside Malelane, on Friday 9 January
2026. [@1,2] Led by acting provincial commissioner Major General Zeph Mkhwanazi, the officers
removed the plants growing along the banks of the Mahlambanyatsi river. [@1,2] Police valued the
crop at about R2.9 million on the street. [@1,2]

No arrests were made during the operation. [@1,2] Provincial spokesperson Brigadier Donald Mdhluli
said the find marked a crucial advance against drug trafficking, and that an investigation had
started to identify those behind the cultivation, who remained unidentified. [@2]
