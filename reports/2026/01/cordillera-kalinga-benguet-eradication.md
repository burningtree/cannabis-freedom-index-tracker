Police in the Cordillera region destroyed marijuana plants and products worth P30.46 million
in coordinated eradication operations in Kalinga and Benguet on 13 January 2026. [@2]

In Tinglayan, Kalinga, joint Kalinga police and PDEA teams under OPLAN MAGINAM and OPLAN
GREEN GOLD 4 destroyed 81,500 plants over about 5,200 square meters: 52,000 plants worth
P10.4 million in Barangay Loccong, 5,000 worth P1 million in Buscalan, 8,000 worth P1.6
million in Butbut Proper, 6,000 worth P1.2 million in Ngibat, and 10,500 worth P2.1 million
in Tulgao East. [@1,2] No one was apprehended as the sites were abandoned. [@1]

In Benguet the same day, teams found 23 plantations with 61,570 fully grown plants, 1,250
seedlings and 15,000 grams of dried leaves worth P14.16 million in Kibungan (Tacadang and
Poblacion) and Kapangan (Sagubo); everything was destroyed on site. [@2] Police said
investigations continue to find more plantations and identify the cultivators. [@2]
