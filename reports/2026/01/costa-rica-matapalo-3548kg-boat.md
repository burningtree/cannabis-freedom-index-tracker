On the night of Tuesday 20 January 2026, Costa Rica's Drug Control Police, Coast Guard, special
support unit and criminal intelligence directorate intercepted a fast boat roughly 40 nautical miles
southwest of Cabo Matapalo on the southern Pacific coast, with help from a US patrol aircraft and
the Drug Enforcement Administration. [@1,2]

Inside the vessel, named Monarca CP01, officers found 6,452 packages of marijuana. [@1] La Nación
reports that each package weighed about 550 grams, for a total of 3,548 kg valued at roughly
2,000 million colones at domestic prices cited by the Public Security Ministry. [@1]

Three Colombian men with surnames Cortés, Vallecillo and Carvajal were detained aboard the boat.
[@1,2] Infobae adds that seven fuel drums of about 200 litres each were seized to extend the craft's
range at sea. [@2]

The boat was towed to the Golfito Coast Guard station for inspection. [@1,2] The suspects were held
for the pre-trial process while prosecutors pursued an international drug-trafficking case under the
joint Costa Rica–United States maritime patrol framework. [@2]
