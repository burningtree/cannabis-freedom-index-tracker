On 18 January 2026 the State Border Service said an unmanned aerial vehicle that
illegally entered Azerbaijani airspace from Iran was used to smuggle narcotics
into the country. [@1,2] In the service area of the Horadiz border detachment,
border troops and the Ministry of Internal Affairs detected the UAV in time and
neutralised it before it completed its run. [@1,2]

Inspecting the scene, officers seized 6 kilograms and 600 grams of marijuana
attached to the aircraft. [@1,2] The border service said operational and
investigative measures were continuing to identify those involved in the
attempted shipment. [@1,2] News outlets published the account on 24 January
2026. [@1,2]
