On 15 January 2026 Togo burned the previous year's drug trade in one fire: more
than 5,291 kg of cannabis seized across the country in 2024, within a total of
5,729.05 kg of drugs destroyed at Agoè in Lomé.[@1]

The anti-drug office and the national anti-drug committee supervised the
operation.[@1] Security minister Calixte Batossie Madjoulba presented the
destruction as part of the government's effort to protect public health and young
people.[@1]

Cannabis dominated the pyre — beside 51.77 kg of cocaine and 8.5 kg of
methamphetamine — a share that foreshadowed the record seizures the authorities
would report for 2025.[@1]
