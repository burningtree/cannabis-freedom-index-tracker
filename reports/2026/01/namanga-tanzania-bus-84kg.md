Police in Namanga, Kajiado County, seized 84 kilogrammes of bhang valued at Sh2.5 million
from a Kenya-bound passenger bus arriving from Tanzania, arresting the driver and conductor,
both Tanzanian nationals. [@1,2]

The bus, registration number T372 DWL and operated by Kidia One Express, was intercepted at
the Namanga One Stop Border Post during routine checks by a multi-agency team of the
Anti-Narcotic Unit, Kenya Revenue Authority, Kenya Wildlife Service and the Police Dog
Unit, acting on intelligence. [@1,2] Sniffer dogs led officers to the bus's fuel chamber,
where a detailed search recovered four tightly wrapped bundles containing 84 packets of
bhang weighing 84 kilogrammes, each packet worth about Sh30,000. [@1,2]

The crew members — driver George Ngoba, 37, and conductor Trisha Lucas, 25 — were arrested
on the spot; the bus and narcotics were detained as exhibits. [@2,3] The two were helping
with investigations and were to be arraigned once complete, while detectives pursued the
main suspect believed to have been waiting in Nairobi to receive the consignment. [@2,3]
