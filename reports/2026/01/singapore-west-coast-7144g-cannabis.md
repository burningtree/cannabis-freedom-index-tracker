On 8 January 2026 Singapore's Central Narcotics Bureau (CNB) arrested a 29-year-old Singaporean
woman for suspected drug trafficking at a condominium near West Coast Way. [@1,2]

Officers entered a room by force after she refused lawful orders to let them in, then seized about
7,144 grams of cannabis, 169 grams of methamphetamine, 9 grams of ketamine, 30 grams of ecstasy,
30 Erimin-5 tablets and paraphernalia. [@1,2] CNB estimated the haul at more than S$94,900 and said
the cannabis alone could supply about 1,110 abusers for a week. [@1,2]

Investigations were continuing; under Singapore law, trafficking more than 500 grams of cannabis can
attract the mandatory death penalty. [@1,2]
