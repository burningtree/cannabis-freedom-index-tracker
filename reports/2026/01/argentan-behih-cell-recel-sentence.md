On Tuesday 27 January 2026 the Argentan criminal court tried Mohamed Behih, 24,
in absentia for receiving drugs and a mobile phone in custody at the Argentan
prison in Orne. [@1] As in earlier proceedings he refused extraction,
videoconference or to sign paperwork, and the court ruled without him. [@1]

About a year earlier, during a planned search at the prison, staff found 1.7 g
of herbal cannabis, a mobile phone and two charging cables in his cell. [@1]
It was his third seizure involving drugs in detention and his second involving
a phone; he had declined to be interviewed on the facts. [@1]

The court noted a long criminal record totalling 109 months of past sentences.
[@1] The prosecutor had asked for three months; the court imposed four months
in firm prison without adjustment. [@1]
