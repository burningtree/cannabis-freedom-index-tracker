Kuwait’s Criminal Court sentenced a Kuwaiti woman to fifteen years in prison and
fined her KD10,000 on 22 January 2026 after convicting her of cultivating,
trafficking and using hashish and psychotropic substances. [@1,2,3] The General
Department for Drug Control had put her under surveillance, raided her home, and
seized narcotics together with equipment used to grow and process drugs. [@1,2,3]

The court found she had cultivated narcotics in her residence with intent to
traffic and consume them, and it rejected defence challenges to the legality of
the search. [@1,2,3] A Kuwaiti man charged alongside her was not sentenced on
use alone because the court found no evidence he cultivated or trafficked; the
accounts describe leniency in his case. [@1,2,3] The reports opened do not name
either defendant or give precise quantities of hashish. [@1,2,3]
