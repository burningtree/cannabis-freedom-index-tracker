After a two-day trial, the Reims criminal court on 13 January 2026 convicted five men who
ran the Champagne Connection, a home-delivery cannabis network advertised on Telegram. [@1,2]

Julien Hoareau, 31, known as "le H", was sentenced to five years in prison for directing
the ring; prosecutors had sought eight years and later told L'Union they would not appeal. [@1]
His brother Thomas received three years with two firm, co-defendant Oussama Kobadi the same,
Ayoub Anebri two years, and the fifth man at most six months firm, against requisitions of up
to seven years. [@1,2] France 3 reported that only Julien Hoareau remained in custody once
time already served was counted. [@2]

Investigators estimated turnover at €865,000 from March 2019 to November 2023 and said raids
had seized 43 kg of cannabis they valued at €500,000, including more than 160 plants in a
northern Reims warehouse in June 2022. [@1,2] Deliverers were paid €100 a day and customers
identified themselves with ID copies on Snapchat, according to the court coverage. [@1,2]

Hoareau told the court he managed logistics but denied being the true boss, citing fear of
reprisals if he named those he worked for. [@1,2] Substitute prosecutor Alexandre Djindian
said the profile of educated, non-consuming defendants showed "drug trafficking can touch
anyone" when money and control are at stake. [@1]
