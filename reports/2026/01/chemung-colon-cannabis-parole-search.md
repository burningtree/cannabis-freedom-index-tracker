On 14 January 2026 parole officers on a Gun Involved Violence Elimination detail in Chemung County
found about 30 pounds of suspected cannabis while checking a parolee at a Wheaton Road residence in
the town of Erin. [@1,2]

State Police said the discovery gave probable cause for Troop E's Violent Gangs and Narcotics
Enforcement Team to search the home with Elmira police drug officers. [@1,2] Investigators seized
about 45 pounds of illicit cannabis, seven pounds of concentrated cannabis, digital scales,
packaging, a ledger, and cash consistent with drug sales. [@1,2]

Carlos L. Colon, 35, of Erin, was charged with first-degree criminal possession of cannabis, a
class D felony. [@1,2] A parole violation warrant was issued and Horseheads Village Court remanded him
to Chemung County Jail without bail. [@1,2]
