On 17 December 2025 vice officers and Kaʻū patrol executed a search warrant at a home in the
92-8600 block of Jasmine Drive in Hawaiian Oceanview Estates, Ocean View; the house was empty when
police entered. [@1,2] They recovered about 397 marijuana plants, 364 pounds (roughly 165 kg) of
processed marijuana, 19,814 grams of marijuana concentrate, 1.2 grams of cocaine and four firearms.
[@1,2] Police estimated the narcotics were worth $1,437,630 and seized more than $14,600 in cash, a
2000 Ford F-350 valued at $40,000 and a Honda utility vehicle valued at $16,000 for forfeiture.
[@1,2]

After further investigation, vice officers arrested 42-year-old Jordin Trevena-Garon of Ocean View
on 5 January 2026. [@1,2] He was charged with five counts of prohibited firearm ownership or
possession, three counts of first-degree commercial promotion of marijuana, first-degree promotion of
a harmful drug, third-degree promotion of a dangerous drug and mandatory firearms registration.
[@1,2] Bail was set at $505,000 and he was held for an initial court appearance on 6 January. [@1,2]
