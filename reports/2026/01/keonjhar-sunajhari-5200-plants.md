The Times of India reported that on Saturday 10 January 2026 a joint enforcement squad raided
eight locations near Sunajhari village in the Kanjipani section of the Bhuyan Juanga range,
Banspal block, Keonjhar district, and destroyed about 5,200 nearly mature cannabis plants.
[@1]

Divisional forest officer Dhanraj H D told the paper the destroyed crop was worth about Rs 1.2
crore on local estimates and that roughly 70 forest, excise and police personnel took part.
[@1] The report said no one was arrested and that illegal cultivation had spread into
Keonjhar from neighbouring districts. [@1]

The newspaper did not publish photographs of the eradication. [@1]
