Two decrees of 14 January 2026 set the rules for the drug tests introduced by the 2025 drug law. Job applicants in the public and private sectors must file a negative test taken within the previous three months; a positive result blocks the application until the person completes treatment, though the decree says it is not a permanent exclusion. Pupils and students can be tested during periodic health checks, or when they show behavioural problems or a reasonable suspicion of use; minors need a parent's written consent, and a refusal can be taken to a juvenile judge.
