Police in Salima destroyed about five tonnes of cannabis sativa at Senga Bay
in a continued crackdown on illegal drugs in the district. [@1] [@2]

Salima Police spokesperson Sub-Inspector Rebecca Ndiwate said the cannabis was
burned in line with provisions of the Police Act and the Dangerous Drugs Act,
explaining that the law allows the disposal of seized drugs after six months
if no suspects are arrested or claims made. [@1] The cannabis had been
intercepted during routine police operations, including roadblocks and
stop-and-search exercises along major roads. [@1]

Salima Police assured residents that enforcement operations against drug
trafficking and substance abuse would continue. [@1]
