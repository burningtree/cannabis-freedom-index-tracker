Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport customs seized 6,794.26 g of cannabis
from a transit freight shipment, Guangzhou Daily reported on 16 January 2026.
[@1,2] The cargo was declared as handmade cotton luxury curtains. [@1,2]

Officers said an X-ray image showed many bagged lumps that did not match the
declaration, so they opened the shipment and found 15 vacuum-packed bags of
brown plant material with a pungent smell. [@1,2] Laboratory testing confirmed
cannabis weighing 6,794.26 g. [@1,2] Guangzhou Daily published customs photos
of the seizure at the airport. [@1]

Customs reminded the public that smuggling or trafficking drugs is a criminal
offence in China regardless of quantity. [@1,2] The reports did not name any
suspect. [@1,2]
