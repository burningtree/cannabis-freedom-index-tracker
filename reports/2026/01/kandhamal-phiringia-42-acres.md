Odisha TV and OdishaBytes reported that a joint team of police, excise and revenue officials
destroyed illegal cannabis cultivation worth nearly Rs 40 lakh in the Phiringia area of
Kandhamal district under Operation Green Mission. [@1,2]

Both outlets said the raid targeted forest land near Kalibira, Dami and Ratang villages under
Ratang panchayat, where officers found plants spread over about 42 acres (roughly 17 hectares)
of difficult terrain. [@1,2] The teams cut the crop down and burned it on the spot. [@1,2]

Police told the outlets the plantation pointed to organised cultivation and that similar drives
would continue, with legal action promised against those involved. [@1,2] Neither report named
any arrests. [@1,2]
