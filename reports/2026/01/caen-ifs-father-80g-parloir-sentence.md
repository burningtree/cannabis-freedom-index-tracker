On 30 August 2025, at the Caen-Ifs penal centre in Calvados, prison staff found
80.6 g of cannabis resin and a mobile phone in a package that had just been handed
to a detainee by his father during a visiting-room meeting. [@1]

The man was already convicted of tobacco and drug trafficking offences. [@1] He
remained in custody when the Caen criminal court sentenced him on Monday 19 January
2026 to eight months in prison for arranging the delivery. [@1]
