On 17 January 2026 Singapore Police Force officers responding to a call for assistance at a
home near Holland Drive found suspected controlled drugs and alerted the Central Narcotics
Bureau (CNB). [@1,2]

CNB arrested a 47-year-old Singaporean man and seized about 4,113 grams of cannabis, 135
grams of methamphetamine, 31 grams of ecstasy, 40 Erimin-5 tablets, paraphernalia and
S$420,383.30 in cash from the unit. [@1,2]

Follow-up on 18 January led officers to arrest a 29-year-old Singaporean in the same area;
a search of his Choa Chu Kang home recovered ketamine, methamphetamine and ecstasy plus
S$180.20, without additional cannabis in that second search. [@1,2] CNB put the total drugs
seized across both days at more than S$123,000 and noted that trafficking above 500 grams of
cannabis can draw the mandatory death penalty. [@1,2]
