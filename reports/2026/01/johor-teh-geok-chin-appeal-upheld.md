Teh Geok Chin, a tiler then aged 47, had pleaded guilty in the Johor Bahru High Court to trafficking
1,024.60 grammes of cannabis found at an apartment on Jalan Muafakat, Larkin, at about 00:10 on
18 April 2019. [@1,2] In October 2023 the court sentenced him to 30 years in prison and 12 strokes
of the rotan, the term Malaysia uses for life imprisonment in this context. [@1,2]

On 3 January 2026 a Court of Appeal bench dismissed his appeal. [@1,2] Justice Mohamed Zaini Mazlan
held that after a voluntary guilty plea Teh could not challenge the conviction under section 305 of
the Criminal Procedure Code and might contest only the sentence. [@2] The High Court had followed the
section 173(b) procedure, reading the charge to him in Mandarin, and the appellate court found no
basis to reopen the conviction. [@1,2]

On sentence, the judge said the High Court had only two lawful options for trafficking at this weight:
the death penalty or life imprisonment with mandatory whipping, and the 30-year term was already
the minimum. [@1,2] The appeal against sentence was therefore dismissed. [@2]
