Early on 21 January 2026 the Kreis Unna police force carried out thirteen
coordinated searches in a case of unauthorised commercial cannabis cultivation
and resale. [@1,2]

Sites in Unna, Holzwickede, Werl, Altena, Neheim-Hüsten, Bergneustadt and
Wachtendonk were raided, alongside four addresses in the Netherlands handled
by Dutch officers. [@1,2] Officers seized several illegal plantations, drug
production equipment and cash. [@1,2]

WDR reported two large grows in Unna and Altena, with about 900 cannabis plants
in a Unna warehouse and another 100 in a flat; police used shipping containers
to remove the plants. [@2] In Holzwickede, where a expected third plantation
was not found, investigators still seized larger quantities of cannabis. [@2]

Six people were arrested in Germany and two more were encountered by Dutch
police. [@1] WDR said all six German suspects were brought before a judge; two
alleged ringleaders from Unna, aged 41 and 55, and two accomplices from
Arnsberg and Werl were remanded, while custody against a 45-year-old from
Holzwickede was suspended on reporting conditions. [@2] The Dortmund prosecutor's
office said enquiries were continuing. [@1]
