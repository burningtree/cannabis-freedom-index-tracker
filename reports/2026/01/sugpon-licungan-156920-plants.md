Police and PDEA agents destroyed marijuana plantations worth an estimated ₱46.8 million in the
highlands of Sugpon, Ilocos Sur, over 17 January 2026. [@1,2]

The Philippine National Police said that, with PDEA Region 1 and local units, it dismantled 37
plantations in Barangay Licungan from early afternoon until near midnight. [@1,2] The teams found
more than 32,000 square metres of cultivation with about 156,920 fully grown plants, 93,680
seedlings and 96 kilograms of dried marijuana. [@1,2]

After inventory and documentation, all material was uprooted and burned on site with samples kept
for laboratory examination. [@1,2] Acting PNP chief Jose Melencio Nartatez said the strike hit the
supply at source and promised continued monitoring to block replanting. [@1]

The Philippine Star reported the same plant, seedling and dried-leaf figures and on-site destruction
after sampling. [@2] Neither account reported any arrest on the mountain. [@1,2]
