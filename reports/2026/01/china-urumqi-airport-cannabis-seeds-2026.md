Ürümqi customs at Tian Shan Airport seized 32 cannabis seeds in the inbound
postal channel, according to a “Customs Release” WeChat notice reported by
Chinanews Xinjiang on 30 January 2026. [@1] Officers inspected mail declared as
a toy from overseas and found one box of Lego bricks with signs it had been
opened. [@1] Six assembled brick groups concealed 32 plant seeds, which
laboratory testing identified as cannabis seeds. [@1] Customs quoted article
352 of China's Criminal Law, which criminalises illegally trading, transporting,
carrying or holding non-inactivated seeds or seedlings of controlled drug plants
such as poppy in larger amounts. [@1] The notice does not report any arrest or
hand-over to prosecutors. [@1]
