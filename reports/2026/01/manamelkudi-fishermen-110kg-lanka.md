Police arrested two Indian fishermen for attempting to smuggle 110 kg of ganja to Sri
Lanka by sea from the Manamelkudi seashore in Pudukkottai district in the early hours of
Tuesday 20 January 2026. [@1]

The arrested men, A. Arokia Rahul (31) of the Anthoniyarpuram fishermen hamlet and K.
Rajeswaran alias Sivasankar (29) of Panangkudi Thope, had fished for over 10 years. [@1]
Rahul's maternal uncle S. Stephen Raj (52) of Sayalkudi allegedly contacted Rahul and
asked him to shift two bags of ganja by boat. [@1] The duo were detained at the
Manamelkudi coastal police station while investigations continued. [@1]
