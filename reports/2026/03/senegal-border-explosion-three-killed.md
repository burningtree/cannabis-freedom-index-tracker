Three Senegalese soldiers died and three more were wounded in an accidental
explosion during cannabis clearing in the Casamance region on Tuesday, March
17, 2026. [@1]

The Armed Forces said the soldiers met their fate while tracking down and
neutralising armed individuals, combating cannabis cultivation and securing
the population; the explosion occurred early Tuesday during operations to
destroy cannabis fields along the border with The Gambia, and the wounded
were evacuated for treatment. [@1]

The blast came a week into an operation that had already neutralized several
members of armed groups engaged in cannabis cultivation, days after one
soldier was killed and six wounded in the Kadialock clash. [@1]
