On Friday 27 March 2026, SGAIA agents of Guatemala’s national civil police eradicated 98,967
marijuana plants in mountainous terrain at caserío Ixcoch, Las Cruces municipality, Petén,
after investigation and surveillance in the area. [@1,2]

The Interior Ministry valued the destroyed plants at more than 49,483,500 quetzales (about
6.34 million US dollars at the figure cited). [@1] La Red 106.1, summarising the same
operation, said the plants were in two fields near Ixcoch. [@2]

Mingob said the eradication followed security protocols and gave no count of arrests or named
suspects. [@1]
