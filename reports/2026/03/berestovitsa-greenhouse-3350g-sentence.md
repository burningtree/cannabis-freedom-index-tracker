Berestovitsa District Court convicted a local man of illegally sowing and growing cannabis and of possessing marijuana without intent to sell, BELTA reported on 6 March 2026. [@1,2]

From May to October 2025 he had grown 15 cannabis plants in a greenhouse on his homestead, dried the harvest and kept at least 3,350.8931 grams of marijuana in his house and outbuildings until police seized it. [@1,2]

The court gave him one year and six months' restriction of liberty for cultivation and two years for possession, combined into two years and six months to be served in an open correctional institution. [@1,2] The judgment could still be appealed. [@1]
