On the night of 11 March 2026, continuing into the early morning of the 12th,
prosecutors and FELCN, Bolivia's special anti-drug force, went about a
kilometre and a half into the community of Machacamarca, near Laguna
Lagunillas, in Rafael Bustillo province, Potosí. [@1] They found and burned
36 greenhouses of cannabis, all of them irrigated. [@1,2]

Aldo Morales Alconini, the departmental prosecutor in Oruro, said the plants
in their natural state came to about 12 tonnes and 6,000 kilograms, 12,600
kilograms. [@1] Speaking later, he described more than 12 tonnes of plants
two and a half to three metres tall and ready to cut. [@2] The greenhouses
were 12 large ones of 50 by 20 metres, 15 medium ones of 30 by 15 metres, and
9 small ones of 20 by 10 metres. [@1,2] The irrigation systems were burned
with the plants. [@1]

Morales said the case is being investigated as trafficking in controlled
substances in larger volumes. [@1] On 16 March he said, on Bolivia TV, that
people from the community had thrown sticks of dynamite at the officers at
the end of the operation. [@2] He also said the landowners of the plots had
been identified. [@2]
