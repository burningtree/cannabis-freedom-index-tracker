Detectives of the anti-drug brigades (Brianco) of Talca and Linares, with the air police brigade of
Chile's investigative police (PDI), seized 1,778 cannabis plants at cultivation sites in the Maule
region, results the PDI announced on 11 March 2026 under Plan Cannabis 2026. [@1,2]

In San Clemente the Talca brigade found three plots in rural and precordilleran sectors holding
1,070 plants between 80 centimetres and 2.5 metres tall, watered and still growing. [@1,2] In Retiro
the Linares brigade found two sites with 243 plants, between 1.5 and 3.5 metres tall, and a barrel
holding 4,346 grams of bulk marijuana, and in Longaví a further 465 plants between 1.8 and three
metres. [@1,2]

The plantations lay in ravines and thickly vegetated land chosen to escape detection, and their
keepers had installed drip irrigation, pools to store water and screens of vegetation around the
plots to protect the growth cycle until harvest. [@1,2]

The prosecutor's office ordered that everything seized be sent to the Maule health service for
analysis and destruction. [@1,2] No arrest was reported in either account of the operation. [@1,2]
