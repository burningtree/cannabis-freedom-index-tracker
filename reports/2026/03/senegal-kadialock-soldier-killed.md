One Senegalese soldier was killed and six others wounded on Thursday, March
12, 2026, after troops clashed with suspected fighters linked to the
Movement of Democratic Forces of Casamance during an operation to destroy
cannabis farms in southern Senegal. [@1][@2]

According to the Armed Forces' Directorate of Information and Public
Relations, a detachment operating under Military Zone No. 5 encountered armed
individuals in the Kadialock area of North Sindian, Bignona department, near
the Gambian border, while destroying fields of chanvre indien. [@1][@2]
Several attackers were neutralized in the exchange, and units continued
operations to dismantle plantations and secure local populations. [@1][@2]
