A Freedom of Information request found that Israel Police issued no administrative fines for personal cannabis possession or use between 2022 and 2025, after thousands in 2020–21. Police said the enforcement framework for the 2022 regulations was still being designed, leaving the fine scheme on the books but unused. [@1]
