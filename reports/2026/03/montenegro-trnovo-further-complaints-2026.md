Police filed supplementary criminal complaints against two men from Zeta, aged 42 and 49, on suspicion of drug production and supply linked to the Trnovo cannabis plantation.[@1] Investigators cited forensic examinations, analysis of traces and other collected evidence; three earlier suspects had already been processed.[@1] The case began with the discovery of about 13,100 plants near Bar in August 2025.[@2] [@1,2]
