A road crash in Techiman led to the arrest of a 48-year-old tricycle rider after police
uncovered a large stash of suspected narcotics hidden inside bags of charcoal. [@1,2]

Robert Tannor was riding an unregistered Motor King tricycle, an aboboyaa, carrying 14
bags of charcoal when it collided with a stationary Kia Rhino truck near Methodist Park
at about 2pm on 8 March 2026. [@1,2] Officers responding to the crash first found the
charcoal bags, but a closer search revealed the bags concealed 397 compressed slabs of
dried plant material believed to be cannabis, all retrieved at the scene. [@1,2]

Tannor suffered serious leg injuries and was taken to Holy Family Hospital, where he
remains under police guard while investigations continue. [@1,2] The Kia truck driver
fled after the crash and police are appealing for help to trace him. [@1,2]
