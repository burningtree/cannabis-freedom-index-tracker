On Monday 9 March 2026, a patrol from the Gendarmeria's territorial prevention and control unit stopped a vehicle during a road check. [@1,2] Officers found the driver in possession of a controlled substance. [@1,2]

With help from the operational and judicial police unit, they searched the vehicle and the man's home and seized 90 grams of marijuana in total. [@1,2] The drugs were confiscated and he was referred to the competent judicial authority for the measures provided by law. [@1,2]
