Authorities destroyed two plantations and uprooted PHP10.3 million worth of marijuana plants
in an inter-agency operation in Sulu province on Tuesday 3 March 2026, officials said.
[@1]

According to the Philippine News Agency, the Army's 1102nd Infantry Brigade commander
reported that the two plantations in Barangay Masjid Punjungan of Kalingalan Caluang town
were destroyed that day: the first site covered about 1,000 square meters with 5,500 fully
grown plants, and the second covered 2,000 square meters with 6,000 plants — 11,500 plants
across 3,000 square meters worth PHP10,350,000 in total. [@1]

The plants were uprooted and burned on site by joint operatives of the 1102nd Brigade,
PDEA-Sulu and police forces, and PDEA is investigating to identify the owners of the two
plantations. [@1] The operation was the second of its kind that year in Kalingalan Caluang,
following the destruction of P18.2 million of marijuana at Masjid Punjungan and Pitogo on
13 February. [@1]

The Inquirer headline for this operation gives the same P10.3M figure, though its article
page could not be opened. [@2]
