Police in Maharashtra's Beed district seized nearly 22 kilograms of ganja
from a farm and arrested a woman in connection with the case, officials said
on Thursday. [@1] Acting on a specific tip-off, a police team carried out a
raid in Lonwal village of Wadwani tehsil on Wednesday, where officials found
cannabis plants being cultivated for commercial purposes on agricultural
land. [@1] [@2]

The police uprooted the plants on the spot and seized 21.960 kilograms of
green ganja, valued at approximately Rs 5.49 lakh, an official said. [@1]
[@2] The accused, Ashabai Shingare, 43, who owns the land, was arrested from
the site. [@1]

A case was registered against her under relevant provisions of the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] Police said the
investigation was ongoing to determine whether the accused was acting alone
or had links to a wider drug supply network, and whether the produce was
intended for local distribution or supply to other regions. [@1] [@2]
