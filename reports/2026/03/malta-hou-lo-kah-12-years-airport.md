Malaysian national Hou Lo Kah, 33, was jailed for 12 years and fined €20,000 after admitting he
imported cannabis through Malta International Airport. [@1,2]

Customs officers stopped him on 18 March 2025 when he arrived from Dubai with a suitcase later found
to hold 28 packets of cannabis weighing 13.984 kilogrammes, with about 16% THC and an estimated
street value of €153,833. [@1] He pleaded guilty on 16 March 2026 before Mr Justice Neville Camilleri,
who also ordered him to pay €6,465.85 in expert costs within two years. [@1,2]

The court heard he carried only a small backpack of clothes besides the drug-filled suitcase and
that he cooperated with investigators after arrest. [@1]
