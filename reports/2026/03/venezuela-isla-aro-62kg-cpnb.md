Officers of the Anti-Drug Division and the Aquatic Spaces Police Division of
the Bolivarian National Police seized 62 kilograms and 811 grams of marijuana
on Isla El Aro, in Angostura del Orinoco municipality, on Sunday, March 1,
2026. [@1]

The operation took place in the Boca de Aro sector after local residents
alerted officers to suspicious movements of curiara-type boats coming from the
settlements of the upper Orinoco River. [@1] Acting on that community
intelligence, officers located the load distributed in four sacks containing
163 wraps of the illicit substance. [@1]
