Under Operation Trinetra in Salumbar district, Lasadiya police uncovered
illegal ganja cultivation amid a wheat crop and seized 624 plants weighing
over 120 kg from the fields. [@1]

Acting on information about illegal cultivation, the police raided a remote
area of Bor Fala in Aggad village under the guidance of senior officers.
During the operation, large numbers of 2-to-3-foot ganja plants, already
flowering and budding, were found standing amid the wheat crop; the plants
were uprooted under NDPS Act provisions. [@1] The local patwari, Khartilal
Meena, was called to the spot and identified the field as belonging to Rupa,
son of Kheta Meena, resident of Aggad Bor Fala. [@1]

The field owner fled during the police action, according to station officer
Bharatsingh Rajpurohit; a total of 624 plants weighing 120.85 kg were seized,
a case was registered against the owner under the NDPS Act, and a search for
him began with assistance from the DST team. [@1] Salumbar district police
also issued helpline number 8764854809 for tips on narcotics cultivation and
transport, promising to keep informants' identities confidential. [@1]
