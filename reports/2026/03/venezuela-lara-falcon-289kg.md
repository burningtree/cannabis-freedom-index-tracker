On Friday 27 March 2026, the Special Operations Group (GOES), supported by the National Anti-Drug Command of the Bolivarian National Guard, carried out procedures in Barquisimeto, Lara state, and in Punto Fijo, Falcón state. [@1] In Barquisimeto, officers detained five people and seized 77 packages of marijuana weighing 46.75 kg. [@1] In Punto Fijo, one man was detained after officers found 446 packages weighing 242.75 kg. [@1]

The ministry put the combined seizure at 523 packages weighing 289.50 kg, with six people detained. [@1] Officers also held two vehicles, six mobile phones and a satellite positioning device as evidence. [@1] It published photographs of the packages laid out in a courtyard and of the phones and vehicles held as evidence. [@1]

The case was handed to the Public Prosecutor's office for investigation. [@1] The ministry presented the deployment as part of the government's campaign against drug trafficking and said it showed its determination to keep drugs off the streets. [@1]
