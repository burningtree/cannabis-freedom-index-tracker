Police and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency destroyed marijuana valued at 16.182 million
pesos across 18 plantations in Ilocos Sur during operations on 6 and 7 March 2026, the Philippine
National Police said in a statement reported on 9 March. [@1,2]

Officers cleared about 13,000 square metres of mountain plantations, uprooting and burning 63,000
fully grown plants along with 27.7 kilograms of dried stalks and one kilogram of dried fruiting
tops, GMA News reported. [@1] Abante gave the same figures and dates. [@2] Authorities kept
samples of plants and stalks for laboratory examination. [@1]

No cultivators were arrested during the eradication. [@1] National police chief Jose Melencio
Nartatez Jr. said the operation showed the force would keep enforcing drug laws wherever
plantations were found. [@1,2]
