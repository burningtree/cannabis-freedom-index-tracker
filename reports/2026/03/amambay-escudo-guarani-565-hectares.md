En Linea Noticias reported that an inter-agency drive under Operation Escudo
Guaraní eradicated 565 hectares of presumed cannabis in Amambay department
between 4 and 18 March 2026. [@1]

Prosecutor Rossana Coronel of the Pedro Juan Caballero anti-drug unit led the
work with Paraguay’s National Anti-Drug Secretariat (SENAD), CODI joint-task
troops and legal oversight, and Brazil’s Polícia Federal accompanied the
raids. [@1] The same outlet said teams destroyed cultivation, drying and storage
sites in the rural colonies of Estrella, San Luis, Tres Palos, Timbori and San
José and dismantled 34 precarious camps used for production. [@1]

An earlier En Linea dispatch on 16 March described part of the same campaign
around Timbori and Colonia Estrella, where CODI units reported 60 hectares
destroyed and four camps torn down, illustrating the phased incursions that
preceded the published Amambay total. [@2]

Neither report named any arrests. [@1,2]
