On Monday 30 March 2026 the Paphos Permanent Criminal Court found a 24-year-old man guilty of
illegal import of cannabis and of illegal possession with intent to supply, and imposed concurrent
prison sentences of 10 years. [@1,2]

The case began on 10 June 2025 at Paphos Airport, where Customs officers working with members of
YKAN, Cyprus's drug police, stopped the man after he arrived on a flight from another European
country. [@2] They found a gross quantity of 16 kilograms and 776 grams of cannabis; laboratory
tests later put the net weight at 16 kilograms and 3 grams. [@2] Police arrested him and seized the
drugs. [@2] The Paphos district unit of YKAN investigated the case. [@2]
