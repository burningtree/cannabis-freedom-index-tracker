Police entered a business hall on the Pol in Losser on the afternoon of 6 March 2026 and found a
working cannabis farm with more than 1,000 plants. [@1,2] They were acting on an anonymous tip to
Meld Misdaad Anoniem. [@1,2]

The building had illegally tapped electricity and water, and an initial investigation showed the
plants had been harvested several times. [@1,2] Specialists from the police's forensic investigation
and national dismantling support unit removed the installation. [@1,2]

No suspect had been arrested when police published the statement on 7 March. [@1] Police said they
were investigating who was responsible for the farm and who had been involved. [@1,2]
