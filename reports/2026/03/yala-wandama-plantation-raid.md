A suspect was arrested by Buttala Police during an operation in the Yala National Park,
where a firearm and cannabis valued at over 5 million rupees were seized. [@1]

Officers from the Buttala Police Station conducted a special raid in the Wandama area of
the park and discovered a well-maintained cannabis plantation containing approximately
13,500 plants. [@1] Police put the value of the crop at over 5 million rupees, and in
addition to the narcotics found a locally manufactured firearm in the suspect's
possession. [@1]

The suspect, a resident of the Kuda Oya area, is to be produced before the Wellawaya
Magistrate's Court. [@1] All confiscated cannabis plants were destroyed by fire, and
Buttala Police are continuing further investigations. [@1]
