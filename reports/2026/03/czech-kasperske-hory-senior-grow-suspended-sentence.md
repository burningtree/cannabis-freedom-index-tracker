He used the drug for relief and told the court he did not know how to obtain it
legally, so he began growing it himself. [@1] The Klatovy District Court
sentenced him to 10 months in prison, suspended for 20 months. [@1] The seized
cannabis and the grow equipment were forfeited to the state, and the verdict is
final. [@1] He told Klatovský deník he would not have survived being jailed
among murderers. [@2]

According to the indictment the grow was in the attic of a rented family house
and ran from at least the first half of 2023 until the end of April 2024, when
police detained him. [@1] He ran several growing cycles, and police seized part
of the harvest. [@1] During the raid officers found almost nine kilograms of
dried cannabis. [@1] Investigators also looked at suspected dealing, but
distribution could not be proved, so the court dealt with growing and possession
for personal use, and the defendant did not deny the charge. [@1]

Had the case been decided a year earlier, he would have faced eight to twelve
years in prison, Klatovský deník reported. [@2] A change to the criminal code
effective 1 January 2026 introduced separate treatment of growing and handling
drugs for personal use, and the court closed the case under a provision with a
lower maximum penalty. [@1] Under the new rules, growing up to three plants for
personal use is legal for adults over 21, four to five plants are an
administrative offence, and larger numbers are a crime. [@1] He now lives in
Železná Ruda, is registered at the labour office and does odd jobs, and told the
court he now has cannabis prescribed by a doctor and his health has improved.
