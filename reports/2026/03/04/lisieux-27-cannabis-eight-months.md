On Wednesday 4 March 2026 the Lisieux criminal court tried a 27-year-old man in fast-track
proceedings and sentenced him to eight months in prison without suspension for cannabis
possession. [@1]

Ouest-France reported that the court sent him for immediate trial because of a series of recent
drug-linked offences: convictions on 24 April 2025 for driving under the influence of drugs, on
26 June 2025 for driving without a licence or insurance while refusing to stop, and on 1 July 2025
for another drugged driving case with refusal to comply. [@1] The newspaper did not give the
quantity of cannabis or the defendant’s name. [@1]
