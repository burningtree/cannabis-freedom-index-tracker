Between 25 and 31 March 2026 the District of Columbia's Alcoholic Beverage and Cannabis
Administration (ABCA) and Metropolitan Police Department padlocked three unlicensed cannabis
retailers and arrested three people. [@1,2]

Closure orders hit Treehouse DC at 1922 I Street NW on 25 March, Pressure Deliveries at 3417 23rd
Street SE on 26 March, and Level Up Smoke & Tobacco Shop at 1938 9th Street NW on 31 March. [@1]
ABCA said each shop posed an imminent danger through illegal sales; the wave brought to 107 the
number of illegal cannabis businesses padlocked since civil closure powers took effect in July 2024.
[@1]

At Treehouse, agents seized 14 pounds of marijuana plus THC edibles, liquids, resin and vape
cartridges, and arrested Patrick Evans, 33, of Alexandria, Virginia. [@1] Pressure Deliveries yielded
10.2 pounds of marijuana and assorted THC products; Rashad Davis, 33, of Waldorf, Maryland, was
arrested. [@1] At Level Up, police seized 3.2 pounds of marijuana, THC wax, two semi-automatic
weapons and ammunition, and arrested Estepahnous Futsum, 42, of Northwest Washington. [@1] WJLA,
citing ABCA, put total marijuana seized across the three sites at 27.4 pounds alongside more than
11 kilogrammes of THC edibles and other concentrates. [@2]
