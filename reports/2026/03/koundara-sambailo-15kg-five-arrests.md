On Monday 9 March 2026 the Brigade spéciale d'intervention de la police (BSIP) in
Koundara searched a general-goods shop in Sambailo sub-prefecture during a patrol and
found cannabis hidden inside. [@1,2]

Police said they seized 15 kg of cannabis and arrested five people, one of them a woman.
[@1,2] The detainees were taken to Koundara central commissariat for investigation. [@1,2]

Both outlets published the national police account of the operation, which police said
formed part of Director General Djénaba Sory Camara's nationwide drug enforcement drive. [@1,2]
The statements invite the public to report tips on the green 117 hotline. [@1,2]
