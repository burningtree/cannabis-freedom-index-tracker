In March 2026 the State Police detained four people in Rīga over the acquisition, storage and
large-scale sale of marijuana and hashish, and over involving a minor in the trade. [@1,2]

On 17 and 18 March officers from the Rīga criminal police, with technical support units, picked up
the group. [@1,2] Police said one man born in 1993 organised sales through meetings and hideouts,
another man of the same birth year was caught at a storage site, and a man born in 2005 was
arrested in a Pierīga forest immediately after taking marijuana from a hideout. [@1,2]

A teenager born in 2009 was used to hand drugs to buyers, police said. [@1,2] Officers arrested him
after he tried to leave his home with 1.2 kilograms of packaged marijuana, vacuum-sealing equipment
and related items in a suitcase. [@1,2]

Searches yielded 9 kilograms of marijuana, 50 grams of hashish, €11,671 and Volkswagen, Toyota and
BMW vehicles. [@1,2] Criminal proceedings were opened for organised large-scale drug dealing and for
involving a minor in crime; police noted that guilt must still be proved in court. [@1,2]
