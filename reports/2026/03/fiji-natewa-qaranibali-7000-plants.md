The Operation Sasamaki team in Fiji's Northern Division seized more than 7,000
green plants believed to be marijuana from a farm in the Qaranibali farming area
at Vusasivo, Natewa. [@1] The raid took place on the morning of 25 March 2026.
[@3] No one had been arrested three months later, police said. [@4]

Some of the plants stood more than four metres tall. [@2] The seized plants were
to be sent for analysis, police said. [@1] A drone located the plantation and
led officers to it. [@1] Police described the plants as believed to be marijuana
rather than confirmed cannabis. [@1]

On 17 June 2026, police spokesperson Ana Naisoro said investigations were
continuing and that enforcement operations, including drone surveillance, had
been intensified in areas known for cultivation. [@4] She said drones had played
a key role in locating illicit farms and supporting operational efforts on the
ground. [@4] "Drones have always been used to comb areas known for cultivation,
and this has significantly assisted operational efforts," she said. [@4]
