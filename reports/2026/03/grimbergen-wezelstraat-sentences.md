On 5 March 2026, Het Laatste Nieuws reported that the Brussels criminal court had jailed four men over a cannabis plantation in a villa on Wezelstraat in Grimbergen. [@1] An unlicensed estate agent who had rented the house received 36 months. [@1] Two Albanian men also received 36 months, and a third received 24 months. [@1]

Police had started watching the house after neighbours reported suspicious activity. [@1] On 22 August they moved in. [@1] The paper said two suspects were stopped in a car and a third was arrested in the villa. [@1] The search found a professional installation of 750 cannabis plants in six growing rooms, and 20 kg of harvested cannabis. [@1] The agent had rented the house with a false identity and a made-up email address. [@1] He had a previous drug conviction and denied any part in the plantation. [@1] The judge found his part proved. [@1]

The Halle-Vilvoorde prosecutor's office, speaking the day after the raid, said the search on Thursday 22 August found an active plantation in a rented house on Wezelstraat. [@2] Spokesperson Ingrid Moriau said officers found 765 young cannabis plants and about 20 kg of harvested cannabis, some of it already packed, in six growing rooms. [@2] The plants and the harvest were seized and destroyed, and civil protection was to dismantle the installation. [@2] Officers arrested three suspects, two of them in the villa, to be brought before an investigating judge. [@2]
