Police across Spain, Bulgaria and Greece arrested 21 people on 23 March 2026 in a
coordinated strike against a network Europol said swapped firearms from the Western
Balkans and Türkiye for large-scale cannabis trafficking. [@1,2]

Spanish Mossos d'Esquadra and National Police, with Europol, detained 18 suspects in
Spain — 14 in Catalonia at Teià, Barcelona, Mataró, Sabadell, Tordera and Lloret de
Mar, and four in Málaga — while Bulgaria arrested three including a Turkish national
described as the group's leader. [@1,2] Investigators said the organisation sourced
marijuana in Catalonia and Málaga for export toward Türkiye and Greece, using
imported weapons as payment in drug deals. [@1,2]

Seizures on the action day included seven semi-automatic pistols, a war-grade weapon,
silencers and suppressors, 587.56 kg of marijuana and 76.54 kg of hashish; Europol
estimated the drugs' illicit market value at EUR 4.4 million. [@1,2] The Mossos said
the inquiry remained open and further arrests were possible. [@2]
