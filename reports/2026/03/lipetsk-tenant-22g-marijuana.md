On 3 March 2026 officers of Lipetsk police station No. 8 detained a
31-year-old local man who had stored narcotics in a rented flat, 48.ru
reported. [@1] The landlord told police after inspecting the property that
the tenant was keeping drugs there. [@1]

During a search officers seized plant-based narcotics that expert analysis
identified as more than 22 grams of marijuana, along with scales and items
used to consume banned substances. [@1] Gorod48 quoted the regional Interior
Ministry as saying the landlord reported the find when he checked the flat
he was renting out. [@2] The tenant, who had previously been convicted on drug
charges, said he kept the marijuana for personal use. [@1]

An investigator opened a criminal case under part 1 of article 228 of the
Criminal Code, which carries up to three years' imprisonment. [@2]
