On Wednesday 4 March 2026, agents of SGAIA, the anti-narcotics analysis unit of Guatemala’s
national civil police, located and destroyed 35,232 marijuana plants in growing phase at
caserío Ixcoch in the municipality of Las Cruces, Petén. [@1,2]

The Interior Ministry put the illegal crop’s estimated value at 17,616,000 quetzales (about
2.26 million US dollars at the rate cited in the release). [@1,2] AGN reported that officers
destroyed the plants on site under standard eradication protocols. [@2]

Neither release names arrests, cultivators or camp dismantled beyond the plantation itself. [@1,2]
