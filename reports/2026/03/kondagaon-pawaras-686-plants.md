Police arrested two men on 27 March 2026. [@3] Cannabis plants, called ganja in
India, were growing hidden among maize crops in Pawaras village, in the Farsgaon
police station area of Kondagaon district, Chhattisgarh. [@1] The plants had
grown three to four feet tall, and Farsgaon sub-divisional police officer
Abhinav Upadhyay said the fields had been cultivated for several months. [@2]

Police acted on a tip-off that the two men were growing cannabis in their maize
fields. [@3] The police team searched the fields with revenue department
officials. [@3] Abhiram Yadav, 28, had 237 plants weighing about 14.96 kg in his
field, and Shravan Kumar Uike, 38, had 449 plants weighing 6.40 kg. [@3] Police
valued the seized plants at about 10 lakh rupees, or 1 million rupees. [@2] Both
were charged under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@3]

Upadhyay said both accused were habitual ganja smokers who had planted the crop
for their own use, and that the initial investigation found no commercial use.
[@1] Police said they were still investigating who else was involved and whether
a larger gang was behind the cultivation. [@1] Former cabinet minister Mohan
Markam said opium and ganja were flourishing in the state and called on the home
minister and chief minister to resign. [@1]
