On Saturday 7 March 2026, around 8 a.m., Bureau of Customs officers at the Port of Clark inspected
three Chinese nationals who had arrived from Bangkok and were due to continue to Hong Kong. [@1]
During profiling and X-ray screening of their hand-carry luggage, officers found suspicious images
and opened the bags. [@2]

Inside they recovered 99 transparent vacuum-sealed packs of dried leaves and fruiting tops believed
to be high-grade marijuana (kush). [@1,2] The Bureau of Customs said the packs weighed about
15,470 grams, with an estimated standard value of 69,799,950 Philippine pesos. [@2] PDEA K9 units
confirmed the presence of illegal drugs. [@2]

The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency's Region 3 office named the suspects only as alias Ang See,
39; alias Kim Tan, 27; and alias Li Cheng, 30. [@1] PDEA Central Luzon took them into custody for
tactical investigation, and the drugs were to be sent to the Region 3 laboratory for examination.
[@1] The operation involved the customs port, PDEA, the national police drug enforcement group,
aviation security, and the National Bureau of Investigation in Pampanga. [@1,2]

The men were informed of their rights and arrested for violating Republic Act 9165, the Comprehensive
Dangerous Drugs Act. [@2] A warrant of seizure and detention was issued by the district collector.
