About 8.50am on Wednesday 18 March 2026, officers attached to the Hume Police
District executed a search warrant at a home on Spring Creek Road, Young, as
part of an ongoing investigation into drug supply in the area. [@2] During the
search, police found several rooms converted to grow cannabis and seized 40
cannabis plants, 3.5 kilograms of loose cannabis leaf and 2.6 grams of
methylamphetamine for forensic examination. [@1,2]

Police arrested a 44-year-old man at the property and took him to Young Police
Station. [@2] He was charged with enhanced indoor cultivation of plants for
commercial purposes, possessing cannabis in an amount above indictable and
below commercial quantity, knowingly allowing use of premises as a drug
premises, diverting or using electricity for a generating system without
authority, and two counts of possessing a prohibited drug. [@1] He was refused
bail to appear before Bail Division Local Court 5 on Thursday 19 March 2026.
[@2]

The Canberra Times reported the search happened just before 9am and published
NSW Police photographs of the alleged grow rooms. [@1] Both outlets noted a
second raid later the same day at another Young address, which is not part of
this report. [@1,2]
