From roughly 10 to 20 April 2026, Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat and Brazil's Federal
Police concluded Operation Nueva Alianza 54 in Canindeyú, with Paraguayan air force helicopters,
prosecutors and shared border responsibility. [@1,2]

SENAD said teams eradicated 95 hectares of cannabis in growth and harvest stages, dismantled seven
narco camps used for production and storage, and incinerated 6,300 kilograms of marijuana ready for
distribution inside those sites. [@1,2]

Combining cleared fields and destroyed processed stock, the agencies estimated about 291 tonnes of
cannabis kept from the illicit market, which they valued at roughly USD 43.5 million in losses to
traffickers at Brazilian destination prices. [@1,2] Work focused on the Morombí, Pindó y 7 Montes
natural reserve. [@1,2]
