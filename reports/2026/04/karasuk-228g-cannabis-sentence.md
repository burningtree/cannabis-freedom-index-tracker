Karasuk district court in Novosibirsk Oblast sentenced a Karasuk man who
already had a criminal record to three years and six months in a general-regime
penal colony for illegal acquisition and storage of a large amount of cannabis
without intent to sell, Novaya Sibir reported, citing the court press service
on 14 April 2026. [@1] The court press office said that on 25 July 2025 he
picked 228.6 grams of wild cannabis tops near highway 50 K-33, packed them in
four plastic bags and stored them at home for personal use. [@1]

On 9 September 2025 police searched his flat and seized 228.6 grams of dried
marijuana. [@1] He told investigators he used cannabis to stimulate songwriting
and guitar playing. [@2] MK Novosibirsk added that he partially admitted guilt
under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal Code and that the court added an
unexpired term from an earlier sentence to reach the final three-and-a-half-year
term. [@2]

The articles said the verdict could still be appealed. [@1]
