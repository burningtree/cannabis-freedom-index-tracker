The Railway Protection Force of the Northeast Frontier Railway seized nearly 60
kilograms of ganja, a form of cannabis, in a drive against trafficking through
its stations. [@2] Three people accused of moving it were apprehended and handed
to the Government Railway Police for further action. [@2] Two of them were held
at Agartala railway station in Tripura on 21 April 2026. [@3] The third was
Delwar Hossain, 26, of Nurul Haque Chowmuhani under Sonamura police station in
Sepahijala district. [@4] He was apprehended at the station's parking area on
the afternoon of 22 April. [@4] A search of his backpack found 6.005 kilograms
of ganja. [@4] The Government Railway Police registered a case against him under
the NDPS Act, the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, at Agartala
GRPS police station. [@4]

The checks ran from 20 to 22 April 2026. [@1] At New Jalpaiguri railway station
in West Bengal on 20 April, officers found about 18 kilograms worth Rs 9 lakh
and 13.8 kilograms worth about Rs 6.9 lakh in two separate checks. [@3] Nobody
was held over either consignment. [@2] At Agartala on 21 April, a joint
operation with the GRP seized 21.48 kilograms valued by the RPF at about Rs
10.74 lakh. [@1] On 22 April, a further 6 kilograms, valued by the RPF at close
to Rs 3 lakh, was seized at the same station. [@1] The three days produced
around 60 kilograms of ganja, which the RPF valued at about Rs 29 lakh. [@1]

The RPF said it had stepped up surveillance and intelligence-based operations to
prevent the misuse of railway premises and trains for trafficking. [@3] It said
such drives would continue. [@3]
