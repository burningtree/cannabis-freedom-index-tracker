About 8.35pm on Tuesday 14 April 2026, Kalgoorlie police, assisted by
Kalgoorlie detectives, executed a search warrant at a home on Genovese Way,
Somerville. [@1] Police allege several rooms inside the home had been
converted to grow hydroponic cannabis, and that more than 350 cannabis plants
and more than half a kilogram of cannabis plant material were located and
seized. [@1,2]

Western Power attended after officers identified an allegedly unlawful bypass
of the electrical supply, and hazards had to be made safe before police could
enter and search the property. [@1,2] A 53-year-old Somerville man was
charged with cultivating a prohibited plant with intent to sell or supply and
possession of prohibited drugs with intent to sell or supply (cannabis). [@1]
He appeared before Kalgoorlie Magistrates Court on 15 April 2026, was granted
bail, and is next due to appear on Monday 25 May 2026. [@1] ABC identified
him as Robert Lee Wills, reported he did not enter pleas, and said WA Police
confirmed the investigation remained ongoing when asked about further charges.
[@2]

Detective Senior Sergeant Jason Skuse, Kalgoorlie detectives officer in
charge, attended the scene as police catalogued evidence. [@2] Neighbours
told ABC a large backyard greenhouse was visible from the street and that
about a dozen police and forensic staff worked through much of the following
day. [@2]
