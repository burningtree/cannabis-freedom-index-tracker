Detectives in České Budějovice caught a man in the act during a cannabis sale in
early April 2026. [@1] The man was 62 years old. [@2] Detectives called him
"Doutník", Czech for cigar. [@1] He sold cannabis from a bench in a park in the
centre of České Budějovice, where buyers came to him. [@1] Most of his customers
were juvenile users, who paid and carried the cannabis away in small bags. [@1]
Police believe he obtained the cannabis outside the regional capital and
distributed it onwards. [@1]

Police detained the suspect and a search of his home found a larger amount of
cannabis. [@1] Police said he had more than ten regular customers. [@1] One of
them bought about 100,000 korun worth of cannabis from him over at least half a
year. [@1] The force said the trade brought him hundreds of thousands of crowns.
[@1]

He was charged with unauthorized production and other handling of cannabis,
known in Czech as nedovolená výroba a jiné nakládání s konopím. [@1] Because he
also sold cannabis to children and on a larger scale, the offence carries five
to ten years in prison. [@1] Police are investigating him at liberty rather than
in custody. [@1] Police spokesperson Štěpánka Schwarzová said he made little
effort to hide his business. [@2] Jihočeská televize reported that he sold to
minors and that he faces up to ten years. [@3]
