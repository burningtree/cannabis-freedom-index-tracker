Police are looking for one person of interest in their investigation of what
they say is their largest weed bust ever: a half ton of suspected cannabis
packed in 29 bags with an estimated street value of $1.6 million. [@1]

A joint operation by the Gang Intelligence Investigation and Interdiction
Unit, the Anti-Narcotics Unit and the K-9 Unit at an apartment building in
Lords Bank Village at 5:30 Wednesday morning, April 8, 2026, led to the
discovery of the 29 bags of suspected high-grade cannabis weighing 1,176
pounds, along with a loaded 9mm pistol holding 12 rounds. [@1][@2]

No one was present at the property when officers arrived, and the residents
were placed in protective custody while police sought the person of interest.
