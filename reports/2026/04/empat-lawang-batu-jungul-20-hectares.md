The narcotics directorate of the South Sumatra regional police, with Empat Lawang district
police, ran a joint operation on Friday 24 April 2026 that dismantled a 20-hectare cannabis
field at Batu Jungul village in Muara Pinang district, Empat Lawang regency. [@1,2]

Police said the case grew from work since February 2026 mapping a distribution network that
reached Java. [@1,2] They arrested the main suspect, with initials PD alias Pinhar, at a
bus counter on Gubernur H. Bastari street in Palembang; cannabis on him led investigators
to the Batu Jungul site, where they found a large active field and dried cannabis ready for
sale. [@1,2]

Officers seized 220 kilograms of dried cannabis in 11 large sacks, four motorcycles, land
ownership papers and maps of the plantation, according to director of narcotics investigations
Senior Commissioner Yulian Perdana. [@1,2] Police said the suspect had run planting and
distribution since 2024 across Empat Lawang, Palembang and Java, and that four other people
in the network were wanted. [@1]

Empat Lawang police chief Abdul Aziz Septiadi said breaking the 20-hectare field and holding
the alleged owner was meant to stop supply at the source. [@1]
