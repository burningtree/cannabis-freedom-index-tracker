Police arrested 15 drug suspects, including 12 high-value individuals, in a
series of anti-drug operations across the Cordillera on 18 and 19 April 2026.
[@1] The operations covered Baguio City and the provinces of Benguet, Mountain
Province and Kalinga. [@1] In Baguio City, the 12 high-value suspects were
arrested with a search warrant at a house in the Slaughter Area, where police
seized 21 grams of suspected shabu worth P142,800. [@2] Three more street-level
suspects were arrested in separate operations in Benguet and Mountain Province
after they were caught using and hiding illegal drugs. [@2] All 15 were facing
charges under the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002. [@2]

In Benguet, a two-day eradication campaign called Oplan Benguet Ultimate
Grasscutter found 11 marijuana plantations in Bakun, Kapangan and Kibungan. [@3]
Officers uprooted 6,300 fully grown marijuana plants and seized 84,370 grams
(84.37 kg) of dried marijuana leaves with fruiting tops. [@3] Police valued the
Benguet marijuana at P24,744,208. [@2] The plants and evidence were documented
and burned on site after samples were taken for the Regional Forensic Unit. [@2]
Authorities said the operations were part of an intensified campaign against
illegal drug cultivation and trafficking in highland communities across Northern
Luzon. [@3]

Authorities burned about P12 million worth of marijuana plants in a joint
operation on 18 April along the boundary of Saclit, Sadanga and Tinglayan,
Kalinga. [@4] The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency–Cordillera (PDEA-CAR), with
police units, found around 60,000 fully grown marijuana plants spread across a
5,000-square-metre (0.5-hectare) plantation. [@4] The plants were uprooted and
burned on site, and the ashes were buried; no one was arrested. [@4] Police
reported a separate Kalinga operation in Barangay Loccong that brought the
Kalinga total to about 66,000 plants worth more than P13.2 million. [@2]
