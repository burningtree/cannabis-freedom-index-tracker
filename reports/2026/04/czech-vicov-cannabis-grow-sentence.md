Miloslav Knol grew marijuana to obtain THC in a family house in the village of
Vícov. [@1] The Regional Court in Brno heard the case on Tuesday. [@1] Knol will
be supervised by the probation and mediation service and must compensate the
damage he caused. [@1] The court also ordered the forfeiture of the equipment he
used to grow the cannabis. [@1] Knol admitted the offence and called his conduct
a mistake. [@1]

He produced cannabis in three cycles from May to 11 December 2023. [@1] He used
technology worth more than 300,000 CZK. [@1] He grew 150 plants in the first
cycle and 250 in the second, from which he obtained 7.5 kg of dried cannabis.
[@1] He sold 5.5 kg of the dried cannabis to another person at 60,000 CZK per
kilogram and wanted to earn up to 1 million CZK. [@1]

Knol also broke into a locked security box in the house and connected clamps to
the wires, drawing electricity illegally for several months. [@1] The illegal
connection caused damage of more than 1 million CZK to the energy company EG.D.
[@1] The company had offered a 12-month instalment plan on condition that most
of the debt was paid in advance, but Knol could not pay it. [@1] Knol waived his
right to appeal, while the state prosecutor kept time to consider one. [@1]
