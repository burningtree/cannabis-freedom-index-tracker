A multi-agency security team intensified the Embu County drug crackdown with
the arrest of four persons of interest in a targeted operation in Embu
Township. [@1] Per the NPS statement of Sunday, April 19, 2026, officers
raided a suspected drug den and apprehended the four inside. [@1]

A house search recovered 549 rolls of cannabis sativa and a metallic
container believed to be a grinder used in processing, among other items.
[@1] All four were taken to Embu Police Station and held pending
arraignment, with exhibits secured for investigation and court. [@1]
