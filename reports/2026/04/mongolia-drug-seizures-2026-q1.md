In the first quarter of 2026 the police anti-drug department recorded 143 drug crimes involving 210 suspects, and 45 more crimes involving 54 suspects in joint operations with intelligence and customs. It said it had stopped an amount equal to 27,671 single doses, without giving a weight or a cannabis breakdown. The figures cover all controlled drugs. [@1]
