The Antofagasta regional prosecutor's office and Carabineros seized 1,845 kilograms of cannabis that had been hidden in a ravine in difficult country near Taltal. [@1] La Tercera, reporting the same day, 23 April 2026, said officers used a helicopter and a search through the hills, and found the stash about 30 kilometres off the road into the desert. [@2]

The operation began with vehicle checks by the Alemania post of Taltal's 1st police station. [@2] Officers stopped a vehicle they thought was being used for a crime and told the prosecutor's office, which sent the OS7 anti-drug unit from the Antofagasta prefecture. [@2] The two people in the vehicle, both Venezuelan adults without regular immigration status, were arrested. [@1,2]

The regional prosecutor, Juan Castro Bekios, called it a seizure of about 1.8 tonnes and said the two would be formally charged in the coming days. [@1] He put the region's drug seizures for the year, with this load included, at 23.8 tonnes. [@1] General Cristian Montre Soto, head of the Carabineros zone, said the find came from checks on the region's roads. [@2]
