Detectives from the DCI in Rongo Sub-County held a suspect under tight
police guard at Migori County Referral Hospital after a road accident
exposed a large narcotics trafficking operation. [@1] [@2]

Samson Ayiera Chacha, 52, lost control of trailer KBS 548H on the
Migori-Rongo route on 24 April 2026; the vehicle veered off the road and
plunged into River Kuja. [@1] [@2] Rescued by members of the public, he was
rushed to hospital with serious injuries while officers secured the scene.
[@1]

Days later a recovery operation retrieved the submerged trailer, towed to
Kamagambo Police Station, where inspection uncovered 1,137.8 kg of suspected
cannabis sativa valued at Sh34.1 million; ANU officers from Isebania took
over the case. [@1] [@2] Chacha remained under guard pending arraignment on
drug-trafficking charges. [@1]
