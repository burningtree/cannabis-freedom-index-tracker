In five days at the end of April 2026 the Pau customs brigade made two cannabis seizures in the
Ossau valley, totalling 255.94 kg. [@1,2] Customs valued the load at more than two million euros on
the illicit retail market. [@1,2]

On 22 April, during a control, a car turned hard toward Spain when officers appeared. [@1,2] It was
later found crashed and empty; the boot held 100.42 kg of cannabis. [@1,2] The occupants were not
caught. [@2]

On 27 April, again in the valley, officers stopped a utility vehicle. [@1,2] A drug dog marked under
the rear bench; dismantling revealed a built-in box holding 155.52 kg of cannabis resin. [@1,2]
