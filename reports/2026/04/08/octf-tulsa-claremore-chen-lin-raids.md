On Wednesday 8 April 2026 the Oklahoma Attorney General’s Organized Crime Task Force (OCTF) raided
two illegal marijuana grow sites and two processing facilities, the attorney general’s office said.
[@1,2] Agents searched KMA 2021 and Wanna-Dab Labs in Tulsa and Danky McNuggy Medical Group
locations in Claremore and Tulsa. [@1,2]

The task force seized more than 58,920 marijuana plants and 1,350 pounds (about 612 kg) of processed
marijuana. [@1,2] Several suspects were arrested, and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement took
nine people identified as illegal immigrants into custody for deportation. [@1,2]

Among those arrested were Qiu Cheng Chen, known as Sam Chen, and his wife Xiufeng Lin, known as
Linda Lin, of Jenks. [@1,2] They were booked on complaints including conspiracy to defraud the
state, conspiracy to manufacture marijuana, aggravated manufacturing in Tulsa and Rogers counties and
a pattern of criminal offences. [@1,2] Investigators accused the couple of running a trafficking
organisation since 2024 that used straw owners to register and operate medical marijuana businesses
in Tulsa and Rogers counties while hiding the true owners. [@1,2]

The OCTF worked with the Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, Tulsa and Rogers county sheriffs,
Cherokee marshals, the Oklahoma National Guard civil support team, state agriculture officials and
several federal agencies; county crews helped destroy the seized cannabis. [@1]
