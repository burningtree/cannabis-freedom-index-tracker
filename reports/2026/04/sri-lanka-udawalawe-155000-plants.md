On 11 April 2026, acting on intelligence received by the Sri Lanka Army's intelligence unit,
Hambegamuwa police and army personnel launched a coordinated raid on a suspected cannabis field
inside the Udawalawe reserve. [@1,2] They uncovered around 155,000 cannabis plants, each standing
about three feet tall, on a cultivation spanning about three acres (around 1.2 hectares). [@1,2]
The seized plants were estimated to be worth Rs 4.5 million. [@1,2]

The cultivation was taken into custody, in what the reports present as part of a drive against
illegal drug activity and for the protection of the country's natural reserves. [@1,2] Neither
report names any arrests or cultivators. [@1,2]
