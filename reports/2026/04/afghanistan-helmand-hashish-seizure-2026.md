Counter-narcotics police seized 300 kilograms of hashish in Helmand province and arrested four people, the Interior Ministry said. In the same round of operations police recovered 2,800 intoxicating tablets in Jalalabad and 1,000 tablet K pills in Herat. The ministry said the suspects would face legal action. [@1]
