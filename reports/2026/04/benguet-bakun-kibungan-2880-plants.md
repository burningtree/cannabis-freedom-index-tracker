On Monday 14 April 2026 anti-drug teams in the Cordillera uprooted and burned 2,880 fully
grown marijuana plants and about 10.3 kilograms of dried stalks, leaves and fruiting tops
at seven plantation sites in Benguet. [@1]

Good Morning Baguio, citing the Police Regional Office Cordillera, said the Benguet sites
were in Barangay Kayapa in Bakun and Barangay Badeo in Kibungan and that the destroyed
drugs had a standard price of 1.81 million pesos. [@1] Municipal police, provincial mobile
force companies, intelligence and drug-enforcement units and the Philippine Drug Enforcement
Agency in the Cordillera took part. [@1]

SMNI News Channel reported the same 2,880-plant Benguet eradication on 14 April. [@2]

All of the Benguet plants were destroyed on site and investigators were still looking for
those responsible. [@1,2] No cultivator was caught. [@1,2]

A PRO-CAR photograph published with the Good Morning Baguio story shows officers uprooting
and burning marijuana in the field on 14 April. [@1]

The same regional operation day also included a separate 5,000-plant eradication in Kalinga
province, which is not part of this report. [@1]
