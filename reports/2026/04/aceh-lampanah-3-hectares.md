On Wednesday 29 April 2026 Aceh provincial police chief Marzuki Ali Basyah
led officers who uprooted and burned three hectares of cannabis at Lampanah
village, Seulimum, Aceh Besar regency, during Operasi Antik Seulawah 2026.
[@1,2]

ANTARA News and Tribrata News reported that the provincial narcotics directorate
had found about 20 hectares of cannabis scattered across several sites in the
Lampanah area, with a police estimate of up to 50 tonnes of potential harvest.
[@1,2] Marzuki said only the three-hectare Lampanah plot was destroyed that day,
with Aceh Besar district police, Indonesian army personnel, local officials and
young farmers taking part. [@1,2]

Both outlets said plants were pulled and burned on site as part of Aceh police
efforts to discourage cultivation. [@1,2] No arrests were reported. [@1,2]

ANTARA published a police handout photograph of the burning. [@1]
