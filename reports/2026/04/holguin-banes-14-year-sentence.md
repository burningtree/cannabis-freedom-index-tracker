A man was sentenced to 14 years in prison by the People's Provincial Court of Holguín for
cultivating and selling marijuana in the Jagüeyes de Puerto Rico area of Banes
municipality. [@1]

The case, registered as Case 2 of 2026 in the Third Criminal Chamber, grew out of 2025
investigations by the Banes Criminal Investigation unit; the National Anti-Drug
Directorate then monitored the suspect under the "Jagüeyes Case" file. [@1] He was
arrested on the morning of 6 May 2025 hiding in bushes near a fenced field, and officers
seized 429 Cannabis Sativa Indica plants, 14 to 77 cm tall with a net weight of 2,420
grams, plus 11,280 pesos in cash. [@1]

Prosecuted for drug trafficking under Article 235.1 of Penal Code Law 151 of 2022, which
carries 10 to 30 years, he received 14 years with repeat offending as an aggravating
factor; the sentence added revocation of civil rights, a 14-year ban on leaving the
country, and confiscation of the cash. [@1] The court vice-president said the trial
respected the accused's procedural rights. [@1]
