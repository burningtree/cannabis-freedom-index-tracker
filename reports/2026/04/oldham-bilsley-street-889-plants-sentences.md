In the early hours of Tuesday 17 June 2025 Greater Manchester Police forced entry to a heavily
secured industrial unit on Bilsley Street in Oldham. [@1,2,3] Inside they found 889 cannabis plants
at various stages of growth, with an estimated potential street value of £986,800. [@1,2,3] The
electricity supply had been illegally bypassed. [@1,2,3] A small living area held four bunk beds, a
cooker, a fridge and a television showing live CCTV from outside; police called the conditions
extremely poor. [@1,2,3]

Officers found Indrid Hysembelli, Xhoi Kasaj and Romeo Tosca, all of Albanian descent, hiding in the
loft and arrested them. [@1,2,3] An investigation later turned up photographs of the men posing over
piles of cannabis. [@1,3]

At Minshull Street Crown Court they were convicted of producing a Class B drug. [@2,3] Kasaj, 30,
was jailed for two years and three months on 23 March 2026; Tosca, 26, for two years and eight
months the same day. [@1,2,3] Hysembelli, 26, was sentenced on Monday 27 April 2026 to three years
and seven months. [@1,2,3]
