Police officers from Obunga Police Station in Kisumu Central Sub-County
nabbed one man linked to at least twelve bales of bhang on Thursday evening.
[@1] [@2]

On a timely public tip-off, officers stopped the person of interest riding a
motorcycle and, on inspection, found 12 bales of a green leafy substance
suspected to be cannabis sativa concealed in yellow polythene bags. [@1]
[@2] The rider was escorted to Obunga Police Station and placed in custody
pending formal processing and arraignment; the motorcycle and narcotics were
secured as exhibits. [@1] [@2]
