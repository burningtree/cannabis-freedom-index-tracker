The Polícia Judiciária, working with the Polícia Nacional, dismantled three cannabis plots at Picos Acima in the municipality of São Salvador do Mundo, on Santiago island, in the early hours of Saturday 4 April 2026. [@1] The operation was carried out under search warrants sought by the Public Prosecutor's office through the criminal investigation department in Assomada and issued by the instruction judge of the Santa Catarina de Santiago court. [@1] It followed investigations that were already under way and continued into the morning. [@1]

Officers seized and destroyed about 413 kg of cannabis across the three plots. [@1] Of that total, 6.1 kg was already packed and ready for distribution and consumption. [@1] The police also seized a Renault Duster, whose owner was suspected of being involved in running the plots. [@1]

The article reported no arrests and did not say what happened to the suspected owner of the car. [@1]
