On Tuesday 29 April 2026 police in the Cordillera found and burned 4,100 fully grown
marijuana plants and about 30,000 grams of dried marijuana stalks across seven plantations
in Benguet province. [@1,2]

Daily Tribune said the sites were in Barangays Badeo and Tacadang in Kibungan, Gadang in
Kapangan and Kayapa in Bakun, and that the marijuana had a standard drug price of about
4,420,000 pesos. [@1,2] Municipal police from Kibungan, Bakun and Kapangan, Benguet
provincial mobile force companies, regional specialized units and the Philippine Drug
Enforcement Agency in the Cordillera carried out the eradication. [@1,2]

The plants were burned on site and police were still trying to identify those behind the
fields. [@1,2] No cultivator was caught during the plantation operation. [@1,2]

A photograph released by the Police Regional Office Cordillera shows officers at one of
the cleared sites. [@1]

The same news cycle also covered an unrelated shabu buy-bust in Mankayan; this report
covers only the seven-plantation eradication. [@1,2]
