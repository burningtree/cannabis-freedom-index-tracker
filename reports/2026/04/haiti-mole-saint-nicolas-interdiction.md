At Haiti's request, a US Coast Guard law-enforcement detachment aboard the Navy ship USS
Billings stopped a suspected drug-smuggling vessel about eight miles off Mole
Saint-Nicolas on 30 April 2026. [@1,2]

With the Haitian government's permission, the boarding team's inspection found about
3,200 pounds (1,500 kg) of marijuana worth about $3.8 million, and one person was
detained. [@1,2] The contraband and the suspect were transferred to Haitian authorities
on Sunday 3 May; other assets in the operation included the cutter Venturous and a
Billings helicopter crew. [@2]
