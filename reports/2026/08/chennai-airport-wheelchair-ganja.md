A 48-year-old Indian passenger who arrived with wheelchair assistance was
arrested by the Air Intelligence Unit of Chennai Customs. [@1] He landed at
Chennai airport in the early hours of Sunday 30 August 2026 on an AirAsia
flight. [@2] The flight had come from Bangkok via Kuala Lumpur. [@3] He cleared
immigration, collected his baggage and tried to leave through the green channel
without declaring any goods. [@2] Officers stopped him. [@2] A search of his
bags found six transparent airtight packets of hydroponic ganja weighing 2,960
grams. [@1] Customs valued the cannabis at Rs 3 crore (30 million rupees) and
seized the contraband. [@1]

He was taken to the Customs office for a detailed examination. [@2] Officers
found he had no medical condition requiring a wheelchair and suspected he was
using it as a ruse to avoid scrutiny. [@2] During interrogation, officials
learned he was acting as a mule for an international drug trafficking network
and had booked wheelchair assistance with fake medical documents. [@2] Officers
were investigating whom the consignment was meant for and whether more people
were involved. [@2]
