Police in Zombo District arrested Patrick Odongo of Aciro village, Pakia Parish,
Warr Sub-County, on Friday 14 August 2026 in a joint operation led by district
police commander SP Job Mutegeki, local leaders and residents. [@1,2]

The arrest followed public anger after Odongo allegedly cut a pipe on the Can Koba
gravitational water scheme on 10 August, cutting off 146 households. [@1,2]
Officers and community members uprooted fresh marijuana plants from his garden and
found dried cannabis in his house; police said he had cultivated and sold the drug
for a long time. [@1,2] He was held at Warr Police Post while investigations
continued. [@1]

Mutegeki said officers had gone to answer the water-pipe sabotage and discovered
narcotics during the search; he warned that cultivation and sale of prohibited
drugs would be pursued across the district. [@2] Village chair Albert Onyay said
Odongo refused to repair the line after cutting it because he did not want the pipe
passing through his compound. [@1,2]
