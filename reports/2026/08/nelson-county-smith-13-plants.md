Jonathan Smith was arrested in Nelson County, Kentucky, on Wednesday after
sheriff's deputies said he danced in a local road and they found marijuana
plants growing at his home. [@1] Deputies were dispatched to Stringtown Road at
about 8:19 p.m. for reports of a man dancing in the roadway. [@1] They found
Smith at a nearby residence and, while speaking to him, said they saw the plants
growing in the yard. [@1] A search of the property yielded 13 plants in total.
[@2] Smith was taken to the Nelson County Jail. [@1]

He is charged with second-degree disorderly conduct, third-degree possession of
a controlled substance, cultivating marijuana — five plants or more and buying
or possessing drug paraphernalia. [@1]
