Guangzhou customs at Baiyun Airport's air-cargo port seized 2,380.96 g of
cannabis in a transit express parcel, according to reporting on a customs case.
[@1,2] Guangzhou Baiyun Airport customs officers were screening a shipment
declared as “cocoa crisps” (可可酥脆) when the X-ray image looked wrong. [@1,2]
They opened five boxes of foreign-brand breakfast cereal and found vacuum-packed
green plant fragments with a strong irritant smell. [@1,2] Laboratory testing
confirmed the material was cannabis weighing 2,380.96 g. [@1,2] The republished
notices do not say whether anyone was arrested; they repeat criminal-law warnings
that smuggling or trafficking drugs of any amount is punishable. [@1,2]
