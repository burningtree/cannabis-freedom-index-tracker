On Sunday 30 August 2026 officers of Carabineros' OS7 anti-drug unit and the GOPE special forces,
backed by a judicial warrant, searched a house in Calama, in Chile's Antofagasta region. [@1,2]
Inside they found packages of marijuana weighing 737 kilograms and 300 grams, plus other items tied
to the offence. [@1,2,3]

The Antofagasta prosecutor's specialised drug unit (Fiscalía SAC) said investigators had traced a
large shipment entering Chile from Bolivia and followed it to the urban address under Chile's drug
law. [@1,2] Calama en Línea says the OS7 inquiry began on 29 August. [@3]

A Colombian man aged 28, described as having irregular migration status, was arrested. [@1,3] He was
formalised for drug trafficking and ordered into pre-trial detention for danger to public safety,
with a 90-day investigation period. [@1,2,3]
