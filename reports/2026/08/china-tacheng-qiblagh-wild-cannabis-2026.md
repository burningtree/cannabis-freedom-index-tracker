Two children who told Qiblagh border police they had seen suspected drug plants
in a village field triggered a clearance of wild cannabis on 3 August 2026,
Chinanews reported. [@1,2]

Officers from the Tacheng border management detachment went with village staff,
confirmed the plants as wild cannabis and uprooted more than 5,000 of them,
collecting and counting every plant, the report said. [@1,2] Police later visited
the children with “little anti-drug guard” certificates to thank them for
speaking up. [@1,2]

The posts did not name the hamlet or say whether anyone would be prosecuted for
planting. [@1,2]
