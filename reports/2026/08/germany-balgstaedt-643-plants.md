Police found a cannabis plantation with 643 plants while searching a property in an outlying part of
Balgstädt, in the Burgenlandkreis, on 5 August 2026. [@1] Two men aged 30 and 24, described by
police as of Albanian origin, were provisionally arrested, and the Halle public prosecutor's office
said it would seek arrest warrants against them. [@1,2]

Investigators estimated that the plantation could produce about 30 kg of cannabis flowers every six
to eight weeks, worth more than €100,000 at sale prices. [@1,2] The plants were seized as
evidence. [@1]

The searches were carried out under court orders in investigations into suspected violations of the
Consumer Cannabis Act and illegal trafficking in narcotics. [@1] Officers also searched three
properties in Halle, where they seized evidence and communications devices; those investigations
target two men of Turkish origin aged 52 and 30 and were continuing. [@1,2]
