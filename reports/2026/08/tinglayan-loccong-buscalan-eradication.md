The largest accomplishment in the PNP's 24-hour nationwide anti-drug operations over the
weekend came from marijuana eradication in Loccong and Buscalan, Tinglayan, Kalinga, where
joint teams discovered and destroyed about 94,500 fully grown plants worth P18.9 million.
[@1,2]

Authorities uprooted and burned the plantation on site to keep the marijuana from reaching
the illicit market. [@1] Although no cultivators were arrested, police said the operation
dealt a significant blow to marijuana production in the Cordillera region. [@1,2]
