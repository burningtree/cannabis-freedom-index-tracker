As part of an intensified gendarmerie campaign, a patrol from the Oulad Frej
centre raided an underground storage vault near the home of the main suspect,
Ahmed M., a repeat narcotics offender, in the commune of Oulad Hamdane, Sidi
Ismail circle, on the evening of Wednesday 26 August 2026 at about 7 pm. [@1]

The search recovered 12 bundles of kif in tops form weighing 350 kilograms in
total, plus 20 bundles of contraband tobacco weighing 76 kilograms, prepared for
resale — over 400 kilograms combined. [@1] The suspect exploited the terrain to
flee before the gendarmes arrived; on the El Jadida prosecutor's instructions the
goods were handed to customs, a case file was opened and a national search notice
was issued for the fugitive. [@1]
