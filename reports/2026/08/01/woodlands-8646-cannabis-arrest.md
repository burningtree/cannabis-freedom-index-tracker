On the morning of 1 August 2026 ICA officers directed a Malaysia-registered car arriving at
Woodlands Checkpoint for enhanced checks. [@1,2] They found controlled drugs in the boot and
alerted CNB, whose officers recovered about 8,646 g of cannabis and 1,569 g of
methamphetamine in total. [@1,2]

The 22-year-old Malaysian driver was arrested for attempted smuggling. [@1,2] ICA said the
drugs were worth more than S$361,000 and could supply about 2,132 abusers for a week, a figure
that covers both substances. [@1,2]

Import or export of more than 500 g of cannabis carries a possible mandatory death sentence
under the Misuse of Drugs Act, the agencies noted. [@1,2]
