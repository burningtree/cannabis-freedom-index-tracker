Police in Katari, Udayapur district, seized 1,122.67 kg of marijuana and
arrested three people. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, a team from the Area Police
Office, Katari, searched the Shantiban forest area at around 4 am on Monday.
[@2] Officers found 52 sacks of marijuana packed in plastic bags and concealed
in the jungle. [@1] The sacks were hidden at different places in the jungle near
a Bolero pickup, Nepal Police headquarters said. [@3] Police also seized a
Bolero vehicle, registration Ba 16 Cha 5269, from the area. [@1] The vehicle was
allegedly used to transport the marijuana. [@2]

The three were identified as Ram Bahadur Magar, 55, Nek Bahadur Magar, 21, and
Prithviraj Magar, 46, all from Dandagaun, Tapli Rural Municipality-1, Udayapur.
[@1] Police said they suspected the three were the vehicle's driver and
marijuana traffickers. [@1] Nepal Police headquarters said the men were arrested
with the marijuana and the Bolero, and that further investigation was under way.
[@3]
