Three men from Patna district were arrested with 62 kg of ganja hidden in a
secret compartment under the rear seat of their white SUV during a vehicle check
on NH-107 in Khagaria. [@1] They were Manish Kumar and Raushan Kumar, both from
Chandmari Nurpur under Shahpur police station, and Gaurav Kumar, the driver,
from Mahuabagh under Rupaspur police station. [@3] The team acted on a tip-off
that traffickers would bring ganja from Chautham towards Maheshkhunt, and set up
the check at Khoradhar bridge on NH-107 under Maheshkhunt police station's
station house officer, Mithilesh Kumar. [@4] They stopped a white XUV coming
from Chautham and found 62 packets holding about 62 kg of ganja in a hidden
compartment under the rear seat, then seized the vehicle. [@4]

Police also recovered five mobile phones from the men. [@3] Maheshkhunt police
registered case 154/26 on 18 August 2026 under section 8/20(b)(ii)(C) of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@4] Police said initial
questioning indicated the men brought consignments from Khagaria and supplied
them in different parts of Patna. [@3] The Hindustan daily estimated the seized
ganja's value at more than 600,000 rupees. [@2] Superintendent of Police Bhanu
Pratap Singh said the drive against drug trafficking in the district had
produced a big success and would continue. [@2]
