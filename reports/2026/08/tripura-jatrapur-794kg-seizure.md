Acting on intelligence from the Tripura Police intelligence wing, officers from Jatrapur police station searched a house at Laltilla Para under Bhavanipur panchayat in Sepahijala district in the early hours of 13 August 2026. [@1,2]

They recovered eleven Sintex drums of dried cannabis buried under a newly built structure on the property, about 794 kg in all. [@1,2] Police estimated the value at roughly ₹1.5 crore. [@1,2]

Officer-in-charge Parthanath Bhowmik led the raid with sub-inspectors from the station and Tripura State Rifles personnel; the Sonamura sub-divisional police officer also attended. [@1,2] The owner, Badal Chandra De, was arrested and booked under the NDPS Act, then produced before the Sonamura court on 13 August. [@1,2]
