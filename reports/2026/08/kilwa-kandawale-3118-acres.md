By 3 August 2026, Tanzania's Drug Control and Enforcement Authority said it had destroyed 3,117.9 ekari of cannabis fields, about 1,262 hectares, and arrested 12 suspects. [@1] The work was a 16-day operation in four villages of Kandawale and Kipatimo wards, in Kilwa district, Lindi region. [@1]

Commissioner General Aretas Lyimo said the plantations were on land owned by villages and hamlets. [@1] He said that, despite the government's plan for how that land should be used, some residents were still growing cannabis on it. [@1] Between 200 and 250 residents took part in the clearing each day. [@1]

In Nandete village, Veronica Mandai and Nassoro Mbonde condemned the cultivation. [@1] Mandai asked young people and women to refuse drugs, and said children were sometimes drawn into the farming without their parents knowing. [@1] Kandawale ward executive officer Mohamed Mpame said leaders would patrol more often and urge people to grow cashew instead. [@1]

The Respondents reported the same operation, the same 12 arrests and the same wards, from the authority's account. [@2] It described the area as 3,117.9 hectares. [@2]
