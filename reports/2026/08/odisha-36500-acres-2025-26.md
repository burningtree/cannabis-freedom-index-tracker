During the 2025–26 cannabis season, from November 2025 to March 2026, Odisha Police destroyed cannabis
growing on 36,508.792 acres. [@1] The fields lay in 1,631 plots, mapped by GPS, under 41 police
stations in six districts, among them Malkangiri, Koraput, Rayagada, Kandhamal and Gajapati. [@1]
6,632 acres of it was government or revenue land. [@1]

## Watching the same land again

In a letter dated 10 August 2026, the Additional Chief Secretary of the Revenue and Disaster
Management Department, Arabinda Kumar Padhee, told the collectors of the six districts to keep
special watch on the plots where cannabis had been destroyed, since growers may plant them again in
the 2026–27 season. [@1] Village revenue staff are to keep plot-by-plot records with GPS
coordinates and report any new planting at once to the police or the state's anti-narcotics task
forces, as the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act requires. [@1] Officials found helping
growers face strict action. [@1]
