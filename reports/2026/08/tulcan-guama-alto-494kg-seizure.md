National Police seized marijuana during operation Cero Impunidad in Tulcán, Carchi province, on 27 August 2026. [@1,2] El Universo said officers found 494.416 kg of cannabis in packages at Guamá Alto and arrested five people, also retaining a pickup, two motorcycles and five mobile phones. [@1]

Teleamazonas reported 400 kilograms seized in Tulcán with the same five arrests and the same vehicles and phones taken as evidence. [@2] Interior Minister John Reimberg publicised the 400 kg figure and an estimate of 800,000 doses removed from the market. [@2]

The Attorney General's Office said that in the early hours of 27 August police intercepted motorcycles and chased a pickup into rough ground at La Ovejería, recovering 262 rectangular packages and eight plastic bags totalling 494.416 kg of marijuana. [@3] Four adults were charged with large-scale trafficking and later received preventive detention; a teenager found on one motorcycle was placed in preventive internment in a separate hearing. [@3]
