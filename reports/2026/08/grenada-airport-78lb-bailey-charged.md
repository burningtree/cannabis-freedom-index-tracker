Police searched the luggage of Malikah Bailey, a 45-year-old US businesswoman, when she arrived at Maurice Bishop International Airport on Sunday 23 August 2026 and found 78 pounds of cannabis. [@1,2]

Officers arrested her and laid a charge of trafficking in a controlled drug. [@2] The load was estimated at EC$702,000 street value. [@1,2]

Bailey appeared at St George's No. 1 Magistrate's Court on 25 August 2026, was remanded to His Majesty's Prison and was due back in court on 3 September 2026. [@1,2]
