Chad's national police presented 29 suspects to journalists in N'Djamena on Monday
17 August 2026 after two weeks of operations across the capital. [@1,2]

Commissioner Mounne Eugene Dezoumbe said raids from 5 to 17 August had targeted
trafficking networks, thefts and fraud; 28 suspects appeared in person at the
presentation at police headquarters. [@1] Seventeen people face drug charges,
including possession and sale of cannabis, Indian hemp and other substances, as
well as selling adulterated alcohol and one attempted bribe of an officer. [@1,2]
Ten others were held over theft and gang offences, and one over fraud, impersonation
and rape. [@1,2]

Police said searches recovered significant quantities of prohibited drugs, packaging
and leaves used to prepare sales, plus hundreds of bottles of counterfeit alcohol.
[@1,2] Lebledparle reported that cannabis and hemp were among the drugs seized. [@2]
Authorities said most suspects had admitted the facts in custody and would be sent
to court. [@1]
