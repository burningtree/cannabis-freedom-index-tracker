Police in South Al Batinah arrested seven Asian nationals in Al Barka after finding more than 185 kilograms of narcotics, including crystal meth, hashish, marijuana and morphine, and more than 120,000 psychotropic tablets. The Royal Oman Police said the group intended to traffic the drugs. The statement did not give a separate cannabis weight or say where the shipment entered Oman. [@1]
