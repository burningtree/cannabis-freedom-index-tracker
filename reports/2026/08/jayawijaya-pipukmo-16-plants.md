Police in Jayawijaya Regency, Papua Pegunungan, found a cannabis plot in Kampung
Pipukmo, a village in Hubikosi district, on Thursday 27 August 2026. [@1]
Residents clearing land found the plants and reported them through the Pipukmo
village head, Wamena Kota police chief AKP Saharuddin said. [@3] The operation
followed patrols and aerial detection with a drone, police said. [@1]

Officers seized 16 plants: 12 still growing and four already harvested. [@1] The
harvested plants measured 120, 114, 129 and 137 cm. [@1] The plot lay about 3 km
from a public road and officers reached it on foot. [@1] Police put the plot at
about 260 cm by 400 cm. [@1] No owner had been identified, police said. [@1]
Police took the plants away for examination and said they were still hunting the
planter. [@4] No arrests were reported. [@4]

The Jayawijaya narcotics unit chief, Iptu Jan B Saragih, said cannabis harms
health, social life and a person's future. [@1] He said police did not want the
plant to spread into community life, especially among young people in Papua.
[@1] AKP Saharuddin said residents had been police partners in reporting
security problems, including cannabis. [@3] Police asked residents to report
cannabis planting and said they would follow up professionally and humanely.
[@1] Jayawijaya police also use drones to monitor forests and remote areas where
cannabis may be planted. [@2]
