FELCN, Bolivia's special force against drug trafficking, burned more than 90
tonnes of marijuana at La Angostura, in the municipality of El Torno, Andrés
Ibáñez province, Santa Cruz. [@1,2]

Officers on patrol and in a ground search found dried marijuana and standing
cannabis plants. [@1,2] They eradicated the plants and incinerated the drug.
[@1,2] The police account, carried by Red Uno, called the operation a
significant blow to illicit activity linked to drug trafficking in that part
of Santa Cruz, and said this kind of work is how the force finds and removes
controlled crops. [@1]

The reports name no one arrested. [@1,2] They give no count of plants and no
area. [@1,2] El Fulgor's headline rounds the figure to 90 tonnes; the text of
both reports says more than 90 tonnes, of marijuana both dried and still in
the ground. [@1,2]
