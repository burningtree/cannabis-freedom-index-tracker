The Barbados Police Service Narcotics Unit arrested and charged three men in
connection with the seizure of 43.3 kilograms of suspected cannabis valued at an
estimated $866,000. [@1]

Those charged were 45-year-old Trinidadian national Keion Kester George,
53-year-old Ryan O'Neal Goddard of Cutting Road, Haggatt Hall, St Michael, and a
54-year-old Guyanese national, each charged with possession of cannabis,
trafficking and possession with intent to supply for offences allegedly committed
on 23 August 2026. [@1] George was additionally charged with entering Barbados
other than through a port of entry and disembarking without an immigration
officer's consent. [@1]

The three appeared before a magistrate in the District B Magistrates' Court on 28
August and were not required to plead to the indictable offences; one was granted
bail of $200,000 while the other two were remanded. [@1]
