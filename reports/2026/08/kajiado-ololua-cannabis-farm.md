On Saturday 1 August 2026 detectives from Kenya's Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI)
Anti-Narcotics Unit and Ololua police, acting on intelligence, raided an illegal cannabis farm
in Kajiado County, uprooted the crop and seized 78.98 kilograms of cannabis sativa packed in
five sacks. [@1,2]

People Daily and TV47 Digital reported that the DCI valued the seizure at KSh 2,369,000 and
arrested a 40-year-old man identified as Joseph Leimyayan at the scene. [@1,2] The DCI said
investigations would continue before court proceedings. [@1,2]

Both outlets referenced a photograph the DCI published on social media, but neither embedded a
recoverable direct image address in the articles read. [@1,2]
