On the evening of 14 August 2026, Faroese police went to a house in Tórshavn,
called Havn in Faroese, after a report of suspicious activity. [@1,2] Inside
they found 6.5 kg of hashish and just over 40,000 Danish kroner in cash. [@1,2]

Two men in their mid-twenties were charged with selling hashish from the
address. [@1,2] At a remand hearing on 15 August they were held in custody for
four weeks. [@1,2] One of the men admitted guilt, Kringvarp Føroya reported.
[@1] Police said they believe the pair had sold more than the amount seized.
