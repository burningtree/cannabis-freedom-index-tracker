Three people were arrested after officers seized 33.2 kilogrammes of
marijuana and 100 rounds of 9mm ammunition during Operation RESOLUTE Shield
in Tobago, according to the TTPS. [@1]

Between 3:00 and 6:00 AM on Saturday 29 August 2026, officers in the Crown
Point Police Station District, acting on information and using State of
Emergency powers, searched a residence at Young Street, Canaan, finding
cannabis valued at $1,105,800 along with the ammunition. [@1]

The suspects — aged 29, 36 and 27, all of Young Street, Canaan — were held
as officers from Crown Point station, the Tobago Divisional Task Force, the
Inter-Agency Task Force and the Canine Unit took part; investigations
continue. [@1]
