At the Le Boulou toll barrier on the A9 (Spain to France), the Perthus inland surveillance brigade of
customs stopped a Spanish-plated heavy goods set after a drugs dog marked the outside of the truck. [@1,2]
Inside a refrigerated trailer declared for food, officers found cartons of cannabis resin hidden in
pallet doors and elsewhere in the trailer. [@1,2]

The resin weighed 495 kilograms; customs estimated its illicit resale value at nearly four million
euros. [@1,2] The driver, who said he was bound for Italy, was held by customs then handed to OFAST
in Perpignan. [@1,2]

On 5 August 2026 the Perpignan criminal court, in an immediate appearance, sentenced him to four
years in prison with remand, a permanent ban from French territory and a customs fine of
€991,750. [@1,2]
