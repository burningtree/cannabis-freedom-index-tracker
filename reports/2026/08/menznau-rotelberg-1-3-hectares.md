On 5 August 2026 Lucerne police shut an outdoor cannabis plantation at Rötelberg in Menznau and arrested three men. [@1] Two of them had run. [@1] A police dog, Quanto, stopped them. [@1] Police said the three were two Albanians and one Swiss, aged between 28 and 42. [@1]

The field covered about 1.3 hectares, and there was equipment for growing and processing. [@1] Analysis found THC in places up to twelve times the legal limit. [@1] Proceedings were opened against six people in all. [@1] The public prosecutor in Sursee is leading the investigation. [@1] Police said the presumption of innocence applies. [@1]

Lucerne police announced the raid on 27 August. [@1] The photographs published with the account show the standing crop and plants hung to dry. [@2]
