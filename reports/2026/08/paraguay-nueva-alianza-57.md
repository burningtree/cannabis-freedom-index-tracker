Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) announced on 31 August 2026 the results of the
57th phase of Operation Nueva Alianza, run jointly with Brazil's Federal Police. [@1] For twelve
days, teams went by air and on foot into the forests of Canindeyú and Caaguazú. [@1] They destroyed
222 hectares of cannabis and dismantled 44 camps where growers lived and worked. [@1]

Senad counted 671,250 kilograms of cannabis as "annulled": about 666,000 kilograms of crop that would
have been harvested, and 5,250 kilograms already harvested. [@1] It put the loss to the growers at
USD 100.7 million. [@1] The operation also involved Paraguay's air force, its internal defence
command and the public prosecutor's office. [@1] The report names no arrests. [@1]

## Inside the reserves

Most of the work was in protected areas: the Mbaracayú Forest Nature Reserve, the Morombí and
Caaguazú reserves and Estancia Pindó. [@1] Paraguay's anti-drug minister, Jalil Rachid, said in
October 2025 that illegal cannabis growing had cleared more than 5,000 hectares of forest in two
years. [@2] In that phase, the 53rd, teams eradicated 98 hectares in Caaguazú, Canindeyú and Alto Paraná.
