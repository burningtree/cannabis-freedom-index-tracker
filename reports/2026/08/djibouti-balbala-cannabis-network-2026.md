The Einguella gendarmerie brigade broke up what it called a cannabis
distribution network in the Balbala district of Djibouti City, finding 88
packages weighing 12 kg at a storage site, with an estimated street value
of 1.76 million Djiboutian francs.[@1,2]

Several people were arrested, held in police custody before presentation
to the justice authorities.[@1,2]
