Border police from Podgorica station and a mobile unit checked a VW Golf driven by S.P., 37, from
Tuzi in Cijevna at about six in the morning on 8 August 2026. [@1,2] In the boot they found two
burlap sacks with 35 PVC packages holding about 38 kilogrammes of marijuana police suspect was
smuggled from Albania. [@1,2]

Police detained S.P. on a prosecutor’s order for unauthorised production, possession and trafficking
in narcotics and temporarily seized the car. [@1,2] The higher state prosecutor in Podgorica later
said he was held for 72 hours, alleging that he had agreed with two unknown people from Albania to
bring cannabis across the green border at night and load it into the parked Golf before the morning
search. [@2]
