Officers of the Police Special Task Force's Special Operations Unit III arrested
a man on 30 August 2026, acting on intelligence received. [@2] He was 32 years
old, and the STF said he lived in Kalalgoda. [@1] Newswire reported that the man
was taken into custody in the Hokandara area while transporting over 2 kilograms
of narcotics in a motor vehicle. [@2] Ada Derana named the substance as ‘Kush’
cannabis and said the arrest was made in a car in the Wanaguru Mawatha area of
Hokandara. [@1]

The STF said it recovered the consignment in his possession during a raid
carried out on information received. [@1]

The raid took place in front of a fuel station within the Malabe Police
Division. [@2] Police found Rs 39,290 in cash with the drugs. [@2] Newswire
described him as a man from Pannipitiya. [@2]

He remained in custody while further investigations continued. [@2] Malabe
police were handling the inquiry. [@3]
