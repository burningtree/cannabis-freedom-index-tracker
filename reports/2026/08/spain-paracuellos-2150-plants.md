The Guardia Civil dismantled an indoor marijuana farm with 2,150 plants in an industrial warehouse
in Paracuellos de Jarama, east of Madrid, the force said on 18 August 2026. [@1,2]

Officers found plants at different stages of flowering, along with lighting, air-conditioning,
precision scales, fertilisers and filters to hide the smell. [@2] The investigation began in mid-May
after information about a possible cannabis farm; officers noticed a strong smell and
air-conditioning units outside the building. [@1,2]

Two men were arrested and a third is under investigation; the Guardia Civil accuses the three of
cultivating the drug and stealing electricity. [@1,2] The force did not give the warehouse's exact
address. [@1]
