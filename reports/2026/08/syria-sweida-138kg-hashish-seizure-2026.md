Syria's anti-narcotics directorate said it had foiled an international
drug-smuggling attempt in the eastern countryside of Sweida governorate. [@1]
The directorate, the Interior Ministry body responsible for drug enforcement,
announced the operation on its Telegram channel on Saturday 29 August 2026. [@1]
It said officers seized and confiscated 138 kg of hashish and 382,000 narcotic
pills. [@2] The drugs were in the possession of groups outside the law in the
governorate, it said. [@2]

No arrests were announced, and the directorate did not name anyone it is seeking
over the shipment. [@3] It said its services were continuing investigations and
security follow-up to identify and pursue those involved, so that the necessary
judicial measures could be taken against them. [@1]

The directorate described the operation as a carefully conducted security
operation and called the smuggling attempt international. [@1] The announcement
gave no destination for the shipment and no value for the drugs. [@1]
