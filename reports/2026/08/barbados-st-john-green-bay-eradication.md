Officers of the Barbados Police Service Special Operations Management Division
launched Operation Green Bay in the early morning of Saturday 29 August, targeting
dense brush and farmland in Welch, Cliff Cottage, Bath Land and Colleton in the
parish of St John. [@1]

The sweep eradicated 5,968 cannabis plants ranging from seedlings to mature trees
eight feet high; the vegetation weighed 356.79 kg with an estimated street value
exceeding $1.4 million. [@1] No suspects were apprehended and police investigations
continue to identify the cultivation network. [@1]

Residents described officers arriving from about five in the morning and searching
the terrain with drones until after midday, with the harvested plants' smell
hanging over the area. [@1] Police asked anyone with information on the crop's
ownership to come forward. [@1]
