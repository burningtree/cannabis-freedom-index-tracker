Shannon Bragg, of Slater Street in Latchford, appeared at Manchester Crown Court
on Wednesday 5 August 2026 after pleading guilty to importing cannabis. [@2] The
27-year-old had landed at Manchester Airport on 2 June after flying from
Bangkok. [@1] She told border control officers she had spent three weeks in
Thailand and had checked in no luggage. [@1] Her hand luggage contained evidence
that she had checked in two cases. [@1] The cases held 32 black packages with a
total of 35.3 kg of cannabis. [@1]

The court heard that Bragg had been offered “a free holiday” by those above her
in the operation if she took the suitcases back to the UK. [@1] Her lawyer,
Hailey Bennett, said “naivety, immaturity, and an element of grooming” played
into her decision. [@1] Judge Nicholas Dean said people who go to Thailand
“either know or strongly suspect what they are bringing back”. [@1] Bragg had
been held in prison on remand awaiting sentence. [@1]

The judge said the offence warranted imprisonment, but that her time in custody
was “punishment enough for her”. [@1] He instead sentenced her to a 12-month
community order. [@1] The order requires up to 10 rehabilitation activity days
and a 12-month mental health treatment requirement. [@1] The judge said it was
“a sadness” to see a 27-year-old, intelligent woman before the court. [@1] He
said he was not convinced she was as naïve as she had indicated about what she
thought she was bringing back. [@1]
