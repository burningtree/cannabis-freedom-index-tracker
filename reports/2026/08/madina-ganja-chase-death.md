Officers from the Mandailing Natal branch of Indonesia's National Narcotics Agency, building on a
surveillance operation in Padang Laru, followed a green Honda Stylo motorcycle with two riders in
black jackets shortly after 01:45 on 20 August 2026. [@1,2] When the pair headed toward Panyabungan
with a large front bundle, officers tried to stop them. [@1,2]

BNNP North Sumatra said both suspects resisted with edged weapons and tried to flee; officers used
what the agency called measured firm action after the situation endangered them. [@1,2] Kumparan
reported two warning shots into the air before the suspects were secured. [@2]

Around 02:40 the motorcycle crashed into a ditch on the road in Darussalam village, Panyabungan Timur.
[@1,2] Officers seized 16 packages of dried cannabis weighing 15,332.65 grams and detained two men
initialled MAL, 25, and ZI, 23. [@1,2]

ZI weakened while being taken to the BNN district office and was moved to hospital, where staff found
a gunshot wound to the lower back; he was declared dead. [@1,2] MAL remained in custody for further
legal process according to the September briefing. [@1]
