On 2 and 3 August 2026, the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA) and police destroyed a
cannabis plantation in Barangay Butbut Proper, in the municipality of Tinglayan, Kalinga. [@1] The
6,000 square metres of plants were hidden inside a communal forest. [@1] Agents uprooted 48,000 fully
grown plants and burned them on the spot. [@1] The authorities valued them at 9.6 million pesos. [@1]

The operation was led by the PDEA's Kalinga office, with a provincial mobile force company, the
national police's drug enforcement group for the Cordillera, the Tinglayan police station and staff
of the provincial district jail. [@1] No suspects were arrested. [@1]
