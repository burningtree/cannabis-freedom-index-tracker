Police arrested five people accused of selling cannabis through online
transactions. [@1] Narcotics director Brigadier General Eko Hadi Santoso of
Bareskrim Polri, the National Police's criminal investigation department, said
cannabis was sold through an Instagram account called King_Garden and parcels
went out with the freight firm Indah Cargo, disguised as textiles. [@1] Police
said the arrests took place in Pasuruan, Brebes, Bogor and Medan in a series
from 17 August 2026. [@2] They were named as Samik Umar Syeban (28), Saiful
Rochman (28), Saiful Maulana (27), Sugito Halim (37) and Lio Yeheskiel Siburian
(22). [@1] Officers seized 9.4 kg of cannabis and some tramadol. [@2]

The case began on 15 August 2026 with a joint operation at Bakauheni port in
South Lampung. [@1] There, officers checking an Indah Cargo vehicle found two
cannabis parcels sent from Medan to Pasuruan and Brebes. [@1] Police then
followed the parcels to their recipients with the freight company's help. [@1]
Samik Umar Syeban was arrested in Pasuruan on 17 August after receiving a parcel
holding 2,000 g. [@1] He told officers an inmate at Malang Class I prison,
Saiful Rochman, had told him to collect it. [@1] Saiful Rochman admitted
ordering the cannabis through King_Garden for sale in Pasuruan. [@1] Saiful
Maulana was arrested in Brebes on 18 August with 500 g. [@2]

Sugito Halim was arrested in Bogor after receiving 1,960 g. [@2] Police said the
cannabis was meant for sale in Serang, Banten. [@2] Lio Yeheskiel Siburian was
arrested on 27 August at an Indah Cargo office on Jalan Jenderal Besar AH
Nasution in Medan. [@2] He was sending three parcels holding 2,000 g of
cannabis. [@2] A search of his rented room in Padang Bulan, Medan Selayang,
found 5 g of cannabis, 22 packets holding 900 g and a box with 2,000 g of
cannabis stems. [@2] Police said he admitted sending parcels to Pasuruan, Brebes
and Bogor. [@2]
