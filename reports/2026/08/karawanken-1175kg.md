On 20 August 2026, shortly before 11:00, officers of the Villach motorway police stopped a car with Slovenian plates on the Karawanken Autobahn, the A11, just after it had entered Austria. [@1] In the boot they found 11.75 kg of cannabis herb, packed as 20 parcels in a cardboard box. [@1] The driver and his passenger, both 30-year-old Slovenian citizens, had not hidden the drugs. [@1]

Police arrested both men and took them to Klagenfurt prison. [@1] The Carinthia state criminal office was investigating. [@1] ORF put the street value of the seized cannabis at about 120,000 euros. [@1]

The Carinthia police told the Krone that the drugs were found in the boot, in 20 parcels in a cardboard box, and that the two 30-year-olds were arrested and taken to Klagenfurt prison. [@2] The Krone wrote that the men had meant to bring 11.75 kg of cannabis into Carinthia. [@2] The same article said investigators were still asking where the herb came from and where the two men had been taking it. [@2]
