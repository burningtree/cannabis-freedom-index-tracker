On the night of Saturday 30 August 2026, Andorran police stopped a
51-year-old resident at the Runer river border as he entered the country in a
private car with his two minor children. [@1,2] During the check he tested
positive for drugs. [@1,2]

In the boot, officers found 229 grams of cannabis, nine cannabis cigarettes and
203 grams of hashish. [@1,2] A search of his home later turned up another
87 grams of cannabis, two grams of hashish, three precision scales and twelve
large plastic bags with cannabis residue. [@1,2] Police suspected he intended
to distribute the drugs. [@1,2]

He was arrested and held while investigators examined his phone. [@1,2] On
1 September a duty judge released him with charges for possession, import and
trafficking of narcotics, without sending him to prison. [@2]
