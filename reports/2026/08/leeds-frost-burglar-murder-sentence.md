On 31 August 2026, Adrian Frost, 61, was jailed for life and ordered to serve at least 27 years
before parole after a jury at Leeds Crown Court convicted him of murder. [@1] Sentencing,
Recorder of Leeds Guy Kearl KC said Frost had assembled an arsenal of weapons to protect his
home-grown cannabis operation and was prepared to use them, adding that Frost could have fired a
warning shot but instead lay in wait with the safety catch off. [@1]

Frost shot 26-year-old Connor Batty through the chest from less than 1.5 metres on the night of
11 September 2025, after Batty and two friends forced entry to his Barnsley Road home in
Hemsworth to steal the crop. [@1,2] The intruders, carrying an imitation handgun, a pickaxe
handle and shears, shouted that they knew what Frost had; Frost first called 999 to report the
break-in, then loaded his illegal 12-bore sawn-off shotgun and opened fire as they burst through
the door. [@1,2] Batty died shortly afterwards; Frost texted his brother that Batty was "dead as
a post", photographed the body, and jokingly asked the dying man if he wanted an aspirin. [@1,2]

Jurors unanimously rejected Frost's self-defence claim after less than a day; he had already
admitted producing Class B drugs and possessing the prohibited firearm. [@1] Batty's accomplices
were also jailed for the attempted burglary: Lewis Bromley to four years and six months, Dylan
Kelly to four years and four months, and Connor Turner to three years. [@1]
