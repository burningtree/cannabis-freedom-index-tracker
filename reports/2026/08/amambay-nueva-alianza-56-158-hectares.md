La Nación and Infobae reported that Operation Nueva Alianza 56 finished on 7 August 2026 after
ten days of raids in forest areas of Amambay department, on Paraguay’s border with Brazil.
[@1,2]

Paraguay’s National Anti-Drug Secretariat (SENAD) said the joint work with Brazil’s Polícia
Federal, Paraguayan prosecutors, CODI troops and the air force eradicated 158 hectares of
cannabis, destroyed 24,485 kilograms of ready marijuana and dismantled 71 clandestine camps.
[@1,2] SENAD estimated that 474 tonnes of potential production plus the processed drugs added
up to 498 tonnes kept from criminal markets, costing traffickers more than USD 73 million on
their pricing. [@1,2]

Infobae noted the sweeps focused on woodland near Pedro Juan Caballero and Capitán Bado. [@2]
Neither report named any arrests. [@1,2]

La Nación published a photograph from the operation. [@1]
