On 25 August 2026 the Kuala Lumpur High Court convicted Faliq Amar Abdul Rahim of two counts
of trafficking cannabis under section 39B of Malaysia's Dangerous Drugs Act 1952 and sentenced
him to life imprisonment from his arrest on 16 February 2022 plus 12 strokes of the cane on
each count, 24 strokes in total. [@1,2]

Police had arrested Faliq in Sentul on 16 February 2022 after he carried a black box toward a
shopping mall; the box held about 9.5 kg of cannabis in ten plastic packages while his wife
Norsyafika Nazwa Nurizan waited in the car. [@1,2] A raid on their condominium the same
evening found about 217 kg in boxes and sacks and paraphernalia for processing the drugs. [@1,2]

Judge Noor Ruwena Nurdin acquitted Norsyafika on both charges, finding no prima facie case
at the close of prosecution and, on the second count, that she knew of the drugs but did not
control them. [@1,2] Defence lawyers Afifuddin Ahmad Hafifi, Amirrul Jamaluddin and Aina
Melina Johan Ariff Lim appeared for the couple; deputy public prosecutor Shahrizzat Amadan
prosecuted. [@1]
