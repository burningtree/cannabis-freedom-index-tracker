Yongin Dongbu police said on 13 August that they had detained two cousins, including a man in his
30s known as A, for growing cannabis and selling it online, and referred them to prosecutors under
the Narcotics Control Act. [@1] From October last year to July, the pair leased two low-rise
multi-unit homes, fitted them with LED lights, hygrometers and insulating sheets, and grew seeds
bought on overseas websites. [@1]

They sold marijuana, cannabis jelly and liquid cannabis at 500,000 to one million won (about $360
to $720) a time, taking cash or cryptocurrency over social media. [@1] They had seeds delivered to
other people's addresses to evade detection, and themselves inhaled cannabis heated in a coffee
canister; a rapid drug test came back positive. [@1]

A resident's report about the suspicious pair led police to them through canvassing, and officers
arrested them on 6 August. [@1] Police seized 35 growing plants, 2.3 kilograms of marijuana, 2.15
million won in cash and about 17 million won in cryptocurrency; police put the marijuana's value at
about 230 million won (about $165,000), or some 4,600 doses. [@1] Buyer investigations continue,
with online drug crime named a focus of the second-half crackdown. [@1]
