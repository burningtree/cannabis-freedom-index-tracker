Police announced on 25 August 2026 that a 10-month investigation had shut down a Vietnamese-organised cannabis grow-house syndicate in Auckland. Twelve people were arrested in 19 raids; police seized about NZ$1.25 million of cultivation equipment, 35 kg of cannabis and NZ$80,000 in cash. Over the operation they found more than five tonnes of cannabis material at more than 50 rental properties, and 11 crop-sitters were deported. [@1]
