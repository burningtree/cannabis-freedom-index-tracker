Three men were arrested in a resident's garden in Desa Makmur Jaya, Darul
Hasanah district, Aceh Tenggara (Southeast Aceh), on Thursday 27 August 2026 at
about 5 pm. [@2] They were S, 47, from Desa Makmur Jaya; K, 51, from Desa Lawe
Beringin in Ketambe district; and UF, 32, from Desa Lawe Pinis in Darul Hasanah.
[@1] The operation followed a community report about cannabis dealing and
involved the narcotics unit of the Aceh Tenggara police and the criminal
investigation unit of the Lawe Alas police post. [@1]

At the garden, officers found a brown backpack containing several packages of
cannabis and another package wrapped in brown paper hidden behind a rock. [@1]
Police said the cannabis was divided into five parcels weighing 310.86, 120.12,
254.3, 12.14 and 11.4 grams. [@1] The total came to 708.82 grams. [@1] Officers
also seized 20,000 rupiah in cash, the brown backpack and 57 sheets of brown
paper that police said were used for packaging. [@1]

Aceh Tenggara police chief AKBP Rujiyanto Dwi Poernomo, speaking through public
relations officer Ipda Patar, said the campaign against narcotics would continue
across the district. [@1] He credited residents with providing the information
that led to the arrests. [@1] The three men and the evidence were taken to the
Aceh Tenggara police headquarters for further investigation. [@1]
