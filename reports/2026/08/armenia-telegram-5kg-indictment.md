The Investigative Committee said H.M. and A.K., who ran a Telegram account together, took 5 kg of marijuana from a spot named by another Telegram user and were arrested straight afterwards, before the deal could be completed. [@1,2]

Investigators also alleged that months earlier A.K. had bought methamphetamine via Telegram and sold it with a group member; public charges were brought over the narcotics trafficking. [@1,2]

One defendant was held in custody and the other under house arrest before the case file with a final indictment was sent to the supervising prosecutor on 21 August 2026. [@1,2]
