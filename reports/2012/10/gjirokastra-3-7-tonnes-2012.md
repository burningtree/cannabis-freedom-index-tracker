Police in Gjirokastër seized nearly 3.7 tonnes of cannabis on Friday 19 October 2012 in several
linked operations directed at trafficking toward Greece. [@1,2] Gjirokastër police chief Ajet Ibro
told Tirana Times that the cannabis was taken in five operations and that seven people were
arrested while four others were still being sought. [@2]

## Tanker at the Greek border

The largest haul was 3,360 kilograms found at the Kakavijë border crossing, hidden in secret
compartments of an oil tanker belonging to the Krujë firm Laerti Trans; police said the driver was
arrested. [@1] Balkan Insight reported that the load was divided into hundreds of sacks inside the
tank. [@1]

Police also seized cannabis in two other Gjirokastër operations the same evening, including 300
kilograms concealed in car tyres and a smaller quantity near the city, according to regional
reporting summarised by Balkan Insight and Tirana Times. [@1,2] Part of the shipment was believed
to originate from Lazarat, a village Balkan Insight describes as largely outside routine police
control. [@1]
