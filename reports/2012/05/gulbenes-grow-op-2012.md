In mid-May 2012, State Police in Vidzeme uncovered an illegal cannabis factory
in a house and farm outbuilding in Gulbene municipality, where marijuana had
been grown for about six months in fitted rooms heated and lit by a powerful
generator. [@1,2] Regional criminal police called it the largest drug cultivation
site ever found in Vidzeme. [@1]

## Seizures and arrests

During sanctioned searches, officers cut and removed more than 2,500 marijuana
plants at various growth stages, with a combined green weight of more than 300
kilograms, and seized several kilograms of dried plant material. [@1,2] Police
valued the drugs at several tens of thousands of lats, citing roughly seven
lats per gram of dried marijuana on the local market. [@1]

Nine people were detained on suspicion of making, growing and selling narcotic
drugs as an agreed group; four were remanded in custody. [@1,2] A criminal case
was opened under Latvia’s group production and trafficking provisions, carrying
five to ten years’ imprisonment. [@1] Investigators said the isolated property
appeared bought specifically for cultivation and that the crop may have been
sold outside Latvia as well. [@1,2]
