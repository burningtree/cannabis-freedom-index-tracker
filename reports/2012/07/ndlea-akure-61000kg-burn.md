On Monday 16 July 2012, National Drug Law Enforcement Agency officers burned 61,000 kilograms of
forfeited drug exhibits in Akure, the capital of Ondo State. [@1] Chairman Ahmadu Giade told guests
that cannabis cultivation threatened food production and that drug barons were diverting arable land
from legitimate crops. [@1]

## Ondo's role in national seizures

Giade said Ondo had led Nigeria in NDLEA seizures over the preceding two years, with 123,452.76
kilograms of cannabis seized and 514 hectares of cannabis farmland traced in that period. [@1] He
gave yearly breakdowns of 367 hectares traced in 2010, 147 hectares destroyed in 2011, and the
statewide cumulative figures announced at the ceremony. [@1] Ondo environment commissioner Sola
Ebisheni, standing in for Governor Olusegun Mimiko, urged communities to report cultivators living
among them. [@1]

## Public burn

Linda Ikeji's blog reported the destruction the same evening with photographs of stacked cannabis,
lighting the fire, and concealment methods including compressed tablets and jerrycans. [@2] Her post
treats the burned material as cannabis alongside other drugs but does not separate weights. [@2]
