Senad, Paraguay's anti-drug secretariat, said its agents eliminated 598 hectares of marijuana in
one week near the Brazilian border in Amambay department, in coordinated work with Brazil's Federal
Police and support from the U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration. [@1] Efe, quoted by El Diario, said
the agency also destroyed 62 drug-storage camps, 1,620 kilograms of chopped cannabis, and 236
kilograms of seeds, and that the fields could have produced about 1,800 tonnes of marijuana mostly
for Brazil. [@1]

## Nueva Alianza VII

Roughly 100 agents, three helicopters, and 15 vehicles searched forested areas where Senad said large
plantations were in growth or harvest stage. [@1] An earlier Senad bulletin on the same operation,
Nueva Alianza VII, reported that by 19 February teams had eradicated 398 hectares spread across 126
plots near Cadete Boquerón and Colo'o, close to Capitán Bado, and burned 44 camps holding 1,320
kilograms of chopped cannabis and 116 kilograms of seed. [@2] Senad technicians told the bulletin
that a harvested hectare averages about three tonnes ready for sale, which the agency used to
estimate a $35.8 million loss to traffickers from the 398-hectare phase alone. [@2]
