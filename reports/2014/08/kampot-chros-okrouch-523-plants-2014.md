Military police destroyed more than 500 marijuana plants in Kampot province on
Friday 29 August 2014. [@1] Sem Soeum, the deputy provincial military police
commander, said his officers found the plants after a gruelling search in the
forests of Chros O'Krouch mountain in Teuk Chhou district. [@1] He said the two
farms were in dense forest. [@1] The crop had an estimated value of $30,000,
according to the military police. [@1] The growers had not been identified. [@1]

The officers went to the mountain after a tip from bamboo collectors. [@1] The
search took three hours. [@1] Sem Soeum said officers found two farms with 523
marijuana plants in dense forest and decided to burn them. [@1] He said the
mission was difficult, and that the officers did not find the way to the plants
until they prayed to the mountain spirit. [@1] The officers burned the plants in
the dense forest where they found them. [@1]
