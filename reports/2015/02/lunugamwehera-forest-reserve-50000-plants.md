Three people were arrested in separate raids on cannabis plantations deep inside
the Lunugamwehera Forest Reserve, a senior police officer said. [@1] A Special
Task Force team from the Deberawewa Division carried out the raids. [@1] The
three suspects were from Angunukolapalessa. [@1] They and the ganja seized in
the operation were to be produced before the Tissamaharama Magistrate, while the
plants were burned inside the reserve. [@1]

Officers seized about 590 kg of finished ganja, meaning cannabis that had
already been processed, in the raids. [@1] They also seized 50,000 cannabis
plants. [@1] Ganja is the local word for cannabis. [@1] The plants were set on
fire inside the forest reserve, the police account said. [@1]

The raids were separate operations against plantations described as lying deep
inside the reserve. [@1] The arrests were made earlier in the week. [@1] The
plants were burned in the reserve, and the ganja was to be produced before the
Tissamaharama Magistrate. [@1]
