Police in Battambang province arrested Ren Sara, 36, on Sunday 9 August 2015
after patrolling officers came across him in a marijuana plantation hidden in
jungle beside a small stream in Tameun commune, Thma Koul district. [@1] The
officers uprooted and destroyed 236 plants from the field, according to Tameun
commune police chief Yeun Bunruen. [@1] A search of Sara's house in nearby Thmey
village then uncovered another 2 kilograms of dried marijuana. [@1]

Sara was initially sent to Thma Koul district police station for questioning.
[@1] Deputy district police chief Heang Buntha said the following day that Sara
had been released on compassionate grounds after thumb-printing an assurance
that he would not re-offend. [@1] Buntha said the grower came from a poor family
and had grown the plants to use himself and to help treat someone in his family.
[@1] “He did not grow the plants for sale, so we have just educated him and
released him,” Buntha said. [@1]
