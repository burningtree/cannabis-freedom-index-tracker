David Wyler, 55, of Poplar Avenue, Nottingham, received five years in prison at
Derby Crown Court after admitting producing and possessing cannabis and
possessing amphetamines. [@1] Police discovered the factory only when a
neighbour reported a possible burglary at the rented Ilkeston property. [@1]

## Factory and earnings

The house was entirely given over to cannabis production. [@1,2] Officers
seized 500 plants; forensic tests measured THC at 29%, which Derbyshire Police
described as the strongest cannabis then found in the UK, against a typical 12%
reading. [@1,2] Detectives said Wyler’s records showed about £600,000 earned
over seven years; police also seized £30,000 cash and three cars. [@1,2]

Detective Sergeant Stuart Kershaw said the setup was sophisticated commercial
production and that the sentence should deter others. [@1] The court listed a
later Proceeds of Crime Act hearing to pursue criminal assets. [@1] Wyler had
convinced his wife he was a respectable businessman while running the factory.
