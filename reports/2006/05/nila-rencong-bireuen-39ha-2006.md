Nearly 200 police and National Narcotics Agency (BNN) personnel spent just over a week on Operation
Nila Rencong II in Bireuen regency, Aceh, from 17 to 25 May 2006. [@1] BNN acting chief Made
Mangku Pastika told reporters in Banda Aceh that the sweep found about 39.7 hectares of cannabis
spread across seven subdistricts, including 15 hectares at Alue Limeung in Jeumpa and 10.5 hectares
at Panton Bili in Pandrah. [@1]

## Estimates on the ground

Pastika said the teams estimated roughly 47,989 cannabis plants with a combined weight of about
14,376 kilograms, and that 1,496 plants were destroyed at the scene. [@1] He said the operation
aimed to break cannabis trafficking networks under Indonesia’s P4GN drug-control framework. [@1]

A Tempo photo caption dated 25 May 2006 shows operation commander Badarul Zaman and Pastika
inspecting a cannabis field at Paya Nie in Bireuen during the same deployment. [@2] The caption
does not add further acreage or arrest figures beyond the Antara briefing. [@2]
