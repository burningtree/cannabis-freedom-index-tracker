Narcotics officers detained four suspects and seized about 1.5 tonnes of skunk
cannabis in a raid in the Urla district of İzmir. [@1] About 100 police officers
and two sniffer dogs took part in the operation. [@1] The four were held in
gözaltı, the Turkish term for police custody. [@1]

Police established that the suspects had rented two plots of land in Demircili,
a neighbourhood of Urla. [@1] Houses stood on the two rented plots of land. [@1]
The suspects brought the drug into Turkey by luxury yacht. [@1] The raid
followed about two months of surveillance. [@1]

The skunk was found to have been brought to Turkey from the Greek islands. [@1]
The drug is a cannabis product that Turkish reports describe as genetically
modified esrar. [@1]

The İzmir provincial security directorate's narcotics branch began the case
after information that drugs were being brought into Turkey by sea. [@1] The
investigation was examining the international dimension of the case. [@1]
