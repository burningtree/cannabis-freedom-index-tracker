On Thursday 19 April 2018 a police team travelled to Ebini on the Upper Berbice River and destroyed
about 30,000 cultivated cannabis plants on one and a half acres of land, according to a statement
by Superintendent Shivpersaud Bacchus reported by Stabroek News. [@1] Guyana Times, citing police
reports, said the plants ranged from one to six feet in height and that officers also found
seeds. [@2]

## Camp burned, one man held

Police burned the plants and seeds and destroyed a nearby camp, along with a Honda water pump and a
spray can found at the site. [@1,2] A 25-year-old resident of Angoy's Avenue, New Amsterdam, was
arrested and was assisting investigators; the opened sources do not name him. [@1,2]

Bacchus announced the operation on Thursday; both newspapers published their accounts on 20 and
21 April. [@1,2] The statements do not report charges filed or link the plot to other Berbice
seizures. [@1,2]
