On 1 October 2018 Si Sawat district chief Narit Songnipitkun said he had been
told that cannabis was being grown illegally on Khao Bo Luk Rang, a hill at Ban
Tha Sanun in Dan Mae Chalaep subdistrict, Kanchanaburi province. [@1] He led
senior district official Chakat Asana, a Volunteer Defence Corps company,
soldiers, Dan Mae Chalaep police and village headmen to the hilltop. [@1] The
party went on foot through bamboo forest up the steep slope. [@1]

At the top they found a temporary hut roofed with plastic, holding smoking
equipment, a cannabis pipe, cannabis seed and some dried cannabis. [@1] No one
was in the area, and officials believed the growers had fled before they
arrived. [@1] Behind the hut, 140 cannabis plants 2 to 3 metres tall were
growing through the surrounding forest. [@1]

Officers cut down all 140 plants, carried them down the hill and handed them to
investigators at Dan Mae Chalaep police station. [@1] The police were to
investigate to find the growers and take legal action. [@1]
