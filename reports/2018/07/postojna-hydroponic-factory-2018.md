Koper criminal police and Postojna officers searched a former shop in Postojna on
12 April 2018 and found computer-controlled hydroponic rooms on two floors with
2,140 cannabis plants at various stages of growth and more than 5 kg of dried
flowering tops in plastic containers. [@1] They removed the growing equipment
and wiring used for irrigation, ventilation and heating. [@1]

The suspect, a 34-year-old from the Postojna area, was abroad when the raid
took place; police arrested him on 14 April at the Bistrica ob Sotli border
crossing and a Koper investigating judge ordered house detention. [@1]

## Sentence

On 19 July 2018 the Koper District Court sentenced him to 19 months in prison,
which he may serve as 974 hours of community work, after he pleaded guilty.
[@2] The judge followed the prosecution proposal, citing his admission and lack
of prior convictions but also the large seizure and the operation’s duration.
[@2] Both parties waived appeal, making the judgment final. [@2] Criminal
investigators had earlier estimated that a single harvest from the seized plants
could have yielded hundreds of thousands of euros at local retail prices. [@1]
