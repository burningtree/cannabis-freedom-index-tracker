Senad, Paraguay’s national anti-drug secretariat, broke up what ABC Color called an international
marijuana trafficking network in five raids across Central Paraguay. [@1] The newspaper said ten
people were detained, including two retired police officers, while an active-duty officer remained
at large, and that agents seized 336 kilograms of pressed marijuana and 94 boxes of allegedly
counterfeit cigarettes. [@1]

## Raids around Asunción

According to ABC Color’s 11 August report, the operations ran the day before publication in the
central zone. [@1] A U.S. embassy narcotics summary written on 13 August said Senad seized 336
kilograms of marijuana on 9 August during raids on properties in Lambaré, Villa Elisa, San Lorenzo
and Capiatá, and that fourteen suspects including retired officer Zunilda Roig were later
imprisoned as part of the same organisation. [@2] The embassy text treats the group as an
international trafficking network but names no further destinations or sentences in the opened
extract. [@2]
