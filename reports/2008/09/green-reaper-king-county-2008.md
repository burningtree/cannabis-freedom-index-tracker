Federal agents and local police executed 13 search warrants on indoor marijuana grow operations
across King County early on 10 September 2008 as part of Operation Green Reaper. [@1,2] The DEA
said the raids targeted command-and-control elements of Puget Sound indoor grow organisations. [@1]

## Plants, cash and arrests

Law enforcement seized 4,578 marijuana plants, three weapons including an assault rifle and a
shotgun, about US$50,000 in cash and 20 vehicles, and took 20 people into custody. [@1,2] Kent
Police Chief Steve Strachan said the case reflected a large-scale criminal enterprise rather than
small-scale cultivation. [@2]

Assistant Special Agent in Charge David Dongilli warned that growers were modifying suburban homes
with unsafe wiring, irrigation and chemicals that could contaminate sewers and leave houses
uninhabitable. [@1,2] Agencies involved included the DEA, Kent, Auburn, Renton, Tukwila and Port
of Seattle police, with support from the Washington State Patrol, Seattle Police, King County
Sheriff's Office and others. [@1,2]
