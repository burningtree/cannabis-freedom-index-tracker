Moroccan authorities arrested four suspected drug traffickers and seized about 12 tonnes of cannabis
during a house search in Ljmiane, in Tétouan province. [@1] The gendarmerie reported eight tonnes of
kif in stalks, four tonnes of ground cannabis and 2.3 kilograms of powdered cannabis resin. [@1]

## Presentation to prosecutors

The four suspects were handed to the prosecutor, Bladi.net reported. [@1] One of them allegedly tried
to bribe gendarmes with 9,800 dirhams. [@1] The article gives no names, travel routes or export
destinations. [@1] It notes official figures that cannabis was planted on about 72,500 hectares in
2005, down from 134,000 hectares in 2003, but those national cultivation statistics are background
rather than part of this seizure. [@1]
