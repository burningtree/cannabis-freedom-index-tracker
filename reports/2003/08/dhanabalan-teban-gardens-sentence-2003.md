Dhanabalan s/o A Gopalkrishnan, aged 33, pleaded guilty to two charges of trafficking in cannabis
after Central Narcotics Bureau officers arrested him at 8.35 p.m. on 24 February 2003 in a flat at
Blk 52 Teban Gardens while he cut and packed cannabis from a larger block. [@1] The first charge
concerned 499.99 g of cannabis and the second 749.99 g of cannabis mixture. [@1]

## Minimum sentence for an assistant

Justice Choo Han Teck noted that Dhanabalan had lost work, accepted $100 per block from his friend
Raman Selvam to pack the drugs, and refused to move the finished packages. [@1] The prosecution
accepted that he was not the main trafficker, had no prior drug record, and had helped investigators
against Raman. [@1] The minimum penalty for each charge was 20 years' imprisonment and 15 strokes
of the cane; the judge imposed those terms concurrently but limited the total caning to 24 strokes
under the Criminal Procedure Code. [@1]
