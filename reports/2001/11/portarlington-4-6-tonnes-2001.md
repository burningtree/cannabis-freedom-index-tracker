Gardaí and Revenue Customs officers intercepted one of the largest cannabis loads in Irish history
late on Friday 16 November 2001 at Portarlington, County Laois. [@1] The consignment of 4.6 tonnes
of herbal cannabis had arrived through Dublin Port on the previous Thursday in a container routed
from Durban, South Africa, via Antwerp, Belgium. [@1,2]

## Hidden in door panels

The drugs were packed inside a shipment of doors stacked flat with the interiors removed, wrapped
in brown or clear plastic. [@1] Detective Superintendent Barry O'Brien of the Garda National Drugs
Unit said the haul was worth about £9 million (€11.43 million) and was many times larger than
anything intercepted in recent years. [@1] Four men were detained under Section 2 of the Drug
Trafficking Act; the Irish Times said they were believed to be from Dublin, Cork and Tullamore,
County Offaly. [@1]

On 19 November the Irish Examiner reported that Sean Whelan, Kenneth O'Mahony, Jason Howe and John
Gallagher, with addresses in County Laois, Cork and Dublin, were remanded in custody after being
charged over the same Friday-night seizure. [@2] Gardaí and Customs stressed that the investigation
was continuing and declined to detail how long the operation had been under way. [@1]
