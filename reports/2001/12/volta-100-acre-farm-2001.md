Ghana's Narcotics Control Board said on Saturday that a combined team of police, prison officers
and armed forces had destroyed a 100-acre marijuana farm in the Volta Region and arrested ten
people in connection with it. [@1,2] The board said those held included a couple and three
students. [@1,2]

## Farm burned after arrests

The Narcotics Control Board said the mature cannabis on the farm weighed three tonnes. [@1,2] It
added that drug barons in Ghana and outside the country had sponsored people to grow the marijuana,
and that the farm was later set ablaze. [@1,2]

The board said more farms had been identified and would be destroyed; the opened reports do not
describe those later operations. [@1,2] None of them name the detainees or any charges filed. [@1,2]
