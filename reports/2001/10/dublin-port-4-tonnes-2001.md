Customs officers at Dublin Port seized just under four tonnes of herbal cannabis on Friday
26 October 2001, in what the Irish Examiner reported as the largest cannabis seizure Ireland had
seen. [@1] The high-quality herb was hidden in a forty-foot container of dried fruit that had arrived
from South Africa within the previous couple of days. [@1]

## Transit route and surveillance

Tom Brady reported in the Irish Independent that gardaí and customs had watched the container after
it reached the port on a ship from Antwerp on Tuesday, hoping to arrest a trafficker who never
appeared. [@2] Officers moved in on Friday morning and announced the seizure. [@2] Brady wrote that
the load weighed 3,992 kilograms, that customs valued it at £14 million, and that detectives
estimated a British street value of about £8 million. [@2] Customs said one man was arrested and
passed to gardaí, while gardaí told the Independent they were holding nobody over the find. [@1,2]
Both outlets said the consignment was mainly destined for Britain. [@1,2]
