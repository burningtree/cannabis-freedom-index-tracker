Robert Price, Scott Weaver and brothers Andrew and Graham Lee received prison terms at Leeds Crown
Court on 23 December 2010 after admitting offences linked to an international cannabis ring whose
northern store police had raided in Wrenthorpe, Wakefield, in October 2008. [@1,2]

## Sentences

Price, 55, of Hollyshaw Terrace, Whitkirk, Leeds, was jailed for eight years; Weaver, 30, of the
same address, for five years and three months; and Andrew Lee, 46, of Wrenthorpe Road, Wakefield,
and Graham Lee, 43, formerly of Bramley, Leeds, for three years and four months each. [@1,2] Judge
Kerry MacGill said the cannabis involved was a huge amount and called the operation professional
and on a grand scale. [@1,2]

## The 2008 warehouse

Officers searching a lock-up on Wrenthorpe Road found about three and a half tonnes of cannabis
hidden in flower boxes, with grinding scales and other distribution equipment, after intelligence
that Andrew Lee had rented the unit. [@1,2] Detective Inspector Stuart Spencer said the premises
had served as the northern hub for an international ring and that he had never seen so much cannabis
before. [@1] Yorkshire Post reporting said Price lived lavishly, with luxury cars and £100,000 in
cash under his floorboards, while further Leeds raids recovered more drugs and £175,000 in cash. [@2]
Police said asset-recovery action would follow under the Proceeds of Crime Act. [@1]
