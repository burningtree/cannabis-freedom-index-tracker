Mark Graham, James Simpson and Jon Dullighan received prison sentences at Mold Crown Court after
admitting their roles in a cannabis production ring that dried and shipped skunk to London and
Birmingham. [@1] Graham, 55, of Abergele, was jailed for four years and eight months; Simpson, 34, of
Old Colwyn, for four years; and Dullighan, 29, of Talacre, for two and a half years. [@1]

## Factory raid

North Wales Police searched three adapted units at the Tir Llwyd industrial estate in Kinmel Bay on
18 June 2010 and seized almost 4,700 cannabis plants. [@1,2] Detective Inspector Iestyn Davies said
the force estimated the operation’s output at more than £1 million a year in street value. [@1,2]
Judge Niclas Parry called it one of the biggest cannabis producers ever found in north Wales and
described the site as a rolling production line. [@1,2] Graham and Simpson had been involved for
years and had invested in property from the profits, the judge said, while Dullighan worked as the
gardener who kept the crop growing. [@2] Police said a financial investigation would seek to
confiscate assets under drug-trafficking law. [@1,2]
