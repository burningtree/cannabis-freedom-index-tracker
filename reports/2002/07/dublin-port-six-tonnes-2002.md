Revenue Customs officers at Dublin Port removed more than six tonnes of herbal cannabis from a
container that had arrived on the previous Thursday, in what the Irish Times called the largest
Irish seizure of herbal cannabis on record. [@1] The container had been routed from Bangkok through
Singapore and Antwerp, and the freight profiling unit flagged it as high risk before officers
searched it. [@1]

## Hidden behind furniture

The front of the container was packed with concrete garden furniture intended to obstruct
examination, according to Brendan Smyth of the Customs and Excise Enforcement Division. [@1] Irish
Examiner reporting the same day said officers used a labrador sniffer dog named Harvey after routine
profiling, and found the cannabis concealed behind heavy concrete benches. [@2] Customs put the
street value at about €15 million and said laboratory analysis showed a high active-ingredient
content of up to 40 per cent. [@1]

In a follow-up operation, a 42-year-old Dutch man was arrested and detained under Section 2 of the
Drug Trafficking Act at Killarney Garda station. [@2] The reports name no other arrests at the port
itself. [@1,2]
