Hilo Vice Section officers destroyed 14,425 marijuana plants, from seedlings to
six feet tall, in a five-day eradication effort that ended on Friday 28 June
2002 in Puna, Hilo and North Hilo. [@1] Lieutenant Henry Tavares said the count
was a significant increase over a three-day May operation that removed 2,973
plants. [@1]

## Arrest at Mountain View

Officers arrested Lenora Aitcheson, 46, of Mountain View, about 10 a.m. that
Friday after finding 911 plants on her property and inside her house. [@1] She
was held in the Hilo police cellblock pending charges. [@1] Tavares said police
hoped to obtain federal prosecution because of the large indoor and outdoor
operation. [@1] On 2 July, vice officers turned her over to federal drug agents
for that purpose. [@2]

Tavares attributed the high plant count to the growing season and said mature
taller plants were easier to spot from helicopters at 1,000 feet. [@1] The
Advertiser reported that, besides Aitcheson’s 911 plants, police uprooted more
than 13,500 other plants in the same districts during the sweep. [@2]
