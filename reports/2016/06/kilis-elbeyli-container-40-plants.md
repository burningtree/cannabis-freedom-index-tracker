A Syrian national identified only as A.H. lived in the container that
gendarmerie teams searched at a konaklama tesisi, an accommodation facility, in
Beşeri village, Elbeyli district, Kilis province. [@1] The facility houses
Syrians who fled their country's civil war. [@1] Officers found 40 cannabis
plants and detained A.H. over the incident. [@1] The Kilis Chief Public
Prosecutor's Office said the search followed a tip-off. [@1]

The plants, known in Turkish as Hint keneviri, were found planted in pots and in
the soil. [@1] They were seized during the search. [@1] The gendarmerie teams
that carried out the search were from the provincial command. [@1]

After processing at the gendarmerie, the suspect was referred to the judicial
authorities. [@1] The prosecutor's office said the investigation into the case
was continuing. [@2]
