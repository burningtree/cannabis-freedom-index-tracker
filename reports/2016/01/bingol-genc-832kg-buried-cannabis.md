The Bingöl provincial governor's office said gendarmerie teams began work after
intelligence that drugs were buried in the ground. [@1] There they found 832
kilograms of powdered cannabis, called toz esrar in Turkish, in 18 blue jerry
cans buried in the ground, the governor's office said. [@2] A drug-detection dog
named Canlı took part in the search, according to the governor's office. [@3]

The governor's office said legal proceedings had begun against two suspects in
connection with the find. [@2] The cannabis was confiscated on the public
prosecutor's instruction. [@2]

The governor's office put the value of the cannabis at about 4.16 million lira.
[@2] It said the seizure had struck a heavy blow against what it called the most
important financial source of terrorism and that the gendarmerie would continue
its fight against drug smuggling without interruption. [@1]
