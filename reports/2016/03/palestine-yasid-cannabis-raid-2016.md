Police raided seven greenhouses in the village of Yasid, north of Nablus, and seized more than 2,000 cannabis saplings. Two suspects were arrested after they tried to flee; the raid was part of a West Bank crackdown that also seized 370 plants and 54 kg of dried cannabis in Jenin. [@1]
