Police arrived to search the Teplice flat of a 25-year-old suspected of loan
frauds. [@1] They found a secret room hidden behind a refrigerator. [@1] The
TOXI anti-drug unit seized 125 cannabis plants the man was drying there. [@1]

Questioned, he said a friend had told him about a cannabis grow in an abandoned
complex in Újezdeček, and that he broke in around midnight with bags and garden
shears. [@1] He cut as many plants as he could carry, drove them home and put
them to dry, saying he wanted the marijuana for himself because he was a regular
smoker. [@1]

Detectives then focused on the grow, in the former Krušnohorské strojírny
engineering works, and found the company renting the building paid unusually
high electricity and water bills. [@1] When the company's director arrived at
the hall, officers detained him. [@1] Inside they found more than 1,000
flowerpots, bags of already harvested cannabis and indoor growing equipment.
[@1] Police also seized fertilisers, wiring, discharge lamps and fans used in
the grow. [@1]

The 40-year-old director, who also acted as gardener, admitted growing the
cannabis. [@1] The 25-year-old was charged with theft and the illegal production
and handling of narcotics and faced up to five years. [@2] The 40-year-old
director faced two to ten years for attempted illegal production, police said.
[@1]
