Police and customs seized a package of live cannabis seedlings during maritime cargo checks on 1 June, the national police reported.[@1] Officers detained a 43-year-old who came to collect it; police said relatives had sent the parcel from Ireland and that examination of its quantity was continuing.[@1] The suspect was referred for an initial court hearing on 3 June.[@1] [@1]
