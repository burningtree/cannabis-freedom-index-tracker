The Tripoli-based Government of National Unity organised a new Anti-Narcotics and Psychotropic Substances Agency with its headquarters in Tripoli and branches to be opened by the interior minister. The agency was given powers over cultivation, trafficking and use, treatment and rehabilitation, and its own budget, including half of the assets confiscated in drug cases. [@1]
