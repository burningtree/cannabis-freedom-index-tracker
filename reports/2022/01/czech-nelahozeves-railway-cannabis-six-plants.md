A 29-year-old man from Mělník confessed to police that he grew cannabis beside
the railway track in Nelahozeves in at least two crops in 2021. [@1] On Thursday
6 January 2022, Mělník criminal investigators notified him that he was suspected
of the illegal production and other handling of narcotic and psychotropic
substances and poisons. [@1]

Between April and July 2021 he germinated three plants at home, transplanted
them into flowerpots and took them to the railway track, where he watered them
and let them grow. [@1] In July he harvested them and used them himself, smoking
the marijuana and making an ointment from the leftover leaves. [@1] He repeated
the process between July and September 2021, growing and harvesting three more
plants, and this time asked a friend to pick him up and drive him away with the
harvest. [@1]

Police stopped and checked the car during that drive and found the freshly
harvested marijuana with him. [@1] Officers seized it and sent it to the
criminalistics and expertise unit (OKTE) in Kladno, whose expert opinion
confirmed 1,266 grams of cannabis plant. [@1] He faces one to five years in
prison if he is charged and convicted. [@1]
