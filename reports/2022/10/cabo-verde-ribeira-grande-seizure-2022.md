The Judicial Police seized about 717 kg of cannabis in Tronco, Ribeira
Grande de Santiago, during field checks of cannabis-growing areas; 17 kg
was ready to enter the market.[@1]

A 38-year-old local man was detained outside flagrante delicto, and a court
imposed preventive detention, the harshest pre-trial measure.[@1]

All seized drugs were incinerated on site after weighing.[@1]
