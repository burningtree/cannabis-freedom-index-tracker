Police in Blansko found five cannabis plants about 180 centimetres tall on the
balcony of a 39-year-old man's flat. [@2] He had grown the plants from March to
August 2019. [@1] He let officers into the flat during their investigation. [@2]
Police seized the plants and sent them for expert examination. [@1]

Police said the grower faced a fine of up to 15,000 koruna for a misdemeanour
under the law on protection against drug addiction. [@1] The fine would be
decided in administrative proceedings. [@2] Spokeswoman Lenka Koryťáková said
the belief that people may keep a small amount of drugs for personal use remains
a widespread myth. [@1] She said doing so is always illegal, as is using drugs
anywhere in a publicly accessible place. [@1]

Blesk reported that police went to the flat in a panel building in central
Blansko after a tip. [@3] The outlet said the man was likely to receive only a
financial penalty. [@3]
