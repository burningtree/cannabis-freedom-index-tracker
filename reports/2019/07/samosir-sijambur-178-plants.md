Samosir resort police in North Sumatra found a cannabis field on Friday 26 July
2019 and held the man they said owned it. [@1] Radar Medan placed the field in
Dusun I Pasar Bengkok, Sijambur village, Ronggur Nihuta district, and put its
size at about 1 rante, a local land measure. [@1] Metro Rakyat, quoting the head
of the drug unit, placed the same find in Panaharan, Sijambur, also at 1 rante.
[@2] Officers held 42-year-old Pardamean Siringo-ringo, also called PS, a
resident of Panaharan in Sijambur. [@2] Ronggur Nihuta subdistrict head Sittor
Silalahi told Radar Medan that police had been at the field since the night
before and seized a man of the Siringoringo clan when he came to it the next
day. [@1] Iptu Natar Sibarani, speaking for police chief AKBP Agus Darojat, said
officers had watched the site for four days and arrested the man without
resistance as he was about to leave a house. [@2] Sibarani said he was a repeat
offender in the same kind of case under Karo police in 2009. [@2]

The drug unit took him and the plants to Samosir police headquarters. [@2]
Officers seized 178 cannabis plants ready for harvest and about 5 kilograms of
picked cannabis leaves, Sibarani said. [@2] Radar Medan reporters saw stacks of
cannabis plants, wrapped and tied in plastic jute sacks, already held in the
yard of the station. [@1]

Deputy police chief Kompol CH. Simanjuntak told Radar Medan he could not yet
give a fuller account, because officers had only just come back from the site
and a briefing would follow the next day. [@1] Sibarani said the case began with
a tip that cannabis was growing in Ronggur Nihuta, which a check by officers
confirmed. [@2]
