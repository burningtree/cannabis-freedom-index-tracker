The Social Services Department reported regular interviews and psychosocial support for 98 people benefiting from the supervised-release law. The figure covered drug users together; the report gave no separate cannabis count.[@1] It documented the support work following the 2016 law’s treatment alternative to sentencing.[@1,2] [@1,2]
