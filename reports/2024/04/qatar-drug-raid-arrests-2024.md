The Ministry of Interior published video of a surveillance operation that ended with a car chase and the arrest of two men accused of trafficking drugs. Officers searched the men and their vehicles and said they found illegal items, but the announcement gave no quantity or charge. The ministry did not say what happened to the men after the arrest.
