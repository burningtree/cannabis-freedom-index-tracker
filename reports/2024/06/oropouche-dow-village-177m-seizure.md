Acting on intelligence that a drug trafficking network in the south-western peninsula
had smuggled a large quantity of narcotics over the weekend, Southern Division Task
Force officers staked out and raided an apartment in Dow Village, South Oropouche,
during the hours of 1pm and 8pm on a Tuesday. [@1]

Inside they found 117 packets of high-grade marijuana: 66 packets of US-grown
marijuana weighing 48.7 kg with an estimated street value of TT$12,170,378, and 51
packets of Colombian-grown marijuana weighing 51 kg worth TT$5,597,097, for a total
of 99.7 kg valued at TT$17,767,475. [@1]

A 63-year-old man of South Oropouche was arrested for possession of cannabis for
the purpose of trafficking. [@1]
