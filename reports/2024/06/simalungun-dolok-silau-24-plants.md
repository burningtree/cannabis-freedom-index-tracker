Police found a cannabis field ready for harvest, hidden among coffee trees in
Juma Lepar, Nagori Ujung Bawang, a village in Dolok Silau district, Simalungun,
North Sumatra. [@1] The field was discovered on Friday 14 June 2024. [@2] Police
seized 24 plants, about a metre tall, from a field belonging to SG, also known
as Cege. [@1] The plants were estimated to be about a year old. [@2] They had
been tied to coffee trunks or long bamboo so they would not stand out. [@2]
Officers uprooted all 24 and took them to the police station as evidence. [@2]

SG was 45, police said. [@3] He fled when officers went to his house, escaping
through the kitchen door into a ravine. [@2] Police said he was still being
hunted. [@2] His wife SMB, 38, and child RAG, 17, were taken to the Dolok Silau
police station for questioning. [@2] AKP Irvan Rinaldy Pane, head of the
Simalungun narcotics unit, said the operation followed a report from local
residents. [@3] Village officials accompanied the officers, he said. [@3]
