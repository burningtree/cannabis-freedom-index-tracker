Litoměřice detectives charged a 33-year-old man from the Lovosice area with the
particularly serious crime of illegal production and handling of narcotic and
psychotropic substances and poisons. [@1] In recent days officers from the
Litoměřice criminal police's TOXI team came for him. [@1] During a search of his
home, police seized cannabis plants, equipment used to grow cannabis and at
least 1,000 grams (1 kg) of dried plant parts that an expert examination
confirmed contained the active substance, police spokeswoman Pavla Kofrová said.
[@1] Police did not specify how many plants he grew at home. [@1]

According to the police account, he grew an unspecified number of cannabis
plants indoors at his home using various items and substances intended for
indoor cultivation, and then dried them. [@2] Police said he kept the dried
plants loose in paper bags and plastic bags and sold them or gave them away to
other people at various places in the Litoměřice district. [@2] He also made and
sold products containing dried cannabis, police said. [@1] If a court finds him
guilty, he faces up to five years in prison. [@1]
