Majuro Atoll Local Police searched the national police jail in Uliga on 3 August 2024 and found marijuana, crystal meth, cash, mobile phones and alcohol in prisoners' cells. The raid followed a sting in which an inmate sold methamphetamine to a police informant. Two inmates were later charged with drug offences, one with unlawful possession of marijuana and the other with unlawful selling of prohibited drugs. A police informant said packages brought by families were not being thoroughly searched. [@1,2]
