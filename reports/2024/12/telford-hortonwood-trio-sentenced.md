Three men have been jailed after a cannabis farm worth £1.5 million was
discovered in Telford in November 2024. [@1] [@2]

Ramandan Kapaj, 27, Emiljano Xhafa, 30, and Genti Binjaku, 45, were each
given a 27-month custodial sentence at Shrewsbury Crown Court on 11
December 2024 after pleading guilty to being concerned in the production
of a class B drug. [@1] [@2] All of no fixed abode, they were arrested on
8 November when Telford's Neighbourhood Crime Fighting Team and
Proactive CID raided an abandoned industrial unit in Hortonwood,
forcing entry with a ripsaw after the men tried to break down a door to
escape. [@1] Inside were fourteen rooms of cannabis plants at various
stages plus a second floor the men had built for more plants, with
tampered electrics. [@1]
