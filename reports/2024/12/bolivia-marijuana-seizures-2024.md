Government minister Eduardo Del Castillo reported that the anti-narcotics force Felcn had seized about 1,269 tonnes of marijuana and 139 tonnes of cocaine between November 2020 and December 2024, and that 13,359 people had been arrested for drug trafficking in that time. [@1]
