In July 2024 the drug-control service mapped 627 hectares of wild cannabis and sent 90 brigades to cut and burn it. The service reported more than 85 tonnes destroyed, including 27 tonnes in Chuy region, 17 tonnes in Issyk-Kul and Naryn, 15 tonnes in Bishkek and smaller amounts in the south. The operation was part of the annual Kara-kuurai eradication campaign. [@1]
