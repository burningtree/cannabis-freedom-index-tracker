In FSM v. Ehsa, customs found 183 g of marijuana joints in a parcel
addressed to a Pohnpei post office box.[@1]

The court upheld probable cause under the possession and trafficking
provisions and rejected search and entrapment arguments at the preliminary
stage.[@1]

The final disposition was not traced.[@1]
