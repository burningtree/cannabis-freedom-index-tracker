Police stopped two men aged 32 and 33 in Ulaanbaatar’s Songinokhairkhan district on 12 September 2024 with about 17 kg of cannabis plants. The case was cited under Article 20.7 of the Criminal Code; no verdict was reported. It was one of the few cannabis-specific seizures reported that year. [@1,2]
