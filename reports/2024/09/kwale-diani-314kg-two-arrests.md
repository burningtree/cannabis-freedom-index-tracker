Authorities in Kwale County arrested two suspects and seized bhang valued at
Sh9 million in an ongoing coastal drug-trafficking crackdown. [@1] The
operation in Diani combined ANU Mombasa detectives with DCI Diani
investigators. [@1]

On a public tip-off, officers apprehended a suspected drug trafficker in the
Chief's area of Diani; his arrest led to 10 sacks of bhang weighing 314 kg
with a street value of Sh9.4 million. [@1] A 30-year-old woman was then
arrested in a nearby house, and both suspects were held with the narcotics
secured as evidence. [@1]

In a separate operation in the same crackdown period, Mombasa officers
raided a Kiembeni residence on credible intelligence and recovered 16 sacks
of suspected cannabis worth Sh25 million, arresting the suspected
trafficker's wife to assist investigations. [@1]
