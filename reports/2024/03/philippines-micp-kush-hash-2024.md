PDEA, Customs and the PNP Maritime Group seized 278.32 kg of marijuana kush, 2.36 litres of liquid marijuana in vape cartridges and 880.25 grams of hash at the Manila International Container Port. The same operation found 1.06 kg of dried mushroom. The mixed cargo showed high-grade flower, resin and vape products moving together through a Manila container port, part of the kush influx the ASEAN drug report noted for 2024. [@1]
