BNN destroyed about seven tonnes wet weight of mostly ready-to-harvest cannabis on four hectares in Aceh Besar, roughly 20,000 plants, on 6 March 2024. Days earlier it had seized 200 kg of dried cannabis and arrested a suspect in the same district. The fields were found with monitoring by the national research agency (BRIN) and the geospatial agency (BIG). [@1]
