Police in Akto County, in China's Xinjiang region, arrested a man identified
only as Mi on New Year's Day 2024. [@1] With the help of a drug-detection dog,
officers then seized 627 grams of raw cannabis plants. [@1] The arrest followed
a tip that drugs might be hidden in his home. [@1] The Akto County Public
Security Bureau's anti-drug brigade had received the tip on 31 December 2023,
the day before the arrest, and quickly began an investigation. [@1]

The county public security bureau said the operation had taken more than 600
grams of drugs out of circulation. [@2] Police described the action as a joint
operation by several police units, with the dog assisting. [@1] The material
seized was 毒品原植物大麻, cannabis in raw plant form rather than a processed product.
[@1]

During questioning, Mi confessed to using drugs. [@1] The case was still under
investigation, and no charge, court date or other suspect was named. [@1]
