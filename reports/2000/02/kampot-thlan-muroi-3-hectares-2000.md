Kampot deputy police chief Keow Tha was suspended on Tuesday over suspected
involvement in the province's cannabis plantations, Governor Ly Sou confirmed on
Thursday. [@1] The suspension came as a 150-strong military police force led by
top military and police officials destroyed a 3-hectare cannabis field at Phnom
Thlan Muroi in Kampot province. [@1] It was the second plantation destroyed in
Kampot in a week. [@1] National Police director general Hok Lundy said police
were investigating others possibly involved, including a Chinese businessman.
[@1]

The field lay 13 km by road from a 7-hectare plantation cut down the previous
Saturday. [@1] Hok Lundy said the first stage was to destroy the cannabis and
the second to bring the plantation's organisers to justice. [@1] He warned that
high-ranking officials would not be exempt from the crackdown. [@1] The 150
military police would stay until all the plants were destroyed, officials said.
[@1] Ly Sou said more hectares of cannabis might still be found in the area.
[@1]

An Asian diplomat called the two operations a public relations move. [@1] The
diplomat said the authorities had known about the plantations since late last
year but did not act because powerful officials were involved. [@1] Khieu
Sopheak, deputy secretary general of the National Authority for Combating Drugs,
said the operations showed the government's commitment and determination to
crack down on cannabis in the area. [@1] Kun Kim, newly appointed deputy
commander-in-chief of the Royal Cambodian Armed Forces, was also present at the
operation. [@1] He said the plantations could not have existed without the
support of former Khmer Rouge forces. [@1]

Ly Sou said the plantations were protected by Kampot-based military and police
officials who worked with a Chinese businessman and Thai partners who financed
the operation. [@2] He said he had known of the plantations for months but did
not have the power to move against them. [@2] He accused Lin Chhan, an RCAF
deputy commander, of involvement with the plantations. [@2]
